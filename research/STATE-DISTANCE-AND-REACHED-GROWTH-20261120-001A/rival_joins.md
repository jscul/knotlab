# What the two rival joins change

Adding direct pairs makes the least possible common-descendant delay on every funded all-live tree equal to half the ordinary holder distance rounded up. Admitting a live common witness gives the same formula on funded all-live trees with at least three knots. Neither rule can join across two adjacent spent holders of a tree. The same frozen state reading can therefore give a delay of two or a delay of infinity under **each** rule.

## The two changes of rule

Take the ordinary complete proposal set. D retains it and adds a binary birth for each pair of distinct live knots directly sharing a distinction that no ordinary group already covers. W replaces each ordinary group G by all distinct groups G∪{w} as w ranges over its live common witnesses. A common witness holds with every member of G. If there is none, G remains. Each included w must supply its own loose distinction. W retains a set just once when several constructions give exactly the same true parents. Both rules keep the all-or-nothing funding rule, the unary birth for each unused opening and the ordinary forecast of actual next-step requirements. These are choices supplied to growth.

After a funded step, each old loose mark has exactly two holders, its old parent and its one actual child. All new loose marks are distinct names held by one newborn each. Two live newborns therefore share no mark directly, and every physical witness of a pair of newborns is an older, spent holder. Hence both added eligibility rules are inactive on **every reached cut**. This proves the first newborn forecast from its full parental overlaps is ordinary under both rules. The two preparations then use ordinary dynamics on their own first rosters. This conclusion uses the same loose-input supply as the ordinary law.

## Agreement on all-live starts and its failure on other trees

On a physical tree the ordinary candidates are precisely the individual length-two pairs through a common holder. With every knot live, D covers all pairs at distance one or two. In any connected all-live tree with at least three holders, each adjacent pair `a,b` has a third live neighbor c attached to at least one of them. The ordinary length-two pair using that third knot receives the middle knot as W's witness. Hence W too covers every length-one pair as well as all length-two pairs. The lower bound `ceil(k/2)` is attained by grouping the unique path in pieces of length two and possibly one. For two three-place holders W has no ordinary group to enlarge and cannot make the neighboring pair meet; D does. The funding qualification has consequences even in the three-holder case: D demands two inputs of the three-place middle holder but it has only one. W uses that middle once in a triple and can grow. On an all-live tree of fixed constant arity, the state ruler is an affine function of k, so these statements yield genuine scalar agreements for the indicated class.

Take the five-holder physical path `0,1,2,3,4` with six places in each holder. For a second preparation keep all path arities and, at each of 1,2,3, make each of four old loose distinctions shared with its own new terminal six-place knot. The three former interior holders are now spent. Each of the 12 new holders has five private marks. Both endpoints still have five private marks. Each tree is lawful. For not-all-equal, the tree transfer permits every prescribed nonconstant word at one holder and every individual erasure witness. For parity fixing the word at one holder leaves only independent attached trees, each completable away from its fixed shared name. Both realizations also passed exact local projection tests under both laws.

All three rules fund both preparations. On the first path, ordinary growth makes 19 first births, D makes 15 and W makes 16. Under each, `0,2,4` is a two-step co-input route and none can cover physical distance four in its first step. A literal common descendant appears on the second cut. On the second path, each spent holder witnesses only its own live neighbors, with blocks of sizes five, four and five. A member participates in at most four binary groups and has five openings. No two live holders meet directly and there is no live common witness to include. All three rules make 44 births with the same parents. Any route across the ordinary meeting between spent 1 and spent 2 would require an initial live co-input pair separated by at least three ordinary meetings. None exists. Subsequent children draw only from their own witnessed part. The tagged endpoints never have a common descendant.

At the two endpoints both preparations have `C=−31⁻⁵`, `ρ=14314576/28629151` and `Dτ=5 log₃31−1`. Parity gives the same canonical bond distance 21 and the same disagreement probability 1/2. The minimum cut giving this bond is not inferred from a similar appearance: the tree cut formula proves it and an independent connected-side enumeration found exactly the original five-holder route. The observed delay is **2 in one and infinity in the other under O, D and W**. Consequently no function of the named marked pair readings, even their combination, gives common descent on every funded acyclic start for any rule in this family. The five- and seventeen-holder witnesses establish existence. A minimum size for pairs of equal-scalar witnesses is not needed for this obstruction. ## Source and whole-state tests

In the seven-knot recurring source, ordinary growth spends all 16 loose distinctions on eight pairs and releases at arbitrarily late cuts by the existing source proof. D proposes 13 binaries, exceeding supply by two at each of five holders. W's eight enlarged/retained sets overcharge four holders by one and another by three. Both rivals stop before any release. The older 21-knot construction gives a contrasting recurring source: its live originals share no marks directly, and every common witness at the initial cut is spent. All three rules give its same 12 initial binary births, individual fresh counts and full true parents. Their subsequent rules coincide, so all its recurring releases are preserved in both laws. Loss of the seven-knot source is therefore an exact cost of these rival rules on that preparation.
