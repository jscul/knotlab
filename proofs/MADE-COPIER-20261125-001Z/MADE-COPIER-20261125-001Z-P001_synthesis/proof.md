# A copier as a pattern the growth makes: synthesis

This synthesis binds the three tickets of `MADE-COPIER-20261125-001` to their records. It contributes no further
proof or experiment. All sources are referee pending.

- [Ticket A](../../../research/MADE-COPIER-20261125-001A/results.md): for no registered class of second patterns,
  base law and stated choice rule does a made first pattern leave two of the second, in form, in built structure or
  in values. Under not-all-equal no made distinction is tied to another. The one case under one rule is gone when the
  start is renamed.
- [Ticket B](../../../research/MADE-COPIER-20261125-001B/results.md): no case of "consume and output two". Where two
  parts of the second pattern's form appear, descended from both, the second pattern alone leaves two as well.
- [Ticket C](../../../research/MADE-COPIER-20261125-001C/results.md): with takes sampled on the census of three to
  five knots, 17 confirmed cases in which two parts of the second part's form are met more often after a made
  pattern joins it, under 8 laws whose takes go by parent steps; no first-pattern form has cases for two forms of
  second pattern, so there is no candidate; in every case the meeting runs through a start knot.

Taken together ([decision](../../../research/MADE-COPIER-20261125-001/decision.md)): no copier was found. Sampling
finds made patterns that leave two of one form, through meetings the start supplies. A copier as a made pattern that
works for a class, through a meeting the growth makes, stays open.
