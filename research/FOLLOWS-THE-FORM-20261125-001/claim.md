# Claim: on built starts, what a first seed's descent makes differs with the form of the second seed

Epic `FOLLOWS-THE-FORM-20261125-001`, ticket A. Frozen with [the definitions](definitions.md) and
[the pre-registration](../FOLLOWS-THE-FORM-20261125-001A/preregistration.md). Evidence class sought: computed,
bounded, by a reading written from these texts on a shared growth engine; referee pending.

## The setting

A start is built from a **first seed**, one **link** distinction and a **second seed** (FF 2). The first seed and the
knot of it that holds the link are held fixed while the second seed runs over a family of forms. The start is grown
under one law, and **what the first seed's own descent makes** is read (FF 4, FF 5): its births round by round.

## The claim

- **C1. It differs with the form.** With the first seed and the place of the link fixed, what the first seed's
  descent makes differs between second seeds of different form, and the difference is carried by the link: with the
  two seeds in one start and not linked, every form gives what the bare start gives.
- **C2. Not by the first round alone.** Some second seeds that leave the same record at every round-1 birth with a
  first-seed ancestor (FF 5b) are told apart in a later round. For those the difference entered the first seed's
  descent after round 1.
- **C3. Not by a count at the held knot alone.** The class of a second seed is not a function of its held knot (its
  places, loose count and number of partners). Whether it is a function of that together with the second seed's own
  births by round is reported, with a prediction.
- **C4. The first seed's line differs in fate.** Across second seeds, within the rounds read, the first seed's own
  line shows two or more of: ends, holds steady, grows. Where it ends, the study says whether no knot with a
  first-seed ancestor is born any more, or whether every such birth is also of the second seed.
- **C5. It sees the near end.** Within the rounds read, on the listed chains with 2 or 3 loose distinctions a knot:
  with no taking rule, two chains that differ two or more steps from the held knot are told apart in few pairs (at
  most 10 in a hundred for each law and depth); with the take of a sole candidate, a chain of four knots is told
  from one of five in at least 15 pairs in a hundred, under the ordinary count and under the cap of three with short
  knots waiting.

Each part is registered as predictions with a "Fails when" entry: replications of what the drafter's probe saw, to be
reproduced exactly, and predictions on held-out material of the same kinds (first seeds and second seeds that
nothing has grown).

## What is supplied

Everything but the growth: the starts, built by hand (assumption A258); the choice of first seeds, of the place of the
link and of the families of second seeds; the laws, among them the take of a sole candidate, a rule added by hand
(A255); the readings; the bounds. **The meeting of the two seeds is supplied**: they share a start distinction.

## What the claim is not

- Not a claim about two parts the growth made that meet through a knot the growth made. That is the fold of emergence
  the owner asks for (A223), and it stays open. The word here is "seed" throughout, for that reason.
- Not a claim that anything reads a form in a fuller sense, or that the first seed's descent makes use of the
  difference. "Differs with" is all that is shown.
- No weighting is named, so nothing is called typical, rare or likely. The held-out family is a seeded sample of a
  written list of like forms, each once; a pass on it shows less than a pass on unlike material would.
- Nothing about living things, minds, physics or time.
