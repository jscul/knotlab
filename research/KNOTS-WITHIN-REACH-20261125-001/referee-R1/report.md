# How many knots lie within reach? Independent referee report
**Confirmed with fixes. There is a Block on the original one-round headline, the inference that the history power determines the roster power, and the assertion that every sampled parity closing passed the canonical knot test.**

The independent growth reproduces the square and cube counts at one round. The registered history test also reproduces. The history count includes all knots born so far. The one-round roster counts the members of one part at that round. A holder step links two knots sharing a distinction. A witnessed step on the roster links two members when another knot shares a distinction with each. The counts below include the source at step zero.

## What holds

### The five arguments

| Lemma | Reason and necessary hypotheses | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E. Which holder steps are parent steps | Without takes of shared distinctions, only a former sole holder and its child can newly share a distinction. All other meetings are between starting knots. Deleting just those meetings gives the parent relation. A shortest route from a made knot can still use one. | 136 independently rebuilt named prefixes. The referee size bound stopped 28 before a further round. No recorded exception in 5,378 birth runs or 320 history runs including swaps. |
| F. The kept history range | Fix the stage and measure steps there. On any later route from an old source to its first new knot, the preceding old knot had to be a member at the fixed stage. Old knots never regain usable loose distinctions. Reaching that member costs at least $s_{\min}-s(u)$ old steps and the next step costs one more. Taking-free growth adds no new meeting between two old knots. Waiting and holding are permitted. The argument applies to history counts. | Of 87 original named comparisons, 84 full ten-round continuations were independently repeated, covering 1,434 source knots. Of 414 registered stage entries, 64 compared zero knots. Of 64,702 reported source checks, 63,515 ran ten further rounds. The 2,827 source IDs present at both recorded lengths have identical common prefixes. |
| R. The shift from a start knot | A shortest route to the set of starts ends the first time it reaches a start, so E gives the same minimum for parent steps. With release and stalling, offshoots or retiring, every member spends or loses all usable loose distinctions in a productive round. Every parent is then one round older than its child. For connected starts, $B(r-e(c)) \le H(c,r) \le B(r)$ follows by going through a nearest start. Under waiting or holding $B$ counts by steps from the starts. | 136 named prefixes, including 85 with the round hypothesis. Independent older-parent examples under waiting and holding. The inequality gives neither an exact finite polynomial nor a general roster power. |
| N. Strength under not-all-equal | Condition on all shared distinctions, under equal weighting of allowed states of a nonempty finite stage. Loose choices at distinct holders then factor. If the shared values at a holder agree, one of its $2^\lambda$ loose assignments is forbidden. Mixed shared values give conditional mean zero. The conditional mean of a signed loose bit is $g_u/(2^{\lambda_u}-1)$, with $g_u=+1,-1,0$ for all shared bits zero, all one, or mixed. Multiply the two means and average to obtain the identity for $\kappa$ and its absolute bound. Both holders must have a loose and a shared distinction. Barred loose distinctions count. | 1,930 original control stages rebuilt, of which 1,763 contain a member pair. All 8,079 member pairs and 32,630 choices of loose distinctions agreed. A separate state enumeration covered all 3,725 small growing-end stages. |
| P. Disagreement under parity | Under equal weighting of homogeneous parity solutions, the difference of two coordinates is a linear functional. It vanishes everywhere or translation by one solution pairs agreement with disagreement. With a connected structure and at most two holders, cancellation at every shared distinction forces the coefficients of its two holders to agree. Thus the only possible nonzero loose-only rule is the sum of all loose distinctions. Two loose distinctions tie and three or more give half disagreement for every distinct pair. | 29,599 recorded parity stages, 5,649 small enough for the original state enumeration. Independent literal controls include disconnected structures, a pinned loose distinction, and a six-knot structure with a two-of-four rule. |

## How it was checked

The referee programs import no reading or analysis module from the study. [The taking-free engine](regrow_engine.py) implements the frozen definitions. [The small taking engine](taking_replay.py) adds takes independently. Only the full final parity replay under the taking laws uses the earlier shared growth engine, whose hash is in its output. Independent growth agrees on all 8,083 early taking stages tested and every positive tied-pair history through its reported round. The full replay checks knot totals at every round and obtains every originally listed deficient projection by an independent calculation. The state enumerations and the canonical checker use projected allowed states, including the explicit six-knot positive and the failing stage examples.

Run from the repository root. The commands below perform the checks sequentially. Individual programs also accept the pilot or prefix options documented in their command-line help.
