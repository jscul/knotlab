The [previous two-output experiment](../FOLD-TWO-OUTPUT-20261115-001/two_outputs.md) found a one-bit group on every shared task and a minimum of two bits on every rewiring. Supplying the local operation added work without changing which groups the solver tried. Here the only newly learned decision is the order in which to try groups.

## Paid comparison and stopping rule

One fixed fit, followed by ten counterbalanced paired repetitions over the 192-rule sequence, using a fresh process per method and repeat on one pinned CPU. Engines share caches within the same arity and method. Charge fixture acquisition, supervisory label generation, feature computation, all fitting/selection, model validation and saving; then charge deployment imports, model reload, cache initialization, ranking, successful and failed candidate checks, joint program construction, exact verification and serialization. Ordinary numerical renaming/structure checks that the training generator performs are timed too. Common held-task preparation and a second scalar scientific replay are outside every solver's clock. Separately show costs with a frozen model already available, with labelled training data already available, and with only the raw training tables already available. The raw-table sensitivity times a fresh exact label pass over each eligible triple before adding the fit and freeze costs. This identifies which conclusions depend on the cost of generating training tasks.

The unit of scientific generalization is the matched pair. Repeats quantify execution variability on the same tasks. Compare medians of complete deployment runs. At each stream prefix take the median of its ten cumulative CPU totals with the full fit charged at position zero. A crossing is the first positive-length prefix where learned CPU is at most the endpoint-selected benchmark at the same prefix. Also report whether it persists through the endpoint. Report the remaining gap at 192 when positive. An all-paid win requires equal exact correctness, lower median CPU and at least eight of ten wins against that benchmark. Report smaller effects, transient crossings and reversals directly. Preserve failed fits, proposals and returns. End at 192 rules.
