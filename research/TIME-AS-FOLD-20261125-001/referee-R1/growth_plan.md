# Plan for independent growth replay

The independent program reads the registered starts, the growth definitions of the studies of a ring in balance and of parts that meet again, and the definitions and recorded readings of this study. The archived run records supply comparison rows. They do not supply parents, takes, demands, return matches, changes of course, or verdicts to the computation. The comparisons use one process with an address-space limit of 3 GiB.

- Compare every birth of the 52 detailed listings kept for the discrepancy in the census column about giving parts off at two or more ticks, including parents, distinct inputs, takes, demand, ration input, and fresh counts. Compare both readings of the return series for those runs.
- Compare per-round totals and return records on a fixed set of named starts, the first, middle, and last of the 227 starts in their registered order, and selected ends of the ladder in run 3. Include no taking, the two primary laws, both clauses of MET taken separately, the three treatments, both knot laws, and the two documented changes in the order of demand or the count of inputs. Include the two-knot start under MET with NEWEST and parity, the reported failure with two joins of twelve parents.
- Grow a selected case of C-iii in each of the three rule families where it was reported. Reconstruct the clock from its first part, compare the own and joint counts per period, and grow the branches with the clock barred, the parts given off barred, all qualifying parts barred, the second part barred, and the masked comparison. These cases were chosen using the reported outcome to check the positive path. Use the registered part names only to identify each case in the comparison record. A negative control with a clock and no taking rule checks the corresponding equal-course reading.

Before running a group in full, time an exact subset that includes a demanding case. If a group is too large, keep a prefix of the fixed order and report the rest as not run. The detailed listings and the fixed set have no weighting for inference to other runs. Completion is the end of the selected comparisons, with differences retained and reported.

The source skipped at the archived prefix boundary is a separate supplement: DW(5,3), in which each common knot meets the next, under COPY-LAW(CANCEL-WAIT), STEPS-3 for offshoots of joins, BY-COUNT, parity, with fresh distinctions counted by parents. Count its distinct tested clock forms, eligible second parts, and their own counts in the first four periods. Bars are outside this supplement.
