# Advisor review before the freeze

`question.md` is the question as sent on 24 November 2026 to the advisor model (Claude Fable 5.2, no tools), with the first
draft of the definitions and the pre-registration. `answer.json` is its reply. `words_proposed.md` is the drafter's note on
the two new words. `words_question.md` and `words_answer.json` are a second, short question on the words chosen after the
review. The frozen texts differ from the reviewed draft as follows.

**Defects the advisor found, and what was done.**
- The test for a part that waits for ever never fired when a roster had two parts. It is now "a part all of whose members
  are short".
- The start clause is exact only when no start knot that acts meets a start knot that waits. The definitions now say so, and
  spell out the two readings that are wrong on the start-clause counterexample.
- A stall read brood by brood would differ from the complete rounds' stall. The stall is now read from the start roster
  only, with the reason that this is enough under ordinary growth; the matching prediction is marked a check.
- The closing bit is read on the present structure, where the frozen exception speaks of a round's only birth. It is now
  called a reading, and every firing is counted against the reference.
- "Remaining births" was undefined when more births were made than planned; a vacuous sentence, references to sections that
  did not exist and two leftover draft lines were removed.
- The comparison did not require the reference's retirements to be made, could not end when a part waits for ever, and
  took its reference from the same engine. All three are changed: the reference is the earlier study's engine.
- The advisor showed by hand that the law R2 fails for a knot that waits twice, on a start of its own design. That start is
  registered as the cycle of eight with three extra dead knots, with a prediction (P14) in place of an open question. The
  thread followed the advisor's account by hand and ran the start on the earlier study's engine; the drafter's prototype
  then found the advisor's order and its image under a relabelling.
- A prediction about "the knots within d" named no procedure. The reading within holder-step distance d is now a registered
  family of laws, T(d), run through the same comparison (P11).
- Predictions that are true by construction are marked as checks; one prediction with no run was dropped and is listed as
  left open; the stop rule no longer has an either-or form that was already met.
- Orders that serve one knot last were added to the uniformly sampled ones.
- A section says what a pass would and would not show, in the advisor's terms: the parent relation, with the release rule,
  carries the round.

**Where the drafter's prototype disagrees with the advisor** (disclosed in the pre-registration): on the new start the least
distance that suffices is 6 holder steps, the brood's extent from the knot that waits three times, not the 10 the advisor's
estimate gives; and a single wait does not always err towards fewer offshoots (R1 makes one too many).

**Words.** The advisor accepted "weaned" and "brood" and listed words used in two senses. The replacements were chosen by
the thread and then put to the advisor (`words_question.md`). Taken from its answer: all-short, status, a barring,
underbuilds, cutoff, x-last order, the brood within d, the names of the two new starts, and relabelling. Not taken:
- "sealed" for a brood every knot of which is weaned. The thread uses "all-weaned", which adds no stem; the notebook
  already uses "seal" of a piece of a structure closed off by a birth.
- "reach" for the extent of a brood from a knot. "Reached" is the glossary's word for a value fixed by propagation; the
  glossary's "tie extent" uses "extent" in the same general sense, the most steps apart, so "extent" is kept and always
  written with what it is the extent of.
