This synthesis binds the three tickets of `REREAD-BIRTHS-AND-MIXED-LAWS-20261123-001` to their records. It contributes no further proof or experiment. All sources are referee pending.

- [Ticket A](../../../research/REREAD-BIRTHS-AND-MIXED-LAWS-20261123-001A/result.md): the re-reading move as a growth law, rule R, classed on old words, the recorded sources, the sparse draws that stall, the two stalling set-ups and pace. Exact computation on named samples.
- [Ticket B](../../../research/REREAD-BIRTHS-AND-MIXED-LAWS-20261123-001B/result.md): ordinary growth with both laws from every part of the 292 structures with a hidden excess. One counting argument and an exhaustive census for the named starts and bounds.
- [Ticket C](../../../research/REREAD-BIRTHS-AND-MIXED-LAWS-20261123-001C/result.md): the same census under rule R, and a census of one added not-all-equal knot with a fresh distinction. Exhaustive for the added knot; partly a sample under rule R.

Taken together ([decision](../../../research/REREAD-BIRTHS-AND-MIXED-LAWS-20261123-001/decision.md)): growth supplies weight and no tie; a part that already has a structure's allowed words and sits at exactly 2 is one birth away from a hidden excess, and that birth takes a loose distinction together with a shared one; rule R, which re-reads only when a knot is short, does not make that birth for any part with at most two holders per distinction, and makes it by a shortage in seven parts that hold a distinction three times.
