# The sharp rate for five-place branch systems

**The greatest steady growth rate in DEAD-CAP's branch class with at most five places per starting junction is 2.3829757679… per round.** The eleven-junction start with two dead hubs attains it. Every finite start covered by the branch equations has upper exponential rate at most this value, including periodic pieces, initial spare distinctions and terminal one-way ends. The rate is the positive root of \(\lambda^3-4\lambda-4=0\). [Proof](../../proofs/FIVE-PLACE-RATE-20261119-001/FIVE-PLACE-RATE-20261119-001-P001_ceiling/proof.md)

The place count matters twice. Once the old meetings and pending pair joins are paid for, a live junction can link a two-member and a three-member block, or two two-member blocks. It cannot connect two three-member blocks. A junction between two two-member blocks can carry at most one spare. At the radius \(r=1/\lambda\), the possible continuations through each block are therefore bounded by the corresponding value for the eleven-junction start. The only local comparison capable of increasing the factor is the spare between two two-member blocks, and even that stays strictly below the available bound. An isolated larger block and a terminal ray have finite series there. Positivity then bounds each complete branch series. Marking a distinction to count a whole round preserves that radius. [Branch equations and the original hypotheses](../../proofs/DEAD-CAP-20261119-001A/DEAD-CAP-20261119-001A-P001_dead/proof.md)

This ceiling applies to steady rates and upper exponential rates. Initial counts can exceed the corresponding coefficients of the eleven-junction example. A point with spare distinctions already gives a strict reversal in degree two. [Profile checks and coefficient controls](../../proofs/FIVE-PLACE-RATE-20261119-001/FIVE-PLACE-RATE-20261119-001-P001_ceiling/check_rate.json)

**Scope.** DEAD-CAP's branch system requires its start conditions, including the disjointness condition on meeting blocks and the absence of an unheld three-parent group in the first round. The five-place cap alone does not imply those conditions. The unrestricted steady-rate question for starts outside this class remains open. The six-place construction in the preceding study exceeds this cap and has runaway growth.

**Evidence.** This is an all-size proof over every admitted profile. Attainment uses one explicit start. No new weighting of starts or finite census is used to infer the upper bound. DEAD-CAP recorded this result as a conjecture. Its numerical comparison and its earlier scope still stand. Independent program referee pending.
