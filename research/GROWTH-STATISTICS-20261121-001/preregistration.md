# Can intrinsic readings recognize shapes and discover differences in growth?

Owner-assigned topology epic, 21 November 2026, serving G1 and G17. Stage A is a blind benchmark on recorded constructions. Stage B is a comparison on ordinary growing holdings. The operational contracts, rule and run-authorization receipts fix the parameters before their respective test data are opened. Development uses separate fixtures. The four source descriptions and published findings are already known to the custodian and coordinator.

## The questions and their units

**A.** Can anonymous observations distinguish cubic meeting-volume growth, two different limiting shapes at the same dimension, and a bounded bond reaching across increasingly many meeting steps? A finite dimension estimate, evidence for a structural description, and an existing proof of a limit have different meanings. Roots nested within a construction measure robustness to the root choice. They do not supply a sample of independent construction families.

**B.** For each knot law separately, how many previously uncatalogued kinds of later behavior can two equally funded search procedures reproduce? The unit for uncertainty is a fresh paired run. The adaptive state starts empty for each run. An additional comparison asks whether two starts with the same early reading can grow differently and whether a proposed extra reading predicts that difference on fresh cases. A tie, a loss to independent sampling, a missed example and an abstention are all admissible.

## In standard terms

A evaluates classification from finite metric and incidence observations on a specified panel. B compares diversity-seeking mutation with independent draws on a common weighted finite set. A later trajectory can exhibit a combination that the study did not name in advance. The measurement format still determines which combinations can be seen.
