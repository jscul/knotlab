## What stands

1. **Under a count weighting the signed form is only a rewriting.** A far edit can move a near reader's statistic
   (ring of four: −15/41 → −33/95), and the signed form writes that as loop terms through the far knot contributing less;
   but the edited count is exactly the unedited count plus the count with the far knot deleted, a nonnegative mixture, so
   the reader sees only a change of its own statistic (signalling), never a correlation the counts do not give.
2. **A signed sum that is not a count exists only in supplied readings** that square a signed sum over unread
   distinctions (SIDE-SIGN's R2; the four-settings calculations).
