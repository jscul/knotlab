# Two countings: definitions (second version, "TD2")

Epic `TWO-COUNTINGS-20261125-001`, ticket `TWO-COUNTINGS-20261125-001A`. Builds on the shared definitions
(`research/RING-IN-BALANCE-20261123-001/definitions.md`, "SD line n") and those of the study of parts that meet again

## 0. Words

- Kept from SD and FD: structure, knot, place, distinction, holder, loose, shared, start, birth, join, offshoot,
  fresh distinction, round, law, ration, treatment, take, eligible, the choice, parent, the birth record.
- **State**, **allowed state** as the glossary. The record's "word" appears only in citations.
- **Counting**: a rule that gives a weighting of states. **Weighting**: what a counting gives (the glossary's word,
  here said with what it weights: of states, of meeting patterns, of histories).
- The record's other names: for counting from the finish, "uniform present words", "uniform stage", "every allowed
  word counted equally", "complete reached-word weighting"; for counting from the start, "old-word weighting", "the
  control weighting", "equally lifted start", "inherited", "transported", "recursive division", "forward weighting".
  Cited, not used.
- **Observers.** The literature's names (Alice and Bob) once, marked; then **the first observer** and **the second
  observer**: each a supplied list of distinctions; nothing observes.
- **Start with its states**: a start together with the weighting of its states (the record's "preparation", not used).
- **Funding** (the record's word, cited only): a round makes its births; it fails (stalls, SD lines 42–43) when a member

  words of the common brief, and "fix" for a counting (say "decides the counting").

## 1. Structures and histories

1. Knot laws: even, odd ("parity"), not-all-equal. A structure may hold knots of several laws.
2. **Allowed states** A(S).
3. **A history** H = (S_0, ..., S_t): a start and, for j ≥ 1, the knots added at stage j (a round, one birth, or the
   supplied setting births). F_j: the distinctions first held at stage j (fresh); every other place of a knot added at
   stage j is an **old place**.
4. **Completions.** For o in A(S_(j−1)), c_j(o) is the number of assignments of F_j that with o satisfy every knot added
   at stage j. A knot added with nothing fresh gives 0 or 1. A stage **extends** when c_j(o) ≥ 1 for every o. If A(S_0)
   is empty no counting is defined.
5. **Product.** When the births of stage j have disjoint private fresh sets, c_j(o) = Π_i c_(j,i)(o).
6. **A common stage** of several histories (the four setting pairs' histories, for example): a stage index s such
   that S_0..S_s are the same in all of them.

## 2. Three weightings, kept apart

1. Of states (what a counting gives).
2. Of meeting patterns or starts (W1, W2 of the copier study).
3. Of histories (the chance of each history given its start: uniform over a law's open choices, the copier study's
   T1, T2; or, for a keyed setting, the state itself selecting the history, 4.5).

## 3. The countings

1. **Counting from the finish** on H: each state of A(S_t) has weight 1/|A(S_t)|.
2. **Counting from the start** on H, defined only when every stage extends: w in A(S_t) has weight
   (1/|A(S_0)|) · Π_(j=1..t) 1/c_j(w|_(j−1)).
3. **Counting from stage s**, s a common stage of the histories compared: (1/|A(S_s)|) · Π_(j>s) 1/c_j(w|_(j−1)).
   On a hand-built structure taken as its own start, with the two setting births as one stage, the family has two
   members (s = 0, the start; s = 1, the finish); with the settings as two stages it has a third, the **mixed stage**
   after one side's setting and before the other's, which is common only to the histories that share that side's
   setting: defined and reported, not predicted.
4. **Lost weight.** Where some c_j(o) = 0, the unconditioned weights do not sum to 1 and no score is defined on them.
   **Counting from the start, renormalised** (its own name): the weights divided by their sum, per history. In a
   pooled statement this changes the weighting of histories (each history's share is multiplied by its kept weight).
   With renormalising, counting from the start contains counting from the finish (take as start every distinction with
   no knot, then add every knot with nothing fresh), so the two are ends of one family only when lost weight is
   excluded. Every ration of TD2 5 gives each birth a fresh distinction, so no weight is lost along a law's history.
5. **Pooled over histories.** Counting from the finish pooled: each pair (history, finished state) once, which gives
   history h the weight |A(S_h)| / Σ|A(S_h)|, non-uniform over the settings when the totals differ. Per history: a
   supplied weighting ν of histories times the counting on each.

## 4. Settings, records, scores

1. **Settings** (REVIEW-LEADS-20261123-001C, `preregistration.md:47`): the first observer's setting a ∈ {0, 1} adds
   nae(x1, x2 or x3, za), one fresh distinction za; the second observer's b adds nae(y1, y2 or y3, zb). Where a study
   read the setting as rule Q's open choice (A239), its own **options** (the shared distinctions a rule-Q birth may
   take) are used.
2. **Records** as frozen by each study (here: (x1, x2, x3, za) and (y1, y2, y3, zb)).
3. **Change from the other's setting**: the largest total-variation distance between a record's distributions under
   the other observer's two settings, over both observers and both own settings. **Hidden**: that change is 0
   exactly. It is relative to the declared record: (s1, s2) moves by 1/3 on the 11-knot structure and is in no record.
4. **Score**: the largest E00 + E01 + E10 − E11 over the four placements of the minus sign and over a declared class of
   two-valued functions of each record. Exact method: enumerate every pair of functions of the record with fewer
   values, the first function's value on one record fixed (global sign); for each, the other side's best response is
   the sum over its record values of the absolute value of the signed combination, in exact rationals. The class
   "all two-valued functions" contains the constants; "parities of a nonempty set of record bits" does not.
5. **Keyed setting**: the first observer's setting is g(o), o its old values. The state selects the history, so the
   countings of 3 are defined on it only by these formulas. Counting from the start: P(o, finished state w) =
   (1/|A(S_0)|) · [a = g(o|first)] / c(o; g(o), b) for w extending o under setting pair (g(o), b). Counting from the
   finish, pooled (THIRD-HOLDER-20261123-001A's reading): every finished state w of S_(a,b) whose old part o has
   g(o|first) = a counts once, normalised over a for fixed b.

## 7. Settled statements the engine checks

2. **Factorization** (1.5).
3. **The exact agreement rule.** At round j the two countings stay equal exactly when Π_i (2^(f_i) − e_i(o)) is the
   same for every allowed old state o, where for a not-all-equal birth i with f_i fresh distinctions e_i(o) = 1 when
   its old places are all equal in o and 0 otherwise (a parity birth contributes a constant). So only which patterns
   of (e_1, ..., e_m) occur matters: they are decided exactly by enumerating, with a SAT solver and blocking clauses,
   the occurring assignments of the indicators of the births whose e_i varies. Nothing is left undecided.
4. **One birth, f fresh distinctions** (replaces the RATION-k run): with p the share of old states (under the weighting
   before the birth) whose old places are all equal, the total-variation distance between the two countings after the
   birth is p(1 − p)/(2^f − p), which falls as f grows.

## In standard terms

The literature's words. A constraint network grows by adding constraints with new variables. Two distributions on
its solutions: the uniform distribution on the final solution set, and the sequential distribution (uniform on the
initial solutions, each solution's mass then split equally among its extensions at each step). They agree exactly
when every extension count is constant (the record's theorem). On a CHSH test whose two settings are two added
constraints, the sequential distribution with the settings added last, under the hypotheses of the lemma, is a local
hidden-variable model (measurement independence holds by construction); the uniform distribution on each final
network is a model with measurement dependence (post-selection).

## Definitions used

- **In the whole structure** (the record's own: SD, `research/RING-IN-BALANCE-20261123-001/definitions.md:130-139`,
  "A knot with place set S passes K1–K3 in the whole structure when the allowed states of the structure, restricted
  to S, are exactly the even assignments of S", and for not-all-equal "the assignments of S that extend to an allowed
  state of the whole structure are exactly the assignments that are not constant"; FD,
  `research/PARTS-MEET-AGAIN-20261124-001/definitions.md:286-287`, "A knot that passes has, in the whole structure,
  exactly its own rule on its places: constrained as a whole, every part free, some state allowed (K1–K3 with
  forgetting)"; AGENTS.md, "Canonical foundation": the system read as its set of allowed states, witnesses quantified
  away). Here: the **induced relation** of a knot is the set of assignments of its places that extend to an allowed
  state of the whole structure; K1, K2, K3 are tested on it by `knotlab.knots.check_knot` (forgetting reading), with

- **On its own**: the same test on the knot's own rule.

## The link the advisor suggested, checked

The record says the 128 of 512 sign assignments of the 11-knot structure's nine parity knots that give the hidden

four-place rule fail; the excess needs that tie to be an inequality (so that the observer's two options differ). No
other knot fails in any of the 512.
