## Complete prebirth state

For a chosen set of original living donors, retain the union M of all their original names. Every original spent support is distinction-disjoint from the other spent supports. Any spent support only partly in M can complete its own law on an unretained distinction. Afterwards each unoccupied living donor completes its own law at a private place. This constructs a whole state from every assignment satisfying the full supports of the retained living donors and the spent supports wholly in M. Restriction of a whole state gives the reverse inclusion. Thus these named factors specify the entire projected relation. The receipt also gives its exact cardinality by parity elimination or exact inclusion-exclusion for not-all-equal. The latter groups the three alternatives for each living donor as unpinned, fixed to zero or fixed to one, then eliminates donors with integer weights. Direct complete projection independently cross-checked the counts at 18 retained names of two neighboring donors in the small M+T source. There were 32,768 parity words and 256,194 not-all-equal words. A failed inclusion has an explicit accepted assignment on M that violates a named target factor.
