# What a newborn's holdings must depend on for a made pattern to depend on which pattern it meets: synthesis

This synthesis binds the two tickets of `WHAT-A-BIRTH-CARRIES-20261125-001` to their records. It contributes no
further proof or experiment. All sources are referee pending.

- [Ticket A](../../../research/WHAT-A-BIRTH-CARRIES-20261125-001A/results.md): under the recorded ordinary count with
  the recorded taking rule, over every numbering of two hand-built starts, the first pattern's first join receives
  2 + M fresh distinctions, M being the births the second pattern's usable members make that round. The course from
  the first pattern only differs exactly where M differs, through a start knot; nothing is composed over several
  births.
- [Ticket B](../../../research/WHAT-A-BIRTH-CARRIES-20261125-001B/results.md): over a listed family of 60 laws that
  use no names, two parts the growth made meet only through a knot the growth made that both descend from (88 pairs
  per knot law under a recorded ration with one added rule); under a second added rule the first part's course
  differs with the second barred, attributed through the meeting, by one value at one birth (280 pairs per knot
  law), which is also whether the parts meet.

Taken together ([decision](../../../research/WHAT-A-BIRTH-CARRIES-20261125-001/decision.md)): for a count nothing new
is needed, and nothing found is more than a count. The recorded law hands over how many births the second pattern
makes, through a start knot; two added rules move the link to a made knot. No pattern responds to which pattern it
meets, and no difference needs more than one value at one birth. A class in which a count cannot be the whole
difference is the open direction.
