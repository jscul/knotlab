**The run reproduces every count the note disclosed and finds no exception to either argument. So the note's verdict
stands as computed: the order that births one at a time need can be shown in the structure's own places, by a ration
that counts places in threes, but the ration writes it, and only from a start whose meeting knots already agree. The
supplied record is moved into the law, not removed. And the earlier study that adopted the record said too little about
where it fails: under that study's own waiting law a birth has parents at different parent steps on 510 of 3,348 census
starts, not on one built start.**

The words. A knot's **parent steps** are 0 for a start knot and one more than the greatest of its parents' for a new
knot. The **stamp** is the record the earlier study adopted (assumption A247): each new knot's parent steps counted in
threes, written beside the structure by whoever runs the growth. A **count form** is a ration that gives a new knot 0, 1
or 2 further fresh distinctions so that its count of places (or of barred places) is one more, counted in threes, than
its parents'; the **place rule** then calls a shared place "a parent place" when the other holder's count is one less.
The **census** is every start of up to five knots of an earlier study, each counted once up to renaming: 3,380, of
which 3,348 have every start knot passing the knot test under parity. The laws are named as in the shared definitions:
RATION-3, CAP-3, FLOOR1 and ORD are rations; waiting, retiring and offshoots are what a short knot does; HOLD lets a
knot keep a loose place for a cancelled group.

## What did not hold as worded

- **The planted error.** The pre-registration said that after one barred distinction is added to a round-2 knot, the
  verdicts that change are exactly that knot's places to its parents and children. That is right on all 200 runs where
  the argument's four conditions hold, and wrong on 21 runs where one of them fails; the place rule's own formula gives
  the right list on 308 of 308.
- **The range by law** in P8b, above.
- **Two scratch figures for the count forms** (the starts on which the place rule is right on every structure) are 4
  and 30 above the run's under two forms. The cause was found: the prototype left out both places of each distinction
  a closing knot holds. No verdict uses those figures.

## What this shows and does not show

- **Supplied or made.** The count is written by the ration and used by nothing else; the start's agreement is
  supplied; the place rule is applied by the run, not by any knot. Nothing here is a pattern that counts, and nothing
  is a clock. No claim is made about physical time.
- **The first argument is checked, not proved here.** The run finds the 515 structures and the 140 restarts it
  predicts; that no function of the structure can name the parents on such a population is the note's argument, not
  refereed.
