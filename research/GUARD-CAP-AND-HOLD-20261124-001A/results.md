**Under the cap of three, as recorded for the ring that returns, the guard holds: births made one at a time build what the
complete rounds build in every order searched, with or without the fillers. When knots hold instead of releasing, it does
not. The guard as frozen stops early, which follows from its own definition; a repaired guard goes on and then makes a
birth the complete rounds never make, at exactly the birth the advisor predicted by hand. The reason is that under holding
a knot's children are no longer all made in one round, and a knot that stays is asked for joins through its own
children's children. A brood is then no longer one round's roster, which is what the guard replays.**

## What the result is

1. **The gap named in the earlier report is closed for the cap.** The ring that returns was recorded under a cap of three;
   the earlier study ran a fixed three. Under the cap what a new knot receives depends on a count among the knots made with
   it, and the guard still matches, at the same distances and with the same failures of the narrower guards.
2. **Holding breaks the guard in two separate ways.**
   - *The test for "finished" is wrong.* A parent that holds is still a member, possibly for ever, so its children never
     count as weaned and never act. This is true by definition and shows only as an early stop.
   - *A brood is no longer one round.* With the test repaired, the guard replays a round on knots that were not made
     together. A staying knot's children of two different rounds are read as one roster and joined; and a staying knot is
     asked, under the complete rounds, for joins through its own children's children, which the guard does not see. Either
     way a birth is made that the rounds never make.
3. **The owner's literal case tests little.** With the cap of three and holding together, the growth ends at round 2 on the
   cycles and the ring, and no knot is ever short on the small starts. The match there is a match of very little.

## What this does and does not show

- **It shows** that the earlier result holds for the two added rules as recorded, to the rounds searched; and that it does
  not carry over to holding, with the reason.
- **It does not show** that no guard works under holding. The repaired guard was one line; a guard that groups a parent's
  children by the round that made them, and reads a staying knot beside its children's children, was not tried.
- As before: the round is replayed from a written record of parents, not done without.

## Deviations and limits

- **The gate's wording (the thread's error).** The pre-registration asked for the demand to agree under every law, while
  its own definitions say that under holding the plan's demand leaves out groups holding a staying knot. The engine's
  author reported the conflict and went on, since nothing built depends on it under a fixed ration.
- **Two gaps in the earlier definitions, found by the engine's author:** the rule for when a barring matches is wrong under
  holding (a staying knot that later retires has children); and the record's depth guard is not defined with the fillers.
  Both readings were recorded before the runs.
- **P9's account named only the repaired guard.** The original guard makes the same wrong join through round 4, once the
  staying knot has finished giving. The prediction's failure condition is unaffected.
- **One engine.** There is no second program here. The checks are the gate against the reference engine, the earlier
  study's unit tests, which still pass, and two hand predictions by the advisor (P7 and P9) that came out to the birth.
- **Unfinished searches.** Of 386 searches over every order, 343 finished and 43 reached the cap of two million states,
  none with a change of verdict. No time budget was used. Three searches took 14 to 23 minutes.
- **The knot test.** All 29 reference structures pass under parity by both methods and under not-all-equal, with the same
  births; the 52 knots made at a mismatch pass under both.
- **Everything is finite:** 29 pairs of start and law, cutoffs of one to four rounds.
- **Evidence class:** exact computation by one engine, with hand predictions confirmed.
- **Scaffolds:** this ticket swapped the ration (the cap for a fixed three), added the fillers, and swapped the release
  rule for holding under four laws. The guard survives the first two and not the third. The record of parents, the rule
  for start knots and the rule for a further reading were not swapped.
