# What original marks on one side can read

Loading the five-ring is detectable from original marks on one side before the reading reaches any original ring knot. Counting knots changes even when the marked distinctions keep their ordinary separation, all their shortest routes and their pair bond distance. On sufficiently long paths, a count differs by exactly two at arbitrarily large original offsets. Both the ordinary and bond readings of fixed radius have finite ranges valid for every later size on a singly attached path. The moving absolute difference and the fixed-radius null answer different questions about the same preparation.
