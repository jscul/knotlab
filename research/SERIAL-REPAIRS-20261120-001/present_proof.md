# What a present reading can guarantee under strict joins-first

On either of the two seven-knot starts, **no fixed ordinary radius guarantees the complete prescribed growth in every weakly fair order after strict global joins-first priority**. A distant piece can stay still while the other makes infinitely many multipart births. Each such birth removes every unary move from the prioritized list, including the pending move on the stationary piece. A positive result is available from the present physical holdings alone if the rule may compare the whole finite stage. It can reconstruct the least unfinished layer and serialize only that layer, including its correct fresh counts, in any order allowed by priority. [Observation and fairness contract](preregistration.md)

## What fairness and locality mean here

The raw rule specifies the allowed birth, its parents, and its immutable number of fresh distinctions. One physical move consumes one loose distinction per parent. It adds one knot and its fresh distinctions. Existing holdings persist. The rule has no separate signal move, writable clock, globally updated marks or external scheduler memory. An ordinary step crosses an actual shared distinction between holders. A raw decision of radius \(R\) may inspect the current joint \(R\)-neighborhood of the proposed parents, their complete supports, loose or shared status and the visible witness incidences. The proof also permits boundary stubs carrying the present degrees of their exterior endpoints by enlarging \(R\) by one. A globally calculated bond reading attached to local marks, a read of all currently eligible events, or a complete competing-piece count supplies a different observation.

Strict global joins-first retains every raw multipart choice if at least one exists anywhere, and otherwise retains the raw unary choices. A run is **weakly fair after priority** if every particular move continually in the filtered list from some point is eventually served. Repeatedly returning to that list is enough only for strong fairness. With **weak fairness before priority**, a forever enabled raw move must be served even if the filter occasionally masks it. A maximal finite run must have no available move. Loose distinctions at a single parent may be exchanged. Fixing their names for a proof allows named action comparisons and supplies no names to the rule.

## The two growing pieces

Initially the ordinary meetings are \(US,VS,WS,SA,AB,BW,AZ\), each carrying nine different shared distinctions. \(U,V\) each hold two loose distinctions. In the quiet start \(W\) holds none and \(Z\) four. In the shifted start \(W,Z\) hold two each. In the prescribed complete growth there are two disjoint sets of descendants, one from the live roots among \(U,V,W\), and one from \(Z\). Every new ordinary meeting belongs to its own piece. Paths between the pieces pass through original holders. This remains true in every parent-closed partial construction, regardless of the order in which its births occurred. [Exact two-piece descriptions](../FRESH-DEMAND-20261120-001/fixed_map.md)

For the proof, the infinite part of each piece can be indexed by pairs \((i,j)\) of nonnegative integers. A step adds one to one entry. On the quiet \(U,V\) piece the pair \((0,0)\) is missing and the two single-step positions are the two roots. On the shifted \(Z\) piece, \(Z\) is the origin. On the other piece in each preparation a finite set of events comes first. These are the subsets of the four initial \(Z\) spokes in the quiet start, or the nonempty subsets of the three live roots in the shifted start. Its last knot produces a unary relay, the origin of the subsequent pair-indexed part. These indices describe the scored growth. The raw reader in the negative result is never given them. [Identified growth including the relay](../DOUBLE-IDENT-20261119-001/line_summary.md)

Three facts follow. First, both pieces contain infinitely many unary and infinitely many multipart events. Second, there are finitely many events up to each fixed depth, where depth is the length of a parent chain back to a source. Every loose slot in either completed piece is eventually spent on a specified successor. Third, in any correct prefix depth equals the minimum current ordinary distance to the seven source holders. Every parent route is present and descends by one. Each new physical meeting changes depth by one, and an original meeting changes it by zero, giving the reverse inequality. Thus after all prescribed knots through a sufficiently large fixed depth and all their successors have been made, every holder still able to take part as a parent is far from the source in both pieces.

## Why every finite raw radius fails under weak fairness after priority

Suppose a fixed-\(R\) raw rule on either source succeeds in every maximal weakly fair filtered run. It may restrict the ready moves in each correct finite prefix. In particular the argument does not assume it offers the whole prescribed roster. On a successful run the scorer can identify the parent incidences and their immutable output counts with those of the target. This identification supplies the proof with piece membership and depth.

**Extension of a finite prefix.** Every reachable finite prefix has a maximal weakly fair continuation. One can assign persistent countable codes to named proposals and fresh slots as required, giving every code finitely many predecessors. When the list is nonempty, take the admitted move of least code. A fixed action on fixed parents can fire only finitely often because every firing uses a slot which never refills. A persistently admitted action therefore cannot wait forever behind finitely many lower codes. Fresh names can be treated up to their admissible renaming. If the list empties, this gives a maximal finite run. These codes witness existence of a schedule. They are absent from the physical rule. Consequently a reachable finite error from which the prescribed structure can never be recovered already contradicts the assumed success. In particular there is a successful infinite reference run \(\sigma\).

Call the two pieces \(A\) and \(B\) in this argument, and keep the original seven holder names separate from these set names. Choose a prefix of \(\sigma\) after every holder through depth \(2R+4\) and every one of its outgoing incidences has been completed. There are finitely many of them. Later in \(\sigma\), choose a time **immediately before a unary event of piece \(B\)**. Such events occur arbitrarily late in a successful run. Since that event passed strict priority, there is no raw multipart move anywhere at that instant. Retain this prefix. From then on keep \(B\) fixed and take, in their original order, all subsequent events of \(A\) in \(\sigma\).

This projected sequence is a legal serial run. Every future change on \(B\) in the reference run occurs beyond the spent original neighborhood. No holder able to be a parent on \(A\) has such a change in its \(R\)-neighborhood. Conversely, an \(A\) change cannot alter an \(R\)-neighborhood of a possible parent on the frozen \(B\). The same holds for a joint neighborhood of parents within one piece. Each \(A\) proposal therefore has precisely its original raw availability and count at the corresponding \(A\) prefix. Each \(B\) proposal has its frozen raw status, and in particular \(B\) admits no multipart move. The names on the new distinctions in the projected run can be chosen consistently with the reference one.

A possible cross-piece proposal deserves separate attention. For rules requiring parents to have an ordinary common witness, no such proposal is possible this far out. Even if a broader local rule can inspect the disjoint neighborhoods of distant candidate parents together, no raw cross-piece multipart event can occur in the filtered list of a reachable projected prefix under the success assumption. Such an event would always pass strict priority. Selecting it would permanently connect a descendant of \(Z\) to the other descendants after removal of all original-to-original meetings. In the prescribed completed structure those parts are disconnected after that removal. The change persists under all further births and under renaming. The original set itself is intrinsic here, since precisely its members meet another holder in at least two distinctions. The prefix-extension argument excludes this irreversible error. Applying these facts at each retained event by induction proves legality, including every retained unary. A unary admitted in \(\sigma\) had no competing multipart on \(A\), and there is none on the frozen \(B\).

The projected run is infinite and contains infinitely many multipart choices on \(A\). No unary is continuously filtered-enabled along its remaining length. No \(B\) multipart is enabled. If a particular \(A\) multipart stays filtered-enabled from some point in the projection, it is also raw-enabled throughout the corresponding suffix of \(\sigma\). During intervening \(B\) moves its local data on \(A\) stay fixed. A raw multipart always passes the filter, so the weak fairness of \(\sigma\) eventually selects it. That selection is retained. This exhausts the fairness obligations in the projection. Yet \(B\) makes no further birth, even though it has infinitely many prescribed successors. The contradiction proves the theorem for each source and both knot laws.

For the particular raw rule offering exactly every ready prescribed birth, even the initial state gives a counterorder. The \(Z\) piece then has only unary moves. Leave it fixed and complete the \(U,V\) piece alone. Exhaust each multipart-only phase, then choose its oldest pending unary. Between successive unary choices only finitely many multipart births are possible, as shown below, so each persistently pending unary on this piece is eventually served. Every prescribed event of this piece then appears by induction over its prerequisites. Multiparts occur infinitely often, so none of the waiting \(Z\) unaries stays on the filtered list. The later freezing argument is what rules out even a raw local rule designed to restrict moves near the source.

**Scope of the negative.** A correct local test for the set of ready births and their fresh counts is compatible with this result. With weak fairness on that entire raw roster, every persistently ready event is eventually made. Strong fairness after priority is also sufficient for an exact-ready rule on these sources. From a finite prefix a suffix containing solely multipart events is finite. Each cap is finite, and each pair-indexed part can then use only the finitely many positions below its already reached axial lengths. A missing axial advance requires a unary. Thus every infinite strict-priority run has infinitely many instants with no raw multipart choice. A persistently ready unary is exposed at all those instants and strong fairness serves it. Multiparts persist directly. Induction over the finite prerequisites of each event gives full completion. These statements use the actual ready roster. The earlier serial rule can make a wrong permanent decision before fairness has any chance to act. [Archived serial counterexamples](../FRESH-DEMAND-20261120-001/serial_proof.md)

## An unrestricted present reading that works with either fairness

There is an alternative that needs only the current physical holdings, makes no whole-future map, and guarantees completion even without fairness. It restricts which previously admissible orders may be taken. In each given finite state do the following.

1. Identify the seven original holders as those with a repeated physical meeting. All source holders have such a meeting and every later parent-to-child meeting is single. Recover depth from current ordinary distance to this source set. This recovers orientation on every later shared distinction. Each original distinction shared at the source still joins two original holders. All other distinctions at an original were originally loose, including a slot now spent on a child.
2. If nothing is loose, stop. Otherwise let \(k\) be the **least depth of any holder still holding a loose distinction**. Gather every holder of depth \(k\), spent ones included. For a nonoriginal holder, its openings at birth are all slots except the shared distinctions to the preceding depth. Count those original openings even if some have since been spent. Reconstruct the complete layer's witnessed sets from its incidences to the preceding layer. At \(k=0\), use the recovered original supports, loose status at the source, and original common holders instead.
3. Run the ordinary complete-round group, funding and one-step demand calculation on exactly this reconstructed layer. An underfunded layer has no next step. The funded calculation gives its multiset of parent groups, including leftover unaries, and the immutable count for each. Subtract the actually built depth-\((k+1)\) children by their full parent sets, retaining unary multiplicity. The remaining occurrences are enabled, with arbitrary matching loose slots at the named parents. Apply the chosen priority within this list. For parity the sole, already implied birth uses the registered zero-fresh exception. Under not-all-equal the ordinary keep clause applies. [Complete-step calculation](../ORDINARY-DYNAMICS-20261120-001/end_operator.md)

**Proof.** At the start this reconstructs the actual original round. Inductively all earlier depths are exhausted and **every member of the least loose depth already exists**, although some of its children may also exist. Its complete roster is consequently recoverable. The births made so far from that layer have exactly the prescribed parent sets and counts. At each parent the remaining slots equal the number of remaining occurrences that need it. Choosing one consumes exactly one such occurrence at each of its parents, so it cannot deprive any other required event of its slot. The layer is finite. Every maximal schedule therefore finishes it after exactly its remaining number of births. It cannot be held back by a higher-layer multipart event, since the guard excludes that event until the current layer is spent. This reproduces the full parent incidence and each immutable count up to permutation of equivalent slots.

The physical implication test is respected during serialization. With more than one birth in the complete layer, before its first event at least one other input is loose. After one sibling is made it has a positive fresh output that cannot be spent while the parent layer remains unfinished. The current event's selected loose inputs are consequently always a proper subset of all opens. Connected parity makes them jointly free, and at least one new distinction extends each old allowed state. If the layer has only one event, its actual state before firing is the full preceding layer, including any permitted final parity closure. For not-all-equal, a birth selects at most one loose distinction from each old parent, which retains at least two other places. Its newly supplied distinctions extend every old allowed state. On the registered starts the connected ordinary presentation still contains the high-arity original knots after deleting the selected loose distinctions. The two-colouring lemma for the other distinctions makes every old knot see both values regardless of the selected values, so the new knot also has its full intended projection. [Two-colouring and the keep clause](../NAE-GROWTH-20261119-001/nae_growth.md)

The guard's reconstruction and induction work more generally whenever the true source set and its original shared incidences are recoverable, or are explicitly supplied, and the complete operator has the stated law. A recovery claim on unit-meeting starts needs its own source-recognition proof. The guard reads no previously stored orientation or round counter, but it **does compare the formation depths across the entire current stage**. On these two sources each finite state has finite diameter, with no common finite upper bound sufficient under filtered weak fairness. The price of this solution is a global layer restriction that rules out the valid mixed-size orders which a less restrictive exact-ready rule can allow.
