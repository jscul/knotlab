# Referee report on whether ordinary parity growth repeats the binding step
**Refuted. Block on “one second-layer knot per connected start, and no two can share a fact.” The one-relation theorem on the named family facts holds.**

One of the study’s own starts gives **two second-layer knots sharing a fact**. Take its three rings of four knots with split attachments and double links. Call their whole-ring parities \(R,S,T\). The alternating starting knots of the first ring form two growth families. Call their parities \(P,Q\), and read each on the fresh distinctions of its own first-round births. The five resulting carriers are pairwise disjoint. Their joint image has eight words and precisely two independent equations,

\[
R+S+T=0,\qquad P+Q+R=0.
\]

Each triple takes exactly **000, 011, 101, 110**. The measured relation passes the canonical knot test with any of the three places designated as contact. At round four all five are class facts read by no single knot, under both the frozen scale of five and the all-tied shortest reading. At round five they pass the stronger test too. Their least flipping sizes are **15, 15, 15, 13, 13**. At scale 12, none of the 168 knots has any nonconstant class fact of its own. Removing one source law frees the first triple alone. Removing one first-ring birth law frees the second alone. Each deletion gives 16 five-tuples. The same five carriers work again at round six, at scale 14. [Carriers, measured words, deletions and canonical checks](ring_overlap_counts.json)

The named six-family tuple still has one equation. The earlier review found both the whole-ring and alternating-family readings. Giving the whole-ring fact its own carrier makes both equations visible together. On these five coordinates the bound layer’s own shortest changes span all eight states, leaving zero further class bits. ## What holds

1. **Births and families.** The replay matched 2,349 starts and 6,481 readable stages. There were **0 mixed births out of 23,149**, including the 20 funded transitions that crossed the cap. There were 8,794 multiparent births with multiple families active. None of the 54,053 cross-family pairs of live knots were candidates. All 7,546 surviving lineage transitions kept one family. All 284 ended lineages had a sole closing birth. [Counts](growth_counts.json) · [Earlier component-preservation proof](../../ORDINARY-DYNAMICS-20261120-001/end_operator.md)
2. **One relation.** All **2,317 nonempty family lists** have exactly the even-tuple image. There are 32 empty lists, 1,326 fixed singletons, 746 tied pairs and 245 lists of at least three facts. A sum of laws cancels every shared distinction only if its two holders have the same coefficient. Connectedness makes that coefficient constant. This works for any partition of the loose distinctions. All 16 merge controls lost one family as forced by the added common witness. [Proof](argument.md) · [Counts](growth_counts.json)
3. **Least flips and bounded parities.** All **11,382** archived family-minimum calls agree with exact minima, including 2,920 fixed parities. The largest finite answer was 15, below the cap of 40. All **14,876** adjacent comparisons of nonconstant family sums increased by exactly two. The referee proof gives this equality at every funded round. A starting parity outside the family span has a flipping change of size at most the larger of four and the number of starting knots. A later parity without a start representative has a fixed-start flip of size at most four. [Proof and generators](argument.md) · [Minima](minima_counts.json) · [Stress checks](split_counts.json)
4. **Scale five.** The same class space of start parities occurs on all **945** round-three/four pairs and all **952** available round-two/three pairs. On 57 of the 945 pairs, its dimension exceeds the family rank by one to four. All tested nonconstant family facts belong to it from round three. Equal dimensions alone give no bound on the future cost of flipping an individual parity. [Raw counts](record_counts.json) · [Independent spaces](minima_counts.json)
5. **Larger scale.** At round four, using one less than the minimum family flip, all 48 measured family facts survive. **36 of 48**, comprising **8 of 12** constructions, are outside the span of all single-knot class facts. The four failures are the paired five- and seven-knot rings, each with both kinds of links. At the same thresholds the corresponding *whole-ring* facts pass on **4 of 12** constructions. On four-knot rings, the two alternating parts each pass, while their sum for the whole ring is locally generated. The four six-knot-ring constructions above the original cap all pass for their family facts at scale ten, giving **12 of 16** on the extended roster. At scale 12 all eight split constructions on the extended roster pass for their *whole-ring* facts, while the even-ring halves already flip. All four formerly failing paired constructions pass at round six at scales 15 and 16. [Local spans and thresholds](scale_counts.json)
6. **Small-scale bindings.** On the 32 ring constructions, all **320 starting-support and 208 later-support** readings per move rule equal sums of connecting-knot attachment parities. The unrestricted search found 19 binding rows at scale five and two under shortest changes, on ten distinct starts. All 63 designated facts are locally generated under the reading that found them. The 14-knot construction passes the stricter test on all four measured stages under both rules. The ten-knot path triple passes under shortest changes at round three. [Local reads](scale_counts.json)
7. **An overlap with five separate preparations.** A second, 48-knot start gives five disjoint single-cycle carriers after one round. At scale 11 all five parities change at the source and all five survive at round one, with zero local class facts. Two disjoint sets of source laws give the two equations. Deleting a law from either set frees just its triple. Deleting both gives all 32 tuples. Fixing any one of the 208 round-eight knots preserves all eight joint tuples. Five disjoint preparations then give all 32 when taken separately. [Supports, law sets and preparations](overlap_counts.json)

**A third-layer knot under a chosen reading.** On thirteen five-knot rings, choose 29 separately carried sums of family facts. At round 24 their least flips have risen from five to 53. At physical scale 52 none of the 1,651 knots has a nonconstant local class fact. Eighteen selected second-layer knots allow exactly the observed 4,096 tuples. ## How it was checked

The replay uses an independent ordinary growth implementation, with the census JSON as input, regenerated labelled draws and rebuilt constructions. It matched **11,496 prior birth records and 1,040 stop records** on 1,901 starts through round three, plus 128 earlier stage sizes on 32 built starts. No study analysis or growth module was imported. The least-change check solves the two-holder parity problem as a shortest odd cycle and compares against literal small instances. A fixed parity and a finite answer beyond the cap are distinct outcomes. The frozen XOR clauses passed all eight truth assignments. Cardinality and model-indexing checks distinguished fixed, 39, 40 and above 40. An empty support is also fixed mathematically, though the original solver indexes its first member.
