# A note: the two recursion leads beside the one relation

`THIRD-LAYER-20261126-001`, at the coordinator's request, separately and only as a note: how the two leads the advisor
named relate to this study. Readings, not results.

**Parity knots composing into a knot** (goal G12, proved in `BASE-THREE-20261021-001`: two knots sharing one
distinction compose into one knot exactly when they can force it to opposite values). Under parity, the sum of two laws
that share a distinction is a law on the rest: a + b + s and s + c + d give a + b + c + d. The one relation of a connected
start among its families' facts is this composition carried through the whole start: summing every law sums out every
shared distinction and leaves the parity of all the loose distinctions, grouped by family. So the second-layer knot that
ordinary growth keeps is the composite of all the first-layer knots of a connected start. That is why there is exactly
one: composition turns many knots into one, and a tower would need several second-layer knots sharing facts, which one
composite cannot supply. A start built in several levels (codes made of codes) supplies them by hand, and growth leaves
such a build as it was.

**A released product that keeps releasing** (`GENERATORS-20261120-001`, nested.md: inside one ordinary structure, under
both laws, a product whose later members take every parent from the product itself can release a product of its own;
which released pattern counts at the second level is an observer's two-scale test). Claim T's families are the start's
parent-closed lineages: no birth has parents in two of them (T1, on every birth read). So from round 2 on every birth of a
family's lineage takes all its parents from that lineage, a product in that study's sense, and the family's fact is
what that product carries. A nested release would be a family whose
lineage later splits into lineages that never again share a child: new families, and so new facts that last, could
appear during growth. The one relation would still hold at each stage, since it is a property of each connected
structure's laws, so a release would add facts to the second layer, not relations among them. Whether lineages split
under ordinary growth was not measured here.

**So, on the owner's third rung.** Both leads are about the growth reproducing a shape (a composite knot; a product that
emits products), and both meet the same limit as the tower: what ordinary growth reproduces carries one relation per
connected structure.
