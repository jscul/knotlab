# Recurring events already in the growth record

This inventory uses the frozen definition and the named ordinary-growth records. A tick counts a newly observable release or turn once, including when several returning phases appear together. Sizes in the prior word proofs count original points of a straight interval. Subtract one to obtain parent age on its original map, and add its anchor age for a later map. The period of the letters in a produced family, its width, the number of new families at a tick and the spacing between successive ticks are four different quantities.

For the return test AᵏB with fixed return lengths a,b, the uniform formula is launch age 2(ka+b)−1 at anchor zero and interlaunch period 2a. It includes the first four rows. The two residual-return edits anchor at age one. Under the delayed source reading any computable integer T(g)>2g is allowed. Choosing T(g)=(2g)² gives sizes 64(n+1)² and successive gaps 64(2n+3), which have no constant period. This is a choice already explicit in the preceding record. It prevents a claim that all admissible recurring event readings advance at a universal constant rate.
