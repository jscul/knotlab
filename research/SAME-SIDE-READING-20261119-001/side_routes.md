First-layer counts illustrate the distinction. Let `S` be the original holders within distance `K−1` from any marked holder, where `K=floor((r−1)/2)` for an incidence radius `r`. A born knot is counted in the first layer precisely when at least one of its parents is in `S`. ## Exact reach of a fixed-radius reading

Let an old shared mark `e` have original holders `h`, and let `v` be born at age `t` with set of original ancestors `A(v)`. Its incidence distance is

$$d_{\rm inc}(e,v)=1+2\bigl(t+\min_{h,\,u\in A(v)}d_{\rm source}(h,u)\bigr).$$

After the first hop to `h`, replace each interior youngest knot between two of its parents by their common older holder. Repeated replacement produces an equal or shorter route whose positive ages ascend to `v` from some original ancestor. Every such ancestor has a chain of `t` parent steps to `v`. The two directions give the equality. A fixed radius `r` can include only knots born through `K=floor((r−1)/2)`. Distances to earlier knots remain fixed, so every fixed-radius ball has its final count at age `K`, if reached. In particular every possible witness at fewer than seven hops already had enough ages to be settled on these panels.

For an all-age null at large offset, take the original holders within `2K` source steps of the marked holders. Any counted knot at age `t≤K` has an ancestor within `K−t` steps of some marked holder. Its other ancestors are at distance at most `2t` from that ancestor by taking the two parent chains through the knot and preserving old distances. Thus all its original ancestors lie in the chosen `2K` region. Compare quiet versus loaded at fixed other loads. Any changed first proposal has one original parent whose activity changes. Every other parent is within two source steps of that parent. If the nearest marked-holder offset `d` to the toggled original ring obeys **`d>2K+2`**, every original holder in the `2K` region retains its entire first child roster. All loose values stay quiet under the minimum extension. The pure-roster induction couples the descendants of this region at all common funded ages. The distance formula then couples membership in the ball. The frozen cold policy and continuing funded spares keep the named comparisons open until their computation caps. The bound rules out a fixed ordinary radius sensing a once-loaded ring at arbitrarily large offset in this family.
