## Physical channel and questions

Each of the 512 possible raw inputs is an arbitrary table of membership in nine distinct cells. Its binary mask uses bits 0 through 8. The sender sees the input and the public wiring for all four questions. It commits to the complete binary payload before the caller chooses a question. The receiver sees the same wiring, the payload and the eventual port index. All source identities, packet length and slot bindings must be computable exclusively from that wiring and fixed learned parameters. No input-dependent length, addresses, timing, shared state, supplementary bits or grading answers can pass to the receiver. The two agents can share a frozen public message convention. The study measures the length of the actual tuple sent.

Each of the four ports has four anonymous clause rows, one holding three signed source occurrences and three holding two. Signs, grouping, source identity and incidence are public. No question ID, Boolean gate name, lookup table of correct outputs, omitted-port label, or family label enters the policies. For scoring only, a row is met when at least one signed occurrence is true, and a port is true when all its rows are met. The groups and the ordering used to visit them are supplied. The interpretation of a literal, how to combine entries within a row and how to combine rows must be selected by terminal answer rewards in the program search. All Boolean actions in decoder computations use NAND, constants and copies. Address routing, iteration over the public arrays and packet I/O are supplied. Computed query candidates offered to the sender must be evaluated by its learned policy on the raw relation.

The varied fit also sees wiring with one or two exclusions, four independent reads, an alias, and repeated questions. Train on the 256 nine-bit inputs of even parity. Thus every possible value of the three or four relevant cells still occurs, while the other half of the raw input tables can be used to check transfer. Fit from the four terminal answer bits and the public per-wiring message width. Select for fewer wrong bits, then fewer transmitted bits, then fixed simplicity or deterministic ties. No intermediate correct values or optimal compression labels are provided. Record the search grammar, candidate counts, transmit alternatives, winner gates, train performance and fit restrictions. Freeze both sender/decoder checkpoints as physical files before evaluating any held wiring. Do not update either on a new question. Use six placements of each of six training families (36 wirings), with the six any-of-three examples as the strict slice. The held roster contains 168 wirings. It includes the three original bits and their disjunction, the same three and exactly-one, and four genuinely independent questions. Hold exactly-one out of both fits.
