# Train a physical choice among feeder cues

This prospective comparison asks whether actual public eat consequences can alter a prepared knot's later action and whether the resulting fixed reader survives longer on fresh starts. The fixed current-only rule attempts ON at a feeder unless its previous actual action was an eat. Every reader supplies one bit per turn and receives the same matched movement tape on its own reset. OFF still uses the uniformly drawn anonymous control. All games end at zero energy. Growth, movement, energy and the persistent feeder cue come from the archived two-opportunity feeder game.

## Public information and offered rules

The device receives the current feeder cue, own energy, previous actual action and anonymous outgoing controls. After a choice it receives the actual ON or OFF, outgoing slot used if any and actual energy after the turn. History and learned credit reside in separately prepared bot-side knots. The game law selects the corresponding physical preparation. World identity, movement tape, current stock and scorer records stay with the game or scorer.

Only a view on which current-only would attempt ON is eligible for a veto. Every veto also requires the actual last paid feeder's one-versus-many menu type to equal the current nonzero type. The four supplied detectors, in fixed priority, are:

1. `strict_return` certifies a return to the feeder that last paid. Staying there preserves certification. A completed return through an intermediate holder with one available control after the final supplied growth opportunity certifies that return. The device tracks the opportunity boundary from its own action receipts and checks the observed departure menu.
2. `approach_feeder` matches the departure holder's persistent feeder cue when the last paid feeder was reached and when the current feeder was reached.
3. `approach_menu` matches zero, one or many controls at those two departure holders.
4. `approach_slot` matches the parity of the actual outgoing control used at those two departures.

The approach detectors retain the latest actual arrival while staying. Missing arrival evidence makes a detector abstain. Agreement on an approach cue is a supplied prediction of depletion. Separate low-order route and receipt registers are initialized afresh for every episode.

## Public training and the feedback control

Process turns in actual episode order. Initialize each score to zero and the selector to `none`. Every training episode plays the current-only script and resets its transient state. Before the actual ON, prepare each physical candidate's veto mark on that view. A charged ON pays a ration exactly when the reported energy exceeds the previous energy minus one. Each veto gets +1 for failing to find a ration and −4 for a paid ration. Abstentions receive zero. No scoring occurs on passes, refusals, unprobed feeder views or hypothetical opportunities. Clip each candidate score to [−7,+7] after each update.
