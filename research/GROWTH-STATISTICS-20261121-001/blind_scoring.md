# Scoring the blind calls

This rule was written before the custodian released observations. The fresh-context reader supplied all numerical cutoffs in `blind_reader.py`. There was no fitting on the four constructions. The reference geometry controls were 12 points on an ordinary Euclidean circle and the same points measured by the taxicab norm. The reader correctly identified both references before testing. Neither reference contributes a test case.

The benchmark weights each requested family equally and each of three roots within a family equally. Roots from the same deterministic construction are correlated. The twelve main records and six records conditional on admission to the dimension-two shape comparison support exact panel counts. There is no population confidence interval for a roster of four deliberately selected constructions. Ranges over roots and the effect of swapping the distance reading describe finite variation.

1. On every main row, publish the numerical exponent, the dimension call (including abstention), the independent local six-neighbor check, and verified long-separation bond hits. The reference meeting exponents are three on the cubic construction and two on both shape controls and the two-shift folded base. The exponent is for volume in **meeting steps**. The recorded cubic theorem concerns bond volume. The folded base contains infinitely many points in a bounded bond ball.
2. On every shape row, publish the candidate or abstention against the held-back reference. The program words for the two recorded limits are round and polygonal. These are finite-sample calls about those references. The separate shape packet has new IDs, matched support and both constructions. Its conditional roster is disclosed explicitly. A row with no corroborating observations earns no positive diagnosis.
3. Join the anonymous panel IDs by their custodian-held correspondence only after individual calls have been locked. For an overall four-family call, a positive bounded-bond signature takes precedence. Otherwise require both the cubic exponent candidate and the local arrangement signature to identify the cubic reference. Otherwise use a positive bond-table shape call when meeting dimension two is supported. All remaining cases abstain. A conditional shape call that passes on its own remains reported even if the combined call abstains. Crosswalk availability alone never gives a positive class label.
4. Report correct, wrong and inconclusive for the twelve cases, the six shape calls and each separate reading. A family meets the benchmark if at least two of its three cases have the correct combined call. All four must meet this threshold for an affirmative answer to blind recognition of the whole roster. Every case remains visible when this threshold fails. The three roots measure sensitivity within a construction.

The dimension-only control can identify a candidate with the sole three-dimensional exponent. It cannot identify either member of the same-dimension shape pair. The six-neighbor test inspects pairs of knots that both meet a given knot. It requires three disjoint opposite pairs with no second common neighbor, and twelve pairs with a second common neighbor. Even an exact finite match establishes only a local arrangement. In the shape test the primary distances come from bonds. As a sensitivity check, apply the same thresholds to meeting steps on the same ordered points. This companion never changes the recorded primary call.

A cold pre-freeze advisor proposed testing the separation of approximate midpoints. It is a reasonable alternative with a different sampling requirement. The frozen primary rule already combines a four-point inequality with errors of fit to all 66 pairwise distances. The sample contains eleven other points per landmark and does not exhaust its possible midpoints. No midpoint-based threshold was fitted or scored in this study.
