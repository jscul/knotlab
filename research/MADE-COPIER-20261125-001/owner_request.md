Please record this as my idea and file it as a lead, to start once the fold-of-emergence study has closed. In my words:

copying would not be a law. Laws are for how the knots interact. A copy function, like other functions, would be a
logical structure that has a meeting pattern with other things. And we would need to prove that the copy/paste pattern
can be generalized.

Keep two things apart in everything written about this:

- a law is the rule the growth follows (which knots join, what a new knot receives, what a short knot does);
- a copier is a pattern the growth makes.

What we have so far makes more of itself and takes no input. What I am asking for meets another pattern and leaves two
of it.

The question: under the cheapest law the fold study finds that lets parts meet, is there a made pattern that, on meeting
a second pattern, leaves two of the second?

Steps, in order:

1. State the claim before testing it. For which class of patterns should the copier work, and what counts as "two of
   it": the same form, the same built structure, or the same values? Say which of these is claimed.
2. Check the record first. In particular the other thread's note that a chain of parity knots hands a value on only
   once, so that sending one value to two places needs a distinction with a third holder. Confirm or correct that from
   the record, and say what it implies for pasting a value under parity and under not-all-equal.
3. The first test: let the existing two-knot part that copies itself meet one other small pattern, and see whether
   anything of the second pattern appears twice afterwards. Use the fold study's paired test: with and without the
   second pattern.
4. Generalize or bound it. Either an argument that the copier works for the whole stated class, or the smallest pattern
   it fails on and why.

Stop at a pattern that leaves two of what it met, with the class it works for, or at a reason no made pattern can under
that law.

Usual standard: a frozen pre-registration reviewed cold by the advisor; an engine written from the frozen text; the knot
test on every knot made, under parity and not-all-equal; every added rule named as a scaffold and swapped; a plain
statement of whether the copier was supplied or made; no claims about living things or minds. Check any new word with
the advisor. Report in the two-table format at its stop.

## How the thread reads it

- **Law and copier are kept apart in every sentence.** The law is fixed before any pattern is looked at and is never
  changed to make a pattern work. A rule added so that something is copied would be copying as a law, which the owner
  excludes.
- **"The cheapest law the fold study finds that lets parts meet"** is the law that study calls MET: a new knot also
  takes one distinction that is already shared, from a knot its parent meets. It looks two holder steps out and reads no
  record of who came from whom.

  just cost complexity.") is his intuition and is kept apart from the evidence.

How the thread reads it:

- **It is his idea, recorded as his** (history of assumption A244), and it changes what counts as "two of it", not the
  law.
- **In the study's terms:** the first pattern and the second become one part, births with a parent in each use up the
  second pattern's loose distinctions, the second pattern's own descendants end, and afterwards there are two parts the
  same as the second pattern was, made by those joint births and their descendants. Before this addition the design
  counted only two that descend from the first pattern alone, which rules this case out.
- **Why it may be cheaper.** A join uses loose distinctions, and which loose distinction goes to which birth makes no
  difference up to renaming; nothing has to be supplied to say which already shared distinction is taken. So a case found
  this way, under a law with no taking rule, cannot depend on the numbering of the start, which is what removed every
  case so far.
- **What it costs.** The two patterns have to be one part for a join to happen. Under a law with no taking rule that
  meeting has to be supplied by the start; under the law that lets parts meet, the takes that bring them together are
  the ones that use the names. And a join uses up both parents, so the first pattern is used up with the second unless
  it is made again among what comes out.
- **It becomes a named arm of the study**, with its own claim stated before any registered run, its own class and its
  own prediction.
