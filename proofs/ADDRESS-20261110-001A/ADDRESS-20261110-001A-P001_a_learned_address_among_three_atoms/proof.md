# A learned address among three atoms

Status: Computed (`check.py`), pre-registered in `research/ADDRESS-20261110-001/preregistration.md`. The search is the targeted search of
`research/LEARN-RISE-20261110-001/posthoc_targeted.py`, frozen here before running: state flips (A50) and learning steps at A26 sites, at most
one unit above the floor, best first toward a target chosen by SAT, at most 300,000 configurations.

## F1: a frozen confirmation of `LEARN-RISE-20261110-001` on fresh starts

On K_3's line orbits 2 and 3, which that epic did not use, with two Fano atoms (at l0 and at distance 1, 2 or 3), the leftover reached the
other atom from all 6 starts, in 25 to 36 moves with 4 to 8 learning steps (440 to 984 configurations). At the end the atoms had swapped
roles in 5 and were both transparent in 1. That epic's post-hoc finding now holds under a frozen prediction.

## F2: three atoms, a chosen pair

On K_3 with atoms at l0 and at distances 2 and 3 (genus 3, floor 2, two units), from the floor state the sight-4 learner leaves (units in
atoms 1 and 2, or atom 1 and a junction), every pair of atoms was reached as the holders of the two units, on all 3 line orbits (9 of 9).
Bringing a unit into atom 0 took 30 to 43 moves with 8 to 15 learning steps; the pair already holding them took none, or 15 flips and no
learning where one unit sat at a junction. At every success the two named atoms act as their lines and the third is transparent.

So learning, at a rise and with the state moving, can place the leftover tension in any chosen atoms: the network holds a learned address,
read off by where the tension sits.

F1 and F2 held.
