Even giving the repairs the correct parent sets leaves that count wrong. Enlarging the knot later changes its original allowed states. [Repair tests](archived_repairs.md)

**With fairness for births that remain possible, a bounded rule works at every size on both starts.** Two ordinary holder steps around the marked pair are necessary and sufficient for its count, even after the third original knot spends its loose distinctions. Four steps from any proposed parent suffice for *every* ready birth and its count. The exact minimum for the whole rule lies between two and four. The rule reads spent holders too. In the continuing parts, two live parents can join when they have exactly one common holder and it is spent. A second common holder means their joint child already exists. **With weak fairness applied after strict global joins-first, no fixed ordinary radius can guarantee completion.** One piece can alternate one-parent and multi-parent births forever. Each multi-parent birth temporarily removes the other piece’s waiting one-parent birth from the list. The schedule therefore satisfies this definition of fairness while the other piece never advances. A whole-stage rule succeeds under both policies. It recognizes the original knots by their repeated meetings and finishes the least unfinished formation size first. It uses no supplied future map, but it restricts which sizes may interleave.
