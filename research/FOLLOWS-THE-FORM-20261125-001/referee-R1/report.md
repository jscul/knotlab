# Independent referee report on the fixed-link seed study
**Confirmed with fixes. No Block.**

Holding the first seed and its attachment fixed, changing the second seed changes what the first seed’s own descent makes. All 90 held-out studies distinguish at least two second seeds. Two stronger readings fail. A difference first *measured* in round 2 can already be present in round 1. And the counts of knots by their steps from the held knot identify every one of the 345 seeds, leaving no pair there with which to test whether those counts suffice. The separate partner swaps do provide that test.

## The fixes

The quoted text identifies the sentence to annotate. Frozen definitions, predictions and historical reports retain their original wording. These are proposed corrections for the current result, guide and checkpoint.

1. **Narrowing — what the link control establishes.** The [ticket result](../../FOLLOWS-THE-FORM-20261125-001A/results.md) says “the link carries the difference” and “with the two seeds in one start and not linked, nothing differs”. Use “The linked second seeds give different own births. When the seeds share nothing, the rules keep their descendants separate through every round both runs complete.” In an unlinked start every parent group, sole candidate and demand comes from knots already joined by shared distinctions within one seed’s descendants. Giving, taking and allocating fresh distinctions preserve that separation. CU can expose a faulty implementation. It has no second route to discover under these laws. The whole-run guard did shorten 375/5,400 unlinked form runs relative to bare. [Control argument](trace_notes.md)
2. **Narrowing — when the difference enters.** The [claim, C2](../claim.md) says “For those the difference entered the first seed’s descent after round 1.” Replace this in the [result](../../FOLLOWS-THE-FORM-20261125-001A/results.md), [decision](../decision.md), [guide, lesson 10.5](../../../publication/knotlab/INTUITIONS.md) and [goals checkpoint](../../../docs/RESEARCH_GOALS.md) with “The first seed’s own recorded births first differ later, despite matching round-1 multisets for all births with a first-seed ancestor.” Qualify “422 to 1,309 pairs a row” with “where short knots retire or make offshoots”. Both hand traces show a round-1 difference the multiset omits.
3. **Narrowing — what ‘form’ and ‘follows’ measure.** In the correction to the [result](../../FOLLOWS-THE-FORM-20261125-001A/results.md), the [later check](../checks/what_decides_the_class/README.md), the [decision](../decision.md), [lesson 10.5](../../../publication/knotlab/INTUITIONS.md) and the [goal notes on supplied and grown meetings](../../../docs/RESEARCH_GOALS.md), replace “the class follows the second seed’s knots by their steps from the held knot” with “Of the 92 pairs with the same held knot and the same collection of loose and partner counts at different steps, all 92 are told apart somewhere, in 8,209/24,564 comparisons.” All 345 complete by-step profiles are unique. The swap test supplies the otherwise missing comparison at fixed by-step counts. A two-step match occurs for only 141/59,340 distinct pairs, and 251/345 seeds already have every knot within two steps. The phrase “any tally” in the later check exceeds the profiles tested. Keep the separate 23/270 partner-swap result and its construction. The registered headline’s “differs with the form” is defensible as a statement that changing the attached seed can change the output.
4. **Narrowing — the two-step comparison.** The [later check’s opening](../checks/what_decides_the_class/README.md) says “with no taking rule it is the same for every pair that agrees out to two steps”. Use “Without taking, retirement and offshoots gave 0/12,690 differences through round 9. Waiting gave 19/6,345 in two studies.” With taking, 2,552/18,612 differ in 54/132 studies. Also replace “They differ there only in loose counts, three or four steps out” with “77 of the 141 differ only in farther loose counts. The other 64 compare chains of four and five knots.” Eleven of the 19 waiting differences are in the first group and eight in the second. For example, end-held chains with loose counts (3,2,3,2) and (3,2,3,2,3) first differ in round 4 for the three-knot first ring under the cap of three with waiting. The profile counts all partners of an included knot, including partners beyond the cutoff.
5. **Narrowing — the 49-study reading.** In the [goals checkpoint](../../../docs/RESEARCH_GOALS.md) replace “In 49 of 90 held-out studies the class is a count profile of the second seed” with “In 49/90 studies no equal-profile pair had different classes. Only 245/86,730 pairs there shared the profile.” In [lesson 10.5](../../../publication/knotlab/INTUITIONS.md) replace “so nothing could have contradicted it” with “245 pairs could have contradicted it, and three studies had no such pair.” Retain the author’s withdrawal and the later 3/267 result. The capability gained is a test on supplied attachments. The goal concerning two parts the growth makes meeting through a knot it makes remains open.
6. **Narrowing — the registered score.** In the [result](../../FOLLOWS-THE-FORM-20261125-001A/results.md), [decision](../decision.md), [lesson 10.5](../../../publication/knotlab/INTUITIONS.md) and [goals checkpoint](../../../docs/RESEARCH_GOALS.md), qualify “Nineteen of twenty predictions hold.” Use “Eighteen rows passed in full, the fate prediction failed, and the sampled knot test passed on the completed portion.” The cut was recorded after 14 minutes of testing. The retained half completed 99/100 not-all-equal runs and 50/50 parity runs, with 113,640 and 62,347 verdicts respectively. Another 114 runs completed outside the half. Subsequent exact checks passed on the 37 omissions. The registered positive claims did not depend on the knot test.
7. **Not a block — prior credit.** The [pre-registration](../../FOLLOWS-THE-FORM-20261125-001A/preregistration.md) says “No record substitutes one form for another at a fixed place.” Qualify it in the result as “The preceding exploratory probe already substituted seeds at a fixed attachment. This study registered the comparisons and tested five further first seeds and 60 further second seeds of the same kinds.” The prior-work gate passes. The earlier study of parts meeting again varied presence, and the earlier registered birth study varied availability. The directly linked third probe already reported the fixed-attachment effect. [Predecessor probe](../../WHAT-A-BIRTH-CARRIES-20261125-001/checks/form_at_a_fixed_place/README.md)
