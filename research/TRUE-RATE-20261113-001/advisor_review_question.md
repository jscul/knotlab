Review cold, at most ~350 words. Goal: a sound true growth rate for the least bond with side branches.

Setting (from our last two studies): one-definition nested NAE family, infinite pattern = tree of copies; ray C_0..C_s; side subtrees hang off ray copy C_j through the 3 arguments of a side SELF call (distinctions of C_j's environment). Least bond L(s) between a top port and an own distinction of C_s, members anywhere; L_ray(s) members on the ray only. Exact DP: summaries (F,G) of subtrees (families of 8-bit port masks over assignments of the chosen members; G with one member forgotten), combined at copies; side subtrees via a 'generic table' of least cost per summary computed as a least fixed point; ray summaries recursed up from C_s; root acceptance. Last study capped the generic table at cost L_ray(s) - 2, so no rate was sound. Data: L <= L_ray always; side branches shorten L in >= 62/226 growing families by 1..3; growth survives where read (75 families to depth >= 8); in 4 families the deficit grows 0,1,1,2,2,3,3 (true rate about half the ray rate).

Plan A (per-family certificate): compute the UNCAPPED generic table (finite: summaries are drawn from a finite universe, least cost per summary by Knuth's least fixed point; terminate when no new summary or cheaper cost appears). Then the ray-summary transition (combine a ray child summary with any side summaries and own members) is s-independent: build the graph of ray summaries reachable from C_s-base summaries, trim to co-reachable to root acceptance, Karp minimum cycle mean = lambda_true (liminf of L(s)/s), with L(s) >= lambda_true (s - t). Gate: L(s) from the graph equals the last study's exact values where reached.
Plan B (structural lemma): any side subtree S used by a bond could be replaced by its 3 port distinctions at cost <= 3 per side subtree, giving lambda_ray <= 3 lambda_true. I doubt this preserves minimality.
Predictions draft: P1 complete graphs for >= 50% of 226; P2 lambda_true > 0 in every complete family (a counterexample is a result); P3 lambda_true >= lambda_ray/3 in every complete family.
Questions: Is Plan A exact (is the uncapped least-cost table finite and correct, and is Karp sound on the s-independent ray-summary graph with fixed-length paths)? Is the side-child product tractable (combine children one at a time with partial-summary dedup)? Is Plan B's lemma true, false, or provable in a weaker form (e.g. per level the bond needs at least one member in the level's region, giving only L >= #levels used)? What is settled by construction?
