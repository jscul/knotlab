(G) admits exactly the map's multi-parent obligations as their parents become present. A move must still have a loose distinction available at every parent. With (G) off, the original current-stage group rule and prior-common-child exclusion apply. With (U) off, a unary is offered when a holder has more loose distinctions than currently admitted multi-parent moves involving it. Unrestricted scheduling may choose any listed move. Joins-first filters out all unaries when any multi-parent move is listed.
