# Candidate components through three full layers

Of 480 lawful starts, 107 funded their first layer under both knot laws.
Every continuing step transferred each old candidate component to exactly
one new component, with **zero cross-component births, splits or mergers**.
Parental transfer was independent across components. Parity's fresh count
could still depend on whether another component was present. Nine actual
parity histories closed on a ternary. Each corresponding not-all-equal
birth received one fresh distinction. [Frozen sample design](preregistration.md)

## Source weighting and coverage

The tree weighting uses a uniform labelled Prüfer word. The other
weighting draws a fresh such tree and adds one uniformly selected missing
meeting. Every meeting has one shared distinction. At ordinary degree
\(g\), each source holder gets \(\max(0,3-g)\) loose distinctions plus
one with independent probability \(1/4\). Each size has 48 draws in each
weighting. The seeds are 20261120701 for trees and 20261120702 for augmented
trees. Both laws use exactly the same physical draws. All sources pass
the literal projection test on every written knot under both laws.

## Actual parents and the component by itself

The independent end operator plans the complete roster and its demands.
The archived ordinary operator then spends literal loose distinctions and
introduces the fresh distinctions. The checker reads the realized parent
sets from the holders of those spent distinctions. It compares all parent
sets, counts and demands between evaluators. For parity the implication
test is on the old row span. For not-all-equal it uses the actual
old-state SAT test, with every projected input assignment also checked
for the observed birth arities. The records retain distinct physical
unary births even when their parent sets agree.

At a positive size the two readers recover every physical common-witness
set of size at least two from the actual parent incidence. For any two
old candidate neighbors, some admitted birth takes both parents. Every
other birth has all of its parents in one old component. These properties
connect each old component's births and exclude any birth taking from two
old components. In the realized steps the checker verified 1,192
old candidate pairs per law. A sole zero-fresh birth ends the entire
live end.

All 658 parity and 663 not-all-equal component restrictions give the
same parent rosters and raw demands as the respective component's
births in the whole step. The calculation keeps its incoming witness
sets and treats its loose distinctions as the entire live end. Every
not-all-equal fresh count agrees as well. The 92 parity differences
arise when the restricted end would have one birth. Forty of these are
ternaries that go from zero fresh in the restriction to one in the
actual whole step. The remaining 34 unary and 18 binary zero-fresh
answers use the archived small-arity exception. A zero-fresh birth of
those arities is outside the canonical knot gate. In the whole step
the corresponding births get two and one fresh respectively. For
example, at age two in `tree/4/01`, knots `10,11,12` make a ternary on
literal distinctions `20,21,22`. Its demand is zero, and another
component makes a birth, so this ternary receives one fresh distinction.
Its end-only restriction has the same parent set with zero fresh.

### Reducing literal holdings

An additional comparison erases only loose distinctions held by other
components and keeps every shared distinction and all the remaining
support rows. This preserves the selected part's actual incoming
holdings. It constitutes a new physical start, checked independently
before growth. Among 627 proper-part reductions per law, 617 leave at
least one support with fewer than three places. Of the ten remaining,
five fail the parity projection gate and all ten pass it under
not-all-equal. All five admitted parity replays and all ten admitted
not-all-equal replays agree with the respective whole-step birth counts
and fresh counts. The 40 ternary budget differences above come from
the end comparison. The tested operation on literal supports supplies
no admitted physical reduced source for those differences.

## Present readings

The full reader uses the accumulating physical holdings to count the
common holders of each pair. The witnessed-end reader gets only live
knots, their loose counts and the witnessed sets among them. Both return
whole candidate components, cyclic whole components and any degree-one
ends of candidate paths holding two loose distinctions. Cyclic components
are compared by their loose counts and witness multiplicities up to
rotation and reversal. A cyclic pair relation may contain a witness
holding three or more of its members, which the record marks separately.
The third reading drops every spent holder's witness set.

Full and witnessed readings agree on all 796 parity and 801
not-all-equal frames, including the empty ends. Independent renaming of
knots and distinctions preserves each read on a funded example of each
weighting. The witness-erasure reading deletes all 1,380 positive-age
candidate pairs per law because the physical witnesses are all spent
parents. It also loses every positive-age cyclic component and two-loose
path end. The full witnessed reading registers 74 cyclic whole components
and 635 two-loose path ends across positive sizes per law.

## Unaries and finite parentage

A live singleton component spends each loose distinction on its own
unary birth. Every surviving newborn with no sibling sharing an actual
parent is itself alone in its candidate component, and conversely. Under
both laws 85 parent occurrences give more than one unary offspring in one
cohort. Every actual parent spends all of its loose distinctions in that
cohort, and no parent recurs at a later size. At size three, 221 newborns
per law have a chain of three successive unary births. These follow
physical parentage and reuse the unary rule on new knots. The sample
ends after the third full layer.
