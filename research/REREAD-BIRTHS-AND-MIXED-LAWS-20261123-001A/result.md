**Stated in its least form, the re-reading move is lawful and it repairs a start, once. It forbids no old word, it
lets most starts that stall at once grow, and it gives distinctions a third and a fourth holder. It never acts in a
structure that ordinary growth has made, because there no knot is ever short.** Rule R: as ordinary growth, except that
a live knot that owes more loose distinctions than it holds pays the rest with distinctions it shares.

## What happened

**Old words.** No allowed word fails to extend at any birth, under either law: 0 of 18.7 billion words over 772,480
rounds, 4.1 million of whose births took a shared distinction. Under parity a birth gets no fresh distinction only when
the parity of what it takes is implied by the old knots.

**The recorded sources are untouched.** None of the seven sources of the review-leads epic ever has a short knot, so
all 28 histories under R are the ordinary ones.

**Starts that stall at once.** Of the review-leads epic's 1,024 sparse draws per law, 744 make no birth under ordinary
growth. Under R, with the oldest (O) or the newest (N) allowed shared distinction paid:

| | Convention O | Convention N |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Still no birth at round 1 (residual stall, or no live knot) | 115 | 98 |
| Alive after 12 rounds, parity / not-all-equal | 588 / 605 | 604 / 625 |
| Stopped by the 5,000-knot cap | 24 | 21 |
| Closed, parity | 17 | 21 |
| Most holders of a distinction: 2 / 3 / 4 | 115 / 521 / 108 | 98 / 549 / 97 |

Eighty draws stall under every allowed choice: some knot owes more than it holds in all. No distinction reaches five
holders.

**The rule acts at round 1 only.** Every payment in these histories is in the first round. Under ordinary growth a
newborn is given as many fresh distinctions as the next round asks of it, so only a knot of the start can be short,
and under R the same held with one exception over every allowed choice of the first two rounds: one draw, under
parity, where 3 of 192 choices leave a short knot at round 2.

**The choice of which shared distinction is paid matters.** The two conventions give different numbers of births per
round on 478 (parity) and 471 (not-all-equal) of the 744. On one draw, O gives 7, 6, 4, 1, 1 births and N gives 7, 9,
13, 18, 23. So rule R is a family of laws until something native fixes the choice.

**A third holder undoes the parity closing.** Under ordinary parity growth a round's only birth takes every loose
distinction and closes the whole. After a payment, 482 (O) and 536 (N) such births are not implied by the old knots
and the history goes on, one birth a round; 17 and 21 are implied and close.

**The pattern of holders.** Of the starts that grow under R, 582 of 629 (O) and 592 of 646 (N) fail the operator test
(the pattern of which knot holds which distinction is not a planar graph, in the literature's words) at some stage,
nearly all by step 3. Of the 280 draws that grow without repair, 33 fail. With three holders the test is a reading of the pattern
only.

**The two stalling set-ups** of the whole-stage-settings study, with its edits as settings:

| Set-up | Ordinary growth | Rule R |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two-sided stall, 5 knots | Each side's outcome depends on its own edit and on the far edit | The round is always made; nothing depends on either edit |
| Parity closing, 3 knots | One side's outcome depends on the far edit | The same: no knot is short, so R plays it as ordinary growth |

**Pace.** On 200 funded draws with one loose distinction added, a changed birth is never more than 3 steps from the
edited knot under either rule. No start of this sample has a knot more than 5 steps away, so the test could not show
the difference it was built for. In the 39 pairs that stall under ordinary growth, the stall changes births at the
start's largest distance in 37.

**The thread's own check.** Round 1 of every one of the 4,096 histories was replayed with groups from the recorded
operator and payments by separate code: the same residual stalls (102 and 85), short knots, payments and births. A
separate enumeration of 6.2 million old words over 3,116 stored rounds, 652 of them with a payment, found none that
fails to extend. The two set-ups were replayed with the same separate code: ordinary growth stalls at three of the four
settings and R makes the round at all four; the closing has no short knot at any setting. The tables of the draws and
of the pace test were recounted from the per-history records.

## What this is about

About rule R as worded here, on these draws (labelled trees on four to eight knots with up to three extra meetings,
seed 2026112301) and these two set-ups. Supplied: the rule; the two conventions and the enumeration of choices; the
replaced closing test; the readings of the set-ups; the measure of pace. The engine's group search was rewritten for
speed and checked against the recorded one on every round small enough (41,967 rounds). The last round before the cap
has fresh counts that are lower bounds in 22 to 26 histories per law; nothing reported depends on them.

**Verdict.** The move can be a law: it keeps every allowed word, removes the whole-round stall and its joint response
to two distant edits, and makes third holders. As worded it is a repair of the start and no more, since ordinary growth
never leaves a later knot short. Whether a start repaired this way ever gains a hidden excess is the question of this
epic's third study.
