# Which parts of a split knot can power a signed measurement?

The two separated readers cannot use a single coherent parity knot to implement either nonzero branch of the required measurement on every input while each individual operation keeps its three physical places lawful. This remains true with known initial amplitudes, private coherent storage, noninvertible heralds, and arbitrary finite classical communication. The positive not-all-equal preparation does implement both branches on **one third** of launches under the same requirement. A joint offer that changes two readers' parity places atomically implements them on **every** coherent parity launch. The three conclusions use the same target, records and ordinary writers. ## 1. Source, target and access

The source places are written in the order $a,c,b$. Reader A holds $a,c$ and reader B holds $b$. Even parity permits 000, 011, 101, 110. Not-all-equal permits 001, 010, 011, 100, 101, 110. Both are nonempty, binding relations, and forgetting any one named distinction leaves all four pairs. The canonical check of the two relations returns K1, K2 and K3. For each relation $R$ compare $|S\rangle=\sum_{w\in R}|w\rangle/\sqrt{|R|}$ with $\sum_{w\in R}|w\rangle\langle w|/|R|$. Real amplitudes and their squared-norm readings are supplied in addition to ordinary lawful words. The [earlier split calculation](../../../research/READS-HOLDERS-SOURCES-20261120-001A/details.md) explicitly allowed a one-reader change to take a basis word outside its relation. The [earlier motion](../../../research/READS-HOLDERS-SOURCES-20261120-001A/motion.md) also kept coherent and record-dephased extensions distinct.

Let $J=XZ$, $M=-J\otimes J$ and $P_t=(I+tM)/2$ for $t=+1,-1$. An ideal two-reader instrument after a target-independent source herald of probability $p$ must give, per full launch,
$$\mathcal I_t(\rho)=p P_t\rho P_t.$$
A positive fine result announced as sign $t$ must likewise give a constant multiple of $P_t\rho P_t$. It must preserve relative signs between the two target states left open by the answer. The [earlier coherent meter](../../../research/TWO-READERS-ONE-SURVIVING-SIGN-20261121-001A/results.md) meets this condition when an independent coherent binary pair is *already* available. Here the source is the entire split knot.

One reader acts at a time with a finite real quantum instrument on its source, data, locally prepared work and keepers. The other reader's quantum systems stay untouched at that step. Readers may share source-independent classical randomness, send measured classical outcomes both ways, and make further operations depend on the replies. The data and optional local probes start independently of all apparatus. Every fine output and every actual intermediate word on a reached input must respect the original relation, including refused outcomes. A local elementary gate may use both of A's named places. The explicit selective construction below needs to change at most one named place at each step. Sources remain physically present, including after transfer to private work. Tracing an environment distinguishes a lost record from a coherent uncomputation. No additional shared coherent reference, shared quantum memory or quantum message is supplied. These are the terms of the [written freeze](../../../research/SPLIT-KNOT-METER-AND-CLOSING-TRIPLE-20261121-001A/preregistration.md).

For comparison, **full-relation legality** requires that, for each locally admitted source and workspace input at a gate, it work with *every* remotely compatible original source word, irrespective of previously acquired remote certificates. Requiring a reversible gate on **every ancillary input** further includes arbitrary private memory, as opposed to a specified blank and its reachable successors. At the other extreme **prepared-state legality** tests support only on the actual coherent state of every reached branch. These alternatives have distinct operational consequences.

## 2. The two parity sectors cannot be joined by an individual operation

Put $A_s=\operatorname{span}\{|a,c\rangle:a\oplus c=s\}$ and $B_s=\operatorname{span}\{|s\rangle\}$, leaving each reader's private systems implicit. The allowed source space is the orthogonal sum of $A_0\otimes B_0$ and $A_1\otimes B_1$. With the target in 00 the initial pure state is
$$\tfrac1{\sqrt2}\big(|\alpha_0\rangle_A|0\rangle_B+
                                   |\alpha_1\rangle_A|1\rangle_B\big)
  \otimes|0\rangle_{D_A}|0\rangle_{D_B},$$
where $\alpha_0=(00+11)/\sqrt2$ and $\alpha_1=(01+10)/\sqrt2$ in ket notation. Each summand alone is product across the readers.

**Branch lemma.** On any one fine transcript the evolved vector remains a sum of at most two product vectors, the first in the original 0 sector and the second in the original 1 sector. Suppose A acts. A possible forbidden output originating in sector $s$ has B's physical bit $s$. The other summand has B's bit $1-s$ and cannot cancel it. Thus if the complete branch remains lawful, the image of the first local factor of each surviving product vector stays in $A_s$. Products remain products. When B acts, the untouched $A_s$ spaces separate the two possible forbidden outputs. Conditioning can kill a summand, but cannot join the two. Induction allows every fine Kraus outcome, adaptive classical transcript, private isometry and retained workspace. If a coarse allowed branch is known to be supported on the law, its positive refinements are also supported there. Independent shared coins add only a classical mixture. This proof already uses the weakest prepared-state reading.

On tracing the persistent named source the cross term vanishes. Thus the returned data on every individual or pooled outcome, even after a local repair, is a mixture of products on input 00. In contrast,
$$P_t|00\rangle=(|00\rangle-t|11\rangle)/2.$$
Each sign has ideal probability $1/2$ and returns a maximally entangled unit vector after normalization. Its squared overlap with any separable target state is at most $1/2$, by Cauchy–Schwarz on product vectors and convexity. Thus no positive target-independent herald can realize the full maps. The same proof excludes a nonzero exact outcome of either sign by itself. A diagonal source is separable at launch. Even unrestricted one-reader operations and classical communication preserve separability on each of its transcripts.

**The answer alone.** Run any putative universal real sign reader on product target vectors. The same induction applies. By linearity the full product operator on a fixed refined transcript maps each original sector into that same sector on the whole target input space, since product vectors span it. After the named bits are traced, its target effect is consequently a sum of real positive product effects obtained by fixing the original sector. For every real symmetric $E$, $\operatorname{tr}(EJ)=0$. Hence every transcript, including a declared refusal, has identical probability on $P_+/2$ and $P_-/2$. Perfect sign reporting distinguishes these two states and is therefore impossible, even with an inconclusive option of positive conclusive probability. This claim about effects uses *real* local operations. The product-input obstruction to returning the Bell state requires only separation and the source law.

The operational loss is specific to the distinction between sectors. A can exchange 00 with 11 and 01 with 10 and read the sign of that involution locally. Its plus probability is 1 on the coherent source, 1 after dephasing only $b$, and $1/2$ after full dephasing. All these projectors preserve every reached word. The coherent source and the mixture obtained by dephasing only $b$ have identical accessible target maps under serial parity access, although within-sector coherence can still be read. On not-all-equal the local exchange of 01 and 10, fixing the equal rows, gives plus probabilities 1, 1 and $2/3$ under the same three preparations. ## 3. A single lawful herald makes the not-all-equal meter

A first measures whether its two source places are equal, storing only that one predicate. The equal outcome retains **both** 001 and 110 coherently. Store $a$ in a blank local bit. On this branch, change $c$ if the private bit is 0, then change $a$ if it is 1. Keep the original value in the private bit. Now both A words equal 01. B copies its value into private work, clears the physical $b$ using that copy, then complements its private bit to match A's. The herald may be sent to B before it touches any target or source. All original named words at the substantive steps are displayed below. Nonselecting copy steps leave the displayed word in place.

| Initial word | A changes $c$ if required | A changes $a$ if required | B clears its source | Returned private pair |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 001 | 011 | 011 | 010 | 00 |
| 110 | 110 | 010 | 010 | 11 |

Consequently the primary positive source produces $\Phi=(|00\rangle+|11\rangle)/\sqrt2$ privately with probability $2/6=1/3$. Both named word paths leave **the same** physical 010 and the same success record. Their private pair is coherent. On the other outcome, the original words are 010, 011, 100 and 101. With the positive source their normalized surviving state factors as $(|01\rangle+|10\rangle)(|0\rangle+|1\rangle)/2$. Its probability is $2/3$. The target is unchanged, both local keepers and the sign keeper are blank, and there are no analyzer flags. On the fully dephased preparation this same herald accepts with $1/3$ but delivers only an equal classical pair. Its failure retains the equal diagonal mixture of those four words. Keeping any record distinguishing 001 from 110, even unread, destroys the useful off-diagonal element on the extracted pair. A genuine coherent uncomputation of that extra copy restores it; labelling its information inaccessible and tracing it does not.

**Optimality.** The reduced B density before the read is $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}3&2\\2&3\end{smallmatrix}\right)/6$, with eigenvalues $5/6$ and $1/6$. For a pure bipartite branch let $e(\sigma)=\operatorname{tr}\sigma-\lambda_{\max}(\sigma)$ on either reduced, subnormalized density. Their nonzero spectra agree. After a local instrument, the sum of the *other* reader's fine reduced densities equals its old density. Subadditivity of $\lambda_{\max}$ gives $\sum_j e(\sigma_j)\le e(\sum_j\sigma_j)$. Therefore the average of this quantity cannot increase down any finite classical history. On input 00 every successful fine output of either exact signed map factors into the required pure Bell target and some private or source remainder. The normalized largest reduced eigenvalue is then at most $1/2$. Its contribution is at least half the branch weight. Hence $p/2\le1/6$, so $p\le1/3$. The construction attains the bound even under one-named-place-at-a-time changes. The bound also applies if legality is weakened to actual-state support, or completely removed. Single-copy local pure-state conversion is known. Here the additional question is which named-word moves attain its limit while the knot persists. [Vidal (1999)](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9902033)

The same $1/3$ bounds a *destructive unambiguous real sign read*, interpreted as average conclusive probability on equiprobable $P_+/2$ and $P_-/2$. A conclusive effect $E_t$ has support in $P_t$ because its probability on the opposite positive sign mixture is zero. Feed the two target bits as halves of two **independent local** maximally entangled probes, retaining their other halves as spectators. On outcome $t$ their joined subnormalized state is $E_t^T/4$. Refine all remaining local registers in real product bases. Every positive conditional spectator vector lies in the same $P_t$ space, since a positive sum cannot cancel an out-of-space probability. Every real unit vector there is maximally entangled: its two-by-two coefficient matrix has equal orthogonal rows, directly from the action of $-J\otimes J$. Thus every conclusive branch contributes at least half its weight to the same bound. The conclusive probability on this probe is $\operatorname{tr}(E_++E_-)/4$, exactly the equal-prior average. The meter attains it while preserving both target states. For a different decision, **require a guess on every launch** of either equally likely sign mixture. On the positive not-all-equal resource the best success probability is $1/2+\sqrt5/6$, about 87.27%. Here is both the upper bound and its realization. Let $\Delta=P_+/2-P_-/2=M/2$, and write the pure source $\sigma$ in its two real Schmidt coordinates. A real product effect, including the sum for any communicated transcript, is invariant under partial transposition on A’s entire private input. Since $\Delta^{T_A}=-\Delta$, its probability difference on the two sign hypotheses can be computed using $D=(\sigma-\sigma^{T_A})/2\otimes\Delta$. If $E$ is the effect for guessing plus, then the success probability is $1/2+\operatorname{tr}[(2E-I)D]/4$, at most $1/2+\|D\|_1/4$. For Schmidt squares $\lambda,\mu$, the four absolute eigenvalues of $\sigma-\sigma^{T_A}$ on the private two-by-two code all equal $\sqrt{\lambda\mu}$, while $\|\Delta\|_1=2$. This gives $1/2+\sqrt{\lambda\mu}$. After the deterministic lawful extraction of section 4, each reader can use its local real Schmidt coordinates and measure $J_{\mathrm{source}}\otimes J_{\mathrm{data}}$. These are real symmetric involutions. The negative product of their two outcomes has mean $2t\sqrt{\lambda\mu}$ given true sign $t$, attaining the bound for each hypothesis. A guess may disturb the data. Exact return, error-free reporting with refusals, and obligatory guessing are three distinct decisions. The coherent parity and both fully dephased cases give $1/2$ for obligatory guessing under serial access. ## 4. Why the different access premises matter

For an A word $x$ let $F_x$ be its compatible B values. A local transition $x\to y$ that preserves the whole original relation on a given ready local input requires $F_x\subseteq F_y$. If this must hold for a finite orthogonal gate and its inverse on all ancillary inputs, each subspace compatible with a particular B value is invariant both ways. Its orthogonal projector therefore commutes with the gate. Coupled A words then have identical $F$. The same arguments hold at B. Literal enumeration checks all 24 A permutations and both B permutations. [Compatibility and reversible controls](../../../research/SPLIT-KNOT-METER-AND-CLOSING-TRIPLE-20261121-001A/access_checks.json)

| Relation | $F_{00},F_{01},F_{10},F_{11}$ | Directed whole-relation A changes including identity | Whole-relation A-only / B-only / simultaneous product permutations |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Even parity | {0}, {1}, {1}, {0} | 8 | 4 / 1 / 8 |
| Not-all-equal | {1}, {0,1}, {0,1}, {0} | 10 | 2 / 1 / 4 |

For not-all-equal the sets of A words compatible with B=0 and B=1 are incomparable. The former contains 11 and excludes 00, the latter contains 00 and excludes 11. Full-relation legality therefore makes **every** local B Kraus operator preserve the original $b$ on its ready columns. This includes noninvertible heralds. Each initial $b$ summand is product and B continues to distinguish the two, so tracing its original bit again gives only a separable target on 00. The effective target effects are real product sums as in section 2, and the two opposite-sign mixtures give identical transcript probabilities. A private register or classical success flag whose value certifies remote restriction to 01 is *precisely* why a reached-word protocol need not obey that obstruction. In section 3 the B reset works on every word that can occur at the time of its offer. For example the same reset with A=00 and $b=1$ would produce the forbidden 000. That input cannot occur on the reset branch.

The herald measures equality without revealing a value. It can be realized with a reversible local computation into a blank flag followed by its ordinary read. Each subsequent controlled flip in section 3 is also a reversible local gate on its own reached subspace. Reversibility on a specified ready register does not require its inverse to preserve the entire original relation for arbitrary register contents. The failure alternative must still be counted. A fully deterministic alternative before heralding is to swap the entire A word into private work initialized to 01, leaving 01 physically, and then swap B into a private zero, leaving physical 010. Every original allowed word survives each discrete local offer. On these six not-all-equal words even the full transfer has a one-named-bit implementation. First copy both local inputs. A moves 00 and 11 separately to 01, as above, leaving the mixed rows alone. Both remaining physical A rows are unequal, so B can clear $b$ to zero. A now moves its residual 10 through 11 to 01 using its original private row as control. The intermediate 110 is allowed because B has already cleared. Every physical endpoint is 010 and the three untouched private copies retain the whole input, including its coherence. [All six histories](../../../research/SPLIT-KNOT-METER-AND-CLOSING-TRIPLE-20261121-001A/access_checks.json) No measurement is needed to move the whole source into the laboratories. A purely private filter can then attain the same $1/3$; the exact replay includes its complementary outcomes. Swapping an arbitrary A private value into the physical carrier would fail the global arbitrary-input test. A deterministic pair of local unitaries cannot convert the single not-all-equal pure preparation to a Bell pair with certainty because it has unequal Schmidt coefficients.

For the whole-source route write the saved A rows in order 00,01,10,11. In the archived filter implementation A clears first and filters its retained row. B transfers only on acceptance. This postponement changes the refusal record: there the named A row is already 01, the old named B bit remains, and neither target is coupled. The unconditional two-reader transfer described above instead fixes the physical source on every launch. The accepted private A map is $K=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}-2&1&1&3\\3&1&1&-2\end{smallmatrix}\right)/5$. Its product with the unnormalized two-column source indicator is $I_2$. The three separate refusal rows are $\sqrt2(1,2,2,1)/5$, $(0,1,-1,0)/\sqrt2$, and $(2,-1,-1,2)/\sqrt{10}$. Their squared effects and $K^T K$ sum to $I_4$. If only a common refusal is read, its three contributions are added incoherently. On the coherent source the refusal weights are $2/3,0,0$ and the successful weight is $1/3$. Dephasing only between B values has the same four weights and loses the useful Bell coherence. On the fully diagonal source, or on tracing a retained tag of the full A row, they become $6/25,1/3,1/5$ and $17/75$ respectively. These probabilities all refer to the full launch, and each refusal leaves the target untouched. With the equal-row protocol, the distinct $1/3$ herald rate on the diagonal preparation still applies. More generally, with an arbitrary finite A alphabet and a single B bit, one A word compatible with both B values suffices to transfer the entire source into private blanks by these same two serial swaps. When no such A word exists, every A word fixes $b$ and the branch lemma applies to any pure source supported there. This gives the structural distinction needed here. For the present uniform preparations the parity side has no such word, while not-all-equal has two.

Prepared-state legality can admit genuinely additional gates. In A-word order 00,01,10,11 the reflection normal to $(1,1,-1,-1)$ fixes the positive parity source, and the reflection normal to $(1,-1,0,1)$ fixes the positive not-all-equal source. In each case its normal is orthogonal to *both* conditional A vectors. Nevertheless the reflection gives at least one allowed basis input a nonzero forbidden component. ## 5. The synchronized parity comparison

A copies $p=a\oplus c$ into a private blank and B copies $b$ into its own. The source is unchanged. The additional atomic offer flips $a$ controlled by A's copy and simultaneously flips $b$ controlled by B's. On even parity $p=b$, so the physical output is $(c,c,0)$ and the private pair is $(p,p)$. The uniform coherent source factors into $(|000\rangle+|110\rangle)/\sqrt2$ on the carrier and $\Phi$ on the private pair. On a fully dephased source it yields a diagonal mixture on the same four joint ordinary possibilities. After the copies the simultaneous two controlled flips form an involution, preserving the whole physical parity relation whenever the two *private* controls agree. Independently chosen unequal controls invert parity. A fully reversible gate on arbitrary unrelated private values would be a stronger requirement.

On 011 the A-only half produces 111 and the B-only half produces 010. On 101 the corresponding halves produce 001 or 100. These are the two branches of total squared mass $1/2$ that forbid either serialization. The two all-ready conditions may be compared before launch. A missing offer then leaves the source and target unchanged. Unexpectedly firing one half is an *illegal* implementation, not a lawful heralded refusal. An ordinary, pre-agreed paired permutation flipping $a$ and $b$ does preserve parity and merely permutes the uniform source words. It deposits no meter. On not-all-equal the proposed atomic clear reaches forbidden 000 on the three allowed words with $c=0$, so it fails that law. Simultaneously complementing all three bits instead permutes the six words but also deposits nothing.

This discrete atomic offer is stronger than continuous product unitary drives lawful throughout. For a single ordinary basis input with private blanks, a product vector in the matched parity subspace has support in only one sector. Along a continuous product-unitary path it cannot change that discrete sector. Even for the coherent preparation alone and product targets, local unitary drives conserve the two reduced nonzero eigenvalues $1/2,1/2$. When the complete source is lawful, the reduced B density commutes with physical $b$, so each sector weight is an integer multiple of $1/2$. A continuous change from its initial sector weight $1/2$ to zero is impossible. This argument concerns continuous unitary realizations of the displayed transfer. ## 6. The ordinary records and surviving signed states

On the private $\Phi$ use the archive's real controlled $J$ at A, controlled $-J$ at B, and two binary $X$ analyzers with flags $u,v$. Put $t=(-1)^{u+v}$. Contracting the two meter terms gives the fine target operator $(I+tM)/(2\sqrt2)=P_t/\sqrt2$. Thus each recorded pair gives $pP_t\rho P_t/2$, and the two pairs for its sign add to $pP_t\rho P_t$. These equations hold with any untouched spectators. They imply sign repeat with certainty and preservation of *every* within-sign superposition when the repeat uses a new admitted coherent meter. On 00 each pair has conditional probability $1/4$, each sign $1/2$. For a signed eigenstate each of the two compatible flags has probability $1/2$, the other two zero. For a fresh not-all-equal attempt one must multiply by $1/3$ at each admission; the chance of two source successes is $1/9$.

With the diagonal equal pair the fine map is instead $p(\rho+M\rho M)/8$ and every flag pair has probability $p/4$. The two supposed sign channels coincide. On 00 the conditional target is the diagonal mixture of 00 and 11, with squared overlap $1/2$ with either required return and no sign discrimination. This also describes parity after the serial copies if the physical sector record is retained or traced. A wrong relative sign of the private source interchanges the two sign labels; a local $Z$ before the read corrects a known phase. A common random phase that has no accessible record gives the diagonal meter.

A complete local binary flag writes 101 or 011, each individually fair on any target after successful coupling. The third ordinary keeper writes 101 for equal flags and 011 for different flags, only if both classical bits arrive. Missing information leaves its blank 110. A source refusal before data coupling has no analyzer flags. A missing writer after a successful read leaves the corresponding physical measurement on the target. These possibilities cannot be distinguished by the third blank alone. Both ordinary laws support the actual controller and keeper transitions. The assigned real data observable originates in the earlier parity closure. The not-all-equal source comparison here supplies no independent closing sign.
