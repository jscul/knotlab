# A local record of which three-exit bites paid

This specification fixes the controller before opening the new games. The action is always the value of physical distinction 6 of the original action knot. Ordinary growth, the supply of food and the assignment of displayed controls belong to the game. The controller sees the public feeder marker, energy, previous action and anonymous departure controls. After issuing a bit it receives the actual outward control used, if any, and the new public energy. It never receives a holder name, version, opening, stock, score, movement draw or evaluation outcome.

## Offers and records

1. Preserve the first receipt word `m` and the original physical action and output registers. The current-only rule attempts a bite on a feeder when the previous action was anything other than eat. An eligible assessment is such a decision with exactly three current exits and a recorded A or B arrival. Every actual outward move refreshes the arrival register. If the displayed departure control `i` has its partner `i ^ 1` in **that departure menu**, its parity supplies A (even) or B (odd). An unpaired actual outward move clears the register. Stays preserve it. It is initially unset. The prediction and its credit refer to the arrival before the action. Input ports and preparation, a program of reversible offers, and output-token exchange are supplied operations. Identical anonymous observations produce the same available offers.
2. Separately store the signed difference between credited eligible A bites and credited eligible B bites in 32 physical two-mark registers, least significant pair first. `10` means zero and `01` means one within each pair. Interpret the whole word in two's complement. Address the score with the physically issued bite mark, the current-eligible receipt, a training write-enable, the pre-action A/B mark, and a successful comparison-energy receipt. A success is `after >= before` on an issued bite, including payment clipped to ten. An ordinary unsuccessful turn costs one energy. Real transitions always load the true energy and update the first receipt. The outcome learner also loads that energy into the credit port. Encounter-count and re-pairing controls substitute respectively a paid receipt on every eligible attempted bite or the registered re-paired paid/unpaid receipt on the *same* port. A supplied paid receipt uses `min(10,before+3)` and an unpaid one uses `before-1`. No arm has a different address, addition or action reader. A miss earns no increment. No training result is selected or compared externally to set the score word.
3. Add or subtract one by offering bit-pair exchanges from high to low. The unchanged lower pairs must all encode one for increment and zero for decrement. These are permutations modulo 2^32. Start from zero and allow at most 2,147,483,647 eligible credits in any fitted history. This makes signed wrap impossible for all orders of the prescribed bounded exposure and requires no clipping. The calibrated unit tests also probe the formal modular boundaries. A disclosed score and its sign are readings of the final marks only.
4. During training the fixed probe emits the current-only bit independently of the score. During evaluation the same action starts from current-only. On an eligible decision, a positive learned word permits A and declines B, a negative word permits B and declines A, and zero retains both attempts. The highest set bit below the sign and the sign mark distinguish positive, negative and zero by disjoint unchanged-mark guards. The controller does not consult the decoded integer when choosing. Every read writes the old blank action word `100` to pass `010` or bite `001`. An expressly declared prefix can force either bit through the same actual action registers. Clamp use is diagnostic and is identified in its receipt; a deployed decision has no clamp. Evaluation disables the learned write and leaves the first local receipt active.
5. Enlarge the original common controller H. Keep its original first-memory and action knots M and K. Add separate holdings R for the three arrival marks and C for all 64 score marks. Each of the four attached knots has one private slack distinction, and H retains its private slack. Every additional public/scratch bank belongs only to H. Marks in each score pair share exactly H and C. The three route alternatives share H and R. Both old action operands share H and K. All regular offers use unchanged controls in H and swap co-held operands with identical holding incidence. The two knot laws are the full even-parity or full not-all-equal relations on each holding. One private slack per holding extends any allowed word of any single holding to the rest. Check K1–K3 on full projected relations and incidence bounds. The dual-mark, one-hot and ready words restrict the preparation. They do not define a new knot law. The two-law check covers all offered transformations and all logged intermediate words.

## Prepared interventions and interface

`PayoffReader(law)` accepts `nae` or `parity`. `begin_episode(training=False)` starts with the arrival unset, blank action, first receipt ready and fresh comparison work, keeping the learned bank. `checkpoint(obs)` prepares the public ports and blank physical action. `read_prepared(probe_bit=None)` returns its actual bit. `decide(obs,probe_bit=None)` combines these calls. `after_turn(obs,bit,outgoing_slot,energy_after,credit_override=None)` verifies the issued mark and authentic public transition. `credit_override` is 0/1 for the count and re-pairing controls and otherwise absent. Reports include `eligible`, `route_cue`, `actual_paid`, `credit_paid`, `score_before`, `score_after` and `issued_bit`. Detailed mode includes whole before/after words and literal offers. The ledger is for observation, with no route back to the action. A public transition that contradicts the issued bit, paid energy law or move is rejected explicitly.

`export_memory()` supplies a checked JSON object with `schema=feeder-payoff-difference-v1`, `bits=32`, a 64-character physical `word`, and its signed `score`. `from_memory(law,export)` prepares a mounted reader. `calibrated(law,'A'|'B'|None)` prepares +1, −1 or zero for fixed controls. `swap_learned(other)`, `clear_learned()`, `restore_learned(export)` and `sham()` at a prepared checkpoint change only the score marks. Reciprocal transfer is a supplied cross-run intervention using actual co-held swaps on each board. A clear exchanges with a prepared neutral word and returns the outgoing word to permit exact restoration; sham exchanges one pair twice. Transfer or restore cannot encode the donor's version, route, memory `m`, control mode, settings or history in other registers. Compare whole states with `.word`, `.snapshot()` and `.nonmemory_snapshot()`. Diagnostic donor exports are separate from the controller's public sensory access.

Before accepting the device, check both laws, both learned signs, zero and leading-bit carries, matched and unpaired moves, stays, all public eligibility gates, the full signed action mark, paid and unpaid public bites including cap, and rejection of inconsistent input. Reproduce exact paired transfer, sham, clear, inverse restoration, first receipt, physical mark support, conservation and neutral semantics. Check that actual credit and encounter/re-paired receipts enter the same circuit while the true first receipt remains the same. Independent primary training, held-out decisions, causal transfers and survival scores are under their own registered protocols. No native derivation of the supplied read/write schedule is assumed.
