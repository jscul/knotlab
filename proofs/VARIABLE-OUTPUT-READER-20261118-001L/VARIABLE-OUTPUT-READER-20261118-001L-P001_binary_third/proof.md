# A legal triple determines every junction after the binary three-output addition

**Theorem.** Start with one three-place even-parity junction. The first two additions have one parent and introduce three fresh distinctions. Exactly one more addition introduces three and it has **two parents**. Every other addition takes two or three parents and introduces two. At any finite stage in this family, any bijection to any reachable mixed-output rival that preserves the whole bond-distance table through four preserves the truth of every marked three-parent join. If a triple may join, the same bijection also carries the complete set of actual junction supports onto the rival's. Thus a hidden physical junction and a live triple cannot coexist under an exact table match with a source in this family. Four is the sharp uniform cutoff for deciding joins. Before the special addition the prefix result of 001J applies. A marked choice uses three loose distinctions with distinct ordinary holders. Each pair of holders must have an ordinary common neighbor, and none may be common to all three. \(K(x,y)=D(x,y)+1\) is the least size of a minimal nonempty parity equation containing that pair. Each ordinary junction gives a parity equation, and there are also derived equations on other supports. A physical junction here means an actual junction of the presentation. Comparing actual supports under the *given* bijection matters even when a different bijection identifies the two complete presentations. Every entry with \(D>4\) is assigned one common value. The law, this current table and an unordered mark suffice. No count of shrinks, history, orientation, junction, or privileged labeling is supplied to the reader. The comparison allows any reachable rival under the full serial law. ## Proof

Write \(B\) for the designated binary addition with three outputs. A shrinking join takes three parents and makes two outputs. After \(B\), a source with \(t\) shrinking joins has \(8-t\) loose distinctions. The exact frontier of the loose holders keeps their counts, their meetings, and the subsets with a retired ordinary common neighbor. An exhausted holder can gain no new neighbor, so this record preserves every available move under relabeling. Closing all transitions gives 28,108 records and 181,530 transitions. No history has more than four shrinking joins. Only one five-loose record allows a triple, and it has no binary predecessor at five loose distinctions. A four-loose record admits only a binary addition, returning to that record. These facts apply to arbitrary history lengths. **Up to three shrinking joins, distances through four force the whole parity-equation space against every reachable rival.** In the wired graph for the parity law, wire loose distinctions to the exterior. Each subset of ordinary sites gives the cut around it, which is the sum of its site equations. With at least five loose distinctions, every four-word comes from a physical four-place site. Any five-boundary enclosing at least three sites can be built as an initial part of the history. The last three sites already export four bits. The balance of inputs and outputs excludes an entering bit at such a boundary. With three shrinks the large five-boundaries are exactly the minimum boundaries containing the starting three sites. Their ordinary sides are closed under unions and intersections.

A bit of a five-place junction that occurs on no such large boundary can force its junction from its short pair readings and an already forced junction at its other end. When the binary three-output junction and the final shrinking junction depend on each other in this argument, their common bit has eight partners at \(K=5\). The five loose distinctions on the earlier prefix satisfy a known equation. Adding it excludes the false support that would put all four incoming distinctions together with the common bit, since the sum would have size two. The short pairs then force the two genuine supports. This covers empty as well as positive menus through the third shrink. The width identity bounds the rival's rank by the source's, so inclusion of all site words gives equality of spaces. The separate structural proof gives every case and verifies the protection conditions. [Short shores and recovery](structural.md)

**A common parity space fixes the actual supports as soon as one triple may join.** The ordinary graph begins with a four-cycle, and every later site joins at least two different older ones. With the exterior included the source representation stays connected after any two vertices are removed. Its augmented vertex stars can be read from the common equation and state spaces by finding the bonds whose deletion leaves the surviving distinctions linked by surviving cycles. The two presentations therefore have the same full collection of augmented stars on the supplied names. There remains a choice of which of those stars is the exterior. If the two exteriors differ, every distinction loose in both lies between those same two vertices. All such distinctions have just one ordinary holder on either side. But a triple that is legal in one must be legal in the other by the equal-state theorem, and it needs three different holders. A single eligible triple therefore identifies the exterior and hence every actual junction. **At four shrinking joins, every reachable rival has an empty triple menu.** Every large four-boundary is a parent-closed prefix containing \(B\) and all four shrinking sites. The only large five-boundary is the five loose distinctions immediately before the fourth shrink at the least such four-boundary. Otherwise reordering would give a binary predecessor of the sole positive five-loose record or a four-shrink history in which \(B\) follows an earlier shrink. Every physical junction before the fourth shrink and the fourth shrinking equation itself survive in any matching rival. If binary joins follow, each contributes a short-pair obligation on new distinctions. The first such join makes six bits pairwise at \(K=4\). The obligations force the rival's full rank and limit it to three three-output additions. A rival with a legal triple would need at least five loose distinctions and could have no large four-boundary. Six bits pairwise at \(K=4\) then cannot occur. This proves permission invariance even through arbitrarily many subsequent binary joins. [Four-loose proof](four_loose.md) · [Literal checks](tail.json)

Taken together, these cases give a table-only reader. One general finite procedure enumerates all reachable mixed presentations at the supplied width, matches their clipped tables under all possible bijections and tests the transported triple. Each addition increases width by at least two, so the search is finite. The theorem proves every surviving match gives the same answer whenever the source is in the stated class. It also proves that the actual support sets agree in every match whenever the answer is positive for even one triple.
