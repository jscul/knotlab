## Exact costs and replay

The two ring states differ on ten original shared distinctions, five on each ring. Every ordinary event contributes one pair of consecutively co-knotted original distinctions. The ordinary events used an odd number of times therefore give a pairing by trails of those ten distinctions. At least one trail crosses between the two rings, at cost at least \(d+1\) in co-knotted-pair steps when their nearest original holders are separated by \(d\) knot steps. The other four trails each cost at least one. Thus at least \(d+5\) ordinary events are needed. They toggle two different private inputs per odd-used response. An exterior event can clear at most two such inputs and a loose two-event clears none. This gives the lower bound \(2d+10\), with the identical pulse cancelling from the endpoint difference. The literal witness independently uses the path pairing and clears each old input pair, attaining 18, 26 and 50, respectively. All intermediate assignments were tested against the literal parity equations.
