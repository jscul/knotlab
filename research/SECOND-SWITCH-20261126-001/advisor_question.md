# The question to the advisor (26 November 2026), before the second-switch study was run

A short colleague's check before I freeze predictions; be skeptical. Same growth system as before (rounds; members are
knots with usable loose distinctions; groups from met sets; short when owe > h; a short member waits; a member that
is not short spends everything and leaves; new knots get f = min(max(D, 3 - p, 1), k) fresh distinctions under a cap
of k). Two starts joined by a meeting through d - 1 link knots.

Known: under a cap of k, a receiver R (three knots each sharing with the other two, site with exactly k places, the
others holding two loose distinctions) grows "the slow way" until round 2d + 1, then switches to fast growth iff the
far end's site holds two loose distinctions; with a far site of three, or a stub, it never switches (through round
22). One more loose distinction at the far SITE acting in round <= d cancels or makes the switch; acting in round
d + 1 or later is never heard (through round 16). The far site itself acts in round 1 and leaves; afterwards the
far end's own members are its descendants, 7 to 14 sharings from R's own knots by round 6 or 7 (the structure between
grows, so the way lengthens).

Question studied next: after both exist, can the far pattern change R's course? Design: R for k = 3..7, d = 3; far
ends a two-knot start (switches R at 7), a one-knot start with three loose and a stub (never); after round t0 = 3..10,
one more loose distinction (and, separately, one barred) at each of the five members of the far end's own knots
nearest R in sharings; grow to round 24 or 6,000 knots; read the first round after t0 that R's own births differ from
the growth without that change, the change's sharing distance at t0, and whether R was in the slow or fast way then
and after.

Predictions I am considering: (1) some such change is heard by R within 24 rounds, at a round at least t0 + the
sharing distance; (2) where R was still slow, a change heard switches it; where R had switched, a change heard never
switches it back; (3) under the ordinary count nothing is heard. Questions: which do you expect to fail and why; is
24 rounds enough given a sharing distance of 7 to 14 and the 2d + 1 timing (two rounds a step?); what control would
show that a heard change came along the line from the far pattern rather than from the knots in between; anything
missing? Under 400 words.
