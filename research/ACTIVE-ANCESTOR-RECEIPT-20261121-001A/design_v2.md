# What each reader can recover and how every minimum is checked

This binding amendment accompanies the [original prospective protocol](design_draft.md), SHA-256 `3e0ba293d0da9d3ee22e5ea90b61b7e9b6d3776cd4f2d5ac9a4b3c26274182c8`. It retains the same twelve physical starts, four sends, two state laws, source and receiver, payment convention and main stop. The independent review identified two physically different objects that can be called anonymous, and a need to resolve all later third-qualified intakes rather than only the very first unqualified ones. No target outcomes precede this amendment.

1. **Two objects, two tests.** The full physical object consists of the actual holders, complete allowed-state law, shared and loose names with their incidence, and the physically recoverable clock frames and ports. A coherent relabeling transports every one of these, including all multiplicities, without changing any physical read. In each arm rerun anonymous reconstruction on a global recoding; simply reordering the c/u role names in a sidecar while retaining the holdings must give identical output up to that same site correspondence. The second, deliberately weaker object is the donor/block map with candidate groups, true first-cut participant sets and individual loose supplies, retaining frame and port designations and forgetting the multiplicities that distinguish 3/4 from 5/4 at the circuit witnesses. When no z is present, attempt an explicit map of the two donor circuits, block incidences, loose supplies and frames on this **second** object. With z, enumerate the component-size pairs after deleting every single ordinary candidate connection and exhibit a distinguishing invariant on this same weaker object if one exists. A difference only in the full physical object gets that limited claim. Full objects differing in the duration of an invariantly identified family cannot be physically isomorphic, even in the z-free pair.
2. **One physical test everywhere.** A c donor is a live original with three names in a seven-place spent witness meeting exactly two live donors. A directed c step passes from its three-name tail to the four-name head of such a witness, both endpoints satisfying that donor test. A third member consists of consecutive such directed steps in either reading orientation; no reversal, shortcuts through an ordinary bridge, repeated step, unsupported extras or externally supplied list of c labels. Passage around a cycle can continue into further copies under ordinary growth. In a traversed true ternary the sole additional private-to-that-block donor is allowed by the standard spine reading; also score the stronger version excluding every such extra. Verify the candidate test on *all* original donors/witnesses, including every port, both clock frames, other returns and ternaries. For every finite arm prove and exhibit actual earlier members of this same family and the exact final possible size anywhere. For the comparison that drops only the c/u discrimination, permit the same directed step through either a seven-place witness with three names at its tail or a nine-place one with five, retaining the four-name head, either tail-donor kind at both ends, actual growth, directions and all other criteria. For each first ordinary receiving input count current such ancestors which admit arbitrarily long continuation, including all mixed-current ancestors in a switched arm. Determining continuation for an entire family uses the original and the all-age theorem; the receiving member's own ancestral read does not by itself prove whether a family lasts elsewhere.
3. **Counting crossed preparations.** At each same send keep every earliest physical member, up to reversal and the existing common-cover equivalence, both sets of endpoint grants, and multiplicity. A strict full price match requires a common seven-field vector for every member in both arms; disclose any distinct tied vector, its physical member and either differing multiplicity. Report strict widths separately even if prices match. Keep each member's chronological true-parent ancestry and the ancestor multiplicity of the selected and c/u-forgetting readings. The asymmetry is in existence of a *current marked ancestor of the same actual send receipt*. Holding m and usable ℓ fixed already fixes N and the receiver's intrinsic count when the inherited entry hypotheses apply. A new discrepancy must identify which actual hypothesis fails.
4. **Stronger receipt predicate and exhaustive stop.** Give the unchanged strict decoder both actual marked parents; it reconstructs its own origin, synchronized launch and signal. Give the stronger reader the same recipient's complete finite recursive marked parent history, including the physical original distinctions. At a prospective ordinary receipt it may infer m from the whole W it recognizes, select its actual incoming parent and ask whether a rank-m member of the fixed c family occurs in it. No surviving/global-future test belongs to this decoder. Each physically admissible route resolving the old parents is evaluated first. Seek the first strengthened receipt across the continuing family *even when the first ordinary strict receipt fails it*. Exhaust all nonretracing right intakes from Rt,Rb₂,a, both actual orientations of W, any lengthened middle, returns past a or b and S detours that have not already completed W. In the SS case traverse consecutive real blocks and forbid a false detour back through the same block. A finite-state breadth-first search can store previous/current donor (or original block), length of consecutive acceptable circuit steps capped at m, a flag that a full rank-m third was already attained, and two prefix-match states for W and its reversal. End a history at the first complete W in that right word. A W completed too early for the added condition rules out later *new* registration of that W in this word. Distinct predecessors at the same minimum yield all qualifying histories. Prove the search and its physical-parent equivalence, or discharge cases by rigorous structural exhaustion. No arbitrary time horizon is an absence certificate. Distinguish retrospective evidence for that member, its observable clock read, and the external theorem that it is earliest across all possible members. Report the counts of the stronger reader separately from ordinary ones, with no duration for nonreceipt. The all-binary and no-extra-original rules are separate named sensitivities, each tested on ordinary minima and their sending-cut ancestry; the complete third-qualified alternate-route minimum is required for the default physically valid block reader and its registered c definition.
