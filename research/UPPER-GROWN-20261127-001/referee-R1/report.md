# Independent referee report on where agreements bind again
**Refuted** — one **Block** on the claimed need for a closed part. One ordinary growth round takes a connected ten-knot start from zero boxes to **18**. All 18 use six disjoint three-place agreement carriers and need both birth laws. The frozen box counts hold, including **49 of 85** closed twelve-knot systems and **zero boxes in all 2,416 sampled stages**. A different upper reading also gives a birth-essential knot *inside* that sample.

A frozen box has six agreements arranged in three pairs. Every choice of one reading per pair excludes a single lower triple, with the excluded value of each reading fixed across its four appearances.

### The strict counterexample

Split each of the 18 shared distinctions in each of the 49 positive closed systems, one at a time. **One of the 882 choices** admits a box after the split, with **18** boxes on that choice. All 371 choices up to relabelling were searched. The separate follow-up's 49 seeded splits all miss the positive choice and their saved counts reproduce. Simply transporting an old carrier to either copy loses the exclusion. On the successful split the new carriers read **both** loose distinctions. [All splits](open_full.json) · [Seeded comparison](open_followup.json)

The resulting open system is exactly one ordinary round from the ten-knot start in the certificate. Its four loose distinctions fund two births, on `(0,1,17)` and `(2,3,18)`. For one box, the two A carriers are `(0,9,17)` and `(2,3,16)`, the B carriers are `(1,5,18)` and `(7,10,13)`, and the C carriers are `(4,6,8)` and `(11,12,14)`. Each of the eight lower triples realizes exactly the seven words other than 111. Each reading varies at a fixed value of every individual source knot. Each of the eight lower clauses has a unique minimal set of sufficient source laws, and each such set omits at least one carrier member. Requiring the holders to contain the carriers misses all eight, even with the size cap lifted. The 14,554 allowed states give **34** six-reading words and **seven** upper words. Removing the first or second birth separately restores **90** or **50** assignments with upper 111. All 18 boxes need every one of the twelve laws. Both symmetries of the start extend over the births. The 18 boxes form nine exchanged pairs. A choice invariant under these renamings cannot single out one box. The ten-knot start was constructed outside the sample, whose starts have three to five knots. ### What the box form misses

- **On a closed ten-knot system and on a sampled grown stage**, four lower clauses suffice for another upper knot. In order, the forbidden all-one triples are `A0 B0 C0`, `A0 B1 C1`, `A1 B0 C1` and `A1 B1 C0`. Read **both** A agreements, **either** B agreement and **either** C agreement. If both A readings are 1, every choice of a 1 from B and a 1 from C violates one of the four clauses. All seven other upper words occur. Each clause is necessary in the four-clause conjunction, no single lower clause determines an upper reading, and all six lower carriers are disjoint pairs or triples. These upper readings distinguish agreement from disagreement and change the frozen upper comparison. [Closed ten-knot witness](upper_four_closed.json)
- The sampled example is the second round of **a four-knot start with three three-place knots and one four-place knot**, census entry 420. Its ten laws admit **17,928** states. The six readings realize **39** lower words, against **41** allowed by the four clauses. All **six** birth laws are individually essential to upper 111. This was the first stage tried when fully searched grown stages were ordered by decreasing archived bond count. It establishes existence. The search stopped on success. [Sampled witness](upper_sample.json) · [Independent enumeration and canonical checks](record_checks.json)
- **With four-place source knots**, a closed twelve-knot system with ten three-place and two four-place laws still holds a strict box. Each distinction is held twice. Deleting the two wider laws separately restores **228** and **28** upper witnesses. A separate system of twelve four-place knots also works, with six distinctions held twice and twelve held three times. Connected closed systems with repeated meetings also admit boxes. The 15 and 72 non-simple loopless three-place classes at eight and ten knots gave zero. So did all 19, 50 and 204 uniformly four-place, twice-held classes at six, seven and eight knots, and the 1,204 specified ten-knot systems with two four-place laws. Changing the law gives a distinct upper step on ten uniformly four-place even-parity knots. [Arity counts, constructions and boundaries](arity_notes.md)
- **With paired upper agreements**, the earlier eight-clause bound holds for single forbidden words on transversal triples. A fixed upper word has eight orientations, and one such clause covers one orientation. Fixed signs per reading are a further restriction. Among the 8! assignments of orientations tested, **1,208** non-product choices give single-word lower clauses and a proper pairwise-free upper relation. These are abstract lower relations. Their realization by ordinary source laws remains open. A separate ordinary grown construction realizes all **37** lower words if two carriers may have four places. ## The fixes

1. **Block — closedness and ordinary growth.** Replace “Boxes need closedness”, “Growth has not been seen to make one” and “Needs a closed part of at least twelve knots inside a grown stage” in [the decision](../decision.md) with “Ordinary growth makes 18 boxes in one round on a chosen ten-knot start. Both births and all ten starting laws are needed for each upper exclusion. The 1,000 sampled starts produce none through round two.” The private-distinction argument holds only for a loose distinction **unread** by the carriers. A latest birth in any finite part containing births has a fresh distinction with no second holder in that part. Looking only for closed parts therefore discards every birth-containing witness. In the [goals checkpoint](../../../docs/RESEARCH_GOALS.md), replace “since growth never changes what earlier distinctions allow” with the projection argument and the counterexample. Carry the same correction to “apparently out of reach” in the [one-split follow-up](../../CUT-BOXES-20261127-001/decision.md) and “Boxes need a closed system of at least twelve three-place knots” in the [rival-growth follow-up](../../CLOSED-BY-CUTS-20261127-001/decision.md) and their goals rows. The [guide's iteration passage](../../../publication/knotlab/INTUITIONS.md) can now distinguish formation on a chosen start from the still-open selection of readings.
2. **Narrowing — twelve knots and the class weighting.** Replace “It is common among closed three-place systems once they have twelve knots, and absent below that” in [the decision](../decision.md) with “The frozen box occurs on 49 of the 85 simple connected closed twelve-knot classes and none of the 27 smaller classes through ten, weighting each class equally.” Also qualify “No closed system with 10 or fewer knots has a box” in [the hand-off](../referee_handoff.md). A different upper reading already works at ten. Equal weighting of *labelled* twelve-knot systems gives **368/661** positive, compared with **49/85** under equal weighting of classes. Change “counting every closed system once needs no weighting” in [assumption A309](../../../docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md) to “each isomorphism class has equal weight.” [Weighting calculation](record_checks.json)
3. **Narrowing — search opportunity and the birth prediction.** Replace “There were 1,691 stages with at least 12 distinctions and at least 8 bonds, where a box could have appeared” in [the decision](../decision.md) with “1,691 pass both numerical filters using the archived searches, and **1,693** pass using full-stage bonds.” The two undercounted stages have **12,486** and **3,100** eligible single-word bonds, versus 6 and 0 locally. In the same section replace “G1 and G2 held” with “G1 holds across the sample after unrestricted checking. G2 follows from zero boxes and had zero independent birth-deletion tests.” Use the corrected denominator and evidence in the [objects entry](../../../docs/OBJECTS.md) and A309's history. ## What was not run

We did not exhaust other starting systems or later rounds, larger closed classes, the uniformly four-place twice-held classes at nine and ten knots, or arbitrary carrier sizes and upper readings. The full lower-bond totals for all 351 large stages were not required for the unrestricted box exclusion. The frequency of the alternative four-clause step in the sample remains unmeasured. Selection on other starts remains open.
