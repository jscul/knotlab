# Held-out feeder-payoff reversal

The held-out test asks whether a reader fitted on the real public energy receipts of one access version survives longer than readers trained on exposure counts or a rearranged receipt sequence. A sealed word is mounted at reset. A mounted reader observes the persistent feeder cue, own energy, previous action and anonymous outward controls. An actual move writes an arrival cue from its outward slot. A slot with an adjacent partner gives A or B by parity. An unpaired final slot leaves the cue unknown. The learned choice applies at a feeder with three exits and a known arrival cue, whenever current-only would attempt an eat. Other cases use the current-only decision.

## Pairing and controls

Use the 128 structures in the frozen held-out fold, after independent founder renaming and ordinary ranking. Use eight movement tapes per structure. Keep both knot laws, both access versions and both ordinary and mirrored anonymous codes on each tape. Mirroring interchanges adjacent outward labels while transporting an OFF draw to the same actual distinction. Pair the stock allocation, movement draws, costs and initial energy. Every policy receives a fresh game copy. Run every episode to actual death, including stalls, refusals, closed approaches and missed meals. A stock allocation is one-use; a declined attempt still pays the ordinary cost and leaves its ration in stock.

Compare each version's fitted outcome word, exposure-count word and rearranged-receipt word with current-only, a calibrated fixed-A word and a calibrated fixed-B word. The count arm uses its frozen neutral fallback when exposure counts tie. Also mount the outcome word from the other version. This crossed word keeps its fitted contents through the episode. The evaluation driver selects which word to mount. The live reader receives the same public observations and feedback in every arm, and issues a single action bit.

The version-blind balanced fixed comparator is the equal arithmetic average of fixed-A and fixed-B on each paired tape. The best single constant is the higher of their whole-fold pooled means for each law, with A chosen on a tie. Compute the paired bootstrap comparison to this best constant by taking the greater fixed mean in each resample. The best constant is a descriptive hindsight benchmark. Fixed-A and fixed-B remain the same across versions, and their individual results are retained. A version-matched calibrated cue can tie the corresponding fitted word.

## Outcomes and inference

Primary outcome is completed turns including death. For each law the primary contrast is fitted outcome minus exposure-count, with equal weight for the two versions, two anonymous codes and eight tapes within each founder. A lower paired 95% endpoint above zero establishes the registered pooled gain. Also report the contrast against current-only, all other named controls, and separate version gains averaged over both codes. A claim of a gain within both versions requires both version-specific lower endpoints above zero. Print each law independently because paired laws share source and movement draws. For each contrast use 2,000 whole-founder resamples. Keep all the founder's tapes and applicable version and coding records together. Use domain `survival-bootstrap`, law, group and comparator as the seed and the inclusive 2.5% and 97.5% percentile cuts. Report paired win/tie/loss counts with their pairing unit.

Report each arm's actual paid rations, overflow, final energy and stock remaining. Reconstruct each lifetime from the initial energy, four units per paid ration and energy discarded at capacity. Report the paired contributions of paid rations and overflow. Separate stocked-but-closed attempts from truly empty attempts and stocked open passes. Describe funded and stalled founder weights separately and keep every episode in the primary analysis.

Report A and B exposure separately on each reader's natural policy path per version and code, including live cue observations, current-eligible observations, issued ON, actual payments, charged nonpayments and passes with an obtainable ration. Natural paths are allowed to differ. As a separate paired diagnostic, play the current-only path and query every reader before applying the common action. Give each reader the actual public consequence of that action. Score availability and opening only from the game ledger after the reads, and report every enable and suppression compared with the other fitted word, count and current-only. The exact common observation counts require no claim of balanced natural exposure. Interpret a crossed-version contrast as a reversal of decisions when the native emitted bits actually invert on those common observations.

Write one raw row per natural episode, carrying source, tape, law, version, code, mounted word, lifetime, terminal and stock accounting, natural exposure and the matching common-path scores. Save a record of the complete public-history hash and a seed replay that reconstructs all individual decisions and consequence records. Save one row per founder, comparison and analysis group. Pin the exact learned-word SHA before opening either evaluation fold. Verify the same learned words after every episode and preserve all observed controls and signs.
