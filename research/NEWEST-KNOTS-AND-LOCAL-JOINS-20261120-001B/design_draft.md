## One policy and its local information

Start with a finite, connected physical presentation and one actual shared name e with two distinct holders w,z. The origin is that named meeting with the endpoint pair unordered. Include every holder within **two ordinary holder meetings of either endpoint** in the full current holdings before deleting e. Keep every individual shared name between included holders, including parallel names, and the mark identifying e. Colour each included holder with its actual loose count and its number of shared names (counting multiplicity). Expose no holder identities beyond the boundary, no identification of outside endpoints, and no joining among outside incidences. Thus a boundary holder's outward count is its total shared count minus recorded internal occurrences. Only the counts, internal incidences and root have meaning under arbitrary consistent renaming. Exact loose and degree annotations are supplied; they are local incidences, not coded references to outside completion. Controls will also erase boundary annotations where they are unnecessary. This is radius two in units of **holder meetings**, with individual incident counts available. It asserts no bound on the number of bits or knots in such a record.

Require w,z spent. Remove e only from the displayed induced multigraph. If w still reaches z there, reject. Otherwise collect every distinct live holder directly sharing with w or z in the original cut. Each side must be nonempty and disjoint. Add their union once to the unchanged ordinary multi-parent groups, deduplicating full true-parent sets across all eligible names. A same-side repeat or a donor meeting one side several times always consumes only one input per group; a donor on both sides invalidates that authorization. Each of the paying live parents supplies its own distinct loose name for that group. Total shortage vetoes the whole cut; every unspent remainder pays for a unary birth. The two spent witnesses keep all old names and never supply a third holding. The rule is applied at every step. For grants, construct the actual provisional newborn holdings using one positive private placeholder each, compute next ordinary and local groups, then grant the old `max(next demand,3−parent count,1)` with the sole implied parity-closing exception. Keep the ordinary law and the preceding **global** separating-name rule as controls. All three rules have the same funding and forecast conventions. Suppression of one local authorization on identical holdings supplies an ablation, not a fourth candidate policy. Original private-slot permutations are represented by consistent renaming.
