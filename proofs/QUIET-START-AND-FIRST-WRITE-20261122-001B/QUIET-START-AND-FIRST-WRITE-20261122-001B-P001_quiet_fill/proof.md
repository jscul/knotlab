# A first write from a quiet founder

Every comparison starts with the same actual and reference word. A minimum fill keeps the entire history quiet. Allowing all lawful fills at the first births permits H to write a deviation with both other newborns quiet. A different comparison holds both other newborns active and identical so that H’s quiet and changed writes reach the **same** later join. Under the primary not-all-equal reference one H-written mark can then be the sole difference anywhere in the reached state and reverse the join. ## What can first be written?

Fix a funded birth, at least one paid value, and a lawful reference extension of the quiet paid word. Let \(f\) be the number of fresh positions. At \(f=0\) the one empty completion is legal exactly when the paid word satisfies the relation. For \(f\ge1\), the numbers of lawful fresh words are

| Law | Number \(N\) | Reason |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Even parity | \(2^{f-1}\) | The last fresh bit is determined by the other bits. |
| Not-all-equal, constant paid values \(b\) | \(2^f-1\) | Only the constant fresh word of value \(b\) is forbidden. |
| Not-all-equal, mixed paid values | \(2^f\) | The paid values already differ. |

Exactly one of the \(N\) words agrees with the supplied fresh reference. Thus some deviation is possible exactly when \(N>1\), and no deviation is forced on a quiet input with a lawful reference. Parity requires at least two changed fresh marks whenever any changes. Under not-all-equal a one-mark change is legal precisely when the resulting full holding stays mixed. Under the stipulated uniform local weighting the probability of any deviation here is \(1-1/N\). The choices at different newborns in the same cut coexist because their fresh banks are disjoint and their inherited values are fixed. Under minimum filling the reference is uniquely at zero difference on a quiet word. Induction preserves it through each funded cut, including a lawful zero-fresh closure. All-lawful filling still contains that continuation. [Earlier quiet induction and minimum-write rule](../../../proofs/WRITTEN-BIT-AND-JOIN-VETO-20261122-001B/WRITTEN-BIT-AND-JOIN-VETO-20261122-001B-P001_guard_cause/proof.md)

The founder’s three private marks \(p,u,j\) pay three one-parent children H, U and J. Their next witnessed groups are the three pairs. Each first child gets two fresh distinctions. For parity the quiet source is 000, and all three banks have reference 00 and lawful words 00,11. For not-all-equal the quiet source is 110, so its two absolute ones already belong to the reference. H and U each have paid value 1, fresh reference 00 and lawful words 00,01,10. J has paid value 0 and lawful words 01,10,11. Its reference is 01 under the primary extension and 11 under the opposite homogeneous extension. Thus an all-lawful first cohort has exactly \(2^3=8\) or \(3^3=27\) physical words, including the completely quiet one. Put \(n=2\) or 3 respectively, \(q=1/n\) and \(b=1-q\). Each of the three first newborns independently stays at reference with probability \(q\). A cut is unordered, so concurrent first writers need no order among them.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Minimum everywhere (M), all three references | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 |
| All lawful first, then minimum (F), parity | 7/8 | 0 | 0 | 1/8 |
| F, either not-all-equal reference | 26/27 | 0 | 0 | 1/27 |

For the last two rows, a quiet first cohort gives three two-parent births at cut two, each with just one fresh place. In the ascending allocation one of the three paid pairs is mixed with the primary reference, and two are mixed with the opposite homogeneous reference. Each mixed pair permits two fresh values and each homogeneous pair permits one. Conditional on a quiet first cut, the second stays quiet with probability \(1/2\) or \(1/4\), respectively. The quiet second fresh triple is then 110 or 100 in birth order. It funds the sole three-parent child, which gets one fresh place under not-all-equal. The mixed triple lets either fresh value through. Under parity all three second writes are determined zeros on the quiet branch, and the third child closes at zero fresh. These comparisons include histories on which the designated H pair never forms.

## The same available target

H’s two actual fresh marks are \(h_0,h_1\). Its two one-parent recipients exist iff both initial groups H–U and H–J are vetoed. An active H suffices on any background. With H quiet, both U and J must be active. If only one recipient exists, H’s other mark was paid to a two-parent child. If no recipient exists, both marks went to different two-parent children. Each available pair of unary recipients is witnessed by H, with no further proposed partner at either, and is funded. Each recipient has its inherited \(h_i\) and two new marks. The full read therefore has six positions. | Other first newborns U,J | Exact backgrounds of this kind | H recipients when H is quiet | H recipients under each nonreference H write |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Both quiet | 1 | 0 | 2 |
| Only U active | \(n-1\) | 1 | 2 |
| Only J active | \(n-1\) | 1 | 2 |
| Both active | \((n-1)^2\) | 2 | 2 |

These four rows exhaust the \(n^2\) realized U,J backgrounds. Every raw word in a row gives its displayed outcome under the fixed binder. H is the **only first writer** with probability \(bq^2\), namely 1/8 or 2/27. Its target then exists and its paid deviation always vetoes. Holding these two quiet siblings fixed and changing H back to reference removes the target at the preceding cut. A matched switch of an **available** join is possible on the both-active backgrounds, of probability \(b^2\), namely 1/4 or 4/9. Both descriptions use the original unconditioned cohort.

For the preregistered display choose U,J’s first nonreference words in order. They are (11,11) for parity, (01,10) for primary not-all-equal and (01,01) for the opposite homogeneous reference. Their exact background has probability \(1/n^2\), irrespective of H’s word. On each, all three initial pairs are vetoed in both H preparations. All six children are then born from one parent each. In the ascending display H holds (0,3,4). Its two recipients hold (3,9,10) and (4,11,12). The founder’s mark 0 ends at H and is absent from the guard. U, J and their descendants can use identical absolute and reference words across the comparison. All their paid inputs and funding through the decision are unchanged.

| Reference | Quiet H and both target records at reference | Changed H and target records under minimum continuation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parity | H writes 00. Both targets hold 000. | H writes 11. Each target holds 101 or 110, independently. Both H marks and at least one other mark on each parent trip the veto. |
| Primary not-all-equal | H writes 00. Both targets hold 001. | H writes 01 or 10. The inheritor of the 1 holds 101. Its sibling holds 001. The changed H mark is the only difference. |
| Opposite homogeneous not-all-equal | H writes 00. Both targets hold 011. | H writes 01 or 10. The inheritor of the 1 holds 101 or 110, and its sibling holds 011. A companion also trips the veto. |

At the quiet target one real joint child takes 9 and 11 in the displayed allocation. Separate one-parent children take 10 and 12. At the changed target all four marks are paid singly to four different children. The source, its assigned values, the two other newborns, the potential joint and the availability of all four payments match. For primary not-all-equal choose H’s 01 and both recipient fresh pairs 01. The complete predecision words in the two histories can then differ only at physical mark 4. Choosing H’s 10 instead makes mark 3 solely responsible. These paired histories are lawful under both F and A. The other U,J families can continue identically through the deciding cut, transporting their birth names if the changed H roster shifts the display numbering.

The parity and alternative-reference obstructions to one sole H mark also hold under A. Parity at the quiet founder fixes even parity of H’s fresh pair. Writing 11 changes both inherited target arguments. Each receiving parent now needs an odd fresh pair, disjoint from the permitted even pair on its quiet comparison. In the opposite homogeneous reference, a parent that receives a changed 1 cannot keep its fresh reference 11. It requires at least one further deviation. Under primary not-all-equal the paid 0 and paid 1 both allow fresh 01. A also admits fresh 10 at both, but that common choice already trips the guard and precludes a pass on the quiet target. Testing the guard with H’s marks omitted reads a subset of a legal state. No physical erasure is required.

## Unconditional outcomes at the deciding cut

The columns partition the initial experiment. “H only” means the H-written marks are the only deviations on the complete proposed parent holdings. “Both” means at least one H mark and at least one recipient fresh mark deviate. “Recipient only” means the parent union vetoes despite both H marks being at reference.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| M, all three | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| F, parity | 1/8 | 1/4 | 1/8 | 0 | 1/2 | 0 |

| A, opposite homogeneous not-all-equal | 1/27 | 4/27 | 4/243 | 0 | 2/3 | 32/243 |

The two missing masses are \(q^3\) and \(2q^2b\). H-hot vetoes have total probability \(b\). H quiet with both siblings active has mass \(qb^2\). Under F its two target parents stay at reference and join. Under A each target has one quiet unary paid value and \(n\) legal fresh words, one of which is the reference. Thus its two parent banks both stay quiet with probability \(q^2\). This gives join mass \(q^3b^2\) and recipient-only veto mass \(qb^2(1-q^2)\). Under primary not-all-equal, conditional on H active, each recipient again has three legal fresh words, including its reference regardless of its paid bit. The probability that the H mark is the sole guard argument is \(1/9\) within this class and \(2/27\) overall. In every “Both” or “Recipient only” case masking the H arguments still leaves a veto. In every “H only” case the sole argument is one named physical H mark.

On *each fixed both-active U,J background*, an H-active first writing vetoes with probability one under F or A. With the quiet H writing the veto probability is zero under F and \(1-q^2\) under A. Consequently the conditional difference of veto probabilities is one under F and \(q^2\) under A. This prices the comparison against fresh activity by the recipient parents without using vetoed cases to choose a background.

## Whole-history support and controls

The following counts are supports on the fixed physical allocation. For a cut with actual roster \(B\), multiply the local bank cardinalities over \(B\) given the entire older history. Sum this product over the preceding histories. For an unrestricted birth use the cardinalities in the first table on its actual paid word. For a minimum birth a legal fresh reference contributes one choice. If the reference fails the actual relation, each of the \(f\) single fresh changes is a minimizer for parity or for an otherwise constant not-all-equal holding. A legal zero-fresh closing birth contributes one. For the target test, the next proposals are all formed before filtering. Each veto frees its obligations for unary payments, and the grants are recomputed from this actual roster. This provides a finite calculation without listing billions of final words.

For example, to recover the probability of a target outcome, each first cohort has mass \(1/n^3\) under F and A. Conditional on that cohort, each second-cut word has mass equal to the reciprocal of the product of its local permitted counts. All third-cut choices given that prefix sum to probability one. This explains why the probability table uses local conditional counts even when the numbers of terminal histories differ substantially. Global legality follows cut by cut from the legality of each new holding, disjoint fresh banks and preservation of all earlier values. The physical replay gives the support and conditional masses for every realized U,J word pair. Every reached target has both parents’ loose payments available when the veto is switched off at the deciding cut. Ordinary growth then retains the group for every such word. A prior grant met the ordinary demand for the next unfiltered groups, so removing vetoes at this stage leaves those obligations funded. In continuous ordinary growth from the founder, by contrast, all three first sibling-pair proposals are retained. H spends its two marks in two joint children and the enrolled unary pair is absent on every history. Ordinary birth counts through the three cuts are 3, 3 and 1. Under M its single word stays quiet. Under F its terminal word counts are 8, 27 and 27 in the order of the three conventions. Under A they are 8, 170 and 170. All three comparisons use a designated H branch, transported lawful references, a supplied fresh filling policy, and an installed whole-parent veto. Transporting mark identities, slot order and payment allocation together preserves both recipients and all readings. Other value-independent binders can change which outgoing marks meet at the joint. They still fund both unary recipients under the stated eligibility condition, and retain or refuse their common child on the same six physical roles. Allowing nonreference fills makes a first signal possible, and selecting a weighting prices it. The all-reference history remains lawful throughout.
