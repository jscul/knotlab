## What the hundred runs with two show

Under COPY-LAW(t) with the meeting built into the start, a start of arm 1 is one start roster whose members are marked
first or second (the roster statement, checked in PB3 and PB4), and its run is that roster's run. Two kinds of second
pattern are left twice from both: the two-knot part (54 runs through round 8) and one knot holding two (46 runs). In
all 100 the same count of parts of that form is left by the second pattern alone and with the first pattern barred, so
the first pattern is not what makes two. In 22 of the 100 the knots descended from Y only have ended by the round
read; in the other 78 they have not, so the frozen text's phrase "on (b) alone" covers 22 runs, not all of those seen.

## What this shows and does not show

- **The owner's trick, as tested.** "Consume and output two" was read as: the second pattern ends as itself and two of
  its form are left, each descended from both. On this class, under these laws, that happens only where the second
  pattern leaves two of itself without the first. Nothing here is a pattern that copies.
- **The hand prediction that failed** says something small about the fold of emergence: with no taking rule, which
  forms descended from both are left can differ with which second pattern the start holds. That is the start roster
  differing, since the run is the marked roster's run; it is not a made pattern reading another.
- **Supplied**: in arm 1 the meeting, built into the start by one of four registered ways, and the marks first and
  second on one start roster; in arm 2 the starts and the taking rule with its way of choosing, which uses names.
  **Made by births**: the parts counted, and in arm 2 the meetings, through takes.
- **Bounded**: eight kinds for each pattern; four start's meetings; six base laws with no taking rule and MET under
  two rations with four ways of choosing; 8 rounds in arm 1.
- **Not run**: 2,718 of the 18,432 runs of arm 2 on the 256 starts, in 14 of 72 jobs (the laws with offshoots or
  waiting under two ways of choosing). The registered sizes understated the cost; the run was cut to a prefix of the
  registered order by a rule recorded before the cut. In four of those jobs parts of more than 24 members were classed
  by invariants and a hash, and one exact test of form, on a part of 18 members, was left undecided; every form a
  reading uses has at most 6 members, so no verdict rests on either. No renaming of a case was run, because arm 2 has
  no case.
- **A soup of separate pieces** (many parts, sampled meetings) is the third ticket's reading and is not tested here.
