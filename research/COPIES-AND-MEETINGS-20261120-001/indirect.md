# A later child of one packet can require an older packet that it never meets directly

There is a one-holding comparison under both ordinary knot laws. A younger packet keeps every one of its own parents, its two pure children per member and its members’ actual fresh counts. At arbitrarily many parent steps after release, one of its distinctions goes to a child that requires a descendant of the older packet. The pure packets never supply inputs to the same birth. In the comparison without the older packet, a different child uses the corresponding opportunity and receives one fewer fresh distinction. The effect is visible in the sum of prices over **all** children of that receiver, even if their names are erased. The two preparations have the same 21 knots and the same 60 distinctions. ## An obstruction for any ordinary presentation

At a complete funded size, write `dist` for the smallest number of steps between sets of live knots, where a step goes between two distinct knots sharing a witness. Use infinity between different parts or for an empty endpoint set. Let \(A_t\) be a continuing packet, with every parent of every \(A_{t+1}\) member in \(A_t\). Let \(D_t\) contain **all** descendants at size \(t\) of another specified release. A knot is in \(D\) as soon as one of its parents is in \(D\), regardless of the other parents. Then

\[
                 \operatorname{dist}(A_{t+1},D_{t+1})
                  \ \ge\ \operatorname{dist}(A_t,D_t).
\]

For a path of \(k>0\) steps on the next end, choose one common parent across each step. These \(k\) parents, in order, start in \(A_t\). Two successive choices occur in the same child. They coincide or are adjacent at size \(t\), since a multi-parent birth uses pairwise candidates. Append a \(D_t\) parent of the final child. This gives a route of at most \(k\) steps. If the two endpoints coincide, its \(D_t\) parent already belongs to \(A_t\). At a positive size the only witnesses between two new knots are their actual parents. This argument uses no identification with a fixed map. Every candidate pair belongs to at least one proposed group. Hence, for disjoint \(A_t,D_t\) at a funded cut, an output that takes one parent from each occurs immediately if their separation is one. At separation at least two it can never first occur later, however many third-party parents participate. A global stall stops the step. A sole closing parity birth can be an output but has no continuing live end. [Ordinary complete-end operator](../ORDINARY-DYNAMICS-20261120-001/end_operator.md) · [Independent growing parts and direct meetings](../GENERATORS-20261120-001/components.md)

In particular, if two disjoint packets are released together as noncandidates, no future child of a current pure member can require a descendant of the other. Their collateral descendants may still meet each other, because both collateral families use a weaker parent condition. A shared earlier ancestor likewise supplies no directed parent path from either packet to the other. Different whole growing parts cannot meet by parentage. Comparing different starts additionally requires checking the funding guard, parity closure and, in the fixed-map class, any relay on a finished part. The obstruction by itself gives no comparison of immutable prices across preparations.

## One productive source, two release sizes

Here are the physical data used below. The nine live original types are \(v,r_1,r_2,r_3,s,t,b_0,b_1,b_2\). Their candidate map \(H\) has the neutral circuit \(v,r_1,r_2,r_3,v\), the pairs \(vs,vt\), and the pairs \(sb_i,tb_i\) for \(i=0,1,2\). Each of the 12 pairs is witnessed by its own spent three-place knot. It holds one distinction toward the tail and two toward the head. Direct the neutral circuit as written, and the other meetings as

\[
 v\to s,\quad t\to v,\quad s\to b_0,\quad t\to b_0,\quad
 s\to b_1,\quad b_1\to t,\quad t\to b_2,\quad b_2\to s .
\]

Give each of the nine live originals as many loose distinctions as its degree in \(H\). This gives 36 shared and 24 loose distinctions. Each candidate has just one witness. There is no triple of pairwise candidates. The identification theorem therefore makes size \(m\) the connected \(m\)-point sets in the unrolled tree, up to its type-preserving covering transformations. Distinct admissible one-point deletions are the parents, and distinct one-point extensions are the children. The point sets must contain a live original. A path of original points has exactly its two endpoint-deletion parents for \(m\ge2\). Let \(N\) be the neutral circuit, and \(P_j=(v,s,b_j,t,v)\). A concatenation records the common endpoint only once. With \(n\ge1\), form two periodic walks

\[
                   W_n^j=(N^nP_0N^nP_j)^\infty,\qquad j=1,2.
\]

They have period \(p=2d\), where \(d=4(n+1)\) counts steps between consecutive visits to a marked type \(b_i\). Each full period contains exactly one \(b_0\) and one \(b_j\). The longest common interval has exactly \(p-1\) points. A longer one would contain both distinct choices of the second marked type. An interval attaining the bound is the common word \(I\) strictly between successive \(b_j\) visits. It starts in \(t\), ends in \(s\), and contains \(b_0\). These facts are invariant under reversal of the whole interval.

Use one computable source test at every size. Read a straight interval on the cyclic part of the recovered original candidate map. For each consecutive pair of marked visits of types \(\{b_0,b_j\}\), with step gap \(g\), require its entire length \(m\) to be strictly less than \(T_j(g)\). Set \(T_2(g)=2g\) and choose any computable integer \(T_1(g)>2g\). For example \(T_1(g)=(2g)^2\), giving \(q=T_1(d)=p^2\). This threshold is part of the stated observer. It does not change any birth. Every endpoint deletion preserves the test. Neutral intervals give productive members at every size. At a fixed size, shifting to a window which adds an unmarked point and drops an endpoint preserves it. From an endpoint at a mark continue to its other \(s,t\) neighbor and then toward \(v\). From \(s,t\) entered from a mark continue to \(v\). At an endpoint \(v,s\) or \(v,t\), replace the last point by an unmarked neutral neighbor of \(v\) different from the preceding point, if present. The replacement shares the other \(m-1\) points. From \(v\) or a neutral point continue on the neutral circuit without retracing. Sliding a fixed window along this continuation connects it to the neutral phases, which are themselves connected. Size one uses the connected cyclic candidate map. Thus the source is connected at each size as well as by accumulated parentage. The cyclic part used here is the surviving submap after iteratively removing types of degree at most one.

Release **B** as every \(p\)-point phase of \(W_n^2\) and **A** as every \(q\)-point phase of \(W_n^1\). All launch parents are in that source. Every full-period window has a consecutive pair of marks of the right type and gap. Shorter windows before the applicable cutoff meet the test. The two endpoint extensions along its periodic walk are its pure children. Directed phases are distinct for these sizes. The special marked type fixes the phase over a full period, and a mark strictly inside a window is entered from \(s\) and left through \(t\), distinguishing reversal. Different \(n\) give different gaps. The single quadratic choice above gives arbitrarily large separation between release sizes as \(n\) increases. ## Exact separation and actual intermediaries

At every common size \(m\ge q\), the separation of the two **pure** packets on the whole live end is exactly

\[
                             m-p+1.
\]

Along any witnessed-end route, choose successive lifts in the unrolled tree aligned at their actual common deletion parent. Each step removes at most one of the original \(m\) points of the first cluster. The intersection of the first and last intervals is consecutive when nonempty, hence it has at most \(p-1\) points. This gives the lower bound even when the route visits arbitrary noninterval knots. For equality, lift a walk following \(W_n^1\) up to a final occurrence of \(I\) and then following \(W_n^2\) from that occurrence. It never immediately retraces a meeting. Slide an \(m\)-point window from one with suffix \(I\) in the first walk to one with prefix \(I\) in the second. Exactly \(m-p+1\) shifts suffice. Since \(q>p\), the two pure packets never supply parents of the same birth. The same lower-bound argument gives separations of at least \(m-q+1\) and \(m-p+1\) from A and B, respectively, to the continuing source at a common size. A shared interval of the corresponding launch length would contain a pair forbidden in a source word of length \(m\).

For \(r\ge p-1\), let \(S_r\) be the \(r\)-point window of \(W_n^1\) ending at that \(I\), starting with \(S_{p-1}=I\). Put \(J_r=S_r b_2\). The knot \(J_{p-1}=I b_2\) is a launch member of B. For each \(r\ge p\), the two actual parents of \(J_r\) are

\[
                                 S_r,\qquad J_{r-1}.
\]

Thus \(J_m\) at size \(m+1\) receives a fresh distinction of a current pure A member and one of a B-descending parent, for **every** \(m\ge q\). Its only B launch ancestor is \(I b_2\). In every size-\(p\) B word the type \(b_2\) occurs, and the only \(p\)-point subinterval of \(J_m\) containing that final point is its suffix. Before A releases, the co-parents \(S_p,\ldots,S_{q-1}\) all belong to the source. The B release is \(m+1-p\) actual parent steps back. At the first opportunity \(m=q\) this can already be arbitrarily long, and it recurs arbitrarily far **after both releases**. There is an opportunity immediately on A's release. Its B-inclusive co-parent is already a candidate of \(S_q\) through their common parent \(S_{q-1}\). The obstruction above explains why a delayed *first* opportunity after a cut separating A from all B-inclusive knots is impossible. No A member contains \(b_2\). The continuing pure A diagram has no B ancestor.

In the fixed structure, withholding the B launch removes a compulsory predecessor of each \(J_m\), by this explicit chain of parents. Every pure A prerequisite is still available. This is a dependence test on the fixed map. Fair delivery of its parents produces the actual knots, regardless of interleaving.
