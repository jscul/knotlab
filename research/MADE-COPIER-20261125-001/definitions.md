## 1. The law, fixed before any pattern

1. **One complete round with taking** exactly as FD 2: the order of births and names (FD 2.1), eligibility, step 5′, the
   choice, demand after the takes, the fresh count by distinct inputs, the IMPLIED closing under parity.
2. **The naming convention** is FD 2.1: a start's distinctions are named by their labels in increasing order. The shared
   distinction e of the designs with Y present from round 0 has the label 1000, so it is named last. The renamings of D 4.5 change it.
3. **The taking rule: MET** (FD 3.3), the fold study's cheapest record-free rule under which parts meet.
4. **The two base laws, both primary under MET.** The fold result's P11 gives, for parts that came apart, STEPS-1 under
   FLOOR1 and MET under CAP-3; MET under FLOOR1 has a confirmed case only for the two-knot start's own two parts, which were
   never one part. So MET is registered under both:
   - **COPY-LAW(t)** = (FLOOR1, t, RELEASE), for t = CANCEL-RETIRE, CANCEL-OFFSHOOT, CANCEL-WAIT;
   - **CAP-3(t)** = (CAP-3, t, RELEASE), for the same three t.
   A **base law** is one of these six, or of the three of item 6.
5. **The second taking rule: STEPS-1**, under the same six base laws. **STEPS-2** is the rule of the arm of parts that
   came apart (D 4.8); its cost: three holder steps and two parent steps of the record.
6. **The form arm's laws:** MET and STEPS-1 under CAP-3(t), and under **FBP(t)** = (FLOOR1, t, RELEASE) with the fresh
   count by parents (FD 2.7's swap "fresh by parents", here a base law of its own): the base laws under which S4 (D 6)
   does not hold, so that a part with a member holding two can be made after the start's own offshoots.
7. **The choice:** each of OLDEST, NEWEST, IN-TURN, BY-COUNT, the same rule text for every birth. All four use names:
   OLDEST and NEWEST pick by name, IN-TURN by the order of births, which follows names, BY-COUNT indexes the candidates
   sorted by name. So the naming convention is part of what is supplied. **Every choice** (ALL-CHOICES): D 2.7.
8. **Knot laws:** parity with IMPLIED, and not-all-equal; every run under both.
9. A law is fixed for a whole run. Nothing changes the law for a pattern, adds a rule for copying, or places a knot after
   the start, except the knots placed by hand in the controls of the readings (D 3.6), which are not run.

## 2. The patterns

1. **The first pattern X** and **the second pattern Y**: two parts of the live roster after a round s, wholly apart
   (FD 1.6). X is the candidate copier. In the owner's first test X is the same (SD lines 29–30) as the two-knot part.
2. **The designs** (starts.json, "designs"). **Made** designs `made-h`, h = 2 to 6: the start `[[0,1,2],[0,3,…,2+h]]`;
   X = knot 0's two round-1 offshoots (knots 2, 3), Y = knot 1's h round-1 offshoots (knots 4 to 3 + h), s = 1.
   Designs with **Y present with X from round 0**, `ystart-<kind>`: the three-knot start's two founders a = knot 1 and
   b = knot 2 with their dead knot w = knot 0, which also holds the shared distinction e; a dead knot z = knot 3 that holds e and meets
   Y's members, knots 4 on; X = {1, 2}, a two-knot part present at round 0 (a start pattern), s = 0. The kinds: one-2,
   one-3, two-22, two-23, two-33, three-222 (one member holding two; one holding three; two holding two each; two and
   three; three and three; three holding two each). made-2 and two-22 are **the control with Y the same as X**.
3. **The meeting round**: the first round t > s at which X and Y are one part again or one part for the first time
   (FD 4.3), read in run A. Every reading of D 3 and D 7 is made at every round after s, and whether it falls before, at or after
   the meeting round is reported with it; with no meeting by the last round read, every reading is reported "before the
   meeting round (none by round T)".
4. **Y's places**: the distinctions held by Y's members after round s. **Y's knots**: Y's members and every knot that
   holds a place of a member (every knot a member meets), with their places and holders after round s. X's places and
   knots likewise.
5. **Made after round s**: a knot made after round s; a distinction that is a fresh distinction of such a knot.
6. **Descent.** A knot made after round s is **descended from X** when it is in X and its descendants (FD 4.2: X, and
   every knot made after round s with a parent among them), including through a parent that is a third part's knot or a
   joint birth; **from both** when it is in X and its descendants and in Y and its descendants; **from X only** when it
   is descended from X and not from both; likewise for Y; otherwise **from neither**. A knot made at or before round s is
   descended from X only when it is a member of X, from Y only when it is a member of Y, and otherwise from neither (start
   knots, dead knots, and the other knots made by round s). A made distinction has its maker's descent. With a third
   pattern Z (D 7.8), descent is read for X and the pattern whose place is read. **Made by X**, for two things (two parts,
   two renamings, two distinctions): in values, at least one of the two descended from X only or from both; in form and
   built structure, every member, or every knot, of each of the two descended from X only. The exact descent of each is
   reported.
7. **History**: one run with every take fixed. **Every choice** (run 4): every combination of the candidates of every take
   of every round s + 1 to s + 4, counted depth-first, and read in full when there are at most 60,000; otherwise
   **drawn**: 20,000 histories per design and law, each take of each round, in the order the round asks for them (FD
   2.1's birth order; for each birth its takes in order), one call `rnd.randrange(len(candidates))` on the candidates
   sorted by name, a take with a single candidate also making its call; one generator per design and law,
   `random.Random('2026112502|<design>|<law>')`, with `<design>` the design's name in starts.json (for example
   `made-3`, `ystart-one-2`) and `<law>` the fold engine's `Law.name()` with `MET(OLDEST)` written `MET(every choice)`
   (for example `FLOOR1/CANCEL-OFFSHOOT/RELEASE/MET(every choice)/parity`); histories drawn one after another. That is
   **the take-by-take weighting**; it is not uniform over histories. A history whose round stops at the fold engine's
   guard is read through the rounds before that round and counted apart as "stopped by the guard". Every history with
   two of Y, and every replayed take list, has every knot tested at every round. A picked history is registered as its
   **take list** (take_lists.json) and replayed by the engine.

## 3. Three readings of "two of it"

All are read after a round t > s in the run named, against Y as it was after round s, at every round; "at some round" and
"at the last round read" are reported apart.

1. **Form: two parts the same as Y.** At least two parts of the roster after round t, each the same as Y was after round
   s (SD lines 29–30), each containing a member made after round s, at the same round. A part whose members have several
   descents is counted under "mixed", with the descents listed.
2. **Built structure: a renaming of Y's knots among the made knots.** A connected set K′ of knots made after round s,
   with one-to-one maps φ from Y's knots onto K′ and ψ from their places onto the places of K′, such that d is a place
   of u exactly when ψ(d) is a place of φ(u), and d and ψ(d) agree on: the number of holders inside the set, whether a
   holder lies outside it, loose, barred; and u and φ(u) have the same number of places. The search backtracks on knot
   signatures (places, loose, barred) and stops at two renamings with no common knot or at 10,000 distinct knot sets
   ("bound hit"); whether the two also share no place is reported. **Strong form**: also, the allowed states after round t
   restricted to ψ of the places equal, under ψ, those after round s restricted to the places of Y's knots (parity: a
   basis; not-all-equal: a solver, at most 14 places, otherwise "not compared").
3. **Values: two usable loose made distinctions tied to one place of Y.** A place v of Y that is free and tied to no place
   of X after round s, and two made distinctions, each tied to v after round t and each usable and loose (one holder, not
   barred) at the same round t. Ties are exact: parity by a basis of the allowed states (two distinctions are tied exactly
   when their columns over the basis are equal; frozen when zero); not-all-equal by sampled allowed states, each
   candidate pair confirmed by a solver. Made distinctions tied to v that are not loose are reported apart. Reported with
   each case: the number of holders of v after round s and after round t, and **two users**: whether the two are later
   taken by different births.
4. **Hand on**: a birth hands v on when one of its fresh distinctions is tied to v.
5. **The second pattern handing on its own value**: two usable loose made distinctions tied to one place of Y, both
   descended from Y only. In form, a part the same as Y made by Y's descendants is Y returning (SD line 87).
6. **The controls of the readings** (run G). On the two-knot start with no taking rule, under COPY-LAW(t), t each of the
   three, 7 rounds: Y = knot 1's two round-1 offshoots (knots 4 and 5), s = 1. Counted after each round 2 to 7: parts
   the same as Y containing a member made after round 1; and, in **counting mode**, the number of distinct connected sets
   of knots made after round 1 each carried onto Y's knots by a renaming (D 3.2's conditions, not limited to two, up to
   10,000). Both counts must equal the fold engine's count of two-knot parts N(t) (starts.json, "counts for P3", the
   two-knot rows). **Placed by hand**: on made-3, made-4, ystart-one-3 and ystart-two-23 under (CAP-3, CANCEL-OFFSHOOT,
   RELEASE) with MET(OLDEST), parity, after round 3 of run A: one new knot for each of Y's knots with one new distinction
   for each of their places, holding them as Y's knots hold theirs, each new distinction barred exactly when its original
   was barred after round s; for each place of Y's knots with a holder outside Y's knots, one further new knot holding the
   new distinction and two new distinctions, and a last new knot holding those two and one new barred distinction (so that
   no new knot outside the set is a member; each of the four designs has exactly one such place). Both readings must count exactly one more
   than before the placing. The placed structure is read, never grown.

## 4. The paired runs

For a design with X, Y and s:
1. **Run A**: the start grown under the law.
2. **B(i), the second pattern barred** (FD 5.1): run A through round s; then every usable loose distinction of Y's members
   is barred; then the same law. In values it is reported, never a condition: "needs Y's births" (no two in B(i)) or
   "does not" (two in B(i), over any place of Y by tie class). **B(ii) is not run in this study.**
3. **B_X, the first pattern barred**: the same with X for Y.
4. **The second pattern replaced**: run A from the start in which Y's knots are replaced by those of another member Y′ of
   the class, X's knots unchanged: on the made designs knot 1 is replaced; on the designs with Y present from round 0
   Y's members and Y's dead knot z are replaced (z is one of Y's knots). Names follow the naming convention, so the start
   is renumbered (e has name 17 with three members holding two, 12 with one member holding two).
5. **Renamings of the start**, each a change of names only, never of knots or of which knot holds what (starts.json,
   "renamings", one entry per design): **n1** names reversed (distinctions named in decreasing order of label); **n2** e
   named first (label 1000 to −1; designs with Y present from round 0); **n3** a label map that puts X's labels after Y's
   (made-h: 1, 2 to h + 1, h + 2 and 3, …, 2 + h to 1, …, h; designs with Y present from round 0: every label less than
   20 increased by 100, e unchanged); **n4** the label map listed, drawn once with
   `labels = sorted(labels); perm = labels[:]; random.Random('2026112506|' + design).shuffle(perm)`, label `labels[i]` to
   `perm[i]`. A case **survives** a renaming when the run from the renamed start, under the same law and rule, has two of
   Y in the same reading, made by X, at some round read (the names of what is found may differ). A case holds **under a
   stated rule** only if it survives every renaming registered for its design (n1, n3, n4 on the made designs; n1 to n4
   on the others).
6. **X alone and Y alone** (starts.json, "alone"): the start without the other pattern's knots, every distinction they
   shared with the pattern's knots barred: for the made designs `[[0,1,2]]` with 0 barred (X = {1, 2}) and
   `[[0,3,…,2+h]]` with 0 barred (Y = knots 1 to h), s = 1; for the designs with Y present from round 0, w, a and b with e
   barred (X = {1, 2}), and z with Y's members with e barred (Y = knots 1 on), s = 0. X's course in X alone is compared
   with X's course in run A on the traces **renamed by first appearance** (FD 5.3), since the two starts are numbered
   differently; likewise Y.
7. **Masked runs** (FD 5.6): for X, M (from round s + 1 no member of X and its descendants is joinable with a member of Y
   and its descendants) and M′ (no member of Y and its descendants is joinable with any other member); for Y, M_Y (the
   same as M) and M′_Y (no member of X and its descendants is joinable with any other member). They act on joinability,
   not on takes, so a difference whose way is a take can be left unconfirmed; the takes of D 7.5 are reported with them.
8. **The arm of parts that came apart** (run 3), on the pair starts of starts.json: for each s from 0 to 4, every part X
   of the roster after round s the same as the two-knot part and every other part Y not the same as X, X and Y wholly
   apart, that are one part again or one part for the first time by round 7 (no limit on the number of pairs; FD 5.8's
   limits are not used); 7 rounds; STEPS-2 primary, MET and STEPS-1. The replacement run is not defined there, so the
   claims C and CF are not tested on that arm.
9. **Comparisons** are through the last round every run of the comparison reached, with the same bounds (FD 5.1).

## 5. The copier, and its verdicts

1. **A copier** (the owner's sense): a first pattern X such that, under one law (a base law, a taking rule and a knot law)
   and one stated choice rule, fixed before the runs and the same for every member of a stated class, for every second
   pattern Y of the class and surviving every registered renaming:
   - **condition 1, made by X**: run A has two of Y, in the reading named, made by X (D 2.6);
   - **condition 2, not of the others**: in form and built structure, run A with Y has, after every round read, fewer
     than two of Y′ (any descent: parts the same as Y′ in form, renamings of Y′'s knots in built structure) for every
     other member Y′ of the class, and the run with Y′ has two of Y′ made by X at some round read. In values the first
     half holds by construction and the second half is condition 1 for Y′, so condition 2 adds nothing in values; X alone
     is reported;
   - **condition 3**: Y is not the same as X; Y's knots are not a renaming of X's; in values v is tied to no place of X;
   - B(i), B_X and X alone are reported with every case.
2. **Verdicts: three questions, each with exclusive answers**, asked of every case with two of Y:
   - **Who made the two**: descended from X only; from both; from Y only; from neither (the exact pair of descents).
   - **What fixed the takes**: a stated rule whose case survives every registered renaming; a stated rule whose case is
     lost under some renaming; a history picked from every choice.
   - **Where X and the linking knot come from**: X made by births or a start pattern; the knots through which X and Y are
     one part at the meeting round (FD 5.7's classes) made by births or start knots.
   A case is **made** only when all three answers are the growth's: made by X's descendants, under a rule that survives
   every renaming, with X and the linking knot made by births. Every other case is reported as supplied, with the answer
   that makes it so.
3. **The fourth mark on the replacement runs** (FD 5.3's fourth mark, read with D 4.4's runs): X's trace, renamed by
   first appearance, differs between run A with Y and run A with Y′, the same X, law, rule and naming. Also read on the
   **parts by descent**: the counts, at each round, of parts the same as each member of the class, by descent.

## 6. Facts used (each argued in P, "Settled by construction")

1. **S1, ties are decided at birth.** Under a law in which every birth either receives a fresh distinction or (parity,
   IMPLIED) adds a rule already in the span, every allowed state before a round extends, whether or not any knot passes,
   so the allowed states restricted to the distinctions existing after round t are the same after every later round. It
   excludes LITERAL, FLOOR0 and ONE-FEWER.
2. **S2, not-all-equal.** Every birth with a fresh distinction: a fresh distinction f of a birth u is tied to another
   distinction exactly when u has one fresh distinction and its inputs are all tied equal before its round; f is then
   tied unequal to each input. So, from a start with no tie, no made distinction is ever tied to another, whether or not
   knots pass. From a start with a tie it can be.
3. **S3, parity.** A birth with two or more fresh distinctions hands nothing on (this uses IMPLIED); a birth u with one
   fresh distinction f has f tied to v exactly when the sum, over the field of two elements, of u's inputs and v lies in
   the span of the rules before u's round, zero included; a passing maker of a distinction tied to v never holds v.
4. **S4, one fresh distinction.** Under MET or STEPS-1, with RELEASE or HOLD, FLOOR1 and the fresh count by distinct
   inputs, every birth other than an offshoot of a start knot receives exactly one fresh distinction (none, under parity,
   when it is implied); under STEPS-2 likewise, except also offshoots of knots whose parents are all start knots. So every
   knot such a birth makes holds **at most one** loose distinction.
5. **S5, S6.** Parity with at most two holders: ties are two-element cuts. Not-all-equal with at most two holders: every
   distinction can change alone. Record facts (survey 1.1, 1.2). The registered designs have at most two holders and so
   no tie; the starts with a third pattern give e three holders, so S6 does not cover them, and their tie classes at
   round 0 are computed (P, run G): none has a tie or a frozen distinction under either knot law.
6. **S7.** A passing knot with three or more places has no two of its own places tied (a two-place knot left by an
   implied birth has both places tied and passes).

## 7. The owner's arm (A248)

His words and the arguments are in [the owner's arm](owner_arm.md). Each reading of this section is decided by a program.
1. **The last round read**, T: the last round reached by every run the reading names (run A, B(i), B_X, M and M_Y; for
   (iii) also X alone and Y alone; for (iv) the run with Z and the run with Z barred). Every comparison is made at T.
2. **Ended** (SD line 91): X has ended after round t when no knot of X and its descendants is a member after round t. It
   then stays ended at every later round (FD 1.1: a knot that is no member never becomes one, and every birth needs a
   member parent). **The round of ending** of X in a run is the first round t ≥ s after which X has ended, or "not ended
   by round T"; it is reported for every run of a reading.
3. **Makes a birth in the last round read**: X has not ended by round T, and some birth of round T of run A has a parent
   among X and its descendants. (A pattern whose births of round T leave it no member, as an implied birth can, has
   ended.)
   **Every member left waits**: X has a member after round T, makes no birth in round T, and every member of X and its
   descendants waits in round T; any other case is reported as "members left, no birth". A bounded run cannot say "for
   ever": "makes a birth in the last round read" says only that.
4. **Changes the other's course**: X's course in run A against B(i), its first differing round, its way (FD 5.5), and its
   confirmation (FD 5.6): **through a meeting** when X's trace in M equals its trace in B(i); otherwise **through a third
   part** when it does in M′; otherwise **unconfirmed**. Y's course against B_X, with M_Y and M′_Y.
5. **Takes from the other**: the first round at which a birth descended from Y only takes (step 5′) a distinction that has,
   at the start of that round, a holder descended from X only; and the reverse; joint births apart; reported against the
   first round of a course difference (strictly before, same round, after, or no take).
6. **Reading (ii), X ended with Y making births, Y still making births**: X has ended in run A by round T; X has not ended
   by round T in B(i); and Y makes a birth in round T of run A. Its rounds of ending in A and B(i) are reported. It is
   **through a meeting** when X has not ended by round T in M. The reverse (X and Y exchanged, against B_X and M_Y) is
   reported with it, and so are the controls with Y the same as X. It names no cause.
7. **Reading (iii), one-way**: X's course differs with Y barred, confirmed through a meeting; X's course in run A differs
   from its course in X alone; Y's course is the same with X barred; and Y makes a birth in round T. The reverse (X and Y
   exchanged, with Y alone) and the controls are reported with it.
8. **Reading (iv), a third pattern**: on every case with two of Y in values under a stated rule or a registered take
   list, on the designs with Y present from round 0 (it is not defined on the made designs). For each other kind j of
   D 2.2 (the control included), Z of kind j named before Y and named after Y (starts.json, "third pattern": Z's dead
   knot holds e, which then has three holders; Z = Z's members; s = 0; **Z barred**: every usable loose distinction of
   Z's members barred after round 0). Read: (a) with Z there, no two of Y after any round read; (b) with Z barred, two of
   Y after some round read; (c) with Z there, no two of Z after any round read (two usable loose made distinctions tied
   to one place of Z, at least one descended from X); (d) Z makes a birth in round T of the run with Z. Each is reported; (iv) holds for a Z when all four do. **Under a take
   list** the run with Z takes as follows: a birth with a parent among Z and its descendants takes by OLDEST; every other
   birth takes what the list's run took at the birth that answers it, translated. A birth answers another when they are
   of the same round and kind, their parents answer one another, and, for offshoots, they have the same number in the
   order of that parent's offshoots of the round; start knots answer by their order in the start's list, start
   distinctions by label, and the fresh
   distinctions of answering births in order. If some birth with no parent among Z and its descendants has none that
   answers it, or a translated take is not a candidate, the run is reported "the list does not apply from round t" and is
   not read further.
9. **Changed by births descended from Y only** (the FLOOR0 and ONE-FEWER row, run O): D0 is the set of distinctions held
   by X and its descendants after round s + 1, the ten with the smallest names if there are more. At the first round t ≥
   s + 2 at which the allowed states restricted to D0 differ from those after round t − 1 (parity: the rank of D0's
   columns over a basis of the allowed states; not-all-equal: the number of assignments of D0 that extend, by a solver),
   the change is **by births descended from Y only** when every birth of round t that receives no fresh distinction and
   is not implied is descended from Y only, and removing those births' knots from the structure after round t gives the
   restricted allowed states after round t − 1. The knot test is read at round t.
10. **The control with Y the same as X**: made-2, a start with a renaming exchanging X and Y (knots 0 and 1, with labels
