## Independent finite stress check

Generate every simple meeting pattern with precisely one ring as a cyclic list of rooted trees. Each rooted tree is encoded by the unordered list of its child trees. Minimizing the cyclic list over the rotations and reversals gives each unlabelled pattern once. Independent flags at holders of degree one or two can change their private supply but never whether they are live. For each subset of the higher-degree holders, designate just that subset live and give *all* live holders the largest allowed supply. Compute whether the live end is connected and whether each can pay for all its first proposals. A failure at this relaxed budget rules out every smaller budget with the same live holders.

In this test a pair seen by two witnesses is charged once. A mutually witnessed triple with no common witness saves one call at each of its three members. Such a triple makes a six-cycle in the incidence of the live holders and witness copies. There can be no fourth member of that group: each connected incidence component has at most one cycle. This also tests the structural step used in the general proof. The finite enumeration is independent of the investigator's decoration census and its two weightings.

## A sharp size boundary

For an ordinary whole that actually closes under the same cap, take a three-knot ring and two leaves. Two of the ring knots each meet a different leaf and are spent. The third ring knot has two loose distinctions, and each leaf has three. This is a five-knot founder with exactly one more meeting than a tree. Its first five funded cohorts have **6, 7, 6, 6 and 4** births at both laws, bringing respectively 14, 14, 14, 12 and 4 fresh distinctions. At the sixth step one four-parent birth at parity takes all loose names, brings none and leaves a closed whole of **35 knots and 71 distinctions**. Its ordinary even equation is the sum of all 34 earlier equations. Reversing just its last sign would admit a quantum operator reading under the previously checked signed criterion. Under not-all-equal, the sixth birth brings one new name instead. This case establishes the sharpness of six for the all-age exclusion and does not change the four-step six-to-eight denominators.
