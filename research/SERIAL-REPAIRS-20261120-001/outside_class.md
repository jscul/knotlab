## A directed local readiness argument

There is a separate sufficient rule with larger freedom of order. Give the reader the direction of actual parent–child meetings and the original-source predicate. Assume a lawful source with initial funding, or certify that funding before any new birth. The rule uses current incidence only. A built knot's initial supply is the number of its current loose distinctions plus its distinctions already given to children. The state of having spent that supply is visible at the knot's own star. Source witnesses are read directly from the already supplied source.

At a proposed birth through an anchor of positive depth, first wait for every parent of the anchor to spend its entire supply. Those parents now display every child they will have, hence every same-depth candidate of the anchor. For each such candidate, perform the same check at its parents. Their complete child lists display the anchor's entire two-step growing-end ball at its birth depth. A candidate's incoming holders recover the witnessed sets within that ball, including witnesses that hold more than one member. Its original supply is recovered from its own star. The two-step demand theorem then computes every ordinary birth through the anchor and its exact nonclosing budget. One must retain spent same-depth members, using their original supplies even after their own children have been written. An actual birth is written only once per planned occurrence. Distinct old slots can be matched to those occurrences in any consistent way, up to exchanging a parent's loose names.

The bound is two witnessed growing-end steps. Each such step uses two physical holder steps through an older parent. Reading the stars of displayed holders within four physical steps, with the incidence identities of their shared endpoints out to five, is sufficient once direction and the source predicate are available. In the doubled fixture the direction and predicate can be recovered by the global procedure above. This argument supplies no fixed radius for performing that recovery on raw undirected holdings.

Under the verified initial funding assumption, each readiness wait concerns strictly earlier births. Later funded nonclosing cohorts supply every subsequent ordinary demand, so a new shortfall cannot appear. Consider the ordinary reference structure for the proof. Induction over birth depth shows that if every persistently eligible event is eventually selected, all earlier required holder lists become complete and each later birth is eventually admitted. A new birth cannot invalidate completion of one of those lists. Since the reader sees complete local cohorts, all writes agree with the ordinary reference. An arbitrary order can starve an independent continuing part. Strict global joins-first can also hide a continuously eligible unary when distinct parts advance independently. For example, take an odd source cycle of at least seven live knots, two loose distinctions per knot, and attach a three-meeting chain from one of them to a separate live leaf. The two interior knots of the chain are spent. Double every meeting and give the leaf two loose distinctions. The cycle's part keeps making only pairs and can keep a multi-parent move available, while the leaf's initial unaries remain outside the priority-filtered list. Fairness of that filtered list allows starvation of the leaf. The globally shallowest reader in the measured comparison has a stronger ordering restriction, so it cannot advance indefinitely while leaving an older finite cohort unfinished.

Under not-all-equal the local read suffices for the budget on a funded source. In parity, the exceptional decision of an implied last birth can require a global read. A sufficient additional test is whether the specific inputs of the proposed birth are all current loose distinctions. To see its correctness for the directed rule, place the finite written record inside the ordinary reference by induction. If the proposed birth is the final one, every earlier event of positive supply has a succession path to it, since every allocated distinction is used at the next depth until closure. With its parents present, all such preceding events have been written and just its inputs remain. If the proposed birth is nonfinal, choose another reference birth of its depth. If that birth is absent, trace back to a first absent event with all its parents present. It is distinct from the proposal and uses a different still-loose distinction. If that birth is already present, follow successors until the path leaves the finite record. It does so before any final birth, since that final birth also depends on the unbuilt proposal. Again a different distinction is still loose. Thus the global test works on the maintained partial records, while a bounded directed holder-step read alone can miss a remote continuing part.
