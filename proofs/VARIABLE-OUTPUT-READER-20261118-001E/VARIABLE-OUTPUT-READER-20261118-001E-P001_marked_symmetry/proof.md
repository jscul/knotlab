# What the width-32 swap can hide

**Exact result.** In the particular width-32 table where exchanging distinctions 13 and 14 changes a parity equation, a proposed triple may join precisely when all three distances between its members are six. This holds across every reachable realization of that table. It requires no names, distinguished starting junction, hidden outputs or growth history. The universal question for other tables is open.

## What the distances reveal here

Take all distinctions occurring in a triple of mutual bond distance six. There are six. They can also be recognized as the distinctions with exactly four partners at distance four. Among these six each distinction has one partner at distance three. The other four partners are at distance six. Choosing one member of each distance-three pair gives exactly eight triples. The direct checker tests the predicate against the physical verdict on every one of the 4,960 triples. A one-entry change readily admits a false triple, and renaming all distinctions preserves the answer. [Anonymous reader and controls](read_table32.py)

A separate exhaustive argument rules out a different history producing the same measurements but different permissions. Each junction contributes a parity equation on three to six distinctions. From the full pair table, short bonds force 14 independent equations. At most 15 can fit at this width. There are just 13 candidate equation spaces to test after grouping the remaining possible generators. Only two reproduce all 496 supplied pair entries. Each is actually reachable and one is obtained from the other by the original swap. Their eight legal triples agree. The earlier theorem that the complete allowed states fix the three-parent permission then accounts for every physical realization of either space. The identification of the two spaces uses only the table and the law. [Proof](scalar_inverse.md) · [Exact certificate](realizations32.json) · [Reconstruction](check_32_realizations.py)

For the exact original permutation, acting trivially on fresh distinctions, the earlier exhaustion of the other available witnesses also prevents the exchanged loose bit ever entering a legal triple under later growth. The new complete classification of this one matrix allows arbitrary renamings and arbitrary realizing histories at this width. Unary chains attached through an unused distinction give explicit further families for which the verdict is fully determined. ## What holds at every width

**Four distinctions.** If a distinction belongs to any legal triple, every parity equation on at most four distinctions that contains it is fixed by the full scalar table. This holds even when comparing with a presentation in which the matched distinction might be forbidden. A four-bit bond around three or more ordinary junctions has a special restriction. Its last three junctions form a path with one, one, and two loose distinctions. None of those four can ever belong to a legal triple, even after arbitrary further growth. The remaining four-bit bonds through a live mark are either its own junction or sums of two identifiable three-place junctions. Their complete list follows from its row in the table. Large shores of four-bit bonds also form a single inclusion chain in any realization. [Live-input theorem](live_four.md) · [Why those four distinctions stay ineligible](frontier_exchange.md) · [Chain theorem](four_bonds.md)

As one consequence, in a single arrangement any transposition carrying a legal input fixes all four-place equations individually. When both presentations have every junction of arity at most four, the table through distance three reconstructs every junction. This allows both fresh counts and gives an all-size reader for that subclass. [Transposition and reconstruction corollaries](live_four.md#an-opposing-transposition-needs-higher-arity) · [Mixed-count replays](check_four_reader.py)

The corresponding five-distinction argument fails on a reachable arrangement. Its five loose distinctions form a bond and all ten mutual distances equal four. Two of its ten triples may join. Eight are forbidden. The surrounding table forces the full parity relation, so these opposite verdicts do not yield an isometry of the full table. [Five-bit example and replay](five_frontier.py)

Every pair in a legal triple has bond distance at least four. For a sharper distinction, suppose one member of a legal triple is held by \(u\). A terminal junction \(v\) used a different distinction from \(u\) and still holds all its fresh outputs. Its star gives a bond of size at most the arity of \(v\) through its consumed distinction and each fresh output. Every bond through the live member at \(u\) and the same fresh output is strictly larger. The two entries distinguish a swap even when \(v\) has three parents. If \(u\) itself still holds three loose outputs, its shared inputs cannot individually exchange with any of those outputs under a scalar isometry. ## What a reading on three distinctions adds

Let \(d_k(S)\) be the size of the smallest bond containing all \(k\) members, or infinity when no such bond exists. In the width-32 certificate \(d_3(\{4,11,13\})=10\) and \(d_3(\{4,11,14\})=8\). The swap changes 200 of the 4,960 ternary entries. This illustrates exactly how a three-distinction measurement detects information that every two-distinction measurement misses. The three-parent verdict still agrees in every case. [Proof and measurement](extra_reading.md#a-three-coordinate-discriminator-and-a-sharp-bound-for-a-different-reading) · [Replay](check_extra.py)

There is an unconditional upper bound for the growth law. For every triple it suffices to record whether the least containing bond has size three, four, or another value. For every quintuple record whether it has size five, six, or another value. These readings recover every bond of sizes three through six. Since they include all junction equations, they determine the entire allowed-state relation, hence every three-parent verdict. The proof uses a recognition criterion for the minimal cuts of a graph and proves its required binary obstruction directly. The least sufficient arity for three-parent legality is unknown. [All-size supplemental theorem](extra_reading.md#recognizing-a-bond-from-its-one-bit-deletions)

## Remaining proof obligation

An arbitrary isometry of two full scalar tables descends to corresponding retained parts. It preserves true triples if it transports the entire allowed-state relation. After the four-distinction theorem there are two sources of uncertainty. Five- or six-place generating equations can involve the marks themselves. A changed four-place equation elsewhere might also alter which ordinary junctions witness the marks' meetings. One must rule out the loss of a loose input, a pair witness, or the distinction between three separate witnesses and a single common one under a simultaneous relabeling of the rest of the table. For a negative answer one must give two legal histories, opposite verdicts on matched marks and equality at every original pair entry. This study has neither such a witness nor a completed general proof.

The verdict here concerns unordered triples. Smaller input groups have separate observability results. [Earlier distinct successors with different smaller-input menus](../../../research/VARIABLE-OUTPUT-READER-20261118-001A/first_three.md)
