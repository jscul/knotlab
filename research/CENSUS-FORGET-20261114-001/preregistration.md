## The question

`FREE-TARGET-20261114-001` certified, on a sample, that every way down forgets in 43 of 66 every-path growers. In those, every bond
from a top port to any copy at depth s, wherever its members sit, has at least 2 + ⌊s/r⌋ members. The test is exact and takes
milliseconds. Across the whole census, what share of the families whose first-ray bond provably grows is certified this way?

weighted by the description weighting over the census's provable first-ray growers.

## Population and words

All 12,772 distinct census families whose first-ray class is `grows`, 13,279 of the 223,261 nested draws. The share is over provable
first-ray growers, a selected population, and is said so in every headline. Strata:
- **chains**: one definition, one SELF call, no other self-holding call. 5,190 families, 5,633 draws.
- **non-chains**: 7,582 families, 7,646 draws. These are the 6,925 with two or more SELF calls and the 657 with one SELF call and
  other self-holding calls.

**Certified to grow** means a forgetting certificate (some r with every product of r per-call relations rectangular, decided exactly
on the finite semigroup). At every depth where a bond exists, it then has at least 2 + ⌊s/r⌋ members. A bond exists at every depth
where the first ray has one (L_any ≤ L_ray).

## Settled by construction (stated, not tested)

- **Chains.** L_any = L_ray, and first-ray growth is the population's entry criterion, so chains are certified to grow by definition.
  They are a labelled row, not part of any prediction.
- **Forgetting forces growth on every path.** So the EVERY-RAY gate below is a consistency check of the code and the argument, not
  evidence for the share.
