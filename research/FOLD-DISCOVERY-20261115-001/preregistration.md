# Discover one fold and measure its total cost

On November 15, 2026, the direct pair search met the supplied-fold experiment's continuation screen. Its recurring eight-rule totals were 199 vs 187 primitive trials at depth two, 336 vs 503 at depth three and 716 vs 810 at depth four. The more general factor-first method missed the screen. On the fixed-nine-input control the supplied fold saved no candidate trials. This successor runs the discovery phase with the direct method and its optimized primitive competitor. [Supplied-fold experiment](../FOLD-DEPTH-20261115-001/scaling.md)

The 24 training tasks use four, six and eight essential inputs. Their designer plants a recurrent two-operation relationship in varied formula positions and includes competing relationships. The discovery procedure receives the primitive solver's actual correct returns, stripped of anything but the primitive expression and opaque training ID. It has no phase-one library or target template and gets no input positions, hidden formulas, category labels, or held costs. No task is a naked three-input copy of the desired fold. The 24 subsequent tests are the structures reserved before the supplied-fold run, four matched pairs at two, three and four applications. They share no complete normalized structure with the first-phase panel, and their odd arities differ from the training tasks. Input renamings alone do not constitute new mechanisms.

Use a single empty primitive cache per training arity, shared between tasks of that arity. Inspect every adjacent pair of internal binary operations in each returned program. Abstract the three operands outside the pair, including whole child programs, to three anonymous inputs. Canonicalize the resulting truth function under input permutation. Rank classes by number of different training rules, total occurrences and smaller canonical truth byte. Retain one class supported by at least two rules; otherwise keep an empty library. Produce an executable NAND realization from a deterministic two-operation primitive spelling. The extractor is allowed to consider any AND, OR or XOR in either place. Seeking a three-input, two-operation fragment, and the training-set prevalence of repeated motifs, are investigator choices. No saved fold, prior physical implementation, local placement labels or externally chosen candidate score enter the fitted library.

Bind source and data digests before training. Save training programs and a complete tally of hypotheses. Freeze the checkpoint before opening held rules for scoring. The transferred entry contains only its truth byte, primitive expression and physical circuit. Candidate supports remain with the scientific record. The generic matcher compiles candidate operand roles using only the frozen truth byte. It orders role templates by descending number of nonconstant restricted columns and then the four-column signature, so a different spelling or input ordering of the same learned operation receives the same placement schedule. It may test any raw pair and synthesize the residual function recursively. The equally informed inlined control can make the same calls and expands their constituent primitive operations. A separately supplied, free reference uses the archived seven-NAND fold in the same generic matcher. Its existence is irrelevant to what the discovery procedure selects. The baseline uses the earlier optimized primitive search with its own subproblem and physical-program caches.

On each paid training task include construction, all rejected searches, physical verification, and emitting the return. Charge the complete one-time extraction and export, including discarded fragment candidates and verifying the selected library entry. Scientific copying, trace writing, fixture production, benchmark repeats and independent audit are outside the cost of deploying one solver. The library is the only state transferred from the even-arity training tasks. In the primary held stream, a method may retain computed public subproblems within each arity across tasks. Also measure empty caches on every task. Time entry loading, installation, search, failed checks, physical build and all-row checking. Each method has five single-worker timing repeats in rotating order. Costs for the final table are medians of full stream times. At each task prefix, compare median elapsed times with all required upstream discovery added once to the library arms. If no prefix is cheaper or equal, report the final gap. Compare the counts both on recurring rules and their breaks at each depth. Every test and program failure remains in the workload. Finish the reserved workload independent of its results.
