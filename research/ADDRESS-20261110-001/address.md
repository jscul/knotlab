# Learning chooses which atom holds the leftover

Epic `ADDRESS-20261110-001` (backlog B81, G15; program learning), 2026-11-10, at the coordinator's go-ahead. Pre-registered in
[preregistration.md](preregistration.md). Proof family: `proofs/ADDRESS-20261110-001A/ADDRESS-20261110-001A-P001_a_learned_address_among_three_atoms/`.

## The question

Can learning place the leftover dissonance in any chosen Fano atom, and does `LEARN-RISE-20261110-001`'s post-hoc role swap hold on fresh
cases under a frozen prediction?

## The answer (computed, pre-registered)

- **The role swap is confirmed.** On six K_3 starts that epic did not use, the frozen targeted search carried the leftover to the other
  atom from every one (25 to 36 moves, 4 to 8 learning steps).
- **A learned address.** With three atoms there are two leftover units. From the floor every pair of atoms was reached as their holders,
  on all three line orbits of K_3 (9 of 9): moving a unit into the empty atom took 30 to 43 moves with 8 to 15 learning steps, and at every
  success the two named atoms act as their lines and the third is transparent.

Together with `LAW-HOLDS-20261110-001` (the leftover sits where the law puts it, in every state) and `RISE-ESCAPE-20261110-001` (moving the
state cannot carry it out), the leftovers behave as a memory: written by learning, held by the law, untouched by the point's own motion,
and read off by where the tension sits.

For the owner's picture: the learned network stores something. Which patterns hold the tension is a choice learning makes and keeps.
