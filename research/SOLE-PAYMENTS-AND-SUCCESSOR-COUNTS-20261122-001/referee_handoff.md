For the first proof, trace every ordinary proposal, including a sole parity closure, a funded unary continuation and two newly interchangeable children. Verify that no other live part can later supply a paying parent to this part. Check that the argument covers intermediate multi-parent handoffs, that a fixed initial word can settle an early receipt, and that a failure of physical enrollment contributes no fabricated silent return. Distinguish arrangements supplied in advance from an allocator that can read already filled values. [Structural proof](../../proofs/SOLE-PAYMENTS-AND-SUCCESSOR-COUNTS-20261122-001A/SOLE-PAYMENTS-AND-SUCCESSOR-COUNTS-20261122-001A-P001_sole_payments/proof.md)

For the second proof, compare named payments before and after the first signal and again after two returns. Separate the whole fresh pair, the individual outgoing mark, the new holder’s full word and the count reconstructed by consulting older physical records. Test the change in access when the older incoming marks are explicitly identified. For a reader limited to a fixed number of contacts, check matched ages and genuine successive payments on the same enrollment. Allow ordinary lawful fillings that remove the difference from all currently loose marks. [Tally proof](../../proofs/SOLE-PAYMENTS-AND-SUCCESSOR-COUNTS-20261122-001B/SOLE-PAYMENTS-AND-SUCCESSOR-COUNTS-20261122-001B-P001_successor_tally/proof.md)

An independent finite verification tested 1,906 possible current arrangements of up to four live knots with one or two loose distinctions under each law, retaining 1,064 funding stalls. The arrangements are a check of the ordinary operator on its declared input type. They carry no claim that every abstract input is a reached physical start. In each law 55 binary births used both parents’ sole marks. Parity closed once and 54 continued through a child with only one parent. Under not-all-equal all 55 continued that way. A second check encoded all physical holdings through the first five-founder return and its successor, holding fixed the valued preparation through the third handoff. Matching the successor’s four actual values and its raw view through one contact still permits opposite values of the preceding signal under each law. The two-contact decoder was checked at all five archived positive returns with a following cut present. These are coverage counts.
