**1. The least information.** For any legal, funded birth, list the other births sharing its parents. Use their shared parents to count the distinct successor groups that require the new knot. Call that count *D*. For a birth with *p* parents, the fresh count is **max(D, 3−p, 1)**. The sole closing parity birth gets zero. Once the parent holdings are fixed, demand values that give the same count after applying the floor can be merged. Every other attainable count must be distinguished. If *k* different answers remain possible, the exact fixed-length cost is ⌈log₂ *k*⌉ bits. A unary birth needs no outside information. [Constructive criterion](https://github.com/jscul/knotlab/blob/main/research/FRESH-DEMAND-20261120-001/demand_proof.md)

**One integer suffices, but no fixed number of bits suffices.** Let *H* be the number of loose distinctions left at the parents after paying for this birth. In the class represented by a fixed map, two parents with *u* and *v* loose distinctions and *t* live knots at their common witness give **D = u + v − t**. The worst-case information there grows logarithmically in *H*. Across unrestricted lawful starts it grows linearly in *H*, already with two parents. One constructed pair has 29 distinctions at each parent. Every bond reading on their full holdings agrees, yet the same birth needs **eight versus ten**. A witnessed shared-parent reading supplies the missing incidence within one step in the fixed-map class and two steps on a general funded continuing stage. **2. Does two versus one survive without rounds?** The answer depends on what a birth can read when it occurs.

- **Recompute after every birth, allowing any admissible move.** The difference survives in **some fair orders**. Make U,V first and it gets **one in both starts**. Make its two other initial siblings first and it gets **two with W spent, one with W live**. These are corresponding choices with different actual siblings. Identical preceding actions executable in both starts give one in both. Either start also admits a fair history that permanently loses the exact U,V birth. [All-order proof](https://github.com/jscul/knotlab/blob/main/research/FRESH-DEMAND-20261120-001/serial_proof.md)
- **Recompute after every birth, with joins first.** The difference survives in **no order in which both marked births occur**. The spent-W start must make U,V first. In the live-W start it gets one if it occurs, and a larger birth can consume its inputs first. Every branch resolves within four moves. [Exhaustive replay](https://github.com/jscul/knotlab/blob/main/research/FRESH-DEMAND-20261120-001/serial_orders.md)
- **Use the fixed map, adding a knot once its parents exist.** The difference survives in **every fair completion**. In the fixed structure, U,V has **two immediate extensions** with W spent and **one immediate extension** with W live. Two versus one follows from that fixed structure, with no round or tick. The map supplies the possible extensions before they are built. Merely permuting a precomputed first step also preserves the count in all 5,040 and 120 orders of the respective starts.
