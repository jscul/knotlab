## Tests and proposed proof

Start at I₄ (35 junctions, 105 distinctions) and I₅. Examine exact thresholds through scalar distance five, with all larger entries treated alike. Recovering the complete choices from such a truncation would suffice. A failure of a truncated reader is only a reason to examine the full distances, never a counterexample on its own. Any negative result needs an isometry of the full table on an actual aligned stage that changes an eligible choice. Grade old-distinction allocations under the predecessor's simultaneous rule, not coordinate names. Use independently computed projections, negative controls and a stage one past the finite certificate as appropriate. A literal cube of side three or a freely chosen serial join does not count as one of these stages.

The three loose distinctions at a live junction are permutable. The current proof ideas test whether the threshold graphs locate their groups. Small altered supports may be localized near the original marks by pure projection factorization or by bounding small cuts after the eight marked distinctions are removed. A finite exceptional region together with an exact transfer argument could prove the all-round result. Test false twins, spurious short bonds caused by the restriction, and whether a reconstruction silently uses the hidden alignment to place observed distinctions. Freeze these candidate ideas as proof design before new evidence. A finite positive alone leaves the quantifier over rounds open.
