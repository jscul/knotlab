# Independent referee report on the made copier study
**Confirmed with fixes. No Block to the 17 confirmations or the bounded verdict that no copier was found. The exact renaming lemma is false as stated.**

A made first pattern is called X and the second pattern it meets is called Y. The strongest positive result is about a fixed Y. In 17 cases, two parts of Y’s form, descended from X alone, occurred more often with direct interaction allowed than in each of three controls. All 17 original cases and all 91,300 screening and confirmation continuations were regrown independently. Every retained comparison count matched. The comparisons kept Y’s form fixed. The main prediction of zero confirmations failed. No first form under one law earned confirmations for two second forms, the requirement for testing a copier in fresh growths.
