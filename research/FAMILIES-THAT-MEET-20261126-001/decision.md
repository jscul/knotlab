## What stands

The start throughout is the generator study's 21 knots
([`GENERATORS-20261120-001`](../GENERATORS-20261120-001/decision.md)), with what lies at or beyond the second
family's own original changed by hand, and the first family's side read (every knot with the first family's
original among its original ancestors and nothing of the second's). Laws take nothing.

1. **Under the ordinary count nothing is felt on this start.** A knot is given as many fresh distinctions as the
   joins it is owed, which is 2 plus the sum over its originals of (pairs − 2) (4,662 knots through size eight,
   and 8,918 on a changed start), so no knot is ever short; knots made without the second family's original are
   the same when its pairs go from two to four (1,360 knots of 457 kinds); and the first side is the same for
   every change tried, through 7 to 9 rounds ([first step](first_step/README.md); 27 of 27 pairs in the
   [third](third_step/README.md)). One original short changes nothing (0 of 60), and the record's own capped
   rations change nothing (0 of 12).
2. **Under a cap of any size (a scaffold, A274) what the first family's side makes does differ with what the second
   family is**, when the difference is at the second family's original (caps of 4 to 8: 30 of 30 comparisons,
   [first step](first_step/README.md)), one step beyond it with that original in four pairs (caps of 6 to 8: 11 of
   27, [second step](second_step/README.md)), or one step beyond it with that original in three pairs (caps of 9,
   10 and 12: 26 of 27 pairs; with waiting also caps of 6 and 8, [third step](third_step/README.md)).
3. **Two or three steps beyond, it does not** (0 of 162 pairs, third step).
4. **The cap that makes knots short also parts the families.** A change crosses only through a group that joins
   the two sides and is kept in one start and cancelled in the other; at one step this is named birth for birth
   (48 offshoots). Where short knots retire or make offshoots, no group joins the sides from round c − 1 on, for
   every cap c from 4 to 9 and every start read that far (106 of 106). Given the settled statement that parts
   act alone past their start (an argument, not refereed), two starts whose sides have come apart with the same
   outside stay the same in every later round: 82 of the 162 pairs at two and three steps and 36 at one step.
   Checked by retiring the second family by hand once the sides are apart (7 starts, 180,852 births the same).

**The lead.** B337 asked whether what one family makes depends on which family it meets, and under which laws.
On this start: under the ordinary count, no; under a cap, yes, for a difference at or one step from the second
family's original, made in the first rounds and kept after the families part. Nothing here is a family acting on
another after both exist: the families are joined and never short without a cap, short and soon apart with one.

**Not established.** Anything about another start, or starts drawn under a weighting. Whether a cap of 10 or more
lets a two-step change through (the sides were still joined when the reading stopped). Why the round is c − 1 (see
the link below). What waiting does in the long run: with waiting the sides stay joined past the rounds read at
caps of 6 and up. Anything under a law that reaches further, where parts that have come apart can meet again.
