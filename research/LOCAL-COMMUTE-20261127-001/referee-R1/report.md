# Independent referee report on commutation fixed at birth

**Confirmed with fixes. No Block.**

The birth rule loses precisely ten of the 1,187 odd closes attainable under whole choices. At **each** loss, every odd whole choice uses **exactly two** of the three whole pairs excluded at birth. The state claim needs a quantifier: every structure admits a choice with a state. On a start already closed, some commuting choices are incompatible with prescribed all-even starting signs.

## What holds

I regenerated the 7,075 starts in archive order, weighting each start equally. Of these, 230 were already closed, 1,819 closed during growth, 1,734 stalled, 3,267 were still growing after round four and 25 crossed the 40-knot cap. All deterministic fields matched on 7,075 main rows and 200 supplement rows, including the dimensions of both sets of choices. All ten post hoc records matched. [Recount](census.json)

- **A1.** Deciding pairs from their holders at birth and carrying decisions forward gave zero violations on 22,044 stages of completed histories and 100 within-cap stages of excluded histories. Across the closures, 508 unheld pair occurrences were omitted. In every case both distinctions had been loose at their common birth round. The direct test found zero pair-set discrepancies. [Pair checks](census.json)
- **A2.** The all-commuting choice gives a state everywhere. Even with initially closed starts assigned even signs, 22,039 of the 22,044 stages also admit a nonzero choice. From an open start, any complete commuting choice admits the prescribed signs through closure. Every consistent prefix extended at **all 14,994 funded transitions** of completed histories. At a fixed start, the number of extensions per prefix is constant at each round. Uniform choice per round therefore gives uniform weight to the complete choices. [Argument](arguments.md) · [Projection tests](sequential.json)
- **A3–A5.** Testing old parity laws on 55,519 births in completed histories identified 1,819 implied births. Each alone took every loose distinction and closed the whole. Birth and whole pair sets coincided at all 1,713 first-round closures. [Arguments](arguments.md) · [Counts](census.json)

Whole choices attained odd at all 1,187 lacking that order and at none of the other 632. ## The fixes

1. **Narrowing — state existence and prescribed signs.** Amend “Every birth choice keeps a state at every round.” The README defines a state by the existence of simultaneous eigenvalues. Its all-commuting existence proof is valid and explicit. Prescribing the eigenvalues also requires their product to match every dependence. Of 230 starts already closed, **149** allow a commuting choice with odd total product. Four knots holding `012, 034, 135, 245` (census index 959) illustrate it. Let only `(0,5), (1,4), (2,3)` fail. The four checks commute and multiply to `−I`. They cannot all have eigenvalue `+1`. Fourteen initially closed starts even have this discrepancy under the natural rule. Of 100 seeded draws on initially closed starts, 28 were marked `kept` with odd product. Use “Every structure admits a state under the all-commuting choice. On an already closed start, prescribed signs must multiply to the reordering sign.” Carry this wording into the notebook, both goal summaries and assumption A314’s “Rests on it.” [README A2](../README.md#L66) · [Decision](../decision.md#L111) · [Notebook](../../../docs/KNOT_INTUITION.md#L2354) · [Goal summary](../../../docs/RESEARCH_GOALS.md#L1857) · [Checkpoint](../../../docs/RESEARCH_GOALS.md#L6159) · [A314](../../../docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md#L6520)
2. **Narrowing — what the ten losses require.** Amend “There the odd close needs a pair of start distinctions that round 1 took apart, and a rule that looks no round ahead can't decide that pair.” Each loss has three forbidden whole pairs of round-zero loose distinctions. Every odd whole choice uses one particular pair and exactly one of the other two. On the four-knot start `01236, 045, 14578, 239` (census index 222), the possibilities are `{(6,8),(6,9)}` and `{(6,8),(7,9)}`. In contrast, 7 and 8 were also loose and taken apart, but their first knot already held both, so even whole choices forbid them. On each of the ten, the present holders already certify that one excluded pair cannot be taken together because its holders have no common neighbor. Allowing that pair alone restores **zero** odd closes. Say “The declared held-twice test excludes both pairs needed for an odd witness.” The formal rule is correct. Its informal summary needs “held apart at birth,” and the stronger claim about all rules using present information has no such test. In the comparison with the earlier natural rule, qualify “wherever one member of a pair is held twice” with “when the pair is decided.” The earlier 112 round-zero failures refer to its 134 still-growing starts. A homogeneous restriction cannot force odd. An affine condition with right-hand side one can select odd. No necessity of nonlinear optimization follows.
