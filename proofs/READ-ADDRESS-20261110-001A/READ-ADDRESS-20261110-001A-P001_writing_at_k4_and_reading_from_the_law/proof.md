# Writing at K_4 and reading from the law

Status: Computed (`check.py`), pre-registered in `research/READ-ADDRESS-20261110-001/preregistration.md`.

Words (2026-11-11). Reworded under the owner's language rule: a knot space is now a drawn knot pattern, its points
distinctions and its lines knots, and an atom (a Fano or torus atom) a piece (Intuitions guide, Glossary). The mathematics is
unchanged; the earlier text is in git history.

- **S, one size past (computed).** `ADDRESS-20261110-001`'s frozen targeted search wrote the two leftovers of three Fano pieces into every
  pair of pieces on every knot orbit of K_4 (15 of 15; up to 3,841 configurations). With K_3 the written cases are now 24 of 24.
- **N1, reading from each piece's own signs (computed).** In 23 of the 24 written configurations the holders are exactly the pieces whose
  own signs make them act as their knots; this read uses no state and no census, only each piece's 21 sides. The exception (K_4, l0 = 16,
  pair {0, 2}) ended in a class where only piece 2 acts as its knot, and the second unit sat inside piece 0, which is transparent. So the
  local read is exact when as many pieces act as there are units, and not otherwise.
- **N2, the state alone (computed).** For each written law, 145 to 180 of 1,000 random states have their over-agreement in exactly the
  holder pair: a random state does not show the address.

So the written address can be read natively from the law, piece by piece, when the number of acting pieces matches the number of units.

Against the pre-registration: S and N2 held; N1 held in 23 of 24, with the exception above. Scope: K_3 and K_4, three Fano pieces (A48), the
moves of A50 and A26, at most one unit above the floor (A49). Every count here is computed on these small cases.
