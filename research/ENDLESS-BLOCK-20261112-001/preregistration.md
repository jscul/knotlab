## The question

Join the two readings of the owner's "each boolean variable could be its own micro infinite series of logic, that's bound to others":
make each distinction an endless series, a harmony (A70), and bind the copies by the harmony's own knots, as distinction substitution
binds blocks. What the earlier theorems already decide is stated first; what is open is isolated, above all whether any reach arises
that is not plain connectivity, the owner's "globally influential".

## Words

As in the guide's glossary: harmony, distinction substitution, between-copy knots, spare distinction, part, tie class, lag, holonomy.
Each copy carries the harmony's own knots. The bindings: **every order** (the harmony's knots in every order across three copies, the
pinning theorem's), **every order, lagged** (with lags on members), and **one order** (each knot (x, y, z) of the pattern becomes
NAE((x, i), (y, i + a), (z, i + b)) for every kind (0, a, b) of the harmony and every i: an unlagged-in-form stitch of three harmonies
by their own kinds). A tie is **cycle-born** when it holds on a closed chain of knots and not on the chain unrolled three times, for
either lift of its far end (the advisor's universal-cover criterion, made finite).

## What the theorems already give (proved by hand before the run; with the advisor model)

- **E1 (endless blocks lock completely).** A harmony with spans below its period P has no spare distinction on its cyclic block: a
  state with a residue that could flip alone would give a state with one position flipped, not periodic (`EVEN-PERIOD-20261112-001`,
  lemma 3). So under every order, the pinning theorem ties every copy to every other, place by place: on a connected pattern the nested
  base has exactly the harmony's states, one shared by all copies, and nothing varies along the outer direction.
- **E2 (lags fold by the loops' net lag).** Lags are twists by shifts, symmetries of the cyclic block, so by the twisted-pin lemmas the
  nested base lives exactly when some state of the harmony has least period dividing g = gcd(P, every cycle's net lag), with exactly
  the states fixed by the shift by g; each copy is then tied inside to itself i ~ i + g. These ties inside a copy are born of a whole
  loop (absent on any chain): reach of a kind that is not plain connectivity, but given by definition (holonomy).
- **O1, one order, what is known.** The nested base is a constraint problem over the harmony's states with one relation R (the
  allowed triples of states across one knot); R contains the three equal copies, so it always lives, and no tie inside a copy beyond
  the harmony's own can arise (the advisor's). Whether a single knot ties the copies, and whether loops make cross-copy ties no chain
  makes, is open: the advisor gives a relation of the form "y = z implies x = y" for which odd and even rings differ, a loop-born,
  Tseitin-like tie, but no harmony realising it was exhibited.

## What counts (frozen)

- **Reach that is not plain connectivity, from the binding itself:** a cycle-born tie in the one-order arm (a tie between copies that a
  closed chain forces and its unrolling does not). E2's cycle-born ties inside copies are expected by definition and do not count.
- **None:** no cycle-born tie in the one-order arm on the range.

## Predictions (frozen)

- **P1, P2 (theorems).** E1 and E2 hold on every cell (states and living as predicted).
