# Which draws can release lasting families repeatedly?

A family launches from a productive source if every member at launch has all its parents in that source. Each member at every later cut must have all its parents in the preceding members of that family. A recurrent source has disjoint lasting families starting at arbitrarily late cuts. The model supplies knots, their loose distinctions and every actual parent. A reader specifies which of those true descendants form a family. The results below hold under both parity and not-all-equal. Parent steps give the order of formation.

## A necessary condition on initial meetings

Suppose distinct holders meet pairwise by shared distinctions. Write an ordinary pair once whenever two original holders meet, whatever the multiplicity. Write a separate role for a holder with loose distinctions and a separate role for any original witness to at least two such holders. A live role belongs to a witness exactly when its physical holder meets that witness. In the identified class the first roster is funded, at least two distinctions are loose, two live holders have at most one common witness and no group of three or more is born in the first roster. There the all-size parent theorem identifies each later birth with a connected finite piece on the unrolled incidence, with true single deletions as its parents and the stated finite-piece relays. If each component has at most one independent cycle, fixing an earlier size gives only finitely many compulsory earlier ancestors through which arbitrarily large later pieces must pass. Hence only finitely many disjoint lasting families can launch. The earlier proof deals also with spare distinctions, the two possible directions around a single cycle and the eventual two-ended relay. This bounds unbounded release even when only true-parent membership, without a particular word reading, is imposed. Before spent live roles and witnesses of at most one live holder are discarded, take two roles for every holder and both alternating incidences for every ordinary pair. An ordinary tree gives two trees on the roles. One extra ordinary pair gives one or two single-cycle components with finite attachments. Removing roles or their incidences can increase neither a component's number of independent cycles nor the total in one old component. Duplicate witnesses for a live pair would make a four-role cycle. In the absence of a duplicate, an unheld compatible triple would make a six-role cycle because the witnesses of its three pairs are distinct. A pairwise-compatible unheld group also contains such a triple when pair witnesses are unique. An ordinary tree has neither configuration. The exceptional cases with zero loose distinctions, or with a single one, give respectively no growth, or a sole parity closure and a not-all-equal relay with two eventual one-parent ends. This proves the tree exclusion independently of a particular loose-supply weighting.

With exactly one extra pair, let the ordinary cycle have length g, counting distinct holders. Alternation gives two cycles of length g for even g and one cycle of length 2g for odd g. Unless g is 3, 4 or 6 there is no four- or six-role cycle. Thus even outside a promised class an unrestricted one-extra-pair example would have to be funded on one of those three exceptionally short ordinary cycles and retain the live roles that defeat identification. Extra multiplicity does not help on a tree. Inside the identified class **the exact minimum is two extra distinct pair meetings**. Unrestricted, among connected starts with loose and pairwise-shared distinctions, the established lower and upper bounds are **one and two**. The constructions below use two extra shared distinctions as well as two extra distinct pairs. A single short cycle allowing initial groups with more than two parents still lacks a general all-size obstruction. ## What the original loose supply permits

Give holder v exactly max(0,3 minus its number of ordinary neighbours) loose distinctions and at most one spare. Assume its component is funded and identified at the original cut. Each live role on a cycle belongs to two distinct witnesses. Funding requires a different loose distinction for at least one neighbour through each witness. The same neighbour cannot count for both because the pair has a unique witness. Hence this role has at least two loose distinctions and its ordinary holder has at least two neighbours. The formula forces **exactly** two neighbours and two loose distinctions. Each of these two witnesses must have exactly one other live role, because any further one would require a third payment. All roles on the cycle therefore have degree two in the entire component. There is no attachment and no spare on that cycle. Each identified component is acyclic or a bare single cycle, whatever the number of added original meetings. The previous obstruction applies. An alternative supply is scientifically material even beyond the sizes sampled here. A later reached cut or an original short-overlap component can lie outside this argument. All such components that actually arose under the primary sampled weighting closed or acquired a separate finite bound. ## When two returns prove indefinite release

Use the earlier full-holdings reader. Let A and B be simple alternating returns based at the same live role, compatible in both orders and without immediate retracing. A physical automorphism orbit M of original live holders is contained in A or B collectively. At least one m in M occurs on B and is absent from A. At a given size the source consists of admitted path words for which at least one mark in M lies in no immediately doubled block. Equivalently, the source rejects a word when every mark lies inside some doubled consecutive block. A word and its reverse have the same verdict. Deletions retain membership and arbitrarily long A-only words keep the source productive.

Repeat W=A^k B indefinitely for any integer k≥1. Write p=k|A|+|B|. Every window on this repeated word is a true knot. Nonretracing in the identified map gives exactly its two deletion windows as parents. The mark m occurs once per period. A doubled block containing m requires two m appearances separated by a positive multiple of p, so its length is at least 2p. Every window of size 2p is two identical full periods containing every mark. Thus all phases launch at size 2p, from two true source parents of size 2p−1. Every later phase has its actual parents among its own preceding phases. There are exactly p distinct phases from that size onward, including after reversal is admitted. The unique m prevents a nontrivial rotation. Its two neighbours on the simple B return are distinct, so reversal cannot match a forward rotation. Two different k have different least periods. If two windows past both launches were equal, their common length would be at least p+q for the two periods p≤q. Equality after a shift p on that window tests all q residues of the q-periodic word, making p a period of it, a contradiction when p<q. An A-only word never includes m and remains separate. These arguments prove all future releases for each certificate. The finite replays independently check the first launches and phases. At each new positive the supplied physical holdings let the reader find the original cut. Every new cohort member retains loose distinctions. All earlier loose distinctions were spent and members of the same new cohort have no direct meeting. Remove the current loose cohort until the first cut where two loose holders directly meet. The original all-live input has such a meeting. Since this happens at no positive-age cut, the remaining holdings are the original ones. The reader can reconstruct the earlier true parent windows by their present intersections, with reflection and renaming admitted. All complete physical ties of the least |A|+|B| and then least |A| are returned. A single periodic word can arise from several traversals, marks or descriptions. We identify rotations and reversals before counting its product. Different returned source tests may recognize the same product, which receives one count. The supplied original size, a permanent marker maintained outside the structure and a stored age are unnecessary. The growing-end alternative still identifies both actual lasting tips of a whole witnessed path. It lacks a general recovery theorem for the marked periodic origins. ## Physical examples at the upper bound

In the smallest sampled positive, nine holders have the ten ordinary pairs (0,2), (0,3), (0,5), (1,2), (2,7), (3,8), (4,5), (4,8), (5,6), (6,7). Give holders 0 through 8 respectively 5,2,4,3,3,4,3,3,2 loose distinctions. The 29 loose and 10 shared distinctions total 39. These numerical names only allow replay. The drawn Prüfer sequence was (2,0,0,5,6,5,4), the two added pairs were (3,8) and (2,7), and only holder 2 received a spare coin. One recovered A is (3,2,6,4), one recovered B is (3,5,7,0,4), and holder 0 is its invariant mark. Each cyclic word therefore has width 4k+5 and launches at parent age 8k+9. The first two widths are nine and thirteen at ages 17 and 25. Across the tied admissible choices there are eight distinct packets of each width by age 32. Both law projections and their two- and three-parent-step physical replays are in the receipt.
