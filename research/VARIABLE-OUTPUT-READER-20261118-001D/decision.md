# Which triples can be read from bond distances at every size?

**The distances identify exactly which branches can be removed.** This is proved for every finite arrangement of the parity growth law with two or three fresh distinctions per join. **The full table decides the surviving triples on every requested control. Its sufficiency at arbitrary size remains open.** [Proof and controls](../../proofs/VARIABLE-OUTPUT-READER-20261118-001D/VARIABLE-OUTPUT-READER-20261118-001D-P001_separator_inverse/proof.md)

## Removing a branch

At each distinction \(t\), set it aside and link two other distinctions when \(D(x,y)\ne D(x,t)+D(t,y)-1\). The connected parts of these links are exactly the physical parts left by deleting \(t\). The proof rules out an apparent break inside any one junction. The only short-bond configuration that could cause one has a single exceptional distinction at a three-parent junction. The growth rule supplies either a short path around it or too many crossings for the required bond to exist. [Separator theorem](../../proofs/VARIABLE-OUTPUT-READER-20261118-001D/VARIABLE-OUTPUT-READER-20261118-001D-P001_separator_inverse/separator.md)

Reject a triple containing a separating distinction, or one whose marks lie on different sides. Otherwise retain the part containing all three and its boundary distinctions. The three parents of a legal triple and their three pair witnesses form a six-junction cycle. One shared distinction cannot cut it apart. Any witness for two parents remaining in the part must itself remain there. These two facts preserve the original verdict. Screening the marks first matters because a severed shared distinction becomes locally loose. The controls include 3,101 formerly forbidden choices that become legal if that screen is skipped. [Physical pruning checks](../../proofs/VARIABLE-OUTPUT-READER-20261118-001D/VARIABLE-OUTPUT-READER-20261118-001D-P001_separator_inverse/controls.json)

## The remaining table

We proved an all-size intermediate result. Whenever a renaming carries the same **allowed-state relation** between surviving triples, it carries their legality as well. This holds even when two different histories have produced the presentations and every unmarked boundary role is hidden. Thus a counterexample to the full scalar reader has to preserve all pair minima while changing the allowed states. Some generating parity equation on four, five or six distinctions must be present on one side and absent on the other. [Allowed-state theorem](../../proofs/VARIABLE-OUTPUT-READER-20261118-001D/VARIABLE-OUTPUT-READER-20261118-001D-P001_separator_inverse/inverse.md)

The previously proposed local distance-four parity bit fails already at width 22. One arrangement with no removable branch contains a legal triple and a forbidden triple with the same three candidate rows. The entire surrounding table agrees through distance four under a renaming carrying one triple to the other. The complete marked tables differ. From the mark opposite the shortest internal pair, count distance-five links between distinctions six and seven away. This gives **five for the legal triple and zero for the forbidden one**. Both have 22 pairs at distance four. [Explicit arrangement and bijection](../../proofs/VARIABLE-OUTPUT-READER-20261118-001D/VARIABLE-OUTPUT-READER-20261118-001D-P001_separator_inverse/continuation.json)

At widths 19 and 20 every reachable arrangement passed the full marked comparison. The two published pairs at widths 27 and 28 differed both before and after pruning. Across the four control sizes, all 544,019 individual deletions agreed with physical incidence, and all 8,745 legal surviving choices were separated from the forbidden choices under arbitrary renaming. [Exact control comparison](../../proofs/VARIABLE-OUTPUT-READER-20261118-001D/VARIABLE-OUTPUT-READER-20261118-001D-P001_separator_inverse/controls.json)

At width 20, **24 of 19,414** full tables do lose an allowed-state distinction. Each of the 24 has an empty triple menu. At width 32 the loss occurs inside one retained part with eight legal triples. Swapping two distinctions fixes all 496 distances while changing a parity equation on six of them. Every symmetry of this table still preserves exactly the eight legal triples. This gives a concrete example of how much the distance reader is allowed to forget. A table can sometimes settle its own case. For each pair, list the short supports consistent with its measured minimum. If only one remains, its parity equation is forced. Use those equations to eliminate impossible supports. If all possible generating equations are accounted for, the table has determined the full allowed-state relation and hence the verdict. This succeeds on every one of the 2,167 width-19 and width-20 arrangements with a legal triple and their retained parts. The outstanding case is whether the lost equation can change membership of a marked triple. An example would require one isometry matching every retained entry, followed by the same comparison on the original matrices.
