# What can change at a paid join when the marked meeting looks identical?

The same complete radius-two record can keep a paid four-parent birth after a distant meeting is added, while the global rule loses the birth and the marked live donors have no later common descendant. A different distant edit can preserve the focal birth under the local rule and change a sibling's fresh grant. A distant deficit can also veto every birth. The three comparisons use actual true parents under both laws. [Protocol](preregistration.md) · [The rule and its scope](local_permission.md)

Every live original has six private distinctions. The two queries are the terminal live holders, one directly touching w and one touching z. The matched second preparation adds exactly one new shared distinction between the two far ends, retaining every previous holding and every private name. The alternative around e now has 2L+1 other meetings. For L≥3 both edited endpoints lie strictly beyond the registered radius. Every internal incidence, parallel identity and exact shared/loose count is the same. Independent permutations of all holders and names preserve this rooted isomorphism.

For L=3 the local rule gives both preparations the same four paying parents and, under its common fixed assignment, the same four actual input names 10, 16, 23 and 41. The four extra charges vanish, yielding four additional unaries and removing that single four-parent birth. The width rises from 39 to 42 and the total fresh grant rises from 236 to 240. The permitted child has the two marked donor ancestries at step one. ## One added private distinction on a paying branch

At w and z have two distinct live neighbors each, (A,A′) and (B,B′). Keep the true four-parent group G. From A attach an alternating sequence of h distinct spent witnesses and h live originals X₁,…,Xₕ. Each spent witness meets its preceding live holder once and its following Xᵢ twice, retaining all three named distinctions. Give A three loose names, A′,B,B′ and each nonterminal Xᵢ two, and Xₕ one. Add one new private distinction only to Xₕ in the edited arm. Its distance from the nearer spent root is 1+2h. The whole annotated radius-two record at e is unchanged even for h=1. Distinct initial live holders do not meet physically. The only initial added group is G, and no added group can act afterward. All plans in the compared prefixes fund.

Write L={A,A′}, R={B,B′} and Yᵢ={Xᵢ₋₁,Xᵢ}, with X₀=A. The first rosters under the local policy comprise exactly L,R,G,Y₁,…,Yₕ. The sole extra edited child U is unary at Xₕ. These groups use every original private opening. At age one G shares an original paying parent with L,R and Y₁. The three G,L,Y₁ share A and give separate ordinary pairs, since that triple is held by A. The remaining Yᵢ form a sequence by common old parents; U meets the end Yₕ in the co-input reading. G consequently receives three fresh under both laws in both arms. For h=1 the other child Y₁ paid by A, also paid by the edited holder, receives two fresh in the base arm and three after the edit. Both its old inputs match under the registered coupling.

**Sharp first unmatched step for every h.** At age one the terminal's inclusive descendants are T={Yₕ} in the original and T={Yₕ,U} after the edit. Multis through any outside member, their complete parental identities and the outside loose counts all match. The argument is direct here: U is a co-input neighbor only of Yₕ, while the original first grants change only through Xₕ. The pre-existing ordinary matching induction therefore applies from this cut. Its protection holds until the outside receiver acquires an ancestor in T, with one more step before an unmatched receiver birth. The co-input distances to T from the children of A and from G are h−1 and h respectively. When h=1 A already has a child in T, and direct inspection of the first funded rosters shows no unmatched A child. For upper bounds, U itself has no matched counterpart. **Why G's own first grant cannot see this private edit.** On any funded start under the bounded rule, add one private distinction to an already live X while holding all old sharings unchanged. Current groups are unchanged. Under the common port assignment all old first parent rosters remain and one extra unary U appears at X. Take any matched first birth G with X outside its true parents. The provisional holders differ by that unary leaf, an extra private placeholder and private-slot relabeling. The ordinary co-input neighbors of G are exactly children sharing one of its true parents. U shares none of them, and the remaining neighbors' complete parent sets stay fixed. Hence every ordinary group through G is unchanged. For an age-one additional name f, both spent ends already had some child whenever they do in the edited arm: every previously live parent paid its whole positive supply. The new unary is pendant, so cannot shorten or create a residual route between two old holders. It cannot touch both sides of f. Every previously admissible f stays admissible and none is newly admitted. G belongs to f's union exactly when it is a child of one of f's ends. Even when U joins an existing union, its one charge to G persists. The distinctness proof for authorizers excludes group splitting, merging or duplication with an ordinary group. The number of multi-parent demands through G and its input count are therefore equal. G cannot be the sole closing birth when a different live X must also pay. Its actual first grant is identical. This statement concerns a +1 at an already live holder on two funded preparations; a changed physical meeting or a whole-cut veto has different consequences. ## A necessary payment can still be far away

Take the four-section sharp-pace tree and remove one loose name from the live holder joining the third section to the fourth. Its arity remains three. Both arms have identical complete radius-two records at the original spent meeting of section one and identical local eligibility. With full funding the first cut pays 12 births and grants 28 fresh distinctions. The central added group's grant is three. In the altered specimen one missing input at holder 15 vetoes the *entire* cut under both added rules. Ordinary growth funds both arms. Local eligibility and all four central donors' supplies persist in the altered specimen, but no child actually forms. The previous rule already had this global funding condition.
