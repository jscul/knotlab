# What the law reads along a nested pattern's levels: pre-registration

Epic `NEST-READ-20261112-001` (`SHORT-DESC-20261112-001` direction 1; G17, with G7), 2026-11-12, at the coordinator's go-ahead.
Frozen before the registered run. The canonical K1–K3 test is unchanged. The law is not-all-equal (NAE) on three-place knots; the
weighting is SHORT-DESC's description code (A79, one weighting of many, A71), conditioned as below.

## The question

In a self-nested pattern (a knot holding its definition's pattern inside itself, level below level), does the law bond a top
distinction to distinctions many levels down through a few others, and does that grow, hold or die with level? Two variants of the
owner's open question: the inner pattern shares distinctions with the outside **only through the knot** that holds it, or **also
directly**. After SHORT-DESC's referee: separate what monotonicity settles from what is measured, and look mainly where the
definition constrains its ports more than a knot.

## In standard terms

For satisfiable positive NAE-3-SAT instances generated by iterated hyperedge replacement, the least size (up to 5) of a minimal
non-shattered vertex set containing a root vertex and a vertex created at replacement depth s, against hypergraph distance and s.

## Words and design (frozen in `census.py`)

- **Variants.** *knot*: SELF only in definitions with three ports (SHORT-DESC's main). *direct*: SELF only in definitions with four
  to six ports; the holding knot is the call's first three arguments (at depth 1 SELF is that knot), and the inner pattern also
  receives the other arguments.
- **Strata.** *Constraining*: some SELF definition, with SELF read as its holding knot, allows on its ports less than that knot
  does (its relation does not contain the knot's, all k ports, the knot on the first three). *Monotone*: otherwise. *Tied* (a
  flag): some SELF definition's states on its first three ports fall in at most two complementary pairs.
- **Selection.** Families by fair coins; each read at the largest depth t ≤ 12 with at most 60 distinctions; kept when alive, with
  at least 8 distinctions, one at level 0, and distinctions at level 4 or more. This keeps slow-growing, mostly chain-like families;
  keep rates are reported per stratum and every claim is scoped to them. Quotas per worker: 60 constraining, 30 monotone; 8
  workers per variant.
- **Level** of a distinction: the number of SELF substitutions above the call that made it.
- **Readings.** One random level-0 distinction u; up to two random v per level. Steps apart d (unreachable counted apart); least
  bond L containing u and v, exact up to five members over all distinctions (the frozen RANDOM-FATES reader), else none within
  five. A **tie-free shortcut**: 3 ≤ L ≤ 5 and L < d + 2. **Baseline**: a uniform pattern with the same n and m (SHORT-DESC's
  replace-one-knot chain, alive), one pair at each nested pair's steps apart.

## Settled by construction or by known results (stated, not tested)

- Steps apart are set by the substitution, not by the law.
- Patterns with no cycle of knots have L = d + 2 (`RANDOM-FATES-20261112-001B`, proved): a **code control** (0 violations, else
  the run is invalid), not a prediction.
- Under a ruler reading (L = d + 2), d ≤ 3 means a bond within five and d ≥ 4 means none: the cap restates the steps, so P2 is
  tested on monotone families with a cycle of knots only.
- A tied port relation ties those ports in every copy below, so ties (L = 2) across levels are forced there; ties are reported
  apart and the main test uses tie-free bonds.
- In monotone families every depth allows on the outer distinctions at least what depth 1 allows; in constraining families it can
  allow less. Influence from deep levels can narrow the top only in the second kind.
