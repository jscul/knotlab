# Can a knot tell its parents from its children by what is built near it? Synthesis

This synthesis binds the one ticket of `PARENTS-FROM-HOLDERS-20261124-001` and the thread's checks to their records. It contributes no further proof or
experiment. All sources are referee pending.

- [Ticket A](../../../research/PARENTS-FROM-HOLDERS-20261124-001A/results.md): a search, on the structures of the one-birth-at-a-time study and on the same grown further, for two shared places, one leading to a parent and one not, that are alike within d holder steps. Exact computation, with one proof by hand confirmed.
- [The thread's checks](../../../research/PARENTS-FROM-HOLDERS-20261124-001/checks/thread_checks.txt): the proved threshold at four rounds by a separate program, and the finished structure of 16 knots with its renaming.

Taken together ([decision](../../../research/PARENTS-FROM-HOLDERS-20261124-001/decision.md)): in half-built states of births made one at a time some
place to a parent and some place to a child are exchanged by a renaming of the whole structure, so the record of parents
cannot be replaced by any look at what is built; on complete rounds a rule of two holder steps exists where a short knot
retires and none of any fixed distance where it makes offshoots; one finished structure keeps no trace of its order.
