## What stands

1. **Growth sorts facts into two kinds** (claim T, the advisor's; computed). The start's families (live knots joined
   when some knot shares a distinction with each) carry facts that last: the least change flipping one grows by at
   least two distinctions a round. On 2,349 starts read no birth mixed families and every other fact checked stayed
   bounded in the sense tested (the count of class facts at the scale of five did not change between rounds 3 and 4).
2. **One relation per connected start** (proved: a sum of laws supported on loose distinctions is all the laws of a
   connected part or none). The families' facts of a connected start obey exactly the sum of all its laws: one
   second-layer knot, the composite of all its knots.
3. **So the step does not repeat under ordinary growth.** Second-layer knots that share a fact would need two relations
   among lasting facts; none of 2,349 starts has them, and by point 2 none can. A third layer can sit only in the
   start's own closed changes, which growth leaves as they were.
4. **Held by no part, at a scale.** At round 4 and the scale one below the families' least flipping change, the bound
   facts of 10 of 12 three-ring families read are held by no part; on the other two (paired attachments, rings of
   five and seven) connecting knots' own facts are still class facts at that scale. This refines the narrowing
   logged today: at small scale the ring bindings are sums of single-knot facts; at a large enough scale they are held by
   no part where read.
5. **On the owner's rungs.** Repetition: met. The tower's first step: met (one second-layer knot per connected start,
   held by no part at a large enough scale where read). The tower's every step: not by ordinary growth (the reason is
   point 2). Recursion: not shown; the two leads meet the same one-relation limit (recursion note).

**Not shown.** A proof of claim T (only the one-relation part is proved); the forbidding cuts, capped groups, rounds
past 4; whether lineages split during growth (a nested release); the advisor's closed 32-knot candidate.
