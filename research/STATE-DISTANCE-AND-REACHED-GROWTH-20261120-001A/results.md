# Does near in the states mean soon in growth?

Two three-place knots sharing one distinction are one holder apart in the not-all-equal state ruler, yet their descendants never meet. The two tested changes of join rule can recover half the ordinary holder distance, rounded up, on funded all-live trees. Neither gives one arrival function of the frozen state distances on **all** funded acyclic starts.

John's founding premise is “Distance is the only form of information.” His note on the law is that it “can be thought of as 'the thing that comes before logic'. Unless the law itself can be derived through logic somehow (which seems like it'd be difficult to test)”.
