# A directed pulse in one parity state

**On a homogeneous chain of four-distinction responses, three occupied responses and ten changed physical distinctions suffice to give a compact state its own direction. Four individual least responses suffice to move a copy by one place. Those three costs are minimal in this code, and every travelling pulse in the stated class reaches a physical weight of at least 14.** The registered four-phase rule chooses the next firing from the present bits. On a bare ring of 31 places the pulse returns after 124 firings. On the bare ring of five places every closed state at size two has a reflection that prevents such a shift. ## What is stored in a state

Fix the accumulated stage at size two and pin its loose bits to zero. Work on a simple source with no first-round group larger than a pair and no two distinct ordinary neighbors witnessing the same pair of parents. Each two-parent child gives one four-distinction circuit, formed by its two inputs and the two old distinctions through the parents' common neighbor. Its two inputs are private to this circuit among the designated four-responses. A unary child contributes no closed change. These statements apply to the simple path and bare rings under discussion. On the path, write \(Q_i\) for the four-response centered on the \(i\)-th ordinary background junction. Neighboring responses share one physical distinction. All other pairs are disjoint. Indices are used here by the proof and evaluator. The rule reads the anonymous response-overlap relation and the two private bits at the unique loose-holding corner of each response. [Earlier least-response proof](../EDDY-WORLD-20261119-001/world_readings.md) · [Literal stage construction](../../experiments/MOTION-AT-EDDY-20261119-001/MOTION-AT-EDDY-20261119-001-E001_motion/motion_stage.py)

On **any closed allowed state** in this setting, the two private bits at a two-parent child have the same value, by parity at that child. At a unary child the single input must be zero. Let \(P\) be the set of responses with private value one. Subtracting the circuits indexed by \(P\) clears every input to a new child and leaves an even set \(Z\) entirely on the old ordinary source. Thus the unique decomposition is

\[
X=Z\mathbin{\triangle}\mathop{\triangle}_{i\in P}Q_i.
\]

Conversely every such state is allowed. The path source is a tree, so \(Z\) is empty and the code gives all closed states. A bare ring has one extra binary choice, its entire old rim. A general initial graph can carry several independent ordinary cycles. The update changes only \(P\) and preserves \(Z\). This is how to run the same coefficient detector when the physical state contains a preexisting circulation. On arbitrary open allowed states a private pair can disagree because of the loose bits, and the stated local decoder rejects such a pair. With a declared allowed reference state \(B\) one may instead read \(X\mathbin{\triangle}B\), provided that difference has the required local private-bit values. In the primary experiment the reference is the all-zero allowed assignment.

## Four phases, with an inverse

On an otherwise empty unbranched stretch write the four occupied sets and the response fired as

| Phase | Occupied positions relative to \(i\) | Fired position relative to \(i\) | Physical weight in the path code |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| \(A\) | \(0,1,4\) | 2 | 10 |
| \(B\) | \(0,1,2,4\) | 5 | 12 |
| \(C\) | \(0,1,2,4,5\) | 4 | 14 |
| \(D\) | \(0,1,2,5\) | 0 | 12 |

Test both reflected templates around the proposed firing. Its response ball of radius six must be a path, allowing truncation at a true end as registered. All unlisted positions in this ball must be empty. Every phase has an occupied response within one of its proposed firing. Each entire packet has span at most five. Therefore any forward match on a clean single packet sees the entire true packet. The same statement holds for the corresponding post-firing templates. The ordered successive gap lists are \(1,3\), \(1,1,2\), \(1,1,2,1\), and \(1,1,3\). Each list differs from its reverse, and no two lists coincide up to reversal. A match on the regular interior thus determines both phase and orientation from the state, with exactly one forward firing. Flipping the four physical distinctions at the indicated response successively gives \(B_i,C_i,D_i,A_{i+1}\). The reflected state travels the other way. A neighboring copy of a *single* four-response differs on six physical distinctions, as in the earlier study. For this directed three-response packet the two successive \(A\) copies differ on 14 physical distinctions. Each of the four intermediate firings changes exactly four.

The reverse rule checks the four post-firing words around those same sites and toggles the indicated response. Each clean packet has one inverse event and each forward step is undone by that inverse. At a specified site, taking the union of its forward and reverse guards defines an involution on physical allowed states: the toggle changes only that coefficient, leaves every other private pair and the static neighborhood fixed, and exchanges its local pre- and post-word. Reversibility of the directed clean orbit does not require the inverse event to be a second enabled *forward* event. If an undirected involution must remain enabled in the very same forward rule and there is always only one enabled event, the next firing necessarily undoes the last. This alternative permits at most a two-state orbit.

One tested update is one local firing. The table contains no counter, past state or named destination. A single clean packet has one forward successor wherever the four required path windows fit. Delaying that event changes its waiting time, so no duration in physical time follows. Two well-separated enabled sites commute when neither toggle changes any bit read by the other's guard. Distinct overlapping guards need not commute. A unique directed orbit on one packet is a more limited property than order independence for all allowed states. In the homogeneous 31-ring all radius-six balls are paths and the blank complementary arc distinguishes the finite span. There are 31 positions and four distinct phases in each direction, giving exact directed period 124 and two disjoint reflected orbits. Grading the shifts uses fixed physical names or marked landmarks that the rule cannot read. After quotienting an unmarked homogeneous ring by all rotations a completed translated copy is the same shape.

## Exact minima and what can break them

Let \(T\) translate the infinite homogeneous chain by one response. A nonempty finite set \(P\) stabilized by a reflection cannot be sent to a nontrivial translate of itself by a deterministic relabeling-equivariant rule or an iterate of it. Indeed the same reflection would fix both \(P\) and \(T^dP\), so the product of that reflection and its conjugate would stabilize \(P\) by the nonzero translation \(2d\). Finite nonempty support excludes this. This also applies in a finite corridor while the complete local data used along the comparison admit the same reflection. Named fixed landmarks can grade the result without becoming available to the guard. An actual structural asymmetry read by the guard changes the hypothesis.

Any one- or two-position set on a line has a reflection. A single consecutive run has one as well. Hence a compact word carrying its own direction requires at least three occupied responses and at least two runs. If there are \(k\) occupied responses in \(r\) runs, there are \(k-r\) neighboring occupied pairs. Each such pair cancels one real distinction twice in the parity sum, giving

\[
|X|=4k-2(k-r)=2k+2r\ge10 .
\]

This is the physical weight relative to the zero reference in the pure path code. For any non-full cyclic word the same count holds with its cyclic runs. The additional old-rim sector on a bare \(N\)-ring instead has weight \(2k+N-2r\ge N\): the private contribution remains \(2k\) and the rim bits are complemented. On \(N=31\) this second sector cannot improve the ten-bit minimum. On a lawful bare seven-ring, adding the old rim to coefficients \(\{0,1,3\}\) gives physical weight nine and a chiral state, since the three cyclic gaps \(1,2,4\) are distinct. That state tests the scope of the lower bound. Chirality by itself does not supply its rule or a motion.

Translating any finite word one place changes exactly \(2r\) coefficient positions, the two run boundaries for each run. Independence of the private pairs forces at least \(2r\) primitive four-response firings between the two copies. The four-phase pulse attains four. Its four distinct phase shapes are also minimal on the infinite regular line for a deterministic repeatable one-place shift. If the first repeat of a shape up to translation and reflection occurred after \(p<M\) of the \(M\) firings for a complete one-place copy, the induced deterministic orbit of shapes would have period \(p\) dividing \(M\). The same spatial isometry \(g\) would act after each \(p\) firings. The initial finite chiral pattern has trivial stabilizer, so \(g^{M/p}\) would be a unit translation. An integer translation of absolute displacement one is no proper power of a line isometry. Thus \(p=M\ge4\). This integer argument does not assert the same phase lower bound for a finite ring with wraparound.

A further bound beyond the registration gives the **minimum peak physical weight of 14** for any nonzero translation of a finite pure-chain state under a deterministic equivariant law using one primitive per event. Every intermediate state must be chiral, meaning that it has no fixing reflection. A reflection of an intermediate state would persist through the remaining events and fix the chiral final copy. Suppose that all physical weights were below 14. The even weight formula permits only three or four occupied positions in a chiral state. With four positions there must be two runs. Two runs of length two always have a reflection, so the four positions form a consecutive triple and a separate singleton. Mark that singleton. Every three-position word adjacent by one toggle to such a four-position word either has a consecutive pair and the same separate singleton, or has three isolated positions. In the first case the pair must lie within the neighboring four-word’s consecutive triple, so we can recover its singleton from the three-word. In the second case the three-word has a pair at separation two, obtained by deleting the middle of the triple. There can be only one such pair, since two would make an equally spaced reflection-symmetric triple. Again the remaining singleton identifies the same position. Removing the four-word’s singleton would leave a reflection-symmetric triple and cannot occur. Thus across every allowed three-to-four or four-to-three step, a uniquely defined physical position stays occupied and fixed. Translation must carry that intrinsic position with the packet, which rules out every nonzero displacement. This proof places no bound on what the guard can read. It establishes a minimum peak physical weight in this code, independently of the ten-bit minimum for a single directed state.

A fully symmetric five-ring shows a further limit, even without a locality restriction on the guard. Every subset of its five response positions is fixed by a reflection. This follows directly for at most two positions, and taking complements covers larger subsets. The extra old rim is fixed by every reflection, so **every allowed state with loose bits zero at size two** has a reflection. For a non-rotation-invariant state its one-place rotation cannot share that reflection, since the product of two appropriately conjugated reflections is a nontrivial rotation of the prime five-cycle. No deterministic incidence-equivariant state law can take that state to its one-place rotation after any number of steps. The same obstruction applies to an old-supported packet on a later stage carrying an extension of that reflection. Decorated rings, active loose distinctions, a carried arrival, and other later states require their own symmetry test. At a junction with several exits the corresponding statement is that the selected single exit must be fixed by the stabilizer of all the local data read. An orbit of two interchangeable exits supplies no unique choice between them.

## Why simpler readers can fail

For any trigger that sees only \(|X\cap Q|\), one uniform subset of the five possible values is allowed to fire. The following argument treats all 32 triggers on an otherwise empty homogeneous line. If zero is enabled the blank region supplies many events. If one is enabled, the empty responses just outside the two extremes each share exactly one active distinction with the packet and both can fire. Otherwise only counts two, three and four can fire. A count-two toggle remains count two; if its successor still has exactly one forward event, that event reverses the toggle. Counts three and four strictly decrease physical Hamming weight. Sustained unique translation returning to the same weight is impossible for every such trigger. On a finite ring the same reasoning applies while the exterior blank stretch is long enough to supply the relevant two exterior matches. The registered pattern guards inspect more of the current state.

An even stronger radius-one obstruction applies to any reflected binary-coefficient reader with one enabled site. A birth outside either extreme sees all zeros, or just the nearest occupied neighbor, and the opposite side of the finite packet has the same reflected view. Such a birth cannot be unique. The occupied hull cannot advance past its old extremes under any number of such events.

## Speeds and covariance of the receiving state

On the homogeneous path one completed copy of the registered word travels one response place, hence one original center-to-center meeting step, per four primitive firings. A primitive firing has Hamming size four and its own maximum distinction-step reach is two. These are different units. For any unique homogeneous reflection-invariant rule of fixed coefficient-reading radius \(s\ge1\), a translation by \(d\) places in \(M\) primitive toggles satisfies

\[
|d|\le (s-1)M/2
\]

on the otherwise empty infinite line. A new rightmost occupied position more than \(s\) beyond the old one sees the blank neighborhood. At displacement exactly \(s\) it sees a single one at the neighborhood boundary, also seen reflected \(s\) beyond the opposite extreme. Neither view can give a unique forward event. Each actual addition can therefore advance the rightmost occupied position by at most \(s-1\). A return to the same population has \(M/2\) additions. The leftmost position gives the other sign. This bounds mean displacement given the reading radius. It is not claimed sharp at each radius. Local dependency of an event through \(h\) successive stages of a parallel read has radius at most \(hs\) in the coefficient graph if only radius-\(s\) data are read at each stage. Parallel stages and individual firings are different measures.

The four-event minimum for a *unit* translated copy gives no uniform \(1/4\) mean speed across candidate rules. For any stride \(a\ge1\), multiply all four mask offsets and toggle offsets by \(a\), require intervening coefficients to be zero, and read radius \(6a\). The same gap and guard argument now gives a translation by \(a\) per four individual firings. The reading radius and physical span grew with \(a\); no new intermediate distinction is created or updated along the skipped sites. There is no radius-independent speed across this family and no inferred physical time.

The one-state code declares an allowed reference for the bit polarity. If every translation of the parity solution space \(X\mapsto X\triangle Z\) must instead be a symmetry of a one-state law \(F\), then

\[
F(X)=X\triangle F(0),\qquad F^2(X)=X .
\]

The formula follows by translating the zero assignment and then applying the same translation twice. A selected unique allowed change cannot depend on the present state under this larger group. Keeping a second allowed **reference** state \(B\) and reading \(X\triangle B\) restores covariance under simultaneous translations of both states. The four-phase event toggles \(X\) and retains \(B\), so it still costs one primitive per firing. This declares more stored information than the primary incidence-only construction.

Two *consecutive* physical states are a different resource. With known zero, two singleton pulse positions \(Q_{i-1},Q_i\) name an incoming direction. On a degree-two corridor the other neighbor is then determined. Updating the second physical pulse to \(Q_{i+1}\) takes two primitive toggles, removing \(Q_i\) and adding \(Q_{i+1}\), or one explicitly composite event. The intermediate state and the arrival record must be specified if the constituents are fired separately. The resulting two-pulse position rule is a nonbacktracking description, with a unique inverse before an end or ambiguity. A token that stores just a local arrival port is another implementation of this directed information.

Under full simultaneous parity translations, the next *single primitive change* selected by a rule on two chronological frames can depend only on their difference. After one elementary event that difference is a single \(Q_i\), with its stabilizing reflection. On the undecorated infinite line only \(Q_i\) itself is fixed by that reflection, so two chronological frames cannot sustain a directed sequence of distinct elementary changes in this class. Three chronological physical states suffice. When \(X_0\triangle X_1=Q_{i-1}\) and \(X_1\triangle X_2=Q_i\), let \(Q_{i+1}\) be the other neighbor of \(Q_i\). Map

\[
(X_0,X_1,X_2)\longmapsto(X_1,X_2,X_2\triangle Q_{i+1}).
\]

The two ordered differences survive simultaneous translations. The same nonbacktracking test in reverse recovers \(X_0\) from the output. The recent change advances one response per firing, while the underlying physical state may retain a trail. On the 31-ring the changed-response sequence returns after 31 firings and the complete three-state record after 62. This minimal three-frame statement counts consecutive full states under this exact covariance and a single primitive per event. The register storing only an arrival port, the fixed-reference pair, and a synchronous recurrence would have different state types and costs.

## Evidence

The proof-side checker tested all 32,768 assignments to the 15 ordinary distinctions of the actual bare-five stage with its loose bits zero. Exactly 64 are allowed. Four are fixed by all five reflections and each of the other 60 by exactly one. Every nonconstant coefficient assignment fails the same-axis reflection test after one-place rotation. On the literal 31-ring the two reflected four-phase orbits comprise 248 distinct allowed physical states, 124 per orbit. The checker also verifies reverse three-frame updates and covariance under a simultaneous nonzero allowed offset, the seven-ring scope control, and strides \(1,2,4\). The peak-weight control checked all 260 and 2,294 neighboring pairs of chiral words with weight at most 12 on rings of 13 and 31 places, respectively. Every pair retained the predicted singleton. The local implementation and its finite search are recorded separately. The earlier EDDY-WORLD study supplied the four-distinction circuits and permissible switches. The staged support, its autonomous guards, their resource bounds and their symmetry limitations are new here. No feedback from these allowed states into the stationary ring's growth was added.
