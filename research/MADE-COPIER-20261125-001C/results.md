**Sampling does hit cases, and none is a copier. In 17 sampled growths, after a pattern the growth made joins a
second part, two parts of the second part's form are met more often than with the second part barred, than the
second part leaves by itself, and than with the two unable to meet; each is confirmed at the registered level on
1,000 fresh continuations. The main prediction was that there would be none. But under each law every first-pattern
form with confirmed cases has them for one form of second pattern only, so there is no candidate for a copier and
the second stage did not run. And in all 17 the meeting runs through a start knot: by the study's own third
question each is supplied by the start, not made by the growth.**

The words. X is a pattern the growth made and Y a part wholly apart from it that the growth then makes one part
with it. The **form reading** is met when two parts the same as Y are present afterwards, Y's form not being X's,
each with every member descended from X only. A case **passes at 100** when that is met more often in 100
continuations with nothing barred than with Y barred and than Y leaves alone; it is **confirmed** when 1,000 fresh
continuations also put it above the fully masked control, at levels that hold the chance of any false confirmation
to 0.01 over its family of runs. A **candidate** is one first-pattern form with confirmed cases for two forms of
second pattern. Takes are sampled take by take; starts are the census of three to five knots.

## How often

- **Where a law has confirmed cases.** The ration of two with retiring and the take one parent step back, under
  parity: 3 growths of 1,000, 0.30% under the census weighting (each start once, one sampled growth a start). The
  cap of two with retiring and the same take, under not-all-equal: 1 growth of 30.
- **Where it has none**, each law separately, at 0.95, the rate of growths holding a case that passes at 100 is below
  0.36% (census weighting) and 0.55% (labelled weighting) under the take from a met knot under parity; 0.30% and
  0.46% under the take one parent step back under parity; 1.2% and 1.9% under the take from a met knot under
  not-all-equal; 10% and 18% under the take one parent step back under not-all-equal, where only 30 starts were run.

## What this shows and does not show

- **The owner's "statistically hit" does happen, for a weaker thing than a copier.** Sampled growths do hold a made
  pattern after whose joining two of the second part's form are met more often. Each such pattern does it for one
  form of second part.
- **The meetings are the start's.** In every confirmed case a start knot is among the linking knots. A meeting made
  only by births was not seen in a confirmed case.
- **Under the laws of ticket A's two taking rules, the take from a met knot gives none**; the cases are under takes
  that go by parent steps, which reach back toward the start.
- **Supplied**: the census, the family of laws, both taking rules and their swaps, the weightings of takes, the
  bounds, the levels. **Made by births**: both patterns of every case.
- No claim about living things, minds, physics or time. DNA and a primordial soup are the owner's pictures.
