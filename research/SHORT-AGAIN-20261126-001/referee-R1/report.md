## What holds

The study closed at `b72f027d6e`. Here *own* means every starting ancestor belongs to the receiver. A *hearing* is the first round in which two growths differ in the receiver's own births, comparing parent-derived names, fresh distinctions and number of places. A *switch* is a different comparison, the first departure of an unchanged start from **round number plus two** own births. The count *d* is the number of joins between the two sites. The made join-only ration reduces the supply of fresh distinctions at joins. The made all-birth ration also reduces it at offshoots, with a floor of one fresh distinction.
