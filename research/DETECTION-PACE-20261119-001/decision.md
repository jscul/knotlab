**There is no single detection pace forced by growth.** Four old knot steps from the loaded loop, a mark can first tell at layer **one**, **two**, or **three**, depending on what it can inspect. The occupied distinctions stay on the loop. What becomes detectable farther out is a change in which knots form. Layers count growth, without assigning elapsed time.
