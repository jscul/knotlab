# What the nearness of a parity stage can select

The growth law fixes a collection of knots and the joint assignments they allow. One can ask which changes between two allowed assignments to admit, which can follow which, or which orderings of those changes mean the same thing. These are different questions. The existing motion rule made decisions in all three places. Numerical speeds below count knot steps per successful event. No update scheduler supplies elapsed time. ## A renaming can constrain a route

Take the induced neighborhood of a knot, keep its center, and forget all the names. Seek an unordered partition of its incident meetings into pairs that **every** renaming of this neighborhood preserves. Equivalently the map sending a meeting to its partner must undo itself, have no fixed meeting and commute with every such renaming. This criterion makes no mention of a packet or its motion.

At a two-meeting knot the answer is its only pair. At a four-meeting knot with four interchangeable legs, a permutation fixing one leg can send its proposed partner to either of two other legs. No partition is invariant. At an intact attachment to a five-knot ring, two steps reveal a connection between the two returning legs' other neighbors. Each exchange of the two returning legs and each exchange of the other two extends independently to the unmarked neighborhood on these fixtures. If a proposed pair crosses the two classes, exchanging only the returning legs breaks it. Pairing within each class gives the unique solution. A missing near connection at a cut can erase this distinction.

The reader reconstructed old holders from which physical distinctions share a knot. Its only rooted input for this test was the induced old neighborhood. On 16 named size-two stages there were 638 old knots. We exhausted both radii at every knot. All 595 two-meeting knots had one partition, and all 28 ends had none. All 15 four-meeting knots had none at one step. At two steps all 13 intact attachments had one and both cut attachments had none. If an entering meeting is given as a further state variable, the partition specifies an exit. **Passing that variable to its partner is an additional update convention**, giving one step per handoff, implemented in the earlier comparison by two ordinary four-distinction changes. A static partition alone has no speed. [Matching receipts](../../artifacts/MOTION-LAW-20261119-001/MOTION-LAW-20261119-001-E001_rules/port_pairings.json)

## Why the growth law and renaming alone leave choices

There are infinitely many distinct local laws on allowed assignments even if the blank is quiet, the law is reversible, the elementary updates commute on every state, and every knot and distinction can be renamed. At a knot with exactly two loose distinctions, exchange their two values if an adjacent knot has size k. This test and exchange use only nearness and the two values. Only one knot holds either of these distinctions, so the exchange preserves every parity equation. The two-value pools at different knots are disjoint, the tests on sizes are static, and each update is its own inverse. For each k at least four, a three-place knot sharing one distinction with a k-place knot separates this law from the other thresholds. Use an assignment with the shared distinction and one loose distinction at each knot set to one. The three-place knot's exchange is visible. The replay checked every allowed assignment on the seven cases k=4 through 10 (2,032 in total) and checked the availability of two-loose pools on all 16 motion fixtures. These rules act on physical distinctions. Their purpose is to distinguish admissible laws, independent of which pulse was prepared. There is also a difference visible **after forgetting all names**. At a knot with precisely two loose distinctions, toggle both whenever some shared distinction meeting a k-place neighbor is on. This preserves its equation. No update in this family changes a shared distinction, so every guard persists, different centers commute, the blank is quiet, and each change undoes itself. For any k ≥ 5 put a four-place knot between two k-place knots, meeting each once. Set both shared distinctions and one loose distinction in each of the outer knots to one. The k-rule changes the total occupation from four to six; every rule with a different threshold leaves it at four. No renaming relates those outputs. Six thresholds were physically checked and every allowed assignment for k=5 and k=6 was exhausted (2,560 total). ## Three tests of independence and their speeds

1. Require all total site maps to commute, treating a disabled site as doing nothing. For a sequence W of updates, a site initially disabled satisfies UᵢW(x)=WUᵢ(x)=W(x). With a quiet background and a finite reading radius, a compact change stays in its initial neighborhood. The sharp largest **sustained speed is zero**. Internal oscillations remain possible. This proof also applies to persistent coenabled events if every individual event can immediately be undone by the very same forward guard.
2. Require only that two distinct **already enabled** events each remain possible after the other and have the same joint result. Other sites may become enabled. On an oriented binary chain take all single-site radius-one guards, and add the motion-independent requirement that each change preserve the number of neighboring disagreements. Exactly seven laws survive. The quiet law is one. The six others choose a common direction and a nonempty subset of the two polarities of disagreement. Both bit polarities having equal standing leaves the quiet law and the two directed transports. Requiring reflection with no given orientation leaves only the quiet law at radius one. A moving isolated disagreement takes **one knot step per individual four-distinction change**, the sharp bound in this small family. A compact pattern has two or more disagreements and their spacing can vary. The all-radius obstruction for the original compact ten-distinction pulse and the larger radius comparisons qualify extrapolations from these seven. ## A compact clockless motion whose direction can be read without names

This positive control uses only the state, nearness and a reference. Its **source** supplies an asymmetry. Choose a ring of length divisible by three, at least nine, with old knot sizes repeating 4, 5, 6. Each old knot has two old meetings and two funded two-parent turns. Growing once leaves zero, one and two single-parent children respectively. All old meetings are single and every two successive old meetings have their own two-parent child. The first-layer holder relation has no three-knot cycle. Two adjacent physical distinctions in a knot recover that holder as the intersection of their closed neighborhoods. An old knot is one all of whose distinctions also meet another holder. The count of adjacent single-parent children therefore distinguishes its three types from the current stage alone. A knot of type j has exactly one old neighbor of type j+1 modulo three and one of type j−1. Either convention orients the corridor without an input name, stored arrow, clock or memory. The type cycle and the choice of one of its two orders have been supplied.

Let Q at each old location be its ordinary four-distinction response. The two private distinctions of Q read its occupation. If they disagree, the local guard rejects any occurrence using that location. Otherwise, on successive locations in the chosen direction enable 0100 → 0110 and 0110 → 0010. Each change toggles just one Q, preserving all knot rules in every allowed assignment. On a blank background one occupied Q advances one step in two changes. A pair of enabled words whose starting positions differ by more than three cannot disrupt one another. For displacements one, two and three there are respectively 32, 64 and 128 overlapping binary words to check; just four admit both updates and in all four both orders remain legal and agree. A privately mismatched location stays mismatched, since toggling a Q changes both its private bits. This proves persistence on all allowed assignments on the stated rings with enough locations. For very short cycles of the directed action the overlapping-window argument needs their own wrap check; the unbounded family can always choose arbitrarily long cycles.

One may instead read every a-th location on the same oriented ring. Separate chains then interleave. Require each chain to have length at least nine and take the source length larger than 2a so that a is the actual shortest separation. The same word argument gives **a steps per two individual four-distinction changes**. Thus there is **no finite largest speed** across finite-radius local rules satisfying the already-enabled test, even with renaming symmetry and a compact pulse, when the family of permissible stages includes these decorated sources. Read radius increases with a. The 9-knot source (27 knots and 96 distinctions after growth) was exhausted on all 512 ordinary code states, both conventions and three strides, with 216 coenabled pairs per test. On the 81-knot source (243 knots and 864 distinctions after growth) the same compact pattern completed full 162-event returns with strides 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 in both directions. A recoding of names and knot ordering gave the same results. The proof of persistence and translation applies on arbitrarily large rings. This example uses recovered old-knot nearness. Its ambiguity and its positive speeds say nothing about a universal speed measured in bond distance.
