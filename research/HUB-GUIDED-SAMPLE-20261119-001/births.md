# From maximal groups to the number of new junctions

For a reached growing end, let $C$ count the maximal sets of pair-compatible junctions, including held sets and isolated junctions. Let $h$ count maximal sets held in a single old block, $i$ isolated junctions, $p$ residual pairs, and $o$ offshoots made from unspent loose distinctions. Every maximal set of at least three that escapes every old block makes a junction. A candidate pair makes one if it belongs to no such larger group. The remaining births are the offshoots. Hence the total is $C-h-i+p+o$. This uses the lawful round on the complete current growing end. A closed roster has no further birth.

**On start-03 the exact correction is +1,039.** Of 334,867,154 candidate pairs, 334,865,913 belong to a larger unheld group and 1,241 are residual. There are 203 held maximal sets, zero isolated junctions and one offshoot. The 27 pairs proved in the earlier study are part of the 1,241. A separate earlier uniform sample of 80,000 candidate pairs observed no residuals. It gave a confidence range for their count and did not determine it. ## A finite test for each pair

Begin with the two proposed parents, their common candidate neighbors, and the old blocks holding both. If no block still holds every current member, the chosen members already witness a larger unheld group. If a common neighbor escapes all current holding blocks, taking it gives such a witness immediately. Otherwise choose a holding block with the fewest common neighbors outside it. Every successful extension must take at least one of those outsiders. For each such choice restrict the common neighbors and drop the blocks the chosen junction escapes. If that outside list is empty, this branch ends. Every recursive call removes a holding block, which proves termination and exhaustiveness.

On success, greedily extend the group while keeping every pair compatible. Every pair of the resulting group now has a covering certificate, and all those pairs can be removed from further searches. For the start-03 count this took 9,189 covering groups in the recorded order. Reversing the junction names and the block order took 42,518 covering groups and recovered the identical 1,241 residual pairs. Python also directly checked failure of the extension test for each of the 1,241 listed pairs. On the preceding stage the separate Python and native implementations recovered the same 56 residual pairs. For the initial sample design there was a cheaper unbiased pair reading. A junction whose closed candidate neighborhood is exactly one block has all its pairs residual. Count the distinct pairs with such an endpoint directly. In the remaining candidate-pair population, sample uniformly with replacement, test each sampled pair exactly, and multiply its indicator by the size of that population. Measured zero hits still receive their proper zero contribution. The exact scans supersede the sampled corrections wherever available.
