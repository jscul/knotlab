# Pre-registration: does the top bind the deep levels?

Epic `LEVEL-REACH-20261114-001` (`ADDR-KNOTS-20261113-001#3`, approved by John 2026-11-14 02:38 UTC; G17, with G1; program topology,
group spacing). Frozen 2026-11-14 before the registered run, after the design ([design.md](design.md)), Codex's build
([codex_spec.md](codex_spec.md), [codex_spec_addendum.md](codex_spec_addendum.md)) and an exploratory pilot on held-out seeds. The
canonical K1–K3 test is unchanged.

## The question

Where knots are placed by address maps within a level and between a level and the next, so that a part's rule depends on where it
sits and the joining grows with the level: does a top level bind the deep levels, and is that typical under the description
weighting? In the program's words: the least bond L(s) from level 3 to level 3 + s, members anywhere in levels 0 to 9.

## The question in standard terms

In NAE-3-SAT instances on the words of length ≤ 9 generated by a random invertible binary Mealy automaton and term placements, the
least minimal non-shattered set (least support of an implied clause, minimal) meeting both word length 3 and word length 3 + s,
s = 1..4, under a prefix-code sampling.

## Object, population, method (frozen)

- As in the design and the spec: k0 = 3 (the whole level: all eight addresses are the top, so there is no designated top address),
  S = 4, N = 9, k_max = 10, the exact search certified both ways; budget **300 s per (description, s)**.
- **Registered population:** the first 2,000 fair-coin draws of W_R (seed 20261121), and the E_R lists (every s = 2 automaton × 5,
  1,000 s = 3 automata × 2) kept only where the relative activity class is bounded or polynomial: 1,288 distinct descriptions
  (job parameters `pop=R w_limit=2000 e_classes=bounded,polynomial`). "By weight" means multiplicity among W_R's draws; E_R is
  unweighted and enters only Q5.
- **Certificates kept** (standing rule after LEVEL-RETURN-R1): every refutation's CNF and LRAT proof, gzip-compressed, named by the
  CNF's SHA-256 (`codex/certificates/`, on disk; hashes in each record).
- **Outcomes per description:** no join; dead (the union pattern); no bond (top and target disconnected at some s); **direct**
  (L bounded: L(s) = 2 at every s, rigid-like, or one constant value ≥ 3); **chained** (the ruler L(s) = s + 2); other decided
  profiles; undecided (any s undecided). "> 10" at some s is decided (no bond of size ≤ 10) and goes to "other".

## Settled by construction (stated, not tested)

- No placement joining two levels: no bond across levels. Tree-only placements: the ruler (gate G-tree).
- L is non-increasing in N (more knots, fewer states), so "chained" and "growing" are readings to depth 9, and bounded readings are
  upper bounds that deeper levels can only lower (gate G-mono: 108 comparisons, none violated).
- Knots joining only adjacent levels compose into a chain of s + 2 wherever they compose, so the ruler is the default; the questions
  are how often something shortens it (direct) and how often composition fails (no bond, other).

## Pilot (exploratory, disclosed)

Held-out seeds (P), 247 of 998 descriptions finished when it was stopped for time (the first in the runner's order, slightly biased
toward fast cases among those in flight): no join 29, no bond 52, ruler 116, constant 3 5, rigid-like 5, other 10, undecided 22 (all
exponential), dead 3. By relative class: finitary 125 (ruler 65, no bond 45, no join 14, constant 1), exponential 114 (ruler 51,
other 10, rigid 5, constant 4, undecided 20, no bond 7, dead 3), bounded 3, polynomial 0. Every least bond found lay within levels ≤
its target. Mean 141 s per description, 13% over 600 s.

## Predictions and failure criteria (frozen; after the advisor's review)

| # | Prediction | Fails if |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q0 | (unconditioned, by weight among alive W_R draws) the three shares none (no join, no bond), chained and direct are reported; none is at least 25% and direct at most 15%. | none below 25% or direct above 15%. |
| Q1 | Among alive, joined descriptions with a bond at every s, the ruler holds at every s in at least 70% by weight, counting undecided as ruler for the upper bound and as non-ruler for the lower. | The upper bound is below 70%. |
| Q2 | Direct (bounded) reach is at most 10% of the same set (upper bound counting undecided as direct). | The lower bound (undecided as not direct) is above 10%. |
| Q3 | (membership only) every least bond found lies within levels ≤ its target. | Any found bond needs a member below its target. |
| Q4 | (confirmation) the non-ruler decided share is higher in the exponential class than the finitary class; 95% cluster-bootstrap interval by automaton class excludes 0. | The interval includes 0 or is reversed. |
| Q5 | (descriptive, two-sided) bounded and polynomial relative classes (E_R, unweighted) against exponential (W_R): ruler and direct shares with intervals. | — |

Reported per s as well (the margin below the target is 5 levels at s = 1, 2 at s = 4). Not measured here, and said so: whether deeper
levels tighten a bond without appearing in it (that needs the pattern truncated at the target level), bond multiplicity, and the
top-specificity control the advisor suggested (moot: the top is a whole level).

## Stop rule

One registered run, its frozen summary, a referee hand-off; report and hold. Nothing beyond level 9, s = 4, the weighting, or the two
populations.
