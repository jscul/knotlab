# What is present after the binary sign

With permission to ask a question outside the allowed words, the two calibrated chances still leave the post-answer state undetermined. A physical operation that translates every word certainly sends a singleton to its forbidden partner. If it also tosses a fair coin, either sign leaves the same forbidden word. A sharp sign *measurement* of that translation has a different conditional state. An allowed-output instrument can have the same initial sign chances as that sharp measurement. All following calculations refer to one stage with the physical rules held fixed.

## One supplied continuation

With full real signed amplitudes and the selective projection instrument, start with $1_S/\sqrt n$, where $n=|S|$, and use $(I+aX_t)/2$ for answer $a=+1,-1$. Write $m=|S\cap(S+t)|$. For an answer of positive probability the exact result at every word $y$ is

$$p_a=\frac{n+a m}{2n},\qquad v_a(y)=1_S(y)+a1_S(y+t),\quad \psi_a(y)=\frac{v_a(y)}{\sqrt{2(n+a m)}},\qquad p(y\mid a)=\frac{v_a(y)^2}{2(n+a m)}.$$

With a full pair, plus doubles both amplitudes and minus cancels both. Each unpaired word has amplitude $1$ at its old position and $a$ at its newly available partner. Thus the forbidden words occurring on either possible branch are exactly $(S+t)\setminus S$. The plus branch puts $(n-m)/(2(n+m))$ there. Every possible minus branch puts $1/2$ there. Ignoring the sign gives $(n-m)/(2n)$. When $m=n$ the plus branch retains the original preparation and minus has zero chance and no conditional state. When $0<m<n$, paired old words have four times the ordinary weight of unpaired old words on plus. The ordinary distribution of the minus branch is uniform on its surviving words, while opposite amplitudes within each pair retain its sign on a repeat. A positive uniform set alone cannot represent this minus preparation. This is the literature's selective projection instrument, a supplied update in this task. [Lüders' measurement rule, English translation](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0403007)

With ordinary even parity, a translation fails precisely those held rules containing an odd number of its marks. At a newly forbidden not-all-equal word it fails exactly the rules whose full mark list now has only zeros or only ones. Any previously satisfied rule disjoint from the proposed change stays satisfied. For the isolated not-all-equal triple and $t=001$, the word order $000,001,010,011,100,101,110,111$ gives plus amplitudes $(1,1,2,2,2,2,1,1)/\sqrt{20}$ and minus amplitudes $(-1,1,0,0,0,0,1,-1)/2$. Both $000$ and $111$ violate the sole knot. The branch chances are $5/6,1/6$ and the forbidden masses are $1/10,1/2$, respectively. With an isolated even triple the same flip has $m=0$. Either sign has chance $1/2$ and assigns half its mass to the odd words. When two not-all-equal rules share the changed mark, both can fail together. For example, changing $00001$ to $00000$ breaks both rules $012$ and $034$. The complete two-rule and six-rule-source reports list every changed mask, every word, mass, newly failed rule and impossible branch. With the same projection instrument, asking the same change again repeats the sign with certainty and preserves any forbidden component. A complete ordinary read produces an individual word with the above probability. The resulting word may still violate a rule. The exact archive contains the joint chance of each sign pair, and its joint forbidden mass, if any other nonzero change at that stage is asked next. On the not-all-equal triple after a plus for $001$, asking $010$ can still leave a forbidden result. On the two distinctions of a full pair, no repair is necessary. Repreparing the original positive indicator deliberately restores the original preparation on all branches. These examples separate a subsequent unconstrained question from a repair rule.

With an extra membership measurement followed by translation of its forbidden branch, legality is restored in every outcome. There need be no observation of individual ordinary words for this procedure. Write $u=1_{S\cap(S+t)}$ and $v=1_{S\setminus(S+t)}$. Forgetting both the first sign and membership gives the corrected density operator

$$\big[uu^T+vv^T+(uv^T+vu^T)/2\big]/n.$$

It restores the original ordinary frequency of every word. It restores the original pure positive preparation exactly when $u$ or $v$ vanishes. For a partial overlap it retains only half the original coefficients between these two word classes. If the repair instead reads the complete ordinary word and then changes it when forbidden, the same unconditioned ordinary frequencies return, while all off-diagonal coefficients are lost. The per-branch ordinary counts for this latter procedure are retained in the archive. ## Effects can be realized in different ways

With $P_S$ the projector onto allowed basis words and $\Pi_{c,a}$ a full joint effect in any one context, put $E_{c,a}=P_S\Pi_{c,a}P_S$ on the allowed subspace. Each $E$ is positive by restriction and their sum is $I_S$. For every preparation initially supported in $S$, $\operatorname{Tr}(\rho E_{c,a})=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho\Pi_{c,a})$. This holds for a whole outcome, including simultaneous multi-reader outcomes. Equal marginals of contexts remain equal on compression. Products of compressed individual effects need not equal the compressed full product. Compatible joint effects can have noncommuting individual unsharp marginals. Positivity and normalization give a mathematically available joint measurement on all the marks, including possibly a collective operation dependent on all settings. They do not supply independent instruments at the two or three actual readers.

With $E_a=(I_S+aP_S X_tP_S)/2$ for one change, its two effects on a singleton each equal $I_S/2$. Acting by $\sqrt{E_a}$ keeps the ordinary word allowed. On plus the conditional ordinary mass per paired word is $2/(n+m)$ and per singleton it is $1/(n+m)$. On a possible minus the singleton words each have mass $1/(n-m)$ and the paired words have zero. All these posterior amplitudes can be chosen positive on this input. On all pairs the compression equals the original sharp operator and on all unpaired words it equals zero before adding $I_S$. Alternatively, after reporting the same probabilities, prepare any fixed allowed word: $\rho\mapsto\operatorname{Tr}(\rho E_a)|s_0\rangle\langle s_0|$. Both instruments remain inside the allowed subspace on every branch. On a singleton both reproduce a fair initial answer while the actual ordinary word stays there. The full projection and these allowed-output instruments already establish nonuniqueness.

With the additional demand that an answer repeat, a single physical singleton with no memory and a fresh support-only rule necessarily tosses a new fair coin on the next call. An outside state is **one** way to implement repeatability. An independent stored outcome bit makes the first answer fair and repeatable while preserving the ordinary word. It abandons set-only descriptions of the resulting preparation. Where a physical allowed space contains at least one complete pair, the same compressed effects admit an allowed-output repeatable instrument even without adding an apparatus state to that space: after obtaining $a$, prepare the signed eigenvector $(|u\rangle+a|u+t\rangle)/\sqrt2$ of a fixed full pair. This can abandon the previously exposed pair's own identity. If retaining one singleton's pair sector and using its one-dimensional allowed space with no memory are required simultaneously, this escape is excluded. Requiring every post-sign state to be another unsigned uniform support also prevents certain minus, since the calibrated mean is then nonnegative on every nonempty support. Each restriction on the post-state has a separate consequence.

With every nonempty support of widths one to three, direct integer normalization found 3,551 positive-probability sign branches and 115 impossible ones. Of the positive branches, 3,436 place some mass outside, 1,132 have unequal positive ordinary masses on their surviving support and 2,850 cannot be represented by an unsigned uniform indicator even up to overall sign. These denominators count preparations, masks and binary outcomes as specified in the freeze. They are not a population of physical knots.
