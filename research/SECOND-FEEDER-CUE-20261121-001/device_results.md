## Exactly what the stable-return mark proves

Let H mean the marked already-paid word and D the marked last-departure word. Before any move both start false. On a stay, H becomes H or an actual payment and D persists. On an actual move after the last possible growth cut, the new H requires the old D and exactly one move control at the departing holder. The new D equals the old H. Before that stable phase an actual move clears both. This is the literal operation on the two banks.

**A raw mark always certifies an already paid current holder.** A true D records the immediately preceding actual movement from a known paid holder to the present holder, with stationary actions allowed to intervene. If the latter now has exactly one control on the stable structure, its move must go back to that same previously paid holder. A stay can add H only on an actual meal. Induction proves the assertion for every legal policy history. Rations never replenish. Recorded holder identities here are for proof and external scoring.

**The raw mark can refer to an earlier meal.** In the original archived returned path, meals were eaten at holders 12 and 25 on turns 5 and 7. On turn 8 the single control at 25 forced a return to 12. At the next query H was true, the latest paid holder was still 25, and old M said one while holder 12 had three controls. The same phenomenon occurs in the fresh forced bank at holders 11 and 23. On the fresh training and held-out current-only paths the raw mark referred to an *earlier* meal at 24 of 3,274 and 46 of 5,950 turn observations per law. None of these older returns passed the original strict vote's old-M test. Across 13,116 stored terminal forced prefixes per law, their 99,068 turn appearances had 5,687 raw certificates, 16 referring to earlier meals, and 66 complete strict votes. All 66 referred to the most recent meal. Distinct terminal prefixes can share turns. The raw and completed counts here use the same appearance weighting. **An eligible strict vote on the enrolled connected starts refers to the most recent meal, even with forced actions.** Immediately after a new payment at W, H is true at W. The only other pending certificate can be D, which refers to the immediately preceding stable departure from some already paid C into W. There is no other stored certified location. Subsequent forced returns until another payment only recover a location already certified or retained. To recover C, the first move from W must use its sole stable control. Otherwise this move clears H and sets D to a departure from W, erasing the older reference. A sole control at departure implies W had exactly one at its payment because every feeder is born with a control and old contacts persist. Old M therefore holds one. In a connected start with at least three original holders, C and W cannot both have just their one mutual control. These two would form an isolated pair, whereas each birth is attached to at least one old holder and preserves connectedness. Thus C has at least two at that stable return and always fails old M's type check. If the meal occurred before the final growth cut, D could not yet have been written by a stable departure before that meal. This also deals with a change of menu at the last cut. Restart the argument at every actual payment. A truth claim about the latest meal therefore belongs to the complete vote with this old-M guard.

**The complete vote is unavailable under the natural current-only policy.** Suppose it were about to fire at feeder A. A must be the most recently paid site by the preceding argument. Previous eat excludes the vote, and a feeder has at least one control, so the last action reaching this eligible query must have moved to A. A true returned H requires a stable move from a one-control holder Y, reached beforehand from already-paid A. The freely reset cursor began on an original nonfeeder. An original Y already has an original neighbor because the founding tree is connected with at least three holders. A newly acquired contact to feeder A gives such a Y a second control, and the old contact persists. A newborn Y older than A similarly had an older parent when it was formed. Hence a sole stable neighbor A requires a younger feeder Y whose only neighbor throughout its life is A. Contact counts increase or stay fixed. Distinct newborns at a single funded cut have disjoint grants, so same-cut peers cannot first acquire this mutual contact at birth.

A first visit to any feeder under current-only comes from an actual move and immediately issues ON. No other actor removes its one ration. Every earlier visit or meal at Y therefore required departing A after A's first meal. If Y was empty when reached by the claimed departure, Y already paid after A, contradicting A's status as latest. If Y was still full, the newly arrived bot attempts ON there and receives Y's ration. An unsuccessful ON followed by OFF requires that Y was already empty and gives the same contradiction. Death before the departure supplies no later A query. This argument permits earlier visits, repeated failed eats, final-cut growth or a refused cut, and intervening stationary turns. It uses only actual paid contacts and a persistent cue. By first possible difference, the hand-fixed strict policy itself follows the same natural history and never diverges. The proof supplies an invariance statement for these reset/game/policy choices. Different probing policies can pass a full feeder without eating and supply real positive opportunities.

The direct natural replay found 0 strict votes among 713 menu-eligible training ON queries and 0 among 1,145 such held-out ON queries per law (3,274 and 5,950 total turns respectively). In both laws the fitted neutral selector routed the current-only action everywhere. This proof was derived after the freeze. The enrolled counts and controls remain the finite study's primary observations.
