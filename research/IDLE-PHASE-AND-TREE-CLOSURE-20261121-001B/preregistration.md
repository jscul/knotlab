## Sample space and exact measures

Choose \(n\) uniformly in \(\{4,5,6,7,8\}\) and one of the \(n^{n-2}\) labelled trees uniformly by its Prüfer word. On each existing meeting put one distinct shared name. At holder \(i\) of degree \(g_i\) put \(\max(0,3-g_i)+b_i\) private names, independently \(\Pr(b_i=1)=1/4\). Apply a uniform permutation to all distinction names on that founder, as in B251. Each fixed labelled tree and binary mask of weight \(k\) has conditional-on-size weight \(3^{n-k}/D_n\), where \(D_n=n^{n-2}4^n\). This denominator counts equal-weight auxiliary choices for the binary extras; it is not the unweighted number of distinct labelled decorated trees. Use arbitrary-size integers and rational arithmetic throughout. For each size retain counts by \(k\), so another Bernoulli probability can be evaluated as a declared sensitivity check (at 0, 1/2 and 1). The quarter law controls the primary decision.

A class is the original holder tree with its binary decorations up to holder permutation. Distinctions are separately named in every actual growth replay. Prove that the meeting adjacency, birth multiplicities, fresh counts, stalling, closure and odd-operator verdict are invariant when renaming holders or reordering a holder's currently private distinctions. The proof must handle duplicate unary parent groups, choice of which loose name is spent on each child, and subsequent funding. At each cut, alternative allocations permute still-private names within their sole holder. The children can be matched by parent groups and multiplicities, then their new private names matched by bijection. If a distinction-name permutation can instead change an endpoint, expose the pair and refine the counting units to include the requisite name order. Integrate the conditional permutation law to one only after this check. Rooting the founder tree at any chosen holder extends any of its permissible local words outwards under both local laws. This certifies every generated founder, including holders with no private name. No founder is conditioned on first-cut funding.
