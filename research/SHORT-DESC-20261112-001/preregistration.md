# Meeting patterns weighted by short descriptions: pre-registration

Epic `SHORT-DESC-20261112-001` (`PREFIX-END-20261112-001` direction 1; G17, with G7), 2026-11-12, at the coordinator's go-ahead.
Frozen before the registered run. The canonical K1–K3 test is unchanged. The law is not-all-equal (NAE) on three-place knots
throughout; "dead" means the pattern allows no state (A72).

## The question

Nothing chooses a meeting pattern; ours is one draw. Under a weighting that makes patterns with short descriptions common (the
owner's "simple realities are more common", which this weighting holds by definition), what is typical and what is rare: how often
patterns die, whether they die more or less often than patterns of the same size under the uniform weighting or with the same
degrees, how much the law ties in the ones that live, and how nested patterns fare as their depth grows and in the limit?

## How this differs from the knots-alone weightings test

That test ("Test what knots alone fix") fixes infinite periodic bases and weights which kind of knot holds them, over the box of
738 knot kinds with a family of short-knot weightings: it varies the law. This test fixes the law (NAE) and weights which finite
meeting pattern it holds, by the length of the pattern's description: it varies the pattern. The two share no weighting and no
pattern.

## The owner's picture of 17:26

The language expresses both parts of it. A definition with three ports may call itself (SELF), so a knot holds the definition's
pattern inside it, and that pattern's knots hold it again, to any depth: a recurring pattern with no layout, placed only by which
distinctions the calls share. Arguments may be shared by any number of calls, so one distinction can meet any number of knots,
not one to one. The infinite limit of a nested family is the owner's logic fractal; it is read exactly below.

## In standard terms

Positive NAE-3-SAT instances generated by a straight-line hyperedge-replacement grammar with a prefix-free code, sampled with
probability 2^-length: the satisfiable fraction against uniform and degree-matched instances of the same size, backbone sizes,
and the satisfiability of self-similar (iterated-substitution) families at every depth and in the limit.

## The language, the length and the weighting (frozen in `dlang.py`)

- **Descriptions.** Definitions D_0 to D_{r−1}, the last one main. Definition i has k ≥ 3 ports, l ≥ 0 own distinctions and c ≥ 1
  calls; a call names K (one knot on three distinct of the definition's k + l distinctions), an earlier definition (on as many
  distinct ones as it has ports), or SELF (in a definition with three ports). With SELF, a depth t ≥ 1: at depth 1 every SELF is a
  knot, at depth t the definition at depth t − 1.
- **Length.** The bits of a prefix-free code: Elias gamma for r, k − 2, l + 1, c and t; each callee a uniform choice among those
  allowed; each call's arguments a uniform ordered choice of distinct distinctions.
- **Weighting.** A description has probability 2^−length (fair coins); a pattern's weight is the sum over the descriptions that
  expand to it (A79; the shortest description alone is the other reading). Caps truncate it: r ≤ 6, k ≤ 6, l ≤ 6, c ≤ 12,
  t ≤ 6, at most 3,000 knots; each rejection is counted by cause. Variants: **main**; **wide**, k ≤ 8; **noself**, no SELF.
- **A pattern** is its set of distinct knots on the distinctions in at least one knot (n of them, m knots); unused ports and
  own distinctions are dropped.

## Settled by construction or by known results (stated, not tested)

- Short descriptions weigh more: the weighting's definition, so "simple realities are more common" holds by fiat here.
- A pattern with fewer than seven knots always allows a state (the smallest pattern with none has seven knots, the Fano plane:
  the classical m(3) = 7). The share of weight below seven knots is a measurement of this code, reported.
- Every described pattern has primal treewidth at most k + l − 1 (11; 13 in wide), through the call tree, so its satisfiability is
  decided in time linear in its size (classical; no publication-hold question arises). Bounded width is the expected mechanism of
  S1, so S1 tests the effect's size and reach, not its existence in kind.
- A flat language with K calls only is uniform given n and m: the uniform baseline is that language at each size.
- **Nested families.** What a definition allows on its ports is computed exactly by enumerating its k + l distinctions. With SELF,
  depth t + 1's relations are a fixed map of depth t's; each three-port relation is closed under complement (16 possible), so a
  family's fate is decided within 16^s depths (s SELF definitions), and the infinite limit (SELF dropped, iterated to the greatest
  fixed point; by compactness the infinite pattern allows a state iff that fixed point's main relation is non-empty) within 4s
  steps. The finite truncations sit inside the limit's, so "forever" implies a living limit; only the converse cell is a finding.
- The canonical checker agrees with the census's K1–K3 test on all 16 complement-closed three-port relations: exactly those with
  three of the four complementary pairs are knots.

## Baselines and readings

For patterns with at least 7 knots on at most 400 distinctions: **uniform**, the same n and m with every distinction in a knot (a
replace-one-knot chain from the pattern, 30 steps per knot, symmetric proposal), and **same degrees** (the switch chain of
`AGE-NEST-20261112-001`, 20 steps per knot). Mixing check: the first 300 per worker again at 60 and 40 steps. On at most 200
distinctions: the **tie share** of a living pattern (pairs equal in every state or unequal in every state, over all pairs; exact, by
the frozen RANDOM-FATES reader), the first 1,500 per worker, with each baseline's on a living draw (up to five redraws); minimal dead
cores, reported. Validation gate (V0): the port evaluation and a SAT solver agree on every expanded pattern, or the run is invalid.

## Smoke test (disclosed)

A timing run of 20,000 tries of main (worker seed 999, not reused) printed outcomes before this registration: 7,147 patterns, 405
paired (the 6% with at least seven knots, less a few on over 400 distinctions); dead shares 0.027 (descriptions), 0.059 (uniform),
0.086 (same degrees), paired differences −0.032 (−0.054 to −0.012) and −0.059 (−0.086 to −0.035); at 100 knots or more 0.17,
0.42 and 0.58. S1 and S2 are therefore replications on fresh seeds. Tie shares and nested fates were not looked at; a 3,000-try
plumbing run of wide (seed 998) was not looked at. The language, code, caps and weighting were fixed before the smoke and not
changed after it; the advisor's review added only the checks and criteria above.
