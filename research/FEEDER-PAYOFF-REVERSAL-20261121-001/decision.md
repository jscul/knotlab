# Two equally common approaches with opposite meal payoffs

Each knot law admitted a reader whose physical memory learned from actual meals which of two equally common feeder approaches to favor. Exchanging that memory reversed the action in all 456 matched decisions per law. The trained reader lived on average 0.0376 fewer turns than the encounter-count reader. Its closed-loop survival advantage was unresolved.

## What is supplied

The predecessor's return cue was certainly empty whenever it was eligible. Reward credit and counting opportunities to use that cue gave the same deployed policy. This study starts from that ambiguity, keeps the original growth, energy and movement laws and runs two new versions of the feeder game. The two earlier games and their raw results remain available. [Previous result and decisive control](../SECOND-FEEDER-CUE-20261121-001/decision.md) · [This prospective test](preregistration.md)

The last actual outward control is anonymously numbered at departure. Where an adjacent pair exists, even supplies cue A and odd supplies B. An unpaired movement clears the cue. At a marked feeder with exactly three current exits, a known approach admits a bite on a stocked ration only if it matches the privately supplied opening. A turn of waiting at a feeder retains the approach, and a denied bite can leave stock for a later return. Consumption still spends the ration only once. Both worlds have the same supply of one ration per new knot, and opposite openings at every eligible holder. Growth itself creates neither the payoff reversal nor the training apparatus. The paired anonymous labelings let the same actual passage arrive with opposite public clues. [Game rule and hidden exceptions](game_design.md) · [Archived and matched movement controls](game_checks.json)

The built reader has 32 pairs of marked distinctions for its signed score. A clamped meal-energy port and guarded exchanges increment the marked word for a credited A bite and decrement it for a credited B bite. Another public port carries actual energy to the ordinary receipt writer under all three credit conditions. The public route record, sensory input and single action distinction retain their separate holdings. At a positively signalled, eligible three-exit feeder, the signed memory allows the ordinary bite proposal or vetoes it on the disfavored approach. A zero word accepts either. Training resets retain only the score word. After release it is read-only in all ordinary games. Swaps, clears and shams are separately supplied interventions. Neither a map, private gate, version selector, movement tape nor true food stock enters this reader. ## Three results

1. **Acquisition.** Under each law the 96-structure training survey covered 6,144 movement tapes and 30,720 query visits. Of 400 visits to three-exit feeders, 48 had no supported clue. Unique first arrivals gave 176 A and 173 B, of which 123 disjoint exact target pairs were possible across 17 founders. Enrollment used 64 pairs on 16 founders, with 56 additional pairs above the frozen cap and three in an incomplete block. Thus naturally occurring survey clues had measurable unequal frequencies. All learners actually experienced exactly 64 of each. Each law, hidden version, reader and order incurred the same 746 turn costs, 64 meals and 53 deaths at the test bite. Under the majority-A version actual pay was 48/64 vs 16/64. The other version reversed both counts. A real +32 or −32 emerged in the signed memory, counting and re-pairing each ended at zero, and alternative order preserved the result under both laws. The chronological credit control rules out either the experienced clue sequence by itself or the ordered energy receipts by themselves as a uniform explanation of the opposing fits. The supplied update is simply the difference in number of meals, proportional to the difference in observed rates because the denominators match. [Acquired state](acquisition.md) · [Complete 3,072 training and control receipts](training_receipts.csv) · [Independent repetition](training_replay_repeat.json)
2. **Frozen action.** The mechanism fold used 48 renamed founders and 64 pass-only routes per founder per law and public labeling. Among 15,360 predecision appearances per version and labeling, 228 were focal. The two labelings yielded 456 exact opposing-version comparison pairs under each law. The 228 base physical opportunities were paired twice, once per supplied labeling. All 456 shams preserved both actions, all 456 transfers reversed both, all 456 restorations recovered the originals, and in all 456 erasures the reader accepted both clues. The rest of each recipient including route and first-memory marks, physical game, and ready action holder was fixed. Opposite behavior was appropriate to both actual gate openings on 348 of the 456 pairs. The remaining 108 were private exceptions for which both orientations' preferred actions were incorrect. The experiment therefore separates control of an action from success on an individual bite. [All matched decisions and counterfactual receipts](mechanism_results.json) · [Spectator seed replay](seed_replay.md)
3. **Survival.** 128 distinct fresh renamed founders × eight movement draws × two anonymous labelings × two hidden versions gave 4,096 paired lives per reader and law. All 99 initially stalled founders remained. Seven arms and two laws made 57,344 actual deaths, with a further 8,192 common-path observations of complete games. Both laws received the same founders and draws and gave identical paths on these tests. The fitted, count, shuffled and current-only means were respectively 9.130371, 9.167969, 9.167969 and 9.167969. The pooled comparison against counting had a paired 95% whole-founder interval of [−0.20416, +0.12842] under parity and [−0.21241, +0.13090] under not-all-equal. The within-version mean was also −0.03760 for each law. Version A and B intervals respectively were [−0.19775, +0.13625], [−0.21241, +0.13528] for parity and [−0.19484, +0.14161], [−0.21533, +0.13624] for not-all-equal. Each control played through the actual fixed readout under matched budgets. The native preference gained +0.33301 turns over its own opposite-regime word in both laws. A single fixed A or B across both regimes underperformed the learned board by +0.16650 turns on the pooled comparison. Pay was 56/77 against 21/77 for the favored and other clue respectively. All four instances at each opportunity had the same current energy, feeder and actual stock. The other 947 launches remain in coverage. The inherited exact knot projection checker validated every reached stage on the 272 structural sources, with 615 earlier keys excluded. Counting encounters and shuffling meal credit both gave zero. Reversing the bite order also produced opposite preferences after identical sequences of energy receipts.
