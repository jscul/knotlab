# Independent checks of the growing-end update

**Stage 1, referee pending.** One added loose distinction changes the fresh count of a ternary birth elsewhere at the next decision, even when its *entire connected end component* is unchanged. The smallest checked source is a hub and three leaves. The same result holds when three long paths separate the old holders. Under ordinary parity the ternary gets **zero versus one fresh distinction**. Under ordinary not-all-equal it gets **one in both preparations**. Both steps are funded, and the two ternaries have identical parent sets. The stopping condition applies to the full parity update in the identified class. ## Positive and negative controls

The end operator uses only the live open counts and the maximal sets witnessed by a common holder. It builds the legal groups, accounts for unused slots as unary births, and reads next demand from which children share an old parent. It computes the usual fresh floor from demand, child size and preservation of old states, with the sole implied parity birth treated separately. The implementation does not call the archived engines. The checker separately materializes its proposed supports, and compares their physical labels, parents, widths, demand and stop status with the original archived subset enumerator using either the parity implication oracle or the not-all-equal SAT implication oracle. On a positive open step, extracting the live set from physical supports and deleting those live knots returns the preceding stage. A closed stage has no live set to identify a last cohort by this procedure.

- All **191** compared archived outcomes agree, including genuine shortages and closed ends. The **141** tested open successors peel to their exact predecessors.
- **704** local fresh-count checks agreed with the global nonclosing calculation at radius two. In the sharp control, two lawful sources have identical radius-one witnessed data at an old parent. The same triple birth has two separate old-parent siblings in one, and one joint sibling in the other. Its fresh count drops from **2 to 1**. These sources fall outside the initial identified class.
- The linked pair of hub-and-three-leaf sources keeps both ternaries open, with one fresh distinction each. The five-knot path with only one middle open stalls for one missing slot. The ordinary bare five-ring on two opens per knot keeps producing the same five-knot end. The one-spare ring and the isolated three-place knot supply additional positive-growth and terminal controls.
- Across all funded nonclosing steps we checked **3,521** distinct-child pair distances and **1,432** nearest contractions for finite old-parent pairs. The arbitrary-child upper bound was attained in **1,196** of the latter pairs. We also checked **1,192** disconnected parent pairs. All descendants of such pairs remain disconnected by end steps in the next layer. Eight closing steps have no successor end distance.

For distinct children with parent sets $X$ and $Y$, the exact open-step distance is $1+\min_{x\in X,y\in Y}d(x,y)$ when there is a route. Each old candidate edge is covered by a birth, and each birth has a complete set of candidate parents. These facts give both directions of the intersection-distance proof, enlarging the usual maximal-clique family to the precise family here. Including a child common to both old parents gives the nearest-child formula $\max(0,d-1)$. This is the distance between **inclusive** child sets. Pure lineages can lose the joint child that attains the bound. ## What share of the current end registers one added distinction

On the four-knot hub source at layers zero and one, **0 of 3** matched roots change at radii zero, one or two. There is one extra isolated live knot in the extended arm, **1 of 4**. At layer two the unextended end is empty and the paired live fraction is undefined. Comparing births separately, the sole matched ternary changes width. Two of the three extended-arm births are unmatched unary children. The changed ternary has no ancestry through the holder of the added distinction. Its fresh count changes through the parity closing condition. The early local null is stronger than a failure of any single registered test because the whole connected rooted views match exactly.

The connected nine-knot path is a separate positive comparison with an extra open at its middle holder. Every source knot has two opens before that addition. The current roots are matched by their full parent sets and by ranks among tied unary siblings. For each match the reading uses only the unlabeled current rooted end within the stated radius and its open counts. Parent identities grade the matching after growth. An inclusive descendant contains the changed source holder somewhere among its ancestors. A pure descendant has that holder as its only original ancestor.
