Diverting that payment to the complementary payer's child gives $G$ **three**. Match the grant $h$ at $R$ and a nonempty corresponding subfamily of its pure first children. Such children must be unary with sole parent $R$. Matching all pure first children gives this conclusion for the entire singleton family. Matching a smaller subset gives it only for descendants all of whose parents trace to that subset.
