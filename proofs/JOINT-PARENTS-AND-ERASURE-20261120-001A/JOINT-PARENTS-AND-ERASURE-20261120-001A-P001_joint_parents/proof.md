## Exact payment and the third's role

Let V be the original donor walk of any thin route segment just used. Count every occurrence, including repetitions of an original clock role. Let h be an original's actual loose supply. A two-member ordinary block fully traversed loses two singleton extensions. A C block traversed through u,v initially contributes two extensions at each of u,v, but in the combined support only w remains to be added, a loss of three. An S crossing includes w as an actual source parent and fully occupies its one-rank step from any of the three originals, a loss of three. Consequently its exact fresh grant is the sum of h on the original V occurrences, plus h(w) for each fully traversed S, minus two for each ordinary internal step and three for each internal C or S step. At a simply touched gate endpoint only the visited singleton is counted. This follows by listing one-rank external extensions at each occupied block in the unrolled incidence tree. Distinct neighboring blocks introduce disjoint extensions. A stored surplus at an occupied original contributes one corresponding terminal per extra loose name. The formula applies separately to the actual emit, both its deletion parents, the receiving launch ancestor, the two receiving inputs and the receipt. It holds at every size on these routes.

Relative to the old through route, a full C adds one and a full S adds three to the grant. A singly touched gate endpoint adds one in either mode.
