# Referee report on facts held by the whole under not-all-equal
**Refuted. Block on the conclusion that rings and ordinary growth erase every exact relation among separately carried values. All frozen numerical predictions hold on their stated rosters.**

A counterexample is already in the study's first 500. A three-knot ring with one loose distinction per knot grows a fourth knot. On its 44 allowed states, three readings on disjoint distinctions take **seven of eight triples**. Every pair takes all four values. Removing **any one of the four laws**, including the birth law, restores the eighth triple. The study recorded **2 of 2** on this very stage. It read the one currently loose distinction. The three readings still take seven triples at rounds two, three and four. [Growth and assignments](carrier_birth.json) · [Independent literal recount](seven_counts.json)

The scope of the positive loose-set result is exact. If all current loose words occur, every combination of individually attainable readings on disjoint subsets of those loose distinctions occurs as well. The following counterexample also reads shared distinctions.

**The four-law counterexample.** In archive order the three source knots are (0,1,3), (0,2,4) and (1,2,5). Their ordinary birth is (3,4,5,6), with 6 fresh. Read F as “0 and 5 agree”, G as “2 and 3 agree”, and H as “1, 4 and 6 all agree”. Before the birth, using “1 and 4 agree” for H gives all eight triples on 26 states. That old reading still gives all eight after the birth. Including the fresh distinction excludes exactly **001**. To see why, suppose F and G are false and H is true. Complementing all distinctions lets us set 1, 4 and 6 to zero. The four laws then exclude, respectively, 01, 00, 10 and 11 on distinctions 0 and 2. Each law removes exactly the possibility the other three leave. This gives a finite relation passing K1–K3 in which all four laws are essential. [Four deletion tables](seven_counts.json) · [Canonical check](carrier_birth.json)

The 44 states form **one** single-change class. Respectively 22, 22 and 26 allowed one-distinction changes alter F, G and H. No single knot determines any of the three as a function. Fixing a single knot retains all seven triples on **2 of 32** feasible local readings. Searching all 105 partitions of sizes 2, 2 and 3 gave 420 reading triples after identifying output renamings. Each function uses every argument and respects global complementation. Eighteen passed the deletion test and three also had each value unread by any single knot. The three choices exchange which pair gets the fresh distinction. Growth supplies that distinction. Choosing the readings remains part of this construction. [Search](carrier_search.json) · [Fixing tests](carrier_birth.json)

**Stronger separation tests.** On a seven-knot ring, compare each loose distinction with a shared distinction three knots along the ring. The seven disjoint comparisons take **127/128** words. If all comparisons held, each of seven triples would have exactly one equal pair. The triples use every pair of seven bits once, and any binary assignment has at least nine equal pairs. Grouping the comparisons into three readings, each true when all its comparisons hold, gives seven triples. For **all 105** groupings of sizes 2, 2 and 3, all **42/42** feasible single-knot fixes retain all seven. Deleting any law frees all eight. This ring stalls. A separate funded nine-knot ring gives seven triples on **three disjoint groups of knots**, each reading free on its group alone. All nine source laws are essential and the same triples persist through two ordinary rounds. Even single distinctions can expose the difference: keeping one shared and three loose distinctions on a three-knot ring gives **14/16**, every three-distinction projection **8/8**, and **16/16** after any source-law deletion. [Seven-knot ring](carrier_seven.json) · [Nine-knot ring](carrier_nine.json) · [Four-distinction check](lemma_ring_check.json)

**Equal weighting.** On a ring of four ternary knots, take the parity of the two consecutive loose distinctions in one half and the parity of those in the other half. In order 00, 01, 10, 11 the counts are **26, 20, 20, 16**, giving an odds ratio of **26/25**. Every one of the 15 proper subsets of the four laws makes the two readings independent, with all distinction identities retained. After ordinary rounds one and two the respective tables are **62, 40, 40, 52** and **558, 360, 360, 468**. The two newly loose bits at the first round themselves have counts 51, 46, 46, 51. The earlier prediction of 50 even states out of 82 concerns the four *shared* distinctions. The four loose distinctions have 42 even states. On a ring of 2k knots, the count for a word with t unequal loose pairs is 5ᵏ⁻ᵗ4ᵗ, plus one if t = 0. Any proper subset of the laws removes the extra completion and gives independent readings. Direct counts agree at four, six and eight knots. [Counting argument](weight_argument.md) · [Counts and deletions](weight_tables.json)
