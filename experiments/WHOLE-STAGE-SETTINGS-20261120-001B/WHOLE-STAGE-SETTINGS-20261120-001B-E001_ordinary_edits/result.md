**Funding minimum.** Fix the observer labels 0 and 1. Enumerate every connected labelled meeting skeleton on two through five holders and every live subset containing the observers. There must be two proposed groups through each observer and none through both. The search found zero such shapes below five holders, 60 at five, and six at five under the stronger separation. For a shape, positive shared multiplicities are enumerated to the declared baseline distinction ceilings. The low settings give each observer one loose mark, so they each need at least two shared distinctions. At every other live holder the least possible loose supply is the maximum of its group demand, one, and three minus its shared count. A spent holder needs at least three shared distinctions. Extra supply beyond these minima can only increase the size in the structural search. Even with the semantic projection filter removed, the lower bounds are 13 and 16. There are 18 and six literal candidates at those bounds. The chosen witnesses attain them under both laws, with every displayed support carrying the full projected law in all four edited preparations. The separate exact not-all-equal search and the wired parity check agree on the minima.

For wider separation use an ordinary cycle of $4r+1$ holders with the two variable-supply holders at 0 and $2r$, for $r \ge 2$. Every holder has two candidate peers through two different witnesses. Each candidate pair must be paid once, and the only low-setting shortages are at the edited holders. The written-holder count is $2r$, the mark count $2r+1$, and the witnessed count $r$. The round still cancels everywhere if either shortage remains. The exact replays use $r=2,4,8$. Their marks share no child in the measured round.

**Closing minimum.** At baseline closure the target’s parents supply all the loose distinctions of a lawful connected parity stage. A single loose distinction would be fixed by summing the old parity equations and would violate the full law of its holder. A two-parent proposal needs a separate common witness, so at least three holders. At three holders that witness is Bob, and the two joining parents must each share two distinctions with him beside their offered mark. The three-holder case attains six distinctions. For a three-parent target the connected skeleton search exhausted 38 four-holder and 728 five-holder skeletons with Bob separate in the witnessed end. Six holders suffice. With three loose distinctions, six supports of arity at least three require at least eight shared distinctions. The six-holder construction attains that lower bound. If four joining parents are allowed with one remaining holder Bob, any target parent meeting Bob must have no target neighbor for Bob to stay witnessed-separate. Its candidate pairs would then all need Bob as witness, holding the whole target and excluding that proposal.

The stronger separation requires Bob to neither meet nor share a witness with any target parent. While Bob is spent, any single holder separating him from all remaining target parents would bound a region containing no loose distinction. Summing its parity equations would impose a nonempty restriction on a proper subset of the separating holder’s support, violating its full law. Thus there are two internally disjoint routes from Bob to distinct target parents. Each uses at least two intermediates, so a target of $p$ parents requires at least $p+5$ holders. The seven- and eight-holder controls attain that bound. Their distinction counts are for the stated doubled-meeting specimens.
