# Can the holdings tell which knots were original?

The same two knots can be the whole original start or a partial first layer from one knot. Their future birth lists can differ at the smallest possible size under each of the two stipulated knot laws. The source membership can be made recoverable in an unbounded class with a physical arity difference. The unrestricted reader needs additional information on some identical displays.

The proof covers the smallest roster, common unary and common pair examples at every arity, the linear cost for one partial layer, and the logarithmic cost of historical membership under the separate complete-boundary promise. At a true boundary all compatible complete starts give the same subsequent ordinary growth. In a known original without a single-meeting restriction, repeated meetings may supply a positive mark. Explicit old flags are additional input. The finite experiment used 640 lawful starts and five independently replayed receipts. The second freeze covers the supplemental inverse-boundary test. Evidence is in-task, with independent program review pending.
