## Parity of the physical supports

Connect each loose distinction to one auxiliary exterior for
the parity calculation. The sum of the physical equations over
a set of knots records exactly the distinctions crossing its
cut. Every parity equation forced by the allowed relation is
such a sum. The held support of one knot is a cut enclosing
that knot. If deleting the knot leaves the other side connected,
the only cuts supported inside its held distinctions are the
empty cut and its full support. Indeed a cut using none of the
connections between remaining holders must keep all of them
on the same side. Thus the projection on the knot is exactly
full even parity. Its full support is constrained, every
proper projection is free, and the zero assignment is allowed.

When the hub of the four-knot source is removed, all three
leaves still reach the auxiliary exterior. When a leaf is removed,
the surviving leaves remain joined through the hub and each
reaches the exterior. The ordinary three-route arrangement
remains connected when one knot is removed. If an end is removed
the other end still joins all paths. If an interior knot is
removed the two unaffected routes still join the ends. Each
remaining route fragment meets one of those ends. At least two
original loose holders remain, so the auxiliary exterior stays
connected. Multiplicity of a meeting changes its contribution
to the support and leaves these connectivity arguments intact.

The machine check stores a connectivity tree for every

## Not-all-equal support check

On the four-knot source, a prescribed nonconstant hub word
extends by opposing one loose value at each leaf to that
leaf's shared value. For a prescribed leaf word, the other
two shared values at the hub can be set differently, then
the other leaves can be completed independently.

On the three-route source, prescribe a nonconstant word on
one holder. Assign opposite values to every doubled meeting
whose two values were free. If a prescribed doubled pair is
itself constant, an affected interior neighbor farther from
the ends has another free doubled meeting. A neighbor next
to an end can use its loose distinctions or the single
meeting to the end. At most one single meeting incident to
either end needs a fixed value from such a repair. The other
two routes let that end take both values. When the prescribed
holder is itself an end, all interior holders already have
an unaffected doubled meeting. In the all-doubled control
an end with one prescribed constant pair can use another
of its free doubled meetings.

The literal not-all-equal checker pins each possible word
on each physical support. Exactly the nonconstant words can
be extended. The receipt contains a verified complete allowed
assignment for every successful pin. The canonical knot checker
accepts each resulting anonymous relation and arity. These
assignments witness what the source permits. Growth reads
