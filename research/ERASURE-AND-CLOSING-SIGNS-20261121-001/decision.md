# Erasing the record of a change and signing a closing knot

These two independent studies take up the two missing operations in the earlier measurement experiment. B286 asks whether a knot can erase a record of which change occurred and then write an answer that depends on a relative sign. B251 asks how often ordinary growth produces a closing arrangement that admits a signed reading, and what a reader in that arrangement could actually record. Each study has its own investigator, prospective design, independent advisor and stop condition.

## Two checks independent of the investigators’ implementations

For the erasure test, take one eligible pair of ordinary words and one idle word. At an eligible pair the added pulse gives equal amplitudes in magnitude for keeping and exchanging the words, with a quarter turn in phase for the exchange. Apply the pulse twice and undo the ordinary exchange. All three words return. The active pair has accumulated a factor of *i*. If the idle word kept its original phase it has accumulated 1, and these two outcomes differ by an observable relative phase after coherent recombination. Giving the idle word a phase of exp(iπ/4) on each pulse instead gives all three the same final factor of *i*. Both prescriptions give exactly the same probabilities when tested on the ordinary words individually. The difference between the two histories of *not changing a value* is part of the new dynamical assumption.

There is a stronger limit for the second choice of idle phase. Every permitted permutation of complete words fixes their uniform sum. The alternative balanced pulse is (I + iP)/√2 for a complete involution P, including its fixed words. It multiplies the uniform sum by one common phase. Any sequence of these pulses, their inverses and ordinary reversible permutations preserves the magnitude of the sum of amplitudes. A single complete input word has sum 1. A balanced real minus pair has sum zero even when tensored with an arbitrary residual workspace. A balanced real plus pair with the ordinary workspace returned has sum √2. This excludes those preparations in the stated closed reversible class. Additional initial coherent references, intermediate measurements and conditioning on their outcomes have to be assessed separately. An external real minus or plus fed to a reader is a different preparation. The literature’s criterion for a phase-sensitive read separates an operation that senses coherence from an ordinary observation of populations. This study locates the distinction in the signed behavior of one addressed exchange and in two actual keeper writes. For the closure test, a second calculation enumerated the forty indexed explosive rings under each law through four steps and all 160 equiprobable choices of a ring with two labelled companions of arity three or four. Permuting the original labels does not alter the incidence counts. Within a founder, changing which of its loose distinctions makes a particular payment only renames marks with the same old holder. Under parity every ring remains open. In each mixed start a companion closes after as many steps as its initial arity. The three-place companion closes with eight knots and excludes the odd operator reading. The four-place one closes with sixteen and admits it. Forty mixed choices have zero admitting components, eighty have one and forty have two. Under this particular weighting the exact chance of at least one admitting component is 120/160 and the fraction of closing components that admit the reading is 160/320. The entire mixed source stays open in all 160 cases.

Under the specified supplementary weighting there are 64 ways to duplicate a subset of the six older meetings. A set of *k* duplicated meetings receives integer weight 7^(6−k) out of 8⁶. Every one of the 64 reaches a five-knot closing whole in one parity step, with 10 + k distinct marks. Each admits a reading after its closing parity sign is reversed. The four-step unsigned not-all-equal comparisons have no zero-fresh closing event on any of these three enrolled finite panels. These exact checks of defined populations are independent of the fresh draws used for the quoted sample fractions and intervals. ## What the two signs mean operationally

The parity sign at an ordinary closing birth is the parity of the distinctions it has consumed. In a connected component with no loose distinctions after that birth, summing all parity equations counts each distinction twice. If every older knot was even, the closing inputs have even parity already. Reversing that one requirement removes every complete ordinary word. Reversing one requirement in each of two disjoint components leaves *each* one inconsistent. A zero count can be read by an outside checker. There is no post-closure word in which an ordinary internal keeper could report that it saw the odd alternative. The comparison with supplied commuting operators has a different preparation and observable.

On five knots with every pair meeting once, the even closure retains 64 complete ordinary words. The single-odd version retains zero, and deleting *any* one signed rule restores 64. An independent three-qubit realization gives the odd closure a complete two-reader measurement. For the five identical question pairs, eight joint outcomes each have probability 1/8. For the twenty distinct pairs, 32 joint outcomes each have probability 1/32. This gives 5,720 zeros among 6,400 entries and certain shared-bit agreement. This finite operator witness does not turn an impossible ordinary word into an available input. [Independent word counts and operator tables](independent_checks.py)

In the erasure study, giving every closed, reversible pulse the same phase convention on idle and eligible words prevents a deterministic clean source of real opposite signs from a complete ordinary singleton. Measuring the auxiliary and retaining an outcome changes that source class. On the two-word orbit one outcome of the alternative instrument projects a singleton onto the real minus combination and the other onto the real plus combination, each with probability 1/2. The source can therefore *herald* a chosen sign if this measurement, selection and its unaccepted launches are supplied. Clearing an ordinary route bit by itself implements neither this source nor the coherent interference read.
