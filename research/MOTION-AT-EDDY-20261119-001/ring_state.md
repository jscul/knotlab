# Transferring the state stored on a ring

This question arose after the primary freeze. Two results distinguish the structural eddy, the closed parity state on its old shared distinctions, and the pulse which can pass its attachment. The shortest transfer below is an allowed sequence. Its choice of events is supplied by the construction.

## What the moving pulse preserves

Take a connected, all-live, funded, simple source with no original cycles of length three, four or six, and the two-parent and loose-distinction properties stated immediately below. A direct check of those properties also suffices when the source has a six-cycle, provided its original three- and four-cycles are still excluded. Use its fully grown size-two stage, as in the straight joined rings of this study. At this stage all old loose distinctions have been spent. Each wedge (two original shared distinctions in succession) has one two-parent child, each remaining unary child has one parent, and all new children still have loose distinctions. Each wedge Q gives a four-distinction least change, two original shared distinctions and its two private parent-to-child distinctions. To see the construction directly in the named starts, consult the catalogue. [Physical responses](patterns.md)

Fix all currently loose distinctions at zero, and express a state as a difference from an allowed reference. At every two-parent child its two inputs must agree. The unary input is zero. Subtract, by symmetric difference, exactly the wedges indicated by those equal ones. This clears each private distinction once, leaving an even set Z of original shared distinctions at every old junction. Conversely every such Z and every subset of wedges gives an allowed closed state, and the representation is unique. Thus Z is unchanged by any sequence of ordinary wedge firings, including every step of the travelling pattern. The minimum size of a *single closed change* which writes a specified simple L-ring into Z is L. Replacing two occupied private inputs by their wedge's two old distinctions cannot increase support size, and the all-ring support itself attains L.

## What becomes possible with temporary loose changes

Allow every four-distinction change that is a smallest response through at least one of its distinctions, including private inputs. For an even subcollection Z of original shared distinctions that has even incidence at every old knot, let n be the least number of wedges whose **original pairs alone** sum to Z. Then changing a raw ring state by Z, with every private and loose value restored, takes **exactly 2n elementary four-distinction changes**. If the number of original distinctions in Z is odd, no such sequence exists. Adding the least changes of size two leaves the odd obstruction in place.

**Classify the events.** Represent a loose distinction as a meeting with one auxiliary exterior used solely to test which changes are even at the real knots. A smallest response through an ordinary input is a cycle through it. A four-cycle on ordinary distinctions has one new child and is its wedge. With zero new children it would be an excluded original four-cycle. Two children would have the same two parents, impossible in this first layer. A four-cycle through the exterior has form exterior, child, old knot, different child, exterior. It uses zero original shared distinctions and two private inputs. This four-change is least through each private input because simplicity, the separated pair of parents and the absence of old loose distinctions exclude any shorter cycle through it. A non-simple even four-set splits into two loose pairs and is least through none of its entries. These alternatives prove the event classification. Each toggles either two original distinctions or zero. A least two-change toggles only loose distinctions.

**Count the firings.** If q different ordinary wedges fire an odd number of times, their original pairs must realize Z, so q is at least n. They leave 2q different occupied private inputs. Every exterior event clears at most two of these. At least 2n events are necessary. To attain the bound fire a minimum collection of n wedges. The outstanding private inputs at each old junction come in even number, by parity of Z there. Pair them arbitrarily at that holder. The two inputs of each pair lead to different children. Toggle them and one chosen loose distinction on each child. Choose the same loose distinction each time that child is used. Each participating two-parent child then has its chosen loose distinction toggled twice, and all private values return to zero in exactly n exterior events.

**Compute n for two rings.** Regard original shared distinctions as points for this calculation and allow a unit step between two whenever they meet at a knot. This finite arrangement is connected. A set of adjacent pairs has its odd endpoints in Z. Splitting its used links into trails matches up the endpoints. Conversely, choose a matching of Z, link each pair by a shortest route, and cancel any repeated links. These two operations show that n equals the minimum total matching distance. They also establish existence for every Z of even size.

For two edge-disjoint five-rings whose closest knots are d steps apart, the closest pair of original ring distinctions is d+1 steps apart in this last reading. At least one of the five matched pairs must cross, since each ring contributes an odd five. The other four pairs cost at least one each. Choose a nearest cross pair, remove its two chosen distinctions, and match the other four on each ring in adjacent pairs. This attains **n=d+5**. Starting with the first whole ring present, the minimum to finish with only the second present is therefore **2d+10**. The three studied separations 4, 8 and 20 cost 18, 26 and 50 events. Every intermediate state of each displayed witness was checked on the full physical table and each auxiliary move against the catalogue of least responses. The bound is for the stated funded, cycle-restricted source class and the stated unit of update. Here d measures the minimum source-junction separation, allowing all available paths.

The original ring's state by itself has five old shared distinctions. No combination of these events can create it starting at zero. Two rings can exchange that state. The full calculation on all 14 named stages tests the span of the entire set of minimum responses, and a second computation constructs shortest transfers. Neither the rising number of junctions in a growing end nor the eddy's structural rate depends on which state occupies a ring.
