## Implications for contact networks

- **Homological networks.** In laws that compose like cycles, networks are
  homological: twisted finite-field cohomology and frame matroids.
- **Networks alone certify nothing.** Every group's networks look graphic, so
  networks alone cannot certify cycle-like composition.
- **Two kinds of binding.** Linear binding lives inside cycle-like
  composition, atomic binding outside it.
- **No distance.** Graphs are recognized, not created, so no distance follows.
