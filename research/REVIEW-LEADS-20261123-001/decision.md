## What the six say together

**No setting arises inside an ordinary history.** Holdings never gain a distinction, values change no birth, parent
groups follow from the holdings, and which loose distinction a knot pays is a renaming (tickets E and C). Every table
with settings in the record, the stalling tables and the tables of ticket C alike, compares different starts. Under a
one-birth-at-a-time rule the order of births is open and does change what happens elsewhere (tickets D and E), but
nothing chooses an order.

**The wall for a hidden excess is the second holder.** With settings supplied and every present word weighted equally,
a distribution with hidden settings and a score of 20/9 exists, and in the same family the score passes the quantum
limit at larger weight ratios. Knots with two holders per distinction did not make it in 506 million pairs, and one
shared distinction cannot by argument. A structure of 18 ordinary three-place knots makes it exactly once six internal
distinctions may have a third or fourth holder (post hoc, [script](posthoc/three_holders_check.py)). Ordinary growth
never gives a distinction a third holder.

**The operator test is a reading of how births are funded and grouped.** It is exact, costs almost nothing and
separates the benchmark constructions. It does not single out sources: sparsely supplied draws mostly pass for twelve
steps, and starts supplied to fund their first joins all fail at step 1.

**Two leads are retired.** A learner made of example knots over window parities does not transfer, and two laws at
once do not turn a frequency into a prediction. Each leaves one thing: the learner's memory failing K3 is a sign that
its examples have no rule in its class, and a counting rule gives growth a carried count.
