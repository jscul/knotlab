# What survives when parity checks arrive out of order

Status: Proved

## Standard statement

Work over the two-element field on Boolean inputs. Fix distinct original inputs \(a,b,(x_i,y_i,z_i)_{i=0}^{h-1}\), with \(h\ge3\). Write \(Q_i=x_i y_i+ab\) and \(F_i=z_iQ_i\). The supplied strict CNF is the exact private-chain encoding of \(F_i+F_{i+1}=c_i\) from [the source proof](../../SHARED-REPORT-CANCELLATION-20261115-001A/SHARED-REPORT-CANCELLATION-20261115-001A-P001_strict_checks/proof.md). Indices on a cycle are taken modulo \(h\). The primitive calculus uses disjunctions of zero equations of formal degree at most two. The original clauses and their private bits are available to every refutation below.

For every nonempty proper subset of cycle checks, its sum is cubic in the original inputs. Even one disjunction of arbitrarily many quadratic zero equations, projected over any finite number of fresh unconstrained Boolean variables, cannot express that sum's required truth set. Within the particular dictionary of raw pair names \(u=ab,p_i=x_i y_i\), one quadratic equation can express the sum exactly when \(u\) and the \(p_i\) for all surviving reports are available. On the identical strict input, four conditional proof lines per connected path suffice to refute every odd cycle in any arrival order. For fixed report arity the construction emits \(O(h)\) primitive deduction nodes and \(O(h\log h)\) bits of explicit proof. Minimal unsatisfiability of the strict source gives a matching \(\Omega(h)\) explicit axiom-use bound. The proof concerns the cost of these representations and certificates.

## Nearest known result

Path elimination in binary parity constraints and inconsistency along a cycle of odd total charge are classical. Dechter's variable-elimination framework includes resolution and bounded-width elimination. The compared objects here are the literal clauses for gated quadratic reports on common input bits, and proof lines with an explicit formal degree bound. The four-line construction, the one-line obstruction, and the exact certificate costs for this encoding are proved below. [Dechter, Bucket elimination](https://ics.uci.edu/~dechter/publications/r76A.pdf) · [Khaniki, bounded-degree resolution](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2020/034/)

## Which reports survive

Let \(S\) be a subset of check indices, \(c_S=\sum_{i\in S}c_i\) and \(D=\{i:\mathbf1_{i-1\in S}\ne\mathbf1_{i\in S}\}\). An interior report occurs twice and cancels. The sum has left side
\[
 c_S+J_D,\qquad J_D=\sum_{i\in D}x_i y_i z_i+ab\sum_{i\in D}z_i .
\]
For a proper nonempty subset \(k=|D|\) is twice the number of maximal runs of consecutive selected checks. Every private cubic \(x_i y_i z_i\) in the display is a distinct monomial of the unique Boolean multilinear form. Equivalently, set \(a=b=0\) and set every other triple to zero. On the three remaining bits the sum is \(x_i y_i z_i+c_S\). Every quadratic has even truth-table parity there, while the restricted sum has odd truth-table parity. The zero set determines a bit-valued polynomial as a function. The empty and complete subsets have constant sums.

Writing \(z_i(p_i+u)\) proves sufficiency of the stated pair names. If a needed \(p_i\) is missing, the preceding restriction makes every other available raw pair name constant while preserving \(x_i y_i z_i\). If \(u\) is missing, set all \(x,y\) and the other \(z\)'s to zero and retain \(a,b,z_i\). All the available local names vanish and \(abz_i\) remains. This proves the necessity only for the specified dictionary and a single equation. In the original named certificate the first multiplication that fails after inlining both names is zero-based row 16. It multiplies \(z_0\) by \(u+p_0\), printing \(abz_0+x_0y_0z_0\) on the raw inputs. The row's selected operand, multiplier and conclusion are in the [source trace](../../SHARED-REPORT-CANCELLATION-20261115-001A/SHARED-REPORT-CANCELLATION-20261115-001A-P001_strict_checks/trace.json).

## One quadratic proof line, even with freely projected witnesses

Restrict attention to the original coordinates used by \(J=J_D\). Extra coordinates in an alleged exact description could be fixed arbitrarily. Both truth sets \(J=0\) and \(J=1\) affinely span all these coordinates. To see this, let \(L\) be a nonconstant affine form and choose a variable \(v\) occurring in it. Since \(k\ge2\), there is a private triple \(T_i=x_i y_i z_i\) disjoint from \(v\). In the Boolean product \(LJ\), \(vT_i\) has the unique source \(v\cdot T_i\). It contains at most one of \(a,b\) and no other complete triple. Thus \(LJ\) has degree four. If \(L\) vanished on a truth set of \(J\), then \(LJ\) would be \(L\) or \(0\), which is impossible.

For \(k\ge3\), no nonzero quadratic or lower-degree polynomial \(H\) vanishes on either truth set of \(J\). If a top monomial \(m\) of degree \(d\le2\) differs from \(ab\), choose a private triple \(T_i\) disjoint from it. The degree-\(d+3\) monomial \(mT_i\) again has a unique source. It contains at most one of \(a,b\), and no second private triple. If \(ab\) is the sole top monomial, \(abT_i\) has a unique source too, since all other terms of \(H\) have degree at most one. The same reasoning includes a nonzero constant. Hence \(HJ\) has degree \(d+3\), whereas vanishing on a truth set would require \(HJ=H\) or \(HJ=0\).

For \(k=2\) there are eight original bits. Evaluate the \(1+8+28=37\) squarefree monomials of degree at most two on each truth set. On the 184 even assignments the matrix has rank 37. On the 72 odd assignments its rank is 36. The nonzero vanishing polynomial on the odd assignments is \(g=(1+z_i)(1+z_j)\). It has support 64, the assignments with both selectors zero. The elimination is exact over the two-element field. [Rank computation](solver.py) · [Checked ranks](checks.json)

Suppose one proof line \(\bigvee_s[P_s(x,w)=0]\) has the desired truth set after forgetting fresh Boolean witnesses \(w\). On **every excluded original assignment** each \(P_s(x,w)\) is identically one in \(w\). In its Boolean multilinear form, each witness-witness coefficient is constant and must vanish. Each coefficient of a single witness is affine in \(x\) and vanishes on the excluded truth set, so it vanishes identically by the affine-span result. Each disjunct therefore reduces to an original quadratic \(A_s(x)=0\). Its accepting indicator \(1+A_s\) vanishes on the excluded set. At \(k\ge3\) all these indicators vanish everywhere. At \(k=2\), an excluded even set yields the same conclusion. An excluded odd set allows only the indicators 0 and \(g\), whose combined support misses 120 of the 184 even assignments. This proves the one-line obstruction for either charge. Quantified witnesses here have no extra premises. The proof below uses several lines with genuine source deductions. The minimum number of such lines between two and four is left open.

## Four conditional lines per path

For the path from report \(i\) to report \(j\) with accumulated charge \(c\), retain the following four lines indexed by \(s,t\in\{0,1\}\):
\[
 [z_i\ne s]\ \lor\ [z_j\ne t]\ \lor\ [sQ_i+tQ_j+c=0].
\]
For any assignment precisely one setting of the endpoint selectors calls for its quadratic equation. Their conjunction thus expresses the exact endpoint relation. The interior reports along a path can realize either bit for each fixed \(a,b\), independently, so every allowed endpoint assignment actually extends through the original path.

The source derives these four lines for an incoming pair by considering the remaining six original bits under each pair of selector values. If the equation fails, the forbidden tuple has a width-eight clause derived from its six strict input clauses by five legal resolutions. If it holds, Boolean choices and degree-two multiplication by a single bit derive each required pair value under the assignment. Combine these equations and weaken to the asserted conditional line with the six assignment failures still present. Cut the six Boolean choices to remove those failures. A constant true equation can be obtained directly. This gives a bounded number of actual primitive nodes per source check. The program uses the [unnamed proof kernel](../../QUADRATIC-ROUTE-TRANSFER-20261115-001A/QUADRATIC-ROUTE-TRANSFER-20261115-001A-P001_quadratic_substitution/solver.py) through the same [strict row lifter](../../SHARED-REPORT-CANCELLATION-20261115-001A/SHARED-REPORT-CANCELLATION-20261115-001A-P001_strict_checks/solver.py).

For paths \(i\)–\(j\) and \(j\)–\(k\), fix the outside settings \(s,t\). For each middle setting \(v\), add the two selected equations. Their \(vQ_j\)'s cancel and the two equal middle failure literals contract to one. Cut the resulting two lines on the complementary middle values. The remaining equation is \(sQ_i+tQ_k+c+c'=0\) under the outside settings. Repeating this deduction for four settings costs 36 primitive nodes in the checked implementation. The argument also works for any supplied quadratics \(Q_i\) once its four initial lines per pair have been derived. A closed path of charge one with equal selectors leaves the false equation \(1=0\) at both bit values. Deletion and a final Boolean cut yield the empty line. The certificate includes the actual selected operands in a join and a check that changing a selected operand is rejected. [Guarded deductions](guarded.py) · [Primitive row trace](comparison.json)

Process the checks in their actual arrival order. The records at any moment are the maximal connected paths already received. An arriving check can create a new path, extend a path, or join two existing paths. Both adjacent records, when present, can be found by a map indexed by their endpoints. The last check closes the one remaining path. Each completed proper join reduces the number of path components by one compared to storing the incoming edge separately, giving \(h-2\) proper joins and one closing composition. At most \(\lfloor h/2\rfloor\) completed parts are live between arrivals, each summarized by four lines (72 or 184 rows in the ordinary proof). This counts facts needed for future joins. The full archived certificate also contains its original evidence and intermediate deductions. Identifiers need \(O(\log h)\) bits; apart from those identifiers each local node has bounded description. This bound for the guarded proofs holds under arbitrary arrival. In the named proof, adding disconnected checks into a single equation can instead take \(O(h)\) monomials on one line. Its linear node count and its encoded length are distinct. ## Parallel depth and the exact cost of a full forbidden row table

With all inputs available together, proof depth can be separated exactly in order of growth from total work. Count an input node at depth zero and a binary resolution at one plus its deeper parent. In the ordinary proof the six-clause input chain adds at most five levels, a path join eliminates three middle bits in three resolution levels, and the last step over eight remaining bits adds eight levels. A balanced grouping has depth at most \(13+3\lceil\log_2(h-1)\rceil\) and linear total nodes. Conversely, since all \(M=\Theta(h)\) strict clauses must be ancestors of the empty clause and each resolution has at most two parents, depth \(d\) can reach at most \(2^d\) distinct input leaves. Thus this family has optimal ordinary resolution depth \(\Theta(\log h)\) and optimal node count \(\Theta(h)\). The checker also measures the longest dependency chain of two actual nine-check proofs with identical node counts. This parallel depth assumes all source checks are already available. [Checked grouping depths](comparison.json)

If \(k\) reports survive, the complete flat table of the single consequence \(J_D=c\) has one row for each failed assignment on the shared pair and the \(k\) triples. For fixed \(ab=0\), one triple has seven zero and one unit outputs. For fixed \(ab=1\), it has five zero and three unit outputs. There are three shared assignments of the first kind and one of the second. Counting parity by the difference between even and odd counts gives
\[
 N_k(c)=\frac{4\cdot8^k-(-1)^c(3\cdot6^k+2^k)}{2}.
\]
For \(k=2\), this gives 72 forbidden rows at charge zero and 184 at charge one. For a six-check cycle, receive its three disjoint even checks first. The single XOR has 454,272 forbidden full rows; retaining the three separate pair relations uses \(3\cdot72=216\) full rows or at most twelve guarded lines. For eight checks the corresponding single table has 31,034,880 forbidden rows. The separate pair relations retain each check's parity and therefore imply their one total XOR. These figures are specific to the complete full-row table convention. Clause minimization, factored formulas and total proof work are different measures.

## All networks of pair checks and a control beyond pairs

Given the wiring list of any finite simple graph of these report pairs, propagate a bit along each connected component from a temporary zero value at its root. If a check conflicts with the assigned values, its edge and the two tree paths to their first common ancestor form a simple cycle of odd total charge. The traversal and cycle extraction take \(O(h+m)\) steps for \(h\) reports and \(m\) checks. Use the original indices and orientations of the extracted blocks in the full strict CNF to replay the ordinary or guarded refutation. Source blocks outside the cycle can be left unused. When all propagated values \(f_i\) are consistent, choose \(a=b=0,x_i=z_i=1,y_i=f_i\). Each private split chain can be satisfied by making its successive bits true exactly while its original prefix has no true literal. This is an explicit strict-CNF model. Recognizing an unknown encoding inside arbitrary shuffled 3-CNF is a separate input problem. [Cycle extraction, original-input replay and strict model](solver.py) · [Matched checks](checks.json)

The exact promised **single-cycle encoding** has an additional decision shortcut. From the number \(N\) of declared variables and \(M\) of strict clauses, set \(R=M/6\). Then \(h=(N-2-5R)/3\) and the number of one-charges is \((R-72h)/112\). Its parity decides this promise problem from the header alone when the exact roster, encoding and single-cycle promise are supplied. It produces no deletion-proof certificate. Two cycles joined by a single zero-charge check give a matched control. Put both one-charges on one cycle or put one on each cycle. The headers and charge counts agree, yet only the first case is satisfiable. Neither this header property nor explicit proof size lower-bounds the time of general SAT deciders.

For a bounded next test, take the four triple checks on label sets \(012,034,135,245\) with charges \(0,0,0,1\). Their sum is false, each three-check subsystem has eight satisfying assignments of the six report bits, and every two-check sum still has four reports. This small control leaves open the best grouping and proof size for families of three-report checks. [Exhaustive six-bit control](checks.json)

## Evidence and limits

In fact, Gaussian elimination decides consistency for arbitrary supplied parity equations on these reports, of any arity. The simultaneous realization above sends any prescribed report vector to original bits, so elimination loses no constraint. Raising the check arity changes which *proof reports* can be passed at constant cost. It leaves this decision shortcut intact. One extra bounded-arity non-affine constraint alongside parity also has a direct test: enumerate its finitely many allowed assignments and test the remaining linear system under each. An attempt to confront the parity shortcut for general SAT must account for growing interacting non-affine constraints and possible equivalent encodings.

The checker rebuilds the guarded and ordinary proofs on the same six-check strict CNF, records the block order of actual guarded input-axiom uses, validates feasible models, checks all arrival permutations from three to eight positions at the scheduling level, and compares all 729 absent/even/odd simple labelled graphs on four vertices against direct enumeration. Its independent sources also test local meaning and reject a changed quadratic pivot. The symbolic construction covers arbitrary length. These are same-task V1 checks. The surviving cubic gives an obstruction for one specified proof representation. The explicit refutations give a total-work shortcut on these inputs. A worst-case lower bound for all SAT decision algorithms remains open. [Exact comparison](comparison.json) · [Checked counts](checks.json)
