# Prospective measurement design for “two knots, one decision”

The owner asks whether, “if we have 2 knots (1 decision) maybe revealing one automatically reveals the other one.” This is one study of two specified complete experiments and lawful controls. No new outcome from either benchmark has been calculated for this design. The earlier perfect tables and their different operator verdicts are public, so the evidence is exploratory. The signed experiments have no admissible ordinary word for their whole. Their local rules remain genuine relations. Treating the signed holdings as a catalogue of questions, permitting certain coherent changes on a separate lawful carrier, preparing two correlated carriers, and measuring them are explicit postulates. The canonical knot test, ordinary growth permissions and ordinary law of a knot are unchanged.

## 1. The one measurement law and its actual inputs

The sole primary rule takes an ordered list of named holdings, each finite local allowed-word relation in its listed mark order, two reader roles, and a ready private apparatus per reader. The finite cases have at most twelve binary marks and twelve places shared by pairs of holders. Actual mark and holder names will be printed in all certificates. Its pre-question compiler may inspect the full *structural* catalogue, including its supplied local odd requirement, but receives no reader's current or future setting, archive probability, favourable sign stencil, known representation, planar/nonplanar answer, acceptance certificate or target score. No setting-pair lookup is permitted. Preparation occurs before the independent setting choices. The first comparison also executes the owner's literal local matching proposal below.

For each local relation enumerate **all** its affine parity identities, represented by a subset of its named marks t and the common bit b = sum over t of the local answer, modulo two. An empty local relation is a setup refusal. Stack the identities to make A and b, and let W be the physical words w with A w = 0, with every sign removed. This is an unsigned change carrier, not the impossible original charged whole. Each named mark e has an ordinary bit w_e in this carrier. A prospective read may flip an actual named mask d_e and acquire the real sign (−1)^(w_e+s_e), where s_e is one setup bit. In symbols,

~~~text
U_e |w> = (−1)^(w_e+s_e) |w XOR d_e>.
~~~

Seek the masks and signs together by exact binary linear equations. Each d_e preserves every homogeneous row of A. Its e-bit is zero, so the operation undoes itself. For e,f in one holding, d_e[f]=d_f[e], so their two orderings give the same signed transformation. For each local identity (t,b) require XOR of its flip masks to be zero, and require sum of s_e over t plus sum of d_e[f] over earlier e, later f in t to be b. Take increasing literal mark order for this word. Its ordinary read sign w·t vanishes throughout W. These requirements test the returned word and relative sign of the actual ordered sequence. Deduplicate equations, keep the map back to their local origin, and return either a checked solution or a binary row combination giving 0=1. Independently multiply the actual returned signed permutations on W and check each required product, involution and local commutator; a bad source must fail a real equation or a physical word read.

Choose one solution without reference to either target table. In increasing e then increasing affected mark f order, prefer d_e[f]=0 whenever a solution remains. Then prefer s_e=0 in increasing e order. This fixes the private sign frame and prefers completely ordinary diagonal reads when they can work. Publish the masks, s, local product signs and null or nonzero changes, together with recoding controls. Passive relabelings transport a fixed compiled device. Re-solving with reversed literal names can select a different apparatus. Keep both their probabilities and/or explicit certificate differences. A rearrangement of a list of marks inside one holding must transport its output slots with it. Compare phase changes at an incidence with actually preparing a different instrument.

The same rule accepts any local relation supported by the finite substrate, including not-all-equal. For a solved device form each local simultaneous projection P(h,y) = product over e in h of (I+(−1)^y_e U_e)/2. An empty output projection has probability zero. Use the largest subspace K of W invariant under all U_e and contained in all kernels of forbidden P(h,y). This constraint rules out invalid local records under subsequent accesses as well. Compute by intersection and invariant closure if needed. On an affine input the solved local identities themselves imply K=W. For a diagonal solution K is obtained by enumerating ordinary w and retaining those whose actual output words satisfy **all** local relations. The enrolled cases fall under these exact methods when applicable; support the finite general definition or report a separately named implementation limit, never silently turn it into physical impossibility. A linear contradiction or K=0 is a setup refusal, computed before settings. The signed switch’s word-only kernel is an ordinary calibration control, not a source of this sign.

The coherent preparation is real and explicitly supplied. Prepare equal amplitudes on |w,w> over W using a basis of its lawful homogeneous changes, coherent independent binary controls and copying into the second initially zero carrier. Remove unused which-word controls coherently. Project one member onto K without resolving w. The public pre-setting herald has acceptance dim(K)/|W|. Conditional on success the common preparation is the normalized vector formed from the entries of the real projector onto K; both local reduced states are the uniform state on K. Trace the measured answer, previous physical state and new latches on every branch. A separately prepared diagonal equal-word mixture on the same coordinates controls what ordinary correlations alone can do. For diagonal K use the uniform mixture of |w,w> over its accepted words; for K=W dephase the equal-word superposition in its native w basis; more generally dephase the accepted doubled state in the full native word basis without silently reselecting. Ordinary legal words sampled directly from their known admissible set give an additional lawful conditional-source control, with its preparation weighting disclosed. Normalizing after acceptance cannot erase the rejected attempts. When the strict solver refuses, the preparation is absent on all attempts and the prepared status register reports this on both sides before either question. A persistent no-answer is a permitted local quantum response on an extended output alphabet; it gives no conditional binary-answer distribution.

## 2. Addresses, controls, records and repeated questions

A reader chooses a local holder by its *actual* ordered mark list. The private setting address is a one-hot bank of held latches representing its role's literal holders. Each involved bit can be encoded by 101 and 011 in a local ternary holding with fixed last bit 1. The reader supplies this address before the local interaction. Route a valid address to the compiled ordinary read of w_e, its actual conditional mark flips d_e, and its sign s_e at each requested place. These are physical marks in the separate local carrier. The rule does not find a valid state of the globally contradictory signed benchmark. Duplicate, out-of-role or unfitted addresses, capacity failure and a missing ready setup return a distinguished local failure; they are not legal triples. All budgeted ordinary attempts have preallocated memory; a separate insufficient-memory control records a failure without silently overwriting the previous answer.

For each requested mark prepare a fresh private binary auxiliary in its first encoded state; make the equal signed superposition of its two states, apply U_e only on its second state, and mix the auxiliary by the inverse two-state operation. Measuring that auxiliary yields the two effects above. Deposit the resulting bit in a fresh, three-place ordinary holding. 110 means ready, 101 and 011 record the two actual answers. A controlled exchange of the two differing marks performs each write. All three words satisfy even parity and not-all-equal, and no click on a ready row differs from a recorded zero. Use a distinct request receipt and fixed local end-of-slot mark to distinguish a missed write, a refusal, an idle reader and a completed request. The opposite wing and its clock never trigger those marks. Name the actual fired pair of local marks or its recorded absence in the replay. A different ordinary guard without a successful write may produce a separate missed-write indicator only when that indicator itself can be read locally. A joint coherent gate, correct sign and interference, local Born projection, funding and routing of the controlled pair exchange and an experimenter's independent choice of address are separately supplied. A legality-preserving exchange is not evidence of an autonomous growth trigger.

The two physical reading registers are separate after preparation. The same distinction label addresses the same signed transformation in both. The right wing uses its transpose on its local half. The real symmetric involutions make the chosen mark the same in this implementation. A common outcome correlation of the fresh paired preparation is distinct from a single writable ordinary mark still available to both distant readers. In the literal matching comparison this last common record instead is a centralized resource. Any physical birth, mutual shared address, keeper trigger, variable program trace or clock signal carrying both settings during the protocol would be a communication channel, and must be included as such. No hidden evaluator log supplied to either reader is admissible as a private transcript.

After a nonzero-probability local answer y, its data state changes by the joint projection and the normalized conditional state can be read again. The answer in its keeper remains even when the current data changes. An immediate repeat uses fresh latches and the actual post-state. An overlapping holding reads its shared names now and every unshared place anew. A changed, disjoint or incompatible question acts on the same current state. The outcomes may erase predictive information about a still-archived answer. No automatic reset between questions. An idle request is the identity and creates no answer. A setup failure or invalid address leaves the prior data and prior valid memory intact, while the attempt itself stays readable. An intentionally missing output keeper is a pre-read admission failure: do not disturb the state on this control. In a valid measurement followed by a deliberately disconnected writing coupling the local data DOES undergo the registered projection with its unread outcome marginalized, and no answer keeper fires. Compare these two failures. A request jointly unsupported in a branch of zero probability has no conditional state or duration estimate.

As an ordinary native control take the same initially lawful fixed holdings with two differently written allowed value words. In the unmodified ordinary rule group permission, cost and the original parents and fresh distinction counts are functions of the holdings. Changing only the value register can change a future read of that register. It cannot by itself manufacture a growth birth. Confirm this at a minimal funded pair and for the actual three-place writing patterns, deriving the available native event or its absence from holdings alone. Use the documented later switch’s nonzero ordinary-firing and zero sign contrast as an already established negative control. The clock’s birth and lost tick show how an actual payment and parent identity can be read. Compare a real local guard with the unchanged keeper attachment from that switch. They do not supply a general coherent trigger. Any explicitly added timing, controller, observation or memory is recorded separately.

## 3. A literal interpretation and one explicit imperfect calibration

The owner's literal comparison uses one unresolved common value when a set of current questions is asked centrally. Given the current requested holders, form all assignments to the union of their named marks such that every requested local relation holds, the occurrences of a shared mark are equal, and each requesting reader retains the values it last successfully read on names common to its previous and current holder. Draw uniformly among complete eligible union assignments. If none exist all asking wings get a failure and retain their previous successful memory. An idle wing retains its own memory and imposes no new condition. On success, a reader keeps the new holder and answers for the next overlap. Earlier written records remain available for comparison but are forgotten by this controller. This rule also serves one reader. The central check, weighting and simultaneous lookup are external operations. Test ordinary law and not-all-equal too. When there is only one shared mark do not reweight a union merely because that mark is one. A separate bias with weight two when the first shared mark is one is a changed comparison, not the production rule.

Keep a fixed signed calibration control in parallel with strict refusal. A raw named read U_e remains unique. At most two holders have it. Only the second holder in increasing literal holder order may reverse its *reported* sign on the local auxiliary and latch. This is an actual additional local controlled exchange rather than redefining the score. Its unmodified raw sign and its calibration bit are both available locally. Seek the smallest count of named shared distinctions requiring such a reversal to satisfy the local product demands by the same compiler. Enumerate by cardinality then lexicographically by the tuple of reversed mark names. Use the first feasible mask, then the same zero-first d,s rule. An endpoint reversal changes the local product RHS by parity of reversed occurrences in each row. Use the calibration at both source projection and local read. If there is no solution, preserve the refusal. An empty mask is the strict rule. This is a *separately marked intervention* run on every catalogue. Report every altered shared agreement and all unaltered ones, valid local answer strings, raw and recorded scores. Count every unconditional attempt, all four disjoint input pairs and all markings producing the minimum under the alternative if feasible. A different orientation can realize a quantum-permitted imperfect table but does not provide a perfect one-decision device. ## 4. Fixed evidence, trials, privacy and stop

Use the original six triples (0,1,2), (3,4,5), (6,7,8), (0,3,6), (1,4,7), (2,5,8). Holder 5 requires odd parity. Alice receives 0,1,2; Bob 3,4,5. For a stronger test Alice asks all its holders in increasing order, while Bob chooses one fixed holder and repeats it in every slot. Retain each shared-bit mismatch on the round where its holder pair is asked, changed answers on immediate repeats of the fixed holder, wrong local words, absent records, any setup failures, and the entire one-sided law M_h of the sweep. Also run an adaptive local script in both directions. Let h be the least holder of the observing role, e its least mark shared with the other role, k the next holder in that local role (or h in a single-holder control). At slot 1 read h with the other side idle. At slot 2 hold the local side idle while the remote side chooses the least permitted holder containing e or the least omitting e, with idle as fallback if an option is absent. At slot 3 reread h.
