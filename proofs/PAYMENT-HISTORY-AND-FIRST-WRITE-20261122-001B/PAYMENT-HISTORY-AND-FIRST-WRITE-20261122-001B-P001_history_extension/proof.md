# The same paid history always admits a quiet joint write

**Theorem.** Consider the admitted finite connected presentations with each distinction held by one or two knots. Fix the law and reference convention, a complete reachable history before its first deviation, and any admitted binding of the next funded cut. Specify every physical payment and every fresh grant. One legal completion assigns the transported reference to **all** fresh marks simultaneously. The same complete past can admit a different completion, but its payments cannot require a first deviation. [Frozen scope](../../../research/PAYMENT-HISTORY-AND-FIRST-WRITE-20261122-001/preregistration.md)

## What the past fixes

Let \(x\) give the actual value on every old distinction. It agrees with the old reference everywhere. The verifier can fix all earlier rosters, payments, coholdings and words. In the current cut fix the actual parent group of each newborn \(C\), the old distinctions \(P_C\) those parents will pay, and the new distinctions \(F_C\) it will receive. This conditions the complete-cut rule on its physical binding before any new value has been specified. It adds no stage or ordering to the growth.

The planner derives proposals from the current live holders, their loose marks and the existing witnesses for their meetings. The installed veto reads the current actual and reference values on the entire holdings of the proposed parents. At this quiet prefix each multiple-parent proposal passes the guard. Funding still requires enough loose marks at **every** parent. A binding specifies which particular loose mark each parent gives to each child. The grant calculation uses the resulting roster and its subsequent structural demand. None of these operations consults a sequence of ancestral assignments to choose a fresh value. [Complete-cut operator](../../../research/ORDINARY-DYNAMICS-20261120-001/end_operator.md) · [Veto and payment rule](../../../research/STATE-FEEDS-GROWTH-20261119-001/definitions.md)

After payment the parent retains that same distinction and its value. The child gains it as a second holder, alongside the marks fresh to its own holding. Previously shared distinctions persist at their existing holders. Distinct obligations at a common parent consume distinct previously loose marks. Newly born siblings can share a parent and depend on the same older constraints. Their fresh banks are disjoint and each new constraint contains only its own fresh bank. A newborn’s inherited values, and the later fact that one of its private marks has acquired a coholder, are physical records. The fill rule uses the values of its actual paid marks and its own relation. The peer identities, earlier payment sequence and the verifier’s reconstructed history do not enter that rule.

## Extension of that particular history

For each newborn put

\[
W_C(x)=\{w\in\{0,1\}^{F_C}: (x|_{P_C},w)\text{ obeys the law at }C\}.
\]

With the old history fixed pointwise, the exact set of joint fresh extensions is \(\prod_C W_C(x)\). Restrict any legal extended word to the individual fresh banks to get one element of the product. Conversely, append any one choice from each bank to the **same** \(x\). Every old relation is already satisfied and every new relation is satisfied by construction. The common old constraints introduce no further condition on the fresh choices once \(x\) and the payments are fixed. This also proves that fixing any lawful tuple of sibling writes leaves the focal factor unchanged. If a fixed sibling word already deviates, that additional condition supplies activity *in this cut*. It establishes no need for the focal child to deviate and no activity required by an earlier quiet history.

It remains to construct the reference in each factor.

- **Parity, positive grant.** With \(f=|F_C|\ge1\), any old paid word has \(2^{f-1}\) completions. Choose \(f-1\) bits freely and the last bit to make the total parity even. In the registered zero-reference comparison every paid value on the quiet path is zero, so the transported all-zero fresh bank is legal.
- **Parity, zero-fresh closing birth.** On a connected presentation the ordinary implication exception applies to the sole birth taking **all** old loose distinctions. Sum the parity equations over every old knot in any lawful old word. A twice-held distinction occurs twice and cancels. Each loose distinction occurs once. Thus the parity of precisely the full collection now paid is zero, regardless of its individual values. The unique empty fresh word is legal, also for a general lawful nonzero old parity assignment. A global condition on this already satisfied payment cannot demand a new bit. [Whole-loose closure proof](../../../research/ORDINARY-DYNAMICS-20261120-001/end_operator.md)
- **Not-all-equal.** Each nonempty funded birth in the admitted domain has a positive grant. Each parent gives one previously loose mark and keeps at least two other places. The old relation permits an assignment with those designated payments homogeneous, so the new not-all-equal relation on its paid places alone is not implied. The ordinary grant is therefore at least one, even when the *actual* payments happen to be mixed. For a mixed paid word all \(2^f\) fresh words are legal. For constant paid value \(b\) only the constant fresh word \(b^f\) is excluded. The first legal fresh reference in transported slot order is all zeros if the paid word is mixed or all ones. If the paid word is all zeros, it ends in 1 with zeros before it. The alternative transport writes \((1-b)^f\) for homogeneous paid values and uses the first legal word for mixed values. Both constructions satisfy the actual new holding because its payments still equal their references. [Not-all-equal grant theorem](../../../research/NAE-GROWTH-20261119-001/nae_growth.md)

The tuple of these references lies in the product. Under the older minimum-difference fill this tuple is the unique minimum on a quiet history. Under all-lawful filling any factor with more than one element permits a first deviation at that child with all siblings at reference. These are support statements. A numerical value can be forced **to equal** its reference. Conditioning a funded row on its paid values also differs from testing K1–K3 on its unrestricted relation. ## The three-loose founder with every payment fixed

Take the archived founder with private marks \(p,u,j\), actual and reference 000 for parity or 110 for not-all-equal. H, U and J are defined by receiving the indicated physical marks. Three unary display slots admit \(3!=6\) assignments of these marks. A permutation can change the numbered slot occupied by H while preserving H’s definition by payment. At the quiet next cut the physical groups are HU, HJ and UJ. There are \(2^3=8\) independent bindings at each such prefix because every parent pays its two private marks to two different groups. Exchanging H’s payments while keeping its old names fixed really changes which of two different joint children comes to hold each mark. At the third quiet cut the three joint children have only one loose mark apiece and fund one triple in a unique binding. This gives 6, 48 and 48 named pre-write histories at the three cuts, per reference. They are display and binding counts, without sampling weights.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Founder pays \(p,u,j\) | Each bank is 00 or 11, reference 00. Eight joint words. | H,U have 00,01,10 with reference 00. J has 01,10,11 with reference 01. Twenty-seven joint words. | Same allowed words. J’s reference is 11. Twenty-seven joint words. |
| Quiet H,U,J fund HU,HJ,UJ | Each single fresh mark must be 0. One joint word. | HU receives 00 and must write 1. Of HJ and UJ, the child receiving J’s 0 must write 1, the child receiving J’s 1 may write 0 or 1 with reference 0. Two joint words. | HU again must write 1. HJ and UJ both receive mixed values, each admitting 0 or 1 with reference 0. Four joint words. |
| All three previous writes at reference | Paid triple 000. One empty word at closure. | Paid triple 110 or 101 in HU,HJ,UJ order. Fresh 0 or 1, reference 0. | Paid triple 100. Fresh 0 or 1, reference 0. |

For the ascending example, H receives 0 and writes marks (3,4), U receives 1 and writes (5,6), J receives 2 and writes (7,8). Here 0,1,2 identify founder marks. At the second cut HU receives (3,5), HJ receives (4,7) and UJ receives (6,8). They write distinct new marks 9,10,11. On the primary quiet path, UJ’s actual paid values are 0 and 1. With every old word fixed, and the other two newborns fixed to their reference ones, both 0 and 1 on mark 11 are lawful. The branch staying quiet then pays (9,10,11) to the triple. All eight second-cut bindings are covered by deciding which child actually receives J’s 1. In 24 of the 48 named primary prefixes the quiet triple is 110 and in the other 24 it is 101.

## Reach of the conclusion

The previously recorded alternative starts from this same quiet, fully paid founder. Under primary not-all-equal H can write 01 instead of its legal 00 with U and J both at reference. Mark 4 then reaches a one-parent child. Its holding is 101 against reference 001, while its sibling holds 001. On that reached funded pair the installed veto sends four payments to separate children. Turning off the veto on those identical holdings admits the common child paid with 11 and 13. On the fully quiet first-cohort continuation H spends its two marks on earlier joint children, so that particular pair never forms. The earlier separate matched experiment holds two *nonquiet* sibling writes fixed to make the pair available on both H arms. This is evidence for a possible paid cause of veto. Selecting a future pair, a veto or a sibling’s deviation would add information beyond the prior payments. [Physical alternatives and guard-off control](../../../research/QUIET-START-AND-FIRST-WRITE-20261122-001/first_write.md)

Induct along any further cuts actually funded on the quiet continuation, applying the joint construction at each available binding. Every proposed multiple-parent group encountered on that branch is quiet and passes the installed veto. In particular, if a specified future group is eligible and funded on every continuation, the quiet continuation admits its join. A different future group may arise only after a deviation, or a whole cut may fail funding elsewhere. A closed end has no further birth. Where the archived parity feedback implementation stops a sole proposed birth below its permitted arity, it produces no new word. The ordinary convention admitting an implied low-arity closure has the empty-word result proved above. None of these stops can require a deviation. If the quiet path continues indefinitely, choosing the explicit reference at every finite cut gives a compatible quiet history throughout. No probability on such a history is asserted. [Arity and funding stops](../../../experiments/STATE-FEEDS-GROWTH-20261119-001/STATE-FEEDS-GROWTH-20261119-001-E001_feedback/growth.py)

Any local payment record arising as a restriction of one of these globally quiet histories therefore also has a quiet continuation. A partial view merely quiet at one holder might be compatible with activity elsewhere, and a recorded earlier veto can already require such activity. Imposing a future successful branch or an added rule that excludes the reference changes the extension problem. Within the declared transition, actual past payments determine what the child inherits and can change its range of possible fresh values. They never eliminate the joint quiet write.
