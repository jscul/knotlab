# Two added simple meetings on six founding knots

Prospective candidate 2 for independent Astra Max review. The original candidate remains unchanged. The primary question is whether six connected founding knots with precisely seven distinct meetings and at most one optional extra opening apiece can fund an ordinary whole closure in their first four cuts. If they can, two additions are the first eligible number of extra simple meetings at this size under the cap. A four-step null leaves a later closing question for each particular funded source with a connected live end. The complete population and all its funding stops remain the endpoint even if an early positive is found.
