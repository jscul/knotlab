## Challenged assumptions

The decision note records four workspace assumptions this epic challenges:
- that three-place winding contradictions may obey the shielding identity;
- that shielding is a two-literal law (it is a law of coins that reach two places);
- that the cylinder event is the analogue of a lone loop;
- that symmetry means lattice symmetry (vertex flips matter).
