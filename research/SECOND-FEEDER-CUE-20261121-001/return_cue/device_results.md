## Why these natural vetoes recall an earlier meal

An intact true X word certifies that the present holder was paid on some earlier turn. Its only new sources are an actual energy-confirmed meal and a forced return from a one-control holder after the last possible growth. Stays preserve a pending departure, including when a different feeder is paid. This proves certainty of an empty site under any policy within this game. It does not identify an arbitrary full feeder. There is a stronger statement about the natural current-only and truthful strict policies on these connected starts. Both eat upon first arrival at any full feeder. Suppose the return mark could veto an otherwise admissible eat at the most recently paid feeder A. Previous eat excludes a veto and every feeder has a control, so a move must just have returned there. The return rule implies a stable departure from already-paid A to one-control Y and a later move back. The initial cursor was at an original nonfeeder. Every original holder already had an original neighbor. A newborn older than A already had an older parent. The old contacts persist and same-cut newborns have disjoint grants. Thus a sole neighbor A makes Y a younger feeder attached only to A. Any previous encounter with Y passed through A first. If Y was already empty it had paid after A. If still full at arrival it paid then, because neither policy ever passes a full feeder on entry. Dying there supplies no subsequent A query. This contradiction also covers an unsuccessful eat on a later return to Y.

For an older certified site C to remain recoverable after the latest meal at W, the earlier stable departure from C into W must be kept while W pays. The first outgoing move at W must take its sole stable control, or that older reference is erased. Thus the first memory holds one for W. C has at least two controls, since otherwise the connected start would contain a disconnected two-holder component. Later rediscoveries can take a different route before the next meal. This proves the one-versus-many discrepancy on natural vetoes with the stated policies and reset. Histories that force the bot to pass a full feeder can break the first-arrival premise.
