# Which reading carries the growing end?

**Of the three proposed readings, three-distinction bond distance is the weakest that passes every requested test.** The pair flip bit fails on meet-once stages. Adding it to the three-distinction reading gives the same passes. A general theorem for one round at every finite size remains open. The new large example shows why proving that theorem by recovering all the parity equations cannot work.

Here “extends” means that the specified old renaming extends under some naming of the fresh distinctions to the entire next pair table. All comparisons fix every distinction in every live junction individually. The pair reading is the smallest bond size minus one. For triples we use the smallest bond size itself and a separate value for no common bond. Either counting convention gives exactly the same permutations.

The flip test asks whether two distinctions can change together while every other distinction stays fixed. This holds precisely when they have identical holders. Ordinary shared distinctions in a meet-once arrangement have different holder pairs, and any loose twins are already fixed at the growing end. Hence adding this bit leaves the marked pair group unchanged at every size on meet-once starts. In contrast, the six-junction start with repeated meetings has closed distinctions whose identical holders the flip bit reveals. ## What is needed once, and what must be checked for iteration

The full triple reading does more in the stated census. **Every surviving renaming on every one of the 30,189 stages preserves the whole parity equation space.** The new junctions take distinctions at the fixed growing end. Fixing their fresh names therefore carries the new equations and fixes the next live supports. Induction gives an extension of the original matching at every later lawful round, including beyond the size bound. The initial observation suffices for that matching. This also holds on all four named stages. All 31 census cases needing untruncated triple sizes were independently reconstructed and all 495 surviving maps checked literally. [All-round evidence and exact residuals](../../experiments/GROWN-END-READING-20261119-001/GROWN-END-READING-20261119-001-E001_readings/carry_census.md)

For **just the next pair table** the numerical triple size can be weakened in the tested population. Asking only whether each triple belongs to *any* bond also passes every requested stage. The two old census failures and the three minimum examples all admit this direct check. At 160 census stages, however, mere existence leaves renamings that change the equation space. The smallest such stage has eight junctions and 15 distinctions. Its swap of 5 and 6 keeps existence but changes a minimum bond size from six to seven. Its two growing branches still carry the pair table at every later round. This single later-round result does not decide the other 159 stages. At starting width 15 there are also four maps that carry the very next pair table but do not preserve the present full triple reading. Thus full triple sizes can discard good one-round matches. ## What is proved at every size

Pair and triple minima recover **every smallest bond through each individual distinction**. If two further distinctions can both participate with the first in a minimum cut, their required sides are compatible. For a collection compatible in pairs, the union of the required minimum sides satisfies all the marks at once. There is a second direct reading: for a marked pair of minimum bond size $m$, add every third distinction that leaves its minimum at $m$. If the resulting set has exactly $m$ members, it is the unique minimizing bond. In all three minimum examples these two operations and the marked equations span all 12 independent equations. This decides every future without searching for relabelings. The proof also gives a reached family with arbitrarily large junctions on which the equations are recovered. [Minimum-bond and rank proof](../../proofs/GROWN-END-READING-20261119-001A/GROWN-END-READING-20261119-001A-P001_transport/proof.md#all-shortest-bonds-at-any-one-distinction-can-be-read) · [Unbounded reached family](../../experiments/GROWN-END-READING-20261119-001/GROWN-END-READING-20261119-001-E001_readings/theory.md#a-reached-family-with-unbounded-arity)

There is a growth theorem even when some equations remain hidden. Suppose an unchanged part touches the growing part at exactly two junctions, and all the distinctions the renaming moves lie in the unchanged part. Reading an interior pair together with one suitable outside distinction gives the least cost of separating those two attachment junctions while crossing the marked pair. Subsequent exterior growth can only lower its own separating cost while the exterior part remains connected. The old readings then determine each future pair comparison, with no re-reading between lawful rounds. [Two-attachment proof and three-attachment boundary](../../experiments/GROWN-END-READING-20261119-001/GROWN-END-READING-20261119-001-E001_readings/obstruction.md)

**Recovering every equation is false in general, even for a reached meet-once arrangement.** A 235-junction start reaches 239 junctions and 643 distinctions. Exchanging two indices in its closed part preserves every pair and triple bond distance, every pair flip bit, and the entire growing end. Yet the image of one junction equation is violated by an explicit allowed change of four distinctions. This hidden change persists while the same matching still carries every later pair and triple table. There are 105 distinction-disjoint ways through the closed part connecting its two large junctions. Every set of at most three marked distinctions in the unsplit core can be held by a bond of size at most 80, too small to separate those two junctions. Attaching lawful growth at two junctions preserves these readings. The proof covers the complete tables. The literal checks cover the growth, equation mismatch, and all types of up to three core marks under index relabeling. [Reached construction and exact open case](../../proofs/GROWN-END-READING-20261119-001A/GROWN-END-READING-20261119-001A-P001_transport/proof.md#a-reached-self-renaming-that-changes-the-equations) · [Literal four-distinction certificate](../../artifacts/GROWN-END-READING-20261119-001/GROWN-END-READING-20261119-001-E001_readings/graft_check.json)

For an arbitrary growing end the unresolved issue is whether a hidden equation can affect a *minimum successor pair bond under every fresh naming*. At three attachment junctions a mixed bond already involves a minimum over three choices of which attachment is on its own side. The preceding two-attachment proof does not separate those alternatives. Both an unrestricted all-size transport theorem and an unrestricted failing next pair remain open. In particular, one-round sufficiency alone would not prove that a single reading transports a fixed matching forever.
