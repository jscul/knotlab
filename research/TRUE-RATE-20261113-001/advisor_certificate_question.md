Check a short argument for soundness. Be terse; list any gap.

Setting. NAE constraint system built by a one-definition iterated hyperedge-replacement grammar: definition D has 3 ports (vars 0..2) and locals (3..), a body of NAE knots K(a,b,c) and calls SELF(a,b,c) (each a fresh copy of D with ports bound to a,b,c). The bottomless pattern is the infinite unfolding from a root copy C_0 whose ports are free. G = port relation of the bottomless subtree of one copy (greatest fixed point; by compactness it is exactly the projection of the infinite subtree onto its ports). The ray: C_{j+1} = the copy made by the first SELF call of C_j, args a = (a0,a1,a2), so C_{j+1}.port[i] = C_j.var[a_i]. A bond is an inclusion-minimal non-shattered set of variables (projection of the allowed states misses a state; every proper subset is shattered). L(s) = least size of a bond containing a port of C_0 and a local of C_s, members anywhere. L_ray(s) = same with members restricted to ray copies; separately proved L_ray(s) >= lambda*(s - t) with lambda in {1/2, 1}.

Claim. If a_i = i for some i (port i of C_0 is port i of every C_s, by identity), and a size-3 bond {port i, local v, w} exists in the subtree of C_s for one s, then L(s) <= 3 for all s >= 1.

Argument. (1) Tree decomposition: the projection of the whole pattern onto the variables of subtree(C_s) equals {states of subtree(C_s) with port triple in R_s}, R_s = projection of the rest of the pattern onto C_s's ports. Exact because the rest meets subtree(C_s) only at C_s's three ports. (2) R_0 = all 8 states; R_{s+1} = projection onto the ray args of [C_s ports in R_s, C_s knots, C_s's non-ray SELF calls in G] (C_s's own subtree below the ray call is excluded, and the remaining side subtrees are exactly G). (3) Computed: R_1 = all 8 states, so R_s = all 8 for all s. Hence subtree(C_s) with its ports free is distributed exactly as the whole pattern, the same for every s. (4) A bond found inside subtree(C_s), truncated at depth 3 with boundary SELF calls replaced by G-clauses (exact, by (1) applied inside), is therefore a bond of the whole pattern at every s, with the same port i (= port i of C_0). Checked by SAT: triple constrained, all three pairs free.

Questions: Is (1)-(4) sound? Is the claimed counterexample to "the true bond grows whenever the ray bond grows" then a proof (by finite computation) for these families? Is it settled by construction (identity pass-through), i.e. does the bond measure reach at all here? Name the closest standard fact.
