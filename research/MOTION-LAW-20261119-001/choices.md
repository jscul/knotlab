# What the preceding motion study supplied

The complete-round growth law supplied the knots, their shared distinctions, and the allowed binary assignments. The travelling result supplied another law. With a fixed allowed reference, particular four-distinction changes as its events, and four local enabling conditions, one prepared ten-distinction state selected its own direction. Parity and relabeling constrain these choices. They leave different update laws, including a quiet one and several that agree on the tested trajectory. [Primary freeze](../MOTION-AT-EDDY-20261119-001/definitions.md) · [Outcome](../MOTION-AT-EDDY-20261119-001/decision.md)

**Readout for the owner.** Changing the names, reversing a coordinate description, or carrying the reference along with an allowed value recoding preserves the result. Keeping a reference, choosing the event menu, specifying the four enabling conditions and their reading radius, supplying a prepared state, resolving or retaining ties, and choosing reciprocal continuations at a seam are substantive choices. The local update reads no count of past events. The ten-distinction initial weight, fourteen-distinction peak, four firings per one-place copy, and return after 124 firings on the 31-knot ring are consequences within their stated classes. The counts, directions, and rates of births come from the growth law and source. [Proof and alternatives](../MOTION-AT-EDDY-20261119-001/minimality.md)

The comparisons below distinguish archived tests, deductions from the rule, and alternatives offered only to expose a choice. This audit rereads the recorded cases and code.
