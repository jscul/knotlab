# Reading the two interruptions

In the direct comparison the two enrolled knots have exactly two loose distinctions available in every arm. One of them, $R$, can tell whether its earlier birth included a particular donor simply by counting how many distinctions it still shares with earlier holders. The extra shared distinction is an actual past payment from that donor. When the other enrolled knot $S$ also received a different distinction from the same donor, the ordinary group rule requires their joint child. Divert either earlier payment to its specified alternative, and both knots still pay two real children. Their joint child disappears. The earlier marks remain shared with the donor while newly loose marks fund the later birth. [Proof and full payment table](proof.md#an-earlier-payment-that-determines-a-later-partner)

There is an important limit to reading this as a local decision. The donor is part of the same shared record. When $S$'s earlier payment is independently diverted, $R$ can have exactly the same holding and still lose the joint birth. A second comparison keeps *joint* children available to all four competing parties in every arm. Each knot's actual donor determines its partner. An extra earlier payer changes the number of marks at $R$ without changing its partner. The partner can also change at identical anonymous local values and mark counts. A knot can therefore carry a sign of what happened even when the sign itself has no power to choose a joint birth. [Two-donor comparison](proof.md#the-same-interruption-with-joint-alternatives-on-both-sides)

The founder holdings changed in the prepared contrasts, and their specified ordinary contacts forecast the earlier events. The absolute starting values are the same and every founding loose mark is zero. With the entire founder held identical, ordinary complete growth fixes the sequence of structural parent rosters and fresh grants. Swapping the names paid to two children can change the route of a tagged distinction. Ordering the writes of a complete cut can change which has already appeared at an intermediate observation. Neither comparison gives an independent change to the complete structural roster. [Identical-founder argument](proof.md#identical-founders-binding-shams-and-interpretation)
