## Completing the outsiders

Write \(B\) for the dominant block and \(X\) for its 396 outsiders. In the classical candidate graph, every maximal clique with outsider set \(S\subseteq X\) contains every member of \(B\) compatible with all of \(S\). Call that common subset \(T(S)\). There is exactly one possible completed clique per \(S\). It is maximal precisely when \(S\) has no further eligible outsider, where eligibility means compatibility with all of \(T(S)\) and with all already selected outsiders.

Outsiders have just 33 distinct candidate interfaces to \(B\). Members of \(B\) with equal interfaces to the outsiders are mutually compatible and have identical other candidates. Any maximal set contains their entire class whenever it contains one of them. There are **174** such interior classes. Contracting outsiders with identical full closed candidate rows gives **180** outside classes. These contractions preserve maximal candidate sets by bijection.

Every selected outsider clique \(S\) is contained in some maximal outsider clique \(K\). For each of the **1,409** maximal outsider cliques, the calculation lists all distinct intersections of the interior neighborhoods of subsets of \(K\). It forms these intersections by successively adding one neighborhood and its intersections with every previously produced set. For each such \(T\), let \(E(T)\) be the outsiders compatible with all members of \(T\). Any maximal completion inside \(K\) uses precisely \(S=K\cap E(T)\). This operation still has interior intersection \(T\), since it contains a subset of \(K\) generating \(T\) and adds only outsiders compatible with \(T\). The final test rejects \(S\) if an outsider in \(E(T)\setminus S\) is compatible with all of \(S\). It counts the remaining selections once each.

The calculation encountered **13,960** distinct interior intersections and tested **1,365,110** distinct outsider selections. It accepted **1,061,954**, including the empty outside selection for the dominant block itself. A separately implemented C++ search enumerated maximal sets directly on the full 354-name quotient, with no outsider intersection step. It found the same total after 2,477,093 calls. The C++ routine also agreed with literal subset enumeration on 37 small graphs.
