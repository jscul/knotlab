## How it was checked

Fresh code rebuilds growth, regenerates the larger draws, computes every tied shortest change, and matches both holders and distinctions by explicit bijections. The shared growth engine served only as a comparator. On 46 sources, both laws, three fresh-count arms and two labellings gave 552 matching comparisons. Across **11,056** funded first preparations and **29** funded continuations, independent enumeration of **9,866,149** allowed changes, including zero per case, matched every exchanged support, minimum and immobile distinction. No author's reading or analysis module was imported. Projected parity relations were passed to the canonical forgetting test. [Growth](growth.py) · [Growth comparator](growth_check.py) · [Exchange](exchange.py) · [Direct enumeration](exchange_audit.py) · [Projections](projections.py) · [Tower reader](tower_readings.py)

**Free-return search beyond the census cap.** Every exchanged distinction is open: restrict the incidence of a grown holder containing it to the exchanged distinctions. This flips it and has even overlap with every move. A return up to renaming therefore requires all old distinctions to be open. Ordinary growth extends all old states and leaves every fresh distinction open. Since each open distinction occurs in a shortest change through itself, even one fresh distinction makes a return impossible. The remaining connected case has one implied birth and one loose distinction at each participating holder. A double meeting already gives an unlawful two-place move.

At three and four knots all remaining meeting patterns fit the census. At five through seven, the complete list of simple connected sharing patterns and marked subsets outside the frozen inputs gave 13,545 additional funded preparations. Of 517 lawful exchanges, 138 matched the source knot count and none of those matched its arities. Together these checks exclude an **immediate free return at three through seven knots** under the stated ordinary rule. In 3,376 selected nine- to eleven-knot preparations, eight were lawful and zero matched the knot count. As a positive control, supplying one birth shared by all members of a four- or five-knot ring with one loose distinction each does give a free return. Ordinary growth does not make that birth. [Reduction and search](counterexample_free.py) · [Complete small range](counterexample_free_atlas.json) · [Larger controls](counterexample_free_witness.json)

Two further targeted searches covered 276 small ring preparations and 255 joins of selected parts. Respectively 59 and 44 funded through three rounds. None kept a common independent pair under both readings. These are one-per-construction counts, including possible renamings. The two nine-knot positive controls had 76 and 78 distinctions at round two, well below the size ceiling.
