# The owner's request

26 November 2026, in the thread that had just reported the third step of
[`FAMILIES-THAT-MEET-20261126-001`](../FAMILIES-THAT-MEET-20261126-001/decision.md).

He had asked, while that step was running:

> Have we successfully completed a single instance of complex behavior using any sort of braiding of the logic? Like
> are we able to build complex starter logic that creates those emergent patterns? What are we at in terms of
> complexity from 1-10? And can you come up with some sort of roadmap to get us to a higher rating? I want to start
> simulating stuff.

The report answered with a scale of ten levels, a rating between 3 and 4, and five directions
([the report as sent](../FAMILIES-THAT-MEET-20261126-001/third_step/README.md#report-as-sent)). His reply:

> Let's do 1, 2, and 3.

The three directions, as that report listed them:

1. **A bench for two patterns.** Join two of the three lasting patterns in one start, one to three steps apart, under
   waiting and under two laws that reach further; read whether either one's course differs from growing alone, and
   for how long. Stop at a pair that differs for good under a swap of ration, or when every pair has been read.
2. **The bench as a toy on the site.** Pick two patterns, a law and the steps apart; watch the rounds. Done when it
   matches the bench on every case of the first direction.
3. **Search small starts for pairs that act on each other.** Every start of up to six knots under waiting, each pair
   of parts grown together and alone. Stop at the first pair that differs for good, or when the census ends.

The scale, the rating and the word "bench" are the thread's. "Braiding of the logic" and "starter logic" are the
owner's words for building a start by hand; the records say "a start".

**Sharpened the same day.** The report's "nothing at level 5" overlooked the time-as-a-fold study's three-returns
cases, which hold only under rules that choose by the names of the start's distinctions
([decision](../TIME-AS-FOLD-20261125-001/decision.md), item 3). So the first direction's stop reads: a pair one of
which still changes the other's course late in the growth, under a law that uses no names, and under a swap of
ration.
