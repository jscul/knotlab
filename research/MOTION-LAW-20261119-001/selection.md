# What order independence selects

**The earlier four-phase pulse cannot keep its unique isolated motion and make every simultaneously enabled pair of events persist through a collision, at any finite reading radius.** Two approaching copies force an extra firing on an isolated phase. On an oriented corridor a different compact pulse passes this test on every binary assignment. A stronger requirement, commutation of all individual site updates on all states, confines every compact disturbance on a quiescent background. In a fully classified radius-one family, conservation of neighbouring disagreements and this weaker commutation leave seven laws. A direction, a bit polarity and the unit in which firings are counted each affect what this result selects. ## State and event

Work on the uniform, two-sided chain of ordinary four-distinction responses of the preceding study. Write \(x_i\) for the common value read at the two private distinctions of response \(Q_i\), relative to the specified zero assignment. Toggling \(x_i\) means firing \(Q_i\), changing four actual distinctions and preserving the parity equations. The private distinctions make the representation faithful. Every finite binary word occurs in the closed chain code. The arguments about local guards also apply to all binary assignments, by their stated local tests. The all-size chain is a mathematical comparison. Every finite window used by a guard can be tested on a sufficiently long physical corridor.

A site map \(U_i\) checks its guard and either toggles \(x_i\) or does nothing. A legal firing means a toggle. The total map, including the identity on a disabled input, is needed in the first theorem. A blank is quiescent if no site fires in the all-zero word. A single update consumes one event, independently of any waiting time. Unless an orientation is explicitly supplied, both reflection and translation of the chain are admitted renamings.

## Two meanings of commuting

**All total site maps commute.** If \(U_jx=x\) and all \(U_i\) commute on every assignment, then \(U_jwx=wU_jx=wx\) for every finite word \(w\) of site updates. All later firings therefore lie in the initially enabled set. If the guard reads within radius \(s\) and the blank is quiescent, this set lies within \(s\) places of the initial finite support. The same finite bound holds after arbitrarily many firings. Every compact disturbance has bounded displacement and therefore zero long-run displacement per firing on every infinite event sequence. The theorem permits internal activity. For example, the reflection-invariant radius-three guard with word `001?100` toggles the middle bit regardless of its old value. Two occurrences whose middles differ by one, two or three have contradictory specified bits. More distant middle flips leave one another’s tests unchanged. Toggling at an occurrence preserves its own guard. Thus the maps are commuting involutions, with a nontrivial two-state oscillation and a quiescent blank. The idle law satisfies the same conditions. Replacing all-zero quiescence by the corresponding condition at a covariantly carried reference gives the same proof.

**Simultaneously enabled events persist.** Here the two-step requirement starts only when two distinct legal firings \(i,j\) are enabled at \(x\). Each must remain legal after the other, and both orders must have the same result. For single coefficient toggles the last clause follows from persistence. A disabled site can subsequently acquire permission.

There is an important special case. Suppose each permitted firing can be undone by the **same forward guard** at its own site. Then the total site maps are involutions. If \(j\) became newly enabled after \(i\), both \(i\) and \(j\) would be enabled at the successor. Persistence when \(i\) is undone would imply that \(j\) was enabled at the starting state. This is impossible. Consequently the two-step property on every assignment fixes the entire enabled set, and the total maps commute on every assignment. It gives the compact-support obstruction above. A directed rule may instead have a separately specified inverse rule. That convention allows the travelling construction below.

The persistence property implies a useful history statement with no clock. In a fair run, an event continuously enabled is eventually fired. Given a legal finite event word \(i_1,\ldots,i_k\), compare it to any fair run from the same starting state, treating a terminal run as complete. The word’s first required firing persists through all other choices until it occurs. Use the two-step property to commute that occurrence to the beginning, one earlier firing at a time. The swapped suffix is still legal and the state after the permuted finite prefix is unchanged. Repeating at most \(k\) times puts the given word first in a finite rearrangement of the fair run. Thus every finite prefix of either fair run can be placed first in the other using swaps of coenabled firings. Every site fires the same number of times in both, allowing infinity. Here its sequence of updated bit values consequently agrees after waiting is erased. If a terminal run exists, it has finite length, and every fair run reaches the same terminal state with the same firing counts. Equal states after the same *total number* of scheduled events do not follow.

The sufficient local commutation condition for history independence is known in the asynchronous computation literature. The argument here identifies the stronger all-map requirement, its restriction on compact changes, and the precise event-history comparison used for these physical responses. [Gács, *Deterministic computations whose history is independent of the order of asynchronous updating*](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0101026)

Agreement of terminal states alone is weaker. On a finite path with blank exterior, erase a one if it has an immediate zero neighbour, or if all four other positions within radius two are one. Every nonempty finite word has an erasable extreme, so every maximal run ends at zero. In a run of nine consecutive ones, the fourth and sixth positions are both erasable. Erasing the sixth disables the fourth while its immediate neighbours remain one. The symmetric local law has the same unique terminal assignment for every order and fails the simultaneous-event test. Even the local firing counts need not follow from endpoint equality in general. On two distinguished binary positions take the two paths \(00\to10\) and \(00\to01\to11\to10\), with exactly these directed single-place moves. Every run from \(00\) ends at \(10\), while the second position fires zero or twice. In this last logical control the two positions have distinct roles.

## All radius-one guards conserving the number of disagreements

Name the position between two consecutive response locations a disagreement when their bits differ. Write \(w_i=x_{i-1}\mathbin{\oplus}x_i\) in a proof orientation. Toggling \(x_i\) complements \(w_i,w_{i+1}\). The total number of disagreements is unchanged by **each** firing exactly when the two values were \(10\) or \(01\). This is a local equality even for assignments with infinitely many disagreements. Thus out of the eight possible rooted three-bit words, only four can be enabled. The eligible words correspond precisely to moving a disagreement of either polarity, rising or falling in the proof orientation, by one place in either direction, into a place with no disagreement. The choice of permitted moves is initially one of sixteen subsets.

1. A fixed polarity cannot have both directions. Place just one such disagreement in a long constant stretch on either side. Its two possible moves use different sites, and either disables the other. A long finite plateau gives the same local witness with another distant disagreement.
2. Two mobile polarities cannot have opposite directions. Make the two disagreements two places apart in the order that points them toward the intervening empty place. Either firing takes that place and prevents the other. Any two adjacent disagreements in this witness must have opposite polarities, as required. It can be realized by a finite run of two ones or by a gap of two zeros between long runs.
3. Conversely, all selected polarities can move in one common direction. Any two coenabled moves use disjoint pairs of disagreement locations, because a common location would have to be occupied and empty at once. They leave each other’s tests true, preserve each transported polarity and commute. Reflect the proof if the chosen direction is reversed.

There are exactly **seven** oriented truth tables satisfying the two properties. These are the idle table, and two choices of direction times the three nonempty subsets of the two mobile polarities. This count gives one vote to each of the 256 radius-one binary truth tables before the two conditions are imposed. It establishes a classification, independent of any initial sample.

Global bit complementation interchanges the two polarities, keeping their directions. It leaves exactly three laws, idle and the two directed transports of both polarities. Reflection interchanges polarities **and** reverses all transport directions. Among the seven, only idle is reflection-invariant without an oriented input. Asking for both symmetries gives the same single law. With a chosen orientation, bit-complement covariance and nontriviality leave precisely the two opposite transports. Requiring every permitted change to copy the predecessor selects one. This last one-sided restriction is a substantive additional input.

A single mobile disagreement with an empty place ahead moves exactly one response place per firing of its own change. On the full line a single disagreement is a change of background between two infinite constant assignments. A compact word on a blank background has an even number of disagreements. With all mobile disagreements moving in one direction, their order is conserved. Their gaps can vary with the scheduler. A compact marked change of shape with a unique speed does not follow for the entire word. If only one polarity is mobile, an opposite stationary disagreement can block it. The statements about speed use successful firings. Delaying an enabled firing for an arbitrarily long finite duration is consistent with fairness.

## A compact traveller under globally persistent directed guards

Here is a further construction, beyond the predicted seven-case classification. Supply an oriented corridor with no clock, preserve the zero reference and use one physical \(Q\) per firing. On any consecutive four locations in their supplied order, permit the two single-bit changes

\[
0100\longrightarrow0110\longrightarrow0010 .
\]

The first adds a neighbour ahead of an occupied location. The second drops the trailing one. On a single occupied location surrounded by zeros each state has one legal forward event. Two firings give the next single occupied location. At ordinary spacing the physical weights are four, six and four. The inverse relation is exactly the same two rules on the reversed corridor, since reversing the order of positions interchanges the first word with the last and fixes the middle word. Each clean packet also has one predecessor. The separately specified inverse does not augment the forward menu.

The coenabled-event guarantee holds on **all binary assignments**, including overlapping packets. For a birth \(i\) the required word runs from \(i-2\) to \(i+1\). For a deletion \(j\) it runs from \(j-1\) to \(j+2\). Two births cannot differ by one or two positions, because either the required preceding one or the succeeding zero is contradicted. The corresponding assertion for two deletions follows from their `0110` word. For a birth and a deletion to inspect one another’s fired bit, the deletion would have to lie at one of \(i-2,i-1,i,i+1\). Three of these sites are required zero by the birth. A deletion at \(i-1\) requires a one at \(i\), also impossible. All coenabled toggles therefore leave the other’s guard untouched. The history proof above applies to collisions on every initial assignment, and applies to the reversed rule as well. Isolated compact transport coexists with order-independent local histories. The histories guarantee by itself says nothing about the survival of an individual packet in a collision.

The interaction has a simple complete description for any finite set of initial singleton pulses separated by at least one empty place. Each occupied run has length one or two and represents one packet. A singleton may grow forward only when at least two blanks follow it. A pair can always drop its trailing member. These operations preserve the number and order of packets, with at least one blank between successive runs. For example, singletons at 0 and 2 force the leading packet to occupy 3 and release 2 before the trailing one can begin its next hop. The leading packet advances indefinitely under weak fairness. For each earlier packet, its successor eventually leaves a sufficient gap if the earlier packet stays in place. Its required birth then remains enabled until firing. Induction backwards gives indefinite motion for every packet. Each packet advances exactly one place per two of its own firings, including after a waiting encounter. The blank gap and the order of completions can vary with the schedule. Opposed headings on a single unmarked track require additional structure.

There is a direct extension to the full physical parity assignments of the same carrier, including unrestricted loose values. Require the two private distinctions at each of the four tested places to agree, and reject that occurrence otherwise. Every \(Q_i\) toggles both of its own private bits and neither private bit of any other response. The set of mismatched locations is therefore fixed for an entire execution. Outside that fixed set the proof applies word for word, with occurrences that touch a mismatched location permanently unavailable. Raw-cycle offsets are invisible to this decoder and are retained. The single-packet speed uses the clean matched sector and a transported zero reference.

Use the same four tested places at equal spacings \(a\ge1\). The residue classes of positions modulo \(a\) do not interact and the proof is unchanged. One isolated occupied response translates \(a\) places in two elementary firings, with reading radius \(2a\). The midpoint physical weight is eight for \(a>1\), since its two responses are then disjoint. Thus even with the **common radius ceiling four**, the choices \(a=1,2\) give different speeds on identical isolated inputs under exactly the same orientation, binary reference, event unit, reversal-as-inverse covariance and all-state collision guarantee. A frozen set of carriers can supply two copies of this oriented channel permuted by reflection, giving a reflection-covariant two-channel variant. Providing such a pair or a single orientation changes the permitted information from that of the unmarked single corridor. The example does not settle compact anonymous transport with a single undirected binary track and the same all-state collision guarantee.

## The earlier four phases cannot acquire persistent collisions at any finite radius

On the unmarked chain, suppose a single-coefficient rule permits all the earlier isolated firings on the four registered phase words and their reflections. Write the right-moving occupied sets, relative to their anchor, and their designated firings as

| Phase | Occupied locations | Required next firing | Furthest ever fired, including next |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| A | 0, 1, 4 | 2 | 4 |
| B | 0, 1, 2, 4 | 5 | 5 |
| C | 0, 1, 2, 4, 5 | 4 | 5 |
| D | 0, 1, 2, 5 | 0 | 5 |

**For every finite reading radius, persistence of coenabled events forces an additional firing at phase C.** Thus no change confined to the collision rules can retain the entire isolated deterministic orbit and provide persistent two-event encounters on all binary assignments. In fact it suffices to demand persistence on the descendants of one sufficiently separated, inward-facing pair. The construction uses a single present binary state, unmarked interior, one \(Q_i\) per event and the same rule under translations and reflection. The proof does not assume reversibility, a clock, quiescence outside the packet, or a bound shared by all candidate rules. This further conclusion was derived after registration.

To prove the reduction, continue each of the two isolated trajectories backwards on the whole line by the displayed periodic recipe. This defines a list of *legal forward* moves starting as far back on either list as desired. Write \(P_p,f_p,M_p\) for a row's occupied offsets, required event and last column. For a right-going packet at anchor \(a\), all its past firings and its next one are at most \(a+M_p\). In the immediately preceding cycle the largest offset relative to \(a\) was \(5-1=4\). The listed offsets of the current cycle give the rest of the column. For the reflected left-going packet at anchor \(b\) the lower bound is \(b-M_q\).

When \(a+M_p<b-M_q\), the combined state with occupied locations \((a+P_p)\mathbin{\triangle}(b-P_q)\) must enable both \(a+f_p\) and \(b-f_q\). Start from one pair far enough back that the parked initial right packet lies outside the reading radius of all required left events through the target, and the parked initial left packet likewise stays outside the required right events. With one packet parked the isolated moves of the other are legal by locality. Index a combined word by how many moves from each list have been performed. At any pair of indices the left and right firings are at distinct sites by the strict bound. At the first row and column the corresponding moves are known from the parked comparisons. Induct over the rectangle of indices. The next left firing survives the last right firing from the preceding row, and the next right firing survives the last left firing from the preceding column. Toggling distinct coefficients gives the required combined binary word in either order. This proves both enabled events at the target indices. The same proof works for all finitely many targets below from a **single** pair of sufficiently distant initial A packets, since each target lies farther along the same two prescribed trajectories. The locality used here is separation along the response chain. Another permitted observation also works if parking the opposite packet sufficiently far away leaves every required one-packet test on these two finite comparisons unchanged. A global bond reading that detects distant support would need a separate argument.

Only fourteen consecutive physical response locations are needed after this reduction. For anchors numbered 0 through 13 seed the two compulsory firings whenever the strict bound holds and the anchors are at most eleven apart. If \(u\ne v\) are compulsory on a word \(X\), persistence forces \(v\) on \(X\mathbin{\triangle}\{u\}\). Close the seeds under this implication using the following complete finite calculation. Each insertion is a necessary consequence under the hypothesized law. Each generated state is reached by a legal firing from a previously reached state.

~~~python
from collections import defaultdict
from itertools import combinations

phases = ((0, 1, 4), (0, 1, 2, 4), (0, 1, 2, 4, 5), (0, 1, 2, 5))
fire = (2, 5, 4, 0)
furthest = (4, 5, 5, 5)
enabled = defaultdict(set)
for a in range(14):
    for b in range(a + 1, min(14, a + 12)):
        for p, left in enumerate(phases):
            for q, right in enumerate(phases):
                if a + furthest[p] >= b - furthest[q]:
                    continue
                word = frozenset(a + j for j in left) ^ frozenset(b - j for j in right)
                enabled[word].update((a + fire[p], b - fire[q]))

while True:
    missing = set()
    for word, sites in enabled.items():
        for u, v in combinations(sites, 2):
            for changed, retained in ((u, v), (v, u)):
                successor = word ^ {changed}
                if retained not in enabled.get(successor, ()):
                    missing.add((successor, retained))
    if not missing:
        break
    for word, site in missing:
        enabled[word].add(site)

assert (len(enabled), sum(map(len, enabled.values()))) == (184, 340)
assert {6, 7} <= enabled[frozenset((4, 5, 8, 9))]
assert 7 in enabled[frozenset((4, 5, 6, 8, 9))]
~~~

The four-position word \(\{4,5,8,9\}\) must enable 6 and 7. Fire 6. The resulting \(\{4,5,6,8,9\}\) is exactly the isolated C word with anchor 4, including the blank exterior. Site 7 must still be enabled, whereas the prescribed sole C event is 8. This is the contradiction. For radius \(r\), one may place the common initial right-going A word at anchor \(-r-10\) and the left-going A word at \(r+23\). All past left firing indices through a seed stay below 13 and all past right ones stay above zero. The two parked supports therefore lie outside the opposite tests on the two axes. Between their starting anchors and the seeds, every required toggle lies between \(-r-10\) and \(r+23\). A homogeneous stretch of \(4r+40\) consecutive response locations, numbered \(-2r-13\) through \(2r+26\), includes the full reading windows with spare margin. Thus an arbitrarily long reached homogeneous corridor gives a finite physical witness for each proposed coefficient-reading radius. A single fixed finite ring, auxiliary state that records how an assignment arose, a coupled multi-site event or just eventual agreement of terminal values each changes a premise. Other asymmetric pulse shapes on an anonymous single track still require their own test.

## Closures, clocked controls and limits of selection

There are also several fully reflection-invariant spreading laws on the unmarked chain. Permit a zero to become one if a one lies within any distance from one through \(a\). All enabled changes persist, since each adds a one. On a finite connected corridor any nonempty initial set reaches the entire corridor under every fair schedule. On the infinite chain every fixed site is eventually reached, by induction on its distance from a seed. Starting from a singleton, the shortest causal sequence to a site \(d\) places away is exactly \(\lceil d/a\rceil\). No event reads beyond \(a\), and successive additions along a shortest sequence of overlaps attain the bound. The foremost change can advance \(a\) places per event on a chosen outward sequence. With parallel dependency levels a singleton fills the entire radius-\(at\) interval after \(t\) levels. These laws share the terminal closure at finite size and the same event unit, and permit different causal speeds already at common radius ceiling two. The growing support retains every intervening change under fair processing.

A short algebraic control characterizes a different class. Give a synchronous map on a uniform binary chain an explicit update round. Require additivity over parity, a finite translation-invariant stencil, and a finite-range inverse. The output bit at position zero is a parity sum of input bits at a finite set of offsets. Encode its stencil by a Laurent polynomial \(p(z)\) with coefficients modulo two. A bijective additive map has additive inverse, and its inverse commutes with translations. Hence the inverse also has a polynomial \(q\). Their product is one. The span of nonzero exponents adds in a product, since both extreme coefficients occur once. Both spans must be zero, giving \(p=z^d\). Conversely every such translation satisfies these hypotheses. Reflection covariance on one unmarked channel forces \(d=0\). On two uncoupled oriented channels interchanged by reflection it gives the paired translations \(d,-d\). A bound on radius gives only \(|d|\) at most that bound. Radius at most one, nonidentity and dependence confined to the chosen upstream direction give unit displacement per **simultaneous round**. This uses an entirely different clock and stored channel type from individual four-distinction firings. The theorem concerns the complete two-sided chain, or a single uniform rule and inverse on unbounded lengths. An isolated finite ring can have further invertible stencils.

The older affine obstruction supplies a further independent hypothesis test. If any allowed state can translate the whole solution space and these translations are required symmetries of a one-state deterministic map, then \(F(X)=X\mathbin{\triangle}F(0)\), and applying it twice returns the input. If zero is moreover fixed, \(F\) is the identity. A transported reference or extra chronological records restores information unavailable under that requirement. Global bit complementation of coefficients in the seven-case theorem is only one recoding. [Affine proof and memory costs](../MOTION-AT-EDDY-20261119-001/minimality.md)

These conclusions distinguish the failure of a specified list of properties to select one law from an existential claim about every possible property. On a finite truth-table space any single rule can be specified by listing its table, or by asking for the unique rule obeying that list. Consequently an unrestricted assertion that no property selects a law is false, while the adjective “natural” needs a specified vocabulary or independent resource criterion to become testable. The disagreement count gives one such motion-independent criterion. It selects idle on the anonymous radius-one class when combined with persistence and reflection. It gives a conditional speed when an oriented mobile disagreement is also supplied. The broader examples show exactly which additional clock, direction, reference or class of states changes the conclusion. Elapsed physical duration requires an additional timing law.
