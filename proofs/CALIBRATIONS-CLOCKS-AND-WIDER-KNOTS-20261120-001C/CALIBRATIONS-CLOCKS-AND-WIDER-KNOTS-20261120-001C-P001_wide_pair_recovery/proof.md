# Pair-table recovery at a repeated wider meeting

Supply uniform weighting on the finite allowed words and observe the disagreement rate on every named pair. Consider two not-all-equal holders with k≥2 common names and respectively a≥1 and b≥1 private names. Their total width is n=k+a+b. Let A and B be their named supports. The interpretation as physical knots requires their actual local projections and the canonical knot gate.

The two forbidden events make all signs on A, respectively on B, equal. They have sizes 2^{b+1} and 2^{a+1}. Their intersection consists of the two constant words. The allowed population size is therefore D=2ⁿ−2^{a+1}−2^{b+1}+2. For distinct names i,j let C(i,j) be one minus twice their disagreement rate. On a forbidden event, the signed sum for that pair is the size of the event if both names belong to its holder, and is zero otherwise. The two constant words contribute two. Inclusion–exclusion gives

$$D C(i,j)=2-2^{b+1}\mathbf 1_{i,j\in A}-2^{a+1}\mathbf 1_{i,j\in B}.$$

The positive pairs are precisely those with one private name of each holder. Their two nonempty classes give the private sets, up to interchanging the holders. The remaining names are shared. The conjunction of the two recovered rules specifies every word. At a bare source all a+b loose names make unary children. Each of the a children of the first holder has a−1 siblings and receives max(2,a−1) fresh names, with the corresponding formula for b. The second birth count is obtained by taking each sibling pair and then a unary birth per loose name left at each child. For sizes (4,4) sharing three and two, then (4,5) sharing three and two, the first counts are 2,4,3,5 and the next counts are 4,6,5,6 under either law.

**An additional check against arbitrary named competitors for the four bare examples.** A competing physical three-place holder would force a mixed triple. Each such triple would have its three pair disagreement rates sum to exactly two. Every target triple instead admits a constant assignment. Thus it suffices to examine covers by supports of at least four names. No name may have more than two holders. Enumerate every such cover of these five-, six- and seven-name carriers, even permitting repeats and superfluous rules. For each cover, intersect the literal allowed-word truth columns and compare its fraction at every named pair. Among 17, 158, 158 and 2,466 named covers, each required table has exactly one presentation, respectively with 26, 50, 54 and 106 words. The named certificate implies the result under any common relabelling. $$2D-(2^{a+1}-2)(2^{b+1}-2)=2^{a+b+1}(2^k-2)>0.$$

The separately advised extension allows a tree of any number of wider holders, with at least two names at each meeting and two private names per holder. Names in the same group have interchangeable pair readings against every third name. Other groups are distinguished by signs on intervening or private names. If C is the correlation between two names in a group of size s, its chance of being constant is p = C + (1−C)2⁽¹⁻ˢ⁾. Two distinct groups X and Y belong to one holder just when |C(X,Y)|p(Z) > |C(X,Z)C(Z,Y)| for every other group Z. Across an intervening meeting the two sides are conditionally independent given its word, giving equality at that meeting. The maximal sets of pairwise co-held groups therefore give the holdings.
