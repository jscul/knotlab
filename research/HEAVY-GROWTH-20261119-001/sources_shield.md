# Literal controls for held-block ancestry

The induction survives the mixed-group test. It needs the stronger statement that **any two parents of a pure child identify the only child using both**. At the first step this is residual-pair uniqueness. At later steps, any group through two facets of a full clique must either be held by their common parent or include a third facet. An outside child compatible with three facets would need two different facet parents, since one parent belongs to at most two of the facets. Their compatibility gives the union facet, and the earlier exclusivity statement identifies the child with that facet. This rules out enlargement of the pure group and proves exclusivity at the new age. [Held-block induction](held_ancestry.md)

The reverse test is needed for two compatible pure junctions whose union is missing one original residual pair. Their two alternative facet parents at the preceding age were compatible and failed to make a pure child. Some actual group must have covered that pair. Its child cannot carry the pure junctions' common parent as well, since two of the resulting parents would already belong exclusively to one pure child. It therefore makes a mutually compatible triple with the two pure junctions, without a common parent. A maximal unheld group covers that triple and prevents their unaccompanied pair from being born. This uses the full stage, including mixed births.

For the live-layer conclusion, a marked proper subset of its initial candidate component suffices. Each old candidate pair has a common child, and each parent spends every loose distinction. Each old candidate component therefore produces one new candidate component. Whenever a pure child exists in the marked component, a child with outside ancestry coexists there. The pure child has a candidate and, from level two onward, at least two parents. Its current loose supply is exactly its demand, so it contributes no unary offshoot. It suffices to exclude unary offshoots from marked junctions at the first step. Positive age and a held block with at least three members guarantee this. The age-zero control shows why arbitrary funded supply alone gives a different conclusion.
