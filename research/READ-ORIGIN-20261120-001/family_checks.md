# Many source interpretations of one present

**Constructed family, independent program referee pending.** A connected unit-meeting presentation with \(4m\) knots and \(9m\) distinctions has \(2^m\) lawful source interpretations. Every interpretation can reach the same literal present by completing some of its funded first births. The marked pairs are all still owed, and the vector of their correct reference fresh counts determines which interpretation occurred. Knowing how many births have already occurred leaves \(\binom mr\) distinct vectors when that number is \(r\). ## The physical family

For \(m\ge2\), take a path of spent knots \(S_1,\ldots,S_m\). Each \(S_i\) meets \(U_i,V_i\), and each \(U_i\) meets \(X_i\). Each meeting has one shared distinction. In the present, \(U_i\) holds one loose distinction and \(X_i\) holds two. Each \(V_i\) holds two loose distinctions except \(V_1\), which holds three. The \(4m-1\) shared distinctions and \(5m+1\) loose distinctions give the stated width. All supports have three or four places.

For a subset \(K\) of positions, delete each \(X_i\) with \(i\in K\) from the proposed source, along with its two loose distinctions. Its distinction shared with \(U_i\) remains at \(U_i\) as a loose distinction. All other original supports retain the same physical names. The source has \(4m-|K|\) knots and \(9m-2|K|\) distinctions. The roles in the present are intrinsic. The knots with at least three ordinary neighbors form the path of \(S_i\). Its first end meets the unique holder \(V_1\) with three loose distinctions. At each \(S_i\), \(U_i\) has two ordinary neighbors and \(V_i\) one. The other neighbor of \(U_i\) is \(X_i\). Thus a renaming of the present carries the ordered positions and their holders. An automorphism fixes every holder. No two different origin subsets become one by renaming.

All sources pass the parity and not-all-equal knot tests. For parity, wire loose distinctions to an auxiliary exterior. Removing \(S_i\) leaves each path segment with a live holder, and its severed \(U_i,V_i\) parts still have loose distinctions. Removing \(U_i\) can isolate \(X_i\) only when it is present, where it has two openings. Removing a leaf leaves the rest connected to a remaining opening. The complement of every written star is therefore connected in the wired presentation. Each star is a bond and has exactly its own even-parity projection. For not-all-equal, pin a nonconstant word at any displayed knot. At each unpinned \(S_i\), choose opposite values on its meetings to \(U_i,V_i\). At most one of those two values could have been pinned by the specified word. Any other unpinned holder has a private loose distinction with which to complete its relation. This realizes every allowed word on every source support.

## What each source still owes

In each source the only multipart groups are the marked pairs \(P_i=\{U_i,V_i\}\) through \(S_i\). A live \(X_i\) has no other live knot that shares its only ordinary neighbor. Live holders belonging to different path positions are at least three ordinary holder steps apart, so they have no common witness. The source roster is all these pairs, together with one unary for each leftover loose distinction. It is funded.

When \(i\in K\), \(U_i\) originally has two loose distinctions. One pays for \(P_i\) and one for a unary. Execute that correct unary as the missing \(X_i\), using two fresh distinctions. Its next demand is one, since the only other first birth from its parent is \(P_i\). Reserve the same surviving name at \(U_i\) for \(P_i\) in every source interpretation. Likewise fix one loose name at \(V_i\) for that pair. The partial first-step replay reconstructs every support and coholder in the common present. This works with all the changes to old parents made together. The emitted births use disjoint inputs, and each preserves the earlier allowed relation. The tested reference admits these partial first steps before the marked pairs.

For \(i\in K\), one first sibling of \(P_i\) comes from \(U_i\) and \(h(V_i)-1\) from \(V_i\). Siblings from opposite parents have no common parent, so each requires its own residual pair with \(P_i\). For \(i\notin K\), only the \(V_i\) siblings occur. The demands are positive in both cases. The marked pair is nonimplied under parity because the source has other openings. Under not-all-equal its old inputs are jointly free. Thus its correct fresh count is \(h(V_i)\) when \(i\in K\) and \(h(V_i)-1\) otherwise.

Already at \(m=2\) the two one-birth interpretations have response vectors \((3,1)\) and \((2,2)\) at the same literal eight-knot, eighteen-distinction present. Knowing the source counts, or the number of emitted unaries, does not decide between them. More generally, at middle cardinality at least \(m-\log_2(m+1)\) bits are needed for a fixed-length description that answers all the marked cost queries on this family. This follows from \(\binom{m}{\lfloor m/2\rfloor}\ge 2^m/(m+1)\). The count is independent of how distinction names are spelled. The construction addresses the reference that retains the planned first births. It makes no assertion about an admission policy that forbids these prefixes. The archived current-state rule at the common present still admits every \(P_i\). Each possible prior child here is unary, so the previous-common-child guard never excludes a pair. After provisionally making \(P_i\) its only live candidate is \(X_i\), through \(U_i\). The raw rule therefore gives one fresh distinction in every interpretation, under either knot law. Even the first anchored pair, whose reference answer is two or three, receives one by that current-state reading. This is a single-event comparison with no schedule weighting. ## Finite replay

The checker exhausts \(m=2,\ldots,10\). There are 2,044 source interpretations, 64,512 literal local source projections and 18,432 ready marked queries per law. Every parity support passes the wired-complement check. Rank independently verifies the small families and the endpoints at the larger sizes. Every not-all-equal support is checked by pinning every word in the actual formula. For each interpretation the complete first-roster oracle agrees with the independently listed pair and unary parent sets, demands and fresh counts under both laws. The checker replays all 9,216 omitted-holder attachments to recover literal equality of the presents. It also completes the entire first roster for one representative at each cardinality. Across 63 such representatives there are 1,377 remaining births per law, each checked against its actual old-input and new-support projections. Joint renaming of holders and distinctions passes at every \(m\).

At \(m=10\) the present has 40 knots and 90 distinctions. With five already made unaries the 252 source interpretations all have equal size and distinct response vectors. Counts use one vote per source interpretation of the specified present.
