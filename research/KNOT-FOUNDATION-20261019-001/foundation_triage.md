## What the labels show

- **Two records stand as foundation results.** These are the founding verdict
  and the arity coordinate distinction. Both are now carried by the restated
  thesis and `KNOT-FOUNDATION-20261019-001A-P001`.
- **Most knot-related constructions reduce to atoms composing.**
  Rules-from-binding, finite-depth reuse, unbounded levels and two contacts
  sharing a recurrence are all trees or threads of atoms (`knotlab worlds`
  checks a 65,536-loop tree). The asynchronous-circuit results are known
  patterns: early evaluation, completion detection, the C-element join, and
  hazards from unacknowledged sharing.
- **The earlier-gate records are not refuted.** Their knot and contact
  verdicts depend on the provenance of a declared source (native authority,
  supplied laws, current versus stale status). The clone-reflection records
  show directly that this status is not a property of the allowed states.
  Under K1–K3 such a verdict has to be restated as a relation before it says
  anything.
- **The independent mathematics stays as it is.** It covers linear
  programming, Ihara–Bass identities and counting bounds.
