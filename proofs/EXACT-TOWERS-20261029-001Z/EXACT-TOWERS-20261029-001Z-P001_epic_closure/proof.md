## Challenged assumptions

The decision note records three, each refined:
- that a law surviving patterns of patterns keeps its emergence at every scale: true of its allowed states, not always of its counts;
- that self-reproduction would build space: it builds hierarchical spaces, all finitely ramified;
- that scale-free correlations mark universally influential structure: only at the hard law.
