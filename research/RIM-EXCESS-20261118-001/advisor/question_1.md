New statements I want to freeze:
Lemma P (bond polygons). If B is a bond of m >= 3 members, then every cyclic order of B with D-sum <= 3(m-2) has D-sum exactly 3(m-2), and in it every member s lies on a D-geodesic between its two neighbours p, n: D(p,s) + D(s,n) = D(p,n). Proof: if the sum were <= 3(m-3) (sums are multiples of 3), removing any member keeps the sum <= 3((m-1)-2) by the triangle inequality, so B minus that member would be constrained (Prop 3; for m = 3 the pair would need sum <= 0, impossible), contradicting the bond; so the sum is 3(m-2). Removing s lowers the sum by delta_s = D(p,s)+D(s,n)-D(p,n) >= 0, and B - s must not be tight, so 3(m-2) - delta_s > 3(m-3), delta_s < 3; delta_s = 0 mod 3 by Lemma 2(i), so delta_s = 0.
Corollary (excess). If B is a least bond holding x, z with b(x,z) = s + 1 + e, then e = (W - 3s)/3 where W = 3(m-2) is the weight of B's polygon: the excess counts, in units of 3, how much longer the bond's polygon is than the round trip x -> z -> x.
Lemma C (candidates). Every member p of such a B satisfies min(D(x,p)+D(p,z)-D(x,z), D(z,p)+D(p,x)-D(z,x)) <= 3e. (p lies on one of the two arcs between x and z.)
Algorithm: for e = 0,1,..., enumerate geodesic polygons through x and z of s+2+e members among the candidates, keep the first with no tight proper sub-collection. Matches brute force on 3,119 pairs.
