# Referee report on whether ordinary parity growth binds whole-held facts
**Refuted. Block on the conclusion that ordinary growth leaves separately carried facts unbound. The frozen disjoint-cycle prediction holds on its recorded starts.**

A **30-knot construction** meets the study’s own cycle criterion after ordinary growth. At round two, three disjoint cycles made of distinctions born in round one take exactly **000, 011, 101 and 110**, even with any one of the 60 knots fixed. The study’s own **sampled ten-knot start** also binds three separately carried path parities. Removing one round-three law allows all eight triples there. Both measured relations pass the canonical knot test. The four triples occur with any one knot fixed to zero. Adding any allowed state gives the same four triples for every other feasible reading of that knot. Whether a next layer itself keeps a further whole-held fact, or the operation iterates, remains open. [Cycle certificate](cycle_certificate.json) · [Path certificate](path_certificate.json)

## What holds

1. **Old distinctions keep their allowed joint states.** All 9,335 funded transitions checked preserved the projection, counting two repeated witness transitions. A newborn knot with a private fresh distinction can satisfy its law for any allowed old state. All 1,850 births without a fresh distinction already had their laws implied. The argument iterates for any fixed old carriers. In particular, every cycle at the first ordinary round consists of distinctions already present at the start. [Growth and projection counts](replay_results.json)
2. **The reduction for cycle parities is exact.** Write the knot laws as the rows of a binary matrix. A parity vanishes on all allowed states exactly when its support is a sum of those rows. A sum of cycles uses only shared distinctions and meets each holder evenly. If its parity is forced, it also belongs to the row span. These three conditions define the study’s space B. The holder count never exceeded two. The independent replay matched all 17,295 census and held-out stage records, including every one of the 10,528 recorded basis vectors. A zero support can still express an identity among overlapping cycles. Noncycle carriers need only the row-sum test. [Replayed spans](replay_results.json)
3. **The frozen negative survives the full recount.** On 17,312 distinct start-and-round keys, 7,961 had nonzero B. Their 14,039 nonzero sums had 17,503 splits. On 2,931 stages at least one split had three to six cycles. Of the 26,712 distinct cycles counted once per stage inside all nonzero B supports, 22,512 changed under a shortest move, 2,632 were nonconstant labels readable by one knot, and 1,568 were constant. **Zero** were unread class labels. Only **three** stages combined a three-part split with at least three individually unread labels of any kind. All were rounds of the *same ten-knot start*, and its sole nonzero B support was already a sum of source laws. The 58 census and eight held-out second rounds with a B direction outside the source laws therefore supplied no such three-label opportunity. The full search reached B dimension four and at most 93 splits per sum. Neither cap omitted a split or a sum. [Denominators and uncapped splits](split_audit.json)
4. **The three built witnesses have B of dimension zero at all 12 stages.** These were starts of nine, thirteen and seventeen knots built to retain two, three and four independent ring parities. None could have met the frozen stop. Through rounds zero to three the sampled ten-knot witness kept B of dimension one. The 240th accepted seven-knot draw had dimensions 1, 1, 2 and 3, with respectively 0, 0, 1 and 2 directions outside its source laws. [Witness counts](replay_results.json)

A two-distinction change explains the frozen negative. Every one of the 4,448 forced sums with a three-or-more-part split contains a pair of distinctions with the same two holders. Flipping both is allowed and shortest through each. A simple cycle using just one changes parity. A simple cycle using both consists of that pair, which either holder reads. Every split covering the pair therefore fails under either move rule. [Full archive](alternate_archive.json) · [Selected witnesses](alternate_witnesses.json)

**The three-cycle counterexample.** Start with three five-knot rings and give each ring knot two loose distinctions. The connecting knots have none. Summing all source laws forces even parity across those three groups of ten. At the source there is just one class, and 32 shortest changes flip each group’s parity. One round spends the thirty loose distinctions, closes each group into a cycle and makes each parity invariant. Follow the ten fresh distinctions of each ring’s five births. At round two they form three new disjoint cycles. All 177 shortest changes preserve their parities, as does every change of at most five distinctions.

Removing any one first-round law frees the triple on these new supports. Removing any one second-round law leaves its four even values. The growth made the carriers and the invariants. Their representatives on source distinctions already obeyed the parity equation. Each branch also carries its fact in isolation: keep the ring, its two attachment knots and its births. The three resulting 17-knot preparations give all eight triples when taken separately. In the connected start a search given only the supports found 3, 6 and 9 eligible cycles in rounds one, two and three. The eight minimal splits in round two and 27 in round three read generations of the same three facts. [Construction and law deletions](construct_born.json) · [Independent search](cuts_construction.json)

## What was not run

An exhaustive census above five knots, unrestricted carrier families, unbounded rounds, not-all-equal growth and feedback into a further layer were not run. For the larger funded-ring sample, 750 start-and-law trajectories crossed the 512-distinction cap. Their remaining stages were not run. One selected witness trajectory crossed 1,024 distinctions in the six-round extension. The path search tested lengths through eight. Four of its selected thirteen- and seventeen-knot stages hit the visit ceiling; the ten-knot counterexample and the 80 eligible census or held-out stages were fully searched at that bound. Minimum representatives were compared only through four distinctions on 17 selected stages. An additional 128 larger labelled draws used the parallel-pair obstruction to decide 128 forced sums without enumerating their raw splits. ## How it was checked

The starts, growth, moves, binary equations, cycle splits and local reads were rebuilt here. The shared growth engine was used solely for comparison. It matched all 150 ordinary checks, including the 15 selected witness rounds, and all three independent reconstructions of the edited start’s ordinary and cut stages. The disjoint-cycle recount enumerated every nonzero B element and every split, retained all tied shortest changes and checked 25 small stages against literal allowed states. The path certificate independently enumerated changes by size, supplied actual assignments for each attained triple and called the canonical checker on the projected relation. The 30-knot construction was also regrown with the general referee implementation and checked by a reader that was given no designated carriers.
