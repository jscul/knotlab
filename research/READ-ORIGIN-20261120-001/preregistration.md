Random names and sampling seeds are for the checker. No trace, dates, attached tag, size mark or selected state is visible to the unmarked reader. The question is which information determines the answer. Running time is a separate question.

## Observation, target and stop

Each finite nonempty connected initial support has at least three places, one or two holders per distinction, at most one distinction shared by any given pair and the exact stated local knot projection in the whole start. Check physical lawfulness, funding, the parity final exception and the not-all-equal keep clause separately. Their order and the names of equivalent loose slots are immaterial. If that *same* presentation itself qualifies as a source, compare its new ordinary roster with what remains of the actual cohort. Keep the old holder and loose inputs fixed for a marked-cost claim. Also ask whether an apparent difference in the next event can persist in the physical continuation, and prove separately what happens at a true complete boundary. In a fixed-map case, distinguish an immediately ready event of mixed depth from one belonging to an unfinished older cohort. Do not study schedule fairness.

Minimize positive examples lexicographically in the number of current knots, then distinct positions. Separate a changed parent set from a different count on one shared ready query. The unresolved eight-knot restart used multiple original meetings. It is a calibration control, not a lower bound for the single-meeting question. Stop at a universal mark or the smallest counterexample, then the side-information cost, alternative marks and both-law sample. The lower bound must account for all arities if called a minimum.
