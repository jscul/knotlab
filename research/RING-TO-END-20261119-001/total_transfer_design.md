# Additional test of the total number of fresh writes

The earlier design minimizes the number of occupied fresh distinctions at **one** newborn. After the main freeze and its first structural comparisons, a smaller rule for the **whole attached start** was proposed. This is a secondary, prospectively specified discriminator. On a hot parent with exactly two loose distinctions at the beginning of its funded layer, use the coupled pair lift at each even-input unary child. Use the original minimum lift at other births. Predict exactly two children each carrying both fresh distinctions on the attached one-spare ring, four fresh writes in total and one directly refused second-layer sibling pair. On the bare five-ring expect ten children, twenty fresh writes and five directly refused sibling pairs. Test on the literal matched first incidence, with all old values fixed. For a strictly name-invariant deterministic birth-local rule on a fixed source, the equi-valued loose distinctions of any one hot parent are permutable. Any such parent has at least two in these examples. Occupying a newborn invariantly therefore requires at least two children of that parent and at least two fresh distinctions per even-input child on the attached source. The bare source also has a symmetry permuting its five parents. Report which result is sharp under individual equivariance, which modulo fresh renaming, and what extra selection would be required to use only one child in the entire source.

Separately test the bare ring's positive hot sector under elementary asynchronous single-port births at which two fresh values are assigned as part of birth. There, the next child's four-place parity calculation reduces to a unary input and two fresh distinctions, and no child ever has to wait for a cold multi-parent group. Each hot open distinction has its own independent event. At depth two enumerate causally downward-closed choices per source port, show coenabled births at disjoint loose distinctions commute up to fresh renaming, and specify the effect of unbounded unfair schedules on full-layer completion. On the held-fixed erased stage use its permitted cold P/V complete successor for the direct test. An asynchronous theorem for this warm sector alone does not define a general tickless cold joining law.
