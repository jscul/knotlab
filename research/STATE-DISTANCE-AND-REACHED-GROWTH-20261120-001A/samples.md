# State readings and shared descendants on fresh starts

With the ordinary law, one uniform pair of live knots per start and one
loose distinction in each supplied, the two mismatches both occur.
On funded original trees, 33 of 46 queried pairs have no common
descendant. On the partnered runaway, every queried pair is far by the
frozen state reading, and 44, 55 and 61 of 64 pairs have a common
descendant on the very next step at ages zero, one and two. The two
laws give identical outcomes on those partnered arrival questions.

## What received a vote

The tree arm draws 64 independent sizes uniformly from 3 through 5,
then a uniform labelled tree at the drawn size and independent
one-in-four surplus coins. The edited arm adds a uniform absent
meeting to the same tree and recalculates the loose supply from the
same coins and the three-place floor. The partnered arm draws 64
independent orientations on the twelve spent witnesses for six live
knots in three partner pairs. Each witness has one distinction with
one of its live holders and two with the other. Each live original
has four loose distinctions. Giving two neighbours to each spent
witness realizes the partnered live holdings under both laws.
These are separate start-level weightings. The two sparse arms are
paired by original tree, and the laws share each physical start and
sampling ranks. Neither the ages nor the two laws provide independent
replicates. [Partnered growth proof](../../proofs/WALL-READ-20261119-001A/WALL-READ-20261119-001A-P001_partnered/proof.md)

Every reached cut from age zero through two received one uniform
query over unordered pairs of distinct live knots and one actual
uniform loose distinction at each. The exact original star
projections passed under both laws on all starts. A reached cut can
still lack a pair. The receipt records each original physical
holding, attained cuts, actual parents and fresh distinctions,
queries and missing-query reason. Exact witnessed separation measures
the first common descendant once the cut is funded. A structural
split is permanent. An initial stall stops all growth even for a
pair with finite witnessed separation. A closing child counts as
one actual birth. These distinctions are retained in the outcome
columns. [Ordinary common-descendant identity](../INFLUENCE-CONE-20261120-001/cones_proof.md)

## Exact state counts and certificates

For not-all-equal the count uses the three labels on each holder
representing inclusion–exclusion over its two monochrome failures.
Shared distinctions contribute two choices if neither holder is
pinned, one choice if their pins are compatible, and zero if they
conflict. Loose distinctions have the analogous one-holder
factor. Contracting these factors gives the exact total allowed
words and the exact signed sum of the two marked bits. Small
complete word tables and the tree transfer independently check
the procedure. On a tree the transfer is the product of
$-1/(2^{A-1}-1)$ across the holders on the marked route, where
$A$ is the actual arity at that holder. The powers-of-three
specialization applies when those arities are three. The sampled
tree origins can have wider arities.

For each marked holder, condition on all distinctions known to
two holders. If it holds $f$ private names, a marked sign has
conditional mean zero when its fixed inputs are mixed. If its
fixed inputs agree, one of the $2^f$ private words is forbidden
and the mean has magnitude $1/(2^f-1)$. The two holders' private
choices are conditionally independent. Therefore, irrespective of
the conditional weights of the shared words,

$$
|1-2r|\le\frac{1}{(2^f-1)(2^g-1)}
$$

for marks at distinct live holders with respective loose counts
$f,g$. In the partnered case those counts are 4, 5 and 12
at ages zero through two, and grow further. Far is forced for
every pair at these ages and at age four. Under parity, any
containing bond crosses the entire loose bundles of both marked
holders. This gives the distance lower bound $f+g-1$. The two
thresholds therefore agree on the complete partnered pair
population, even while answering different state questions.

For the exact parity calculation, wire the loose distinctions to
one auxiliary exterior. Keep each shared and loose distinction's
own unit of capacity. A bond cut has connected parts on both
sides. A terminal contraction gives a lower bound. If the
constrained minimum has a disconnected part away from the
exterior, any feasible connected side containing both marked
holders has to exit one of its terminal components through
an actual ordinary neighbour. Branching on each such exit
exhausts the viable choices. A connected candidate and its
connected complement give a valid upper witness. The remaining
smallest lower bound gives a rigorous interval on cap exhaustion.
The connected whole loose cut bounds every queried stage, except
for the separately certified two-loose canonical absence.

## Partnered replay past the small literal cuts

The current witnessed relation on $n$ live partnered knots
contains every pair except its partner. Thus the exact finite
arrival is one for a nonpartner pair and two for partners.
The exact pair proportions for next-step arrival are $4/5$,
$6/7$, $8/9$ and $4084/4085$ at ages zero, one, two and four.
The age-four actual population has 4,086 parent rosters.
The check reconstructs their 4,096 possible transversals, omits
the ten witnessed exclusions and checks the resulting set
against the literal ordinary proposals on the complete age-three
holdings under both laws. The separate age-four sampler takes
two distinct allowed roster ranks uniformly per draw, then
draws independently from each holder's actual private ranks.
The private count at that cut is $(2^{2043}-24)/2$. The
deep bound determines whether each query is far without
materializing those holdings. The exact bond values and the
exact signed state rates at age four remain outside this range
of literal state calculations.

A deterministic companion independently checks complete current
pair tables at both first and second explosive cuts. It sets
the doubled end of every original witness at the lower-numbered
partner class. Independently swapping the two members within any
of the three original partner pairs preserves the physical
holdings up to renaming and transports actual true-parent
rosters. The exact table contains seven pair orbits covering all
28 first-cut pairs, and ten covering all 45 second-cut pairs.
Direct pinning of the two marked bits confirms one representative
at each cut against the signed count. This built control has
no sample weight.
