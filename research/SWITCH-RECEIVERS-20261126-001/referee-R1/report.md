## How it was checked

[Independent growth](growth.py) implements the groups, spending, waiting, release, next-round demand, ordinary and capped counts, and the record's one fewer from the [round definitions](../../RING-IN-BALANCE-20261123-001/definitions.md). It constructs both joins and the stub and tracks full own-birth descriptions by parents, multiplicity, fresh distinctions and places. The archive discards individual birth signatures after saving the counts, partitions and first differences. The recount reads the counts directly. Regrowth compares full descriptions across senders without a hash and checks the resulting partitions and first differences against the archive. No author engine or reading module was imported. No growth-engine discrepancy changed an archived count.

The mixed-law replay used seed 20261126 and sampled uniformly without replacement within each receiver size, taking 1, 4, 5 and 5 census starts of sizes one through four. It added the three bench entries and five extra cap-three positives. All 28,692 round-by-round own-birth and shortage counts in 1,900 sender runs agreed. The full cap-three replay was separate. The JSON tables give exact selections, counts, windows and comparisons. [Sampled replay](replay.json)
