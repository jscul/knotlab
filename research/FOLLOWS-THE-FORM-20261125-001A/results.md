descent makes differs with the form of the second seed, and the link carries the difference. Nineteen of twenty
registered predictions hold. The four exact replications are among them: a reading written from the frozen text
reproduces the drafter's probe in all 270 studies. On five first seeds and 60 second seeds that nothing had grown:
with the two seeds in one start and not linked, nothing differs (90 studies of 90); linked, the forms fall into three
or more classes in 87 studies of 90, and 59% to 87% of the pairs of forms are told apart; some pairs are told apart
only after round 1 although every round-1 birth with a first-seed ancestor is the same (422 to 1,309 a row where

the first seed's line shows two or more fates in 37 studies of 90 where at least 40 were predicted, so that its fate
differs with the form is not shown as registered. And in 49 of the 90 studies the class is decided by the held knot
together with the second seed's own births by round: there, what is seen is a count profile of the second seed.**

The words. A start is a **first seed**, one **link** distinction and a **second seed** that holds the link on its
**held knot**. **What the first seed's descent makes** is its births round by round, each by kind, number of parents,
places, fresh distinctions and takes, over the knots descended from the first seed and from none of the second. The
**wider record** is the same over every birth with a first-seed ancestor. A **study** is one law, one first seed and
one family of second seeds; two second seeds are in one **class** when the first seed's descent makes the same
through the last round every run of the study reached, and are **told apart** otherwise. The **unlinked start** has
both seeds and no shared distinction. A **row** pools three rations and five first seeds.

## The held-out material, in a little more detail

- **After the first round.** With no taking rule and short knots retiring, 591 of 26,550 pairs are first told apart
  in round 2 (none later), and 422 of them have the same wider record of round 1. Where short knots wait, pairs are
  first told apart as late as round 6 with no taking rule and round 9 with the take.
- **The fates.** With no taking rule and short knots making offshoots, the first seed's line grows for 801 of 900
  second seeds, is taken up for 61 and is steady for 38. Under most laws most second seeds leave it growing, which is
  why few studies show two fates. Lines with no births at all appear where short knots retire or wait (60 and 64 with
  no taking rule; 218 and 199 with the take).
- **Depth, with the held-out first seeds.** With no taking rule and short knots retiring or making offshoots, the
  first seed's descent tells a single knot from a longer chain and nothing else: not even the held knot's loose
  count (0 of 155), which the probe's first seeds did tell (56 to 89 of 155). What is seen near the link depends on
  the first seed.
- **The wider record** gives more classes than the own descent in 69 studies of 90 and the same in 21.
- **The last common round** is 9 in 72 of the 90 studies; 3 to 7 in the rest, most of them with the take, where a
  run is stopped by the guard or the knot bound.

## What this shows and does not show

- **Shown, on built starts**: what a seed's descent makes depends on what it is linked to, through the link, in
  many classes, and not only through what happens in round 1 or through the knot that holds the link.
- **Tempered**: in more than half of the held-out studies the classes are those of a count profile, the held knot
  with the second seed's own births by round. "Form" is then too large a word.
- **Not shown**: that the first seed's fate differs with the form (C4 fails); that anything reads a form in a
  fuller sense; anything about two parts the growth made that meet through a knot the growth made.
- **The meeting is supplied.** The two seeds share a start distinction.
- **Supplied**: the starts, the seeds, the sites, the families, the laws, the readings, the bounds, the thresholds.
  **Made by births**: everything after round 0.
- **Held out, of like kind.** The fresh first seeds and second seeds were chosen after the probe from the same kinds
  of form. No weighting is named, and nothing is called typical.

- **The knot test was cut after it had started**, not before: its estimate of 7 minutes could not hold, since single

  minutes into the run and recorded then. One test of that half was not run; 36 outside it were not run. No knot
  failed in any test made.
- **PD1 holds only with "at most" read as inclusive** (one cell is exactly 10.0%).

  ordinary count, against 0.08 to 0.4 stated). The whole ticket took 57.6 of its 60 minutes.
- **Readings recorded by the program's author: 36**, all before the gate except the timings and the cut. The frozen
