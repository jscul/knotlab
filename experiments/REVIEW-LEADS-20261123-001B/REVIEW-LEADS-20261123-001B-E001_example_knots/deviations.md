# Deviations and readings (REVIEW-LEADS-20261123-001B-E001)

Every item below is a place where the frozen pre-registration (`research/REVIEW-LEADS-20261123-001B/preregistration.md`, a078a4f959) left a choice or the generator could not give exactly what was asked. Items 1 to 5 were fixed when the panel was built and hashed, before any learner code existed; items 6 to 14 were fixed in the code before the first learner ran on the panel (the gates stage). Nothing was changed after values were seen; the only edit after the gates stage was adding a "below three without the constant" column to the knot-size table (item 8), made before the scoring run. The pre-registration was not edited.

1. **No seed parameter in the generator.** The relative-pixel generator ranks designs by a salted hash and has no seed. Seed 2026112302 is put into the salts (`DESIGN_SALT`, `POSITION_SALT`, `ORDER_SALT` in `panel.py`). Its functions `make_drawing`, `turns`, `bitmap`, `normalize`, `bitmap_support`, `dihedral` and `DIMENSIONS` are imported unchanged from its `panel.py` (sha256 in the panel manifest).
2. **Fresh designs: rotation-class exclusion, 16 L/T pairs.** The pre-registration excludes "every design that occurs in an earlier panel at any position or quarter-turn". The generator's own filter also excludes mirror images (dihedral classes). With the dihedral filter the E and F bases leave only 14 admissible pairs before disjointness, fewer than the 16 needed; with the literal rotation-class filter 30 pairs are admissible. I used the rotation-class filter (the literal text). Consequence, recorded in the manifest: 10 of the 32 selected designs are mirror images of a design in an earlier panel, and 6 selected designs share a dihedral class with another selected design (three mirror pairs). All 3264 earlier bitmaps (every `*.jsonl` under the four named panel folders that carries a `pixels` field) were excluded by exact bitmap and by rotation class.
3. **Selection rule.** The generator's quota table (8 pairs) cannot give 16 pairs. I ranked every admissible L/T pair of both bases (5-by-2 and 4-by-4 boxes, every interior stem column, at least one bend) by the seeded hash, kept the first representative of each ordered pair of rotation classes, and took the first 16 whose rotation classes are disjoint from each other. D(2k-1) is the L and D(2k) the T of the k-th pair, so letters alternate and the two letters of a pair share step lists (as in the generator). Composition: 3 pairs on the E base, 13 on the F base; 6 horizontal, 1 vertical, 9 compound bends.
4. **Positions.** "8 positions drawn by the seed": for design d the 16 anchors (rows and columns 0 to 3) are ranked by a seeded hash and the first 8 are kept, in that order. The varied arm trains the first 2 of them. Each design therefore has 8 positions x 4 turns = 32 images, 1024 in all.
5. **Condition (a) is empty in the narrow arm.** The narrow arm trains every one of a design's 8 positions at turns 0 and 1, so "trained designs at turns 0 and 1 at positions not trained" has no item. Condition (a) therefore exists only in the varied arm (192 items). I did not add positions beyond the 8. Condition (b) uses all 8 positions of the trained designs at turns 2 and 3 (64 items in the narrow arm, 256 in the varied arm); condition (c) is D17 to D32 at all 8 positions and four turns (512 items). In the narrow arm D5 to D16 are neither trained nor in (c) and are not scored.
6. **Window border for F1 to F3.** F0 uses the one-cell border (9 x 9 = 81 windows). The pre-registration states the border's purpose, counts that do not depend on position, but not its width for the larger windows. A one-cell border would not do that for a 1x3 or 3x3 window (a lit pixel in the outer column would be seen by fewer windows than one in the interior). For F1 to F3 each window of size h x w is therefore counted at every position where it overlaps the image, i.e. the image is blank-padded by h-1 rows and w-1 columns on each side (1x3: 80 windows, 3x1: 80, 2x3 and 3x2: 90, 3x3: 100). Feature sizes: F0 15, F1 14, F2 126, F3 511 non-blank pattern parities, plus the constant: 16, 30, 156, 667 memory distinctions. Families are cumulative for L1+. Because of this padding every feature is translation-invariant, so all 8 positions of one design at one turn give the identical feature vector and every learner answers them identically; the effective number of independent items is the number of (design, turn) pairs, and every count in the tables is a multiple of 8.
7. **"Kept knots".** Read as all 64 example knots of the arm. L1 has an allowed memory word iff the whole system is solvable; if it is not, L1 answers nothing (its own sign that its distinctions are not enough). No knot is dropped on arrival (the count of knots that contradicted those before them is reported only as information).
8. **The knot's size.** The constant feature is always odd, so every example knot contains the constant memory distinction. "Fewer than three memory distinctions" is reported both with and without the constant.
9. **L2 enumeration.** The not-all-equal knot of an example forbids every memory word that gives all its odd-feature distinctions the example's label value. A test image is answered y' only when every allowed word makes the other label all-equal on its odd features (the label is then forced). No allowed word means unanswered.
10. **C1.** The integer implementation (`IntegerMeans` of `conventional.py` of PIXEL-LETTERS-20261122-001, imported from its path), trained by the same emitted-guess-and-correctness-bit protocol in the panel's presentation order; the check that its totals equal the label-wise sums of window histograms passed in every arm. The knot version of that study was not run. Ties go to action 0 (L), as there. Margin = |cost(T row) - cost(L row)| in exact fractions. Matched abstention: within each condition and arm, C1 answers the n items of largest margin, where n is the number the learner answered in that condition; ties by item id.
11. **Planted-rule gate criterion (not frozen in detail).** Planted word drawn from `random.Random(2026112302)` over the 16 F0 memory distinctions (constant included); 20 random 8x8 images (independent fair pixels); 2000 fresh random images. Pass iff the knots allow a word, no forced answer is wrong, and all 2000 fresh images are forced and right. (For random images the F0 feature vectors span only 11 of the 16 dimensions; the 20 examples reached rank 11.)
12. **Gate checks run on every arm and learner where a word exists.** Second implementation: dense GF(2) ranks (adding the test knot with each label and testing solvability), for L1 and L1+ in every arm and label regime; and, at F0, enumeration of all 65,536 memory words (L1 and L1+ at F0). Exchange and clearing: only where an allowed word exists (narrow true L1 and L1+, varied true L1+); with no word there is nothing to exchange.
13. **P3 and P4 are stated without an arm.** Reported per arm; the verdict text says which. P4 is evaluated for L1 at F0 on the shuffled training sets of both arms, pooled over conditions a, b and c.
14. **Shuffle.** `random.Random(2026112303).shuffle` of each arm's list of 64 training labels in presentation order (the same seed applied to each arm's list); 30 labels changed in the narrow arm, 32 in the varied arm.
15. **Plumbing check.** Before the scoring run the table and verdict code was run once with random labels in place of the scorer key (the scoring stage with `selftest`); its output directory was deleted. No real label was read by that run. The real scoring stage then ran once.
16. **Not run:** the knot version of C1 (the integer version reproduces every decision per the study); any further window family beyond F3; any run with a different seed.
