# Writing in sequence

Status: Computed (`check.py`, with `seq_lib.py`), pre-registered in `research/ADDRESS-SEQ-20261110-001/preregistration.md`.

Words (2026-11-11). Reworded under the owner's language rule: a knot space is now a drawn knot pattern, its points
distinctions and its lines knots, and an atom (a Fano or torus atom) a piece (Intuitions guide, Glossary). The mathematics is
unchanged; the earlier text is in git history.

On 14 patterns (three Fano pieces on every knot orbit of K_3 and K_4 and on the first three orbits of K_5, one size past K_4; four pieces on
every orbit of K_3), addresses were written in turn by the frozen targeted search, each write starting where the last ended, and after each
write one random upper-level flip was undone by the learner M1. Six writes per three-piece pattern (every pair twice), eight per four-piece
pattern (every triple twice): 90 writes.

- Every write reached its target (90 of 90; 14 of 14 first writes, 76 of 76 later ones).
- Every written address was held after its disturbance (90 of 90): the acting pieces after the return were the address just written, never
  the previous one and never another set.

So the memory holds one address at a time, is rewritten cleanly, and keeps no trace of earlier addresses at these sizes. Neither the frozen
interference outcome nor the frozen failure occurred. Computed on small cases (K_3 to K_5, three and four pieces).
