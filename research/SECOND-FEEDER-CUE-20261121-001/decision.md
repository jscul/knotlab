# Remembering a feeder after a meal

A locally written record of a paid return let a bot act differently at two feeders with the same persistent signal and the same number of exits. On a fresh bank it gained 70 turns across 512 whole games under each law compared with the stronger current-only bot. A supplied count of opportunities to use the certified return predicted the entire deployed behavior without reading the meal outcomes.

## Question and evidence

The old keeper remembered only whether its last paying feeder had one or several exits. In 688 of its 2,354 unchanged matched decisions per law there was a distinct previously emptied feeder with exactly the same number of exits as the query. The archive also yields 366 pairs with different current feeders, equal exit counts and opposite stock after both histories had eaten at the same feeder. The two fresh fits below retain the ordinary growth opportunities before turns one and five, the previous game action bit, the permanent feeder cue, and the local public movement and energy observations. **What the knots remembered.** A meal wrote a mark. After the last growth opportunity, leaving a marked feeder and returning through a holder’s only exit could restore that mark. Four of the 69 pairs with strictly opposite two-turn payoffs showed this effect. None of the 36 pairs with unequal return lengths or the three with opposite visit orders did.
