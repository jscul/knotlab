## F2: three pieces, a chosen pair

pieces 1 and 2, or piece 1 and a junction), every pair of pieces was reached as the holders of the two units, on all 3 knot orbits (9 of 9).

learning where one unit sat at a junction. At every success the two named pieces act as their knots and the third is transparent.

So learning, at a rise and with the state moving, can place the leftover tension in any chosen pieces: the network holds a learned address,
read off by where the tension sits.
