## What the ordinary exchange determines

In the three-word test, \(a=101\), \(b=011\) and \(c=110\) are all allowed by both knot laws. Swapping two distinction names implements each of the three pair exchanges on these words. The physical ordinary word permutations obey \(PQP=R\), where \(P\) exchanges \(a,b\), \(Q\) exchanges \(a,c\), and \(R\) exchanges \(b,c\). The same identity holds on all four even words and all six not-all-equal words. Recording which offers fired can distinguish the two descriptions. A state-only reading discards that history.

Supply the calibrated moving-pair pulse \((I+iP)/\sqrt 2\) and let \(z\), with \(|z|=1\), be the amplitude on an idle word. Two pulses give \(iP\) on the moving pair and \(z^2\) on the idle word. Normalize the two-pulse action by its common moving amplitude \(i\), so its relative idle factor is \(x=z^2/i\). Every value of \(z\) satisfies the inherited ordinary word equalities. For a stronger comparison, **supply** the principle that pulse implementations of the same clean complete permutation agree up to one common phase on coherent inputs. The direct \(R\) and the sequence \(PQP\) differ by factors \(1,x,x\) on initial words \(a,b,c\) when all three pulse addresses share the same calibration. One common factor is possible precisely at \(x=1\). Requiring the completed \(P\) alone to be an involution only gives \(x^2=1\). The additional description of the same ordinary exchange removes that second possibility. It fixes the compensated two-pulse phase and leaves the two individual choices \(z=\pm\exp(i\pi/4)\). On a fourth idle word the direct/indirect comparison gains a fourth factor \(x^2\), and the first three words already give the same criterion.

The actual inverse of the balanced pulse restores every input for every unit phase. This follows directly from unitarity. Two forward pulses followed by the ordinary swap's inverse are a different sequence. On two moving words and one idle word, a calibrated unsigned undo leaves the complete amplitude map diagonal with entries \(i,i,z^2\). Its ordinary probability table is identity for all \(z\). Requiring it to be the identity on every coherent preparation up to one global phase would already impose \(z^2=i\). The ordinary growth and motion laws contain no such requirement. [Explicit inverse and double-forward checks](phase_check.py)

The shared calibration across addresses is consequential. Write \(x,y,w\) for the normalized idle factors of the complete \(P,Q,R\) implementations. On \(a,b,c\), the ratio of the indirect to the direct amplitude is \(y/w,x,x\). A family of signs can obey all the same complete permutations projectively: choose a sign \(s_j\) at each word, and assign the idle factor \(s_i s_j\) to the exchange of \(i,j\). Each completed exchange is then the ordinary permutation conjugated by the common diagonal sign matrix, up to one common factor for that exchange. This is an exact representation of all ordinary compositions at the level of coherent states up to global phase. The physical comparison depends on whether the preparation and readout use that same frame.

A full swap also needs a calibration if used as a coherent undo. Give its moving action the common phase \(\alpha\) and its unchanged word the phase \(\beta\), initially using the same moving-pair reference everywhere. Comparing \(a\) with \(c\) after two pulses and that inverse reads the ratio \(i\beta/(\alpha z^2)\). Ordinary truth tables put no phase bound on an arbitrary reversible undo. The alternative in the approved design first mixes \(a,c\), applies \(P\) twice, and directly recombines \(b,c\), using no full undo. If each moving pair has the same calibrated reference, the probability of recording \(b\) is \(|i+z^2|^2/4\). It is \(1/2\) for \(z=1\) and \(1\) for \(z=\exp(i\pi/4)\). A separate *one*-pulse experiment returns to the initial word with probabilities \(5/8\) and \(1/8\) at the two surviving roots. It can therefore distinguish choices left open by perfect two-pulse substitution.

The remaining reference is observable. Allow phases \(p,q,r\) in the directed active exchanges \(a\to b,a\to c,b\to c\), respectively, with the inverse coefficient on the reverse move. The directly recombined \(b\)-amplitude is \((ip+qz^2/r)/2\). Its probability depends on the phase of \(qz^2/(pr)\) around the two alternatives. An ordinary word basis change applied to the entire apparatus leaves this ratio invariant. Changing only a coupling relative to the other two can change the recorded probability. As a calibration control, standard \(z=\exp(i\pi/4)\) with the ordinary coherent undo gives return probability one, while a valid signed permutation frame for the full undo changes it to zero, leaving the no-undo read certain. The literature already distinguishes controlling a physically specified implementation from controlling a unitary given only as an operation. The present test derives a constraint from three named knot exchanges and checks the actual one- and two-pulse ports. [Independent matrices and all ordinary words](phase_check.py) · [Araújo et al., *Quantum circuits cannot control unknown operations*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.7976)

There is also a consequence of the frozen involution and preparation tests that removes the third moving reference. Prepare \(a,c\) with \(Q\), make **four** identical forward offers of \(P\), and recombine with the actual inverse of that same \(Q\). The two moving words return with phase \(-1\) independent of the directed exchange phase. The idle word returns with \(z^4\). The \(a\)-port probability is \(|z^4-1|^2/4\), giving **0 versus 1** for the two recorded pulse laws. The direction phase of \(Q\) cancels and no full coherent swap is used. This is an additional reading of the already tested maps. It changes neither the original compensated protocol nor its stop. It still requires a repeatable coherent pulse, the inverse reference and preservation of coherence until the ordinary read. A stable common phase on each gate cancels from the probability. Four pulses alone identify only this function of the relative phase. [Independent symbolic comparison](phase_check.py)
