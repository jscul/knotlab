# Varied letter drawings audit plan

This lane owns run authorization and independent result replay. The producer
owns the experiment source, frozen definition, fitting, blind inference, and
score release. The design review and scientific referee are separate.

## Run authorization

- Regenerate bitmap support and identity independently. Check 64 acquisition
  slots per arm, exact weighting, label and turn balance, the registered
  drawing-selection rule, all declared differences in repeated presentations,
  and scorer-row order. Check that no held turn or held drawing support enters
  fitting or tuning. Check orbit disjointness, preserve the shared ancestry of
  deformed straight templates, and distinguish deliberate control overlap.
- Compare the learner and ordinary twin to their locked predecessor source.
  Trace row metadata, emitted action, the environment correctness bit, and
  writes to held ternary words. Check that blind inference restores only
  physical adaptive state and that the score phase cannot access the test key
  before verifying blind and fit releases. Check that original and opposite
  schedules share the same raw rows with only the registered reward change.
- Exercise rank ties, impossible law words, malformed restores, and receipt
  and budget guards with synthetic inputs. Challenge the donor protocols
  using original, opposite, varied, and narrow recipients. Hold input, law,
  recipient action convention, and readout fixed through swaps. Verify that
  same-convention arm exchange and opposite reward exchange test different
  claims. Any numeric twin memory remains confined to its comparator.
- Verify frozen digests, exclusive phase writes, rejected early joins, and
  pre-transition external fit and blind digest anchors. Compare all budgets,
  prospective success conditions, and nonclaims with the frozen definition.

## Result audit

- Replay all training receipts and fitted words independently. Recompute
  every candidate's rank from the training inputs, all ties before the size
  preference, and the resulting membership, calibration, and storage address
  contents. Preserve ambiguity even where groups issue the same observed bit.
- Recompute every blind action, registered comparison, narrow-varied and
  opposite donor control, and exact scorer join. Keep cell, class, pose, and
  drawing weighting visible along with representative successes and failures.
  Verify original, sham, cleared, restored, and transplanted checkpoints and
  their outcome contrasts. Cross each arm's fitted membership marks with the
  other arm's calibration in both directions. Authenticate phase anchors first.
- Attribute observed differences to the exact comparison of the two supplied
  acquisition panels. Preserve the alternative that changes in acquisition
  counts, mixtures, or ancestry explain them. Report ties, the numeric twin,
  incomplete transfer, and the limits of the physical readout. Consume fresh
  label/count associations, held predictions, and scores only on explicit
  release from the coordinator.
