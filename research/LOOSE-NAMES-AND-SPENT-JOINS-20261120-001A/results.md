## What holds at any finite size

Conversely, all fresh names born in it are interchangeable. If there is a pair, its class therefore gives the complete set of loose names in that knot. If there is no pair, the knot has exactly one loose name. The theorem admits earlier knots with repeated meetings and arbitrary earlier ages. Its proof uses the requirement that different inputs of one ordinary birth come from different witnessed parents.
