## What the operators consume

The archived complete growth step uses holding identity, live counts and shared witnesses to enumerate maximal groups and suppress jointly witnessed proposals. Each donor pays in the order of its loose names, and full funding is tested before it creates any child. For the grant, lookahead uses the proposed parent rosters to compute the next multiple-group demands. The connected ordinary operator identifies parity implication by checking for the sole birth taking all old loose names. The full parity wrapper instead reduces the input support in the span of the old equations. The not-all-equal wrapper checks whether either of the two homogeneous input assignments has any allowed old completion. Its outputs to funding are implication flags on the entire relation. The rivals first obtain the ordinary scratch plan, truncate its fresh grants according to the chosen fixed policy and build the actual reached holdings. The next proposal and all shortfalls are recomputed on those holdings. These paths are invariant under which old allowed word is displayed. Independently projecting each delivered input from the old physical relation verifies all of the implication flags of both laws.

Allowed new words, structural receipts and chosen state values are distinct data. Each group with positive grant supplies a private completion for any old paid values, and the independent fresh sets can be completed simultaneously. A zero-grant group retains an old word exactly when its actual paid values satisfy its law. The archive checks the input projections before applying this test and records the canonical K1–K3 diagnostics on the source and reached supports. For the earlier old-word counts, it enumerates the complete initial word set and multiplies independent whole-child completion counts. Closed and stalled attempts leave the previous holdings and their allowed states in place.
