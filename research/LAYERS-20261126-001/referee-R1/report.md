# Referee report on whole-held facts after parity growth
**Refuted. Block on the claimed obstruction to two independent facts.**

A connected nine-knot start grows from one class to four in one ordinary round. No single knot reads either ring parity or their sum. All four joint values occur, even after fixing the values at any one of the twenty grown knots. This establishes the two-fact prerequisite. A growth-made rule binding those facts into a next layer and recursion remain open.

## What holds

**1. The frozen counts reproduce.** All 7,975 rows and their scores match. Of 7,075 connected census starts of three to five knots, 5,111 grew. Of 900 labelled draws of six to eight knots, 417 grew. Eight grown starts in each set had four classes. In 15 of the 16, the single-knot reads span both independent class bits. In the sixteenth they span one. Thus no *funded frozen round* has two bits independent modulo all single-knot reads. At the source, 26 census and 28 held-out starts had four or more classes. Their single-knot reads span every class bit. The census counts starts once up to renaming within its twelve-distinction limit. The held-out weighting accepts labelled draws with replacement, up to eighteen distinctions and 300 draws per size. One repeats an earlier extension start up to renaming. [Full recount and per-start readings](frozen_recount_results.json)

**2. Single rings, joined rings and the tree.** The ten-knot tree goes from one class to two. The sum of its nine old shared distinctions labels the classes and no single knot reads it. Of the original 30 joined-ring specifications, 22 kept two classes in rounds one and two and eight kept one. One construction was specified twice. The eight apart controls gave one independent bit per ring of size at least four. These counts were rebuilt from the recipes. The closed study committed no per-case raw table for its tree and built-ring explorations. [Built results](built_baselines_funded.json) · [Ring baselines](built_baselines.md)

**3. The per-piece prediction failed.** Both the predicted count and the predicted label held on 130/978 eligible census starts and 28/157 eligible held-out starts. The respective failure counts were 848 and 129. Outside the conditions, one/two/four classes occurred on 3,785/341/7 census rounds and 212/43/5 held-out rounds. The third prediction, that the local reads span all labels on every four-class held-out round, failed on one of eight. The archive's scorer identifies that case as a candidate. [Scores, candidate list and freeze](record.json)

**4. Ordinary growth preserves the old allowed states.** The projection was unchanged on all 5,528 funded frozen rounds. A private fresh distinction can satisfy its birth's parity equation for any allowed old state. A birth without one must already have its parity implied. Distinct births have disjoint fresh distinctions. The original 22 built forbidding controls all had a fresh distinction at every birth, so none could remove an old state. Applied to the frozen inputs, the two rival fresh-count rules did remove old states on 155 and 213 rounds respectively. [Projection checks](frozen_recount_results.json) · [Earlier keep-clause result](../../NAE-GROWTH-20261119-001/nae_growth.md)

**Counterexample under the frozen moves.** Join two four-knot rings through a ninth knot with three shared distinctions, two to opposite members of the first ring and one to a member of the second. Give the ninth knot zero loose distinctions, the member it meets on the second ring three, and the other seven ring knots two each. There are no double meetings. All three frozen conditions hold. The allowed/move ranks change from 19/19 to 30/28. A second count by support size found all 69 distinct shortest moves through the 50 grown distinctions. All nine source and twenty grown supports pass the canonical knot test. Two births take inputs from both rings. The old ring parities take four joint values before growth and become invariant under every shortest move after growth. The predicted parity of *all* old shared distinctions is their sum. Following those same labels gives four classes through round eight. Funding the ninth knot with four loose distinctions gives only one whole-held bit after round one. [Size-by-size recount](joint_check.json) · [Eight-round certificate](built_witness.json) · [Funding controls](built_search.json)

**Independent extensions.** In 1,024 new labelled draws at 9, 10, 12 and 16 knots, 87 grew and 21 had at least four classes. One ten-knot start gained a second whole-held bit and kept both through round four. All four label pairs occur at every allowed reading of every single knot. Under another weighting that funds every knot, all 1,024 draws grew and only one had four classes at round one. It had one bit beyond the local reads. Separate thirteen- and seventeen-knot constructions gained three and four independent ring parities. A 25-knot control already held its two bits at the source. **Alternate moves.** Literal enumeration covered all 143,104 allowed states across the 16 eligible frozen rows. With every change of at most five distinctions admitted, twelve rows had one class and four had two, with the remaining bits locally readable. In a different connected nine-knot preparation, two four-knot rings share distinctions with one intervening knot through double meetings. Under the same bound of five it had zero, zero, two and two whole-held bits at the source and rounds one, two and three. All 78 distinctions participated at round two.
