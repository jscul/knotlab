# Which paid histories a two-meeting reading preserves

A pair of spent knots can authorize the same paid children and fresh grants as the global separating-name rule on **every original holder tree**, at every funded age, including the first two opportunities for the spent pair to act. The two rules also preserve ordinary growth on the specified seven- and 21-knot recurring sources. On longer circuits the bounded rule can authorize a genuine new child that the global rule excludes permanently from the two marked families. All statements here apply to parity and not-all-equal. ## The information read at one shared distinction

Let e name an actual meeting between different holders w,z. Count a step through one shared distinction. Include all holders at most two steps from either endpoint in the supplied physical holdings. Retain the internal individual shared distinctions, the marked e, and the numbers of loose and shared distinctions held by each included knot. An outward incidence contributes to a boundary holder's count. Its outside endpoint and other outward identifications remain hidden. Arbitrary consistent changes of holder and distinction names, including exchange of the endpoints, preserve this reading. Its reach is bounded even at arbitrarily high arity. The number of entries can grow with arity.

Require both endpoints spent. Remove e alone within that display. If the ends then lie in different parts, take their full direct live-neighbor sets L and R. When both are nonempty and disjoint, add one group with every member of their union as a paying parent. Keep all ordinary groups. Each distinct group costs each actual donor one distinct private name. Remaining loose names pay for unaries after every group is funded. The whole cut stalls on a deficit. Both spent holders retain e, and each paid input goes from one to two holders. The original payment proof therefore extends every old allowed word and gives every new nonclosing holder its full local law, under its earlier finite connected hypotheses. Its argument assigns a private fresh distinction to each new support and uses at most one previously loose distinction per paying old holder in that support. The lone closing parity support has the equation obtained by summing the old ones. Zero fresh in that case differs from a stall. The two laws supply these state constraints. The decision to use this particular participation rule is an extra assumption. **Exact reach.** After deleting e, distance to the nearer of w,z does not change. A shortest route from either endpoint that starts by traversing e would be shorter starting from its other endpoint. The display is thus the union of two radius-two sets measured after deletion. If w reaches z inside it, follow a route until it passes between these sets. They intersect, or a meeting joins their members. There is then a residual route of at most 2+1+2=5 meetings. Conversely every vertex along a route of at most five is within two of an endpoint. For radius r the same argument gives 2r+1. A parallel name contributes a residual route of length one. If a live holder pays on both sides it gives a route of length two. Erasing boundary counts leaves this connectivity test unchanged, while the live status of each direct neighbor remains available. The complete annotated records can distinguish some physically different origins that give the same local decision. In the usual literature, dependence on only a fixed-radius input is a locality condition. This study adds actual two-holder names, donor payments, whole-cut grants and repeating sources to that reading. [Naor and Stockmeyer, *What Can Be Computed Locally?*](https://doi.org/10.1137/S0097539793254571)

**Different groups and actual charges.** Choose a live donor u on the w side and v on the z side of an authorized e. If an ordinary group contained both, they would have a common witness, giving a route from u to v of length at most two. The two terminal meetings through w and z then give a bypass of e of length at most four. Every meeting of that route has a live endpoint and cannot be e. If an additional group for a different name f contained both, they would have a route of at most three through one of f's spent holders or through both joined by f. All meetings except possibly f have live endpoints. The remaining meeting is f itself, distinct from e. This argument still works if f and e share a spent holder. If their two spent holders coincide, the second parallel name already gives a length-one bypass. Repetitions in any walk shorten to a route. Adding w–u and v–z produces a bypass of e of length at most five, contrary to eligibility. Thus even a *single opposite-side pair* occurs in no other proposed group. Each qualifying name has a unique added union and every other qualifying name gives a different one. Counting each distinct union once cannot merge two permissions. Different unions may still charge the same live holder. Repeated names between the same donor and its spent neighbor count as one donor. The small funded checks include a live holder incident to both sides and a parallel incidence on one side. ## Equality at the first two opportunities and forever after

Consider the following sufficient class of starts. For every original shared name whose endpoints are both live or both spent, if the name is nonseparating globally, its endpoints have some alternative of length at most five after that one occurrence is removed. Mixed pairs need no condition. This includes all simple holder trees and every tree of holder pairs with arbitrary parallel named meetings on its links.

At the supplied cut each globally separating eligible name qualifies locally, and the hypothesis excludes every extra locally qualifying name. Thus the two policies propose the same groups, assign the same charges and either both stall or have the very same whole first parent roster. Couple their admissible donor-slot permutations. For their first prices, attach a private placeholder to each actual first birth. Every originally live holder supplied inputs and has live provisional children. Every originally spent holder has none. Distinct newborns meet only their own former parents. Therefore a candidate spent pair with a live donor on each side at the provisional cut must consist of two previously live originals with a shared name e.

An originally nonseparating e of this kind already has its short alternative and still has it. If e originally separated w,z, its removal splits the old holders into two parts. Its separation is lost only if some new child C has parents p,q in opposite parts, since each newborn has only old parents and no two newborns share a name. Two parents of any ordinary group are joined by at most two original meetings. For an extra group the bound is three by using its two spent witnesses. Choose such a route of length at most three between p,q. Any route between them crosses the original separating e. Going from w to its side's parent, through C to the other parent and back to z gives an alternative of length at most four in the grown holdings. Thus the radius-two determination at age one also equals the global result. In fact a new spent–spent group could not have straddled this particular initially live–live e: its short route uses live-to-spent meetings and its own distinct spent–spent meeting. An ordinary straddler therefore gives the sharper bound three here.

The two provisional age-one group rosters agree, so every demand at every newborn agrees. The fresh grant is the larger of one, the arity floor and that demand. Using one placeholder to forecast is valid because the ordinary and added tests inspect whether a holder is live, independently of its positive number of private names. Actual extra fresh names are single-holder incidences and do not change which old holders meet. If parity instead closes with one child, the provisional next multi-parent roster is empty and the agreed actual grant is zero. At every funded cut the current live births spend all their private names. Thereafter a spent holder touching a live donor must be its immediately preceding true parent. From age two two such eligible spent holders would themselves have been born together, hence share no name. The proviso for future grants holds by the same argument. The complete true histories, every grant, every closing birth and every whole-stage veto therefore agree for the two policies on this class. They agree on individual physical inputs under a common admissible slot assignment. Without such an assignment each record is defined up to the corresponding input-name permutation.

The argument for ages two and later, the positive 5/17 repair and the finite or permanently separated marked tree outcomes were established for the global rule. This equality theorem transfers those results to this bounded rule. In particular, with the same original endpoint readings, it gives delays two on the five- and seventeen-knot preparations and infinity on the 37-knot shield. The seven all-live and 27-knot five-spent comparison gives three versus four. The sharp upper bound 4t−1 on the initial ordinary holder separation of two ancestors of a common step-t birth also holds here: each first group's initial parent separation is at most three, and each true adjacency at age one projects to two initial parents coinciding or directly meeting. The earlier route proof gives 4t−1 from those two facts. Its tree preparations attain equality for every t, now with identical actual grants under both policies. ## Sources on which all three histories agree

On the seven-knot, 25-name recurring source there is only one original spent holder. Each original shared name has an alternative of length at most four, using its three- or five-knot circuit or the second parallel name. No name authorizes added growth at either initial opportunity, including provisional lookahead. All later groups are ordinary by the preceding-cohort proof. On the 21-knot, 60-name recurring source no original spent holders meet, and no two originally live holders meet. These two exclusions cover the original cut and the provisional age-one cut. The same reasoning gives identical histories on every orientation of the six-live partnered explosive preparation. The independent source proofs thus keep their original conclusions under both added policies: persistent products recur indefinitely in the seven- and 21-knot histories, while the partnered explosive recurrence makes every member inherit every two-cuts-earlier member and precludes such disjoint releases.
