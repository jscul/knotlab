## What stands

1. **[Refuted by R1 as worded.] No recorded stage passed the disjoint-cycle test; on the sampled ten-knot start, three
   disjoint path parities bind at round 3.** Under parity with the shortest changes as the small changes, on every stage
   read (7,961 stages with forced relations, not the 7,713 of an unfinished prefix: every census start of three to five knots and 900 fresh starts
   of six to eight, rounds 0 to 2; the witnesses to round 3). No relation the knots' laws force splits into three or more
   separate cycles that are each a fact no single knot reads.
2. **The argument behind it.** Facts carried by old distinctions keep the relations of the start forever (refereed);
   every relation forced among cycle parities lies in one space, the cut of a set of knots with no loose distinction that
   is also a cycle (the literature's bicycle space over two values); on the witnesses with two, three and four facts that
   space is empty at rounds 0 to 3.
3. **On the owner's rungs**: repetition shown; a tower not, though the facts it would bind exist; recursion not shown.

**Not shown.** Not-all-equal; the forbidding cuts (which can fix a loose value); other readings of a small change on the
census; larger starts and more rounds; a proof that no element of B ever binds.
