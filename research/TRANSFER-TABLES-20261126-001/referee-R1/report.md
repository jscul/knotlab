# Independent referee report on tables under the one-round transfer law
**Confirmed with fixes. No Block for the registered study.**

Across the 30 reader splits, the largest CHSH with signed Pauli products is **exactly 2** under each registered counting and lift. Under those countings, the pair lift gives a larger table only after supplying equal-phase coherence and rotated local reads. All 30 splits have a knot in the whole of the pair lift’s reached states. Freely changing a local read leaves the other reader’s answer distribution unchanged. A different, supplied weighting on the same 13 words gives signed Pauli products **67/31**.

## What was rebuilt

One founder allows the four even words on distinctions 0, 1 and 2. Each distinction pays for one birth with a fresh pair, giving the four listed scopes 012, 034, 156 and 278. Each lift reaches the quiet word once and four words from each of the three active starts. Both lifts therefore reach 13 words, but only the quiet word belongs to both supports. Counting from the start gives it weight 1/4 and each other word 1/16. Counting each reached word once gives 1/13. Ignoring the transfer and enforcing only the four listed parities gives 32 words of weight 1/32. Among all assignments of nine distinctions to two nonempty readers and an unread part of size one or two, 30 unordered splits keep every listed scope from meeting both readers. The arithmetic below uses independently generated states and splits. A subsequent replay of all 39 permitted lifts across the original implementation’s three mode names agreed word for word. **Value reads and signaling.** For a fixed reached word the four binary answers give CHSH +2 or −2. Averaging over any single weighting gives absolute CHSH at most 2. I also tallied the four joint outcomes for every pair of nonempty parity reads, 2,934 setting pairs per weighting. Summing over either answer always gave the independently tallied local marginal, with zero discrepancy in integer arithmetic. For the coherent version, summing the remote read’s two projectors gives the identity, so every local marginal is independent of the remote setting, including for rotated reads. Direct application of complex local projectors gave a largest marginal discrepancy below 7 × 10⁻¹⁶ on the 150 registered cases. [Exact tallies and direct projector checks](read_tables.json)

**Signed Pauli products.** I represented a product by its flip mask and sign mask and summed correlations with an integer Walsh transform, including the sign for every Y. Each weighting used 582,390 nonidentity local product pairs summed over the 30 splits. For each pair of settings of the smaller reader, the two signed choices on the other reader maximize the sum and difference of the two correlation rows. Requiring two distinct nonidentity products on each reader still gives 2 on 24 splits. On the six singleton splits the maxima are 1 under start counting, 10/13 under counting each reached word once, and 0 under the listed finish. Extending the search to constants and repeated settings gives exactly 2 on every split. One witness for 2 with distinct products has distinction 2 unread. On the larger reader, the parities of 01 and 134 both equal distinction 2 on every allowed word. On the smaller reader use the parity of 78 and the value of 7. The two identical correlations with 7 cancel, and the two with 78 both equal 1. The three preregistered spot correlations rebuilt to 1/2, 1/2 and 1 in their stated order. **Why the unrestricted scores are maxima.** Fixing the unread word gives an unnormalized pure state of Schmidt rank at most two. For Schmidt coefficients u and v, put m = u² + v². Its largest CHSH contribution is 2√(m² + 4u²v²). Summing these optima bounds the mixture from above. In each violating split there is just one rank-two term. The larger reader can distinguish its support from the remaining terms. The certificate constructs one pair of reads per reader that attains the sum, and checks negativity separately by partial transpose. For example, with 2 unread and start counting, half the weight can attain 2√2 and half can attain 2 with those same reads. The rotated table is a genuine mathematical excess for the supplied state and reads. The transfer law itself specifies the allowed lifts and supplies neither operation. The original seeded optimization by itself produced lower bounds. The independent bounds and attaining reads agree with all 150 archived rows to their printed precision. **Controls.** On the coherent even pair the negativity is 1/2, signed Pauli CHSH is 2 and unrestricted CHSH is 2√2. On the three equally weighted words 00, 01 and 10, these are 1/3, 7/3 and 2√13/3. With reads X and Z on each distinction, the three-word correlations in the order XX, XZ, ZX, ZZ are (2, 2, 2, −1)/3. This control really can exceed 2 with the searched products. **Knots in the whole.** On the pair support all 36 pairs are free. Of the 84 triples, 76 pass K1–K3. Four allow four states, 24 allow six, and 48 allow seven. Testing all 511 nonempty scopes finds no larger knot. All 76 triples also pass the repository’s canonical checker. For instance, 078 excludes exactly 011 and 100, with every two-distinction part free. The six preregistered triples alone cover all 30 splits, and on every split some passing triple wholly held by the two readers meets both. The four listed parities together with one of the extra triple indicators per birth admit exactly the 13 words. Multiplying these indicators by 1/13 gives the uniform reached weighting. Multiplying by 1/4 and by 1/2 for each paid input equal to one gives the start weighting. The four listed scopes alone admit 32 words. The minimum support has 27 knots. Taken together, their local patterns admit 49 words, including 011000000, which the minimum lift cannot reach. Its weighting does factor on the four listed holdings, where the three child relations each have a constrained proper part. The 32-word finish also has a knot meeting both readers on all 30 splits when larger scopes are tested, yet its weighting factors between readers given the unread values on all 30. Both registered pair-support weightings fail that conditional independence on precisely 15 splits. ## Fixes

1. **Narrowing — name the registered countings.** “Under the recorded transfer law no table exceeds the classical limit with the model's reads (values, changes, combined products), under either lift, any counting, any split.” Write “under each registered counting, on the 30 registered splits.” The README also says “The transfer law's weighting factors over these knots, as the advisor showed by hand.” The seven-factor construction above is for the two registered pair-support weightings. To falsify the unrestricted wording, give words 101011101, 101011110 and 101101101 count 100 each and each of the other ten pair-lift words count 1. Digits run from distinction 0 to 8. With 2 unread, take A = (X₃X₄, Z₃) and B = (X₇X₈, Z₇). The four correlations are (220, 176, 176, −98)/310, giving **67/31**. For founder 101, the two lift choices have count table `[[100,100],[100,1]]`. Its nonzero determinant shows where this supplied weighting departs from the independent registered choices. It leaves the support, knots and free-setting no-signaling result intact. [README, lines 47 and 52–54](../README.md) · [Exact witness](reweight.json) · [Independent sum](reweight.py)

2. **Narrowing — distinguish changing a read from changing the preparation, and correct what was checked.** “Nothing signals.” “Under the transfer law, value reads never exceed 2 and never signal (entailed, checked).” The original value routine returns a CHSH score and never compares marginals. The independent marginal test above supplies that check for freely set local reads. The transfer does carry preparation information. With founder distinction 2 fixed to zero, preparing 000 gives 00 at 78, and preparing 110 gives 11 there with certainty under the pair lift. At start weighting, the joint masses of 0 and 7 in this part are `[[1/4,0],[0,1/4]]` before normalization, as reported in section 2 of the factoring referee. [README, line 54](../README.md) · [Decision, line 10](../decision.md) · [Original value routine, lines 73–87](../tables.py) · [Rebuilt example](read_tables.json) · [Earlier referee, §2](../../FACTOR-WEIGHTS-20261126-001/referee-R2/report.md)

3. **Not a block — mark empty value-read searches as unavailable.** The output field is “value reads, largest CHSH”; the even-pair control prints `"value reads, largest CHSH": 0.0`. The routine initializes `best = 0.0` and ends with `return best`. A singleton reader has just one nonempty parity, so there is no pair of distinct parity settings. This affects six of 30 splits in each registered case (30 stored zeros), plus the even-pair control. If repeating settings is allowed, repeating Z on both readers of that control gives 2. The maximum over splits for each registered case is unchanged. [Original routine and control, lines 73–87 and 239–242](../tables.py) · [Archived output](../tables_out.jsonl.gz) · [Defined and empty cases](read_tables.json)

4. **Narrowing — the nine-distinction cap applies at the finish.** “The factoring study's failing control (dividing over the whole growth at once): no sampled start with at most nine distinctions fails, so it **did not exercise the test**.” Of 300 starts drawn without replacement from the 7,075-start census with seed 20261129, 85 funded no round. The other 215 funded two rounds and finished with at least 11 distinctions. The nine-distinction cap admitted **zero histories and zero determinant tests**. As a check on the difference, the 14-distinction finish cap admits 17 histories and gives 684 nonzero determinants among 1,188,944 positive-mass tables. Three of those histories start with seven or eight distinctions. One starts with seven, finishes with 13 and has 90 failures, including a determinant of 1/1620 before normalization. [README, lines 40–41](../README.md) · [Rebuilt selection and witnesses](division_control.json)

5. **Narrowing — require the knot test before inferring contact.** “Correlation made by a law is contact.” The minimum lift itself supplies a counterexample. The fresh distinctions 3 and 4 each have occupied weight 1/4 under start counting, yet never occur occupied together. Their covariance is −1/16. Their constrained pair is a proper part of every candidate knot containing both, so each such candidate fails K2. The pair lift’s 30-split conclusion rests on its passing triples. [Decision, lines 33–35](../decision.md) · [Exact minimum-lift counterexample](induced.json)

6. **Narrowing — qualify “half” by weighting.** “Where the excess comes from: on the split with distinction 2 unread, half the weight is a pure state in which the ring reader's quiet start goes with 00 at the far pair and its active start with 11, and both readers can tell which half they are in (each holds the value of distinction 2 as a parity of its own).” The rank-two term has weight 1/2 under start counting and 5/13 when counting each reached word once. Its complement then has weight 8/13. This accounts for the two different maxima. [README, lines 35–37](../README.md) · [Conditional weights](unrestricted.json)

7. **Not a block — correct the counting name in the code header.** “Two countings of the reached states: from the start (the four starts equally weighted, each birth's permitted lifts equally weighted) and from the finish (every reached state once).” The implemented and preregistered finish counting uses all 32 words of the listed laws. “Every reached state once” names the separate 13-word counting. The numerical output labels these correctly. [Code header, lines 5–7, and counting loop, lines 198–202](../tables.py) · [Preregistration](../preregistration.md)

## Reproduction and limits

I read the decision, README, preregistration, both study scripts, the original transfer law, the repository instructions and section 2 of the earlier factoring referee. The three frozen file digests matched. I knew the published predictions and had seen the archived scores. The primary reconstructions generated their own words, splits and scores without importing either study script or reading the compressed results. I then compared the rebuilt rows with the archive. The factoring control used its original ordered census as input and rebuilt the growth, filters and determinants.

From the repository root, run the scripts in this folder with `.venv/bin/python -B`, one numerical thread (`OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 MKL_NUM_THREADS=1`) and `prlimit --as=2147483648 --` before the interpreter. Timed prefixes used the same configuration as their full runs. For [read_tables.py](read_tables.py) use `--pilot` then no arguments (0.054 s and 0.61 s). For [unrestricted.py](unrestricted.py) use `--limit 1` then no arguments (0.09 s and 0.15 s). For [induced.py](induced.py) use `--pilot` then no arguments (0.22 s and 4.32 s). For [division_control.py](division_control.py) use `--prefix 20` then no arguments (0.069 s and 0.37 s). [reweight.py](reweight.py) prints the exact positive-weight witness. [verify_record.py](verify_record.py) checks the saved results against the frozen rows and the original transfer law (`--pilot` then no arguments, 0.021 s each).

**Not run.** Coherent scoring of whole-growth division had no eligible case under its frozen cap. The wider control replay checked determinants only. More founders, later transfer rounds, a not-all-equal pair transfer, and optimization over all supplied weightings were outside this review.
