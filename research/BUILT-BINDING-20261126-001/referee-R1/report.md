# Independent referee report on built bindings
**Confirmed with fixes. Block on the claim that the held-out starts have no binding. The change in class facts in (c) and the ordinary-growth origin argument hold.**

Growth changes which parities survive small changes. Across the 32 built starts, the least change that flips a ring parity has size **5, 7, 9 and 11** at rounds zero through three. Thus the original distinctions already carry the bound class facts at round one under changes of at most five. Round two supplies disjoint carriers made of new distinctions. Every designated carrier needs all its members if read in isolation, yet every one has a smaller equivalent reader elsewhere. On the even-length rings its two proper parts carry independent facts of their own. [Ring measurements](ring_counts.json)

There is also a binding on the **nineteenth accepted eight-knot start in the 900-member held-out sample**. At round two, three disjoint sets of sizes 2, 2 and 3 give exactly **000, 011, 101 and 110**, even with any one of the 20 knots fixed. All 102 allowed changes of one to five distinctions preserve the three parities. Three disjoint sets made entirely of new distinctions read the same facts. Their equation already held at the start. [Literal certificate](heldout_certificate.json)

## What was not run
