# Interpretation: birth rules for the doubling law

Run of the frozen contract `BIRTH-RULE-20261109-001A-E001` (exploratory), 2026-11-09, launch `birth-rule-a1` (under a minute); 16 runs
(four rules, P = 1 to 4), every reading exact through a full cycle of the state.

## The hypotheses

| Hypothesis | Verdict | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1: copy and zero leave no stage balanced after a doubling | **Holds** | No stage of copy or zero is balanced at any doubling of any P, the first included: a new ring holds no distinction of its own, so the contacts on it cannot bind it. |
| H2: open plus copy leaves no stage balanced after the first doubling, P = 1 included | **Refuted** | With P = 1 the 6 × 6 and 12 × 12 stages are balanced; with every P the 6 × 6 stage is. All fail at 24 × 24 (P = 1) or 12 × 12 (P ≥ 2). |
| H3: open reproduces the control | **Holds** | Balanced at every doubling for P = 1 and 3 with no hearing; unbalanced at 24 × 24 (P = 2) and 12 × 12 (P = 4), as `DOUBLE-BALANCE-20261109-001A` read. |
| H4: no rule keeps a stage balanced at a doubling lag where own cells hear another ring | **Refuted, narrowly** | Open plus copy keeps the 6 × 6 stage (108 own cells hearing another ring) and, at P = 1, the 12 × 12 stage (540) balanced. No rule keeps a stage balanced with hearing at 24 × 24. |
| H5: under copy the old rings stay bound at no more doublings than under open | **Holds** | Under copy the old rings are bound only at the first doubling; under open at every doubling for P = 1 and 3. |

## What it shows

- **Open birth is needed for the new rings.** A copied or zeroed ring bears no distinction, so no contact can bind it: every such stage
  is unbalanced, as A44's reason said.
- **Inheritance buys two doublings.** Adding the parent's value to an open distinction lets each new ring hear its parent from birth and
  keeps every stage balanced through 12 × 12 at P = 1, the first time in this line that a growing network is balanced while its rings
  hear one another; by 24 × 24 it fails at every P.
- **The frozen decision rule.** H4 failed, so open plus copy at P = 1 was read further, to the size limit: it does not sustain balance,
  and no rule read replaces A44 for growth that keeps going.

- One tube at g = 2, four rules, P to 4, sizes to 24 × 24.
