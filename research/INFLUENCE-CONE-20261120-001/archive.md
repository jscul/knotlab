## Funding and confirmation

The archive does not exercise the global shortage exception. Each admitted source already funds its groups. Adding one loose distinction at an existing live holder leaves those groups unchanged and increases available supply at that holder, so it cannot create a source shortage anywhere. Every later reached ordinary cut has its next group costs funded by the preceding lookahead. A real shortage in a different initial preparation could cancel a funded receiver's output without producing a knot at all. Globally implied parity closure is a distinct outcome: it gives an actual last birth, with zero fresh distinctions. Neither outcome is present in these archive comparisons. [Ordinary funding and closing clauses](../ORDINARY-DYNAMICS-20261120-001/end_operator.md#where-the-finite-radius-rule-holds)

The initial forecast predicts exactly one extra unary at the edited holder. Its only provisional neighbors are the other members of that holder's child roster. All share one parent, so no unheld group may include that unary. It adds one prospective pair with each of them and affects no other demand. The literal first-step roster and fresh counts match this calculation on all 84 edits under both laws. At every scored cut the inherited independent complete-growth engine reproduces the physical spent and fresh slots and the full demand vector. Independently permuting both knot and distinction names in the pair on draw 91 preserves all three initial rulers and the priced parent structures at all three cuts.

A separate reconstruction from literal spent slots recovered all three source rulers and the first actual common descendants. Its independent incidence comparison reproduced all 1,278 roster and fresh-count verdicts per law.
