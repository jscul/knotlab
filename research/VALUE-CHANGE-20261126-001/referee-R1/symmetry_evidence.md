## Why the enumeration covers both invariance conditions

Each knot is one label subset. A distinction is uniquely given by its two holders, which differ by one label. A holder symmetry preserves the least number of successive single-label changes between any two knots. Toggling a fixed set of labels can take the image of the empty subset back to the empty subset. After that, the symmetry permutes the singletons. Once that permutation is removed, every subset is fixed. Its least number of changes from the empty subset gives its size, and its least number of changes from each singleton then tells whether it contains that singleton's label. Thus the holder symmetries are generated by single-label toggles and label permutations. The ones fixing the founder are generated by label permutations alone.

The rebuild uses one bit for each orbit of unheld distinction pairs. The bit is on when those pairs anticommute. Each knot-pair equation is the parity of the distinction swaps required to interchange the two products. The closing sign is the parity of anticommuting pairs whose occurrences interleave in the full product. Direct cancellation checked the interleaving formula on every orbit and on a basis of the solutions. Reversing the knot order preserved the sign for every basis element. A nonzero sign functional on the solution space assigns half its choices to each sign. A zero functional assigns them all to even. [Independent rebuild](symmetry_check.py)

The pilot used the first two orbit columns of each of the four configurations and took 0.00268 CPU seconds. The full calculation took 0.01333 CPU seconds and 12,832 KiB peak resident memory in a single process. Both used `.venv/bin/python -B` with a 1,900,000 KiB virtual-memory cap. Unrestricted commutation choices and other closures lay outside this check. [Pilot results](symmetry_pilot.json)

## Replay from one founder

The independent parity replay accepts a single knot with all its distinctions loose. The three-place founder gives 3, 3 and 1 births and closes with 8 knots and 12 distinctions. The four-place founder gives 4, 6, 4 and 1 births and closes with 16 knots and 32 distinctions. The matching sends the founder to the empty subset and identifies each singleton by its shared distinction with the founder. Every grown distinction has two holders, and the matching sends them bijectively to all subset pairs differing in one label. The round of a knot is its subset size, and its parents are all the subsets obtained by deleting one label. [Replay and matching](symmetry_growth_results.json)

For each replay, the check recovered every candidate symmetry from the image of the founder and of its neighbors, then verified that the resulting map preserves each pair of holders. The resulting maps agree as sets with the label permutations and toggles described above. There are 48 holder symmetries for the eight-knot closure, of which 6 fix the founder. The corresponding counts for sixteen knots are 384 and 24. The pilot tried the first two candidate maps for each replay and took 0.00294 CPU seconds. The full check took 0.00742 CPU seconds and peaked at 14,484 KiB. The replay used the same command environment and memory cap as the sign calculation.
