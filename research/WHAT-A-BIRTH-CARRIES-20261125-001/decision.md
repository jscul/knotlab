## The answer

The direction asked what the least is that a growth law must let a birth receive for a pattern the growth makes to
have a course that depends on which second pattern it meets.

**For a count, nothing new is needed; and nothing found is more than a count.**

1. **The recorded ordinary count already hands a count over (ticket A).** Under the recorded ordinary count with the
   recorded taking rule, the first pattern's first join receives 2 + M fresh distinctions, M being the births the
   second pattern's usable members make that round, on all 16,880 runs, in every numbering of the start. So the first
   pattern's course differs with how many of the second pattern's members were left usable. The link is a start knot:
   the meeting is supplied by the start.
2. **The link can be a knot the growth made (ticket B).** Under a law that uses no names (a recorded ration with short
   knots waiting, and one rule added by hand: a birth takes a shared distinction only when exactly one is on offer),
   two parts the growth made meet only through a knot the growth made that both descend from, for 88 pairs under each
   knot law. No start knot holds a distinction through which they meet. There one part's course does not change with
   the other.
3. **With a second added rule the course changes through that made link, by one count.** Where an offshoot receives
   as many fresh distinctions as its parent had joins asked of it (at least two), 280 pairs under each knot law meet
   through a made knot both descend from, and the first part's course differs with the second barred: attributed
   through the meeting on every pair, by one value at one birth. That value says whether the second part's two
   members made their join, which is also whether the parts meet at all.
4. **Nothing is composed.** In no numbering of ticket A and on no pair of ticket B does a difference need more than
   one value at one birth. Where two second patterns give the same count, they are not separated.
5. **So no pattern read anything.** The law passed a count: through a start knot under the recorded rules, through a
   made knot under two added ones. The two parts of ticket B are alike, two branches of one made knot.

The filed stop asked for the first law under which a made pattern's course differs between two second patterns,
through their meeting, under every numbering. Read to the letter it is met, at no added cost, by the recorded law of
ticket A; ticket B moves the link from the start to a made knot at the cost of two added rules. Read as the owner's
question, whether anything made responds to which thing it meets, it is not met: what passes is whether, or how
many, never which.
