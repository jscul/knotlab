## Setting

A growth law acts in rounds on a structure of "knots" (each knot holds several "distinctions"; two knots are partners
when they share one; a distinction held by one knot only is "loose"). A start is built by hand: a FIRST SEED (one of 15
small structures), one LINK distinction, and a SECOND SEED (a small structure of 1 to 5 knots: chains, a three-armed
star, rings of 3 or 4, each knot with 0 to 4 loose distinctions). The link is held by one knot of the first seed and by
one knot of the second seed, the HELD KNOT. Every later knot is born from parents; its descent is "of the first",
"of the second" or "of both". For a run we record, round by round, the multiset of births that are of the first seed
only, each birth as (kind, number of parents, number of places, fresh distinctions, taken distinctions).

A STUDY fixes the law (18 laws: 3 rations x 3 treatments of knots that owe more joins than they have loose
distinctions (retire / make offshoots / wait) x a taking rule or none) and the first seed, and runs every second seed.
Through the last round R* common to all runs of the study (9 in most), two second seeds are in the same CLASS when the
first seed's own births are the same in every round; otherwise the pair is TOLD APART. A PROFILE of a second seed
DECIDES the class in a study when no two second seeds with the same profile are told apart.

## Statements I propose to write

S1. The earlier reading is withdrawn: the registered number 49 stands, but it was a function tested on almost no pairs,
    and on 345 second seeds the registered profile decides the class in 3 studies of 268; 27% of the 51,435 pairs that
    share it are told apart.
S2. No tally of counts tried decides the class: not what the second seed's own births are (15% of 23,358 sharing pairs
    told apart), and not the seed's own knots without their arrangement (33% of 24,656 pairs told apart, at least one
    in 238 studies of 268).
S3. What decides it is the arrangement of the second seed near the held knot: with no taking rule, the seed out to two
    steps decides the class in 133 studies of 135 (19 of 19,035 sharing pairs told apart, all under one ration with
    waiting; none of 12,690 where short knots retire or make offshoots) and one step does not (24 of 135). With the
    taking rule two steps decide in 79 of 133 and three in 123 of 133.
S4. So on built starts what the first seed's descent makes follows the form of what it is linked to, near the link, and
    "a count profile" does not describe it. Each single birth still turns on a small comparison (can a knot pay the
    joins it owes); the form gets through because that comparison is made knot by knot.
S5. Limits: seen material, one program, no argument for "two steps"; beyond two steps the seeds vary only in loose
    counts, never in whether a knot is a member, so "two steps" may belong to the seed list.
