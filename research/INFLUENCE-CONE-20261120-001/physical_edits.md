# Which part of the start was changed?

The two interventions below give different answers to “how far is the changed knot?” Both are actual edits of physical holdings. The first changes a relation the receiving knot already belongs to at the start. In the second every such relation near the receiving knot agrees out to the proved radius. Both start pairs use the ordinary law unchanged.

## A knot can witness a join its own family never makes

Take six live knots in a cycle, indexed 0 through 5 for the proof. Each consecutive pair holds one shared distinction and each knot has three loose. Remove the holding at 0 of the shared distinction with 5. Keep that distinction at 5 and keep **every other knot's set of distinctions exactly**. The edited physical arrangement is the connected six-knot path. Knot 5 has one more loose distinction, and 0 ceases to witness 1 and 5 together. Both starts pass both complete local projection gates. Each holder has private positions; deleting any one holder leaves each remaining component connected to the wired exterior. Prescribing one allowed not-all-equal support leaves another free place at every other holder. All were funded.

In each start, 0 and receiver 1 belong to opposite parts of the physical bipartition. Every growing-end witnessed step goes between knots in the same part. Thus 0 and 1 are at infinite witnessed separation in **both** arms, and are one ordinary physical holder step apart. The exact-two-parent ruler also gives infinity. Their actual descendants stay disjoint on every continuing cut. Yet the first roster through untouched 1 changes from one **three-parent** birth through 1,3,5 plus two one-parent births to one **two-parent** birth through 1,3 plus the same two one-parent births. It has three children in both cases, as spending its unchanged three openings requires. The fresh count at the changed multi-parent role is 6 versus 4, although the two parent groups themselves are different. The two matched one-parent prices each stay two. Giving just one additional loose distinction to 5 on the intact cycle matches every live opening count of the edited path and retains the three-parent group. This isolates the witness change in the roster comparison. Labels do not select an output; the two versus three parents are distinguishable after arbitrary renaming. All physical rows and two positive steps were also checked.

There is no distance violation for the full change in what is *witnessed*. Projecting both witness tables away from 5 removes their difference, and in the union of the two starting witnessed ends 5 lies one step from 1. Describing only the owner of the edited holding, 0, would put the source at infinity. The changed evidence actually occurs at the receiver's starting neighborhood. Here the changed witness can be located from the holdings. Measuring from its two live members gives a different distance from measuring at the knot whose row was edited.

## The sharp funded example with all affected live sites charged

A second comparison adds one shared distinction between a spent holder and live holder y. Its surviving witness block changes from {x} to {x,y}. All old loose counts are fixed. The two sites x and y are **both** d witnessed steps from the receiver in both starts. Under both laws, the same child in the receiving family acquires one extra loose distinction at depth d−1, and the receiving family produces one more actual birth at depth d. For d=2,3,4,5 every physical birth was calculated through depth d in both arms. At depth d−1 the receiver family’s fresh counts consist of twos with one 3 in the first preparation or one 4 in the second. At depth d its total birth counts for the four controls are 4/5, 5/6, 6/7 and 7/8. No earlier fresh profile or birth count differs. The first child with both ancestries appears at d.
