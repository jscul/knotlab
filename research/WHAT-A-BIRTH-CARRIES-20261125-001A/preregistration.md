## The question

The owner asked the thread on 25 November 2026 (by the record's dates) to work through its four top-valued
directions. This epic takes the first, in the thread's words: "What is the least a law must let a birth carry for a
made pattern to read *what* it meets, not only that something is there."

Before any new rule is designed, this ticket asks it of the recorded laws:
- under the ordinary count, MET, and a choice by names (take the oldest; take the newest as its swap);
- does the course from X only differ with which second pattern it meets, whatever the numbering of the start?

The owner's fold of emergence (A223) is a pattern the rules make, whose making depends on another pattern, and which
makes a pattern of its own (the third property). This ticket tests none of the third.

## In standard terms

In the literature's words: a synchronous hypergraph-rewriting system with a label-ordered choice. The question is
whether the sets of continuations of one component, taken over every relabelling of the start, are disjoint for
different contacted components.

## What a result would and would not show

It would show:
- whether, under a listed law, the course from X only differs with which of a small class it meets;
- whether one value the law uses already separates them.

It would not show a fold of emergence. The third property is untested, and where one value separates them the law, not
the pattern, did it. Results are stated as "under take-the-oldest (or take-the-newest), whatever the numbering". "Which
second pattern" is how many members a hand-placed bar left usable.

**The result sentence for each outcome:**
- **Disjoint for some pair:** "Under ORD and MET, X's round-2 join receives 2 + M fresh distinctions in every numbering;
  the names were not used (a sole candidate). The link is Y's start knot holding the start's shared distinction. The law
  handed a count over, and the count does not separate a = h−1 from a = h." Add the verdict on (h−1, h) under each take.
- **Not disjoint for any:** "This contradicts S4." No other sentence is written until the runs are replayed birth by
  birth.
- **Undecided:** the cells as "n decided, m undecided", with the verdict on the round-2 prefix.

**The owner's sentences for each outcome:**
- **Expected (disjoint except (h−1, h)):**
  1. "Under the ordinary count and MET, on two small starts, the course from X only differs with how many of Y's members
     were left usable, in every numbering."
  2. "One value does it: X's first join takes the start's shared distinction, its only candidate, and receives one fresh
     distinction for each birth Y's usable members make that round."
  3. "The law handed a count over, through a start knot; it does not separate h − 1 from h, and no pattern read
     anything."
- **(h−1, h) also disjoint:**
  1. "As above for the count."
  2. "Beyond it, the two cases with equal counts also differ, from round 3, in every numbering, so something is composed
     over several births."
  3. "It is under names (state the take), through a start knot, on two starts; it is the one result here worth designing
     on."
- **Contradiction or undecided:**
  1. "The runs disagree with the hand argument, or n cells stopped at the guard."
  2. "No verdict is reported for those cells."
  3. "The disagreement is being replayed birth by birth."

## Stop rule

Stop when every registered run is done and each claim has its verdict per take and knot law, on its stated prefix.
- A cell with undecided runs is reported "among n decided, m undecided", never as "none".
- Disagreements with S4 or the disclosed prototype are replayed birth by birth.
- Deviations are recorded before the affected run.

## Words

"The course from X only", "the values used at X's births", "answers", "the meeting round", "the same meeting", "the
linking knots", "the compared prefix", "handed in one value", "composed over several births", "no difference", "needs

has three senses (RD 0), named where they matter.
