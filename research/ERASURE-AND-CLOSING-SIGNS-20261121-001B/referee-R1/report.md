# Independent referee report on the closing sign in ordinary growth
**Confirmed with fixes. No Block.**

Ordinary parity growth forces the closing sign to be even when all older signs are even. At the five-knot minimum, all 64 older ordinary states survive even closure. Reversing only the closing equation leaves zero. The later study of a whole with no state can use that result, including the distinction between deleting a rule and projecting a relation.

## Why the sign is forced

Add the older equations of the closing component modulo two. Every shared distinction cancels, leaving exactly the loose distinctions consumed by the zero-grant birth. Their parity is the sum of the older signs. Conversely, cancellation at a shared distinction requires including both its holders or neither. In a connected closed component the only dependence is therefore the sum of all its equations. On $h$ knots and $m$ distinctions, compatible signs give $2^{m-h+1}$ ordinary words. An odd total gives zero. Each component has its own condition. [Closing-law proof](../closing_law.md)

At the minimum, the older holdings are `{1,6,7,8}`, `{2,3,4,8}`, `{0,3,6,9}` and `{4,5,7,9}`. Their sum is the new holding `{0,1,2,5}`. Enumeration found eight even input words, each occurring eight times before closure. Each older state had one even extension and zero odd extensions. The canonical checker passed K1–K3 for the even closing projection and for the isolated odd rule. The projection from the reversed whole was empty and failed K2 and K3. Changing one older sign as well restored 64 states, now with odd closing parity. [Canonical checks](allowed.json) · [Ordinary word enumeration](minimum_results.json)

## Fixes

1. **Not a block.** In `populations.md`, “A closed piece from an earlier turn is present on **127** further funded mixed cuts, counted separately from new closures.” There were **96** such cuts. Of the fourth cuts, 65 carried one previously closed component and 31 carried two. Thus $65+31=96$ distinct cuts contained $65+2(31)=127$ component occurrences. The archived field counts occurrences correctly. The new-closure and admission rates stand. [Population report](../populations.md#funded-cuts-and-the-four-mixed-signs) · [Physical recount](rankings.json)

2. **Narrowing.** In the historical closing-sign entry of `docs/RESEARCH_GOALS.md`, “The whole then allows no state while every part does.” Every proper subset of the **equations of one connected odd component** admits a state. Projecting its empty full relation still gives an empty relation. At the five-knot minimum, each single-rule deletion restored 64 states. In the first mixed draw, with one odd component, all 16 deletions inside it restored whole states and all 51 outside deletions left zero. With two odd components, all 67 single deletions left zero. The study’s proof gives the qualified statement. 3. **Narrowing.** The same entry in `docs/RESEARCH_GOALS.md` says “Signs on births that receive a fresh distinction change nothing, so closing is the one place a sign matters.” Reversal preserves the older states and their extension counts. It changes which complete named words are allowed. In the second tree draw, the birth on `{2,3,17,18}` had 65,536 complete states at either sign. The all-zero word was even only. Setting just the fresh distinction 17 to one gave an odd-only word. Flipping 17 pairs the two sets.
