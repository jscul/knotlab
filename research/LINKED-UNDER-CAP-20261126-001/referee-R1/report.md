**Confirmed with fixes. Block on “once, then never” as a general limit.**

A second addition at a live member of the far pattern can change the receiver’s own birth count. With the same receiver, far pattern, meeting, cap and sign, the receiver made **17 knots with neither addition, 29 with the first alone, 29 with the second alone and 17 with both** in round 15. The second addition cancelled the first response. This meets the study’s definition of a hearing, which compares the twice-changed growth with the first-only growth. It leaves open the stronger question of receiving two successive messages. The original round-7 schedule still gives zero observed second hearings.

## How it was checked

[The independent growth code](growth.py) reconstructs starts, met sets, groups, shortness, births, parts and classes from the recorded definitions. A member holds a usable loose distinction. A part connects members through common knots; a class connects parts through shared distinctions. “Linked” asks whether a class contains a current member strictly owned by each end. Strict ownership requires every starting ancestor to belong to that end. A hearing compares the receiver’s own births, including their parentage, fresh counts and numbers of places.

[The seeded replay](replay.json) used seed **20261126**. In each of six census panels it sampled one entry linked after round 8 and one outside that category. The 12 pairs and all 156 of their placements matched the archive. The repeat sample covered 11 pairs and 15 actual edits, including four second edits, with strata for cap, missing target, lost far members and first hearing. It reproduced 234 archived baseline link readings and 278 changed-growth link readings round by round. Another 352 compatible archived predecessor readings of births, shortness and holder steps matched. Target, sharing count, first difference and common endpoint were checked wherever saved. The receiver’s own birth **count alone** gave every sampled first hearing. An initial comparison against regenerated author output was discarded. All 864 reported round comparisons use committed data.

From the repository root, run the commands serially. Each pilot uses the same reading on a smaller input. All referee growth runs used a single process, with per-process limits of at most 3 GiB and a combined allocation below 16 GB.
