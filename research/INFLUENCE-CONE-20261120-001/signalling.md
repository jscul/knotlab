# What a receiver can learn from a terminal birth

Fix two possible physical starts and one unchanged set of receiver knots. A binary reading distinguishes the starts if its two outputs are disjoint and the receiver has access to that reading in each preparation. The sender setting is an external choice of initial holdings for this comparison. The ordinary law supplies no later operation for selecting such a bit. Actual parental paths, ordinary adjacency and the reading of the receiver's own holdings are three separate objects. [Definitions and decoder fixed in advance](preregistration.md)

## A positive local event in both preparations

Let three live receiver knots each hold one loose distinction. Each of their three pairs is witnessed by a separate spent knot, with two shared distinctions between each witness and each of its two receiver knots. Thus the three are a candidate group, jointly held by no one knot. Add a second path of 2L ordinary holder steps between two of the spent witnesses, using double shared distinctions on every new meeting. The middle holder Z is initially spent, or holds one additional loose distinction. Take L≥2, so Z has no growing-end witness link with any receiver. It is a separate single live component when supplied. The physical start stays connected; the remote and quiet versions have exactly the same three original supports at the receiving end. Every original holder has at least two ordinary neighbors. Deleting one holder leaves the rest connected to a loose receiver through the cycle and the two attachments of the added path. Every parity star therefore projects to its one prescribed equation. For not-all-equal, prescribe any allowed row at one holder and set the two shared values opposite on every unaffected pair of positions belonging to a doubled meeting. Every other holder has another such meeting. This extends the prescribed row. Both preparations fund the sole local three-parent group, and the remote singleton, when present, has a funded unary.

The physical three-input birth **occurs in both arms at the first parent step**. Under parity its written size is three without the remote supply, and four with it. Its fresh count is respectively zero and one. The local reader can decode the entire chosen bit by counting the distinctions held by that actual child. A completed child is a positive observation on both arms; seeing a subsequent event or waiting for silence is unnecessary. There is no mutual descendant of that child’s receiver family and Z in the stocked preparation. The child survives as a written knot even if it has no loose distinction. At its formation the ordinary holder distance from Z to the designated original receiver is L+1, unbounded as L increases, while the witnessed growing-end separation is infinite. Lengths L=2,4,8 were rebuilt under both laws, with 9,13,21 old knots respectively. The earlier reached-stage and stretched comparisons provide a separate control. Under not-all-equal this child has four holdings with one fresh distinction in *both* preparations. A single draw of values on the old local inputs is weaker still: under parity the four even input assignments in the quiet arm are also possible in the remote arm. An odd input row certifies the latter, whereas an even row alone cannot distinguish. If a reader is instead given the complete projection of the **global** state set onto those marks, the list already has four versus eight rows at depth zero. That inference conditions on all knots' rules across the whole preparation. The unaltered three *physical supports* do not encode such a projection as an extra datum. Under not-all-equal all eight local input rows extend in both arms. No probabilities on assignments are needed.

**Exactly one remote condition for a funded separated part.** Each currently live witnessed component has an actual child if the plan funds. Child components from different parts have no shared parent; a route between old neighbors lifts to a route of children, so every old part has one next part on a continuing cut. Once at least two parts are live, the complete plan can never again have a sole birth. Their internal developments are then independent under either law. If the receiver part and its funding are kept fixed, the only structural data needed from disconnected parts to compute the receiver's whole continuing parity future is whether *any* other part is live. Thus in this setting it is possible to resolve presence versus absence of a supplier, while two already live and separately funded remote parts with different internal holdings cannot be distinguished by this channel. If the receiver on its own would never close, this bit is never exposed. A locally prospective sole birth of p inputs has fresh zero at parity closure versus max(3−p,1) with a remote live part. The confirmed three-parent example attains the one-bit distinction with both outputs physically observable. No general one-bit bound on all physically adjacent or witness-changing edits follows. **Other channels.** Under both laws, the global shortage rule cancels the complete plan when one original holder cannot pay. It can remove a remote receiver’s births in the first attempted step, at arbitrarily large witnessed separation on a connected end. A promised post-attempt observer can tell a canceled plan from a received child. A receiver that only records births has a positive observation on the funded arm and silence on the other. A clock-free two-symbol receiver cannot certify that silence by the mere passage of time. A funded, continuing ordinary plan already pays the next step's demand, so this uncontrolled shortage belongs to supplied starting cuts. It cannot be reintroduced by endogenous continuing growth. The funded local witness comparison offers a second positive/positive encoding in both laws: a corresponding child has three versus four fresh distinctions one step before the two families' first common descendant. This signal uses a change to the initial witness relation while both families grow. [Funding controls](guards.md) · [Funded matched contrast](physical_edits.md)

**Old holdings.** Growth appends knots. The written list on any matched old knot is fixed at every depth. On a funded next step every one of its previously loose distinctions acquires a second holder. Those positions never become loose again. Thus its loose/spent status after a funded first step cannot indicate a remote event happening several parental steps later. The identity and arity of its new co-holders or the written support of a newly formed descendant are richer readings. These distinctions matter when asking how an observer somewhere in the system learns anything.
