# How large is round nine of start-03?

**The 16 held-out estimates average \(4.70\times10^{12}\) junctions, with an empirical central 95% spread of 58.9-fold. A factor-ten estimate is unresolved.** What is proved is that at least **16,777,243** junctions are born in round nine. The independent sampling design also gives a **one-sided 99% lower confidence limit of 734,650,722,465**. The latter is statistical. All figures concern the one fixed start-03. The weighting in this experiment is over search paths, sampled parts or candidate pairs within that stage. ## The exact part

Round eight has 28,065 live junctions, 365 maximal blocks, 334,867,154 candidate pairs, and 993,164 parent incidences. The last figure exactly matches the preceding round's loose distinctions. Let \(C\) count maximal sets of mutually joinable junctions, and let \(P\) count the residual candidate pairs that belong to no larger legal group. Exactly 203 maximal sets are held by a block and there are no isolated junctions. The sole junction with just one parent has one candidate neighbor. That parent holds all its candidates, so its demand is one and its two loose distinctions give one offshoot. At all other junctions the fresh count equals the group demand. Hence the total is exactly **\(J_9=C-203+P+1\)**. The same accounting on round seven gives \(28,099-91+56+1=28,065\), the known answer. [Reconstructed stages](../../artifacts/NINTH-ROUND-SAMPLE-20261119-001/NINTH-ROUND-SAMPLE-20261119-001-E001_estimate/stage.json) · [Exact and sampled corrections](../../artifacts/NINTH-ROUND-SAMPLE-20261119-001/NINTH-ROUND-SAMPLE-20261119-001-E001_estimate/round_total.json)

The structural search exhibited 24 partner pairs in round eight. Every choice of one junction from each pair extends to a maximal mutually joinable set. Different choices stay distinct. Of the 203 held maximal sets, those covering a whole such choice account for only one distinct choice. Thus at least \(2^{24}-1\) groups with three or more parents survive. We also verified 27 distinct residual pairs, including the sole neighbor pair of the one-parent junction. Adding that junction's offshoot gives the proved bound. No assumption on how often a random search finds the 24 pairs enters the count. [Literal 48-junction witness](../../artifacts/NINTH-ROUND-SAMPLE-20261119-001/NINTH-ROUND-SAMPLE-20261119-001-E001_estimate/precision_rectangle.json) · [Independent verification of the sharper subtraction](../../experiments/NINTH-ROUND-SAMPLE-20261119-001/NINTH-ROUND-SAMPLE-20261119-001-E001_estimate/round_total.py)

As another check, 80,000 candidate pairs drawn uniformly with replacement gave zero residuals, with a two-sided exact binomial 99.9% interval of 0–31,815 residual pairs for this stage. The exact 27 pairs show why a sample with zero hits cannot set the correction to zero. At the scale of the held-out estimates this uncertainty changes only their last digits. ## Sampling and spread

After exploring ordinary random descent and population sampling, we fixed a fresh 16-run design and the rule for reporting its lower limit before looking at its results. Each run samples disjoint branches of the maximal-set search with known selection weights. Its total is unbiased under the independent randomization model. The whole runs, treated as independent observations, gave the following figures. [Fixed design](../../experiments/NINTH-ROUND-SAMPLE-20261119-001/NINTH-ROUND-SAMPLE-20261119-001-E001_estimate/heldout_design.md) · [Estimator and controls](../../experiments/NINTH-ROUND-SAMPLE-20261119-001/NINTH-ROUND-SAMPLE-20261119-001-E001_estimate/methods.md)

| Quantity | Held-out result |
|---|---:|
| Smallest and largest estimate | \(3.67\times10^{11}\) to \(2.90\times10^{13}\) |
| Mean and replicate standard deviation | \(4.70\times10^{12}\), \(7.62\times10^{12}\) |
| Largest divided by smallest | 78.9 |
| Empirical central 95% upper/lower ratio | 58.9 |
| Largest run's share of the total over all runs | 38.5% |

These are estimates of \(C\). The correction for \(J_9\) above has no effect at the displayed precision. The spread describes actual replicates and is not a two-sided confidence interval for the answer. For the lower limit, let \(g_1,\dots,g_4\) be the means of the four fixed batches of four. Under the weighting, \(E[g_i]=C\) and \(E[\prod g_i]=C^4\). Markov's inequality at 0.005 on each of the arithmetic mean and the product gives simultaneous one-sided 99% coverage for the larger lower bound. The product gives \(734,650,722,639.957\ldots\) for \(C\). Rounding up and applying the exact 203, 27 and 1 terms gives the stated limit on \(J_9\). The result makes no distributional or tail-shape assumption beyond nonnegativity and independent randomization of the specified estimator. [Markov calculation](../../artifacts/NINTH-ROUND-SAMPLE-20261119-001/NINTH-ROUND-SAMPLE-20261119-001-E001_estimate/estimate_heldout.json)

## Why the spread stayed wide

One verified legal maximal set contains 16,531 literal junctions. Under plain uniform-child descent its path to an exact suffix of the search has probability below \(10^{-351}\) even after merging junctions with identical closed candidate neighborhoods. In the literal ordering examined the reciprocal probability exceeds \(10^{371}\). If the raw total were at most \(10^{18}\), this single path alone would require more than \(10^{333}\) independent literal descents for a relative root-mean-square error of at most nine. This is a conditional variance bound for the stated proposal. [Exact path factors and suffix](../../artifacts/NINTH-ROUND-SAMPLE-20261119-001/NINTH-ROUND-SAMPLE-20261119-001-E001_estimate/path_risk.json)

An independent, fixed-threshold audit of 100,000 more paths also finds that the very largest weights carry almost the whole mean under one tested proposal. A second estimator instead inserted junctions successively, branching only once or twice at each insertion. Its 256-particle calibration at round seven had mean 28,386 against the exact 28,099. At round eight its 12 degree-ordered replicates spanned a central factor of 432. Few ancestral groups survived resampling. Neither the positive calibration nor a narrower batch under one ordering establishes the missing upper limit.
