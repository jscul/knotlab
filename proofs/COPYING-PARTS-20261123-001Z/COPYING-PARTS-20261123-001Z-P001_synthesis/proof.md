contributes no further proof or experiment. All sources are referee pending.

Taken together ([decision](../../../research/COPYING-PARTS-20261123-001/decision.md)): under rations and treatments added by
hand a part of two knots becomes two of the same every two rounds, at a rate set by what its short join does; its knots pass
the knot test; it is common among small starts under those laws; and its copies never meet again. Nothing here is a property
of ordinary growth.
