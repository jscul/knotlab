Cold review, about 350 words: flaws, anything settled by construction presented as a test, overclaims.

Setting: nested NAE families from a macro language. A ray definition S has c >= 2 SELF calls, and the copies form a tree. L_any(s) is the least bond (minimal non-shattered set) containing a port of main and an own distinction of ANY copy at depth s. Members may be anywhere in the infinite usual-limit pattern, side subtrees included. Population: 66 sampled families that grow along every descending path when members are restricted to the path (L_path has lambda > 0 on complete graphs; EVERY-RAY-20261113-001). Other studies read members anywhere only for the fixed first-ray target: BRANCH-DEPTH, TRUE-BOND, TRUE-RATE. TRUE-RATE's exact side-summary table closed in only 24 of 226 families.

Plan:
(0) Existence is settled: L_any <= L_path, which is finite at all large s.
(1) Growth certificate, the forgetting test, for every target. R_i is the relation between a copy's ports and its child's ports through SELF call i. The copy's own distinctions and every other call's subtree (as the limit relation G) are projected out. Let S_r be all products of r factors R_{i1}...R_{ir}. If for some r <= 12 every product in S_r is rectangular (the product of its projections), then by BRANCH-DEPTH's gap argument any bond to any depth-s copy needs a member in every r-level stretch of its target's path, side subtrees included. Hence L_any >= 1 + ceil(s/r). Rectangularity persists in longer products (A.(XxY).B is rectangular).
(2) Boundedness certificate: BRANCH-DEPTH's exact fixed-size saturation (k members anywhere; finite marked port tables closed under placements) generalized to a choice of call at each level. Some eventually periodic path then has a bond of at most k members at every depth, for k = 3, 4, 5.
(3) Otherwise unresolved. Exact small-depth values by a brute force over the unfolded tree (s <= 3), descriptive only.

Predictions (blind): P1, the forgetting certificate holds in at least a third of the 66. P2, at least one of the 66 is bounded with side support. Shares carry Wilson intervals, extrapolated to the stratum, about 1,500 to 2,100 such growers among 6,925 first-ray growers.

Questions. Is the gap argument valid for a FREE target with members anywhere? Can members in subtrees branching above a stretch reconnect below it? Is "every length-r product rectangular" the right condition, or is it sufficient only? What does the fixed-size test need in order to stay finite with a choice per level?
