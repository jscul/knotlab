# Protected Foundational Thesis

Status: canonical statement of the research thesis. These commitments define
the question that was always intended; irreducible jointness is intrinsic to
the word `knot`, not an optional strengthening added after a result. The
commitments are not all proved theorems. Evidence may support or challenge
them, but a task may not silently replace them with a different question.

## Transcendental logical base

Base logic is transcendental in the project-specific sense: logical identities
and logical laws do not presuppose space, position, direction, distance,
neighborhood, or relative placement.

Before emergence, logical identities may be themselves, but they do not yet
exist relative to one another in an intrinsic spatial sense.

## Protected modal-plurality interpretation

Status: protected interpretation, not theorem. At the interpretive level, the
project treats every internally coherent admissible base as co-real. An
admissible base declares its primitives, native rules, semantics, admitted
maps, and at least one nontrivial coherence witness relative to those
semantics. Co-reality does not make every arbitrary or inconsistent
combination admissible.

No global selector privileges one admissible base or world branch as the
uniquely real one. This does not forbid typed laws, history selection,
probability, or other measures inside a branch. Such structures are
branch-internal and require their own formation and evidence; they do not
induce a measure over, or a selector for, the modal totality.

Co-reality extends from each admissible base to every world branch coherently
admitted by it. Branch-relative actuality and branch-local selectors
may distinguish states, histories, or continuations inside one declared
branch; they do not revoke the co-reality of other coherent branches or become
a global selector.

Co-reality is non-temporal. It is not physical simultaneity. Different
admissible bases need not share space, time, contact, laws, dimensions, or a
containing world, and no contact across admissible bases may be inferred. The modal totality is an interpretive
schema, not a formed native set, carrier, world, or infinity theorem.
The schema is open-ended rather than restricted to a fixed finite roster.
That interpretive open-endedness is not a theorem that infinitely many
pairwise inequivalent admissible bases have been formed.

Our observed branch is postulated to be a member of this plurality, but no
current artifact identifies its formal admissible base. Observations restrict our
branch-relative self-location among empirically compatible branches; they
neither create a branch nor globally select one. A model-existence or
nonselection theorem is compatible with this interpretation but does not
prove it, and an obstruction to one native infinite-world carrier does not
refute it.

The interpretation changes neither the contact-first formation order nor its
evidence gates. First emergence remains native logical-knot formation.
Projection, quotienting, observation, and self-location are downstream
representational or interpretive operations and do not create emergence.

## Emergence, atomic topology, and distance

First emergence is a logical knot:

```text
two distinct native recurrences A and B
one contact object k shared by identity
k is one irreducibly joint relation of A, B, and k
k participates essentially in both recurrence laws
removing either recurrence destroys the contact qua contact
the complete contact persists under every admitted map.
```

The genuine touch is the smallest relative existence:

```text
A is A
B is B
A != B
k relates A and B without identifying them.
```

That one touch is simultaneously the first atomic logical topology and
minimal contact-distance. From within `A`'s native recurrence context, `B`
now exists relative to `A` through `k`; from within `B`'s context, `A` exists
relative to `B` through the same contact. This loop-relative placement is
intrinsic data, not an observer's viewpoint or an imported coordinate.

The contact is one indivisible contact unit: the smallest available
qualitative measure of relative existence. It has no independently supplied
magnitude, direction, coordinate, path length, or metric axiom. Composed
logical topology and extended/path/metric distance are later constructions
from multiple contacts.

Identity-sharing alone is insufficient. Two decomposable attachments `A-k`
and `B-k` make `k` a common hub, not the one touch between `A` and `B`. A
genuine touch is relationally joint: it cannot be factored into independent
one-loop relations while preserving contact, and removing either loop removes
the contact role itself.

One recurrence alone forms no topology because it supplies no relative
existence. One complete logical knot does form the first atomic logical
topology. A contact network is not required for that first topology; it is
required for composed topology, relational profiles involving further loops,
paths, separation, and possible metric structure.

An explanatory mnemonic is that a native recurrence is an atom of persistence
or self-continuation, whereas a complete logical knot is an atom of intrinsic
relation. `Self-continuation` does not mean that a loop computationally solves
or eliminates itself. Each recurrence is witnessed independently and does not
owe its recurrence identity to the other. What depends jointly on both
recurrence roles is the contact qua contact: erasing either role destroys that
relation even when the other recurrence survives as a recurrence. Emergence
and atomic topology are therefore dependency-like only in the qualified sense
of irreducible, identity-shared, two-sided-essential, persistent relational
dependence. Ordinary causal, syntactic, graph, dataflow, function-call, or
common-hub dependency supplies no topology.

## Canonical knot test

Adopted on 2026-10-19 by the repository owner. The test came from
`research/OPERATIONAL-ARITY-BOUNDARY-001/canonical_correction_request.md`.
The owner settled erasure as forgetting the same day
(`research/KNOT-FOUNDATION-20261019-001/canonical_correction_request.md`).
The working definition and its intuition are in `docs/KNOT_INTUITION.md`.

The semantics is relational. A system of constraints defines the joint states
it allows, and witness variables are quantified away. No executor, scheduler
or checker may supply an input: a constraint that should hold only while a
loop runs carries that loop's presence as a literal. A verdict depends only
on the allowed states, never on how the constraints are written.

A loop is a logical structure that can be entwined with another. It
contributes one open distinction: a variable, or a named pattern such as its
parity, that the structure leaves free. The contact is one more variable `c`.
Erasing a loop forgets it, so its distinction is no longer constrained by
anything. A relation over the distinctions of loops L1, …, Ln (n ≥ 2) and `c`
is a knot when:

- **K1 binding:** the relation is constrained as a whole;
- **K2 loss on erasure:** forgetting any one variable, a loop or the contact,
  leaves the others unconstrained;
- **K3 coexistence:** some joint state is allowed (K2 implies it).

In short, a knot is constrained as a whole and unconstrained in every part.
Forgetting the contact must also free the loops, so loops tied directly to
each other do not count.

K1–K3 decides the contact conditions of the emergence definition above:
- **Shared identity:** the contact is one variable.
- **Two-sided essentiality:** fixing the contact changes how the loops'
  distinctions relate, and every variable of a knot does this for the other
  two.
- **Irreducible jointness:** every shadow on fewer variables is
  unconstrained, so a knot cannot factor into `A-k` and `B-k` attachments.
- **Loop-erasure contact loss:** this is K2.
- **Persistence:** every map that preserves the allowed states preserves the
  verdict.

Which logical structures count as native loops is interpretive. K1–K3
applies to their open distinctions. A checker that tests clause syntax, or
that lets an executor supply an input, does not decide K1–K3. State every
knot or contact claim as a relation and check it with
`PYTHONPATH=tools python3 -B -m knotlab check`.

**Source condition for infinite systems.** Adopted on 2026-10-20 by the
repository owner; it can be reopened
(`research/HOLES-AT-INFINITY-20261020-001/canonical_correction_request.md`).
In an infinite system an open distinction may be supplied by a finite loop or
only by the system's ends (`HOLES-AT-INFINITY-20261020-001`). A knot is
**native** at a solution when each of its variables is flipped at that
solution by some change of finitely many of the system's variables.
- **Native knots carry emergence.** Under this reading, emergence in an
  infinite system is a native knot. It then depends on the law and on the
  solution (`FROZEN-WORLDS-20261020-001`).
- **Finite systems are unaffected.** In a finite system every change is
  finite, so every knot is native.
- **Both verdicts are recorded.** Every knot claim about an infinite system
  states its K1–K3 verdict on the allowed states and the source of each
  distinction: loop-supplied, end-supplied or fixed. Reopening the decision
  therefore needs no new derivation.

An earlier reading, adopted and replaced on 2026-10-19, treated a loop's
variable as its presence and erasure as setting it to absent. That reading
keeps only the atom `¬A ∨ ¬B ∨ c` (`KNOT-FOUNDATION-20261019-001A-P001`).
Every knot of that reading remains a knot under forgetting.

## Protected CNF arity thesis

The project thesis is:

1. A strict 3-CNF substrate is the minimum candidate language capable of
   natively realizing the irreducible logical-knot mechanism.
2. A strict 2-CNF substrate cannot natively realize that complete mechanism
   under the same contact-first semantics.
3. Both languages may serialize or externally encode any fixed finite witness;
   serialization is not formation.

`2-SAT` and `3-SAT` name decision problems. Formal work must state the native
strict 2-CNF or strict 3-CNF substrate and operations being compared.

Under the canonical knot test, items 1 and 2 are theorems about definability:

- **Census.** For two loops and a contact, exactly 34 relations pass K1–K3.
  They come in five classes, from a single three-literal clause (the atom,
  which hides the least) to parity (which hides one full bit, the most any
  three-variable pattern can).
- **No 2-CNF knot.** No 2-CNF with any number of witnesses defines a knot.
  Relations definable that way are closed under Boolean majority and
  determined by their two-variable shadows (Schaefer 1978; Baker and Pixley
  1975), while a knot shows nothing in any smaller shadow.
- **Width three suffices.** Clauses of three literals, with witnesses that
  name intermediate results, define every knot. Without witnesses, a knot on
  m variables needs clauses of width m.

The threshold concerns how many places a relation has, not how many inputs an
operation takes: an operation with two inputs is a three-place relation, as
the commutator result of `OPERATIONAL-ARITY-BOUNDARY-001` shows. Primitive
operation arity, term-essential arity and strict clause arity are different
coordinates, and none may stand in for another without a proved reflection
theorem. Whether the loops themselves can be formed natively is outside these
theorems and open, as stated above.

The intended 3-CNF mechanism is not “three literals therefore emergence.”
What decides is the relation, not the clause syntax. A clause whose three
variables are open distinctions passes K1–K3, but three literals over a
repeated variable, or over variables fixed elsewhere, do not. A parity
relation passes only when its inputs are open. Strict 2-CNF is not a proposed
source of emergence anywhere in this project. A pair of binary clauses sharing
`k` is a common hub: with one loop erased, the other loop and `k` stay tied,
which fails K2. A purported native 2-CNF witness is admissible only as a
counterexample audit under K1–K3. If valid, it challenges the thesis rather
than becoming a second emergence mechanism.

## Current evidence alignment

Two proofs replace Proof 0009 as the evidence for the thesis, each with a
Lean 4 kernel:
- `KNOT-FOUNDATION-20261019-001E-P001` proves the census, the 2-CNF
  obstruction, the width facts and the parity chain under the forgetting
  reading.
- `KNOT-FOUNDATION-20261019-001A-P001` proves the same boundary under the
  earlier absence reading, together with the diagnoses of Proof 0009 and of
  the earlier Lean kernel below.

Proof 0009 (`CONTACT-001I`) reached the same verdicts, ternary present and
2-CNF absent, and its majority argument is sound. Two features of its
semantics are not inherited:
- **Executor gating.** Its clause-transition executor checks a loop's clauses
  only while that loop runs, which supplies the loop's presence as an input.
  Under that rule, cross-wired 2-clause guards (`¬B ∨ c` for A, `¬A ∨ c` for
  B) behave like a knot. Read relationally they are a common hub. Writing the
  gating as a literal turns each guard into the three-literal clause.
- **Syntactic jointness.** Its jointness test requires one clause naming both
  loops and the contact.

The earlier Lean kernel (`CONTACT-FORMAL-KERNEL-001A`) states loop erasure as
"the relation ignores the contact once a loop is erased". That condition
accepts the 2-CNF relation `(A ↔ B) ∧ (A → c)`. That relation fails K2 under
either reading, because it ties A and B directly.

By the guarded identity above, a complete knot is the first atomic logical
topology; by itself it forms no composed contact network, path, separation,
or metric. Neither result implies a complexity separation or `P != NP`.

## Encoding firewall

The following may describe, preserve, or compute information about a knot, but
they do not thereby originate one:

- forced bits or serialized records;
- solution-preserving reductions;
- auxiliary or Tseitin variables;
- implication or incidence graphs;
- equal labels, values, or synchronized copies;
- quotients and projections;
- XOR or parity outputs computed from values fixed elsewhere;
- external decoders, observers, or simulator control flow.

Every such construction must be classified as native formation, faithful
external presentation, solution-only encoding, or interpretation.

## Contradiction protocol

A proof may challenge this thesis. When it does:

1. state the exact native semantics and failed commitment;
2. give the positive counterexample or obstruction;
3. mark the protected thesis `challenged`;
4. stop dependent promotions; and
5. request conceptual review before rewriting canonical meaning.

Bare static semantics for both CNF languages is a required control, not a
terminal answer to the native arity thesis.
