## What the full reader receives

The recognizer's only input is the unlabelled junction–distinction incidence, given as one support per junction with arbitrary names for the distinctions. A distinction held once identifies a loose supply. In a reached, nonclosing complete round the loose holders are exactly the most recent children. Erasing those children and retaining the other literal supports gives the sole possible predecessor. The reader verifies the observed transition using every parent set, spent loose distinction, fresh count, and implication test. It repeats that inverse until the first rejected predecessor, then checks the recovered source's canonical supports. This identifies the actual source here. The reverse test otherwise identifies the earliest compatible source under this complete-round law.

There is a second check on the recovered levels for these all-live starts. Each junction in its birth layer has a child in the immediately following layer whenever that layer is present. Iterating along descendants gives a chain to a loose holder whose length equals the age difference. Every ordinary step through a shared distinction changes the level by at most one, so a path to a loose holder has at least that length. The maximum of these minimum lengths is the observed stage's age. The evaluator verifies every age and named parent group against the original stages. It also independently permutes the holders, distinctions, and order within the supports at ages 0, 2, and the last retained age of each case. All 30 comparisons recover the same transported carriers, supplies, and parent sets. The recovered source's simple cyclic parts are found in its holder relation. Their leftovers are the loose distinctions in excess of what the required joins consume. The separate ring model retains those leftovers and supplies each ring's own join demand. The background comes from the unique longest path in the forest remaining after the ring-exclusive source junctions are removed. For the cut control C1 this method retains the 17-junction path and leaves the two cut branches outside it. Every recovered ring holder is exhausted in its final observed frame. This locates a carrier away from the current loose holders using the complete incidence. Later pure children supply an independent count of the same routine at the measured sizes. ## What remains after erasing older holders

The second reader sees only the loose holders and their immediately incident older junctions, with the supports retained and both name sorts anonymous. It tests each bipartite orientation against the exact fresh-count law. At ages two and later it admits the actual current side in all 181 components from 140 cuts. Both roles remain possible on the bare R0 cuts. The direct rule gives a circulation certificate there and finds branching residual returns in T4 and T8 at ages 2–5. Those returns certify self-feeding in the current end under the lawful two-layer interpretation. They leave the individual ring contribution unresolved. [Residual-return criterion](../HEAVY-GROWTH-20261119-001/pure.md)

The untyped isometry audit exhausts the finite panel of 150 positive-age cuts. A holder's loose count and the multiplicity of every shared distinction between holders suffice to encode the full two-typed incidence. Each verified holder map is checked by lifting it to a map of all retained distinction identities.

- **71 cuts.** Stable incidence colors already preserve each ring cohort as judged by the evaluator, with no current-side marker.
- **5 T8 cuts.** Individualizing one member of the ambiguous two-holder color cell forces its stabilizer to preserve both cohorts. Its only other possible image gives a verified full incidence isometry that swaps the two copies. Thus the unordered family is fixed under every isometry.
- **43 cuts.** There is no ring cohort.
- **31 R0 cuts.** A verified isometry swaps the two stages and moves the bare ring's current cohort to its predecessors. These cuts are all isomorphic across ages and have no intrinsic eddy.

Of 11,175 pairs of cuts, 10,709 differ in holder or distinction count. The remaining 466 are matched on both counts. The sole cross-case pair, T4 and T8 at age one, differs by an exact incidence-color invariant. The other 465 pairs are the identified R0 cuts across ages. All within-cut checks were decided. Every exact symmetry preserves unordered eddy-cohort membership, and the cases have distinct incidence across cases in this finite panel. A finite catalogue compiled after scoring could recover those answers by exact incidence matching. Such a catalogue would carry the evaluator's outcomes and leaves recognition on unseen starts open.
