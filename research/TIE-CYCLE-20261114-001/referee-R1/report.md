## 1. Theorem I (point 1)

**Why it holds.** Rectangular relations, with the empty one, form a two-sided ideal I of the product semigroup S. By Ramsey (or
Simon factorisation), every long word has a contiguous idempotent factor, which the ideal absorbs. Conversely, powers of a word whose
product is a non-rectangular idempotent never become rectangular.
- **It is classical.** For nonempty I, S/I is nilpotent iff every idempotent lies in I.
- **The edge cases.** Note them: I = S, and I empty.
- **"0 of 12,759 violations".** This is a consequence of the theorem and a code check, not a prediction.
  - **What Codex compared.** The exact graph decision against independently evaluated non-rectangular idempotent witnesses, for all
    6,931 non-forgetting families, with no mismatch.
- **The verdict.** **Narrowing** (the label of H3).

## 3. Orders and labels (point 2; Codex)

**The method.** For each non-rectangular idempotent E of the residual families (no tie cycle, not forgetting), Codex formed its core
{x : xEx}, the mutual-reachability classes and their one-way comparabilities.
- **Conserved quantities.** It tested for a nonconstant conserved endpoint quantity f(start) = g(end), exhaustively over Boolean
  functions.
- **The scale.** All 100 residual semigroups and 159 idempotents were regenerated by a second set-based implementation.

| "Only order" means every residual idempotent… | Training | Held out |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| has comparable core classes (the published test) | 52/55 | 44/45 |
| …and conserves no endpoint quantity | 52/55 | **43/45** |
| …and every endpoint has a unique core class | 51/55 | 43/45 |

**The parity.** Held-out family `4f5934b2e118` has a single idempotent E = 0x0058a01008051a00, on word [1,1]. It has one-way
comparabilities and also conserves start[0] ⊕ start[2] = end[0] ⊕ end[2]. It is the note's "one tie-free parity", counted inside
the 44 as order. So "an order ... nothing is carried" holds in 43, not 44.

**Core labels.** They are not endpoint labels. Ten idempotents have incomparable core classes and no conserved endpoint quantity.
For example, training `9ef44064b996` is labelled "tie" by its core, but its rows at 000 and 111 reach every core state.

**What the classification is.** It is a data check, not a theorem. Explicit complement-closed relations refute the exhaustive
dichotomy in general.

**The verdict.** **Block** (the prose "the only other way is an order; nothing is carried" and the exhaustive dichotomy). H4 passes
as frozen under either reading.
