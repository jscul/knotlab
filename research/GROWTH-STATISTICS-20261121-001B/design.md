# Comparing two ways to find different growth

Can remembering different early behaviors and editing their starts produce more reproducible kinds of later growth per unit of work? An independent search can win if the edits keep returning to kinds its parent already showed. A tie can also be informative. The comparison measures two search procedures under a declared weighting. Its result concerns the kinds distinguished by this reading and the two budgets.

## What grows

Each initial distinction has one or two holders. A declared meeting gives its two holders one shared distinction. All other pairs have disjoint holdings. In a complete ordinary round, the live holders propose the maximal jointly unwitnessed groups of at least three whose every pair has a common witness, and the remaining witnessed pairs. Each group requires one different loose distinction from each member. A shortage at any one holder stalls the whole round. Each remaining loose distinction generates its own one-parent birth. The prospective children of each old holder give the witnesses for the next round. If a birth has $p$ parents and owes $D$ uses in that lookahead, its ordinary fresh supply is $\max(D,3-p,1)$, with the inherited zero-supply exception for the sole parity birth on a connected start. A completed round with no live holder left closes. The assigned round orders finite formations for this reading. The compact complete-round operator and the older engine that writes all physical holdings implement this same law. Choose the number of holders uniformly from four, five and six. Generate a uniform labelled tree by drawing its Prüfer word uniformly. Give it one additional previously absent shared pair with probability one half, uniformly over its missing pairs. At each holder of degree $g$, give it $\max(0,3-g)$ loose distinctions and one further distinction with independent probability one half. The two laws use this same generator. Another Prüfer word or selection of the added meeting can yield the same physical arrangement. Those possibilities contribute to its sampling weight. They never supply two witnesses for the same discovery. The predecessor used a quarter-probability spare and reported substantial initial stalls. Check the canonical knot relation for each source arity three through six. This catches a prescribed rule whose actual projection has already lost words, including at a holder with no loose distinctions. Growth and costs are recorded separately for the two laws. ## What the searches can see

Canonically encode a founder up to renaming holders and distinctions. Sort holders by degree and loose count, then minimize the encoded pair incidences over all orders within each equal class. SHA-256 of that code with the frozen prefix, reduced modulo five, gives development on residue zero, search on residues one through three, and a separate fresh reserve on residue four. A holdout proposal incurs its generation, canonicalization and rejection cost. It supplies no growth reading. Three in-family founders deliberately used to read public reference trajectories are explicitly withheld from search and the fresh reserve. Distinct seeds alone would let a renamed development founder return in the test. [Source encoding and weighting](../../experiments/GROWTH-STATISTICS-20261121-001B/GROWTH-STATISTICS-20261121-001B-E001_search/source.py)

The independent arm draws new starts with replacement. The adaptive arm keeps representatives of distinct early readings. It can draw a fresh independent start or edit one representative. Its single-token edits change a Prüfer letter, the optional extra shared pair, or one spare bit. A stored extra pair that has become a tree pair after a Prüfer edit makes the proposal invalid. The policy does not get a free replacement. For a retained founder the key is the sign of the change between the first two birth counts, the number of different parent bands seen in these rounds, whether a three-or-more-parent birth appeared, and the status known by the second round. The bands are one, two and three or more parents. Keep the first founder in each occupied key. After the shared first proposal, restart independently with probability one quarter when the archive is occupied. Otherwise choose an occupied key uniformly and choose an edit class uniformly. An empty archive uses an independent draw. A repeated canonical start within an arm is charged for its proposal and identification and receives no second growth evaluation. Both searches can measure the same things. The archive may use only the first two rounds and the starting holdings. The scorer alone sees the later observations, the run's qualifying pairs and the shared reference dictionary. The archive resets for every arm, law and replicate.

## What earns a discovery

Let $b_t$ count the children in completed ordinary round $t$. For each still-growing history observed through the fifth complete round, record the signs of $b_3-b_2$, $b_4-b_3$ and $b_5-b_4$. Append two flags describing rounds three through five together. One asks whether any birth had one parent. The other asks whether any had at least three. This description accepts every combination of those five entries. It discards labels, magnitudes and the size of the start. A different exact count with the same five entries earns the same score.

The common reference consists of 128 qualified, independently sampled development founders, weighted with replacement, plus registered public control traces with sufficient parent data. Freeze the reference founders, code and dictionary before the search. A qualifying kind must be absent from this dictionary and must occur on two different canonical founders **within the same search run**. Verify both by reconstructing all five rounds with the independent engine on the literal holdings. A known whole-presentation closure or its not-all-equal continuation, a resource cutoff, a stall and a partial trajectory each have their own accounting. None earns a new kind on that ground. This definition can miss genuine late behavior, and an entry missing from a small dictionary can already be known in the literature. Every source here with four or five founders also belongs to the earlier complete parity census of connected arrangements of at most five knots with three to five places each. An example at those sizes can reveal a missed joint description or a difference at the specified early reading while using previously covered founders. Report the raw trajectories and their relation to prior examples. [Earlier complete census](../TOUCH-CENSUS-20261118-001/touch_census.md)

The primary score is the number of distinct qualifying, independently reproduced kinds in a run. Record a zero for every completed run that earns none. Also report any one type seen once, any prior-dictionary hit, continuations and all censoring and exit counts. For a reported surprise choose the smallest verified pair actually observed. Compare pairs first by the larger founder count, then their total founder count, the larger distinction count, their total distinction count, and finally their sorted canonical codes. Report the full two physical starts, enough parents and fresh counts to replay the observation, and the encounter's law. This ordering establishes a minimum in the observed qualifying records.

## What looked alike before the third round

Separately, two distinct founders look alike at the supplied early reading when their founder count, $b_1$, and $b_2$ agree. They give a counterexample to that reading's determination of the third count when their observed $b_3$ differ. A shared censor or a zero inserted after a stall cannot qualify. The additional reading $L_2$ is the total number of distinctions held by exactly one knot after the second completed round. The prospective sign is fixed. Within a matched pair, the founder with more such distinctions is predicted to have more third-round births. Equal $L_2$ abstains. Equal $b_3$ leaves no larger count to predict. The independent engine replays the reported search example whether the feature succeeds on the fresh reserve or fails. For a funded third round, the number of births equals $L_2$ less the sum of $p-1$ over its births with $p$ parents. Each such birth spends $p$ loose distinctions to produce one child. The feature therefore omits exactly how many births were saved by joining parents together. A reversed prediction can be checked by counting those savings in the two raw third-round rosters.

The fresh reserve is small enough for exact enumeration. There are 864 possible colored meeting patterns before lawfulness and the hash partition. At four holders there are 18 tree patterns and 18 patterns with an extra meeting. At five the counts are 54 and 94. At six they are 189 and 491. These counts also follow without producing a growth reading by averaging the number of spare assignments fixed by each symmetry of each unlabelled meeting pattern. The exact census grows only the reserved lawful identities, one per physical pattern and law. It includes every eligible unordered pair in each matched stratum. Give the numbers of all starts, lawful starts, stages observed at each needed cut, matched pairs, equal third counts, tied feature readings, concordances and reversals. Cross-tabulate both ties so a single pair is never silently dropped twice.

For the weighted test, sum the original labelled generator probabilities over all encodings of each fresh founder, thereby retaining sampling weights even though each founder is grown only once. There are $n^{n-2}$ labelled starting trees and $M=(n-1)(n-2)/2$ possible added meetings on each. A pattern with an added meeting and one cycle of length $c$ can be obtained from exactly $c$ starting trees. Weight a pair of distinct founders by the product of their masses conditional on the matched population. Compare the directional rule with a fair uninformed ordering, which has half expected accuracy on unequal third counts. Value ties in the feature make the rule abstain. Give the exact accuracy among predictions, coverage of eligible differences, and a half-credit-for-abstention sensitivity. Also read deterministically paired disjoint founders within the strata. Pairs in the complete census reuse founders. On this finite reserve the weighted result is exact, and the case counts do not supply independent binomial observations. ## Costs, uncertainty and stopping

Run 48 fresh paired repeats for each law. Each arm receives 80 milliseconds of process CPU for proposals, founder qualification, all measured growth, reading, archive choice, search bookkeeping and discovery candidate identification. It receives a separate 50 milliseconds for choosing and verifying its witnesses on literal holdings. Unused verification time is recorded. Both arms face the same 16,384-proposal safety ceiling, at most 192 births per complete round, 1,024 provisional lookahead groups, and 32,768 generated common-witness pairs. Hitting a ceiling marks the relevant unobserved result. Report any boundary work in progress, actual search and verification CPU, wall time, attempted and finished proposals, funded starts, five-round observations and the independent replay count. Warmup, frozen reference construction, the fresh census, runner startup and output are common expenses measured separately. The matched ceilings across the 192 arms allocate 24.96 CPU-seconds. At the draft ceiling of 2,048 proposals, 9 of 20 adaptive development arms stopped at that ceiling and no independent arm did. This raised a concern that unfinished CPU allowances could drive the result, so the safety ceiling was raised on both arms before the production comparison. All 40 arms in the repeated development check then reached the CPU boundary. The largest finished 2,558 proposals. The developmental timing on 24 admitted starts of each size found agreement in all 70 law/start histories that completed the five-round comparison.

For each law compare the paired number of verified kinds at the frozen CPU budgets. Recompute the contrast at the lesser completed proposal count of each pair. Each truncated prefix receives a fresh 50 milliseconds of verification CPU for this diagnostic. Its separate expense measures the cost of testing whether more proposals paid for the difference. Present the two score columns, all adaptive wins, ties and independent wins, and the probability of any reproduced type per strategy with exact 95% binomial intervals across repeat units. Use a two-sided exact sign test conditional on non-ties and Holm's adjustment across the two laws. Estimate mean paired score difference by resampling whole pairs 10,000 times under a fixed seed. With 48 non-ties, the conservative per-law 0.025 sign test requires 33 wins in one direction and has 87.7% power if that arm wins three quarters of non-ties. With 24 non-ties it requires 18 and has 60.7% power. Forty-eight observed ties give a two-sided 95% upper limit of about 7.4% on a fresh run finding any difference at this budget. An all-zero score also requires reporting the number of fully observed and replayable starts and the coverage of the reference dictionary.

Stop after those 96 paired runs, their frozen checks and the fresh census. A different early reading, longer horizon, higher cap or additional predictor requires a new comparison. The search can return a collision, suggest a feature that fails on the reserve, favor either method, or find no reproducible surprise. The actual funded holdings and counts come from the ordinary law on a particular start. The choice of starts, their weighting, the signs and parent bands read at five rounds, the dictionary, the archive edits, the available CPU and the choice to compare forecasts by sign come from this experiment.
