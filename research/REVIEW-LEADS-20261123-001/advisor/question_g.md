Cold design review before one small follow-up study is frozen. Give (1) the strongest objection, (2) one concrete change, (3) anything the predictions get wrong by plain reasoning. At most 250 words. Do not praise.

Setting. A structure is a set of two-valued distinctions and rules ("knots") each holding 3 or more of them; a distinction has one holder (loose) or two (shared). Ordinary growth proceeds in parent steps: groups of 1 to 3 knots that are near each other each pay one loose distinction to a newborn knot, which also gets fresh loose distinctions; nothing is ever removed. A "source" is a start from which, for ever, new families of knots are released: after its release every member of a family has all its parents inside that family, and the source goes on. Reading T: a stage passes when the bipartite graph (knots and distinctions; an edge when the knot holds the distinction) is planar. Failing is monotone in the step.

Just measured: of 280 funded random starts per law (labelled trees on 4 to 8 knots with 0 to 3 extra meetings, sparse spare supply), 247 pass T at all 12 steps; an adjacency-blind rewiring with the same births fails within a few steps. All seven structures on record that keep producing (five sources and clocks, three explosive starts) fail T at step 1 or 2. In that random population an earlier study found no source at all.

Follow-up question: does being a source require failing T?

Design. Populations with recorded verdicts from an earlier study: (Q1) the 53 single edits of a built seven-knot source (one loose distinction or one meeting more or less): 16 proved to be sources, 33 proved not, 4 undecided; (Q2) 192 random starts on 9 to 12 knots with two or three extra meetings and a supply sized to fund the first joins: 5 proved sources, the rest not certified (some hit a size limit). For every start: first failing step within 12 steps or 5,000 knots, and growth kind (stalled, bounded, growing). No verdict is recomputed.
Predictions: P1 every one of the 21 proved sources fails T by step 3 (a passing proved source would be a counterexample and the main finding). P2 some start proved not to be a source fails T (failing is not sufficient). P3 report how many funded starts pass T at every stage; if fewer than 10, declare that "passing implies not a source" was not tested with any power. If P1 holds with power, the thread then tries to prove it, on the idea that a core which keeps growing around the families it has released must be joined across them.
