# Two shared distinctions and one unread name

**Theorem.** Two three-place knots share two readable distinctions. A reader holds two places of the first knot and a separate reader holds the remaining place. Put both shared names at the first reader, or divide them between the readers. In both placements the largest probability of returning the two complete signed projections on independent data from a single declared source is **zero**. This holds for each ordered pair of even parity and not-all-equal and for both the positive equal-amplitude source and its fully diagonal preparation. It holds for every finite adaptive local instrument satisfying the reached-word requirement in §1. Each physical knot has its full canonical projection in all eight holdings.

There is an operational difference between the two not-all-equal placements. With both shared names together the obstruction holds under arbitrary separated one-copy operations. With the names divided, allowing a local offer to rely on cancellation specific to the prepared state gives a protocol of probability **$1/45$**. Calibrating its last filter raises its probability to **$(7-\sqrt{13})/90$**. Two readable admission flags suffice. The second number is a lower bound for that changed definition. The location of the forbidden step and both full refusal accounts appear in §6.
