## What stands

> **Correction, logged in the corrections log.** The first version of this note said that some changes at the start
> of a growth of dimension two "weaken as the owner's lamp does". A control run before the referee shows that the
> readings without names find no difference at all between two unrelated starts of dimension two, so that statement
> is narrowed below to what those readings can see. The counts stand.

1. **Read locally, a growth of dimension two forgets its start.** In the readings without names (each birth by its
   kind, parents, fresh distinctions and places, and its parents'), two touching knots and three knots in a row,
   compared with each other, differ in no birth from round 4 to round 32 ([the control](control.json)). A change at
   the start then shows only as a fixed difference in the round's count: for the marks and moves on two touching knots
   (4 of 4) and the middle mark and two end-to-end moves on three knots in a row (3 of 9), a fixed 4, 5 or 1 births a
   round against a round that grows by two in each growth, so its share falls as one over the round's count, to round
   32. That is the design's verdict "weakens" by its letter; it says the local readings are blind to all but a count,
   the owner's "logically small" in the growth's own terms, and not that a change spreads more thinly. Parity gives
   the same in all 161 jobs run under both knot laws.
2. **By descent, a change re-makes one whole chain and nothing else.** A mark at one of two touching knots re-makes
   every birth of the round's births nearer the changed knot (62 of 62 at round 32) and none of those one step
   further (0 of 66); at the middle knot of three in a row, 63 of 65 and 0 of 68. Nothing in it weakens with the
   rounds: the change reaches a fixed part, about one half, of every round.
3. **Every other change holds or takes over.** On the bare rings (dimension one) and the golden-ratio eddy a change
   alters the kind of growth and takes over; made marks in dimension two add a part that keeps growing, and hold
   (shares 0.29 to 0.54); on the seven-knot generator everything holds or takes over.
4. **A struck change is not undone.** A pulse (a made mark whose new offshoot is barred after one round) never dies
   out (0 of 155): the ordinary count had already paid a partner for a join with that offshoot, and the unused payment
   grows on its own (read birth by birth in one case).
5. **Two changes can partly cancel:** 4 of 30 pairs of made marks do less damage together than either alone.
6. **Between patterns, nothing weakens with the steps.** Under the ordinary count no change shows in a joined
   pattern's own births (0 in both settings, all readings). Where something shows, under the record's one fewer with
   waiting or under the cap of three, the fraction of far changes that show is the same at every distance (two of
   seven at d = 1 to 8; the two-loose far site at d = 2 to 5), and the share when it first shows does not fall (it
   rises from 0.12 to 0.24 under the cap).

**Not established.** A weakening of the lamp's kind anywhere: a change whose effect is shared out among a round's
births. That local readings forget the start for starts other than these, or for rings and eddies of one rate.
Anything past round 16 (32 for the cases of finding 1); a weakening between two patterns.
