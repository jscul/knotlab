## From a proposed newest family to its next roster

Put C(x,y)=1−2d(x,y), where d is the pair disagreement probability. For a newest loose f the indices at the minimum of its signed row are exactly its other companions in its holder. Swapping two freshly supplied siblings preserves their rows against every other name. In a verified newest holding these are all the loose siblings. At positive age two simultaneous births have disjoint supports. Every earlier loose name paid for exactly one of their seats. These known formation and row identities admit both arbitrary source arities and repeated source meetings. [Earlier proof and its hypotheses](../../../research/LOOSE-NAMES-AND-SPENT-JOINS-20261120-001A/role_proof.md)

The additional step reads the **partition of the proposed old inputs**. Remove the entire proposed fresh set. Recursive division leaves the previous marginal intact. If two previously loose names had the same holder then swapping them preserves all other rows on this restricted carrier. Their mutual signed mean is negative, since one of them and every other companion attains the same strictly negative minimum in the other's row. Distinct loose names with different holders cannot have these two properties together. Indeed, write A and B for their companion sets. Equality of the exterior rows would imply that A is constant with a given sign exactly when B is constant with that same sign, by saturation of the loose-row minimum on A and on B. Each set is nonempty and full projection gives positive mass to this event. On it the two loose signs agree. Otherwise they are independently fair conditional on the other names. This follows at a uniform source by flipping either loose bit and at a reached cut by independent extension at distinct newest holders. Their mutual mean in that case is **positive**. This proof accommodates extra tied minimizers at sources with repeated shared names.

Therefore equality of the two exterior rows **together with a negative mutual entry** determines exactly who had the same old parent, conditional only on the proposed fresh family being genuine. Each group of same-parent inputs gives all the proposed newest knots that this old parent witnessed. These are all current relevant witnesses, since a newborn meets just its parents. Apply the ordinary rule for witnessed groups, fallback pairs, and the unary births funded by leftovers. The proposed parents' own fresh counts must agree with the demand that was computed on the preceding cut. This necessity covers the possibility of trying to omit an entire proposed holding. Any genuinely reached not-all-equal cut has at least one loose name. Its previous lookahead has already funded its current demand.

This gives a three-way equivalence at every finite positive age under inherited weights. Given the true loose names, the table yields all latest holdings, their individual loose sets, the old input partition and the whole next priced parent roster. Given the latest holdings it yields their loose sets by the earlier orientation theorem. Conversely, every current live holder occurs at least once in the next full named roster. Its old lookahead provided at least its demand, and every private bit left over funds a unary birth. Reading the distinct parent supports that actually occur therefore gives exactly the latest holdings. This equivalence concerns the full roster with named parent supports. A birth count on its own carries less information.

## Which candidate families survive the price

In addition to the negative minimum and the table's equal rows, the independent replay uses three necessary checks. First, the probability of constant companions is minus the loose row minimum. It is read exactly for two or three companions and bounded above by every proper triple's constant probability for larger holdings. Second, for one or two inputs I and q fresh names the conditional sign identity gives
\[
 |I|(2^q-1)C(f,z)=-\sum_{i\in I}C(i,z)
\]
for every exterior z, including an old input. Third, an old holder must have at least three places, all of its other loose names and every genuinely shared companion appearing at that input's old row minimum. ## Why the apparent one-knot history is impossible

On the remaining unary branch the 29 earlier names comprise 0 through 28, with 8 the only loose one. Its negative row minimum is −2769/41527 on exactly 3, 5, 6 and 7. All two- and three-name subsets of these four have greater constant probability than 2769/41527. Thus the old holder of 8 was necessarily H={3,5,6,7,8}, even at a source. Let E be the event that its four companions are constant, of probability q=2769/41527, and let J={23,24,25}.

If the older stage was itself reached, its only loose name 8 was the only fresh name of H. Each of the other four inputs, in particular 5, had just been loose in a separate previous holder. Removing 8 preserves that previous marginal. Row 5 minimizes on J with signed value −20737/290689. The probability that all of J is constant, read from its three pair entries, is 114239/581378. Every subset of J is constant at least that often. The required constant probability 20737/290689 is strictly smaller. No older holder of 5 exists in that interpretation.

If the 29-name stage was a uniform founder, delete its holder H and its sole loose bit 8. Every allowed word of the residual system has one extension on E and two otherwise. To obtain the uniform residual law from the founder marginal, multiply by (1+1_E)/(1+q). For every companion y in H and other surviving name z, it follows that
\[
 C_{\rm residual}(y,z)=\frac{C(y,z)-C(8,z)}{1+q}.
\]
Here C(8,z) is minus the signed contribution of the constant event since 8 is forced opposite the common sign on E and has conditional mean zero elsewhere. Name 5, formerly spent, has become loose. Its new row has minimum −1/14 exactly on J and is zero everywhere else. Its other holder's companions must be among J. In the uniform residual their constant probability is at least that of J, which is at least 114239/620144 by the positive reweighting above. This exceeds 1/14. This argument exhausts the alternative of an arbitrary uniform founder without a roster, arity or depth promise. The independent post-freeze audit derived the reweighting from literal extension counts.
