**What it found.**
- On small starts no ration carries a change beyond one step. To a ration, every knot the two make together is
  the same thing, a join of two parents owed two, so a ration leaves all of them short or none.
- Where short knots retire or make offshoots, a change reaches a pattern's own knots from the knot beside them and
  no further, under every ration. A cancelled join changes what one child is, never how many there are.
- Where short knots wait, two seven-knot sources joined through up to seven link knots hear each other: under
  "a join receives one fewer than it is owed", all seven marks in the other source, first in rounds d + 3 to
  d + 5 at distance d; a mark on the k-th link knot in round k + 3 whatever d is. Under the ordinary count, and
  with retiring, none.
- What carries it is a line of waiting knots: 32 to 53 knots at round 16, a knot short in 15 rounds of 16. It is
  nearer a fuse than a conversation, and I have not seen it pass twice. The ring, the two-knot part and the small
  starts do not hear this way.
