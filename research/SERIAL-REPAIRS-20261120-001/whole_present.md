# A serial rule using only present physical holdings

**Claim.** Suppose every original knot is incident to some original meeting with two or more shared distinctions, and growth takes at most one loose distinction from any given parent at a birth. The following single rule reproduces the ordinary complete operator, including all parent sets, their multiplicities, the exact fresh count at birth, a funding stall and the law-specific closing case. It works on both seven-knot starts even under weak fairness after global joins-first. It works under every maximal order of its own moves. Its reading may reach the diameter of the current ordinary holder graph. The rule restricts the available orders by formation size. It supplies neither a predicted cluster family nor a stored birth date. [Implementation and finite checker](../../experiments/SERIAL-REPAIRS-20261120-001A/SERIAL-REPAIRS-20261120-001A-E001_repairs/cohort.py)

1. Recover the original knots as the holders in repeated meetings. Two already written knots never acquire another shared distinction with one another. A newborn takes at most one distinction from each parent. These facts make the test invariant under renaming and exact on the stated sources. Assign a holder its minimum number of ordinary steps to any recovered original. On a correct partial build, each parent incidence beyond the source goes from one formation size to the next, and every built knot has a complete descending route. Hence this minimum is its depth. The reader computes it anew from present supports.
2. Find the least depth of a knot with a loose distinction. Take **all** holders at that depth, including the ones that have since spent their last loose distinction. At depth zero the original supply of each equals its number of positions other than meetings with other originals. At positive depth the corresponding supply equals its arity less the incoming distinctions from the preceding depth. The witnessed sets come from the other original holders at depth zero and from the preceding depth thereafter. These are the inputs to the exact ordinary operator.
3. Compute the complete list of parent sets and the immediate output count of each from that current information. In particular the demand comes from the intersections of the computed parent sets. No future knot is observed. Subtract the already written children of this depth by their present parent incidences, with multiplicity at a single parent. Allow any of the remaining births on still loose distinctions of its parents. For joins-first, prioritize the multi-parent births in this list. A funding stall or an empty end yields no move. The parity exception applies to a sole birth of the recovered cohort. Not-all-equal uses its own keep clause. [Ordinary operator](../ORDINARY-DYNAMICS-20261120-001/end_operator.md)

**Proof.** At the start the whole current cohort exists. For an incomplete cohort, every still owed birth has one available distinction per parent: the cohort demands exactly its original supply there and each previously built child consumed precisely one of the owed uses. Conversely a loose position left at this depth means a birth is still owed. Thus the minimum depth advances precisely on completion, and all the next holders then exist. Removing existing obligations never creates a new one. Choosing a different still loose distinction of a parent changes only the matching of exchangeable positions. By induction the parent sets and the immediate counts match at every finite size. In particular the same parents can have repeated one-parent offspring and the same set of original ancestors can give different immediate parent sets.

Each cohort is finite, and every authorized move discharges one obligation in it. All maximal serial orders finish each reached cohort, under either priority and with no extra fairness promise. The complete-step source provided by the rule consists entirely of holdings already written, though it can be arbitrarily far from one proposed parent. This is an implementation of complete-size coordination. It excludes some allowed mixed-size orders of the supplied map, including making a deeper join in one piece while the first layer in the other is still unfinished.

**Knot laws.** Within a cohort the newborns use disjoint old loose inputs and distinct fresh positions. A birth with at least one fresh position can extend every satisfying old assignment under either law, by choosing its new values. The sole parity birth with zero fresh positions was already implied. Unwritten siblings therefore impose no additional constraint on the old assignments of a written prefix when their fresh positions are forgotten. Reordering a complete lawful cohort preserves the allowed relation up to renaming of fresh and exchangeable loose distinctions. This transfers the ordinary operator's admitted cases and inherited final-birth convention. It claims no new foundation for those laws.

**Exact seven-knot check.** For the first four birth layers the checker independently builds the target from its archived identified description. It passes only current physical holdings to the reader. It permutes knots and distinctions separately at every checked prefix, compares the complete eligible parent-set multiset and every immediate count, then checks joins-first against its separately filtered reference. Every proper subset of the current cohort was traversed, along with the equivalent prefix state at its successor layer. In the spent arm the four cohort sizes were 7, 10, 9 and 7. There were 1,788 proper subsets. In the live arm the sizes were 5, 4, 5 and 7, giving 204 proper subsets. Those counts apply to each law before priority filtering; the receipt gives the exact filtered counts and numbers of event orders. A single recoding per subset is a renaming check. Invariance for all recodings follows from the reader's definition. A unit-meeting source cannot be intrinsically recognized by this particular marker. Supplying its original members as extra data extends the same proof of the arithmetic, while changing the reading. Recognizing a source and enforcing global progress are two different requirements.
