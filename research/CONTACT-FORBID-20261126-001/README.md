# The contact cases: forbidding that answers both readers through a shared birth

Epic `CONTACT-FORBID-20261126-001`, under G18, at the coordinator's go-ahead after the owner asked to investigate the open
directions further. It takes the door the forbidding study's referee left open
([`FORBID-EDITS-20261126-001`, referee R1](../FORBID-EDITS-20261126-001/referee-R1/report.md)): the placements where a
newborn knot holding distinctions of two readers makes forbidding answer both readers' settings, and the sampled starts
where one reader's edit changes the inputs of the other reader's own birth. Question: does forbidding there give anything
above the classical bound with free settings and no signalling, or is it signalling? Computed; referee pending.

## What was read ([`contact.py`](contact.py), [`tally.py`](tally.py), `contact_runs.jsonl.gz`)

The forbidding study's own growth, edits, weightings (U, S), readings and complete records (A289), unchanged. The
placements: all 232 of its contact addendum where forbidding answers every reader's own setting (163 one-holder
placements under not-all-equal and 68 under parity, all under one fewer; one three-reader placement), and the two
sampled starts the referee named (seven knots, index 1007, readers (3,6) and (2,0); six knots, index 699, readers (5,0)
and (3,2); addressed edits), under every law. For each reader: the largest total variation of its complete record
between the two far settings (over its own setting), and whether the shape of its births (which births, their parents,
inputs and fresh counts) changes, which it can read off its own structure with certainty.

## What was found ([table](contact_table.md))

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 163 contact, not-all-equal (one fewer) | 163, both readers | 163 (total variation 1) | 163 | CHSH 1.06 |

| 1 contact, three readers | 1, every reader | 1 | 1 | Mermin 1/2 |
| starts 1007 and 699, every law | all | all | all | CHSH 0.0012 (not-all-equal), 2 (parity, the added values) |

- **It is signalling, every time.** Wherever forbidding answers both readers' settings through a birth of two readers,
  each reader's own births change with the other's edit: a reader can read the far setting off its structure with
  certainty at some setting of its own. The same holds at the two starts where one edit changes the other reader's
  birth inputs. The single values stay fair throughout, so a reading of one value hides the signal; the complete record
  does not.
- **Nothing exceeds the classical bound even so** *(R1, Block 2: with the study's readings only; a reading that uses the
  changing birth exceeds it while the records signal)*. With the study's readings, the largest score is 1.06 under
  not-all-equal and exactly 2 under parity (the added values, equal by the kept preparation).

**So.** The joint response at a contact is a far edit reaching the near reader's own births: a contact carries the
setting across, which in Bell's terms is communication, not a correlation without it. This closes the door the referee
left open: no case read gives an excess without signalling.

*What was known before the run:* the forbidding study's flags already marked every one of these records as moving
with the far setting; the run measured how (in the shape of the births, every time) and how much (total variation 1). No
prediction is claimed beyond that.

## What this does not say

- Readings other than the study's (any function of a reader's complete record): with the record moving, a reading that
  uses it would signal; a reading that hides it is a coarser record, not the reader's own.
- Larger starts, more rounds, other edits; the contact placements of the addressed edit were not read (the addendum read
  one-holder and three-reader placements only).
