Cold review, about 400 words: soundness of a proof, flaws, and a sound design. Be concrete.

Setting: nested NAE families (the usual limit, complement-closed). The ray definition S has SELF calls whose arguments become a child copy's ports. L_any(s) is the least bond (minimal non-shattered set) containing a top port and an own distinction of SOME copy of S at depth s reached through S calls, with members anywhere. Population: about 5,900 census families whose bond provably grows along the first ray, but which do not forget (some non-rectangular idempotent product). Strata: I, an identity cycle (a port handed down unchanged around a cycle of calls), 5,622 families; T, a tie forced through knots, 5; O, a one-way order idempotent with no conserved label, 91.

Theorem B (to check). Suppose that for every large s some position k is reachable from the top ports in exactly s steps of the identity graph (edges j→k when a call's k-th argument is the copy's port j). Suppose also that S's block has a knot {k, x, y} with x and y own distinctions occurring in no other constraint and in no call's arguments. Then L_any(s) ≤ 3 for every large s. Proof sketch: the copy at depth s along that path has port k equal to the top port p. W = {p, x, y} is constrained only through NAE(p, x, y); p takes both values by complement closure. So the projection on W is exactly NAE, which is constrained, and every 2-subset of it is free. W is therefore a bond containing a top port and an own distinction. The tie variant: port k is tied (equal or opposite in every allowed state) to p, giving NAE(±p, x, y).

Design. (1) Apply B census-wide (syntactic and cheap). (2) On a frozen sample of the families B does not cover (stratum I) and on all of O and T, run the min-over-paths DP (members on the path, an upper bound on L_any). Bounded over paths is then bounded per depth, proved by an exact residue test. (3) Where still unresolved, run an exact members-anywhere direct search at depths up to about 6 as finite readings, keeping an undecided cell. Predictions (blind): P1, B covers at least 30% of I by weight. P2, among sampled I not covered by B, the path bound is bounded in at least 70%. P3, the bounded share in O is at least 20 points below I's.

Questions. Is B sound? Watch three things: minimality when p also appears in other constraints at the top; whether "occurs nowhere else" must hold in the whole pattern; and the residue coverage. Can B be widened soundly, for example to a third member in a side copy, as in TRUE-RATE's carried size-3 bonds? Are the predictions informative, or settled by construction?
