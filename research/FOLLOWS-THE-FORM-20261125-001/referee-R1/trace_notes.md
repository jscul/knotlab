## Why unlinked seeds agree

In an unlinked start every knot and every distinction belongs to one
seed. As long as this holds, the members met by any one knot all
belong to its seed. Every joinable pair and every larger group therefore
stays with one seed, with unchanged owing, shortness and birth parents
when the other seed is removed. For a taking offshoot the candidate
suppliers meet its parent. For a taking join they meet all its parents.
In either case they belong to that seed, and the other seed cannot add
a candidate or change a sole candidate into two. After the takes the
same argument applies to the demand groups. All three rations read
only the newborn's own inputs and, where used, its demand. Every fresh
distinction is new, so the separation persists through each completed
round. Within-seed order of giving loose distinctions and of naming
births is preserved when births of the other seed are removed.

The registered control used not-all-equal. Under these laws an unlinked
second seed cannot change the first seed's own output on a common
completed prefix. The 3,000-knot bound and the guards do count the
whole run. In the archive, 5,400 of 5,400 unlinked forms agree with
their bare runs through the rounds both complete. Of these, 375 stop
earlier than their bare run, all at a guard. In 300 the bare run goes
to the round limit, and in 75 it too is guarded, later. The last common
round across the 61 runs in each study is 3 in 15 studies and 9 in 75.
Thus the 90 agreements check the implementation of separation. They
give no independent test between different ways of acting through
the supplied link.

The separately specified parity rule checks whether the inputs of one
birth are implied by the equations before the round. With disjoint seeds,
every equation from the second seed uses distinctions absent from the
first. A sum of equations equal to inputs wholly in the first seed
projects onto a sum of first-seed equations equal to those inputs.
Conversely that same sum works with both seeds present. Thus this parity
check also preserves separation. An unlinked parity run was outside the
registered control.

## Start-knot membership

A start knot with no usable loose distinction at the outset can never
acquire one. Under retiring or offshoots, every start member either has
its usable distinctions barred, or gives each one to a birth, including
each loose distinction released when a group is cancelled. The old knot
gains no places. Holders persist and a bar is permanent. This proves
the membership assertion for every later completed round. In the
registered families the archive records zero exceptions among 4,500
retiring runs and zero among 4,500 offshoot runs. With waiting, 3,108 of
4,500 runs have a start member after round 1. A spent start knot can
still be a common knot. For instance, start knot 0 above meets both
round-1 own offshoots 9 and 10, which supply their join in round 2.

## Replay

Run these commands from the repository root in a shell capped at 2 GiB.
The shared growth engine emits the literal births, parents and distinction
lists. The trace reader reconstructs both multisets, takes, demand groups
and ration checks independently, testing every subset of these small
rosters. It compares both rounds in all four runs with their archived
multisets. The two pairs were selected after inspecting the held-out
outputs to illustrate the mechanisms, and supply no frequency estimate.

```sh
ulimit -v 2097152
d=research/FOLLOWS-THE-FORM-20261125-001/referee-R1

.venv/bin/python -B "$d/trace_read.py"
.venv/bin/python -B "$d/trace_controls.py" --time-first
.venv/bin/python -B "$d/trace_controls.py"
```
