Taking all other siblings first leaves one slot at each parent; that last pair still makes the tick. ## Recalculating after every birth

Starting entirely from the original holdings, one unrestricted priority follows the highest currently enabled ancestor of the first mark and breaks ties by ascending parent list. All three sources make the same first 14 structural parent choices. On the bare and other-continuation sources, two indispensable ancestors born at events 10 and 12 receive one loose distinction each and spend it elsewhere. Their necessary joint child is lost forever. On the same-continuation source those two births receive two and four slots, leaving one and three available for the joint child at event 15. The unique marked turn fires at event 20. Every one of its 20 events descends only from the old originals. The difference is therefore demonstrated for these physical preparations and this fair-extendible order; isolating initial extra supply from the effect of the new sharing requires another comparison. Per law, the twelve registered from-original order and preparation cells give one firing, two permanent losses and nine unresolved outcomes.
