# One local ring switch for every separation in a specified family

An arriving ten-distinction pattern at one attachment can exchange which of two five-knot eddies carries a stored parity state. The reader is local with a fixed maximum range independent of the separation. It gives a set of permitted physical four-distinction changes. From the stated input **every maximal order** restores the identical arriving pattern, clears all temporary loose changes, and exchanges the stored state in exactly 2d+10 events, where d is the original number of knot steps between attachments. A procedure required to select exactly one named physical change at every state cannot do this from the same input. The result is about the state kept by an eddy. The knots carrying that state stay in place. This uniform construction was devised after the primary predictions were frozen. ## Stage and what the rule can read

Use a plain path with two five-knot rings attached at distinct four-meeting knots, d ≥ 4 steps apart. The path outside either attachment has 4 to 30 steps up to its end. The source sizes fund the separate first child of every two-meeting path. Place one spare at original ring distance two from each attachment and the four standard near-end spares on the path, as in the literal source construction. The two possible ways around a ring first reach its spare after two and three steps. The class has no triangles or competing two-parent children. All sizes and the qualification for ordinary events agree with the earlier transfer theorem. The underlying growth is the funded identified cluster growth of the earlier eddy and path study. Its rate and its growing end are independent of the parity assignment at this fixed stage. [Source qualifications and fate](../EDDY-WORLD-20261119-001/theory_fate.md) · [Constructor and reader](../../experiments/MOTION-LAW-20261119-001/MOTION-LAW-20261119-001-E001_rules/uniform_exchange.py)

Use which physical distinctions share a knot. Intersecting the closed neighborhoods of two co-knotted distinctions recovers their holder on these stages. Exhaustion of its distinctions distinguishes an old holder from its still-loose new children. A degree-four attachment finds its returning legs within five old knots, distinguishes the leg nearer its spare, and recognises its outward path leg by an end within 30 steps before any other attachment. Every other local test uses those features or immediate meetings. There is no source index, assignment history, destination name, prescribed d or remote-ring test in the update. In particular, even a wrongly preloaded receiver cannot be detected at a remote donor.

Prepare the exact arrival used in the prior work. Relative to the donor at 0 with the receiver on its positive side, it occupies ordinary four-responses centered at −3, −2 and +1. At the donor, the four private distinctions of the two outside responses are on. They are outside all write supports and remain on. The receiver lacks this mark. Where an inspected distinction overlaps a pulse identifiable by this test, the guard subtracts that pulse in the binary assignment, locally. Ring meetings can be read raw. Until the write is complete the whole moving packet need not be separately recognizable in its original form. The proof coordinates used here are absent from the rule.

## Ordinary changes, followed by loose corrections

For an old two-meeting path u,v,w let its ordinary change use its two old shared distinctions and the private inputs at u and w of their unique child. At a ring write its old knots in the order 0,c1,c2,c3,c4,0 using the leg nearer the spare first. The following local tests all include the precondition that both relative private inputs of an ordinary change are clear.

1. At the marked donor with its five ring values on and its inner meeting off, use the ordinary change from c1 through the attachment to the inner path. This exchanges the selected ring meeting for one occupied inner-path meeting. While the selected ring meeting is off, clear the two other pairs of consecutive ring meetings by their ordinary changes at c2 and c4, each when its pair is on.
2. At an interior plain path knot with just one occupied relative incident old meeting, offer the straight ordinary change. If adjacent to the marked donor along its inner leg, wait until its ring is clear. The change advances the single occupied old meeting by one, and marks that change's own two private inputs. The used change cannot immediately fire backward because its private inputs are occupied. At the unmarked receiver, an arriving occupied inner meeting and an entirely empty ring admit its corresponding attachment change. While its selected ring meeting is on, the two missing pairs of ring meetings can fill at c2 and c4. Outside the corridor the prepared relative state is blank throughout this part.
3. At an old knot with exactly two active private inputs on two different selected children, an exterior four-response can clear both, changing one loose distinction at each child as well. At first use of a child allow any one of its loose distinctions. At second use select its unique occupied loose distinction. Permit the first correction at the receiver's c3 after its whole ring is occupied. Elsewhere accept a correction if some incident child already has an occupied loose distinction. The donor's c3 can be held until both incident children have one. These tests are on the present assignment alone.

For a locality bound, initial recognition of an outward path leg costs at most 30 old meetings. A query at an ordinary center may involve the nearest attachment or a private input of an ordinary response one old meeting away. Only the pulse within four old meetings of an attachment contributes a reference correction; its four marker bits are local to it. The local neighborhood for identifying shared distinctions, their holders and the exhausted old holders adds at most three distinction steps. These successive searches fit within **40 co-knotted distinction steps from the event center's support**, independent of d. Dynamic binary values need be read only at distinctions having a holder within eight full holder steps of the tested old knot (largest measured five). Layouts with arbitrarily long *outside* arms are outside this radius proof. This locality is measured by shared nearness, with no claim of the same radius in bond distance.

## Why every maximal order has the same end

Before any correction, the old changes have the following successive possibilities only: the donor entry, its two complementary pairs in either order, the d−1 internal path changes from donor to receiver, the receiver entry, and its two complementary pairs in either order. Relative old values and the distinct private input pair just used enforce these stages directly. Cleanup has no seed before the receiver is full. Thus exactly n=d+5 ordinary changes occur before cleanup. Their old differences are precisely the two rings.

Consider these n selected changes as n distinct children, each with its two private inputs at the two old holders of its defining two-meeting path. Each affected old holder is the endpoint of two such paths. The resulting incidence is one cycle. To see connectedness, follow every other old path knot through the children centered at the skipped old knots. There are two strands along the path, and at each end the attachment child and two complementary ring children join one strand back to the other. Equivalently, at the donor replace the missing strands at path indices −1 and 0 by c1 and 0. One strand ends at c1, the other at 0; the children centered at c2 and c4 connect these ends via c3. The receiver closes the opposite ends in the same way, regardless of the parity of d. This also proves that there are n affected old holders. The two children incident to each c3 identify the seed and the last holder without names.

Initially all n old holders have their two active private inputs, and every loose pool is clear. After one correction at the seed, the cleaned holders form an interval growing from that point around the cycle. At every boundary there is exactly one occupied loose distinction in the child across it; wholly cleaned or wholly uncleaned neighboring holders have none between them. This follows at once from first-use choice and second-use clearing. A cleaned holder has zero active private inputs and cannot clean again. Until every holder is clean, either an interval boundary can advance or the donor c3 can make the last correction. Holding that last holder cannot block the other boundary. No ordinary change starts up again: both rings now hold their terminal totals and all relative path meeting values are zero. Exactly n corrections are made in every maximal run. All private inputs return to the arrival values. No loose distinction retains a mark. The endpoint is physically identical across all runs, including the values of every originally distinguishable loose member. The minimum 2n follows independently from the earlier lower bound for these endpoints. The scheduler need supply neither names nor a tick.

## Checks and scope

On the four-step case, enumeration modulo the permutations of loose siblings examined **all 33 reachable classes and all 46 class transitions** under this uniform rule. Expansion by the exact sibling orbit sizes gives 365 literal physical states. A separate traversal reached all 365 states and all 2,381 literal transitions. There is one literal terminal at 18 events. The earlier, less restrictive exchange controller intentionally allows more interleavings and has a different full count. Each controller reaches the same literal endpoint. [Full-interleaving comparison](exchange_checks.md) · [Uniform receipts, raw controls and recodings](../../artifacts/MOTION-LAW-20261119-001/MOTION-LAW-20261119-001-E001_rules/uniform_exchange.json)

The eight deliberately placed pairs had separations 4, 8, 20, 31, 32, 47, 80 and 97. Schedules were sampled with one equal weight per currently enabled physical change; four fixed seeds were used per placement, as well as a reflected arrival, two name recodings and an additional exterior-guard comparison. Those finite samples check implementation. The all-separation, all-orders statement above comes from the proof. At 97 steps the reached size-two stage held 294 knots and 811 distinctions. Its two radius-40 neighborhoods were disjoint, yet the transfer completed at 204 changes.

The switch alone expects the pulse in its arrival phase, a loaded donor and an empty receiver. The zero state, either stored ring without a pulse, and the arrival without storage are silent on the listed controls. A remote occupied receiver admits the same first move at the donor. Applying the unfenced switch by itself to a pulse wholly outside its attachment can also enable premature straight moves. Excluding old-path locations that see an external end before an attachment silenced the four such controls. For the 20-step case the earlier through-rule brought a distant pulse to arrival in 104 firings; after exchange it traveled two further steps in eight firings. A component-reading controller can compose these phases at its marked state. A single bounded-radius rule governing the entire approach, switch and departure on arbitrary backgrounds is still open.
