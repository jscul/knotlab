# When the allowed states determine growth

**Under not-all-equal, the complete allowed states decide every later birth at every finite size** for a connected lawful start with at most two holders per distinction, at least three distinct names per holder, and no implied rule. They decide every named holder as well, with exactly one exception up to renaming. On the six pair names of four mutually meeting holders there are two ways to assign four ternary rules. Every distinction is already shared in both, so each gives zero births. Under even parity, the same eight words on five names still permit either four births or three. The all-size proof uses three elementary observations on complete words.

1. **To find whether x and y occur together**, keep one common assignment on everything else and look for allowed 01,10,11 with forbidden 00. Such a witness exists for every co-held pair. Repeated meetings and three mutually meeting holders cannot defeat it. In the proof at most two constraints need to be strengthened to work for all three settings at once. A contradiction while forcing individual values would already forbid a local word the original system allows. An unresolved odd sequence would require at least three strengthened full two-place constraints.
2. **To read a loose distinction**, collect every forbidden word repaired by flipping x. Across these words, all their possible single-name repairs are precisely x and all the names co-held with x. If that collection projects onto exactly the not-all-equal law, it is x’s only holder. When x is shared it is the union of two incomparable holders, so its projection forbids additional mixed words. On the 2,349 representatives through ten the test recognized all 5,981 loose and excluded all 16,502 shared occurrences.
3. **To move from one known holder to the next**, read which names have exactly the same holders as a shared x. Overwriting such a pair by opposite values in either order preserves every allowed word. A name with an additional holder fails this test at a local word where it is the only 1. This tells us precisely which names are shared between a known H and the other holder of x. The remaining names of that other holder are exactly x’s partners outside H. Connectedness then recovers the whole start.

For a closed start with a repeated meeting, set two of its shared names to opposite values. This removes their two holders in every rival and exposes a newly loose name at a surviving holder. Reading that common holding and going back to the original words recovers the entire closed preparation. With every meeting single, any competing arrangement without even one holder in common must consist of alternate ternaries on precisely four mutually meeting holders. The six-name example is therefore **the whole connected exception at all sizes**. Its two named choices use the triples of pair names mentioning, or avoiding, each holder. Both have 18 words. After erasing any one rule they have 26. The two presentations are isomorphic. One bit can identify the actual named choice, and zero additional bits decide all not-all-equal growth. Ordinary growth preserves the model hypotheses and the essentiality of each rule. Every funded nonempty not-all-equal step leaves a fresh loose distinction, so its reached stage has uniquely recoverable holdings even when the seed has been forgotten. The probability of a constant word on q names is a sum of even-order signed products divided by $2^{q-1}$. Knowing those probabilities identifies all not-all-equal rules of zero violation rate, whose conjunction recovers the words. The earlier two four-place preparations give the lower bound: their 154-word state sets differ, but their complete probability tables agree for every pair and triple. Eight of 70 four-name tables distinguish them. Without probabilities, two p-place holders sharing one name can exchange private names and still realize every pattern on fewer than p names. Both support bounds are sharp. Under parity these same smaller tables are uniform, so p is also necessary for complete probabilities. [Proofs, witnesses and reading scope](marginals.md)

Pair disagreement rates recover all words when every physical not-all-equal rule is ternary. For a grown stage whose largest holder has at most five places, four-name probabilities suffice to recover both the states and future births. At larger known arity p, readings through the largest even order at most p always suffice for the word set. The strict four-name lower bound uses supplied closed starts. A sharper bound confined to positive-age stages with a specified start distribution would need a separate argument. The co-holding test above fixes every outside value; pair disagreement rates average those values away.

Every sampled parity source and reached stage has an explicit rival. For a connected lawful irredundant parity start with two or more holders and at least three loose names L, the sum of all rules is parity on L. No nonzero sum on a proper subset of L is possible: cancellation at every shared name forces its two holders both in the sum or both out, hence all or none by connectedness. Replace one row by this sum. The same equation space remains, and L has full even projection. Every original name remains held once or twice. The new arrangement is connected. After removal of the old row H, each remaining part must contain an original loose name: if a part lacked one, its row sum would be a nonempty parity constraint on the names joining it to H. This would be a proper subconstraint of H unless H met only that part and had no loose name; the latter would leave no place for the nonempty original loose supply. These parts now all meet the new L holder. The old row is different from L because at least one shared name met it. The new basis is irredundant. Three supplemented reached stages had only two loose names. Exhausting all legal parity covers on each gave exactly two named holdings. A different holding need not give different growth in every case. The older five-name witness and the ten-name census establish actual birth differences, including 112 ten-name classes with different fully unmarked growth.
