# Ranking

Ranked by significance to the theory. The ranking covers proved results since the knot test was adopted on 2026-10-19, grouped by epic family, and weighs three things: whether the canon rests on the result; whether it answers a core question (what emergence is, space from a law, or where hardness enters); and whether the program's own perspective did the work. Computed results and conjectures are left out. Provenance is noted where it matters.

The full dated list of results is in [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md); results without a tier there are not in the ranking.

## Tier 1: What the theory stands on

- **The knot test itself** (2026-10-19). K1–K3, with erasure read as forgetting. Everything else is stated in its terms. The core is classical (Schaefer; Baker–Pixley); the reading is ours.
  [KNOT-FOUNDATION A](proofs/KNOT-FOUNDATION-20261019-001A/KNOT-FOUNDATION-20261019-001A-P001_relational_knot_boundary/proof.md), [KNOT-FOUNDATION E](proofs/KNOT-FOUNDATION-20261019-001E/KNOT-FOUNDATION-20261019-001E-P001_forgetting_reading/proof.md)
- **How much a knot hides** (2026-10-21). Two-literal laws hide nothing, a knot hides at most one bit, and only parity hides a full bit. This is the quantitative form of “the boundary between two and three is emergence”, and the canon now states it.
  [EMERGENCE-BITS A](proofs/EMERGENCE-BITS-20261021-001A/EMERGENCE-BITS-20261021-001A-P001_emergence_measures/proof.md), [EMERGENCE-BITS B](proofs/EMERGENCE-BITS-20261021-001B/EMERGENCE-BITS-20261021-001B-P001_emergence_accounting/proof.md), [BASE-THREE A](proofs/BASE-THREE-20261021-001A/BASE-THREE-20261021-001A-P001_three_regimes/proof.md), [BASE-THREE B](proofs/BASE-THREE-20261021-001B/BASE-THREE-20261021-001B-P001_regime_carriers/proof.md)
- **Where openness comes from** (2026-10-20). On infinite systems a knot can be opened by a loop or by an end, and K1–K3 cannot tell which; the law decides. This forced the adopted source-aware reading.
  [HOLES-AT-INFINITY B](proofs/HOLES-AT-INFINITY-20261020-001B/HOLES-AT-INFINITY-20261020-001B-P001_ends_supply_openness/proof.md), [HOLES-AT-INFINITY C](proofs/HOLES-AT-INFINITY-20261020-001C/HOLES-AT-INFINITY-20261020-001C-P001_logical_ends/proof.md)
- **Pairs make no topology** (2026-10-19). Two-place rules never make a knot under the strict reading. Under least violation they make only the not-all-equal triangle. This underwrites “2-clauses are logic, not topology” and maps both readings of the competition question.
  [KNOT-COMPETITION A](proofs/KNOT-COMPETITION-20261019-001A/KNOT-COMPETITION-20261019-001A-P001_competing_pairs/proof.md)
