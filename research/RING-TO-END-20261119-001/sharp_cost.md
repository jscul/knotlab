# The least whole-source transfer and the meaning of every order

With the old values retained, one hot even-input newborn needs at least **two newly occupied fresh distinctions** to have a nonzero state. Each such child has its own fresh distinctions. A stricter question counts all writes in a deterministic rule whose *physical first-layer assignment* respects every symmetry of the loaded input, including interchange of equal loose distinctions at their old parent. A weaker rule can admit all tied choices up to renaming, at a different coordination cost. [Transfer proof](transfer_proof.md)

On the singly attached one-spare ring the five active parents have 2, 3, 4, 4 and 4 loose distinctions. Under hot veto their children are all unary, one per loose input. The children of any one parent can be permuted arbitrarily because all its old loose inputs are zero. Their fresh distinctions are themselves permutable. A nonempty deterministic write at an even-input child requires at least two writes at every child in that parent's orbit. In this sample *every* such orbit has at least two children. There is precisely one active parent with two loose distinctions. Give exactly those two children their full pair at birth, leaving the minimum lift elsewhere. The rule reads one local count and matches the lower bound of **four total fresh writes**. The erased and carrying states on precisely the same first arrangement give respectively 56 and 57 second-layer births, and 315 and 317 total distinctions. If one also admits explicitly choosing a single physical child, a pair there can already change a verdict. Such a choice requires a different symmetry or decision convention.

On the bare five-ring rotations of the ring and interchange of its equally valued loose distinctions make all ten first children one orbit. The smallest nonempty deterministic result therefore has **twenty fresh writes**, two at each child. The second layer has 20 births versus 15 after erasure, and 75 versus 65 total distinctions. The parent-two guard above works on both samples. More permissive tables have different coverage. This is an exact minimum within fresh-only local extensions of the registered hot unary births. Reading an exhausted holder’s state from farther away, changing the previously quiet old inputs, changing which proposals exist, choosing single representatives or trading another local predicate for fewer writes changes the problem. ## Independent events and fused births

The elementary positive event on the already hot bare sector reads a currently loose distinction at a hot parent. Spend it at a unary child with two fresh distinctions. If the spent value is even, set both new ones. If it is odd, set one, admitting the two symmetric choices. The old bit keeps its value. Each child now lies on the growing end with two loose distinctions and is hot. Every structural multi-parent proposal touching it would be vetoed. Two sibling spends can be executed in either order even if they have the same parent: spending one leaves the other loose and the parent hot, and the two new sets of distinctions are disjoint. Equating the fresh-name choices at an odd input by a renaming gives agreement. This computes exactly the hot bare-ring unfoldings of the coupled complete law. It does not prescribe a general asynchronous dynamics for cold groups on other starts.

For the first two layers there are ten root-port events. Each enables two independent events. A root together with its two possible successors has five downward-closed event sets. Across ten roots this gives **9,765,625** possible sets. Every order executing the two-layer causal past gives the same structure and state up to the stated renaming. The number of full named event orders, before equating histories, is $30!/3^{10}$ because the root has to come before its two successors in each disjoint triple. The per-depth birth count is $5·2^t$ at every positive depth $t$. Any fair unlimited execution fills every finite depth by induction on event prerequisites. A schedule that perpetually advances one branch can starve another and fail to complete a whole layer. This separates agreement of outcomes from a liveness requirement on an infinite execution. The attached case is proved for each finite completed plan, with the inherited complete-layer cold rule.

If the writes are separate and growth need not wait, schedule any subset of the ten writes before the next full growth. A sibling pair is refused if either child was heated. Exactly 1, 15, 90, 270, 405 and 243 of the 1,024 subsets therefore give respectively 15, 16, 17, 18, 19 and 20 births. The subsets have one vote each, not one per full schedule. Subsequent attempted writes on the consumed old pairs are no longer allowed changes. Even a fair rule for continuously enabled events permits the two extreme schedules. Fusing the events is an explicit causal choice; the freedom to reorder independent births remains.
