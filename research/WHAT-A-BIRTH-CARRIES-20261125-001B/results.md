The words. X and Y are two parts of the roster, wholly apart from each other after a stated round. They **meet only
through made knots** when the knots that link them at their meeting are all made after the start. A linking knot is
an **ancestral common knot made by births** when both parts descend from it. The **course from X only** is what the
births descended from X alone build, with all names forgotten; it is compared between the run with Y usable and the
run with Y barred. **Attribution** asks whether the first value that differs at one of X's births holds a knot
descended from Y only. **m** is the least number of such values, at distinct births, that separate the cases: m = 1
is "handed in one value", m of 2 or more "composed over several births". The laws: FLOOR1 gives a birth just enough
fresh distinctions for three places; CAP-3 caps them at three; a short knot waits, retires or makes offshoots. The
take of a sole candidate and the two owing rations are rules added by hand for this ticket.

## The case, birth by birth

On the ring of five knots with three loose distinctions each, under the owing ration: a join of two start knots in
round 1 makes an offshoot in round 2. Two parts of two members each, wholly apart after round 4, both descend from
that offshoot. In round 5 each part's two members would join. X's join then has demand 7 if Y's join is made and 6
if Y is barred. The join itself receives one fresh distinction either way; the difference reaches X's course in
round 6, through that join's owing, since its offshoots receive as many fresh distinctions as it had joins asked of
it. The bars on Y give two cases only: none or one of its two members usable, against both. With one member usable
there is no meeting. So the value handed over says whether Y's join exists.
