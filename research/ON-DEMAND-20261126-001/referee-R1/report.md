# Referee report · Growing a local reader’s view on demand

**Refuted. Block.** All 2,256 frozen not-all-equal reach comparisons agree. The study’s own pre-freeze examples nevertheless contain six failures per law at the exact reach claimed. A smaller region can work, a larger region can stall, and a still larger region can work again. A separate 25-knot example exhibits this failure within the support of the registered tree-plus weighting. [Hand-test witnesses](hand_audit.json) · [25-knot witness](weighted_example.json)

## What was not run

- Beyond the 110 rounds per law excluded by the full-growth cap, the original run omitted two tree-plus starts entirely. Independent replay recovered the first five rounds of the memory-limited draw (index 14) and the first six of the time-limited draw (index 50) under each law. The remaining three scheduled rounds per law were not recovered. The 11 additional agreements per law are separate from the frozen score. [Recovered prefixes](omitted.json)
- Mid-growth admission, readers born during growth and exhaustive checks of arbitrary starts or admissions were not run. An additional first-round funding check found no induced shortage in 32,605 reader–horizon choices on the 266 funded scored starts or 102,105 strict one-knot omissions. It covered all 889 two-step reader–superset choices on the 148 funded six-knot starts. [Funding checks](all_readers.json)
- Five-knot physical replay covered round one or the initial stall on every start and followed selected sets of 89 not-all-equal and 104 parity starts through their saved ends. The other growing starts were not independently regrown through their final saved rounds. All comparison-log rows were audited. [Selection and round counts](engine_audit.json)
- The original five-tree exploration could not be identified for replay. The freeze contains its generator but no seed, selected readers or result file. The constructed starts establish counterexamples without estimating their probability under a weighting.

## How it was checked

The independent growth enumerates joins from holdings, pays each group, creates offshoots, computes fresh distinctions from the next round’s groups and carries parent identities. The census uses the shared atlas for historical numbering. No author growth, reading or analysis module was imported. A second implementation uses physical holdings, and the 25-knot calculation independently enumerates candidate subsets. The canonical checker was run on projected joint states.
