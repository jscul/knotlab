# The earlier comparisons and the remaining question

On a funded ordinary step, a knot pays one loose distinction to each child in which it takes part. All its loose distinctions are paid, each to a different child. Thus a changed partner and a changed child count are two different observations. For a grown knot to make a different number of children its own earlier grant must differ. [Ordinary complete growth, steps 1–4](../ORDINARY-DYNAMICS-20261120-001/end_operator.md)

The two-family comparison on 21 founding knots followed a pure receiver member with 34 children in each preparation. One changed child received 33 versus 34 fresh distinctions. The payments to all 34 children led to 1,170 versus 1,174 fresh distinctions in total. One preparation suppressed the older release. A separate physical comparison kept both releases and changed a common descendant. Neither observation counted more children of the same member while keeping both launches. A pure member belongs to the continuing packet by its actual parents. A mixed descendant can still carry an earlier donor's payment. [Earlier indirect encounter](../COPIES-AND-MEETINGS-20261120-001/indirect.md) · [Two surviving releases](../COPIES-AND-MEETINGS-20261120-001/start_edits.md)

On funded, continuing cuts, matching the complete restricted family of witnessed subsets in the receiver packet's first witnessed neighborhood, and the packet's own loose counts, fixes all its future pure child rosters. Merely changing a more distant fact cannot yield the requested witness. If two releases are already apart by at least two witnessed steps when the later one appears, a pure receiver and an inclusive donor descendant can never become partners. The registered question asks whether a changed witnessed fact at launch can alter the same later member's grant and its actual payments. [Protection proof](../INFLUENCE-CONE-20261120-001/cones_proof.md) · [Which pure and mixed encounters are possible](../COPIES-AND-MEETINGS-20261120-001/indirect.md)

A separate physical comparison changed an earlier common payer and thereby changed which of two newborns had a child with the enrolled partner. Each remained funded for two children. Its prepared diversion is a control for a birth changing partners at a fixed count. [Earlier payments and later births](../EARLIER-EVENT-AND-LATER-BIRTH-20261122-001/paired_records.md)
