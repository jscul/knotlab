## Three actual holders at a fixed cut

The memory holder M contains three marked names m with private slack 0. The action holder K contains another three names a with private slack 1. A coupling holder H contains all three m and all three a, but neither private slack. H also contains a writable private three-name receipt register t, raw public input banks, and one private slack. Every name has at most two holders. M, K and H separately have the actual NAE or even parity constraint, with each local projected relation required canonical on its full unrestricted support. A fresh input bank may be connected only to the same H as every corresponding swapped operand; every guard uses unchanged names of H. A compiled action or recent feeder identity is never put in an input bank.

M and t use the three words ready 110, one 101, many 011. One/many means the menu offered exactly one or at least two anonymous movement controls at the most recently *successful* eat that had either type. Sites of the same type are ambiguous. A meal at zero controls is explicitly unaddressed and leaves this type record unchanged. The action words are ready 100, OFF 010 and ON 001. Its single, fixed action distinction is the third a bit. That bit alone is delivered to the game. At every regular decision a is physically initialized to ready. Both ordinary output words require a nonidentity local exchange from ready. Disconnected readout keeps a ready and the fixed output bit OFF. The memory and action registers have distinct names and different word codes.

## Public energy receipt and write

The host provides the bounded public energy before and after the turn, the publicly issued preceding action, and the number of anonymous controls in the *preceding* view. Expose only the action bit, the two energy encodings and the old menu type (0, one, many) to H. These are literal input bank values. The schedule enumerates equality tests at H. Previous ON, energy after at least energy before, and old menu type one/many open the corresponding H offer. This is the proof obligation for an actual paid meal with the archived unit turn cost, gain four and capacity ten. Reject inconsistent observations or distinguish failure clearly. No write may be supplied with consumed, stock flags, an expected output, a world id or a site label. The positive witness must bind its prior action and public energies to the actual game execution, including the cap. Externally forcing a matched prefix is a separately marked control.

For either paid type, a local H transposition of two t names forms 101 or 011 from ready. Physically exchange the **values of the M and t registers** only on the public receipt condition. An implementation using solely the admitted physical operations is enrolled. For each unordered pair of legal trit words p,q, offer three controlled swaps in order: interchange M's p/q positions if t=q, interchange t's p/q positions if M=q, repeat the first offer. Each is additionally guarded on the fixed receipt conjunction. In product space these three offers exchange the off-diagonal words (p,q) and (q,p) and fix the others. The three unordered pairs therefore implement the actual register exchange. M now holds the newly observed type and t holds its former word. On refusal M stays fixed. Retain t's old word as outgoing information when reinitializing this turn-local scratch. Replacing a prior unknown receipt is lossy unless old information exits; the supplied interface can exchange the three equally held t names with three fresh private ready names and retire the previous word. The test compares selection of the first or last receipt through two distinct writes, a repeated same type, retention across idle or unsuccessful turns, disabled writer and physically illegal direct one-bit edit. One prior-payment witness has former M=ready in either history, so the ejected t is the same on its discriminating write.

## Query before advancing the game

Load just the actual current persistent cue and menu type into separate public input names. The full, fixed list of co-held conjunctions examines M and these current inputs. Cue absent requests OFF. Cue present and current one/many equal the stored one/many requests OFF. Any other positive cue requests ON, including a fresh M and a zero-control menu. Disjoint guarded H transpositions of a move ready 100 to the two different answer words. The same memory can select either action when queried with the other menu type. A failed OFF request with no available control remains a refusal in the game. A matched bare one-bit read, clamped stored word, writing disabled, transfer disabled, crossed cue, physically recoded references and detached readout are separately graded. No scorer chooses which offer is issued on a given input.

At decision time prepare the same ready action and same public input in the two branches of *each* history. A physical transposition of two m positions alone transports one 101 into many 011 and conversely; all other names, game state, current public observation, previous action and readout remain fixed. The sham offers the same transposition twice or the identity, leaving preparations identical in effect. Test both exchange directions, disabled coupling and arbitrary old action values before preparation. Record both complete before/after states and both produced a words **before** advancing the game or observing a reward. Only then advance each game from its own exact unmodified counterfactual snapshot with the stored single action bit. No invisible field is an input to the comparison. Intervening on the complete memory requires changing its two physical names together. A single arbitrary bit flip is a separate potentially unlawful control.

## Proposed API for the game implementer

`KnotKeeper(law, *, writer=True, reader=True)` initializes three lawful holders and the external preparation. `after_turn(cue, slots, energy_before, action_on, energy_after)` accepts only public values, takes the locally guarded receipt and returns its trace. `before_decision(cue, slots)` returns the actual third a bit as integer 0 or 1. It uses a fresh-ready action, clears temporal input banks after use, and leaves the emitted action recorded until the next preparation. `checkpoint(cue, slots)` prepares the ready action and current query without coupling. `sham()` and `exchange_memory(other)` act on the marked M alone at a checkpoint. `read_prepared()` delivers a bit without secretly resetting or advancing a game. `memory_word`, `action_word` and `snapshot()` are physical audit views. Code will expose a separate fast summary for held-out natural games, using exactly the same transitions. Slot arguments may be public counts or the tuple of anonymized local controls. The keeper never sees a Game object.

The game authority owns natural play and enumeration of paired prefixes. Enroll a fixed-seed/directly specified positive with one prior paid one-control feeder versus a distinct paid multiple-control feeder, identical current public view including exact energy/menu/previous field, and exact snapshot/sham/reciprocal comparison. For a *certain* useful action, prove from both complete histories and all anonymous paths admitted by them that the currently tested feeder is empty in one and full in the other, with at least one lawful move and a successful alternative. A known two-step return through a single-control site in the quiescent stage can force a revisit, and persistent, different control counts can exclude an earlier meal at the current feeder. Separate oracle-favored but observationally ambiguous cases and same-type collisions. The receipt-type keeper and causation through its one fixed output can be valid even where certainty or a survival advantage fails; arbitrary unseen founders do not inherit certification. Report any extra coupling, preparation/clearing entropy, writable contact, external schedule, distinct-sibling/phase context, supplied route, reward or training. A changing receipt in this device is finite memory. A claim of learning requires different evidence.
