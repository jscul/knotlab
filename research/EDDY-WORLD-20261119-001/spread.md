# How far a smallest parity change reaches

All fixed original path and ring distinctions have the same minimum flip count and physical reach at matched sizes. At source, changing any one requires two other flips and reaches one co-knotted-pair step. At every measured positive age, it requires three other flips and reaches two co-knotted-pair steps. Every tied smallest change has the same reach at its age. The number of ties does respond to an attachment, including the bare and cut controls.

| Mark and stage | Other loose labels may change | All other loose labels held fixed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original path, age 0 | 2 other flips, distinction reach 1, holder reach 0 | Impossible |
| Original ring, age 0 | 2 other flips, distinction reach 1, holder reach 0 | 4 other flips, distinction reach 2, holder reach 2 |
| Original path or ring, every positive age | 3 other flips, distinction reach 2, holder reach 1 | Same minima, radii and ties |
| Current loose mark, every measured age | 1 other flip, distinction reach 1, holder reach 0 | Impossible |

At age zero every minimum response on an original shared mark changes one loose distinction at each of its two holders. At positive ages the minimum responses use only shared distinctions. All 55,581 current loose marks have an equally local response by changing another loose distinction at the same holder. ## Matched comparisons and stage coverage

The cost and reach match at every original path position in B, J0–J2, T4, T8 and C1 at common ages. The same holds across matched original ring marks in the isolated, single and double placements. In particular the predicted longer response in deeper B than on an isolated ring fails at equal size. On fixed marks, the transition from age zero to one increased the free minimum and both free radii by one on all 162 comparisons. On all 1,575 subsequent one-round comparisons those quantities were unchanged. The separate choice of pinning at each age allows the pinned ring cost to drop from four to three as formerly loose labels acquire their second holder. The exact artifact preserves the fixed-label transitions, original source offset and actual holder offset separately. ## Exact reading and persistence

The symmetric difference of two allowed parity states has even incidence at every physical holder. A loose distinction is represented computationally as a link to an exterior holder, whose evenness follows from the sum over physical holders. With the marked distinction deleted, a shortest alternate holder walk between its endpoints gives the fewest extra flips. Every minimizing support arises this way. Indeed an even support splits into simple closed walks and keeping only the one through the mark can only lower its count. The breadth-first walk layers count all shortest paths with distinct parallel labels. They also compute the least and greatest maximum physical reach across those paths. The exterior has no physical step. For distinct marks, the co-knotted-pair step count is one plus the minimum physical holder step count between their holder sets. Pinning every other loose distinction removes its link from the alternate-walk calculation. From any allowed state, adding the same even change gives another allowed state, so the available changes and their minimum count are state-independent. [Replay implementation](../../experiments/EDDY-WORLD-20261119-001/EDDY-WORLD-20261119-001-E001_world/world_flip.py)

The fixed original marks have the same response size and reach at every later lawful full round of these starts. Their source graphs are simple and have no cycles of length three or four. For each original shared distinction from u to v, take any other original neighbor w of v, or use the mirrored choice at u. The first round gives one child joining the two candidates u and w through v. It produces a physical four-cycle through the marked distinction. The literal first-round parent sets verify one child for each such two-step source meeting. Adding a round can create a physical triangle only if two parents of a new junction were already directly joined and shared an old holder, which would have been an old triangle. All original junctions also use their loose distinctions in the first round. From then on an alternate walk through the exterior needs at least four links, one physical and one loose on each side. Hence every minimum alternate walk through an original shared mark has exactly three physical links, holder reach one and distinction reach two.

In that first round the number of shortest alternatives becomes the sum of the original degrees of u and v minus two. Each other neighbor at either endpoint supplies one alternate walk. Every three-link alternate walk must have that form because the source has no four-cycle and two children of that round share no distinction. Later, the retired original holders acquire no further neighbors. So a three-link alternate walk between them cannot gain a later junction. Before the first round the tie count is the product of the two source loose counts. These checks explain the measured multiplicity effects at the seams.

There is also a broader bound on the **count** for a fixed historical distinction. An appended parity stage cannot reduce the old minimum because every new allowed change restricts to an old allowed change. Any old minimum uses either zero or two loose labels. When a round gives those two labels to different children, one fresh flip at each child restores evenness in a nonterminal round. If both go to the same child, their changes are already even there. The minimum count therefore increases by at most two per nonterminal full round. The radius conclusion above for the fixed original probes uses the four-cycles. It is separate from this count bound.

The oracle matched 2,130 literal probe-policy results by exhaustively comparing even subsets, independently using the actual co-knotted-pair steps. Named controls include unequal tied radii, duplicated distinctions, impossibility, and a case where the exterior lowers the flip count while leaving the ordinary step calculation intact. The renaming control jointly permutes the holders and distinguishes the labels anew. The reported responses compare two allowable states at one fixed size. A transport test would additionally need an admitted update and a persistent marked state or feature whose two steps-apart readings to distinguishable fixed landmarks change.
