## A confirmed return with a cost for choosing wrongly

On turn 12 of one pair, both branches had earlier eaten at feeders 7 and 9. One branch had just eaten at 9 on turn 9, passed to a one-exit holder and returned by its sole control. Its current feeder had to be the same emptied 9. The other branch had eaten at 9 on turn 7 and reached still-full 12. Both currently saw a feeder with three exits and three energy after a move; their first memory indicated several exits. The public rule certified the empty side and left the full side unresolved. Actual holder numbers are scorer annotations. [Seed 10790761379035050250, tape 7525429045699153207](panel_pairs.json.gz)

The payoff control actually passed or tried on the first turn, then tried on each surviving branch on the following turn. In this example that left five energy after passing at empty 9 and one after trying there. The values reversed at full 12. Across all 388 primary pairs, the two strict energy advantages both held on 69; these accounted for 69 of the 210 pairs with at least two energy.
