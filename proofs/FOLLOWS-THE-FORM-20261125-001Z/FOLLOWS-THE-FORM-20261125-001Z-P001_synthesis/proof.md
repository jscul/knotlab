# Does what a first seed's descent makes differ with the form of the second seed: synthesis

This synthesis binds the ticket of `FOLLOWS-THE-FORM-20261125-001` to its records. It contributes no further proof
or experiment. The source is referee pending.

- [Ticket A](../../../research/FOLLOWS-THE-FORM-20261125-001A/results.md): on starts built from a first seed, one
  link distinction and a second seed, with the first seed and the place of the link held fixed, what the first
  seed's own descent makes differs with the form of the second seed, and with the seeds unlinked it does not.
  Nineteen of twenty registered predictions hold, the four exact replications of the drafter's probe among them. On
  held-out seeds: three or more classes in 87 studies of 90; 422 to 1,309 pairs a row told apart only after round 1
  with every round-1 birth with a first-seed ancestor the same; the class never a function of the held knot alone,
  and a function of the held knot with the second seed's births by round in 49 studies of 90. The prediction about
  the first seed's fate fails (37 studies of 90, 40 needed).

Taken together ([decision](../../../research/FOLLOWS-THE-FORM-20261125-001/decision.md)): on built starts, what a
seed's descent makes depends on what it is linked to, beyond the first round and beyond the linking knot, and often
no further than a count profile of the second seed. The meeting is supplied by the start. The same between two parts
the growth made is the open direction.
