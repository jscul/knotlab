## What stands

1. **Under the ordinary count, some changes at the start of a growth of dimension two weaken as the owner's lamp
   does.** Read without names (each birth by its kind, parents, fresh distinctions and places, and its parents'),
   the damage of a mark or a move on two touching knots (4 of 4), and of the middle mark and the two end-to-end moves
   on three knots in a row (3 of 9), settles to a fixed 4, 5 or 1 births a round while each growth's births grow by
   two a round: its share falls as one over the round's count, to 0.01 to 0.08 at round 16, and the damage is still
   exactly 4, 5 and 1 at round 32. Parity gives the same. On two touching knots the damage lies wholly among the
   births nearer the changed knot; those one step further are untouched.
2. **Every other change holds or takes over.** On the bare rings (dimension one) and the golden-ratio eddy a change
   alters the kind of growth and takes over; made marks in dimension two add a part that keeps growing, and hold
   (shares 0.29 to 0.54); on the seven-knot generator everything holds or takes over.
3. **A struck change is not undone.** A pulse (a made mark whose new offshoot is barred after one round) never dies
   out (0 of 155): the ordinary count had already paid a partner for a join with that offshoot, and the unused payment
   grows on its own (read birth by birth in one case).
4. **Two changes can partly cancel:** 4 of 30 pairs of made marks do less damage together than either alone.
5. **Between patterns, nothing weakens with the steps.** Under the ordinary count no change shows in a joined
   pattern's own births (0 in both settings, all readings). Where something shows, under the record's one fewer with
   waiting or under the cap of three, the fraction of far changes that show is the same at every distance (two of
   seven at d = 1 to 8; the two-loose far site at d = 2 to 5), and the share when it first shows does not fall (it
   rises from 0.12 to 0.24 under the cap).

**Not established.** That the weakening is a property of endless chains (the reading in the note), or that it gives
one over the square in dimension three; anything past round 16 (32 for the weakening cases); starts other than these
six; a weakening between two patterns.
