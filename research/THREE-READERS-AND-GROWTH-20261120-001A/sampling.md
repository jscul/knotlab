## How exact counting reaches the fixed hosts

With three marked distinctions, build an eight-by-eight integer table. Its entry at $x,y$ counts all ordinary outside assignments compatible with both words. Under not-all-equal, each written rule on the unread names expands as one term for any values and two signed terms excluding a constant value. For each $x,y$, an excluded constant is active precisely when the marked part of that knot is constant in that value in at least one of the words. An unread distinction with two unconstrained holders contributes two completions. Two holders forcing opposing values contribute none. Consistent forcing contributes one. A corresponding count applies to loose unread names. Sum over the three choices at each holder using exact integer arithmetic. The largest case has 113 distinctions. Under parity a linear rank and translation calculation gives the same common-completion table. A common integer factor cancels on normalization and is saved with the placement so its physical counts can be recovered.

Across the panels there were 260 distinct complete tables up to this positive integer rescaling. Every probability and certificate was checked exactly against its full list of candidate answers.
