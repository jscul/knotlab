# How far apart are the two growing parts?

A junction step crosses one shared distinction between its two actual holders. The two attachment knots stay four or eight junction steps apart for every size. The least number of junction steps *between the two current pure growing parts* is different. Write d for the attachment separation and t for the round, so the cluster size is t+1. Count only pure ring junctions with at least one source knot exclusive to that ring. Their least separation is d+2 at size one and d+2t at every size t+1 ≥ 2. These are all-size conclusions for the two registered placements.

**Why paths reduce to old junctions.** The complete round joins old parents only when every pair has an old common holder. Fresh distinctions of a birth are initially loose and two children of the same round share no distinction directly. Thus a passage from one old parent through a new junction to a different old parent can be replaced with a passage of equal length through an old common holder. In these all-live starts such a common holder at positive parent age belongs to the preceding age; at source age it is another source holder. A return to the same parent can be removed. This proves that adding complete rounds preserves ordinary junction steps between any two old knots.

Now take a shortest route between two positive-age junctions in the current stage. Whenever an interior knot of positive age occurs between two of its parents on that route, apply the replacement. Each parent has one less unit of cluster size. Each substitution decreases the sum of the ages on the route. Choose among shortest routes one with minimum such sum. Its ages descend monotonically, may make consecutive steps among the source junctions, and then ascend monotonically. The only steps that stay at one age are in the source. If the two junctions have no common ancestor in the positive ages and their source sets are disjoint, this route descends all the way to the source and traverses an ordinary source route. Its exact length for two junctions at age t is 2t plus the least source separation between one ancestor on either side. Descending parent links realizes all these source choices by definition.

The two pure ring parts have disjoint source sets and cannot have a common positive-age ancestor. At size one the nearest permissible holders are immediate ring neighbours of their attachments, at separation d+2. At each size t+1≥2, a consecutive cluster in the unrolled five-ring can contain its attachment and an exclusive ring point on each side. Their minimum ancestral separation is precisely d. This gives the formula. In particular the nearest growing parts are 14 and 18 junction steps apart at size six. At the same size there already exist junctions spanning both rings in each placement. A junction made from both rings and a step between the two pure cohorts answer different questions. Neither is a motion of the original rings.
