## Where the difference sits

Four junctions A, B, C and D have precisely the five meetings AC, BC, AD, BD and CD, numbered 0 through 4. C and D each have three distinctions. At the marked stage there are two separate connections through the rest between A and B. These make AC and BC interchangeable in the whole current table, even against every distinction outside the four junctions. In the next round a single junction takes the three loose distinctions on A's side and introduces one fresh distinction. A pair on that side can now belong to a three-distinction bond. A pair taking one distinction from each side still needs four. The argument is about connected cuts on both sides, as required for a genuine bond. All 666 current readings agree there, and the new bond is {0,2,37}. Six is proved least for this particular five-meeting construction, including histories where one of the four junctions appears during growth. ## The least extra reading in the original meet-twice example

In the old numbering, one Boolean comparison between the marked pair 0,1 and its proposed image 2,1 settles the swap. Ask whether the two can flip together in an allowed state with all other distinctions held fixed. For 0,1 they can. For 2,1 they cannot. Equivalently, there are 21 common bonds for the first pair and one for the second. This is a different pair reading available from the current allowed states.

If the reading is restricted to the least bond through the marked distinctions, use a third distinction, 15, at the growing end. The smallest current bond through {0,1,15} has size four. No bond contains {2,1,15}. A single yes/no question about existence already rejects the swap. Each of the two Boolean comparisons classifies all 48 pointwise-live current matchings in that example and keeps exactly the 16 that extend. ## Exhaustive lower bounds

The complete census has two bounds. With an arbitrary connected admissible start and any positive number of lawful rounds, there is no counterexample at **11 or fewer current junctions**, with no initial arity or width cap. There is also none at **22 or fewer current distinctions**, with no cap on initial junction count or on the round. The search grades an entire group of marked permutations by its generators. It checks the actual next table for generators whose extension does not follow by preserving the equations. A proof covers every source with at most two newly added live junctions, including sources too wide for a table run. [Size-independent small-live proof](../../proofs/GROWN-END-MEET-ONCE-20261119-001A/GROWN-END-MEET-ONCE-20261119-001A-P001_pointwise/proof.md#all-sizes-with-at-most-two-live-junctions-at-a-reached-stage)

The remaining sources can be counted from their immediate predecessor, which is itself an admissible connected meet-once start. If that predecessor has n junctions and the current stage has N, no predecessor junction supplied more than N−n loose distinctions. All its compulsory parent groups are fixed by its live sites, and the rest of its loose distinctions each give a one-parent junction. This gives a finite complete generation without a per-junction loose cap. At current width W and at least three new live junctions, the total arity gives 3N ≤ 2W−3, so W ≤ 22 requires examining at most 13 current junctions. The first sweep tests 5,566,765 predecessor and loose-count assignments and the second tests 76,259. These are counts of assignments on representatives of ordinary meeting patterns; symmetric assignments can be counted more than once. The two sweeps overlap.

At the threshold, a third complete sweep covered every reached stage with at most 12 junctions and 23 distinctions, irrespective of age. It checked 249,219 predecessor and loose-count assignments. Exactly three arrangements failed, all at the joint minimum. They are pairwise nonisomorphic. Removing the live junctions and regrowing each possible preceding round gives earliest starts of (7 junctions, 16 distinctions), (8,17) and (8,17). Each is one round before failure. Thus seven is the least original starting size among histories attaining both reached minima.
