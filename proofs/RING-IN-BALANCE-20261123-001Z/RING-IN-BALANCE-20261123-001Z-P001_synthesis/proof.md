# A ring in balance: synthesis

This synthesis binds the three tickets of `RING-IN-BALANCE-20261123-001` and the thread's checks to their records. It
contributes no further proof or experiment. All sources are referee pending.

- [Ticket A](../../../research/RING-IN-BALANCE-20261123-001A/results.md): the ring that returns and gives parts off, on real knots, with the knot test under both knot laws and each added rule swapped. Exact computation and two arguments.
- [Ticket B](../../../research/RING-IN-BALANCE-20261123-001B/results.md): dead knots, knots that wait, and the round read from one knot. Exhaustive census of 86,032 starts and exact computation.
- [Ticket C](../../../research/RING-IN-BALANCE-20261123-001C/results.md): with no round supplied. Exact computation and one argument.
- [Checks](../../../research/RING-IN-BALANCE-20261123-001/checks/): the engines compared with the pilot's programs and with each other.

Taken together ([decision](../../../research/RING-IN-BALANCE-20261123-001/decision.md)): under rules added by hand a ring
returns and gives the same parts off, with every knot passing the knot test; the return needs waiting with release, not the
cap of three; a dead knot makes returning somewhat easier; the round can belong to a part of the live roster but is still
needed inside it; and small parts that copy themselves appear under other rations, which was not predicted. Nothing here is a
property of ordinary growth.
