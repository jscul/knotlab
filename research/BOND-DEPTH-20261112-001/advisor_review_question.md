Setting: NAE on three-place knots. Nested families: a 3-port definition D with own distinctions and calls (knots K, SELF). A copy of D at level j has its 3 ports (args from the parent copy) and fresh locals. The infinite (usual-limit) pattern lives; below any finite level, the subtree under a SELF call contributes exactly the limit relation G on its 3 args (greatest fixed point; compactness). A bond = inclusion-minimal set W of distinctions whose allowed joint states are not all; least bond L(u,v) = min |W| over bonds containing u and v. Question: from a top distinction u to a distinction v at level s, does L grow with s or level off, typically?

Method (spine DP): fix the ray of copies C_0 (main) ... C_s (each made by the first SELF call of the previous); other SELF calls (side branches) and non-spine structure summarized by their relations on their 3 args. Each copy is a block; consecutive blocks share the 3-distinction interface I_j (args of the spine call). For a choice of W_j (among distinctions created in block j), transitions T_j(alpha_j) subset of 8x8 interface states. W constrained iff some word alpha has no accepting run; minimal iff for every x in W the automaton with x projected out accepts all words. DP over blocks with state (F, G): F = family of reachable interface-state sets over all words; G = same with exactly one x already projected out (union over x); end: constrained iff empty set in F; minimal iff empty set not in G. Minimize sum |W_j| by shortest path over (F,G) states. This generalizes our exact bandwidth-3 bond DP (validated earlier against brute force).
Limitation: W restricted to the spine's own distinctions, so L_spine >= L (side branches could shortcut). Plan: validate against brute force on small cases (all W up to the pattern's size on tiny depths) and on chains with no cycle of knots (proved L = steps apart + 2); measure how often L_spine > brute-force L where both are computable.
Settled: the ends carry finite-state information only (top window periodic in depth, proved), but that does not bound bonds inside (chains: L = d + 2 grows linearly).
