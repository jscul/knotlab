# Closure from the proposed inputs

**Proved for connected lawful ordinary parity growth. Independent program referee pending.** Let an actual funded proposal take one loose distinction from each of p parents. For p at least three, ask for the cardinality b of a smallest bond through any p−1 of the proposed inputs. The birth closes exactly when b equals p. In particular, a single arbitrary pair in a ternary proposal has bond distance 2 exactly at closure. Physical connectedness, eligibility of the group and funding are hypotheses.

Wire all loose distinctions to an auxiliary exterior. A parity bond through given loose distinctions is a minimal nonzero cut. The shore away from the exterior must contain their holders. A legal group of three or more cannot have members on opposite sides of a single ordinary bridge. Across such a bridge each two-step common-holder route needs one parent to be a bridge endpoint. The endpoints cannot themselves form a candidate pair, since a common holder would give a second route. Thus one side has a single parent at the endpoint, and the other endpoint meets it and every parent on the far side. That one knot would hold the whole group, making the proposal ineligible.

A shore containing p−1 chosen parents and missing the last therefore pays at least p−1 loose and two shared crossings. A shore containing all p parents pays at least p. Equality requires zero additional loose or shared crossings. Physical connectedness makes this precisely the whole physical presentation with the proposed inputs as its entire loose set. Conversely that loose set is itself a bond. The p−1-input criterion follows; the sole final birth is implied and requires zero fresh distinctions. Under the guide's three-place definition the binary closing pair lies in no qualifying bond. Under the algebraic reading that admits a two-place tie its minimum is two. A continuing binary proposal has a finite minimum of at least three in both readings. There is no exhausting unary proposal on a lawful connected parity start or live reached stage.
