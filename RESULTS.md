- **2026-10-20 · Tseitin formulas need size 2^Omega(treewidth) on planar and minor-free graphs** (`CELL-SIZE-20261020-001-D001`).
  If every edge outside a bounded set lies on a short cycle with short detours avoiding it, Tseitin formulas need size 2^Omega(treewidth) in resolution and in polynomial calculus with or without twin variables over characteristic other than 2. This covers plane graphs with short faces and d-dimensional grids, and through wall topological minors every graph excluding a fixed minor, planar graphs included (resolution and PCR).

  On a connected graph of maximum degree at most 3, the toggle width equals cutwidth + 1, so an impossible charge network of three-distinction knots is refuted by linear local reasoning over a field that cannot compute in its group exactly at cutwidth + 1. In general the toggle width lies between cutwidth + 1 and cutwidth + floor(maxdegree/2).

## Composing like cycles: the algebra of knots

Which laws let knots compose the way cycles do, and what that forces.

- **2026-10-20 · Knots compose like cycles exactly in difference laws whose distinctions do not split** (`KNOT-CIRCUITS-20261020-001-D001`).
  A law with a Mal'tsev polymorphism composes its knots like cycles, so that any two sharing a distinction leave a constraint once it is forgotten and form matroid circuits, exactly when every subalgebra of its polymorphism algebra has a unique maximal congruence; Boolean laws do exactly when affine. Elsewhere knots fuse or meet at hubs, so cycle-space structure among knots marks difference laws whose distinctions do not split.
  [proof P001](proofs/KNOT-CIRCUITS-20261020-001B/KNOT-CIRCUITS-20261020-001B-P001_circuit_composition/proof.md), [epic closure](proofs/KNOT-CIRCUITS-20261020-001Z/KNOT-CIRCUITS-20261020-001Z-P001_epic_closure/proof.md)
- **2026-10-20 · A law recognizes its space rather than creating it** (`KNOT-SPACE-20261020-001-D001`).
  A law's allowed states carry a graph exactly when its knot matroid is graphic, and a sphere when it is also cographic: they determine a 3-connected graph exactly and a 2-connected one up to Whitney twists. Presence is decided by Tutte's excluded minors, not by how the law was written, so a randomly written local law can carry a sphere while almost every balanced code carries none; the mathematics is classical.
  [proof P001](proofs/KNOT-SPACE-20261020-001B/KNOT-SPACE-20261020-001B-P001_recognized_space/proof.md), [epic closure](proofs/KNOT-SPACE-20261020-001Z/KNOT-SPACE-20261020-001Z-P001_epic_closure/proof.md)
- **2026-10-20 · Composing knots like cycles reduces to rectangular knots, not to a difference operation** (`MALTSEV-CONVERSE-20261020-001-D001`).
  Composing knots like cycles does not force a difference operation: parity beside an unrelated law does it without one. The converse reduces exactly to rectangularity of knots, since rectangular knots compose like cycles precisely when no two meeting at a variable split its range; this is proved on two values, and every tested three-variable knot on up to four values supports it.
  [proof P001](proofs/MALTSEV-CONVERSE-20261020-001B/MALTSEV-CONVERSE-20261020-001B-P001_converse/proof.md), [epic closure](proofs/MALTSEV-CONVERSE-20261020-001Z/MALTSEV-CONVERSE-20261020-001Z-P001_epic_closure/proof.md)
- **2026-10-21 · Composing like cycles is affine structure modulo the tops** (`AFFINE-TOPS-20261021-001-D001`).
  A law composes like cycles exactly when its ranges have tops, its ties are bijections modulo them, and a ternary polymorphism is Mal'tsev modulo the tops of its knot ranges, which proves the series' Conjecture 7 through rectangular top images. The tops of knot ranges are then elementary abelian p-groups with polymorphisms acting affinely, and every system carries a direct sum of matroids representable over finite fields, all of which occur.
  [proof P001](proofs/AFFINE-TOPS-20261021-001A/AFFINE-TOPS-20261021-001A-P001_rectangular_tops/proof.md), [proof P001](proofs/AFFINE-TOPS-20261021-001B/AFFINE-TOPS-20261021-001B-P001_affine_tops/proof.md), [epic closure](proofs/AFFINE-TOPS-20261021-001Z/AFFINE-TOPS-20261021-001Z-P001_epic_closure/proof.md)
- **2026-10-21 · Composing like cycles is decided on the tops** (`CYCLE-TOPS-20261021-001-D001`).
  In every law whose circuits compose like cycles, each system has exactly the circuits of its image modulo the tops, because each variable and its blurred copy are parallel elements; ties become bijections between tops, and every reflexive pp-definable relation lies below its range's top. The earlier conjecture that such laws are Mal'tsev on all their tops fails for disjoint unions, and holds in every sampled law when restricted to the ranges of knot variables.
  [proof P001](proofs/CYCLE-TOPS-20261021-001B/CYCLE-TOPS-20261021-001B-P001_decided_on_tops/proof.md), [epic closure](proofs/CYCLE-TOPS-20261021-001Z/CYCLE-TOPS-20261021-001Z-P001_epic_closure/proof.md)
- **2026-10-21 · Tops can hide any constraint problem** (`CYCLE-TRACTABILITY-20261021-001-D001`).
  Every finite structure is homomorphically equivalent to a law that composes like cycles, and every finite core to a core that does, so composing like cycles alone admits NP-complete constraint problems, even among cores; the core of such a law need not compose like cycles, and it can be non-Taylor. With constants, or for a core with no nontrivial automorphism, composing like cycles does give polynomial time, and both hypotheses are needed.
  [proof P001](proofs/CYCLE-TRACTABILITY-20261021-001A/CYCLE-TRACTABILITY-20261021-001A-P001_hidden_hardness/proof.md), [epic closure](proofs/CYCLE-TRACTABILITY-20261021-001Z/CYCLE-TRACTABILITY-20261021-001Z-P001_epic_closure/proof.md)
- **2026-10-21 · Composing like cycles tests codes for linearity and certifies tractability with constants** (`CYCLE-UTILITY-20261021-001-D001`).
  No statement of the composition characterization turned up in a bounded literature scan; its uses are a composition test for linearity of codes, under which the non-Pappus almost affine code fails through an explicit three-copy system, and a tractability certificate for laws that keep composing like cycles with constants. Counting is not covered, and constants can destroy composing like cycles, so whether every such law is tractable is open.
  [proof P001](proofs/CYCLE-UTILITY-20261021-001A/CYCLE-UTILITY-20261021-001A-P001_utility/proof.md), [epic closure](proofs/CYCLE-UTILITY-20261021-001Z/CYCLE-UTILITY-20261021-001Z-P001_epic_closure/proof.md)

Composition sees only which sets are constrained, so a dominating difference law hides any fibre pattern inside the components of a fibre graph.

  A knot link remembers a loop exactly when its transfers permute blocks, for any number of values, and in a law composing like cycles its networks remember twisted first cohomology over a finite field modulo the tops, with frame-matroid free sets. ## Native complexity: hubs, counting and approximation

What networks of knots can decide, count and approximate without hubs.

- **2026-10-24 · Native emergence is approximable except where Fibonacci meets a twist** (`NATIVE-APPROX-20261024-001-D001`).
  Given McQuillan's winding theorem, native counting with pins is approximable for the 26 windable knots, because the knot axiom K2 is his terraced condition; with the four topological knots this settles 28 of 34.
