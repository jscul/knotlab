# Supplemental comparison of two permitted words

This checks a consequence of the approved B258 primitive-root control. It does not replace the study's design, preparation, keeper or decision. After the study freeze, an exploratory one-bit purity calculation suggested that two copies could test the phase of the five-place winding-two rival even with an unknown common phase reference. Before computing the conditional comparison, we specify it here. The analytic prediction is visible from the signs at weights one through three.

Use the same five-place not-all-equal knot in two independent copies. Make the positive-magnitude preparation and the preparation with the specified first or second primitive root separately. In the first copy fix four ordinary distinctions to 1000. In the second fix them to 1100. Order the unpinned bit last. Include the probability that the full-word preparation supplies these specific exteriors before conditioning. On success both choices of each last bit must be allowed. Swap just those last bits. Verify on words that this is a permitted involution at the two fixed exteriors. Read the signs with the supplied projectors (I ± swap)/2. On ordinary parity check exactly which swapped words are lawful and whether a nontrivial read on the selected carrier is available. Show distributions on the two-copy support, the incoherent controls and the total selection probability. Also report what a uniformly weighted ordinary starting word does under the swap.

The question is whether the sign probabilities differ for the phased input and its own positive magnitudes. Compare the first and second primitive fifth roots, and conjugation of each. Derive both probabilities from the conditional two-by-two density matrices and independently from the corresponding four amplitudes, keeping the symmetric, reversed and absent branches. Compute the correlation after a common change of basis on the two unpinned bits, and after different untracked reference changes. Compare with a real or an uncalibrated ordinary swap observed only on complete input and output words. Charge the ability to prepare two clean copies in compatible frames, the two pins, an addressed comparison and the coherent sign-reading rule.
