## Separate current readers

The full reader receives current supports. In a continuing complete frame it tries removing every loose holder, then checks that the remaining supports predict exactly those births, their parent sets, their fresh counts, and a partition of every prior loose distinction. It repeats as long as this check passes. On each ring fixture the earliest compatible frame has the expected simple ring blocks, and the recovered parent sets select the current all-own-parent families. The evaluator independently follows the literal event records. Each tested open frame gives the same result after independent renamings of knots and distinctions. The reader can fail at a closed or incomplete frame. Its output identifies a compatible history in the promised complete frames.

The end-only reader sees the current loose knots, their counts, and their complete shared-witness relation. It reports whole connected parts, cyclic sets of the fixed size, and roots of witnessed degree one with two loose distinctions. Parts correspond at every continuing step in the panel. Every old witnessed pair has a common child, every live parent is represented, and the child sets of different parts are themselves precisely the new parts. The closing parity star exits that correspondence at its last step.

## Reconstruction and verification

The checker generates maximal unheld groups and forecast demand from literal shared distinctions, independently matching the archived ordinary end operator on each completed layer. Ten trajectories per law give 60 completed parity transitions and 63 not-all-equal transitions. There are no stalls or capped layers in these declared trajectories. A cap of 1,024 births per layer protects the literal builder. The four matched one-ring arms per law are additional comparisons, with the same left-only control used for the two seam separations. The script uses the repository environment's NetworkX and PySAT, and writes the receipt by default. A replay using `--out` from a temporary working directory and a different Python hash seed reproduced every byte in 26.07 seconds with 122,080 KiB of peak resident memory.

The source projections are exact Gaussian elimination for parity and all pinned local words with a SAT solver for not-all-equal. Physical replays apply the same fixed birth map in earliest-ready and latest-ready orders. They require every actual consumed distinction still to be loose at its specified parent, preserve every older local projection, and end with exactly the same supports. Parity input projections follow by summing checks on the connected ordinary holder arrangement, with direct elimination as an additional small-instance control. The not-all-equal inputs are checked for every possible word in each replay. Basic projected types through five places also pass the canonical K1–K3 checker. Latest-ready genuinely interleaves sizes, for example with 244 and 424 backward age transitions on the two-ring fixtures. These are linear extensions of the supplied complete map. The present-only serial policies remain a separate experiment.
