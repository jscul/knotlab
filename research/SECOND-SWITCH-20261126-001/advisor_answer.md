# The advisor's answer (26 November 2026)

To [the question](advisor_question.md), asked before the second-switch study was run.

**Expect (1) to fail, or pass only weakly.** Your own known facts point that way: a change at the far site in round d + 1 or later was never heard, and the way lengthens as the structure between grows. At two rounds a step, a change 7 to 14 sharings away needs 14 to 28 rounds after t0, so up to round 38 for t0 = 10. If the way lengthens by a sharing every two rounds or faster, the change never arrives at all.

**24 rounds is not enough for a null.** The 6,000-knot limit will also stop fast runs earlier. Freeze a null as "not heard through round N at sharing distance s", not "never". Record the sharing distance every round, not only at t0, so you can compare the rate of lengthening with the rate of travel. Keep the nearest members (distance 7) with t0 = 3 as the decisive cells.

**(2) joins two claims; split them.**
- **Heard versus switched:** a heard change may shift R's births for a round without changing its way. Freeze an operational test for slow and fast before the run.
- **Cancelling:** an extra loose distinction earlier could cancel as well as make the switch, so "heard switches it" is too strong.
- **Few slow cells:** with the two-knot start, nothing at distance 7 or more with t0 of 3 or more can arrive before round 7. The "R still slow" cells therefore come almost entirely from the never-switching start.

**(3) should hold but may be empty.** If no member is ever short under the ordinary count, nothing can be heard by construction. Say so rather than counting it as a confirmation.

**Controls for "along the line":**
- **Difference set:** each round, record which knots differ between the changed and unchanged runs. A heard change must show an unbroken chain of differing knots from the changed member to R, with arrival ordered by sharing distance.
- **Same change in between:** make the same change at a knot in between, at the same sharing distance from R. If it is heard equally, the far pattern is not what matters.
- **Severed line:** bar a link knot at t0 alongside the change. If R still hears it, it did not come along the line.

**Missing:**
- **Comparison:** compare births by counts or canonical form, not knot identifiers, since an added distinction renumbers everything after it.
- **Truncation:** compare only rounds that both runs complete; the limit will cut the two runs at different rounds.
- **Lower bound:** "t0 + sharing distance" assumes one sharing per round. Check whether a group from a met set can carry influence across several sharings in one round.
- **Multiplicity:** there are about 800 runs, so "some change is heard" is nearly unfalsifiable. Freeze what fraction, in which cells, counts as failure.
- **Scaffold:** the five nearest members are a hand choice; register it and vary it.
