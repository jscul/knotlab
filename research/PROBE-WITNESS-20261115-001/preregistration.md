# Two outside settings as a proof that one bit is insufficient

We test the step suggested by the [earlier ordering experiment](../TRIPLE-ORDER-20261115-001/triple_order.md). A low-cost observation could exclude a large class of candidates during both search and checking. A rule here has two Boolean outputs specified by complete truth tables. Eligible groups contain three distinct raw inputs that affect both outputs. All methods must return the minimum intermediate width across those groups and a physical NAND program producing both outputs with the stated group separated by that many wires.

## Witness and fallback

Hold the outside inputs at two settings shared by all local assignments being compared. For each of three assignments to the group, record the pair of outputs at each used setting. If the resulting signatures are all different, a one-bit summary would give the same message to two of the three assignments even though an outside setting distinguishes them. The witness needs three original rows if one setting suffices and at most six for two settings. Finding a witness can require more reads. The principal method sets all outside bits to zero or all to one. It caches readings of a global raw row across triples for this task. The verifier independently recovers those same rows from the original tables and can cache them too.

Silence from the probes is inconclusive. A successful witness excludes only one bit. During search it can replace an exact group check only once the remaining question at that group is whether one bit suffices. Exact search must first construct a feasible program at two bits or use exact tests while a wider incumbent is in play. During verification the chosen group is checked fully. Missing evidence on other groups falls back to exact exclusion, and a claim that three bits are necessary still requires excluding two. The complete physical program and its cut must pass the same checks as the baseline. False, malformed, or inapplicable purported evidence cannot purchase an exclusion. This is a restricted witness for the usual count of distinct columns in Boolean functional decomposition. ## When to try learning

Calibration compares four fixed pairs of complementary global bit patterns. Scheme zero uses all zeros against all ones. Schemes one through three alternate zero and one in blocks of one, two or three raw positions. Clear the grouped positions before inserting their local bits. All four have the same two-setting budget.

One diagnostic may observe the calibration tables for free to select a scheme separately for each group. It chooses a scheme yielding three signatures at the cheapest predicted discovery/checking length, with a stable tie order, or skips probes when none can certify. Four abstention diagnostics each keep one fixed scheme and use free knowledge only to skip it when it would fail. This controls for simply picking a better constant scheme and abstaining. The advised runs then actually execute the selected probes, read and check the evidence, do the exact fallback, build, serialize and verify the programs. Each runs its own complete, initially empty cache. Free advice is withheld from the primary evaluation. These diagnostics test achievable witness coverage and one favorable choice strategy. They are not timing optima for every possible selector.

A learned context chooser is warranted if this menu uses different schemes and its full median deployment CPU improves over both the fastest of the four fixed schemes and the fastest of their four abstention diagnostics, with at least seven of eight paired CPU wins against each. Cheaper earlier witnesses at the same coverage can count. If only knowing when to skip helps, test a skip/try learner when the fastest single-scheme abstention diagnostic beats both the fastest fixed pair and the matched never-probe method, again at least seven of eight times with a lower median. Any warranted learning then uses separately scoped training, fresh disjoint evaluation, a frozen selector, all fit costs, and the exact fallback. The clairvoyant menu and fastest abstention diagnostic are also priced against the archived generic primitive. An advantage confined to other probe methods is classified as within-probe headroom; a paid learned advantage would need to survive that primitive too on an independent reserve stream. Otherwise, when neither selector criterion passes, stop after the fixed test. Report coverage-only opportunities, actual CPU effects, and unresolved costs whichever outcome occurs.
