## The answer

The owner asked for a copier that is a pattern, not a law: a logical structure the growth makes, which on meeting a
second pattern leaves two of it. Three tickets looked for one in three ways.

1. **Built and tried on a class (ticket A): no copier.** For no registered class of second patterns, base law and
   stated choice rule does a made first pattern leave two of the second, in form, in built structure or in values.
   Under not-all-equal no made distinction is tied to another, so the values reading cannot hold from these starts.
   One case under one rule is there with the second pattern and is gone when the start is renamed.
2. **"Consume and output two" (ticket B): no case.** Where two parts of the second pattern's form do appear, descended
   from both, the second pattern alone leaves two as well: they are its own doing.
3. **By sampling (ticket C): cases, and no copier.** Sampled growths of the census do hold a made pattern after whose
   joining two parts of the second part's form are met more often than without it: 17 confirmed cases, under 8 laws
   whose takes go by parent steps. The main prediction was that there would be none. But each first-pattern form does
   it for one form of second part, so nothing is a candidate for a copier; and in every case the meeting runs
   through a start knot.

So: **no copier was found.** What sampling finds is weaker on two counts. A pattern that leaves two of one form is
not a function that copies what it is given. And a meeting through a start knot is supplied by the start, not made.
