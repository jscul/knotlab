**The smallest ambiguous present has two knots and five distinctions.** Two three-place knots share one distinction. If both were original, either can make a single-parent birth. If the second was the first child of the first, its two loose distinctions must go to joins with its siblings. Every holding and every allowed state agrees under either knot law. At **two knots and seven distinctions**, even the *same* ready birth on the *same* input receives **three fresh distinctions versus two**. [Witnesses and minimality proof](https://github.com/jscul/knotlab/blob/main/research/READ-ORIGIN-20261120-001/minimum.md)

**One bit answers “start or middle?” when both are possible.** Recovering the whole remaining list of births can take information proportional to the number of knots. One display with 4m knots hides m independent choices, each changing a different pending birth. At a known complete layer, predicting all later growth takes **zero origin bits**. Recovering which earlier layer was the start can still take logarithmically many bits. [Information proof](https://github.com/jscul/knotlab/blob/main/research/READ-ORIGIN-20261120-001/information.md)
