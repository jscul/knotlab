Davis and Putnam use existential variable elimination by resolution (https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs357/DP60.pdf). Boros, Elbassioni, Gurvich and Makino (Proposition 14 and Remark 3 in https://www.keisu.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/data/2007/METR07-35.pdf) already imply NP-hardness for an abstract switching network with each pad in two opposite positions, by their pair of directed networks with disjoint cuts. Noncrossing matching with a finite-state reader has the classical nested-word automata formulation of Alur and Madhusudan (https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~alur/nw.html). Identify precisely what the guarded stage constraints must retain at an interval endpoint.
