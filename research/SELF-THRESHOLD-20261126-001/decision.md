## What stands

1. **A threshold read from what a knot is owed gives late hearing from afar, but only as the cap of three.** Under HALF
   (no more fresh distinctions than ⌈D/2⌉ + 1, D what the knot is owed), the same six far changes are heard late in
   the census (101 receivers against five senders) as under the cap of three, and 124 in the 28 pairs the cap keeps
   linked, at rounds 13 to 23. On every pair where HALF hears, its whole record equals the cap's: there every birth it
   holds back is owed exactly four, where HALF gives three. Where HALF departs from the cap (145 census pairs, births
   owed five or more) neither hears.
2. **A threshold read from what a knot was given hears nothing.** Under PLACES (no more than p + 1, p the distinctions it
   was given), 0 far changes are heard in the census or in the 28 linked pairs, although the pairs stay linked and, on
   25 of the 28, the growth with no change is the cap's. The deciding birth is a join given three and owed four: p + 1
   gives it four, the cap three. PLACES at joins alone gives PLACES's whole record; PLACES at offshoots alone gives the
   ordinary count's.
3. **The line closes as an obstruction learned.** On every history read in it, a far change made after both patterns
   exist reaches the receiver late only where a join owed four is given three. The ordinary count never gives a join
   less than it is owed (the funding argument of the two-receptions study); a law that does is a rule added, whether
   its number is fixed or read from the demand.

**Not established.** That a self-read threshold carries a change by allocations the cap does not make: no history was
found where HALF, or the hold-back referee's binary-count ceiling, gives less than the cap and a far change is still
heard. Not-all-equal only; one join, d = 3.

**Intuition (an exact link to look for, not evidence).** Under the cap the far change sets off a burst before it is
heard: in all 124 late hearings of the linked pairs the changed growth makes 1.5 to 3.8 times the births of the
unchanged one in the round before (in 4 of the 6 census hearings). A join left short is like a site at its threshold
in a sandpile (Bak, Tang and Wiesenfeld, *Physical Review Letters* 59, 381, 1987, the literature's word "avalanche"):
a change travels far only when it tips a site already at threshold, and the ordinary count, which funds every join,
keeps every site below it. The cheapest test is direction 3.
