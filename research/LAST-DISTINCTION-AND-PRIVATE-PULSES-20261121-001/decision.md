# A clock’s last distinction and two private sender returns

The two questions concern different growth rules. The serial question asks what happens when two births can spend the very same last distinction of an indispensable clock knot. The ordinary question asks whether a continuing receiver can both register two genuine returns of a separate sender and count a change in which of its own ticks is funded. Both studies use parity and not-all-equal.

## Research position

**B282.** Yes. A birth paid by the attached knot can spend the clock’s last required distinction and permanently erase a tick.
