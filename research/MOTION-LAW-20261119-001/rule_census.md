# What independence of order selects in one-state parity updates

Strong commutation leaves several distinct quiet laws, all with bounded reach from a finite disturbance. A weaker requirement, persistence when two events are already enabled, admits genuine local propagation. In the conservative nearest-neighbour class, persistence and reflection together select only the identity rule. This classification has exactly the stated alphabet and conservation requirement.

The exhaustive test aligns the two local words. For displacement d up to the reading radius r, the joint word has 2r + d + 1 bits. If the second event changes its own bit, strong commutation requires that the first output agree before and after that change. Apply the same requirement with the roles exchanged. The enabled diamond imposes both equalities when both events are initially enabled. Then either remains enabled after its competitor and both orders reach the same two-change state. At displacements greater than r neither writes a bit the other reads. Radius two requires 64 joint inputs one slot apart and 128 two slots apart. The bitset census tests every table against these constraints, and a separate literal composition checks every radius-one table and selected radius-two tables, including the next displacement where independence is automatic. There are three idempotent maps of one bit for each fixed assignment of the surrounding bits. With three reflected surrounding assignments at radius one and ten at radius two, this independently gives 27 and 59,049.

The reach obstruction applies to every radius. On a state x, a different site i cannot change what site j's application would write if the totalized maps commute. In one composition only j writes the j bit; equality of the compositions therefore fixes that output. Thus a site initially disabled remains disabled until its own first fire, which can never occur. Quiet at zero then confines a compact seed to its initial radius-r neighbourhood for every event history. With idempotence, the initially enabled sites each fire at most once and any fair order produces the same endpoint. Multiple laws have that property, including the nontrivial local rules in the table.

## Conservation and the role of orientation

Among all 256 oriented rules, 16 conserve the adjacent-disagreement count at each event, seven also preserve every co-enabled event, three of these respect complement, and one of these respects reflection. The single rule with both symmetries is identity. It is also the only strongly commuting conservative persistent rule.

An event complements its two incident disagreement indicators. It preserves their sum exactly when the outer bits differ. Write the four possible firing words in left, own, right order as 001, 011, 100 and 110. Adjacent fires can only come from the joint words 0011, 0110, 1001 and 1100. In each, the first fire makes the other event's outer bits equal. Excluding these four pairs leaves the empty firing set, the four singleton sets, and the two sets {001, 110} and {011, 100}. Complement preserves each two-word set and exchanges the singles within them. Reflection exchanges the two-word sets and fixes none of the singleton sets. The independent local enumerator checks all 256 tables and agrees with this argument. Both the bitset filter and a direct composition on each of those 256 tables leave identity as the only persistent rule. The corresponding reflected radius-one counts are four and one. The proof by four short words applies at radius one. The radius-two conclusion is exhaustive finite classification.

Each eligible reflected word has one Boolean choice, whether to fire. Two adjacent co-enabled words are forbidden because the first event makes the other's neighbours equal. At a separation from two to the radius, the two enabled words together imply that each word after the other's fire is also enabled. The resulting Boolean implications are Horn clauses. A proposed word belongs to some rule exactly when repeatedly adding its forced words avoids every forbidden pair. At radius three, ten eligible words pass this test. Directly checking all 1,024 subsets of those ten agrees with the exhaustive count and recovers all sixteen rules. At radius four the exact count comes from variable branching with unit propagation, multiplication of disconnected constraint components, and a factor of two for each isolated free choice. No search cap was reached in either count.

Fourteen of the radius-three rules commute outright. The other two both have the enabled diamond and are idempotent. For the pair of firing words **0101100** and **1110010**, only the first can create a new fire, of the second kind, three slots away. Its reflected overlap gives the opposite direction. For the pair **1011000** and **1100101**, only the second can create a fire of the first kind, again three slots away. Exhausting the joint binary inputs gives only these arrows and their reflections. In either rule, an initial enabled event may therefore give rise to one more event type, with nothing further enabled by that type. Idempotence rules out re-enabling at the very same site by its own fire. A compact seed can affect only slots within six steps of its initial support. Thus every radius-three rule in the census has bounded reach on a compact seed, including the two that fail strong commutation. There is a separate sharp boundary for reversible site maps. Only identity is reversible through radius three in this class. The neighbours differ, and the enabling condition is unchanged by its own toggle. For every separation of one to four slots and every choice of reflection for each reading, two such guards require conflicting values at some shared slot. Thus a firing cannot share its reading range with another enabled site. It also cannot create a nearby enabled site, since its own guard remains true after the toggle. With an orientation supplied, the two nonidentity complement-equivariant rules move any disagreement one slot in their respective direction per event. ## Completed wider compact-motion window

The preliminary 250,000-visit run stopped after 334 starts. A second implementation completed the entire wider test. Among all 386,016 reflection-invariant radius-four rules simultaneously preserving each enabled event and conserving adjacent disagreements, **none admits even one translated copy of any nonempty compact binary support of span at most 12 within 16 individual firings** on the blank homogeneous chain. This includes every way of choosing among enabled changes. It covers 2,048 starts up to translation, of which 1,087 required direct expansion after reflection. It neither classifies larger supports nor rules out a later translation.

At a common absolute support, keep a prefix that is no longer and needs a subset of the permitted local firing words already implied by a second prefix. ## Carry to physical parity states

The code reads each occupied response from its own two private distinctions. Toggling a response changes that occupancy and adds an even change of four real distinctions. Other private pairs retain their values. On a homogeneous unbranched response path this lifts the overwrite calculations to actual parity states. A path's original shared distinctions record successive occupation disagreements, with zero padding at the tested ends. The literal background control checks all 128 states on seven consecutive responses and all 896 conservative persistent updates there. These preserve parity and the count of changed original shared distinctions. This carry applies to the tested unbranched code and uses its binary reference. If all translations of the parity-state space are admitted while the reference is erased, equivariance makes the enabled choices identical at every state, since translations relate every pair of states. Quiescence then forces identity. Reflection and complement were tested separately from that full reference-erasing symmetry.
