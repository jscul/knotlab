# When a reached stage can be read from pair rates alone

Use the uniform weighting on the finite allowed joint words, the unsigned not-all-equal law, and all **named** pair disagreement rates. The source and its ordinary first reached stage are physically lawful and have no implied rule at the source. A supplied class of stages is as follows. Take any number of connected parts defined entirely by three-place not-all-equal holders. They may contain arbitrarily many mutual or repeated meetings. Connect those parts and individual larger holders in a tree, one shared distinction per meeting between parts. There may be no ternary part, or a single ternary part may comprise the entire stage. Equivalently, contract each connected group of physical ternary holders to one part; between the resulting parts there must be just one fewer shared distinctions than parts, with the whole connected. All positions are observed. The class includes every single-meeting holder tree of arbitrary arities and every all-ternary stage.

**Theorem.** The pair rates of a stage determine its full named allowed relation among all stages in this class, without being given its parts, holder sizes, source size or ages. The earlier all-size full-state theorem and the supplied ordinary growth rule then determine every subsequent birth count from the initial reading. The first stage after ordinary growth is allowed to leave the structural class later. The theorem conditions the alternatives on the displayed structural promise. The finite search also tests rivals outside that promise. [Full states determine not-all-equal growth](../THREE-READERS-AND-GROWTH-20261120-001B/classification.md)

## What the uniform rates measure

For a binary name i let its sign be +1 on 0 and −1 on 1. Complementation of all physical names preserves every not-all-equal rule. Hence each sign has mean zero, and its correlation with a second sign is
\(C_{ij}=\mathbb E[s_i s_j]=1-2r_{ij}\).
All distinct pairs have full projection. Here is one check of this useful fact. Shorten by deleting one named distinction from every holder using it. Each such holder has at least two places left and at most two full two-place rules were created. The alternating colouring of remaining names gives both values at every constrained holder unless a connected part is a closed odd sequence consisting of two-place rules. Such a sequence would require at least three full two-place rules. Thus the shortened system has a word. Assigning the deleted distinction either way gives two allowed words differing at that name alone. Complementation then excludes perfect agreement and perfect opposition with any other named distinction, establishing \(|C_{ij}|<1\) and the four possible ordered pairs. [Detailed colour completion argument](../THREE-READERS-AND-GROWTH-20261120-001B/recovery_argument.md)

When two systems meet only at t, the number of allowed completions on either side is the same at t=0 as at t=1, by complementing that entire side. Consequently their uniform join has the original uniform marginal on each side, and their remaining assignments are independent given t. For a on one side and b on the other, complement symmetry gives \(\mathbb E[s_a\mid s_t]=C_{at}s_t\). Therefore
\[
C_{ab}=C_{at}C_{tb}.
\]
The same observation applies through any finite sequence of these joins. For two names of a bare q-place not-all-equal holder, all \(2^q-2\) mixed words occur. Exactly \(2^{q-1}\) differ on the pair. Its correlation is
\[
t_q=-\frac{1}{2^{q-1}-1}.
\]
In particular, this remains the pair correlation within every larger holder in the stated tree of parts, even if other parts contain cycles.

For an ordinary single-meeting tree, the correlation between a and b is the product of the corresponding \(t_q\) over the unique chain of holders between them. Multiplication of correlations for transmissions through an observed binary separator is established mathematics. The earlier project result had only ternary rules and the constant −1/3. Here the varying factors recover wide individual holders as well, and permit arbitrary internal ternary parts in the next argument. [Evans, Kenyon, Peres and Schulman, *Broadcasting on Trees and the Ising Model*](https://users.cms.caltech.edu/~schulman/Papers/evansKPS00Ising.pdf) · [Earlier ternary result](../../proofs/DISTANCE-ORDER-20261117-001A/DISTANCE-ORDER-20261117-001A-P001_betweenness/proof.md)

## Reading the parts and the wider holders

For every triple a,b,c, retain it exactly when its three pair disagreement rates sum to two. In each word a mixed triple contributes two disagreements; a constant triple contributes zero. Since every allowed word has positive weight, the retained triples are exactly those that never have one common value.

No retained triple can span a one-name join. Suppose two words or parts are being joined at the named t and the triple uses places from both sides beyond t. If t itself is in the triple, each side need only realize one pair. If t is absent, realize the two pins on the side containing two, take the resulting value of t, and realize that value and the one remaining pin on the other side. Full pair projections give these completions. Thus every triple straddling a join realizes every possible pattern. On the other hand any connected subset within a part keeps all its original projections through further joins, since either value at the joining name extends. In a tree, a triple not contained in any one part must straddle a join with a name strictly on each side. One way to see this is to remove an end part: its only common name is its attachment, so if the triple uses a name strictly in it and another strictly outside, that join suffices. Otherwise remove the end part when the triple is not contained there and continue.

A single not-all-equal holder of four or more places has full projection onto every triple. Thus the retained triples are all inside ternary parts and include every true ternary holder. Their connected groups recover the full set of names in each ternary part and distinguish the parts from each other. Their conjunction is the exact rule on that part, since it includes all its physical ternary rules and adds only rules already true. This also covers a four-place end part whose three free names, when pinned, can force its joining value: with all three pins on that side and none elsewhere, the joining value is free on the other side.

For a,b not in a common recovered ternary part, run this second test. Declare them held together if there is **no third distinct named c** with \(C_{ab}=C_{ac}C_{cb}\). If a and b are not in a common wider holder, they lie in no common part, so their unique chain through the parts has a distinct intermediate joining name t. Conditional independence proves the product equality at t, including when a correlation vanishes.

Conversely, suppose a,b belong to a common wider holder H. Their correlation there is \(t_q<0\). A third name in H gives the positive product \(t_q^2\). A c outside H belongs to a branch entering H at a single name z. When z=a or z=b, the product is \(t_q C_{zc}^2\), which differs from \(t_q\) because \(|C_{zc}|<1\). When z is a third name of H, the product is \(t_q^2 C_{zc}^2\), again nonnegative. A boundary name c itself held by H is already included in the first case. This proves the test, and the absolute-value version works as well because each extra factor has magnitude less than one. A wider holder contains at most one name of any particular ternary part, so the first exclusion discards none of its pairs.

Take maximal sets of four or more names all of whose pairs pass this test. These are precisely the physical wider holders. A holder plainly supplies all its pairs. A set of pairwise co-held names distributed over several wider holders would give a cycle between parts: pick two names together in a holder H and a third outside it, and the two pair meetings to the third would return to H by distinct attachment names. This is excluded by the tree condition. A physical holder cannot be properly contained in another full-law holder. The inferred ternary rules and the recovered wider holders therefore define the whole allowed set. No source names, newly born names or previous rosters enter this reading.

## Reading disconnected members of this class

The same conclusion holds for any finite union on disjoint names when each connected presentation satisfies the class above. Its number of connected parts and their names need not be supplied. Join pairs of names whenever their measured correlation is nonzero. Across physically disjoint parts every such correlation vanishes by complement symmetry and independence. Inside each wider holder the shared value is the strictly negative \(t_q\). Inside any physical ternary holder the three correlations sum to −1 by the ternary test. At least two are nonzero, since a single nonzero value would have to be −1 and every distinct pair has absolute correlation less than one. Those two named pairs connect its three positions. Propagating through every holder meeting proves that the connected sets of names in this observed relation are exactly the individual physical components. Apply the preceding decoder separately on those sets.

Full local projection prevents any physical holder from crossing a genuine product partition of its word set: every proper projection of its isolated rule is full, whereas the full holder excludes two constant words. Therefore the componentwise full-state reconstruction is valid even when the partition is not given. The sole named ambiguity within a component is a closed six-name presentation. It has no live holders, and any of its allowed words extends independently of all other components. Replacing it by its alternative changes neither the globally selected parent sets nor any subsequent implication check. The union's full word set therefore determines its ordinary growth counts, including the global all-or-none funding veto.

## The class occurs in actual growth

Start from one k-place not-all-equal knot for any \(k\ge3\). The first cut gives k unary children. Each shares one old distinction with the original holder and receives \(k-1\) fresh distinctions, since its k−1 siblings have that common parent and the minimum is two. The first stage has \(k^2\) names and is a tree of k-place holders. Each unordered pair of those siblings gives one next birth, and their loose supply meets that demand exactly. The next count is \(k(k-1)/2\). Literal checks included k=9, one past the full initial census.

There are also unbounded examples with cycles and repeated meetings. In one ternary part take **012, 134, 234**. Only 0 is loose; names 3 and 4 form a double meeting. A local mixed word at 012 extends by assigning 3 and 4 opposite values. A local mixed word at 134 extends by assigning 2 appropriately if 3 and 4 agree and then assigning 0 to satisfy 012. The same holds on 234. Each displayed rule has a violating assignment satisfying the other two: to violate 012 set 0,1,2 to zero and 3,4 to one; to violate 134 set 1,3,4 to zero, 2 to one, and choose 0 freely; exchange 1 and 2 for the last rule. Either value of the loose distinction extends.

Take \(m\ge1\) disjoint copies of this ternary part. Attach one wider holder to their m separate loose names and give it \(h\ge4\) additional loose names. The parts meet at one name apiece, so all local projections remain full. Each old row remains essential by extending its witness at a new loose name. Every constant word on the new wider holder can be extended when its own rule is removed, so that holder is essential too. It is the only live holder, so ordinary growth adds exactly h unary holders, each meeting it at a different name and each with \(h-1\) fresh places. This first stage has \(5m+h^2\) names, contains m genuinely cyclic ternary parts, and has next birth count \(h(h-1)/2\). Both m and h can grow. The pair reader found the exact physical presentation on the constructed examples, including three distinct cyclic parts joined by one seven-place holder. ## What a fresh distinction contributes

The proof works because a one-name join is an observed binary separator, and because the ternary rule is itself recoverable from pair rates. It works equally on a supplied seed with that structure, even at an individual holder with no loose distinction. One fresh name at a newborn suffices for recovering the physical presentation from **full words**. It does not imply the single-attachment condition of this pair reader. As an actual example, the three ternary holders 013, 024 and 125 can fund a child taking inputs from all three and just one fresh name. The reached four-place child touches the old structure at three distinct names; the stage falls outside the theorem, while its table still determines the next birth in the bounded census.

For another test, let x be private at one holder. Given all other signs, x is equally likely to have either value unless every other sign there equals s, in which case x is forced to −s. With two other signs a,b the conditional mean is \(-(a+b)/2\). At four places the conditional mean is \(-(a+b+c+abc)/4\). Correlating x with an outside r now involves the four-name moment \(\mathbb E[abcr]\). The exact control gives \(1/21=5/84-1/84\), so dropping that higher-order term gives the wrong answer. The ternary identity holds exactly in its separate control. This establishes why the private-name argument alone does not provide the pair-only reconstruction proof. It does not supply a pair of different births at an equal rate table.

A cyclic example from the advisor also tests a simpler sign rule. Put the five-place holder 01345 beside 026 and 217. All three have private distinctions. The shared names 0,1 occur together in the wider holder, yet \(C_{01}=+3/67\) on its 134 allowed words. The advisor's positive sign and magnitude are correct; the displayed system has eight names. Its four-place version has seven names, 62 words, and \(C_{01}=-1/31\). These are supplied starts, and all four numeric comparisons were checked against literal words. A sign of disagreement alone cannot identify co-holding across a cycle through a wider holder. The general pair-to-birth problem for all reached stages with such cycles remains open.
