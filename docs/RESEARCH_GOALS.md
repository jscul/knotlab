## B248 · Can the pair table distinguish loose names from spent names on a connected reached stage? (G5) · 2026-11-20 · selected

The frozen test called for one wider connected table, with age and holdings hidden. Under each of two word weightings, the 29-name table identifies twenty loose names and ten next births. ## B249 · Can a join across consecutive spent holders preserve old states, arrival and recurring release? (G18) · 2026-11-20

The paid join preserves old states and the seven- and 21-knot recurring sources. Equal marked state readings can still give different arrivals.
