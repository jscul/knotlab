# What the two readers actually experience during training

The paired versions use the same growing sources and public approaching moves. Version 0 opens a stocked three-exit feeder for the even cue on three of four training pairs and for the odd cue on one. Version 1 reverses the opening on each individual pair. The private setting determines the ordinary game's actual bite and energy receipt. It is never given to the learner. Exactly one bite in each even/odd pair pays in either version. A denied bite is a paid energy cost and can leave the ration present.

## A prospectively selected route, then a real bite

Use the first 96 distinct keys in the training fold of the new source bank, both parity and not-all-equal, the original training names, and movement tapes 0 through 63. Always pair the same founder and tape across laws where their ordinary growth agrees. The source selection never uses the result of a game. Survey every pass-only route for every potential query turn 2 through 6, including stalls and deaths. A move supplies a usable clue only when the actual zero-based outward slot `s` has its consecutive partner `s XOR 1` in the *departure* menu. Even means A, odd means B. An unpaired move clears the clue. Scored encounters require a known clue, a positive persistent feeder sensor, a previous realized move, and exactly three current controls. Growth before turn 5 is part of the ordinary game. All stock on a pass-only prefix is untouched. The public survey records the number of founders, routes, all reached decision prefixes, all eligible visits including later repeats and their clue counts.

For training nominate only the first scored encounter on each tape. A selected episode therefore contains exactly one scored encounter **even when all transit observations are counted**, and its routing history is available to the reader. Group nominations by founder, law, actual holder, decision turn, growth formation and exact current public view including energy and anonymous control menu. Within each group sort the A and B nominations by replicate and pair in order up to the smaller count. This is maximum disjoint pairing within that group; each tape is nominated at most once per founder/law. Record surplus A, surplus B, later eligible visits and founders without pairs. Names and the private ledger establish matches for the scorer. They are withheld from the reader.

Use unsigned big-endian BLAKE2s-64 with personalization `FDPTRAIN` of compact ASCII JSON `["FEEDER-PAYOFF-REVERSAL-20261121-001",domain,...parts]` for the choices below. Rank all disjoint pairs within each law by domain `rank`, founder key, decision turn, even replicate, odd replicate; break a tie lexicographically by those same fields. Use the first sixteen complete four-pair blocks or all available complete blocks if fewer. Keep the incomplete suffix and cap surplus visible. Never repeat a matched pair to fill a block. A shortage of four pairs means training did not obtain a complete block and cannot establish reversal.

Every selected pair contributes two independent resets. Each bot executes a clamped OFF at every earlier turn and one clamped ON on the scheduled query, all through its physical action bit and the ordinary movement control. The scorer verifies both routes end at their matched, previously unbitten feeder with the correct clue. Each bite costs one turn and the game returns its actual energy, including death where it occurs. A probe stops after that bite. Deployment has its separate whole-life measure. All versions and credit controls receive the same selected routes. Each block contains exactly four A and four B qualifying observations **and** four attempts of each cue. Two pairs present A first and two present B first, chosen by sorting the four slots with domain `arrival-order`, block index and pair position. A technical second presentation inverts the within-pair order on every pair; every other formation, tape, opening, exception, pair sequence and comparison stays fixed.

In each four consecutive blocks, the exceptional pair position visits each of the four positions once in the order obtained by sorting positions by domain `exception-position`, four-block group and position. The last incomplete group uses the leading positions of that permutation. For each training bite use the target-specific opening override; it never changes another feeder and it opens one of the two approaches. The usual game prepares the outcome and updates the public energy; the reader infers a paid bite from this observable change. Each version receives four actual payments and four denials per block. The ordinary and exceptional positions and the order masks are independent of law, version and private stock.

## Outcome, exposure, and re-pairing

Give the same physical apparatus and neutral learned word to each arm. Clear episode-local inputs on every reset and retain the learned word across resets. The outcome arm uses its actual public energy receipt. The count arm gives every qualifying bite one paid comparison signal irrespective of its real energy change. For the shuffled arm, rank the four pair positions by domain `shuffle-payer`, block index and pair position. Credit A as paid on the first two pair positions and B on the other two in both versions and both presentations. Each credited paid/unpaid signal comes from a genuine donor bite in its own matched pair. That donor and recipient share their exact pre-bite energy, feeder and game turn. Depending on the actual outcomes this is the identity or the exchange of the two receipts. These controls change only the comparison input. Every arm still performs the real bite and receives its true energy. The scorer retains the original receipt, its donor, the routed credit and all intermediate physical score states.

Independently fit all three arms from neutral in the second within-pair presentation. At fixed order the entire experienced public *pre-bite* routes, eligible clue sequence and action bits must agree across versions. Across opposite version **and** opposite order, the chronological pre/post energy and paid/unpaid sequences must agree, including the public energies of all transit turns. Thus one can compare two learned words when the first or last reward, its timing, every other reward in the sequence and the total food are identical. Count and constrained-shuffle arms must receive matching routed clue-and-credit streams across versions at each fixed order. Report exact trace hashes, per-law opportunity and cue margins, actual costs and diets, and final learned words, including incomplete or tied outcomes. The primary chronological presentation supplies the frozen policy for the mechanism and survival banks.

The public reader API is `PayoffReader(law)`, `begin_episode(training=True)`, `decide(obs, probe_bit=0|1)`, `after_turn(obs, bit, outgoing_slot, energy_after, credit_override=None|0|1)`, and `export_memory()`. The private game uses `ReversalGame(world, tape=..., version=..., training_exception=(actual_target,exception))`. The saved policy has `policies[law]["0"|"1"]["outcome"|"count"|"shuffled"]` containing the direct physical export, with an independently fitted second set under `alternate_order`. Timing, query selection, first-hit restriction, hidden openings, counterbalancing and the single-bite training endpoint are supplied experimental choices. Behavioral deployment never receives a training query turn, holder name, version label, stock or donor.
