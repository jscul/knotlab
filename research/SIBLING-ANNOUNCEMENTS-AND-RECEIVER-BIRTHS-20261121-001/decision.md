## Evidence and design decisions

The reached four-place source under each law supplies the four children and their shared and loose distinctions. Both studies check the actual payments. The announcement study derives a best conditional rate once its instrument is supplied. The receiver study asks which information an actual child can obtain through its own birth or inputs and which questions remain available when that happens. An accurate average guess, an error-free publicly written record and a supplied measurement on a shared name give different operational claims.

The announcement advisor required a fixed orientation, diagonal success effects and all original launches as the denominator. The receiver advisor required a revision so a candidate flag and the three original questions had to coexist at a single reached cut, unless an actual paid transport preserved both possible questions. The review records give the objection, response, exact design digest and timing for every version. Each study started as soon as its own approved freeze passed preflight. The independent reconstructions check alternative weightings, access to the true paying names, and whether the stronger sibling reading can exceed the entire ordinary class.
