# Before reading the proof

Starting from the statement, I rebuilt each rank by maximal pairwise sharing, removed families with a common witness and kept the uncovered pairs. I assigned a new identity to each child, even when two children have the same points. A sorted multiset of chain labels specifies lengths. Deleting one distinct label or retaining the multiset at an eligible core parent gives all predicted parents. Rank-zero knots have no pendant points. I allowed the last birth one extra fresh distinction when it begins a relay.

The ten constructed starts included seven successive three-knot groups, with chains at both ends, and two overlapping groups with separate chains. The first nineteen admitted start/law comparisons agreed in both readings and in parent identities, including both endings for a finished piece. The next search uses freshly drawn starts with spent witnesses for specified subsets of live knots, including extra pendant choices. A finite cutoff on the core records an untested start.

My prospective proof has three sharing cases, according to whether the common parent is shortened twice, lifted twice, or shortened and lifted. For unlike vectors, the two nodes specify both a lower parent and a candidate upper node. The hard step is ruling out an additional common neighbor mixing these families. With one vector a potential obstruction is a common unmarked core parent alongside pairwise marked parents. I have no general derivation of closure for nonempty sets of bases from the twin exclusion.

A one-parent extension must use a single chain at its own offshoot. Above rank one a core holding one base has a parent holding it by union. The offshoot has exactly open minus one pure children, paired with its siblings at the starting knot. With one chain the offshoot also has a core child. With two chains there is a second extension. At the initial one-loose parity boundary the late-views admission rule needs to be explicit. At a chain-free last birth the formula without the relay adjustment gives zero even when the actual count is one. Empty sets in the lift condition also need a first-rank convention.
