# What the feedback reader can distinguish

The five knots of the one-spare ring on the 17-knot background can carry the same local activity in two ways. Occupy its five original shared distinctions, or occupy two loose distinctions on each of the five original knots. Each knot still has even parity. On the same geometry the sixteen tables give exactly the same first knot roster, fresh counts and bond distances in these two preparations. The table sees which parents have a deviation, so it cannot tell whether that set of parents was made active by a ring. Applying the recorded decisions to a blank input reproduces each physical roster. All 144 jointly translated assignment/reference checks reproduce the original decisions. On a funded layer let \(k_v\) count occupied loose distinctions received by child \(v\). The minimum extension leaves \(k_v\bmod2\) occupied fresh distinctions there. Thus the new total equals the former total minus \(2\sum_v\lfloor k_v/2\rfloor\). Under cold pass/hot veto an occupied input always goes to a single-parent child. The total is then conserved at every complete layer. On these two preparations a round starting with enough distinctions for ordinary demand can always fund the subset of proposals that survives veto, and all newborns receive at least their next ordinary demand. The explicit histories keep two or more growing end knots, since the two background ends each retain a unary descendant with two loose distinctions. In particular the two preparations sustain respectively zero and ten occupied loose distinctions at every later size.

At size two both preparations have 61 knots and 178 distinctions. At size six the original-ring preparation has 341 knots and 1,011 distinctions. The ten-loose preparation has 621 knots and 1,590 distinctions. These are exact computed values. The latter state is an intervention on the growing end. The experiment gives no mechanism by which a ring on two spent holders automatically makes those ten occupied loose distinctions. [Joint variation and other controls](locality.md)

These are all the physical proposals on each named source at size one. All the proposals have two parents. A veto acting on either ring converts one two-parent knot into two one-parent knots, adding one birth. A pair which sees both rings can be vetoed only once. This proves the coverage formula and accounts for every difference in the table. Both rings retain their five stored values. At all five separations, with the receiver's loading fixed, toggling the other ring leaves the first count adjacent to the receiver and its radius-five bond-ball knot count unchanged. That ball contains 15 knots and 20 distinctions for a quiet receiver, or 18 knots and 28 distinctions for a loaded one. Changing a proposal in common and changing growth internal to the receiver are different tests. The all-size statement about receiver-only ancestry has been proved for the four- and eight-step preparations. [Proof](locality.md)

On the ordinary size-two bare five-ring, one allowed four-distinction response makes exactly one live knot active. All twelve off-diagonal tables change the next total number of knots or distinctions when that response is made before growth. None of the four constant tables changes those totals. Scheduling the same response after the births preserves all their parity relations and retains the quiet structure. For cold pass and hot minus, respectively 35 and 33 distinctions sit on 15 knots, and the latter stage is short of the funding for its next layer. This is an explicit failure of commutation between old-value changes and irreversible growth. The positive permutation result concerns births within a layer with a fixed input state. The choice of two loose distinctions per knot is immaterial up to a renaming preserving the original geometry. No weighting over starts or stored assignments is inferred.
