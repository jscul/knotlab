# Referee report on correlations under weightings of not-all-equal states
**Refuted. Block on the claimed independence of separated parts and on the general “only in the weak sense” conclusion. The frozen pair formula on ring-free starts and the three reported ring means hold.**

Under the equal weighting, two readings of four distinctions on **the same 14 three-place knots** agree on every corresponding one-, two- and three-distinction table. No knot holds two of the four in either reading. Yet even parity occurs in **20,126** versus **20,190** of the **38,982** allowed states. All sixteen four-distinction patterns occur in both readings. Fixing any allowed reading of any one knot still leaves both parities possible in both quartets (84/84 checks). The equality of the proper marginals applies before that conditioning. The canonical test confirms a knot at each listed three-place rule and a free relation on each quartet. Direct enumeration and independent inclusion–exclusion give the same unconditioned counts. [Four-distinction tables](joint.json) · [Independent signed counts](pair_factors.json) · [Projected relations](record.json)

The narrower independence claim already fails on a chain of six knots. Let knot $i$ hold $(2i,2i+1,2i+2)$ for $i=0,\ldots,5$. Multiply the signs of loose distinctions 0 and 12 at the two end knots, and separately those of 5 and 7 at the two middle knots. The two sets of knots share no distinction. The counts $(++,+-,-+,--)$ are **406, 324, 404, 324**. The joint mean is **1/729** and the separate means multiply to **1/6561**. The routes used by the two products share the middle knots. ## What holds

1. **Pair formula on ring-free starts (W1, frozen).** All **761/761** exact means agree on the 15 eligible starts, including all 529 pairs no knot holds together. The other 232 read two places of one knot. The census contains 7,075 connected three- to five-knot starts with at most twelve distinctions, up to two loose distinctions per knot and at most two shared by each pair of knots. On a finite ring-free start, flipping all values on a part off the route pairs its completions at opposite values of the shared sign. At a knot of $a$ places there are $2^{a-2}-1$ completions with the outgoing sign equal to the incoming one and $2^{a-2}$ with it opposite. The conditional multiplier is $c_a=-1/(2^{a-1}-1)$. Multiplying along the unique route proves W1 for arbitrary places in this class. [Census](census.json) · [Earlier mixed-arity derivation](../../STATE-DISTANCE-AND-REACHED-GROWTH-20261120-001A/tree_delays.md)
2. **Ring means (W1r, frozen).** The counts of equal and opposite values are **(26,56)** on the four-knot ring, **(62,128)** after its edit, and **(10,16)** on the three-knot ring. The means are −15/41, −33/95 and −3/13. The normalized two-route expression gives all three. Each measured pair is held by one knot. The five- and six-knot rings also pass for every pair of shared distinctions, both before and after the tested edit. [Exact ring counts](joint.json)
3. **Ring parity (W3, frozen).** Of the 82 states of the four-knot ring, **50** have even parity on its four shared distinctions and **32** have odd parity. The sign mean is 9/41. [Ring count](joint.json)
4. **Independence (W2, frozen failure).** On the comparisons the code made, **7,094/7,445** are independent. Every one of the 351 failures uses at least one disconnected knot set. With three-place knots, **252/252** inside comparisons and **1,399/1,426** exposed comparisons are independent. “Inside” means that every selected distinction has all its holders in its reader’s set. With a wider knot, the counts are **858/910** inside and **4,585/4,857** exposed. [Eligibility and repeated readings](census.json)
5. **One-round completions (unpredicted comparison).** Ten of the 15 starts grow. On those ten, **6/453** pair means retain the old route product, all six on pairs held by one knot. **0/305** retain it across two or more knots. Just **294/2,680** recorded independence tests pass, all 294 on the same five-knot start. The other five starts stall and contribute no comparisons to these denominators. [Recount](census.json) · [Growth replay](growth.json)
6. **What composition explains (argument).** On a ring-free start, every even sign product has one contribution. To find it, remove each knot in turn. Select a place of that knot if its part of the remaining start contains an odd number of the marked distinctions. This is the unique selection that uses an even number of places per knot and counts precisely the marked distinctions an odd number of times. A nonempty even selection at a knot contributes $c_a$, whatever its size, since the two forbidden constant states have sign product +1. Thus the mean is the product over the knots actually used. This matches **16,064/16,064** even sign means on the 15 starts. Two such products are independent exactly when their selections use disjoint sets of knots. At a knot used twice the local contribution to the joint mean is 1 or $c_a$ instead of $c_a^2$, strictly increasing its absolute value. In a start with rings several selections can contribute, and both the signed sum and its normalization matter. The full local rules still determine the equal weighting. In particular, one shared distinction separating the two readings suffices at every arity.” Use this for “separated parts are independent” in the [study note](../README.md) and the [goals checkpoint](../../../docs/RESEARCH_GOALS.md#L6165). Replace “Compare the completions-weighted means with the grown stage's equal-weighted means → equal, or not” in [the proposed next direction](../decision.md#position-and-next-directions) with “Test an independently specified formula on old distinctions against their exact marginal at the grown stage.” Grouping finished states by their old values guarantees equality for every old statistic at any finite stage.
