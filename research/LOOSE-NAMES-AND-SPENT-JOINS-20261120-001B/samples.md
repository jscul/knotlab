# Fresh funding, first births and common descent

The separating-name rule gives a new paid birth in **2 of 64**
fresh labelled trees under each law. One of those trees also gives
a second such birth at a reached cut. The rule restores the
queried common descendant on **all 64 deliberately funded
spent paths**. Removing one loose distinction vetoes the entire
new first cut on every paired path. The two marked state readings
still agree across 53 of those 64 paired edits. All 64 partnered
runaway preparations retain their ordinary growth.

## What counted as one observation

Each weighting supplies 64 independent start draws. The sparse
arm uses a uniform size from three through eight, a labelled
Prüfer tree and independent one-in-four surplus coins over the
three-place floor. The partnered arm uses six live knots in three
partner pairs, one spent ternary witness per nonpartner pair and
twelve independent orientations of the doubled meetings. Each
live original has four loose distinctions. The spent-path arm
uses a uniform length from two through six spent holders, with
independent live boundary widths of three or four at each holder.
A separate paired preparation removes one loose distinction from
one uniformly chosen live terminal. All 256 physical preparations
from those 192 draws passed the exact original local projection
gate under each law.

The same uniform pair of distinct live originals is queried in
both rules and both laws. In the spent-path comparison the two
queried terminals come from opposite ends of the spent sequence.
Each original marks one uniform loose name. In each paid and
shortened pair, the marks are sampled from their common private
names and kept fixed. Every positive cut follows the same two
original ancestries through actual parents.
Each age and preparation retains a separate denominator. The laws
and rule choices are paired observations of the same starts.

The receipts give two-sided marginal exact Clopper–Pearson 95%
intervals and conservative endpoint assignments for unresolved
outcomes. The denominators separate lawful origins, funded first
cuts, qualifying physical names, materialized births and the
subsets conditional on funding or eligibility. A shortage vetoes
the whole cut. An empty reached cut after a closing child is a
different outcome. A current-birth or forecast cap retains the
status and any already established funding and parent roster.
The literal budgets are four attempted cuts, 256 births and
8,192 forecast multi-parent groups.

## Why the spent paths pay, and what they cost

Write $m_i$ for the number of live terminal neighbours of the
$i$th spent holder and $b_i$ for its number of adjacent spent
holders. Its terminals each owe $m_i-1$ ordinary payments.
The rival adds exactly one group for each adjacent spent pair.
That costs each such terminal another $b_i$ current loose
distinctions. The paid preparation gives exactly $m_i-1+b_i$
loose distinctions to each terminal. Removing one at any
terminal leaves sufficient ordinary supply and creates exactly
one rival shortage, at that terminal. No original arity drops
below three. Every unchanged marked ruler in the paired
shortage control is explained by the dropped mark lying off
the designated holders' route.

At origin, ordinary groups remain within one of the $s$ live
terminal sets. Rival groups connect every neighbouring pair of
those sets, so the marked endpoints have exact co-input
separation $s-1$. After the first paid cut the rival on this
preparation becomes ordinary. Every spent candidate touching a
live newborn then belongs to the preceding funded live set.
The original live terminals have no direct meeting with one
another. From age two onward the preceding positive cohort has
no direct meeting within itself. Consequently a paid predecessor
followed by ordinary co-input descent gives exact first
arrival after $s-1$ steps. The first ordinary cut without the
additional groups leaves those same endpoint populations in
different witnessed parts forever.

## Partnered control and state readings

The partnered origins have no shared distinction between two
spent witnesses or between two live holders. At the first reached cut
the possible newly spent witnesses are the previously live
originals, which have no direct shared distinction. From
the second reached cut the same-cohort argument applies.
Thus the separating-name rule preserves the partnered
runaway at all ages. Each literal history gives 8, 10 and
24 births before the next 4,086-roster cut reaches the birth
cap. The actual fresh prices and complete parent and input
rosters agree across rules in all 64 orientations under each
law. The queried marks first share a descendant at step one
on 50/64 draws and step two on 14/64.

At the tree and spent-path origins, a single shared name
per ordinary meeting gives exact marked correlation
$\prod_j[-1/(2^{q_j-1}-1)]$, with $q_j$ the actual arities
along the ordinary holder route. The canonical parity bond
distance is $\sum_j q_j-2k-1$ over that same route of $k$
holder steps. Every off-route component has a live leaf.
Keeping the mandatory route cuts each such component once.
Including more of a component cannot reduce its cost below
one. The receipts retain exact probabilities and the symbolic
holder distance $\log_3(1/|C|)-1$.

The partnered origin uses the inherited exact state-count and
connected containing-bond methods with the frozen limits.
All 64 origin queries are exact under each law. The private
counts of four give $|1-2r|\le1/225$ and canonical bond
distance at least seven directly. The observed signed
correlations are all negative, with greatest magnitude about
$2.06\times 10^{-5}$. The actual parity bond distances range
from 13 through 19. The bound and the exact values are saved
separately. These state readings are at the queried origin.

## Verification and scope

The receipts contain 3,186 step attempts and 2,597 materialized
cuts across laws, rules and paired preparations. At 1,875 reached
attempts the archived provisional demand is rechecked against
actual next groups. Each eligible shared name is independently
checked by literal deletion and the restored connectivity is
checked after a paid use. Sixteen seeded first-cut control
comparisons verify roster and price invariance after knot and
distinction recoding. Seventy small connected-cut censuses and
22 complete allowed-word tables independently check marked
state readings. Runtime was 0.82 seconds for trees, 8.07 for
spent paths and 17.59 for partnered orientations. Peak memory
was 207,316 KiB. The receipts reproduced byte for byte with a
different Python hash seed. Independent scientific referee
pending.
