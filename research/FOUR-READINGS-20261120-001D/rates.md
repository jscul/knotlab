## Interval coverage

The lower endpoint uses the exact one-sample binomial lower limit on certified successes. The upper uses its upper limit on successes plus unresolved draws. Each tail has error probability at most 2.5%. The all-draw simultaneous limits divide that error across all 420 weighting, law, reader, event and interpretation cells. Independence between different table cells is unnecessary. Within each cell the preregistered draw units are independent with replacement. For 0/256 detections the one-sided 95% upper bound on detection is 1.16%. For 0/96 it is 3.07%. A bound for a detector does not control an unresolved existence claim. A separate deterministic proof can fix a whole-host fraction at zero or one even when the displayed design-based interval is wider.
