# An unread companion fixes its shared physical distinction

When the companion shares the only place at the second reader, the best probability of an exact signed return is **zero**. When it shares one of the two places at the first reader, a positive coherent not-all-equal source gives the sharp probability
$$q=1-\frac{\sqrt5}{3}\simeq 0.254644.$$
Either companion law gives the same optimum. Every source with even parity at the first knot, and every fully dephased preparation, has optimum zero under serial lawful access. ## 2. The unread names prevent changing the shared physical value

$$\sum_r\sqrt{\pi_r}\,K(|f_r\rangle|\psi\rangle)|e_r\rangle.$$

If the whole output is lawful, the surviving $K(f_r\otimes\psi)$ must therefore still have physical $s=r$. The same reasoning applies after each prefix of the transcript. The inference uses the specified actual coherent state only. Positivity makes every fine constituent of a lawful coarse density lawful, and lets independent randomness be averaged afterwards. Thus every final fine target map and stored classical history agrees with first dephasing the source *between* the original $r$ values, even if the actual protocol never read $s$. Within-$r$ coherence can remain useful. In particular a positive pure target after any accepted record must have exactly that same target on each of its nonzero $r$ contributions. This argument permits coupling the data before announcing success.

## 5. Why a table conditioned on the unread words gives a different answer

The conditional word tables identify the extra information an analysis can accidentally supply. With a not-all-equal companion the unread words 01 and 10 have probabilities $1/3$ each. On either, the first knot permits its entire original triple. The unread words 00 and 11 each have probability $1/6$ and fix $s$ to 1 and 0 respectively. These are mathematical probabilities in the registered preparation. No primary reader has learned which event occurred. In fact an accessible effect certifying the unequal outcome without any false positive would have to annihilate both accessible exclusive vectors $f_0,f_1$. It would also annihilate their coherent sum on the unequal outcome, so it could never fire.

If an additional outside reader were actually to measure and announce the one bit $[d\ne e]$, the positive first not-all-equal source would conditionally become its original fully usable isolated preparation on the unequal outcome. The old isolated rate $1/3$ would apply on that outcome's $2/3$ mass. On the equal outcome, which has mass $1/3$, the original sector proof still gives zero with $s=b$ or $q$ with $s=a$. Thus the two averages with that extra classical input would be $2/9$ and $(5-\sqrt5)/9$ respectively. Each is attainable with the added flag, as the reader can choose the corresponding conditional procedure. Resolving both tail bits in the ordinary word basis would leave the same bounds. A parity companion's two bits already determine the immutable $s$, and a first parity knot remains subject to its own serial obstruction. The dephased source stays a mixture of products after an ordinary tail read.
