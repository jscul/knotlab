# A lower bound independent of the constructed family

Let W be the current number of distinctions. The wired incidence representation of an open, admissible, positive-age stage is connected after deletion of any one junction. Its ordinary junctions have at least three distinctions each. The exterior has at least two incidences (a single one would be a forced distinction). Two ordinary junctions meeting the exterior are both live and share no ordinary distinction with one another, since they were born in the same preceding complete round. Thus with n wired junctions there are at most floor((2W+1)/3) junctions including the exterior. Closed or stalled stages have no later pair discrepancy.

The enumeration admits every graph satisfying these necessary conditions. In particular meeting multiplicities have no separate cutoff. It enumerates every biconnected underlying simple graph at the bound, every positive distribution of at most W distinctions over its links, and every admissible choice of the exterior. Counts include symmetric presentations more than once. For n through seven the simple supports come from the graph atlas. For n≥8 the computation uses nauty's geng with the biconnected option and the stated edge bound. The program enumerates both connected shores of every possible bond.

The complete readings through order three identify all minimum bonds at a single distinction, by the minimum-cut argument of the preceding study. They also identify every bond of size at most three and every unique minimum bond through a pair. For each allocation the program tests whether the resulting invariant collection spans the parity equations, adding the known exterior and live-junction supports if required. Multiple parallel names appear together in every cut, so representing that collection on one column per bundle gives its exact binary rank. Whenever this rank is full, every admissible marked renaming preserves all equations. For every case whose rank is still short, a separate literal calculation expands **every** distinction, enumerates every permutation preserving its pair table and fixing all live supports, filters by every triple including absent values, and checks each junction's image in the parity equation space. This stage does not assume the permutation preserves parallel bundles. The equation-transport theorem then gives every later reading.
