# How much of the origin must be supplied?

**A start/middle answer can take one bit. The full continuation can require a number of bits proportional to the number of current knots.** There is a connected, single-meeting presentation with \(4m\) knots and \(9m\) distinctions in which \(m\) independent decisions about the source remain hidden. Each changes the immutable count of a different, still-ready two-parent birth. Every current holding, including every allowed state for the chosen knot law, is identical. With the promise of a **completed** ordinary layer, forward prediction instead needs zero origin bits. These statements concern information conditional on a given presentation. They do not estimate running time. [Frozen question and weighting](preregistration.md) · [B214, reading the start back](../../docs/RESEARCH_GOALS.md)

## Exactly what an answer costs on one display

Fix the current physical presentation \(C\), the knot law, the history promise and the question. Write \(J\) for its number of knots and \(n\) for its number of distinct positions. Let \(\mathcal O(C)\) be its compatible original holder subsets. The initial question allows a lawful restart at \(C\) or a nonempty proper subset of one funded cohort. Use the subset itself as the historical answer. For the prediction question, associate to each \(O\in\mathcal O(C)\) its response \(\Gamma_C(O)\). This lists the remaining exact parent sets, their multiplicities including unaries, and the fresh count at each birth. A restart uses its own ordinary roster. Keep closure, a funding stall and an empty list distinct when the question asks for their causes. For a single marked query restrict the response to that query.

If there are \(K\) different answers with this display fixed, the least worst-case fixed-length advice is

\[
 \big\lceil\log_2 K\big\rceil .
\]

Two different answers cannot share an advice word. Conversely the possible answers can be listed and the index transmitted. For a source subset \(K=|\mathcal O(C)|\) when literal holder addresses are available. For a continuation \(K\) is the number of distinct responses, which can be smaller. In the one-cohort protocol, equality of the whole remaining roster and its immutable counts also suffices for equality of the future after that cohort. Write those same births using the same available parent slots in both interpretations. They reach the same complete physical boundary, from which the forward rule below is independent of origin. For the binary question *“was this the start?”* the price is zero when all compatible interpretations agree and one otherwise. This is about the actual history. Whether \(C\) **could** be a lawful start is a property one can check from the supplied presentation and law.

The naming convention matters here.

- An addressable reading compares the actual holders. One attached membership flag at each holder costs at most \(J\) bits and transports with the holder under every renaming. An indexed message about subsets presupposes a way to refer to those holders.
- For a wholly anonymous **orbit** answer, count compatible source subsets, or continuation menus, modulo all automorphisms of the displayed presentation, fixing any supplied marks. The same formula holds with the orbit count. A common canonical list is possible in principle by finite enumeration.
- An unlocated scalar message cannot select a particular **literal** subset or menu moved by a symmetry of the unmarked display. An equivariant decoder's output at \((C,\text{message})\) must be fixed by every automorphism of \(C\). An index can specify the orbit; producing one specific placement in that orbit needs a physical address or a corresponding mark. The same distinction applies to the entire response function if one common message must answer subsequent queries. A message conditioned on one already marked pair is a different instrument.

### Why the source subset suffices

For each old holder in \(O\), retain every distinction on its current star. A distinction meeting two holders of \(O\) was originally shared. One meeting only one original was originally loose, including a position subsequently taken by a child. Every original distinction is recovered in this way. Existing knots never acquired a new distinction at a later birth. In a proper subset of the first cohort, the new holders form an independent set of currently live holders. A nonsole funded birth has positive fresh count and none of that cohort's children have spent it yet. In particular every currently spent holder is certainly old under this one-cohort promise.

Compute the ordinary groups, funding, one-step demand and fresh counts on the recovered source. Read each already built child's parents from its shared distinctions to \(O\), compare its current fresh count with the prescription, and subtract it from the corresponding roster entry. This leaves the exact remaining menu. It gives a finite compatibility test for a proposed \(O\) after the source's allowed-state and funding gates are checked. It also gives an upper bound of one flag for each currently **live** holder under the one-cohort promise. The source flags carry no prior commitment as to which of one parent's remaining equivalent slots must be spent on a future event. Such a commitment, if demanded literally, is separate information. The holder-subset upper bound extends to deeper presentations with the following precise promise. The reference is the ordinary deterministic sequence of full cohorts, and the observed construction has built a parent-closed subset of its births with their prescribed immutable counts. The continuation may either finish the least unfinished cohort or admit every unbuilt reference event whose parents exist. This covers the established fixed-map equivalence on its own domain. A serial law that recomputes a different roster after every physical move has a different reference.

Under this promise the minimum current ordinary distance of a holder to \(O\) is its formation depth. An existing descending parent route attains that depth, and no physical meeting changes depth by more than one. Simulate the reference from the reconstructed source through one more than the largest displayed depth. Each complete layer is finite. Match the displayed holders in increasing depth by their full incoming parent sets, thereby finding their actual positions within the simulated layers. Future queries beyond the current menu can be handled by further finite simulation.

The only repeated birth with the same literal parent set in a cohort is a unary at the same parent. Its copies have the same immutable count, since permuting them preserves the whole provisional roster and its lookahead. To check the matching argument, take two embeddings of the displayed downwards-closed set in the reference that agree at the source. Having aligned them through one layer, the images at the next layer have the same exact parent sets. In each unary fiber extend the required correspondence on its displayed members to a permutation of the whole fiber. Multipart parent sets have only one birth to match. Each such relabeling extends to the later reference: it transports witnessed groups, unused-slot multiplicities, demand and every new parent set under the same rule. Induction aligns the two embeddings on the entire finite display. Thus the menu on the actual marked parents is independent of the auxiliary naming. For a finite prefix only a previously born child of a proposed parent can have consumed that parent's slot, so the comparison needs no unobserved order of firings. This proof covers structural parent incidence including already observed attachments, up to exchange of equivalent loose slots. It assumes the promised complete reference really describes the serial prefix. [Fixed-map case and its scope](../FRESH-DEMAND-20261120-001/fixed_map.md)

## A connected family with \(m\) independent hidden choices

For \(m\ge2\) take a path \(S_1,\ldots,S_m\) of spent holders. Attach \(U_i,V_i\) separately to \(S_i\), and attach a leaf \(X_i\) to \(U_i\). Give the current \(U_i\) one loose distinction, \(X_i\) two, and \(V_i\) two except at \(V_1\), which has three. All ordinary meetings are single. There are \(4m\) holders, \(4m-1\) shared distinctions and \(5m+1\) loose distinctions, hence \(n=9m\). The spent holders identify the path. The unique three-loose leaf identifies its first end. Each \(S_i\) is consequently fixed, as are its two different neighbors \(U_i,V_i\) and the leaf of \(U_i\). The holder action is rigid even though loose positions at one holder can be exchanged.

For every \(K\subseteq\{1,\ldots,m\}\), declare the \(X_i\) with \(i\in K\) to be already grown children and every other holder to be original. Its source is obtained by deleting each such \(X_i\) and its two fresh positions and restoring its link at \(U_i\) as a second loose distinction. Fix the same names and holdings in \(C\) in every comparison.

**Lawfulness under both laws.** Every candidate source is a tree with at least three places at each holder and loose distinctions at every ordinary leaf. For parity, wire loose distinctions to one auxiliary exterior. After deleting any holder, every remaining ordinary component contains a leaf of the original tree and reaches the exterior. Each singleton-holder cut therefore has both sides connected, so it is a minimal nonzero parity support and its star has exactly its intended parity projection. For not-all-equal, prescribe any nonconstant allowed assignment at one holder and work outward along the tree. At another holder its meeting with its predecessor has a prescribed value and it has at least one further position. Give one such position the opposite value and freely fill the rest. Assigned child links are the inputs to the next independent subtrees. This extends every allowed local assignment. Either local relation is nonempty, constrains the whole star and becomes free on forgetting any one position. The same proof applies to \(C\) itself.

**The actual funded first roster.** The only eligible multipart groups are the distinct pairs \(P_i=\{U_i,V_i\}\), each witnessed by \(S_i\). The other neighbors of \(S_i\) are spent. If \(X_i\) was original it meets only \(U_i\), and no other live holder has \(U_i\) as an ordinary neighbor. A candidate cannot cross from one site to another. Each \(U_i\) can pay for its pair and has one unused slot exactly when \(i\in K\). Each \(V_i\) can pay and has its other slots left over. An originally present \(X_i\) makes only unaries. The round is funded throughout. The unary through \(U_i\) for \(i\in K\) has just its sibling \(P_i\) at that parent. It receives two fresh distinctions by the unary floor under either law. Write exactly those \(|K|\) unary births in any order, taking the designated original slot now seen as the \(U_iX_i\) link. The result is exactly the literal \(C\). Every \(P_i\) is still pending. With \(K\ne\varnothing\) this is a proper nonempty partial round; with \(K=\varnothing\) it is a genuine restart. There are always other loose slots, and neither computation uses a sole-birth exception.

Let \(v_i\) denote the fixed initial loose count of \(V_i\). The provisional siblings of \(P_i\) are its \(v_i-1\) unary siblings through \(V_i\), and the one unary through \(U_i\) precisely when \(i\in K\). Unaries through one parent are held together there. Siblings through different parents have disjoint parent sets. Thus each yields one residual pair through \(P_i\) and none enters a larger unheld group with it. Its demand is at least one, so its fresh count under both laws is exactly

\[
 f(P_i)= v_i-1+\mathbf 1_{\{i\in K\}} .
\]

All these marked pairs are identified in \(C\) itself and can consume the same currently loose inputs in every comparison. Their answer vector recovers \(K\). The effect also persists after the marked birth fires. All its places are immutable. In each complete continuation there is precisely one **new** common holder of \(U_i,V_i\), namely \(P_i\). Both parents exhaust their inputs in their original first cohort, in which this pair occurs once. Its total arity \(2+f(P_i)\) therefore distinguishes the physical continuations even when this new holder subsequently spends every loose slot.

Consequently the full source and the whole continuation menu have at least \(2^m\) distinguishable answers on this one rigid \(C\). They require at least \(m=J/4=n/9\) bits even under the anonymous-orbit comparison. Within this specified family these \(m\) bits also suffice exactly. One fixed \(P_i\) alone has two possible costs in this family and needs only one bit. If \(K\) is drawn uniformly from all subsets, the conditional entropy of the menu given \(C\) is exactly \(m\) bits. That is a weighting on these explicitly built origins.

Even an accurate start-versus-middle bit, exact original size and exact number of prior births leave a linear obstruction. Promise \(|K|=\lfloor m/2\rfloor\). There are then \(\binom m{\lfloor m/2\rfloor}\) different menus at the same proper middle, each reached from exactly \(4m-|K|\) original holders and \(9m-2|K|\) original distinctions. The required number of bits is at least \(m-\log_2(m+1)\) because the central binomial coefficient is at least the average of the \(m+1\) coefficients. A pointer to a known original \(S_1\) gives no additional information in this family. To see the symmetry qualification separately, remove \(V_1\)'s extra open in the \(m=2\) case. Reflection then exchanges the two sites. The literal origins omitting \(X_1\) and omitting \(X_2\) are exchanged. An unattached scalar cannot point to which physical site has the larger pair cost, although the two decorated displays are in the same anonymous orbit.

**By-size statement.** With upper budgets of \(J\) current holders and \(n\) distinctions, take \(m=\lfloor\min(J/4,n/9)\rfloor\) when this is at least two. The flag upper bound is \(J\). On a proper partial round all current holders have at least three places and each distinction has at most two holders, so \(J_{\rm actual}\le 2n/3\) as well. The worst-case advice on bounded-size displays is thus \(\Theta(\min(J,n))\). In particular it is linear separately in knots and in distinctions when the other quantity is unrestricted. For each exact distinction count \(n\ge18\), the same lower comparison works with \(m=\lfloor n/9\rfloor\) and \(n-9m\) additional opens at \(V_1\) in all arms. They add precisely this many unary siblings to its pair in every arm and leave the \(m\) independent differences intact. The explicit sparse family has \(n=9J/4\).

One can prescribe every sufficiently large **exact** knot count as well. For \(J=4m+s\), \(m\ge3\), \(0\le s\le3\), attach one permanently original leaf with two loose distinctions to each of \(s\) different \(V_i\). Its only neighbor is \(V_i\). No other live holder meets that \(V_i\), so the added leaf has only unary births and creates no candidate pair. The source trees remain lawful and each \(U_i,V_i,X_i\) remains identifiable. This gives \(n=9m+3s\), and extra opens at \(V_1\) reach every larger exact \(n\). The lower bound is therefore at least \(\lfloor J/4\rfloor\) on each of these exact joint sizes. As a direct check, \(m=s=3\) with eight extra opens gives exactly 15 knots and 44 distinctions. The ordinary operator gave the stated marked counts and two fresh distinctions on every required unary in all 16 source-and-law cases. No claim is needed about all possible exact sizes below this range or about the law of an arbitrary sample. ## Complete boundaries separate prediction from recovery

**Forward prediction.** At a promised complete boundary, the next ordinary cohort and its fresh counts need **zero** origin bits. If the stage is open and has positive age, all its live holders were just born. Their ordinary neighbors are their actual parents, since no later child has been written. The physical common-holder tests on the live end are therefore exactly the complete operator's witness tests. The current number of loose distinctions at each live holder is its full supply. Grouping, funding, provisional demand and the law-specific implication test all give the same answer if this very presentation is taken as a new source. If the initial end is underfunded both readings stall. With no loose distinctions both have empty continuation. Repeating the argument gives the same future complete layers, up to the choice of equivalent slots. It does not recover which earlier boundary the observer actually started from.

**Supplementary mathematical result developed after the contract freeze.** Under the promise that every previous cohort was completed, recovering the historical set of original holders has sharp worst-case order \(\Theta(\log J)\) bits, also \(\Theta(\log n)\) on the sparse examples below. To prove the upper bound, start at an open positive-age boundary. All newly written holders are live and all older ones are spent. Indeed a nonclosing funded cohort gives every birth at least one fresh distinction, while consuming every prior loose distinction. The new holders form an independent set. Delete **all** live holders and their private fresh distinctions, restoring every shared input to its other holder as a loose distinction. This uniquely recovers the previous complete physical presentation, with its actual remaining names, in one operation. If this result cannot have been an allowed predecessor, stop. Otherwise the same reasoning applies there if origin at still earlier depth is possible. There are at most \(J\) possible nonempty source sets, including the choice to start at the given stage. They are nested and their holder counts strictly decrease. Every one is invariant under automorphisms of the display because the deletion procedure is intrinsic. On an open boundary the number of elapsed complete cohorts, or the exact original holder count, singles out the actual source without further advice.

An all-spent boundary requires a separate argument. Under not-all-equal every birth of a nonempty funded cohort gets fresh distinctions. Hence an all-spent presentation under that law can only have been supplied already all spent. Under connected parity, a positive step with no fresh distinctions at all has exactly one birth. Its inputs comprised every previous loose distinction, so it was the sole, implied final birth. Guess its identity among at most \(J\) holders. Removing it restores its input distinctions as loose at the old holders. For each lawful choice this gives one open predecessor, hence one of the unique backwards chains just proved. Including a possible all-spent source at the current stage gives at most \(1+J(J-1)\) literal candidate subsets. The source and knot conventions can rule out some of these guesses. The indexed or anonymous-orbit information upper bound is still \(O(\log J)\). A closed presentation's symmetries may prevent an unattached message identifying a *literal* last holder. Elapsed time in this argument counts actual completed nonempty cohorts, without assigning additional ticks at a stall or an already closed stage.

**Matching lower bound at open boundaries.** Start from a cycle of five ordinary holders, every original meeting single and every holder having two loose distinctions. Its five live candidates form another five-cycle, since two roots with a common neighbor are two positions apart on the original cycle. There are five residual pair births and no larger group. Each parent participates in two pairs. On the provisional next layer two of these pair births share a parent exactly when the two corresponding pairs meet at a member of the candidate cycle. This gives a five-cycle again. Each of its births receives exactly two fresh distinctions, and the calculation repeats forever under either law. No repeated ordinary meeting is created. After \(t\) complete cohorts,

\[
 J_t=5(t+1),\qquad n_t=15+10t .
\]

The initial stars are lawful for parity by connected deletion and for not-all-equal by their private opens. Lawfulness of an accumulated boundary as a new source can also be checked directly. Every new pair takes two members of a ten-distinction loose set. They are jointly free under parity, as the only nonempty relation supported just on the preceding loose set is its full parity. For not-all-equal, after setting these two loose distinctions aside, the remaining connected incidence has at least two unpinned positions at every ordinary holder and has loose tips elsewhere. The two-colouring lemma assigns both values at every old holder. Either pair input can then be prescribed arbitrarily, and all other births have fresh slots to complete their local constraints. Each new star consequently has its intended full projection, and old projections persist because every former allowed state extends. [Keep clause and two-colouring lemma](../NAE-GROWTH-20261119-001/nae_growth.md)

At the same literal final display, each of its \(t+1\) complete boundaries from the initial five onward is a possible original subset, by the forward restart argument. Their distinct cardinalities make them inequivalent even under arbitrary renaming. Historical recovery needs at least \(\lceil\log_2(t+1)\rceil\) bits although every one of these histories predicts exactly the same continuation. This establishes the logarithmic worst-case lower bound independently of the linear bound for a proper unfinished cohort.
