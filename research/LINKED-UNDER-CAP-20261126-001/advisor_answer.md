# The advisor's answer (26 November 2026)

To [the question](advisor_question.md), asked before the census of linked pairs was run.

**Advisor's reply**

**Most likely to fail: prediction (1), the "none to round 16" half.** It is a universal negative over about 3,200 pairs per receiver kind, extrapolated from one receiver kind against 43 far ends. Linked pairs already lasted through round 12 under "one fewer than asked". I would expect exceptions where:

- the far end is itself a switch kind, or the same kind as the receiver, so the two grow in step;
- under cap 3, some census receiver has short members that wait a long time. A waiting member keeps its distinctions, so it keeps a link alive.

Freeze the prediction per cap, and give a number for "a few".

**Prediction (2), the "unlinked, never" half, is at risk from the definition.** "Linked" reads members only, but there are three ways across that skip it:

- **Shortness.** Owe > h is read over a group formed from met sets. If one group can contain knots of both parts, a far change shifts who is short, and so when the receiver's members spend. That is a timing channel with no shared distinction.
- **A common knot that is not a member** (waiting, or spent but still held). If it later stops being short, it gives births from both sides.
- **The ordinary count.** If the count a new knot is given reads anything outside its parents, the cap passes it across.

So also read "linked through any knot" and "share a group", and freeze the prediction on the widest of the three. If the narrow reading still shows no exception, that is the stronger result.

**The "heard in most" half needs tightening.** Being linked in the round of the change is not enough: the changed member must be an ancestor of a holder of the shared distinction, and the link must last until the change arrives. Predict it for that subset and state a threshold.

**What else to read:**

- the number of shared distinctions per round;
- the round the link ends and what ends it (a member leaving, or the cap cutting);
- the delay from the change to the first differing birth;
- whether the difference is in count, timing, or only in which distinctions;
- a control: the same change in a pair that was never joined, to rule out a relabelling artifact;
- which distinctions the cap drops, which is an ordering scaffold, so rerun a sample under another order.
