# What the two birth rules supply

This synthesis indexes the three closed exploratory studies and their independent calculations. The mathematical assumptions and the limits on access are kept with the individual records.

- The [birth-sign study](../../../experiments/BIRTH-SIGN-WEIGHT-20261127-001A/BIRTH-SIGN-WEIGHT-20261127-001A-E001_closing_sign/result.md) constructs a change from what one parent leaves free, and compares all 64 answer probabilities under different readings of the resulting relative phase.
- The [joint weighting](../../../experiments/BIRTH-SIGN-WEIGHT-20261127-001B/BIRTH-SIGN-WEIGHT-20261127-001B-E001_joint_weights/results.md) assigns positive weights to a current round, exhibits dependent siblings under a fixed founder and checks the horizon sensitivity of counting all completions.
- The [two fixed questions](../../../experiments/BIRTH-SIGN-WEIGHT-20261127-001C/BIRTH-SIGN-WEIGHT-20261127-001C-E001_local_questions/result.md) give a CHSH score of 2.1078403054 under the coherent preparation. The named-word diagonal control gives zero. A listed one-bit toggle used in place of the change question gives two.
- The [independent calculations](../../BIRTH-SIGN-WEIGHT-20261127-001/review/report.md) cover both fixtures, a second horizon witness, the complete signed-permutation comparison, both dephasing controls and whole-word reading probabilities.

The decision and the evidence boundaries are in the [joint report](../../BIRTH-SIGN-WEIGHT-20261127-001/decision.md).
