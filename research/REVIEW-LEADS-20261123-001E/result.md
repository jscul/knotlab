**No ordinary continuation of a start performs any of the edits by which the stalling studies made their tables and
their signals: every one of those tables compares different starts. Inside one ordinary history three things are left
open: the fresh values, which loose distinction a knot pays to which child, and, under the one-birth-at-a-time rules,
the order of births. The second changes the named distribution of the old words under not-all-equal with every present
word weighted equally, and it is a renaming: it is not a setting (see the correction below).**

## The five checks

| Check | Result |
| --- | --- |

| R2, values never steer | 0 of 2,688 valued versions differ in births, parents or fresh counts (307,200 births compared). The injected join veto is flagged in 20 of 20 rows |
| R3, allocations | Every allocation enumerated at the first funded step of the seven-knot source (5,184 classes), the five-founder clock (32) and the smallest two-sided stall (32, and 32 at its second step). Later births and the number of allowed words never change |
| R4, parent groups | A function of who holds what alone: 0 of 168 stages not covariant under renaming, 0 of 1,152 random allocations and 0 of 168 valued versions change the groups |
| R5, serial order | Replayed: at the tenth birth two births can each take a knot's last loose distinction; one choice gives the clock its tick at birth 21, the other makes it impossible. Both laws |

## What an allocation does

| Law | Weighting | Largest change of the old distinctions' joint distribution between two allocations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parity | either | 0 |
| Not-all-equal | old words equal, divided among extensions | 0 |
| Not-all-equal | every present word equal | 0.101 on the seven-knot source, 0.162 on the clock, 0.161 and 0.164 on the stall |

The smallest example has three knots and six distinctions: A = (0, 3, 4), C = (1, 2, 5), W = (0, 1, 2). A and C have a
child together with one fresh distinction, and A has a child alone with two. A pays one of its loose distinctions 3
and 4 to the shared child and the other to its own. There are 30 old words and 138 grown words either way. The two
allocations weigh an old word by whether its distinction 3, or its distinction 4, differs from distinction 5, and the
two weightings of the old words differ by 5/23. The thread recomputed this example separately and got the same
numbers, and 0 under parity.

One more thing was measured outside the frozen definitions: an allocation at one knot changes no other single knot's
loose distinctions in distribution, and changes their joint distribution with that knot's own by up to 0.081 two
shared distinctions away.

## What this is about

R1 and R2 restate the definition of ordinary growth and a recorded proof; the replays with injected faults show that
the engine and the recorder agree with them. R3 is the finding. It is exact on the structures enumerated and says
nothing beyond them. Allocations at the second funded step of the seven-knot source and the clock were sampled, not
enumerated.

**Verdict.** The stalling studies' stop rule, that a violation with signalling is not entanglement, was applied to a
sender ordinary growth cannot be. Inside one ordinary history the values, the allocations and, under a
one-birth-at-a-time rule, the order are open. Values change no birth. An allocation changes only names. That leaves
the serial order as the one thing inside a history that changes what happens elsewhere.

## Correction on 23 November 2026

The first version of this note, and the thread's report to the owner the same day, called the payment allocation "a
setting nobody had used" that "moves joint weights at a distance". That reads too much into R3. Two loose distinctions
of one knot have the same holder and no other, and parity and not-all-equal knots do not tell their places apart, so
exchanging the two carries the old allowed words onto themselves. The two allocations give one finished structure
with two names exchanged. The measured differences (5/23 on the three-knot example, up to 0.164 elsewhere) are the size
of that exchange on the named distribution. Every count in this note stands; the reading "a setting" does not. The
present-words study found the same thing from the other side: its allocation class cannot exceed 2.
