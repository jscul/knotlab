# Once a change from afar is heard, is a second heard?

Third study after the first checkpoint of `SHORT-AGAIN-20261126-001`. A check by the thread, not a registered study;
computed; referee pending. Program: [`again.py`](again.py), [`drive_again.py`](drive_again.py); table
[`again_table.md`](again_table.md) ([`tally_again.py`](tally_again.py) on `again_runs.jsonl.gz`).

It was run straight after the jam study's full pass, as the ring line's "sent again" reading applied to every pair
of that pass in which a receiver hears a change from three steps or more (821 pairs and laws). **No expectation was
written before it was run**, and the advisor was not asked again; its point on the ring line (a second change goes at
a current member of the sender, not at a knot that has acted) was followed.

## What is read

For each pair and law: the first change heard from three steps or more (in the jam study's order) is made at the
start; after the round it is heard, one more loose distinction is set, in turn, at each of the five members of the
sender's own knots nearest (in sharings) to the receiver's own knots, and the first later round the receiver's own
births differ from the growth with the first change only is read; then a third the same way. 12 rounds or 1,500
knots, three steps by a meeting, waiting.

- **Under one fewer than asked a second change is heard** in 120 of 376 pairs under each ration: the same eight
  receivers, each from 15 senders, every one set after round 6 or 7 seven sharings from the receiver and heard in
  round 11 or 12, in a round in which the receiver has a waiting own knot.
- **A third could not be read.** It is set in round 11 with one round left; by the last round read these
  structures hold 562 to 2,366 knots and their births grow by 1.4 to 1.7 times a round, beyond what the engine can
  follow to round 20. Not run, not a negative.
- **By the time the first change is heard, the sender is further away.** Its own knots nearest the receiver are 7 to
  14 sharings from it, where they were 3 at the start: what grew between lengthens the way. Under the cap of three,
  where the switch moves two rounds a step, a second change 10 to 14 sharings away would need more rounds than were
  read.

## What this does not say

- Twelve rounds; the third change is not run; one choice of where the second change goes (the five nearest members).
- That a second change is heard says the receiver can still be reached, not that it is told anything new: what
  reaches it is again the freeing or holding of a waiting own knot.
- Scaffolds as in the jam study (the meeting, the link knots, the change, the one-fewer rations).
