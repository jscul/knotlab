# Audit of the recorded tables

The inventory has 48 printed data lines representing 63 numbered labels (1–46 and 48–64). A label can collect a family, distinct implementations or several games. By the inventory's closing tally, 18 labels go to (a), six to (b), 32 to (c), five outside H1 and two have split classifications (40 and 41). Row 13 also has a supplied reading. These are counts of labels, not counts of different tables. [Inventory](../inventory.md)

The table below records the decisive operation in each group. Kind (a) weights a common old state by the product of the two local completion counts, permitting zero. The full model has local acceptance and rejection outputs. Its conditional table of accepted outputs can violate CHSH. Kind (b) uses a joint response, including access to a remote setting or its consequences. Kind (c) uses a specified extra preparation, weighting, order or reading. The kinds can overlap. H0 concerns what decides births. H1 concerns the existence of an allowed whole in the stated substrate. An implementation and a second implementation that gives the same output table can have different kinds.
