## Why parity remains zero

For even parity, the two sibling knots read entirely for value have their two paid values determined. At the third sibling knot the paid value is the changed alternative plus a fixed parity of its other two values. The original knot then fixes the focal paid value in terms of that same alternative. Once a focal triple is specified, its parity is the focal paid value, and there is exactly one compatible choice of the changed sibling value and all four paid values. The character of the four erasure signs times the sibling change sign reduces, up to a common sign, to either +1 on every focal triple or the parity sign of that triple. These give the product preparation with three plus changes or with three minus changes. Their fixed excesses are −1/8 and −11/8 respectively.
