Setting. Nested NAE families in the usual limit (decreasing limits, exact projections of infinite subtrees), complement-closed, living. The ray definition S has 3 ports and own distinctions, and SELF calls; copies form a tree. In scope: no other self-holding definition with own distinctions, no delayed S. L(s) = least bond (minimal non-shattered set) containing a port of main and an own distinction of some copy of S at depth s reached through S calls, members anywhere. A port p of main is carried to position k of a copy c when it is the same distinction by identity along the call word. A census study (CARRY-BOUND) found, over 5,789 non-forgetting families: 5,074 bounded at every large depth (window certificates; 86% by a knot {k, x, z} at a carried position k with x own), 632 growing (after deleting coordinates no knot ever reads, the relations forget; gap argument), 83 undecided. Every family left unbounded on a residue had no carried position in a knot with an own distinction there.

Theorem R (to freeze). If at depth s some copy c holds p at position k and S's block has a knot {k, x, z}, x own, then L(s) <= 3 unless z is tied opposite to p or to x in every allowed state. Proof: {p, x, z} is non-shattered (z = p collapses to x = not p, a 2-bond); singletons are free; a 2-subset is non-shattered only as a tie; {p,z} equal or {x,z} equal forces {p,x} opposite, a 2-bond; otherwise the bond inside contains p and x. Lemma C (conditional gap, for the converse). Let T be the carried coordinates. Suppose every product M of r SELF relations is rectangular conditional on T: for each value of the T coordinates (equal at both ends), the slice is a product.
