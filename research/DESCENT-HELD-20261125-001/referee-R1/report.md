# Referee report on a count of descent held by the structure itself

**Confirmed with fixes. No Block to the study’s central conclusion.**

The study shows how a ration can write the order into a knot’s places. The restart obstruction and the fourteen-law census reproduce. The hand-off overstates the converse, and explicit counterexamples limit the claims about closing, least periods, and parity.

## What holds

**1. Restart.** Identify the two copies of a structure by a renaming that preserves every mark. A reader then gives the same answer at corresponding places. At each retained parent place the records require opposite answers, forcing one error across the pair. R1–R3 supply the legal grown occurrence. R4 supplies the conflicting start record. For other holder or taking rules the same reasoning requires a defined parent classification that actually differs. For complete rounds the next marked structure depends only on the present one. Induction gives identical continuations up to renaming, and earlier incidences persist. Literal names also agree when the input names, ordering and naming convention agree. A prefix of a round has no such continuation guarantee. [Argument and definitions](../note.md#argument-1-the-restart-lemma-argued-not-refereed)

- The two-knot exchange exists in **10/10** pairs of a ration and a lone start size, spanning seven rations. It exists in **0/30** cases with a written count. A nonclosing birth repeating its non-start parent’s pair of parent count and demand has the same place count, since both counts equal the number of parents plus the same ration.
- Among 3,348 parity-kept census starts, prefixes from all 223 smaller starts and from the 220 parity-kept sources give the **same 515 identities**. Four occur after a complete first round and 511 occur only within that round for the tested sources and laws. Allowing any subset of the first round’s births gives **1,234**. A prefix is a part of the completed round with its marks. 
- All **140** applicable restarts agree in names, marks and births over their common continuation. **130** cover two rounds, **10** cover one, and **11** have no further birth in the compared rounds. Seventeen of the 140 preserve barred marks and test continuation outside the allowed start class. Four others had ended. On the 126 deliberate hand-ins under the count forms, all 882 former parent incidences get the predicted verdict on both sides. Of those hand-ins, 83 carry barred distinctions and so are outside the registered class of starts. [Restart counts](growth_counts.json)

**2. Writing and reading the count.** Write (a) for agreement of meeting start knots, (b′) for agreement of each birth’s parents in parent steps counted in threes, (c) for preservation of each knot’s count in threes, and (d) for absence of a positive addition suppressed at closing, evaluated with the actual base count of zero. A join stays in one connected piece of the start. Induction within that piece gives each new knot its start count plus one more than the greatest of its parents’ steps, counted in threes. The two holders of a shared distinction therefore have equal counts if both are start knots, or consecutive counts in the recorded order. A parent-closed part retains each included knot’s places and marks, so the comparison survives there. Given (a), (c) and (d), the first failure of (b′) gives two different parent counts. No child count can be one more than both. For the two total-place forms, (c) is automatic, unequal start counts already misclassify one side of their shared distinction, and an unmet closing target fails at the closing birth. This proves their stated equivalence. [Argument and place rule](../note.md#argument-2-counting-places-in-threes-written-by-the-ration-argued-not-refereed)

- **20,677** eligible runs get every place right, including **2,125** without a birth. The converse applies to **1,800** of the **5,426** runs with mixed parent steps in threes. Independent regrowth reached all **1,968** mixed births in their first failing rounds. Each had different parent counts and at least one wrong parent place. Unequal counts at the start already give a wrong place in all **45,142** runs in that stratum.
- For the barred-count form under retiring, start agreement, parent-step agreement and the closing condition give the correct count at birth. A parent that has retired cannot give another distinction. A later retirement therefore changes the comparison by the difference of the two holders’ subsequent additions. In threes, at a place of x with other holder y, put e = addition(y) − addition(x). A parent is missed when e is nonzero, a child is miscalled when e is 1, and a meeting of start knots is miscalled when e is 2. Independent regrowth of **all 3,372** eligible runs compared the predicted and actual error at **4,072,730** shared places counted across snapshots. They agreed throughout. In 618 runs two meeting start knots retired. No eligible run exercised a retired parent meeting its retired child. [Replayed condition counts](condition_counts.json)
- Deleting the additional barred distinctions preserves usable loose distinctions, meetings, shortness, groups, and parents. This couples births through the base law’s closing round under parity and throughout the shared bounded run under not-all-equal. **616/616** comparisons agree to that boundary. Of **48** comparisons with a base closure, **22** have a subsequent birth and **26** have none. The usable form agrees with the base birth lists in **28/308** comparisons. Under the two barred forms, **68,505** and **84,437** of **110,052** new knots apiece receive a positive addition. These shares weight each new knot once. [Named runs](named_counts.json)

## How it was checked

The referee scripts use their own growth and analysis code. The census uses the declared atlas, and the independent rounds construct groups, births, demand, fresh counts and barred marks from the frozen definitions. The original third count **did** reuse the earlier complete round. I compared all seven of its counted identity sets to the per-start records. Independent growth matched the retained fields on all 1,232 named runs, every start through four knots, 50 five-knot starts sampled with seed 223515, and the first 60 of the registered six-knot sample. On the original 20-pair gate the archive retains round totals and equality flags. Its original intermediate labelled structures cannot be compared entry by entry.
