## Start 11 also closes

The exactly reconstructed age-eight roster has 58,894 children. Sixteen explicit children are compatible with every other age-eight junction. Every maximal compatible set must include all sixteen. Their age-seven parent sets have just one shared closed-neighborhood class, numbered 637 in the quotient. Thus every maximal set that an age-eight block could hold must be held by the block formed from that class. The exhaustive extension check finds age-eight child 74 outside this block and compatible with every junction in it. It follows that every maximal compatible set at age eight is unheld. There are therefore zero residual pairs, and the previous group-only rounds exclude offshoots. Every actual age-nine child contains the sixteen universally compatible age-eight parents. The whole age-nine roster is a single maximal block and closes by the subset law. If M is its size, the last birth is at age 9 + M − 1 and the first empty round is at age 9 + M. The replay finds eight disjoint incompatible pairs at age eight with every cross-pair compatible. Each of the 256 ways to choose one junction from each pair extends to an unheld maximal group. Two selections that disagree on a pair cannot extend to the same group. Thus M ≥ 256, the last birth is at least age 264 and the first empty round is at least 265. The exact value of M remains open. The full incompatibility graph has 348 isolated universally compatible junctions and one nontrivial component of size 58,546, so component factorization alone leaves the large count together.
