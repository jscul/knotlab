# What can separate, and when a direct meeting is possible

Take the whole present at a funded size. Put two loose knots in the same connected part if there is a chain of loose knots between them whose consecutive members have a common holder. For the complete growing end this uses the real current witnesses, including spent knots, and the positive loose counts. The two knot laws give the same candidate groups. Every two knots with a common witness are either parents of a two-parent birth or both belong to a larger proposed group. Each funded parent spends all its loose distinctions, using one-parent births for the surplus. Every birth has its parents in one part. Form the incidence between the parents and their children. Within each old part this incidence is connected, and distinct parts have disjoint incidence. At the next size siblings have their old parent as witness. Their only possible witnesses in the stage at that size are their old parents. Consequently the new parts correspond bijectively to the old parts on each funded continuing step. A shortfall can cancel the whole step. A sole implied parity birth can leave the whole end spent. Neither exception creates a new part. This gives the pre-existing whole-part obstruction a direct parentage proof. [Ordinary growth](../ORDINARY-DYNAMICS-20261120-001/lineage_proofs.md)

Consider two different finite packets released at the same size. Their later continuations are disjoint, and every parent of each continuing member is in the same packet. For a direct child of both packets there must be a common witness for the two proposed parents. At positive sizes it must be an actual immediate parent of both. At the first step after release their parents may still overlap in the source. After one intervening generation of disjoint all-own parentage they cannot overlap. Thus the only possible positive delay for a *first direct birth from the two pure packets* is exactly one parent step. Previously released autonomous objects cannot acquire a new direct contact after an arbitrarily long independent run under this reading. Mixed descendants may take part in further events at every later size. It does not follow that pure rates or values change. [Sharp example](meeting.md)

This argument concerns complete cuts through the ordinary fixed parent structure. Every fair delivery respecting that structure realizes its finite parent ideals. An opposite writing order within a planned cohort also preserves its incidence up to renaming. A procedure that recalculates after every single birth can create different structures. On the separately screened large-growth archive, opposite orders produced different numbers of distinctions by the fourth event for every one of the fourteen qualifying starts under both policies and both knot laws. An observed raw serial reunion is therefore a result about that rule. [Serial comparisons and qualification](explosive.md)
