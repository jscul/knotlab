Cold review, about 400 words: soundness of a lemma, flaws in a design, and whether the predictions are informative. Be concrete.

Setting (as in your earlier review, attached below in brief): nested NAE families in the usual limit, complement-closed; the ray definition S has SELF calls; copies form a tree. L(s): the least bond (minimal non-shattered set) containing a port of main and an own distinction of some copy of S at depth s reached from main through S calls, members anywhere in the infinite pattern. Population: about 5,700 census families whose first-ray bond provably grows but which do not forget (strata I identity cycle, 5,622; T tie, 5; O order, 91).

New design, replacing the earlier steps (1) and (2):

Lemma W (window certificate). For an S-call word w of length s, let c be the copy at its end and J_w the joint relation of main's ports with c's ports in the pattern with c's subtree removed: J_w = Start . R_{w1} . ... . R_{ws}, where R_i is the projection of S's block, with every other call replaced by its usual-limit relation and the chosen call's subtree removed, onto (parent ports, chosen call's arguments). Track also which ports of c are the same distinction as which ports of main (identity along w), and merge them. Claim: the projection of the whole pattern onto main's ports together with all distinctions of c's block is exactly {(t, b) : (t, b|ports) in J_w, b satisfies c's knots and its children's usual-limit relations}. Hence every bond found in this window is a bond of the pattern, and U(s) = min over words of length s of the least window bond is an upper bound on L(s). (J_w, identity map) takes finitely many values and its length sets repeat, so U is eventually periodic and computed exactly. Theorem B and your widened B become readings inside the window.

Negative control: run the window on 300 families that are certified to forget (every r-product rectangular, so the gap argument proves L(s) >= 2 + floor(s/r)). A window bound at every large depth there is a bug.

Growth side: the gap argument on a live quotient. Remove port coordinates whose distinction lies in no constraint anywhere (no bond contains a free distinction), and test forgetting on the reduced relations.

Predictions (blind): P1, the window bounds L at every large depth in at least 80% of I by weight. P2, O's window-bounded share is at least 20 points below I's (O's share and Wilson interval reported alone). P3, among window-bounded I, the bound is at most 3 in at least 90%. P4, all 5 T families are window-bounded. P5, the live-quotient growth certificate covers at most 5% of the population. Undecided (caps) counts as not bounded; a sensitivity excluding it is reported.

Questions. Is Lemma W exact, including when two ports of c are the same distinction, when main is a wrapper, and with capped length sets? Is "the chosen call's subtree removed" the right definition of R_i for exactness, or must c's own usual-limit relation be left out of J_w? Is the live quotient sound, and is there a stronger sound growth argument for non-forgetting families (for instance through the idempotents' conserved labels or orders)?
