## What stands

1. **Dropping the whole does what the owner's picture says, and no more.** Read part by part (each part weighted by the
   states it allows), the three-reader and square patterns give certainty where a list of values cannot, every part
   allowed and the parts agreeing; but two readers reach CHSH 4, the no-signalling maximum. Part counting alone holds
   nothing below 4.
2. **Growth's smallest closure carries the three-reader contradiction** (the advisor's fixture). A ring of three knots
   closes in one ordinary round onto four knots on six distinctions, every two sharing one; with the closing sign odd, all
   64 probabilities equal the three-reader target. Growth closes it even.
3. **The even closing sign is assumed, not entailed.** The closing sign is exactly the cost of reordering the closing sum
   (−1 for each pair that does not commute and interleaves, checked at all 115 stored operator witnesses and the fixture).
   With all values commuting the cost is zero and the sign even: that is A295, the assumption beneath A126.
4. **Rule R (values commute exactly when some knot holds both) forces the odd sign** at the four-knot closure, where it
   is the literature's three-reader reading, and at 12 of the 121 closed patterns growth reaches (one vote per type); it
   makes every knot definite at only 21 of 173. All eight frozen predictions held.
5. **R is not native.** It changes at a birth: before the closing birth the three loose distinctions are pairwise
   non-commuting under R, and one knot holding three such values has no parity. R can be read only off the finished
   pattern, so a whole returns in another form: the finished pattern decides which values commute.
6. **The bound needs a whole of another kind.** The overshoot to 4 is marked by the absence of any state over values that
   pairwise commute or anticommute (R3), not by R1; part counting must include products of knots inside a part (32 words
   against the state's 16 on two four-knot closures sharing a knot).
7. **The fold link:** "the closing sign is the cost of reordering" holds exactly; the step to time as a fold is verbal
   (nothing in the model orders the sum, and where R1 holds the sign does not depend on the order).

**Not shown.** That growth can make an odd closing; any commutation rule native to growth; part counting with R against
a quantum state for every context; anything past seven knots beyond the named controls.
