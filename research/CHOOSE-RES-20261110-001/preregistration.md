# Pre-registration: choosing the resolution

Epic `CHOOSE-RES-20261110-001` (backlog B62, G15), written on 2026-11-10 before any run, at the coordinator's go-ahead.

## The question

A class-forced pair on a torus can be resolved into any of the three other classes (`LEARN-HANDLES-20261109-001`): three different
resolutions, read here as the "action or resolution" of the owner's picture. Learning without a target mostly took the short way around.
Given a target class, can a learner that sees only the lines at a hole reach it, and what does choosing cost?

## Spaces and starts

- The 19 thin tori torus(N, 1, 1), N = 4 to 40 even, and the two Fano tori, with the first 50 starts of `LEARN-HANDLES-20261109-001A`
  check 5 on each (as regenerated by `NATIVE-TARGET-20261110-001A`).
- The grown tori of `GROW-HANDLE-20261110-001A` grown at the dissonance (K_2 to K_4, every line orbit, both chiralities, the three
  bijections that give a torus: 60 spaces), each from its extended state with a seeded split.

## The rules

- **C7, target class.** The target class c is supplied from outside, as A26 allows a target. The learner keeps the class its own sign
  changes have made so far (the sum, over the sides it re-signed, of each side's contribution to the two class functionals; at the end
  this is the class of its whole change). It moves the first hole into the over-agreeing face w0 by the outside graph distance, as R7
  did, and the second by the distance, in the four-sheeted cover that the class functionals define, to the copy of w0 whose sheet would
  give class c; never straight back unless forced. Budget 50 V².
- **Without a target.** R0 (random) and R3 (sight of radius 3) of `NATIVE-TARGET-20261110-001`, read for the class they reach.
- **The cheapest walk** into each class, as in `LEARN-HANDLES-20261109-001A` (a cheapest perfect matching of that class), for comparison.

## Expectations, frozen

- **Q1 (the price of choosing; proved).** Any resolution into class c changes at least LB(c) signs, the size of the smallest cycle of
  the torus in the class change (`LEARN-HANDLES-20261109-001` theorem 3.2). On torus(N, 1, 1) that is 2 for the class across the short
  circumference and about N / 2 for the other two, so choosing a long resolution costs a number of steps that grows with the torus.
- **Q2 (C7 chooses; computed).** C7 reaches every target class from every start, within twice the cheapest walk into that class on
  average.
- **Q3 (the default; computed).** Without a target, R0 and R3 resolve mostly into the short class: more than 80% of runs on every thin
  torus of at least 20 points.
- **Q4 (grown tori; computed).** On the grown tori both cycles of the class change lie in the small atom, so every class is cheap: C7
  reaches every class from every start in at most 10 sign changes.

## What would count against them

A target class C7 misses (it resolves into another, or not at all) counts against Q2; R0 or R3 landing in a long class in a fifth or
more of the runs on some thin torus of at least 20 points counts against Q3; a grown-torus class C7 needs more than 10 sign changes for
counts against Q4.
