## Challenged assumptions

The decision note records five workspace assumptions this epic challenges:
- that correlated polarities suffice for a three-literal twist to matter;
- that four-place not-all-equal with a visible spoke is the natural three-literal world;
- that three-literal contradictions have no counterpart of the two-literal topology;
- that the twisted sector is the untwisted one plus a seam;
- that twisted not-all-equal colourings exist on every small triangular torus.
