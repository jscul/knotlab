# Transferring a once-loaded ring state to the growing end

Start on the bare five-ring and the same one-spare five-ring attached to the 17-knot two-dimensional background as in the state-feedback study. Also check the isolated one-spare and other singly attached rings. The once-only preparation toggles the five original shared distinctions and keeps *every loose distinction at reference*. No further external loading is permitted. A111 supplies the transported reference. A114's ordinary rule passes cold proposals and vetoes hot multi-parent joins. Its replacement unaries are ordinary growth actions. The new question concerns a **direct** effect in layer two attributable to state *actually on the new growing end*.

## Candidate laws and minimality

Freeze the same structural proposals, funding and counts, preserve old values and every knot's parity, and read only the current parent and the newly forming child's inputs and fresh count. Use the preceding minimum A113 assignment on quiet or odd inputs. On a hot unary with an even spent input (hence the spent bit is zero), compare the following:
- Leave every fresh distinction quiet, as in A113. A one-occupied-fresh candidate is a parity control.
- If the child has exactly two fresh distinctions, occupy both during that birth. Otherwise take the original extension. This variant requires no choice among a symmetric set of more than two fresh distinctions for its extra even signal.
- When there are at least two fresh, occupy any unordered pair during that birth. All tied choices must give the same physical growth up to a renaming preserving the old coloured arrangement. Distinguish that convention from fixing every old or fresh name.
- Compare performing the extra pair as a *separate* optional or delayed event with no veto on the next growth until it has fired. Under every maximal order, including fair orders where consumption disables a pending event, this may fail. A fused birth is a supplied causal dependency. The existing complete-layer joining law still defines the available group roster; a global tick or an external destination is absent. State precisely which unordered serializations are proved to agree and which independently enabled updates can conflict. If a genuinely order-free global-state version fails, give the actual bad ordering.

Report least in units of the number of occupied fresh distinctions in one modified knot, number of altered transfer cases, reading range and, separately, total physical writes for a symmetry-respecting deterministic family on a named ring. A locally active parent must receive no information about a source label, an age or an external destination. A law that reads one holder beyond a live parent can inspect an old exhausted ring with zero transfers; include that comparison and label what it reads. We make no universal ranking across inequivalent rule signatures.

## Design, predictions and controls

1. Record actual first-layer incidence, fresh values and parity. At that exact same physical incidence, erase *only* the extra two-fresh deposits while retaining the original ring and all variables. This erasure is allowed on each child. Under the identical structural reader compare layer-two permitted groups, knot births and distinctions with/without the deposited signal. Also score the quiet source and the ordinary minimum-lift loaded source. A difference wholly due to the first layer's geometry cannot satisfy this test.
2. Under preserved old values and A113, a quiet input at every loose distinction gives no occupied fresh output. Under the same reader a child cannot carry a new even signal on a singleton. Two is therefore the predicted sharp per-child write cost if an atomic parity-preserving lift is admitted. Predict for the all-eligible variant that each old hot parent with L open contributions makes L active single-parent children and the extra next births on the same incidence equal the number of their unordered sibling pairs. For the exact-two guard retain only those eligible under the funding floor. Design-stage calculations predict bare five-ring +5 under either, and the one-spare attached ring +4 for exactly two fresh, +22 for the more permissive rule. Test all counts without hard-coding them into the growth implementation. For the named bare ring, a symmetry-respecting simultaneous rule can need twenty occupied new distinctions across ten unary children even though an individual permitted transfer needs two.
3. Check arbitrary old input allocation and fresh-name ties. Exhaust local hot unary cases, compare representatives and order changes directly. Count birth orders analytically where the full enumeration is too large. For a small stage, exhaust serial orders or use a quotient with a proved all-order argument. Verify conditional parity extension, its old-state projection, and K1–K3 for child supports. Keep globally strict pointwise identity distinct from a representative agreed up to a renaming. Test a separate-transfer bad order on the bare ring and a least-response comparison at fixed incidence. Working prediction: fusion permits every event order defined by the complete birth law, but independent unconstrained growth and deposit cannot be guaranteed merely by fairness. Try to state a purely local readiness condition or give its missing prerequisite.
4. Test a few further layers on a properly funded small sample, with finite caps 6,000 knots/32,000 distinctions and up to six completed layers unless an all-size proof settles persistence sooner. A successful two-layer claim requires a direct physical veto on the held-fixed comparison. Evaluate whether subsequent activity is inherited, refreshed or sourced by an added rule. Weights are one per named source and preparation, one per permitted local lift or physical order when counted. There is no weighting on all possible growths. The adviser suggested holding the erase control literally fixed and separating transfer cost from a global scheduling assumption.
