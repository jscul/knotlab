**Confirmed with fixes. Block on the claim that the other 1,025 census closures need a selection.**

The 1,028 odd **minimal** sets and their two kinds reproduce. Minimality is tested against subsets of *either* sign. An even minimal subset can therefore disqualify an odd set from the minimal list. ## What holds

1. **Hand items 1–2.** Cancelling two occurrences of each distinction gives one sign change for each alternating failing pair. Exchanging adjacent knots toggles exactly the cells using those knots as a cross pair. Every pair can be put next to each other, so even coverage is necessary and sufficient for order independence. For knots *u,v*, multiply the numbers of meetings each has away from the other, then subtract the products for each common third holder. This gives the **integer** coverage, including multiplicities. The formula matched at all 31,994 pairs in the frozen abstract range including isolated knots, all 77,403 pairs of the complete population rows, and all 345,688 pairs in the eight-knot extension. Word cancellation also matched alternation on every choice and order for the four-knot and two-against-three fixtures, 7,872 comparisons in those two fixtures alone. [Hand proofs](hand_arguments.md) · [Direct word checks](hand_counts.json)
2. **Hand items 3–4 and G.** Four mutually meeting knots have three cells. The literature's K₂,₃, two knots each meeting the same three others, has six. Both are minimal odd. The ring of four and the ring of five with an additional knot meeting all five have two and twenty cells respectively, with no qualifying subset. The ring of three with a separate meeting and the doubled meeting with a separate meeting are minimal even with three and two cells. The literature's Petersen graph is minimal odd with 75 cells and 25 interleavings in the displayed order. An order with zero interleavings proves the literature's outerplanar case even. The four-knot closure from census start 17 has one minimal set, odd. The ring-of-five closure from census start 5088 has none. The natural rule keeps a state at **145** of **1,713** round-one census closures and closes **53** odd. [Proofs, including Petersen minimality](hand_arguments.md) · [Abstract fixtures](abstract_counts.json) · [Birth counts by round](birth.json)
3. **P1.** With one vote per full pattern up to relabelling, 36 classes are minimal. Four are odd. The 32 even classes comprise the ring of three with a separate meeting and 31 four-knot patterns with doubled meetings. The three simple odd classes are the four mutually meeting knots and the two-against-three pattern with zero or one inert meeting. Its variant with an inert doubled meeting gives the fourth. Independently extending through **all 12,346 simple patterns on exactly eight knots** gave 92 whole-choice sets, 49 odd, **zero minimal**. Literal cell kinds still depend on multiplicities. [Class counts, independent enumeration and reduction](abstract_notes.md)

## How it was checked

All scripts below belong to this referee check. The growth replays call only the archived start decoder, sampling and growth interfaces allowed in the brief. The abstract enumeration generates patterns independently and then compares them with the atlas and archived outputs. The main population replay groups cells by their four holders and sums their cross-pair parities over every subset. A second census calculation constructs eligible pairs at decision time and scans literal subsets. The counterexample was also checked by direct word cancellation. The earlier reader study supplies only the comparison for whether *another* choice splits. Run sequentially from the repository root. The last command checks the two independent census computations and produces the reconciled counts.
