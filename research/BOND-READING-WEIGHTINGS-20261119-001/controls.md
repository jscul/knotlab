The loose count at a junction is the excess of three over its ordinary degree, with a floor at zero. The augmented weighting draws one extra non-tree meeting in both small populations. A resulting unicyclic labelled meeting pattern has one history per link of its unique cycle. The recursive weighting draws a previous parent at each insertion and then uniformly renames all holders. ## What the independent reader checks

On each nonterminal representative, enumerating support-minimal binary sums of the junction equations gives precisely the same literal bonds as enumerating cuts whose two parts are connected in the classical holder graph. The reader records minimum cardinalities, including absent bonds. For orders 0 and 1 it uses adjacent exchanges within each class of currently interchangeable movable names. For higher orders it constructs a stabilizer one held name at a time. For each still-possible image of the next name, it searches for one full table-preserving permutation with that image. These witnesses and the stabilizer fixing the next name generate the whole group. Each accepted generator is tested on the equation span. If a lawful successor exists, preservation of the entire current span is equivalent to carrying all strict-future readings of arbitrary order. A closed or demand-stalled stage has no strict future, giving order 0. [Transport equivalence](../BOND-READING-SCALE-20261119-001/limiting-reading.md)

The archived five-junction, eleven-distinction example has strict-future order exactly **3**. Independent direct enumeration of all its movable permutations gives group sizes 120, 120, 120 and 8 at orders 0 through 3, matching the stabilizer generator closures. Its recorded permutation preserves all 55 pair entries and changes the span. On the thirteen-junction, 91-distinction control, the recorded span-changing permutation preserves all 4,095 pairs and all 121,485 triples, including 1,117 absent triples. The audit establishes a lower bound of 4 for that control. Both remain able to complete a next round. [Prior literal witnesses](../BOND-READING-SCALE-20261119-001/lower.md)

## Independent audit on the sampled stage stream

For sampled stages with at most 15 ordinary junctions and 48 distinctions, the control takes the smallest draw-key hash in each available weighting, initial size, age and next-round status. It independently rebuilds the starting supports, checks the actual lineage, checks the successor status, and measures the exact strict-future order. The 17 selected rows cover 15 distinct support fingerprints. Fifteen rows have exact order 2, including a case with an available closing round. The other two are already closed and have order 0.
