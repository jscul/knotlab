# How the two founder weightings were counted

A founder is counted by labelled meetings between its \(n\) original knots and one binary flag at each. Each flag independently has probability \(1/4\) of giving an extra loose name. For a particular set of \(k\) flags the numerator is \(3^{n-k}\) over \(4^n\). The requirements computed from the holder degrees apply after the added meeting.

A labelled resulting founder with ring length \(l\) can have arisen through exactly \(l\) labelled tree-and-edit histories. Removing any meeting on the ring gives a connected tree. Removing any other meeting disconnects a whole attached branch. A decorated class with \(a\) symmetries preserving its meetings and flags thus has \(n!/a\) distinct labelled representatives and \(l n!/a\) ordered founder histories. Multiply by \(3^{n-k}\) for their mass numerators. Each choice of sizes six, seven and eight has probability \(1/3\). The subsequent permutation of distinction names contributes conditional mass one. Permuting the unspent names of the same holder before assigning them to children carries every funded allocation to any other, and equal-parent unary children can be bijectively matched. The result preserves grants and the whole physical stage up to relabelling. There are three independent checks on the enumeration.

1. Starting at every unlabelled tree, add each missing pair, identify identical meeting patterns by exact isomorphism, and enumerate the action of every symmetry on the flag subsets. The number fixing a chosen subset gives its decorated symmetry count. The native engine then grows each founder under each supplied law. At every paid cut, the independent witnessed-end calculation recovers the complete parent roster, next demand and price. A calculation on the old even-parity equations checks each proposed zero grant. To see the labelled formula, first choose the \(l\) ring members and one of their \((l-1)!/2\) unoriented cyclic orders. For \(l<n\), the rest is a rooted forest leading to these \(l\) members. A forest has a word of \(n-l\) recorded parents, the last a chosen ring member. Remove the least outside leaf at every step and record its parent. Given a word, repeatedly remove the least remaining nonroot absent from its unconsumed suffix and join it to the next recorded parent. Such a nonroot exists because the last letter is a root. This pairs all forests with \(l\,n^{n-l-1}\) words. With \(l=n\) the empty forest has one choice. Thus the exact count \(U_{n,l}\) is
\[
 \binom nl\frac{(l-1)!}{2}
 \begin{cases}
 l\,n^{n-l-1}, & l<n,\\
 1,& l=n.
 \end{cases}
\]
Summing \(U_{n,l}\) gives distinct resulting labelled sources. Summing \(lU_{n,l}\) gives exactly \(n^{n-2}(\binom n2-n+1)\) labelled tree-then-edit histories. The corresponding mass denominators multiply these numbers by \(4^n\).
