Cold design review before one study is frozen. Give (1) the strongest objections, (2) concrete changes, (3) anything the predictions get wrong by plain reasoning, (4) a cheaper or sharper test of the same question, if one exists. At most 350 words. Do not praise.

Setting. Structures: two-valued distinctions; knots hold 3 or more of them (even parity, odd parity, or not-all-equal). Ordinary growth, per round: a knot is live if it holds a loose distinction (held by it alone); live knots that some one knot meets form groups by a fixed local rule; each live knot owes one loose distinction to each group containing it; if a knot owes more than it has, the whole round stalls; each group gives one birth taking one loose distinction from each parent; every remaining loose distinction gives a one-parent birth; a birth with p parents gets max(D, 3 - p, 1) fresh distinctions, D being a one-round lookahead of what the next round will ask of it. No distinction ever gets a third holder. Births never depend on values.

Bell-type reading (all supplied): Alice has x1,x2,x3, Bob y1,y2,y3; setting a adds a not-all-equal knot on x1, x(2+a) and a fresh distinction, likewise b; every allowed word of the finished structure counts equally; "hidden excess" = CHSH above 2 with each side's complete record independent of the far setting, exact arithmetic. A census found 292 small structures with a hidden excess. "Parts": connected sub-collections of their knots holding all six observer distinctions.

Just established (three studies, referee pending).
- A birth with a fresh distinction forbids no old word; only a not-all-equal birth with two or more inputs re-weights old words. So growth supplies weight and no tie.
- Ordinary growth lifts no part with at most two holders per distinction to a hidden excess (12,147 parts).
- Rule R (a knot short of loose distinctions pays with a shared one) acts only at round 1, never in the recorded sources, and lifts no two-holder part: the parts it repairs are far below 2 and the parts at exactly 2 have no short knot.
- One added not-all-equal knot with one fresh distinction lifts 27 of the 51 two-holder parts that sit at exactly 2 (hidden); every working knot takes one loose distinction and one to three shared ones, never loose ones only (0 of 2.05 million sets); the least takes a knot's loose distinction and one of the same knot's shared distinctions. All 27 parts have one live knot with two loose distinctions, so ordinary growth gives it two one-parent births.
- Seen in exploration on ten of the 27: if each of the two one-parent births also takes one shared distinction of the parent and gets one fresh distinction, the part reaches 55/27 or 109/54 (hidden) when exactly one of the two births is not-all-equal and the other parity; both parity: 2; both not-all-equal: 2 in 30 of 40 cases.

Proposed study. Rule Q: ordinary growth, except that a one-parent birth takes, with its parent's loose distinction, one distinction its parent shares (read at the start of the round; it gains a holder); a birth then gets max(D, 3 - inputs, 1) fresh distinctions, so such a birth gets max(D, 1). Shortage stalls the round as in ordinary growth. Under parity a birth gets no fresh distinction only when the parity of what it takes is implied by the old knots. Which shared distinction: convention O (oldest), N (newest), or every choice.
Tests. (a) The seven recorded sources (structures known to keep producing; one is a bounded clock making five births every step), both laws, ordinary vs Q-O vs Q-N, 16 rounds or 20,000 knots: outcome, births per round, growth kind, most holders of a distinction per round, first step failing a planarity reading. (b) 1,024 sparse random starts per law (744 stall at once; 280 funded): the same readings; whether a funded start stalls later. (c) Every old word extends, by enumeration on small stages. (d) Census: the 12,073 two-holder parts with no hidden excess (6,312 stall at once and make no birth), under Q for 3 rounds or 20 distinctions, O and N plus every round-1 choice (at most 64, else 62 drawn); each newborn even, odd or not-all-equal (all assignments up to 4,096, else drawn); hidden excess at each stage; a gain is "through a re-read" if it is lost when every re-reading birth is made even parity. (e) For the 27 liftable parts: whether some round-1 choice and law assignment gains.
Predictions. G: code checks (with re-reading off the engine equals the recorded one; tables equal word-by-word enumeration on drawn stages; allowed observer words never change; a stage-1 table with exactly one not-all-equal re-reading birth with one fresh distinction and all other newborns parity equals the added-knot table for the same set). P1: some two-holder part gains at round 1, through a re-read. P2: every part that gains at any stage sits at exactly 2 (hidden) at stage 0. P3: at least 20 of the 27 liftable parts gain. P4: no history stalls after round 1 under Q. P5: the bounded clock is not bounded under Q. P6: under convention O some distinction reaches at least 6 holders.
