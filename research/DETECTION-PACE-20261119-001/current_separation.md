# When the entire reading must stay far from the attachment

Take the single one-spare five-ring on the straight funded path, loaded once on all five shared ring distinctions. A cold proposal passes, a hot one is vetoed, and the fresh distinctions take their minimum extension. The sole supplied mark is the old outward shared distinction at offset \(d\), in either direction. Evaluate current knot–distinction incidence. Erase all vertices fewer than \(2d\) incidence hops from the original attachment and all their incidences. Only the two vertex types and the root are marked. The cutoff can be imposed by the investigator, then the reader operates on the displayed data alone. Compare the connected part holding the mark, or all the retained parts with the same mark. A step is two incidence hops. A stage includes all earlier knots and every physical distinction. [Registered observations](definitions.md)

For arbitrary \(d\) on sufficiently padded paths the following thresholds are sharp. For example, it suffices to have at least \(4d+8\) old steps on each side of the attachment. Padding can be fixed once for each offset. At all earlier nonnegative layers every structural reading on the indicated observation agrees, and at the first layer and **every later complete layer** some structural reading differs.

| Whole observation supplied | First structural difference |
| --- | ---: |
| Connected part of the outward mark after the cut | \(\max(1,d-1)\) |
| All parts after the same cut, outward mark identified | \(\max(1,d-2)\) |

In particular, when every distinction consulted must stay at least four old steps' worth of current incidence distance away from the attachment, the first difference in the mark's connected part is at layer three. On the original 16-mark source it contains 42 knots quiet, 47 loaded. At layer two the rooted parts really are isomorphic. On the 32-mark source the corresponding counts are 74 and 79. A root-centred ball of unrestricted radius and a region chosen by source ancestry are separate experiments. ## Which vertices survive

The old attachment is \(s\). Give each original knot its old ordinary step distance from \(s\), its *offset* here. A knot born at layer \(u\) has current step distance from \(s\) equal to \(u+m\), where \(m\) is the smallest offset of any of its original ancestors. An ascending parent path from a closest original gives the upper bound. Replacing an excursion between two parents through their child by the earlier witness gives the reverse bound, recursively removing the highest interior excursion. The two proofs use the original physical parity start, including its ring. Thus a knot survives exactly when every original ancestor has offset at least \(d-u\). A shared distinction survives exactly when both its holders survive. A loose distinction survives exactly when its holder survives. This last rule follows from its incidence distance being one plus twice the smaller holder distance. In particular the cut creates no spurious loose distinctions. [Old-to-current distance proof](../SAME-SIDE-READING-20261119-001/local_proof.md)

The cold and loaded histories have the identified presentations on sets of \(u+1\) connected points. Each must contain an original point. Removing one point while retaining those conditions gives a parent. Adding one gives an output distinction. These are coordinates for the proof, erased from all comparisons. Beyond offset two each of the two outgoing sequences of old path points has the same contacts and spare continuation in the two preparations. Pure descendants of sources at offset at least three have matching entire stars. Their original offsets and identities are matched for the purposes of this proof, so the current-separation cutoff and the mark are also preserved. Consequently at age \(n\) with \(d\ge n+3\) the complete clipped observations are isomorphic. This covers all readings of their anonymous incidence. ## The first isolated difference

Set \(d=n+2\), \(n\ge1\). Through cohort \(n-1\) all retained knots have minimum original offset at least three. Only knots of the newest cohort with minimum offset two can add a difference. Write \(q=b_{s+2}\) for the nearest even path point on the right. Above it the even sequence runs outwards in steps of two. In quiet growth its inward neighbour is the attachment, of offset zero. Under load it has a private inward spare with an endless continuation, arising from the released slot on this two-open holder. The quiet sequence restricted to offset at least two consists of the outward side of \(q\). In the loaded sequence a size-\(n+1\) interval through \(q\) can additionally use one through \(n\) inward spare points. Every actual parent of any of these extra intervals still contains \(q\). Deleting \(q\) disconnects the inward stretch from any outer original, or leaves only spare points. Every parent consequently misses the older cutoff. Distinct newest knots have no mutual contact. Each extra knot and its loose distinctions form an isolated part in the clipped incidence. The left half supplies the same \(n\) extra knots.

The common outward interval from \(q\) has matching retained parent, plus two choices of fresh distinction in both histories, one inward and one outward. The odd outward sequence starts at offset three under the present cutoff and agrees. The only original ring knots at offset two are \(c_2,c_3\). In the quiet point presentation these two do not share an old witness with one another or with an outward original of offset at least two. Alone, \(c_2\) has one original spare, terminal because the holder had three opens, and \(c_3\) has none. Thus at most one quiet ring-only top can be lost at \(n=1\), and none at \(n\ge2\). Any changed ring-only tops cannot meet an older retained knot. At least \(2n-\mathbf1_{n=1}>0\) more knots survive in the full loaded exterior. All the differences are outside the old mark's connected part, which remains isomorphic. No mark at any larger separation can gain an earlier difference in its own further-cut component on this family.

## When the difference joins the outward mark

Now set \(d=n+1\), \(n\ge1\). Cohorts through \(n-2\) have minimum offset at least three. Cohort \(n-1\) is the preceding calculation with minimum two (at \(n=1\) it consists of the retained originals). At age \(n\) a new knot of minimum offset one can touch an older retained knot only by deleting its *sole* original of offset one and leaving a valid connected cluster. On the right odd sequence this is \(b_{s+1}\) attached to its interval extending toward \(b_{s+3}\). On its other side in quiet are \(b_{s-1},c_1,c_4\), also of offset one. Under load the possibilities are \(b_{s-1}\) and two terminal spares. Keeping any additional such point would prevent deletion of \(b_{s+1}\) from leaving an eligible parent joined to the right. Therefore the only top reaching the right older odd sequence across this boundary is the outward interval with that single inner original. It has the same retained contacts and four fresh distinctions in both arms. The analogous left and ring cases cannot join the right older part. Passing from it into another original branch would require retaining two originals of offset one, or passing the offset-zero attachment. Neither can attach such a top to a right retained parent. All other common outward intervals and their eligible parents match.

On the right even sequence, quiet growth still cannot use the attachment at this cutoff. The loaded private spare at \(q\) supplies exactly \(n-1\) additional size-\(n\) intervals in cohort \(n-1\), and \(n\) size-\(n+1\) intervals in cohort \(n\). They all now belong to the mark's component. From an interval meeting the spare, alternately delete one inward spare point and add one outward point on the other side in those two cohorts. This joins it to the all-outward interval. Keeping a farther original in that interval, successive removals of the innermost original raise the minimum offset by two as age drops by one. This supplies a retained path to an outward old knot at offset at least \(d\), which is on the old marked component. Padding ensures these outward points are real as far as required. The grades above exhaust the possibilities. The loaded component therefore has exactly \(2n-1\) more knots at this layer. The left side follows by reflection. Each future \(d\) uses a finite choice of padding. At fixed \(d\) the persistence proof below needs no additional length.

## Persistence

At a completed age \(T\ge d\), every current newest knot survives because its offset plus \(T\) is at least \(d\). Every newest knot has a loose distinction. Previous knots spent all their loose distinctions. Thus the displayed knots holding a loose distinction identify the last cohort wherever it occurs in the clipped observation. Erase those knots and their fresh loose distinctions. Keep the input distinction of each older displayed parent, now loose if its child disappeared. This recovers exactly the preceding clipped stage: every child was inside this cutoff, so growth lost no formerly displayed loose input. The few old knots outside it could never have supplied a displayed loose input. In the connected observation, take the component of the mark after erasing, since a new knot may previously have joined older components. Old shared incidences persist, so the earlier marked component is recovered exactly. This is an anonymous inverse requiring neither original names nor growth history. A difference in the component at \(T=d-1\) consequently persists through all later sizes. The union can itself select the component of its mark. Combined with its isolated witness at \(T=d-2\) and the small-offset checks, this gives the asserted per-layer result.

These claims concern arbitrary invariant functions of the displayed typed incidence with its induced boundary. A globally minimized bond anchored by far marks may consult the erased component; that is extra input. The smaller all-age bound for a genuinely fixed bond-radius reading remains available in its own convention. Allowing actual occupied values and a view reaching the ring gives an age-zero comparison. On the exterior from \(d\ge3\) even the original occupied ring distinctions are absent.

## If the reader is also given the allowed values on every visible distinction

The same *first structural thresholds* hold if the observer can query the restriction of the whole stage's allowed-state relation to all the displayed distinctions jointly. Here is the extra lower-bound argument. It is enough to cover the indistinguishable prefixes and the one connected equality at \(d=n+2\). In each nontrivial such case \(d>n\). All erased inner knots form a connected set because they make a ball about the attachment. At least one of them has a truly loose distinction: a descendant of the attachment lies at distance \(n\), with a loose output. Regard a loose distinction as ending at a common exterior on which no independent equation is imposed. Forgotten distinction variables connect the erased inner knots, that exterior, and every retained boundary knot at step distance exactly \(d\). Such a knot has a preceding neighbour at \(d-1\) on a shortest route to the attachment. Knots farther out have no forgotten incident distinction. If only the marked component is shown, every other erased part also joins the forgotten component by an erased link on its path inwards, or via its own loose distinctions.

After projection, the remaining independent conditions are even parity at each displayed knot strictly beyond the boundary. To see sufficiency, fix visible values satisfying those rows. On a tree of forgotten distinctions joining the remaining incident knots to a loose end, first set all off-tree forgotten values to zero. Working from terminal knots towards that end can satisfy their outstanding parities one at a time. Conversely a knot with every incident distinction visible still enforces its row. On every isomorphism used in the negative cases, the original offsets and ages of the common intervals are preserved. Thus the boundary subset at exact step distance \(d\) is carried along, whether or not it is supplied as a marking. In the connected equality at \(d=n+2\), the only additional matched top intervals start at the same \(q\) in the two preparations and have the same minimum offset. The all-reading equality therefore includes this projected relation. Positive structural readings remain available. A *cost* minimized over complete bonds allowed to leave the observation is a separate oracle.

For a reader supplied instead with the **actual prepared values** on all displayed distinctions, the marked component first differs at the source only for \(d=0\). ## What could give a bound beyond the path

Suppose two fully grown histories on the same source satisfy the old-to-current distance formula above and agree on the entire incidence of the region whose original ancestors all have offsets at least \(b\). Require that agreement to preserve original ancestors, birth layer and the old mark. At layer \(n\), every vertex in an exterior with \(d\ge n+b\) is then matched: an age-\(u\) retained knot has minimum original offset \(d-u\) or higher, hence at least \(b\). The ancestor-preserving agreement respects the further cutoff, both-holder rule and marked component. This yields an upper bound of one additional old step of present separation per layer, with an initial allowance set by \(b\). On the single original path \(b=3\) works. Identifying such a \(b\) from the first changed proposals on a general funded background is the missing implication. The unequal-arm comparisons show that even a valid upper bound need not predict the first positive on every arm. [Unequal-arm controls](backgrounds.md)
