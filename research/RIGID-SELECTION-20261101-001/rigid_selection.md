# Does composing like cycles force rigid selection?

Epic `RIGID-SELECTION-20261101-001` (program topology), 2026-11-01.

## The question

- **The approval.** The owner said "Yes, let's continue" to BEYOND-TWO-20261101-001's recommendation:
  - test the remaining cycle-composing laws for global failures that keep a knot;
  - then try to prove rigidity for every Mal'tsev law.
- **Why it matters.**
  - BEYOND-TWO's bridge (blurred Z3) both selects and composes like cycles, but its selected spaces are rigid: after a line is
    removed, only ties remain.
  - The knot's selected spaces keep a knot.
  - If every law that composes like cycles were rigid, native composition and emergence-keeping selection would exclude each other
    at every number of values.

## Answer

- **No: composing like cycles does not force rigid selection.**
  - Over Z_(p^k) with k ≥ 2, global failures keep knots.
  - Blurring gives a five-value law with all four properties: it composes like cycles, selects globally, counts hard, and keeps
    emergence inside its selected spaces.
- **But selection always spends the top layer.**
  - After any removal, the top layer of the solutions is one line, exactly as over a field.
  - Whatever emergence survives lives in the lower layers. At most one variable taking all values joins in, through that shared line.
  - Fields have no lower layer, hence ticket A's rigidity.
- **At two values the knot stands apart.**
  - No two-valued law that composes like cycles keeps emergence inside its selected spaces.
  - The knot keeps emergence there: it has a single layer and keeps it.

## Reading

- **Failures that live in a carry.**
  - **Example 1.** Its right-hand side is 2e₀, so the system is solvable mod 2 and fails only mod 4. This is a lifting obstruction:
    the cancellation that defeats it appears only once the carries are counted.
  - **What it costs.** Selection by such a failure costs the top digit. What it leaves free are relations between lower digits and
    the one shared top line. For example, the kept knot x₂/2 ≡ x₀/2 + x₁ (mod 2) is parity between the 2's digits of x₀ and x₂ and
    the units digit of x₁.
  - **The owner's harmonies.** In the owner's terms, the failure is a harmony that closes only at the second digit. It spends the
    first digit and leaves the others relational.
- **The knot has no carries.**
  - Its failures need no cancellation: its spaces are sign-nonsingular, so every term agrees.
  - It keeps three-place emergence in its only layer.
  - Among all the laws met so far it is the only one that keeps top-layer emergence inside its selected spaces.
- **The 2-versus-3 boundary, once more.**
  - At two values, a law that composes like cycles leaves only ties, which are pairwise, inside its selected spaces.
  - The knot leaves three-place knots there.
