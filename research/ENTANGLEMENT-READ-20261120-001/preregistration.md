## Targeted check of embodied settings

Before measuring, build two identical equality constraints from parity knots. Each uses four three-place knots indexed 0,1,2,3 with one shared distinction for every index pair except 0,1. Give 0 and 1 one terminal each. Attach one such group between A0 and B1, another between A1 and B0. Use Alice's bond (X,A0,A1,P), Bob's bond (Y,B0,B1). Mark X,Y as selectors and the displayed output pairs in this order. Freeze the syntax and replay the same construction under not-all-equal, at the start and at the first grown stage if funded. Report any failure to qualify. Sum of the parity constraints predicts the two terminal equalities. This makes the test adversarial to the assumption that mutually independent, fair settings guarantee a fair comparison of latent states. Test the entire pair, the selected bit, and the same-state external-index control under U,L,T. A result on this built start is an existence witness subject to its scaffolds.

## Parity, signalling and the least addition

Test the GHZ four parity requirements and the literature's Mermin–Peres square of six requirements. Enumerate common sign assignments, locally allowed pieces, overlap compatibility, a parity-sign repair and omission of one context. Search native exact projections using the common-state witness argument over *all* finite families rather than bounding a pattern search by size. Distinguish inconsistent chosen pieces, absence of every global support assignment, a supported local event with no extension, and incompatible probability tables. Prove which of these is excluded by an actual common global support and which requires a common weighting too.

External positive control: with a fair shared output bit R, change just context 11 from two equal bits to two opposite bits. Verify both marginals, the bias/removal of the mask as a signalling control, and the unchanged rule as a negative control. A probability t of the change in that one context tests sharpness. “Least” counts altered context rules relative to an all-equal four-context protocol, with costs of the fair bit, the context access and its weighting named. No claim of smallest physical mechanism. A CHSH score alone never certifies a quantum realization. Call a score above the quantum bound superquantum, and make a quantum-compatibility claim only with an explicit realization or a full-table criterion. Statistical dependence for endogenous selectors need not imply an available causal signalling operation.

A separate analytic test asks whether, for uniform affine parity states, an embodied affine selector and two affine response bits can exceed 2 while both complete marked pairs hide the other selector. This strengthens the comparison for parity only.
