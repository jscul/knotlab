# Referee report · Predicting a local reader’s view from a start

**Confirmed with fixes. No Block.** The frozen predictor gives both recorded views on 705 of 706 accepted uniform six-knot starts. Its universal prediction fails on 42 starts across the four distinct test sets, as the study reported. Only 95 of the 100 longer runs originally reached round 40. Independent growth now confirms all 100 through round 40 under not-all-equal.

## What holds

1. **A prediction from the start.** For each valid set of the finite map, the formula counts ways to distribute the remaining length among its selected endless chains. It distinguishes chains of length one to describe parents. Our independent implementation reproduces all 51,568 forecast rounds, both settling cuts, acceptance and the original class labels. It uses only starting information. The original classifier does call the shared first-round grouping routine to label the stratum. Acceptance, the counts, the predicted dimension and both settling cuts use the separately computed groups. The first reading counts births by kind, number of parents, fresh distinctions and places. The second adds the sorted descriptions of the parents. 2. **The frozen comparison.** The 6,532 archived forecast records have the published uncompressed SHA-256 digest. Recounting 48,933 saved birth rounds containing 7,557,415 births reproduces every one of the 84 start–law miss entries. Through the saved rounds, both readings agree on **705/706 uniform six-knot starts, 159/199 stratified six-knot starts, 15/16 accepted hand-built starts and 70/70 accepted seven-knot draws**, separately under each law. The corresponding sample sizes before acceptance are 2,000, 199, 17 and 1,000. On all 86,036 census starts of one to six knots our first-round groups equal the shared engine’s roster. ## What was not run

Of 6,532 held-out archive records, 1,240 uniform start–law records were independently grown through round one only. Their entire saved views were scored. Of the 3,157 five-knot fitting identities, 3,135 received archive and map checks only. The full six-knot census was classified from its starts. Direct growth to the target horizon for the entire census, a seven-knot census, parity growth to round 40, and growth past round 40 were not run. The two omitted sharing-cell starts were checked only in round one for the new comparison. There was no fresh preregistered test of the revised rule. Only parent descriptions and their multiplicities are archived for comparison. The older fitting controls’ auxiliary bound diagnostics were outside the scored predictions and were not rerun.

## How it was checked

Both reconstructions start from the same holdings. One enumerates allowed core sets and expands their generating functions. The other makes each round’s joins and offshoots, assigns fresh distinctions under the chosen law and carries the actual parent descriptions forward. Both are independent of the author’s growth, reading and analysis code. The sampling audit uses the shared atlas for numbering and compares its own grouping rule to the shared engine’s first-round roster. The uniform replay includes all immediate stalls, up to 30 accepted starts per frozen stratum using seed 20261132, the known miss, a cap-truncated start and a sample of cycle refusals.
