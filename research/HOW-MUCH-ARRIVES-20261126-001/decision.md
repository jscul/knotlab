## What stands

> **Referee R1, confirmed with fixes and one Block, folded in** ([report](referee-R1/report.md)). **Block:** four of
> the seven fixed-count changes were moves that leave a start knot with two places, below the three a start must
> have, and in 98 of 126 moves and 1 of 29 marks a start knot is short in round 1, where the code let it wait and the
> ordinary law would stall the round; so those rows are not ordinary growth from admissible starts (358 of 465
> single-change cases are). The four moves, repaired with a second shared distinction so that the donor keeps three
> places, give the same fixed count of 5. **Narrowings:** "forgets its start" holds for the two starts compared, not
> for dimension two in general; "re-makes one whole chain" counts unmatched births of both growths, and nothing here
> measures how an amount divides; pulses, pairs, setting B and the parity swap each narrowed as below. An earlier
> correction, before the referee, is also in the corrections log.

1. **On two ordinary starts of dimension two, a mark at a start knot shows locally as a fixed count.** Two touching
   three-place knots and three such knots in a row, under the ordinary count with no knot ever short, from admissible
   starts. A mark at either of the two touching knots, or at the middle knot of the three, gives a damage, read
   without names (each birth by its kind, parents, fresh distinctions and places, sig0, or with its parents' as well,
   sig1) and after pooling a round's births, of exactly 4, 4 and 1 births a round from round 4 (sig0) or 7 (sig1) to
   round 32: the count gap between the two growths, so its share falls as one over the round's count. The four moves
   repaired to admissible starts give 5 likewise. These two starts look the same in sig1 from round 4 to 32 (sig0
   from round 2); other starts of dimension two do not (a six-knot ring with one leftover against the two touching
   knots, and two rings of five with the same proved rate, differ beyond the count gap in every round).
2. **By name, the births further from the mark are unchanged.** In the round's births one holder step further from
   the marked knot, nothing differs by name in any of 32 rounds; in the shell nearer it, almost everything does (62 of
   62 unmatched births of both growths at round 32 for the touching knots, 63 of 65 for the middle mark). The changed
   growth's births there with the marked knot among their ancestors number 29 and 33. Nothing in these readings
   measures how a conserved amount divides, so no weakening of the lamp's kind is shown, and none is ruled out.
3. **The other rows are mostly not ordinary growth.** On the bare rings and the generator every move, and in several
   other rows, a start knot is short in round 1; the code let it wait. Under those treatments a change takes over on
   the rings and the eddy and holds elsewhere (sig1: 340 holds, 115 takes over, 7 weakens of 465, 3 too short); "holds"
   is the remainder, and its shares move both ways.
4. **A struck pulse keeps its damage, unless its advance payment is struck too.** All 155 pulses keep damage to their
   last recorded round under the strike as specified (56 reached round 16). In every case the ordinary count had paid
   one more fresh distinction to the joins of the struck offshoot's siblings (513 of 529); barring that payment as
   well restores the unchanged growth one or two rounds after the strike, in 155 of 155 (the referee's control).
5. **Pairs:** at round 16, 4 of 30 pairs of made marks give less damage than either alone in both readings without
   names, the joint damage being the count gap; by name none does.
6. **Between patterns:** under the ordinary count no change shows in a joined pattern's own births. Under the record's
   one fewer with waiting, two of seven far marks show at every d = 1 to 8, at round d + 5, where the jammed growth
   made no births. Under the cap of three, the share at first arrival rises with d (1/8, 2/11, 3/14, 4/17); at a
   common round it falls (51/59, 51/65, 17/27, 4/17 at round 11), because the farther change arrives later.
7. **Parity** gave identical output in 161 of 161 jobs, but no saved round could take every loose distinction, the
   one way the knot law could differ here: not a test of the knot law.

**Not established.** A weakening of the lamp's kind, or its absence: no reading measures how an amount divides.
Which starts of dimension two look alike locally (the referee's criterion question). Anything past round 32.
