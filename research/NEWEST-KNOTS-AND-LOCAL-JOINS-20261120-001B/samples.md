# Fresh paid comparisons for the radius-two join

The radius-two rule gives the same histories as the global rule
on all **64** fresh labelled trees. Three trees first gain a paid
group at a reached cut. In the matched distal-edit sample, **47
of 64** edits leave the complete radius-two reading and its paid
four-parent birth unchanged, while the global rule loses that
birth. All **64** partnered runaway starts retain every ordinary
parent and grant. These results agree under both knot laws.
[Frozen design](preregistration.md)

## What received a vote

The first population draws a uniform tree size from three through
eight, then a uniform labelled Prüfer tree and independent
one-in-four surplus coins added to the arity-floor loose supply.
The second draws twelve independent orientations of the repeated
meetings in the six-live partnered start. The third draws a uniform
branch length $L$ from one through six. It keeps the same original
sharings and loose distinctions while adding one shared distinction
between the two branch ends in only one of the paired preparations.
Each spent original has one distinct live terminal and one live
branch. Every live original has six loose distinctions.

Each of the three populations has 64 independent start draws. Both
laws and all three policies use the same physical input in each
comparison. Trees and partnered starts get one uniform distinct
live pair. The branch comparison uses the two designated live
terminals adjoining the original spent holders. Each marked
holder supplies one independently uniform physical loose name.
Every later reading follows those original ancestors through
actual parent rosters. The two variants of a branch draw form
one matched comparison. Repeated branch lengths are separate
draws from the frozen finite population and reuse their
deterministic physical replay.

All **256** physical preparations from the 192 draws passed the
exact original star projection tests under both laws. The
receipts preserve separately the offered groups, their funding,
actual births and inputs, actual fresh grants, empty closing
cuts and later caps. Each conditional share has its own
denominator. Exact marginal Clopper–Pearson 95% intervals and
conservative endpoints for unknown outcomes are in the receipts.
The marked arrival was decided on every draw. Ages, laws and
paired policies are reported separately.

The equality of the global and radius-two histories includes
the first forecast and all subsequent budgets. On an original
simple tree every separating physical meeting is recognized
locally. At age one, the only possible witness endpoints are
two adjacent previously live originals. Either a first group
straddles their original meeting, or that meeting still
separates the old holders. An original added group has
live–spent–spent–live routes and cannot straddle a meeting
between two live originals in a tree. If an ordinary group
straddles that meeting, a cross-side pair within it has an
ordinary two-meeting route across the tree link. Its actual
child gives a three-meeting alternate route after removing
the old link, wholly visible within radius two. Thus the
age-one permissions and the first forecasts agree under both
rivals. At later cuts previously live providers are members
of the same positive cohort and share no actual distinction
with one another. This proves the observed global and local
identity on the specified tree population even at later caps.

Every partnered start is paid and yields identical literal
cohorts under all three policies, with sizes 8, 10 and 24

recurrence. The original spent witnesses never directly meet
one another, and the original live holders never directly meet
one another. These exclude an added group at origins and at
the first reached cut, respectively. The same preceding-cohort
argument then gives continuing identity, including all grants.
The queried knots have delay one in 55 of 64 draws and two
in the remaining nine. Their overall finite-arrival share is
64/64 (94.40–100%).

## A distal meeting and the paid central birth

Once the tested central name is removed, the added end meeting
gives one alternate ordinary route of length $2L+1$. A route
of at most five meetings fits inside the display. Conversely
every displayed holder remains within two meetings of at least
one of the two roots after deletion. If both roots are joined
in the display, their two radius-two sets meet or have a shared
incidence between them. In either case the roots have a route
of at most five meetings. The length-one and length-two edits
therefore remove the central permission. For length three and
above, the two altered tips lie outside the display. All
visible holders, all internal distinction identities, and all
visible full loose and shared counts agree between the two
preparations.

The open source pays the central four-parent birth under
both rivals for every length. Adding the distal meeting
makes the central name fail the global separating test for
every length. Ordinary growth leaves the two marked terminals
permanently separate under all these preparations. In the
radius-two policy the edited first paid child appears exactly
when its two records agree and retain a permission. The
edited lengths one and two remain separate at later cuts.
Their old cycle has length at most six, so any potential
age-one live-to-live old sharing on it has its alternate path
visible. Edited global histories likewise have no further
eligible old sharing since their old live-to-live meetings
are on the same cycle. In the open histories every marked
rival arrival happens at the first cut.

## Independent replay checks

There were 4,549 attempts and 3,571 materialized cuts across
the paired laws, policies and physical preparations. On 2,239
subsequent complete attempts the preceding forecast is
checked against actual group demand. Eligibility is
independently recomputed by naming and deleting each physical
shared distinction in the full or rooted display. For a paid
group the newly formed support supplies a rooted alternate
route even after removing its old authorizing name. Twenty-four
first-cut comparisons repeat selection, funding and full priced
parent rosters under an independent recoding of both holder
and distinction names.

Two sparse originals give a sole closing child under parity,
at steps two and three respectively. Each appears under all
three policies. An independent reconstruction from the receipt
verifies that the sole ternary's support is exactly the binary
sum of all old supports and that it has zero fresh names. In
the six paired not-all-equal histories the exact same parent
rosters and consumed names produce a ternary-input child
with one fresh distinction. The raw records retain a closing
birth, a following empty cut, a shortage and a cap as separate
statuses. The sample runs took 0.94, 13.11 and 4.58 seconds
for trees, partnered orientations and distal pairs respectively.
Peak resident memory was 130,996 KiB. The gzip receipts have
fixed headers and replay byte for byte under a different
Python hash seed. Independent scientific referee pending.
