# The ordinary parent and price map with one shared payer

**Identification theorem.** On the 34 clock preparations in the enrolled family, original age n−1 has one ordinary current knot per translated copy-class of the finite connected supports of rank n described below. Every connected immediate predecessor retaining an original is a true parent. Every possible connected one-rank extension accounts for one loose distinction. The statement applies to all finite ages under parity and not-all-equal. It includes the two actual ternaries at the same original donor and their joint child. The surplus floor, full old-state projection and original physical candidate groups are part of the statement. The 16 original supply challenges are separate finite controls.

The previous all-age identification has disjoint marked triples. Its last grouping argument uses a vector-space representation, the fact that each candidate pair determines one containing extension, the ordinary distinction floor and the four-rank bound for different translated copies. We prove the missing conditions when two triples meet at p. Its standard subspace alternative has a proof in the original note, including the classical source. ## Connected supports in the five-role region

Replace each original marked triple by one *bookkeeping* block incident to its three live donors. Retain C or S as an actual physical distinction in the original witness arrangement. Ordinary pairs retain separate two-point blocks. Use the universal covering tree of the point/block incidence system and the terminal points for original surplus loose names. A donor of original supply two has a linear continuation from each of its surplus terminals; at supply three or more its surplus terminals have no such continuation. The claimed action and the floor are checked below. Translating all parts of one support together gives the same physical knot.

A support is finite, connected, retains a real original and contains the full S triple whenever it contains two of its points. In particular this requirement iterates across p. Put
$$r(D)=|D|-\#\{\text{complete S blocks in }D\}.$$
The two affected triples are L={u,p,w} and R={p,v,t}, where u=c₂₀, p=c₂₁, v=c₂₂ and w,t have no original neighbor outside their own block. Each w at the other two compensation sites is likewise private to its own gate. Both ways of counting the common p as an independent coordinate are forbidden.

In the SS case assign the five original points the five distinct nonzero vector lines of a three-dimensional binary counting space represented by u, p, p+u, v, p+v. The two admitted planes are span(p,u) and span(p,v); their intersection is exactly the actual p. The vectors u+v and p+u+v have no original point. The allowed connected closed restrictions to this whole region are the empty one, any of the five singletons, either of the two complete triples, and the five-point whole. Indeed a connected support occupying both outer sides must pass through p, since its unrolled incidence structure is a tree. If it contains any further member of a side with p present it must include that whole side. A support may leave the region for an exterior component only via its unique attachment point. This proves the list even if most of the physical knot lies outside the region.

The dimensions of these nine allowed restrictions are 0, five times 1, twice 2 and once 3. For all of them, intersections of their spans give precisely the span of their ground-point intersection. For a pair of touching global supports their restrictions also have the right sum. The only problematic case in the list would be two disjoint singleton restrictions on opposite sides, neither equal to p: their vector sum generates a plane absent from the list. Such two supports cannot touch outside the region. Any exterior common point determines one attachment point to the region that both must contain. If they touch inside, that point already lies in both. In the other cases the span of the smallest closed union is the sum, as can be checked from the nine restrictions. In particular combining a whole left triple and a whole right triple gives all five original points in rank three, with the shared original counted once. The absent vector lines cannot appear as a native predecessor on a connected history.

Use the ordinary binary rank-two representation for each other disjoint S block, one coordinate for each remaining point, and one three-dimensional coordinate per disjoint copy of a genuine SS region. If at most one of L,R is S its usual binary rank-two coordinate suffices, and C constrains only which original points can be reached in one step. The complete vector representation now gives the stated rank and correct meet and sum for connected touching supports. The physical parity and not-all-equal laws have exactly the same original group structure here. The vectors merely prove the count.

## Removable parts, funded groups and short lifts

One connected rank-increasing extension adds an adjacent point and closes the touched S blocks. This operation cannot jump two ranks: if it reaches the common payer p through one gate while the other gate already contains a different point, connectedness would already have placed p in the old support. Inside the SS region from a singleton one may add its incident full triple, from p either full triple, and from a complete side the whole region. Conversely any containing connected support one rank larger comes from such a first neighboring step. Its reverse must retain a genuine original.

The rank-two initial exception is physically determined. A C witness holding the twelve named distinctions excludes the triple, giving the three binary groups. Three separate witnesses on the same bundle form one unheld triple and give a single three-parent birth. No fourth live donor can enlarge that triple: its possible neighbors lie within one marked block, and the two blocks through p share only p. There is no additional original candidate cycle among three different blocks. Other candidate pairs therefore give their ordinary binary births. All loose names left after paying these groups give separate unaries. In particular SS gives two distinct trios at the same cut. They share the one original p, which pays them with different private names.

At rank at least three the old removal proof only needs distinct admissible removals to have rank-two intersection below the support, and any three genuine removals to have rank loss at least three in their common intersection. If a full SS region is downgraded, its only eligible internal hyperplanes are L and R; each keeps p. They intersect at p, two ranks down. If an exterior attaches away from p on both sides, neither downgrade is available. If the exterior attaches only at p, both can remain available, but a third removal must alter another independent coordinate. If an occupied side is only L or R its three singleton downgrades are simultaneously possible only when the support is exactly that rank-two triple: any exterior attaches through a specified original, so at most one of those singletons can retain connectivity. All other disjoint coordinates have the earlier removal lists. Thus the rank loss properties hold for the global support. Intersections of two connected supports with a point are connected by their common tree-span; any auxiliary point present in both brings its real anchor with it. In a full five-role region alone the two immediate parents are exactly L and R, including every one of their physical donors. No third immediate parent exists.

A support containing at least two real points admits at least two removals. Follow the terminal blocks of its occupied incidence tree. On a terminal full S block the two unattached outer points can contract to the attached one as a single rank removal. A common block loses an exterior point. Terminal auxiliary branches lose their last point. A singleton attachment through the shared p does not create a third exit from its opposite side; the explicit SS list above supplies the correct removals even where nearly the entire support is the five-point region. A support with only one real point consists of its private surplus terminals and portions of their chains. It has one predecessor exactly on a single branch from that real point.

For uniqueness of lifts, every nonidentity covering translation has an axis with at least four distinct point positions per period. Contracting each occupied gate to its original path pair removes only a terminal w and restores the known original candidate route system, whose shortest reduced return has at least four pair steps. The previous axis proof consequently finds at least four new axis points when a support D contains B and a nontrivial translate of B. These are point positions before the first axis projection of one translated copy F. To see why counting rank retains them, inspect each S block newly completed in D but incomplete in F. If its unique extra donor w is new, charge this block's rank subtraction to that point. Distinct blocks have different extra donors even when sharing p. None is on the axis, since its only remaining exits lead to terminal or nonperiodic auxiliary chains. If w already lies in F, F has neither traversed endpoint of this triple, since F is closed. Both of those endpoints are new in D. They cannot both lie among the counted points before F: the route between them uses this block, and w then projects between them, also before F. Pay the rank subtraction with the other endpoint. There can be at most one such exceptional gate, as a connected F containing w but neither of that gate's endpoints can occupy only w and its own external auxiliary branch. The required four rank increments remain. This excludes, as before, two copies of one support in a closed connected region only one, two or three ranks larger. At a positive cut any candidate pair has one coherent lifting with a shared rank-(n−1) predecessor, and its connected join has rank n+1. All members of any candidate clique lift consistently relative to a fixed one: a conflicting copy could otherwise fit in its own enlargement by at most three ranks. Distinct rank-n subspaces that mutually meet in codimension one either all contain the same codimension-one subspace or all lie inside one rank-(n+1) space. Indeed taking A,B, set H=A∩B and V=A+B. A third not containing H spans itself by its two different intersections with A,B and hence lies in V. A space outside V then meets V exactly in H and cannot meet that third in codimension one. Here the former common H is a connected, physically present parent; the latter V is the connected joined support just proved. A child with three or more immediate predecessors gives an unheld group by the rank loss property. It is maximal since an additional candidate would have to be another immediate predecessor of that same V. A two-parent list cannot be swallowed by an alternative larger group because the pair already fixes its unique V. This establishes every genuine group and prohibits hidden additional parents, in particular at the immediate SS meeting and at every later receiving step.

The projected supply equals the number of one-rank extensions. At source the candidate degree pays every original group; remaining supply gives the designated private unaries. At a later multi-parent support every extension is a distinct multi-parent group, and the infinite connected cover provides at least one extension. Its grant is its complete demand, accounting exactly for all these groups. One-parent supports and the private branch floor have the earlier proof: supply h≥3 at the root gives h−1 other extensions at its sole terminal and no onward chain; original supply two gives one other group plus one continuing unary from the floor of two. No physical name is spent twice. Parity can close only upon taking all loose names in a connected history. The disjoint, indefinitely continuing clock frames give multiple current groups at every age and exclude that case. Not-all-equal never implies the proposed all-input knot here: taking one name from each true parent leaves at least two places at each preceding knot, and the old-state completion proof applies. Every funded birth preserves the previous allowed states through a positive fresh supply. Original ordinary input freeness and the canonical local state laws are also directly checked on the physical preparations.
