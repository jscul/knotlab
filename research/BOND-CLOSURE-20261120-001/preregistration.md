## The question and the readers

Use a nonempty finite connected physical presentation with one or two holders per distinction. A lawful source knot has its full local knot relation after the allowed assignments of the whole start are projected onto its actual support. A proposed funded birth takes one loose distinction from each of its p different parents. One loose distinction unused by any group makes its own single-parent birth. A parent shortage prevents the whole round. Score “takes all loose distinctions,” “already implied,” “fresh count,” and “stage closes” separately. If no inputs or the whole round stalls, there is no such birth. The archived closing exception to the arity floor is explicit for a final two-input parity support; also report the result if it were excluded. Formation layers count size. No numbering, preassigned location, history or source label enters any reader.

For a collection T of proposed inputs write b(T) for the minimum cardinality of a physical bond containing all of T. The pair bond distance of distinct inputs is b({x,y})−1. Diagonal pair distance is zero. Where a genuine singleton minimum is supplied, distinguish it from a diagonal entry. Record infinity if no bond contains all marked distinctions. In parity this means a minimal nonzero row support. In not-all-equal use the actual minimal nonfull allowed projections unless explicitly running an incidence-only control. A wired exterior permits exact parity cuts; it is no physical knot or step. Never run parity cut weights under the other knot law as a substitute for its allowed states.

Three observation strengths are distinct. (i) One query on an arbitrary prescribed unordered subset of the proposed loose inputs, with their total count p known. For “smallest single reading” require a size that works whichever subset of that size is selected, including adversarial renaming. (ii) All exact readings on subsets up to a given order, with the inputs marked together, permitting comparison, maxima or adaptive queries. Do not infer a lower bound for (ii) from equality of a single query in (i). (iii) The same deck on the union of the parents' whole current physical supports, with the proposed loose inputs distinguished. Exact exhaustion should be tested under the weakest sufficient input. If a uniform lower bound is claimed on a larger domain, prove how a marking behaves there. If the exact finite-arity minimum for a stronger reader remains unknown, state its upper/lower bounds and also whether any constant suffices over all finite sizes.

For the full update the default bounded-bond reader at a distinction receives the rooted ball of distinctions at pair bond distance at most R, with exact induced distances and local incidence, loose counts and witnesses when present. Give the union of the balls around all specified inputs as a stronger control, and say explicitly when the parents' complete stars or all exact cross-ball distances are being supplied. Do not smuggle in the global roster or all remote stars. Separate determining a given join's output from identifying all proposed parent groups, next demand, and detecting a stall. The other step rulers are shared-parent distance at the growing end and ordinary holder or distinction steps in the whole stage. Fixing a numerical bond radius independently of source size and largest knot is stronger than bounding a support's query order. A state-derived bond reading may depend on the whole physical presentation even when its queried distinctions are nearby.

## Analytic predictions before computation

1. Closing ternary proposals register pair bond distance two on every pair. In a continuing proposal a size-three bond through a given pair would require a single shared distinction separating some of the three parents from the others. A legal unheld group of at least three cannot cross a bridge. Audit this argument directly; do not assume that a randomly encountered triple is a proposal. A single loose input is expected to be insufficient on the four-knot control.
2. On success, all p loose inputs always decide exhaustion by their least containing bond having size p. Predict a sharper single-reading bound of p−1 at p≥3 and seek matching lawful examples. Test whether any bounded order, including all entries through that order, suffices across unbounded arities; favourable structure in the identified fixed-map class must be reported separately. Examine arities one and two, singleton, chosen tuple versus every tuple, positive-age versus arbitrary lawful starts.
3. If the predicate can always be read, predict that no one fixed numerical bond radius determines the complete update at every size. Test first on an arbitrarily stretched family in the bond ruler (large old knot width can directly make small balls singletons), and distinguish closure from demand on funded continuing histories. Unbounded numerical radius does not follow from unbounded query order by definition; each requires its own witness.

- **Random three-route paired starts.** Use 128 draws with seed 202611201463.
