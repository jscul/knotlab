# Which information determines fresh distinctions?

**All-size proofs for finite connected presentations and the two archived knot laws. Results are referee pending.** A complete funded birth P takes one loose distinction from each of its p parents. Each other birth sharing at least one of those parents can affect the fresh count. List those siblings once apiece, retaining which marked parents they use. Link any two that have a common old parent, including a parent outside P. Through the marked birth, count the maximal groups of linked siblings sharing no one marked parent, with at least two siblings in each. Then count one more use for every sibling in none of those groups. The sum D is exactly the number of distinct successor groups needing a distinction from this birth. The count uses actual incidence and is computed before forming those successors. [Explicit formula and proof](../../../research/FRESH-DEMAND-20261120-001/demand_proof.md)
