# Transferring route proof length when proofs can multiply two linear expressions

Status: Proved

## Accepted conclusions

- Signed-variable and constant substitution transports every syntactic degree-two polynomial-resolution DAG through exact compiler projections with at most twice as many source nodes for duplicate-free source clauses. Work in the explicit proof encoding is polynomial. [A, candidate-local-delta](../../QUADRATIC-ROUTE-TRANSFER-20261115-001A/QUADRATIC-ROUTE-TRANSFER-20261115-001A-P001_quadratic_substitution/proof.md)
- Pang v2 Theorem 7.2(a) at degree two and characteristic two gives an exponential proof-node lower bound with high probability for 8n random strict ternary clauses. A quadratic line can express a relation no affine disjunction expresses, and checking unrestricted semantic weakening on such lines is coNP-complete. [B, known-overlap-found](../../QUADRATIC-ROUTE-TRANSFER-20261115-001B/QUADRATIC-ROUTE-TRANSFER-20261115-001B-P001_degree_two_source/proof.md)
- With high probability the induced guarded formulas require exp(Ω(r)) refutation nodes in the listed encoding and exp(Ω(r^(1/3))) on a fixed catalogue of r stages, even with degree-two multiplication over F2. Both encodings admit an explicit checked nonlinear proof replay.
