# Advisor review before the freeze

Three exchanges with the advisor model (Claude Fable 5.2, no tools), all on 24 November 2026.

- `words_question.md`, `words_answer.json`: the owner's phrase "axis point". The advisor's answer led to **ancestral common
  knot** for the object and **ancestor-taking** for the law, which the owner then approved for the definitions; "take" for
  the move; "shared" as held by two or more.
- `question.md`, `answer.json`: the cold review of the first draft of the definitions and the pre-registration, with the
  drafter's `open_questions.md` and `words_proposed.md`.

What the cold review changed is listed in the last section of the frozen pre-registration. In short:

- **The argument** that a birth taking only what its own parents hold cannot rejoin separated parts was true but not a
  proof as written, and its hypothesis was pairwise where it has to cover a third part. It is now one induction, for parts
  that are wholly apart.
- **The definitions** had gaps that decided results: the order of births and of names; what happens with several ancestors
  the same number of steps up; a choice rule that claimed to use the structure and used the record; removal of the second
  part where that leaves an ill-formed structure.
- **The paired test**: the way a difference comes about is now decided by its first differing step and confirmed by a
  masked run; three verdicts replace "supplied or made"; the four marks are named and not ranked.
- **The ladder**: a cost for each law in place of a single order; a law that needs no record of descent, which the advisor
  stated and predicted by hand; controls and swaps added (a late start of the recorded rules, one birth placed by hand, a
  ration that follows demand, the start's names reversed).
- **Scope**: what a positive would not show is stated in the advisor's terms.

After the review the drafter reran its prototype under the revised definitions. Two of the advisor's hand predictions
were borne out and one was not (reversing the start's names does not remove the smallest case). The reruns also showed
what the first draft had missed: every case found for parts that came apart disappears when the ration is swapped. All of
this is disclosed in the pre-registration and is still to be repeated by an engine written from the frozen text.

Words not taken from the advisor's table: none of substance. "Root", the owner's candidate, was not adopted, for the
advisor's reason: the record of births is not a tree, since a join has two parents.
