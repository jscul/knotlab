The canonical K1–K3 test is unchanged. Law: not-all-equal on three-place knots.

## The question

`EVERY-RAY-20261113-001` found 66 of 300 sampled first-ray growers whose least bond grows along every descending path of copies,
with members kept on the path. Freeing the bond can only shorten it. Its members may then lie anywhere, side copies included, and
its target may be any copy at depth s. Call the result L_any(s). Does it still grow? If some pattern's L_any grows, the law counts
depth inside that pattern with nothing chosen: no ray, no target and no support. If none does, counting inside along copies is
ruled out for these patterns.

## In standard terms

In an infinite iterated hyperedge-replacement NAE instance (a tree of copies), the object is the least minimal non-shattered set
containing a root port and some vertex at derivation depth s, over the whole instance. Growth is certified by rectangular powers of
port relations; boundedness by a fixed-size tree-automaton saturation.

## Settled by construction (stated, not tested)

- **Existence.** L_any(s) ≤ L_path(s), which is finite at every large depth for the 66 (they grow over paths on complete graphs).
- **The gap argument.** Let R_i be the relation between a copy's ports and its child's ports through SELF call i, with the copy's
  own distinctions and every other call's subtree projected out (each by its exact limit relation G). If a stretch's product
  relation is rectangular, a set with no member strictly inside the stretch has allowed states equal to a product, so it is not a
  minimal non-shattered set across it. Copies share distinctions only through call arguments, and a passed-through distinction makes
  the stretch non-rectangular, so side subtrees cannot reconnect around a rectangular stretch. If every product of r factors is
  rectangular, every bond to any depth-s copy has at least 2 + ⌊s/r⌋ members (the advisor's sharpening of 1 + ⌈s/r⌉). This is
  BRANCH-DEPTH's lemma, generalized to every call.
- **Persistence.** A product with a rectangular factor is rectangular, so the set of r that work is upward closed. Whether some r
  works is decided exactly on the finite semigroup generated by the R_i: either all products from some length on are rectangular,
  or a non-rectangular product recurs on a cycle.
- **The certificate does not see side support.** The relations are the same with members on the path or anywhere. A forgetting
  certificate therefore proves growth for both. It is a real test here because EVERY-RAY certified growth by cycle means, never by
  forgetting.
- **Bounds.** A forgetting certificate gives a lower rate of at least 1/r; L_path gives an upper rate λ_path. No exact rate is
  claimed.

## Per family (the 66): the three verdicts

1. **Grows (L_any), certified.** The forgetting test succeeds. The least such r and λ_path are recorded.
2. **Bounded (L_any), certified.** BRANCH-DEPTH's fixed-size saturation, generalized to a choice of SELF call at each level (a
   subset construction over the call-labelled graph), finds an eventually periodic path. Along it a set of at most k members (k = 3,
   4, 5, anywhere) is a bond containing a port of main and an own distinction of the depth-s copy, at **every** depth from an onset,
   with acceptance checked at each step of the cycle. A bond means minimality is certified, not only non-shatteredness.
3. **Unresolved.** Neither verdict holds within the caps, or the semigroup test finds a recurring non-rectangular product but no
   bounded certificate at k ≤ 5. In that case the recurring product names the path to feed to (2).

Both certificates on one family is a contradiction and invalidates the run.

## Runs and stop rule

`job.py` for the shared runner (`docs/tooling/shared-runner.md`), one case per family. Codex builds it and runs the gates and a
20-case smoke with `--foreground --limit 20`. The thread launches the full run: 2 workers, 7 GB per case, MemoryMax 15 GB,
30 minutes per case, retries for timeouts and memory at the same settings once, a 20-hour runtime limit, registered in
`docs/LONG_RUNNING.md`. The budget is sized so that memory stops do not decide the unresolved bucket. Stop after one run, its frozen
summary, a referee hand-off and the report. The advisor model was consulted before freezing.

## Clarification before the full run (2026-11-14, after Codex's hand-off; seen: its 20-case smoke and the relation gate)

Codex's hand-off (`codex/REPORT.md`, 23 minutes) built the certificates. Its gates passed: the independent R_i on all 66 with 0
mismatches, the controls, no double certification, and a mutant that changes an R_i. It ran a 20-case smoke. It flagged two
ambiguities, decided here:
- **Depth when main ≠ S** (9 families). Count the target's depth from the entries of the flattened main block, the convention of
  EVERY-RAY's L_path and the census, so that L_any ≤ L_path compares like with like. A boundedness verdict does not depend on a
  constant shift.
- **Gate (b)'s range.** A bounded certificate claims every depth from its onset. Witnesses are checked from the onset through the
  onset plus two periods; depths before the onset carry no claim.

What has been seen: the relation gate computed the forgetting test on all 66 (43 with a finite r, 23 with a recurring
non-rectangular word), and the smoke's verdicts on 20. The predictions stand as frozen.
