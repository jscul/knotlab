their admissible first choices, under each knot law and each schedule.

## A witnessed reading of the complete source

For a reference birth, collect the live marked parents and their first
witnessed candidate neighbors. Use the witness identities retained on
pairs inside this set, including pair readings reached on a second
witnessed step. Witnesses include spent old holders. Every multi-parent
group containing a marked parent lies within this set, since all its
members must be pairwise candidates with that parent. A witness holds
the entire group precisely when its identity occurs at every member.
This supplies the true groups through each marked parent. Their counts
and the marked parents' loose counts then determine each unary
multiplicity at those parents.

Keep each reconstructed sibling once and retain its parent set. Two
siblings can be candidates after the complete birth precisely when
their parent sets overlap. A subset of siblings and the marked birth is
held when they have a common marked parent. Count the maximal unheld
groups through the birth and add one for each uncovered sibling. This
computes the raw complete demand. In 102 of the 335 pair births and
three of the seven ternaries, an unheld group combines two of the
sibling comparisons. All 775 raw demands agree with the independent
complete roster under both laws. Applying the fresh floor also agrees
in every case. The actual old input projection handles the implied
exception separately. There is no implied first birth in these draws or
the two controls. This check reads a complete source through witnessed
steps. The two-step count here is in the witnessed growing end, with
pair witness records retained. [Complete-source demand formula](../FRESH-DEMAND-20261120-001/demand_proof.md)

## Sampled present-state orders

For each funded draw one seeded prefix per law and schedule samples
uniformly from the current admissible parent sets. The joins-first
schedule draws from the multi-parent subset when available. Each prefix
runs through at most 12 decisions, including when the original mark has
already fired or become impossible. Independently, one of the funded
source multi-parent sets is chosen uniformly per source from those with
such a set. There are 145 marked draws. Two funded unicyclic draws have
only unary births. A marked birth requires exactly its original parent
set. A missing mark is permanently lost when an original parent is
exhausted or another child has already joined all the marked parents.

The marked outcomes and matched source-birth counts agree across the
two laws. Parent rosters are compared by original parent-set
multiplicity. Only 13 of 147 uniform prefixes and three of 147 joins-first
prefixes produce the entire source roster without extra original-parent
groups. Four and two of these, respectively, also agree on all its fresh
counts. Under parity, completion of the source roster is already
permanently impossible in 130 uniform and 142 joins-first prefixes by
shortage or the earlier-common-child exclusion. The corresponding
not-all-equal counts are 130 and 144. A mixed birth with both original
and newly formed parents is directly incompatible with the complete
operator, which consumes all original loose distinctions at its first
step. The two censored marks are still enabled at the observation cap.
These finite prefixes put no measure on infinite fair orders.

### Law-specific stops in the full prefixes

All not-all-equal prefixes reach the decision cap. Under parity six
uniform and nine joins-first runs exhaust all moves. In five uniform
and 43 joins-first runs, the archive proposes a zero-fresh birth of
arity less than three. This is the sole proposed action in each of those
frames. The evaluator rejects it under the canonical knot gate. The
remaining 136 uniform and 95 joins-first parity prefixes reach the cap.

In `unicyclic/6/09`, the first legal ternary `(1,3,4)` agrees at one fresh
distinction. The second event has mixed parents `(0,2,5,6)`. The independent
complete source roster instead asks for the remaining pure ternary
`(0,2,5)`. The mixed event takes all four currently loose distinctions
and has zero demand. Parity admits its eight even input states, so the
birth takes zero fresh distinctions and closes that presentation. The
literal not-all-equal projection permits all 16 input states, so the
same event takes one fresh distinction and the prefix reaches the cap.
This is an observed two-decision law difference on a draw outside S3.
