# All sixteen laws on the same attached ring

Each entry gives quiet → loaded. The ring sits on the same knot of the 17-knot background in both preparations. Its five original shared distinctions are the only changed values. P applies the original rule, V refuses a multi-parent join, + supplies one extra loose distinction and − removes one down to its minimum. The first action is used on quiet parents and the second when any parent has a deviation. Every eligible proposal and every member of each named ball is counted once.
