# A readable sign and joint weights at birth

Two exploratory comparisons for G18, Comparing growth laws, commissioned by the owner. The new rules use a current birth proposal or a simultaneous round. The ordinary law provides the control. The investigators derived their candidate outcomes before opening these runs. The first test measures whether ordinary access can read the sign. The second measures consistency at different stopping rounds and dependence between the fresh values of siblings.

## Prior mathematics

Signed shifts and parity measurements have the usual operator algebra. Our question is what maps are selected by the old parent roles and where a reader has access to their changes. [Gottesman](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9807006)

Urn mixtures give exchangeable positive weights. The two-qubit correlation criterion gives an independent check on any optimum reported with supplied coherent reads. Here the objects carrying the weights are the current offspring of a not-all-equal founder. [Diaconis and Freedman](https://statistics.stanford.edu/technical-reports/finite-exchangeable-probability) · [Horodecki, Horodecki and Horodecki](https://doi.org/10.1016/0375-9601(95)00214-N)

## Frozen questions and controls

### Can a pre-birth parent determine a relative sign at the four-knot close, and which readers can see it?

Derive the allowed change invisible to each individual parent from the old relation. Apply the sign of its loose value at the change. Compare the three old parents, their product at the close, the scope of the changes, and all outcome probabilities under the declared reader alternatives.

The close with the change/sign removed, an ordinary mixture at the same probabilities, relabelling, and one larger fixture with the same parent symmetry. Independent check compares a relative phase, an overall sign, sequential changes, and a free reader on its two named distinctions.

Stop at the triangle of parents and the stipulated four-parent control after resolving the sign and the reader separately. Preserve any nonunique choice or control failure.

### Can current simultaneous births assign joint weights, and can one such kernel also match uniform completion counting at two stopping rounds?

Compare the first-round distribution at two stopping horizons. For a rival, divide weight equally among current joint completion classes under permutations preserving the founder and its values, then over their representatives. Compute the reader table, determinants, ordinary value and supplied coherent scores, and knots on the conditioned and full supports.

Independent draws with the same marginals, uniform named completions, and a second weighting with class multiplicities. Compare symmetric signed changes against rotated coherent reads and distinguish data needed for each.

Stop after the two stopping horizons and the one founder including all start words for the full support. If a supplied coherent score exceeds two, test the permitted local operations before drawing an operational conclusion.
