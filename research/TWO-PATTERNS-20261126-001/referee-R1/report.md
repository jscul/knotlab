## What was not run

The 54 clock-stopped pass records were not completed as part of this referee replay. The discarded mixed-version bench launch supplies no additional cases. The owner's broader request for every start of up to six knots was not exhausted by that paired construction. ## How it was checked

Raw records were parsed by new programs. No study reading or tally module was imported. Seed 20261126 gave 44 draws across bench and census strata restricted to completed rows with at most 2,500 terminal knots and at most two recorded seconds. Eight deliberately chosen checks included all cases attaining the latest own hearing. Original per-round lists of marked and stub births were not archived. Those lists were regenerated to check the saved first difference. Only the toy cross-check executes the author's growth engine, against the independent site implementation.
