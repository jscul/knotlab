# Local updates that spread, move, and meet

Two binary patterns on the fixed 81-knot path move at different rates under one supplied local law. Both reach one visible meeting mark, wait if needed, and depart in reversed directions after one joint change. Every admissible complete schedule gives the same two histories and the same joint change. Removing the mark lets the patterns stop in 50 different states. A separate rule that reflects them when they approach each other has one terminal state and 50 different meeting places. ## One physical binary state with a visible meeting mark

The size-two parity stage of the 81-knot path has 79 response locations. Two neighboring responses share one distinction. Each response also has two distinctions private to it, which agree on the closed states used here and identify occupancy relative to the zero allowed state. The whole physical assignment is the parity sum of the occupied responses. The evaluator orders the locations along their recovered chain, and the rule treats both orderings alike. This construction keeps the allowed reference from the earlier study. [Response code and reference](../MOTION-AT-EDDY-20261119-001/minimality.md)

The rule reads a complete local group of occupied responses with consecutive occupied positions at most two response steps apart. Two intact words, written from their first occupied position, are \(\{0,1,3\}\) and \(\{0,1,2,4\}\), with both reflected forms. Each has a singleton beyond a gap of two, which gives its heading. The short word moves up to one response step, the longer word up to two. Each new copy clears the old one. It leaves at least a three-step gap to another group, or five to a stationary meeting word. Shorten a proposed stride if the available space requires it. The meeting word is a run of exactly seven occupied responses, isolated as a group. When both neighboring words point at it and are each five steps away, one event reflects both words in place. Each then departs under the same motion rule. A lone arrival waits.

The two packet types and the meeting mark are parts of the same physical assignment. The marker uses 16 distinctions. The packets use ten and twelve, as follows by counting two private distinctions per response and two shared distinctions at the ends of each occupied run. The three-occupied short word already has the minimum number of occupied responses that can break reflection for an isolated pattern on the homogeneous chain. The longer word supplies an observable choice of stride. This example has no designated clock, input port, or separate heading register. The additional resources are the visible meeting mark, the two supplied word tests and a composite action. The formation rule is passive throughout.

The events are local in the response-step ruler. For a packet event take the singleton at its advancing end as the root. Recognizing its group requires a read at most six steps away. The nearest forward group can affect an intended move beyond the ordinary three-step clearance only if a meeting word begins within six steps. Reading its seven occupied positions and the two positions just beyond requires at most 14 steps. For the joined event, root the read at the middle of the meeting word. The nearer occupied position of each packet is eight steps away. The four-step packet span and two-step isolation check put the farthest query at 14. A gate-centered occupancy reader of radius below eight would see the same input with either waiting packet removed. It could not distinguish the registered joined event from that solo wait. This is a lower bound for that rooted decision and response-occupancy interface.

The compiled move toggles the symmetric difference of the old and new word, which is four ordinary responses. Their actual summed parity change uses 12 distinctions for a stride of one, 14 for a full stride of two, and 12 for the clipped one-step move of the longer word. The meeting event toggles the two interior positions changed by each reflection. Its four toggled responses change 14 distinctions together. All reachable actions were checked on physical parity assignments, and their separate response toggles each change four physical distinctions. Their composition supplies the stated effect. Scheduling the constituents separately would require its own enabling rule and intermediate-state test. The meeting reflection has an inverse on the local pair of words. The tested forward guard fires on two inward packets and releases two outward ones.

## Order independence and the need for a place to meet

For this paired launch, the motions on the two sides of the mark are independent until each arrival. The two short histories have 18 and nine inward events. Each paired arrival enables one joint reflection. After it, the departing histories have 28 and 14 events. The last event of the stride-two word uses its clipped stride. The state graph has \(19\cdot10+29\cdot15=625\) states. The number of complete schedules is
\[
\binom{27}{18}\binom{42}{28}=247746643157871000.
\]
These are exact event interleavings. The sample still has one designed paired launch and its reflection. Every interleaving before or after the joint event is an ordering of two chains of mutually independent actions, so it is related to any other by adjacent swaps of commuting actions. The replay checked all 554 co-enabled squares in each case and found one terminal state in each. Either single launch waits after its inward history without a reflection.

Removing the mark keeps the same move permissions and removes the joined event. The two inward heads start at response positions 13 and 65 and approach to the three-step clearance. Either arm can take a finite prefix while the other waits. Their final positions can be \(p,p+3\) for every \(p=13,\ldots,62\). Indeed, let the stride-one word reach \(p\) first. The other word then advances to \(p+3\), using a final clipped step if needed. There are 50 terminal states and a failure of the two-step square near meeting. Thus the main rule has order-independent histories in the marked launch class and fails terminal agreement on the full binary response code.

The extra direct-scatter variant permits the two unmarked words to reflect jointly at the three-step gap. It then sends both to the same respective ends in all complete schedules. The replay found 2,986 states and one terminal state, with all 50 physical meeting positions between the two inward heads. Distinct meeting histories even rejoin at exactly the same intermediate state. Terminal agreement therefore does not entail identical event histories. The narrowing is about physical meeting location. Fairness permits arbitrarily long finite delays for one free packet, so it cannot by itself fix the location of first proximity on an unmarked chain. The stationary mark and the waiting guard supply that information in the positive control.

## Full commutation and monotone spreading

Consider a one-response event at position \(i\) totalized as \(T_i(x)=x\oplus e_i A_i(x)\). A disabled update is the identity. Here \(x\) is the response-occupancy word, \(e_i\) is the single-position toggle, and \(A_i\) is the binary enable predicate. Suppose \(T_iT_j=T_jT_i\) for all assignments and every distinct \(i,j\). Taking the \(i\)-th coordinate in that equality gives \(A_i(T_jx)=A_i(x)\). An initially disabled site stays disabled under every other update, and its own disabled update changes nothing. All future fires therefore lie in the initially enabled set. If the blank assignment is quiescent and the guard has finite radius \(r\), a compact disturbance can only cause fires within \(r\) of its original support. This proves zero persistent translation speed in the fully commuting one-response class on an otherwise blank infinite chain. The enabled-event square in the positive control is a weaker property. It permits new actions to be enabled along a causal chain.

For every positive integer \(r\), the add-if-near rule occupies a vacant response if any occupied response is within \(r\) response steps. Each event toggles one genuine four-distinction response. The set of occupied responses grows by inclusion, and the private distinctions of each newly occupied response remain set. On a finite chain every fair order has the same least closed set. Every site admitting a finite derivation is eventually reached, by induction on its derivation. A derivation of depth \(k\) reaches at most \(rk\) response steps from the seed. Consecutive jumps of exactly \(r\) attain this on a long enough chain. At \(r=1,2,4,8\) the left- and right-priority orders had different histories and all filled the same 79 positions. A two-seed test also filled them. The reach count is sharp per causal depth even though an execution can delay any enabled event.

Any nonempty finite response word that remains included in its later state cannot reappear as a nonzero translate of the same entire word. A translate has the same cardinality, so inclusion would force equality, impossible on a two-sided infinite chain for a nonzero translation. The persistent private distinctions distinguish this accumulating result from a compact travelling pattern. Parity can cancel other physical distinctions when two responses are occupied together. If the monotonicity axiom is imposed directly on the physical support, the same cardinality argument applies directly there.

## Parallel additive rules and ordered frames

In an explicitly synchronous model, a scalar binary rule at radius \(r\) may assign
\[
x_{t+1}(i)=x_t(i-r)\oplus x_t(i+r).
\]
This example attains reach \(rk\) after \(k\) logical ticks from one occupied response on a long enough chain. At the fourth tick exactly the two extremes are occupied. More generally any finite-range linear scalar rule on the infinite chain acts on finite-support Laurent polynomials by a multiplier \(p(z)\). A nonzero compact word \(f\) returning as a translated copy after \(k>0\) ticks would satisfy \(p(z)^k f(z)=z^d f(z)\). Cancellation in the Laurent polynomial ring gives \(p(z)^k=z^d\). An operator with distinct extreme exponents keeps distinct extremes in every power, so the multiplier must be a monomial. Reflection fixes only the monomial of displacement zero. Therefore this scalar symmetric family has sharp causal reach examples and no nonzero compact recurring translation. The computation on the literal finite path tracks every change, including ticks that alter multiple responses.

Trying the same scalar assignment one site at a time can change the result. With only position \(i\) occupied, attempt the updates at \(i\) and \(i+r\). Clearing \(i\) first leaves the empty state. Updating \(i+r\) first leaves both occupied after the two attempts. A simultaneous read from the old state is an additional rule.

Two ordered frames instead give the reversible map
\[
(x_{t-1},x_t)\longmapsto \left(x_t,\,x_{t-1}\oplus T(x_t)\right),
\qquad T(x)(i)=x(i-r)\oplus x(i+r).
\]
The first frame can be recovered by adding \(T(x_t)\) to the new second frame. Take two counterpropagating unit patterns stepping by \(r\) per tick and read their binary sum. That sum obeys the two-frame recurrence. At tick three both arrive at the same position and the current scalar sum cancels. The following frame displays both again as they separate. The replay records the two ordered frames and the two supplied signed channels, each interpreted in its own copy of the response code. This is reversible additive interference under a clocked two-frame law. The joined state change in the clockless marked control instead alters both subsequent directions.

These bounds have distinct units. The primary scattering law reaches at most two response steps per composite travel event, attained by the long word. The flooding family attains \(r\) per elementary four-distinction cause, and the scalar synchronous family attains \(r\) per logical tick. Varying finite \(r\) removes a common reach ceiling within either family. The predecessor supplies a separate compact pattern family with all offsets scaled by an integer \(a\), attaining \(a\) response steps per four primitive firings at radius \(6a\) on a sufficiently long homogeneous stretch. Even with the same elementary event count convention, a largest speed across that family would require a restriction on the allowed radius. The response ruler, action size, read radius and time convention remain explicit supplied parts of each comparison. [Compact scaled rule](../MOTION-AT-EDDY-20261119-001/minimality.md)
