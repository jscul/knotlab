# Writing at K_4 and reading from the law

Status: Computed (`check.py`), pre-registered in `research/READ-ADDRESS-20261110-001/preregistration.md`.

- **S, one size past (computed).** `ADDRESS-20261110-001`'s frozen targeted search wrote the two leftovers of three Fano atoms into every
  pair of atoms on every line orbit of K_4 (15 of 15; up to 3,841 configurations). With K_3 the written cases are now 24 of 24.
- **N1, reading from each atom's own signs (computed).** In 23 of the 24 written configurations the holders are exactly the atoms whose
  own signs make them act as their lines; this read uses no state and no census, only each atom's 21 sides. The exception (K_4, l0 = 16,
  pair {0, 2}) ended in a class where only atom 2 acts as its line, and the second unit sat inside atom 0, which is transparent. So the
  local read is exact when as many atoms act as there are units, and not otherwise.
- **N2, the state alone (computed).** For each written law, 145 to 180 of 1,000 random states have their over-agreement in exactly the
  holder pair: a random state does not show the address.

So the written address can be read natively from the law, atom by atom, when the number of acting atoms matches the number of units.

Against the pre-registration: S and N2 held; N1 held in 23 of 24, with the exception above. Scope: K_3 and K_4, three Fano atoms (A48), the
moves of A50 and A26, at most one unit above the floor (A49). Every count here is computed on these small cases.
