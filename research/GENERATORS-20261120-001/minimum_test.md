# Size comparison after the first construction

This is a secondary test proposed during proof work, after the 21-knot construction and the seven-knot necessary condition were known. No result of this window comparison has yet been measured. The primary reading and its predictions remain archived. Minimize knots first and distinctions second within the class with the proved cluster map.

Take the seven-knot start with ordinary meetings 01,04,14,24,26,34,35,56, double 01, and give holders 0 to 6 respectively 3,0,3,3,3,2,2 loose distinctions. Its two return words are A=(4,0,2,5) and B=(4,0,3,6), up to reversal and exchanging the two arms. For each even n≥2 set U=A^n B and V=A^(n+1) B. Compare the two periodic walks U^5 V and U^6 V. Let L_n be their longest common straight interval up to period shifts and reversing one walk, then set K_n=L_n+1. For the secondary reading the source consists of intervals avoiding any length-K_n factor of those two periodic walks, in either arm orientation, for every even n. This reading has the proposed products in its definition. The stronger minimum under it concerns possibility, not discovery of the products by an unspecified local observer. Every final choice of reader must be reported.

Predict that the two length-K_n packets release together and have an actual mixed successor. Compare within the same start with the pair obtained by exchanging the arms of only the second packet. Predict these parties have the same individual productions and no mixed successor between them. Check n=2,4,6,8 using the two exact periodic words, their reversed forms and structural arm interchange. Each word contains marked gaps n or n+1 distinct from every other allowed index. Check that all launch parents still belong to the source and that an interval at the first mixed size recovers both endpoint-deletion parents. Fixed-map identification, anonymous recovery of original holdings, both laws, counts, and lower-bound authority come from separate gates.

One further proof uses the pre-existing 21-knot source. With fixed outer gap s=4(n+1), let U0=A^n P0 and U1=A^n P1 in the notation of the [encounter test](meeting_test.md). A product may consist of all intervals of freely concatenated U0 and U1 avoiding a doubled consecutive word whose half contains b1. We predict it can emit its own all-parent packets by U0^ell U1 for ell≥1, first at length 2s(ell+1). This is an additional reading and is recorded as such.
