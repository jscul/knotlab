## Counting and validation

The update acts on the whole source. A funding shortage vetoes the whole cut, and a parity child receives zero fresh distinctions if its actual inputs give a check in the complete old equation space. In 15 disjoint parity sources, one child closed its component on the same first cut in which another component continued. We independently replayed all source gates and growth rosters in the physical engine and checked all **3,576** funded allocations by explicit transport of old loose slots and fresh names. The rate calculation compares width, the multiset of exact reduced rates, and the sorted multisets of rates incident to each distinction. Exact canonicalization then tests the entire weighted table and counts its symmetries. Parity pair rates depend on which two-place checks belong to the old equation space, so they require linear elimination. For a large not-all-equal stage, expand each holder's indicator as one minus the indicators of its two constant words. This associates three terms with each holder. A shared distinction makes two selected opposite constant values incompatible. An unpinned distinction contributes two choices. Eliminate the newest holders first and combine repeated rate queries only through exact physical symmetries. At 232 names, 18 query types suffice. The largest intermediate tensor held **19,683** entries, and every rate table fit the registered limit.
