## Partnered runaway

The physical start has six live knots in three partner pairs. Twelve
spent holders witness the pairs other than the three partner pairs.
Twelve more spent knots complete the holdings of the witnesses, giving 30

The partnered theorem supplies all later parent sets, funding and
nonclosure. The law-specific parity closing exception never applies, so

rosters. A child holds one distinction from exactly one parent in each pair
of the previous cut. Omit the choices already held together by a common
witness. At age four there are 4,086 actual parent rosters, in 2,043 pairs.
Each of the 24 age-three knots witnesses one distinct prohibited choice
across these pairs. The age-five population is exactly **2^2043 − 24**.
Each sample takes a uniform integer rank in this population, seed
20261120405. Ordering the choices as integers and skipping the 24 prohibited

only.

**Two-step ancestry.** Fix a knot at age t and consider any actual birth at
age t+2. If that birth lacked the earlier knot in its ancestry, every one of
its age-t+1 parents would hold a distinction from the earlier knot's
partner. The set of those age-t+1 children that take from the partner holds
exactly one member of every complementary pair. The later birth's parent
roster would therefore equal that entire witnessed set. The rule excludes
such a group. It follows that every age-t+2 birth has every age-t knot as a
two-step ancestor.

A lasting family must include the entire age-t cohort at any cut after
its launch, by applying the all-parent rule back from a member two steps
later. This leaves no disjoint ongoing source or second family. There is no
relay or two-loose end of a witnessed path. Thus there are no qualifying
releases, direct meetings or indirect meetings under either frozen reader
or the broader existence criterion. Funding is exact. Inclusive ancestry
spreads to the entire cohort after two steps.

The full-holdings reading records the actual parents and their arities.
The growing-end reading uses only live knots, loose counts and witnessed
sets. These suffice to read the paired form at every current cut. The
receipt gives the exact loose counts and witnessed set sizes as expressions
in the partnered count recurrence through age 21. This is a symbolic
forward replay of the sampled queries. The literal check rebuilt 42
births through age three under each law, checked all 4,086 age-four parent
rosters against the complete preceding physical holdings, and verified the
24 excluded age-five choices. For the smaller age-two, age-three and
age-four rosters it also checked two-step ancestry on every birth.

## A jump followed by a sole top

The archive's initial sample was cap-7-06/91. It has six knots and 23
distinctions and was retained for a jump in its birth count. The literal
projected states pass K1–K3 on all six supports under both laws. Age 12 has
67 knots, followed by 322 at age 13 and nine at age 14. We uniformly sampled
the 67 actual age-12 knots with replacement, seed 20261120406. All 96 votes
were retained, including repeats. The literal cap was 384 births per step.

We propagated each actual parental ancestry set and verified that all 67
age-12 knots contribute to the sole age-22 knot. Every funded parent before
that birth spends its loose distinctions, so the sole top has the entire
earlier cohort as ancestors at each intervening cut. Two independently
lasting packets launched before the sole top could never both survive it.
The contribution of each query is inclusive descent. Identifying one
query as the cause of a release would require a matched comparison.

Under parity, the sole top has zero fresh distinctions and the growth
closes. Under not-all-equal, this same birth has one fresh distinction.
Its unary child at age 23 has two loose distinctions. That child releases
two unary knots at age 24. These are exactly the two degree-one holders of
the whole witnessed path and each has two loose distinctions. At age 25
they form a shared child with two fresh distinctions and each has its own
unary continuation. The literal replay checks the shared inputs and both
true unary parent rosters. This can also be predicted from the age-24
growing end, which exposes the pair and both funded unaries. A single late
growing end recognizes the continuing pair of ends up to interchange.
Verifying descent from a particular age-12 query requires its older parent
links. [Prior relay recognition and continuation proof](../GENERATORS-20261120-001/controls.md#the-two-ends-of-a-relay)

The witnessed path grows by one member at each parent step. Each interior
holder uses two loose distinctions on its two witnessed pairs. Each end
uses one on its sole pair and one on its unary child. The resulting path
again has two loose distinctions per holder, so both pure end families
last indefinitely. The literal history verifies the rule through age 28.
At any path cut with m holders, the ancestors d steps later occupy
consecutive indices. If a later knot has index j, its ancestors at this
cut have indices max(0, j-d) through min(m-1, j), by taking the union of
its one or two parent intervals. At d ≥ m−1 this includes an initial
end. Each of three disjoint lasting packets would need one of the same two
ends. This bounds the number of lasting packets by two in the whole
not-all-equal tail and excludes recurrent release of disjoint lasting
packets. The two releases here are the two families of one relay.

The certified first-collateral test excludes the twins because their first
meeting is direct. Later joint ancestry is possible. A broader matched
indirect change is unresolved under not-all-equal.
