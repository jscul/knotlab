A census-wide follow-up to a finite certificate. For a nested NAE family (ray definition S with SELF calls), R_i is the 8x8 port relation through call i (others by limit relations). "Forgetting" means that for some r every product of r factors is rectangular. Then, by a gap argument now under review, every bond from a top port to ANY copy at depth s (members anywhere) has at least 2 + floor(s/r) members. Population: all 12,772 census families (13,279 draws of 223,261) whose first-ray bond provably grows. Strata: 5,190 single-call "chains" (one definition, one SELF call, no side copies, so L_any = L_ray, growth by construction); 6,925 with two or more SELF calls; 657 single-call with other self-holding calls. Existence per depth comes from the first ray (L_any <= L_ray). Run the forgetting test on all of them. Report the weighted share of proved counters (chains plus forgetting) over the 13,279 draws, with the undecided cell (non-forgetting), worded per depth, conditional on the gap argument's review.

Predictions: P1 proved counters >= 45% of first-ray growth weight (chains are 42.4%); P2 forgetting among the c >= 2 growers is between 8% and 25% by weight (a sample gave 43 of 300). Gates: no forgetting family is bounded over paths in an earlier sample (since forgetting forces growth on every path); reproduce 43 of 66; independent re-derivation of every certifying product.
