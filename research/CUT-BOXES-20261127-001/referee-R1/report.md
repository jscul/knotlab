# Does opening a system prevent agreements from binding again?

**Refuted — Block on the closure conclusion. Both frozen predictions hold on the 49 registered cuts.**

Exactly **one of the 882 possible single cuts** of the same 49 closed systems gives a strict box. That opening gives **18**. All eighteen read both loose distinctions, bind six disjoint lower carriers in the frozen form, and need all twelve source laws. Ordinary growth produces this very open system in one funded round from a connected ten-knot start. The start has zero boxes. Either birth alone gives zero. Together they give eighteen. The eighteen form nine pairs exchanged by renamings that preserve the births. None is individually fixed. ### The decisive opening

Use the closed system in the first row of `cubic_12_3.jsonl`, sixth in the sorted list of box-positive systems. It has sixteen closed boxes. The registered draw splits distinction 16 between knots 7 and 11. Instead split distinction **0** between knots 0 and 1, giving knot 1 the fresh copy **18**. In the archived numbering, pair the agreement readings as follows.

| First upper comparison | Second upper comparison | Third upper comparison |
| --- | --- | --- |
| (0,1,10) and (3,4,17) | (2,6,18) and (8,11,14) | (5,7,9) and (12,13,15) |

Each of the eight choices of one reading per column forbids exactly 111. All 72 tests of one lower reading against one source knot leave a fixing at which the reading can vary. Each of the eighteen carrier members can flip its reading by a single allowed change. Read **disagreement** in each column. Seven upper words occur, with each pair free. If all three columns disagreed, choosing the true reading in each would violate one of the eight clauses. Dropping any one clause from those eight restores 111. Dropping any one of the twelve source laws from the actual system also restores it. The source realizes **34** of the **37** six-reading words admitted by the eight clauses. Both yield the same upper relation. Across the eighteen boxes the corresponding counts are 33, 34 or 35. The frozen definition requires the eight triple images. [Direct truth tables and canonical checks](witness_states.json) · [Images of all eighteen](reading_identity.json)

The same incidence and carriers arise when ordinary growth adds two knots to the ten-knot start. Its four loose inputs fund two births, each with one fresh distinction. Both fresh distinctions enter the six carriers. The start and the two individual-birth controls have **85,877**, **133,592** and **138,465** lower bonds respectively. The funded two-birth round is the first stage with a box. It has **201,425** lower bonds. Identifying the two fresh distinctions would make the first and second columns share a carrier member. This explains how an opening can create a new admissible choice of readings while destroying every old box. The [predecessor's referee certificate](../../UPPER-GROWN-20261127-001/referee-R1/open_witness.json) supplied the candidate. The [independent growth replay](witness_states.py) and [before-and-after search](reading_identity.json) establish it here.

In **39/49** the identical readings also qualify before opening. In the other ten, opening separates carriers in different upper comparisons. Each witness needs six source laws, forming three separate pairs. This construction was already known on the open grown ring.
