## The paired decisions

Every pair compares two distinct reached prefixes before the queried action. Require the same founder, law, naming, decision turn and formation, identical current observations including the positive feeder signal, identical first-memory word and the same positive number of previously paid meals. Verify the number of exits of each physical feeder from its actual holdings at the decision, including when it changed since the meal. The reader has no access to these scorer checks.

| Ledger label | Additional conditions |
| --- | --- |
| `primary` | Both prefixes use the same movement tape. One currently visits empty A and the other currently visits full B, with A and B distinct actual feeders of exactly the same degree. Both prefixes previously emptied A and neither emptied B. |
| `cross` | Apply the primary definition across the two different tapes in each predefined pair of replicates (0,1), (2,3), …, (14,15). Admit both assignments of empty/full to the two tapes. |
| `secondary` | Within the same tape, both prefixes query A. One previously paid A and now finds it empty. The other previously paid B and now finds A full. B is a distinct feeder, previously unpaid by the empty branch and of exactly A's degree at the query. |

In primary and cross pairs A is fixed as the empty query and B as the full query. When a secondary pair admits several B choices, choose the first paid B in the full branch satisfying the condition and occurring on both routes if one exists. Every unordered comparison appears once per named panel. Additional earlier meals are saved explicitly. Record whether both spent sets match, whether both last ate A, whether last meal turn and pre-meal energy match, whether both previously visited A and B in each branch (also through the current visit), their order of first encounter, and moves since the last earlier visit to the respective query. Summarize unchanged and changed routes and each realized order. Search the complete within-founder set of 16 tapes for an exact identical-history primary-type collision, including pairs outside adjacent tape numbers. List any more general witnessed ambiguity separately. A missing witness in the finite bank does not establish identifiability.

Use each admitted pair once for paired discrimination. Report program-level pair counts, the number of unique visits in those pairs, all positive post-meal visits without a partner, and the whole visit denominator. Give by-founder counts and the equally weighted mean among founders supporting a pair together with the number of founders without eligible pairs. Report each law separately. Shared founders or identical law histories do not supply independent replicates. Causal discrimination requires opposite useful actions when the original structure words differ and the reciprocal swap reverses both, with all nonintervened quantities fixed within each branch. Score the full primary set before stating any conditional result for public certificates, distinct second words or both-visited pairs. Also retain incorrect opposite actions, same-word nonflips and unresolved cases. The selected positive witness prefers correct reciprocal reversal and two public certificates, the same controls at the decision, both branches having visited both feeders, identical last meal turn and pre-meal energy, different traversed routes, then earliest decision turn and lexicographic founder key, tape and prefixes. Learning and survival use the separate frozen training and survival comparisons.

Separately test when opposite choices actually pay energy in a common two-turn continuation. Clone each branch at the real query. One clone tries ON and the other passes OFF through the normal movement control. On the next turn both clones try ON if alive. Score energy after those two decisions and assign zero after first-turn death. A strict two-sided gain requires OFF to finish with more energy on empty A and ON to finish with more on full B. Preserve ties, wrong-direction differences, the whole pair denominator and the energy-at-least-two subset. Output both counterfactual energy values for each branch and the joint strict-gain flag; keep the primary stock outcome separately. This short continuation is supplied and does not establish a survival difference.

The consumer reads a gzip-compressed JSON array from `panel_pairs.json.gz`. Each primary row has `seed`, `source_key`, `law`, `panel`, `turn`, exact query degrees, actual A/B identities, and `empty` and `full` objects. Each branch supplies its own `tape`, `replicate`, `attempt_turns`, `meal_turns`, ordered paid holders, actual `route`, complete `public` rows and the public certificate. Use the held-out renamed world from `bank.sample_pair`. Public rows are (feeder, energy, previous, menu count, action). Previous 0/1/2/3 means start/eat/move/refused. Action 0 is an attempted eat, slot+1 a realized outward move, −2 a refused move, and −1 the current undecided row. Branch routes and paid holders are for scoring and physical replay only.
