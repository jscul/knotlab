## Exact separator calculation through age two

Introduce one bookkeeping exterior to hold the loose distinctions. The XOR of the parity equations on any set of knots gives the support of its cut. When either shore splits into components, a component gives a proper nonempty cut subword. When both shores are connected, any contained cut word must place each whole shore on one side and either keep both together or reproduce the full cut. Thus inclusion-minimal nonempty supports are exactly the cuts with both shores connected. A pair read enumerates the consistent two-colour assignments on its marked endpoints that make each mark cross. [Parity cut interpretation](../EDDY-WORLD-20261119-001/bending.md)

The solver eliminates knots in min-fill order, keeping the exterior in every bag. Only actual distinctions contribute to connectivity and cost. The table for each separator records its current colours, the exact partition induced by same-colour connections already processed, and whether the sole inside component has finished. Each physical crossing is charged once, when its first endpoint is eliminated. Shared distinctions on the same two holders are counted with their multiplicities.

Combining two factors joins their connectivity partitions on agreeing colours. Eliminating the last visible member of an outside component rejects it unless the component remains connected through the separator to the exterior. An inside component may close only once and then disallows every further nonempty inside component. The root accepts exactly one closed inside component. Keeping the cheapest record with each such summary preserves all possibilities for every future bag. This supplies an exhaustive lower bound conditional on each orientation. A separate traversal checks the two connected shores and all crossing distinction identities on every returned upper witness.

The packed implementation prunes a partial cost above the cost of an older verified shore, where available. Its partial costs are nonnegative, and such a shore supplies a current upper bound only after checking it again in the new literal incidence. The two age-two arms each verified all 120 older upper bounds. The search tested all requested orientations and resolved every pair within its limits. The largest bags, including the exterior, had sizes six and four at age one and eleven and six at age two, in quiet and loaded order. The tighter search agrees with the unpruned packed replay entry by entry, including each retained orientation optimum. The pure Python version independently gives all age-one minima. The separately built tables agree on all 480 readings across both arms at ages one and two. The age-one replay also reproduces all 88 earlier exact readings and satisfies all 152 earlier strict lower bounds on these marks. ## Lower bounds by routes through age six

For \(e_3,e_4\) there are only two consistent orientations. For each, contract endpoints of the same prescribed colour and assign to each link the number of physical distinctions between its representatives. A set of routes from the prescribed inside class to the exterior class, with aggregate use bounded by each link's multiplicity, certifies that at least that many distinctions cross. Shore connectivity can be relaxed for this lower bound. The output lists the routes and their multiplicities, with checks against the literal input incidences. An upper witness is a separate list of actual knots whose cut was checked to have both shores connected.
