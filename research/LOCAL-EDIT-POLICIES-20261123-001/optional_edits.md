# What an optional spare can reveal

A single ordinary round needs enough loose distinctions at every live holder. On a given source write D(i) for the number of groups requiring a distinction from holder i, and h(i) for its original loose count. Fix the live holders and their witnessed incidences. Give each observer a possible empty edit and any finite menu of additions to those already-live holders. The additions can depend on the observer's pre-edit record. Group formation depends on the witnessed incidences, so the same D applies. The ordinary round funds precisely when h(i) plus the additions at i is at least D(i) for every i. This is an instance of the [ordinary growth operator](../ORDINARY-DYNAMICS-20261120-001/end_operator.md).

Let F be this funding decision and suppose each observer sees a persistent local distinction gain its holder exactly when F=1. On a fixed preparation F with both empty edits is at most F after any pair of additions. Suppose F has the same marginal law when either party changes its setting, including the empty edit. Its expectation at every pair of edits then equals the expectation with both empty. A nonnegative difference of Bernoulli variables of mean zero is zero almost surely. For a finite menu the same probability-one set works at every pair. Thus the common decision is already fixed by the prepared case for every permitted action. If the only joint input to otherwise locally generated readings is F, a shared variable drawn before the settings supplies a common answer for both parties' questions, giving CHSH at most two. The argument makes no assumption that a party knows its own demand. Inducing different group rosters by making spent holders live is a distinct operation.

More directly, if funding occurs with probability p after two compulsory repairs but is impossible when Bob withholds his spare, Alice's probability of seeing a birth changes by exactly p. If her published old mark is one on half of the funded trials, the probability of reporting one changes by p/2. The truth of the first statement is independent of what values would have been produced by any fresh distinctions. With voluntary withholding, concealing the source labels or waiting until after intervention to read a new bit is insufficient to conceal the common funding record in this device.

The [executable](optional_edits.py) reuses the frozen source presentations and marked holders of the [earlier fixed-start experiment](../../experiments/WHOLE-STAGE-SETTINGS-20261120-001B/WHOLE-STAGE-SETTINGS-20261120-001B-E001_ordinary_edits/result.md). It recomputes a literal round for zero, one or two extra distinctions per party, checks the group roster and the two actual local owners, and compares both knot laws. Each of the nine setting pairs has equal weight within its named start. Of 33 earlier sources, 30 have eligible marks: 2/3 controls, 3/3 built sources, 11/12 sampled labelled trees, 11/12 matched extra-loose starts, and 3/3 archived explosive sources. All 270 marked trials per law on these 30 sources funded. On each of two different, separately built shortage starts, 4/9 setting pairs funded under each law. Their complete table of funding values in row order (0,1,2 spares for Alice) is (0,0,0), (0,1,1), (0,1,1). This is an existence control and a check on exactly these labelled samples.
