# A released product that can itself keep emitting

Use the ordinary 21-knot, 60-distinction source. The types, straight intervals, original holdings and both endpoint parents are recovered from its present physical holdings as in the [encounter proof](meeting.md). There are no added distinctions, values or actions. This is a second level of the full-structure reading. A product's own offspring can leave it, in exactly the all-parent sense used for the first release. The more restrictive requirement of preserving every loose count on growing the product alone is a different test.

For each n≥1 take two equal-length nonretracing circuits U₀=AⁿP₀ and U₁=AⁿP₁, of length s=4(n+1), with one of b₀,b₁ exactly every s positions. Allow any concatenation of these circuits in either orientation as a family of straight intervals. At sizes m≥2s recognize those windows which contain no consecutive identical blocks VV whose half V contains b₁. Call this the continuing product at spacing s. The language is finite-state for any fixed two words before the square exclusion. Membership in the exclusion is a finite test on the current interval. At launch s is itself read as the distance between consecutive marked positions. Testing both orientations makes the answer independent of a chosen traversal. Distinct n give disjoint packets thereafter because every window of size at least 2s contains two consecutive marked sites at that exact spacing.

1. **Release and persistence.** Every 2s-site window at launch contains a pair of marked sites s apart. The two parents have 2s−1 sites, so they still belong to the outer source. The square exclusion is closed under taking subintervals, and a straight interval has exactly its two endpoint deletions as parents. Thus at every later size each continuing member has all its parents in this released product. The all-U₀ intervals persist at every size, and intervals containing a sufficiently isolated b₁ still belong. Membership requires no birth log.
2. **It belongs together.** A permitted 2s-site window has at most one b₁. If both its marked sites were b₁, its two s-halves would agree, since U₀ and U₁ differ at only their marked place. Extend the window by using b₀ at each new marked site. Its one-step shifts and their (2s+1)-site unions remain permitted and connect it to the all-U₀ periodic family by actual parent links. Those periodic phases are connected the same way. Heredity connects every later member to this launch set. This proves connectivity of the accumulated product. It makes no assertion of connectivity of every induced live cut at arbitrary size.
3. **Its own products.** For each ℓ≥1 repeat U₀^ℓ U₁ indefinitely. Its unique b₁ per period gives the period p=s(ℓ+1). No word shorter than 2p can contain the excluded square: the b₁ in its half would recur a positive half-length smaller than p. Every 2p-site window is two copies of its period and therefore leaves the continuing product. Its two (2p−1)-site parents both still belong to the product. From then on each of the p phases takes both parents from the same new packet forever. For unequal periods a shorter-period launch window contains two b₁ positions at its own period; no longer-period member contains such a pair. The releasing product and all its emitted products have disjoint continuing members. The continuing all-U₀ family ensures the releasing product is still active after every emission.

This gives actual parentage at both levels inside one unchanged ordinary structure, for parity and not-all-equal. A single periodic packet, considered just with its two pure transitions, is instead a bare cycle: every proper subpacket loses a member to erosion.
