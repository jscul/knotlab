## What stands

1. **Both recorded countings factor over knots**: on 4,303,444 two-by-two tables (pairs no knot holds together, every
   assignment of the rest; 300 sampled census starts kept at 14 distinctions, parity and not-all-equal, three arms, up to
   two rounds), counting from the finish and counting from the start never fail; a control that divides over the whole
   growth at once fails on 7 not-all-equal growths.
2. **Restricting to a bound whole-held fact leaves ring readers separated** with the middle unread or read for values
   (e = 0 on every restriction, rounds 1 to 6); with the middle read for changes it raises e from 1 to 2 for the other
   two rings, still sorted.
3. **The event-ready square is never available on sampled starts**: 13 of 300 have a pair with one shared pair, none with
   two, under all three arms.

**Not shown.** Larger structures and later rounds; that no growth law could make a non-factoring weighting.
