# What survives conditioning on the actual payments?

**No past payment can require the first deviation under either tested knot law.** On every reached, funded cut of a wholly quiet history, fix all the old holdings and their values, the proposed parents, the actual payment of every old distinction and the grants for every newborn. One continuation still gives every new distinction its transported reference value at once. Sometimes it is the only continuation. Sometimes a first deviation is also lawful with exactly that same past. No odds, planted bit, change of an old value or tie-break enters this statement. ## What a knot actually retains

A parent pays a *loose* distinction it already holds. It retains that same distinction and value. The child becomes its second holder. This physical fact can record which knots share a distinction. The complete round spends each outstanding loose mark once. An old holder that has paid all its loose distinctions cannot spend those marks again. Old reference and actual values persist. Newly created values belong to new holdings.

At the stage of forming a child, the model fixes one existing paid distinction per participating parent and the child's new bank. The guard has already read the current holdings and references of **all** proposed parents. The value rule has the inherited paid values and the new relation, and minimum filling, when applied, also compares with the fresh reference. Older contacts can change which birth is proposed, how many new distinctions it receives and what values it inherits. The rule has no further input for the sequence in which older payments happened. A newborn has made no outgoing payments before its own values are filled. The proof's pre-write condition is a restriction of one complete round, not an added event at which the child consults a log. The program's birth receipts let the investigator reconstruct the past. They do not give the newborn a separate memory or a history-to-value operation. ## Joint quiet extension at fixed history

Take an arbitrary finite connected lawful presentation in the recorded class, with each distinction held by one or two knots. Start with actual equal to its lawful reference. Condition on any reachable finite past before the first departure, however much of it the investigator can reconstruct. Condition also on an admitted funded next roster and **any particular legal allocation** of all its payments. This grants the investigator more information than a local knot has. No value of the newly created bank, later receipt, veto or future survival requirement enters this condition.

1. Old shared distinctions keep the same physical value in every holder. A loose distinction paid now retains its value when shared with its actual recipient. Each old loose mark is used once, even when one parent owes payments to several simultaneous births. Marks that already have two holders cannot pay those obligations. Every newborn obtains its own newly created marks. Thus after the complete old word and physical binding are fixed, the constraints on the new values factor by newborn. An allowed choice on another newborn's disjoint bank cannot remove a focal bank's locally allowed word.
2. For a positive grant under parity, every paid word has a completing new word of the required parity. The transported zero reference extends by zeros on the quiet histories in this test. In the exceptional zero-fresh birth, the sole child takes all loose distinctions. Sum the old parity equations: every already shared distinction occurs twice and cancels. The remaining loose values have even sum. This proof applies to *every lawful old parity word*. The sole child's inherited word already satisfies the constraint. There is nothing it must write.
3. Under not-all-equal an admitted birth has at least one fresh distinction, including the single remaining join. If the paid reference contains both values, any extension stays mixed. If it is constant, setting at least one new value opposite suffices. Both archived reference conventions therefore extend on every binding. The primary convention chooses the first legal fresh reference in its transported slot order. The alternative uses all opposite values on a homogeneous paid reference. These are choices of reference within separate comparisons. No choice of an actual fresh word follows from them under unrestricted lawful completion.
4. Use the local fresh references simultaneously. They leave every old value untouched and satisfy every new relation. This supplies an all-reference element of the exact joint completion set. Under minimum-deviation filling it is uniquely at zero cost. Under all legal filling it remains an element. Old holdings and references persist, so the argument repeats at every funded extension, even when later allocations may depend on the actual past. Every ordinary funded structural continuation from this quiet prefix, with its physical allocations, can be followed quietly as long as it exists. The whole-parent veto passes each proposed join on that path.

The above check discharges that reference premise after binding the actual payments. Any available local record of such a past also admits the quiet continuation, since the full past does.

A stopped or unfunded round makes no new holding. Conditioning on a branch reaching a target that can form only after a veto is a different question. Once a different value has already appeared, a later payment may require a compensating value. Likewise, fixing a simultaneous sibling to a nonreference word already makes that cohort active. It cannot remove the quiet option at a different child with quiet paid values. A new rule that reads older payments and forbids the reference would change the transition.

## Two continuations of the same material history

Reuse one three-loose founder and the same physical allocation in all examples. The absolute word of the founder is 000 for parity and 110 for not-all-equal, equal to its reference. The first paid marks p,u,j are numbered 0,1,2. They go into three unary children H,U,J. H creates fresh marks 3 and 4. With its p payment and the two other simultaneous newborns' actual words fixed at reference, H's lawful pair is 00 or 11 under parity. Under either not-all-equal reference it is 00, 01 or 10. In each case 00 is quiet. These are continuations of *one* actual paid prefix.

The second cut tests a longer recorded past. Let every first bank be at reference. The three proposed and funded births have parents HU, HJ and UJ. Each parent contributes to two simultaneous births. In the displayed allocation their paid pairs are (3,5), (4,7) and (6,8), and their single fresh distinctions are 9, 10 and 11. No old value or payment allocation differs across the completions below.

For the primary not-all-equal reference the UJ child actually inherits 0 and 1 on marks 6 and 8. If these second-cut banks stay quiet, a third child takes all of 9, 10 and 11. Parity gives it the single empty fresh word. ## Extent of the check

For each law/reference case the source has 6 assignments of the three distinct old marks to the three named unary display slots. Naming the child by its actual incoming mark removes this display permutation. At the next cut each of the three actual parents can allocate its two *different old marks* to two different partner obligations in 2 ways. That gives 8 physical arrangements per fixed first-slot convention. The last binding is forced. The resulting 48 named complete binding histories per law and reference each admit the all-reference continuation. This is a coverage denominator and assigns no weighting.
