This synthesis binds three independent kinds of evidence to the [frozen experiment](../../../research/LOCAL-EDIT-POLICIES-20261123-001/preregistration.md). It contributes no further proof or experiment. All sources are referee pending.

- The [conditional policy theorem](../../LOCAL-EDIT-POLICIES-20261123-001A/LOCAL-EDIT-POLICIES-20261123-001A-P001_policy_test/proof.md) tests what remains after conditioning mathematically on both old records, while letting each controller act only on its own. With the same old marked bit at both addresses, a common visible gain and equal gain marginals at the fixed addresses, the CHSH excess bounds from below the information visible as total variation in either direction. The proof also treats optional additions on separate persistent live components and verifies a six-knot example where a birth reveals a previously unknown old input.
- The [ordinary source](../../../experiments/LOCAL-EDIT-POLICIES-20261123-001C/LOCAL-EDIT-POLICIES-20261123-001C-E001_hidden_demand/result.md) has 62 knots and 109 old distinctions. The full law of the known record at each party is independent of both shortage labels. Eight ordinary births occur on a match. Fixed-address parity has maximum CHSH 9/4, with zero complete local marginal change. The corresponding not-all-equal score is about 1.50000193028.
- The [policy enumerations](../../../experiments/LOCAL-EDIT-POLICIES-20261123-001B/LOCAL-EDIT-POLICIES-20261123-001B-E001_policy_enumeration/result.md) sum every old assignment and stall. Sending at the address named by the old mark or the opposite address changes the receiver's joint parity mark-and-gain law by total variation 1/4 in either direction. The conditional generation of extra local values verifies that optimization over complete observations attains exactly 1/4. The not-all-equal optimum is 74504950140625 / 4653626441955572083. Withholding the spare versus sending it even blindly gives gain variation 1/4 under either law.
- The [wider optional-addition panel](../../../research/LOCAL-EDIT-POLICIES-20261123-001/optional_edits.md) includes both knot laws, labelled sampled and built starts and three archived explosive starts. The outcome of the theorem is consistent with actual local births including the test beyond the compulsory two-address menu.
