## The founding three and its next child

Write a for the current clock tip at the modified site and b for its possible next marked original. In the full preparation one spent holder sees a,b,z, a second sees a,w, and a third sees b,w. Their sets of names are disjoint. No spent holder sees w,z together. All other original pair accesses at that site are exactly those provided by these three holders. The only mutually witnessed unheld triple is therefore G={a,b,w}. The triple {a,b,z} has a common witness, and both possible four-donor lists require the absent w,z access. A candidate outside this site can join at most one of its real donors. Contracting each of the two displayed sites to the old a–b passage restores the predecessor's source without a three-step reduced return. This leaves the two genuine residual pairs A={a,z}, B={b,z}. In particular, a–b is absorbed once into G. It does not give a separate two-donor birth. With only z the three paid inputs lists are ab,az,bz. With only w the one list is G. Removing both leaves ab. Every w has a second loose distinction after paying G. It gives a genuine unary at the same cut. The donor z pays its two groups with distinct loose names.

At the next cut G and A share a, G and B share b, and A and B share z. There is no common parent. A fourth member avoiding each common witness would have to arise inside this same original region. There is none. In particular the unary on w cannot meet A or B. The full region therefore gives one child having exactly G,A,B for its parents. The following proof establishes the same identification in the presence of the two real exterior continuations and at every later clock occurrence.
