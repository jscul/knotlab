# Prospective survival game and held-out comparison

The owner selected movement from B208 and the one-bit “food here?” interface from B304. In this game food is the presence of one unconsumed ration at the current actual holder. It is a supplied property. This experiment measures survival on fresh grown starts. B304's separate prediction of a hidden knot law remains open.

## World and game

Draw 3 through 7 labelled founder holders uniformly in number, then a uniformly labelled tree by a uniform Prüfer code. Give each founder max(0, 3 minus its degree) plus an independent fair zero-or-one private distinctions. A supplied fair branch either uses these counts directly or raises each count to at least its ordinary first bill, retaining its existing private count. A stable seed supplies all randomness. Keep every admitted stall and closure. Founder histories are lawful under both laws. Run the existing unconstrained ordinary grouping and fewest-fresh growth functions. Record the true parents, paid distinction from each and actual grants. Both laws receive the identical founders. Exact decorated-tree isomorphism keys forget founder names and preserve private multiplicities. Never use a private world field in a policy.

Initially the cursor is at a uniformly drawn founder, chosen once per seed and shared by all policies and laws. Founders have no rations. Before game turns 1 and 5 offer at most one complete ordinary formation apiece, stopping growth earlier on stall or closure. Every funded new holder starts with one ration. Earlier holders and spent rations persist. This is a finite slice of ordinary growth and a supplied schedule in game turns. Formation order carries no elapsed game time. A policy can eat or choose an anonymous local slot for a specific shared distinction. The move must follow that exact distinction to its other actual holder. Display labels, seed, hidden law, global holder names, formation count, other rations, neighboring degrees and turn counters are unavailable to policies. The permitted information is current food (one bit or blind), own energy, own previous action and its local movement menu. An encounter's slot labels are a seeded permutation independent of the law and food. Supply matched independent per-turn random streams for choosing eat versus move and for uniform tie-breaking over actual ports. No forecaster has an externally provided route.

Every bot starts at energy 6 with capacity 10. On a turn, apply its action, charge one energy, add four if it ate a present ration, then cap the result at 10. An empty eat remains legal and costs one. Reaching zero is death including that last turn. No food regenerates. With the two complete opportunities there are finitely many rations R, so no episode can exceed 6+4R turns regardless of policy. Observe every episode to death, including growth stalls and unsuccessful eats. No survival censoring.

## Policies and frozen split

The strong blind script tries eating initially and after each move, then moves. A second blind comparator gives eat and move equal probability independent of port count. The food-sensing script eats when food is present, otherwise moves. With zero exits every bot must eat. All use the same actual move tie rule. One standard tabular Q learner sees exactly the sensing bot's observation and can return the same action IDs. Exchangeable control slots share a move value and ties among them use the same uniform rule. Give one reward per completed turn including death, with no meal reward. Use zero initialization, discount 1, no terminal bootstrap, alpha = 0.35/(1+n/24)^0.6 at n prior visits to a state/action; linearly decay exploration from 0.35 to 0.05 over training, then use a fixed greedy policy. Greedy ties/unseen states move if possible. State = food, energy, previous action and number of anonymous local slots.

Predeclare a single final fitted model. Mix the two laws during 12 passes over 128 distinct founder keys and two laws (3072 training episodes). Use 64 structurally disjoint evaluation keys with four matched tapes each under each law. Stable, domain-separated BLAKE2s seeds use compact JSON of [20261121,domain,...parts], eight-byte digest, personalization KRAT2026. Partition a founder key before play. Hash remainder zero modulo four is reserved for evaluation. Keep the first distinct admissible keys from the seeded stream in each fold, at most 8192 candidates. Exclude all development and designated spectator keys from both sets. Report unmatched target bank sizes if the stream exhausts and the actual unique denominators. Initial laws are paired; hold out whole founder structures even under renaming. The training order shuffles the 256 eligible seed/law cases per pass, without evaluation feedback. Use identical per-start/per-replicate action tapes for all evaluation arms and both laws. Apply the trained greedy policy without updating; verify its hash. Predeclare spectator seed 208304 and tape 0, both laws, independent of performance. If it has no births use it as a recorded stall and also show the first seed after it with a funded initial cohort, selected solely on that structural criterion and excluded from both banks.

Primary outcome is number of completed turns until death. Report mean by law and policy, paired learner minus sensing and sensing minus strong blind, with 95% percentile bootstrap intervals over the 64 whole starts (2,000 resamples, four tapes kept together); keep wins/ties/losses and seed/tape results. Compare action agreement with sensing on the learner's own visited states. Detect identical under-law histories, floor/ceiling effects and weak access to food. Bill food, hunger, cursor, repertoire of controls, anonymous slot labels, external policies, randomness, observation, reward and spectator layout as game scaffolds. No claim that the knots generate food, sensation or a learned controller.

First verify lawful different starts under both laws, seeded reset, real movement, charge-on-empty and permanent consumption, then automatically run training and held-out play unattended. Stop at the measured comparison whatever its sign and a seed-replayable spectator artifact. This is a bounded empirical game experiment. The independent review must precede freezing its design.
