# One change to a connected start

**One extra loose distinction.** On the witnessed path X₀,…,X_d, supply the left endpoint and each intermediate knot with two loose distinctions. Place a spent three-place witness between each neighboring pair, holding one distinction with its left knot and two with its right. Supply the right end with one loose distinction, or with two. Both preparations are connected and lawful under both laws. At size two the left unary product exists in both. The second right opening lets a right unary product be born. Each outward tip continues on its own. Their witnessed separation at size m≥2 in the two-product preparation is d+m−1. [Complete physical comparisons and the endpoint reading](mediator.md)

To count effects use consecutive intervals in the identified path. Give its old endpoints indices 0 and d in the proof. The left product releases at [−1,0]. The optional right one releases at [d,d+1]. The left-ended form stops at d without the additional supply and has no right boundary with it. At size m the intervals containing the left release number min(m−1,d+1) and m−1 respectively. There are max(0,m−d−2) extra left descendants, each containing the right release. The first extra is [−1,d+1] at size d+3. Its two actual parents [−1,d] and [0,d+1] carry the two separate ancestries. Thus an unmarked count of later left output changes by one at that size and the difference then increases by one per size. Already at d+2 the corresponding left-descended interval [−1,d] has a changed fresh count from the prepared right end. The pure left member retains one unary and one two-parent child, each receiving two fresh distinctions. This separates the earliest changed cost, the first new mixed birth, and a change to the intact receiver's own children. Whole physical cuts were compared at d=2,3,5,8.

**Keeping both products in both preparations.** Start from two paths l₀,…,l_a and r₀,…,r_b, with fresh two on every path knot and its separate one-left/two-right witnesses. Two spent holders z,y connect the full start in both versions. The knot z holds two distinctions with v=l_a, y holds two with w=r₀, and z and y hold one between them. Initially neither spent holder witnesses a pair of live knots across the paths. Add one new distinction shared by z and w. This gives the required witness for v,w, without altering any old loose count. The two outside releases anchored at l₀ and r_b exist and remain pure in both versions. In the comparison without the new shared distinction the two witnessed growing parts are separate forever. With it, the new map is one two-ended path and their first common birth is at size d+3 for d=a+b+1. No earlier shared output is possible. Both individual inclusive counts remain m−1 and individual inclusive fresh totals 2(m−1). Only their joint output changes. At (a,b)=(2,2),(2,4),(3,5) this was compared through sizes 9,11,13 respectively, one beyond the first common birth. One new shared distinction at an existing witness is also enough to advance the first meeting of descendants from size 11 to size 8 *within one already productive part*, leaving both releases in place. One loose distinction next to an existing right release instead leaves the first joint birth at size eight but changes its fresh count from two to three, with both products present. As a null, an additional distinction directly shared by two live knots leaves all later production fixed when it does not change the witnessed pair relation or the loose counts. These alternatives separate a changed physical holding from a newly available join. One added distinction is the least positive edit of this kind. In the three-return source a change to a single holding of an existing distinction suffices with no change to either the 21 knots or the 60 distinctions. An already connected seven-knot source supports mediated meetings with zero edits. No change to the start can give two simultaneously released noncandidate pure products their first direct contact after an intervening separate generation under the ordinary law.
