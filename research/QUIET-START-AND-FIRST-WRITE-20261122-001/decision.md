## Final report as sent

**Yes. Minimum filling makes a quiet start stay quiet. Under both laws a birth can write the first deviation if it may use any lawful fresh values. Neither law forces that deviation.** The reference itself remains a lawful choice at every funded birth.

The clearest case starts with a completely quiet not-all-equal founder. One newborn wrote **01** on two new marks whose reference was **00**. Its siblings stayed quiet. It paid the changed mark to a later knot. At the proposed join, the two parents held **001 and 101** against references **001 and 001**. That paid mark was the only deviation anywhere in the reached state. With the veto on, four payments funded four separate children. With it off on the same state, two payments funded a joint child and the other two funded separate children. [Decisive physical record](first_write.md)

Under parity the first nonquiet write had to be **11**. Both marks were paid, and each recipient had to write a further deviation. Under the alternate not-all-equal reference, the changed payment also required a companion at its recipient. A separate matched comparison held both siblings' first writes fixed and changed only the designated newborn’s lawful first fill. The same funded join then changed from allowed to vetoed.
