## What holds

- **Items 1–3.** Each failing pair’s two holder pairs interleave exactly when its cycle has an odd number of descents. Of the 24 orders of four holders, 4 have one descent, 16 have two and 4 have three. Reversing a cycle flips four listings. Reversing a reference flips an even number. Thus the sign is the sum of forward listings modulo two in every setting. Balance and weak balance both give 2F ≡ 0 (mod 2), where F is the number of failing pairs. A two-colouring gives two forward listings per cycle. Equal cycles cancel in pairs. At double coverage the sign is also the parity of the same-order pairs. Separating a pinched knot there is unique up to names and preserves sign and orientability. The cohomological statement in item 3 applies component by component. [Hand deductions](hand_check.md)

Two independently drawn order settings agree pointwise on 49,266 small-code choices, on the reconstructed minimal listings, and on **all 80,005** choices from the seeded patterns. The latter come from 154 accepted patterns in 450 attempts, including one with no nonempty choice. There are zero sign or implication failures.
