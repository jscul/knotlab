its set of age-eight parents. Two of these children are candidates in the

age-nine roster consists of the children sharing an age-eight parent. A

## The replacement test

A replacement for one of the 35-and-0 children would have to avoid all
5,808 of its universal parent names and still meet the nine companions.
The six old names \(7120,8326,15714,10065,12040,8626\) are pairwise
compatible, avoid that universal set and meet every companion. They leave
176 names of the universal set compatible with all six.

This answers the relaxed test that allows any old compatible set meeting
the nine companions. The full test requires an actual maximal old support
that avoids the replaced child and meets every other member of the full
round-ten group. The remaining children of that maximal extension and
those potential competitors are unresolved. The certified lower bound
on size supplies no upper bound at the preregistered replacement threshold.
