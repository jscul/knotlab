## What the search and selectors see

Use ten new nine-positive predicates on three ternary labels. Three are invariant under all six simultaneous label permutations. Other tasks preserve only some permutations or only the identity. A matched task agrees with x=y on every training answer and interchanges its held answers at (1,1,1) and (1,0,0). The same target-independent split holds out the nine inputs satisfying z=x+y+2 modulo three. No candidate search or blind ranking receives the task name, specification, true symmetry group, constructive solution, certification labels or a counterexample. Input triples and the three-label alphabet are public. The supplier of tasks, truth tables and paid certificates is separate from the task-local fit.

At the training input pairs actually observed for each of the five nonidentity label permutations, compare the answers on both ends. Ignore fixed points. Retain a permutation if at least two distinct unordered moved pairs are observed, their endpoints include both answer classes, and all compared labels agree. Use the retained permutations to transport observed answers to publicly enumerable unseen inputs. Take weighted votes. For a candidate the penalty is the sum of the absolute vote margins on those inputs where its predicted bit differs from the majority. Skip zero margins. Fit these five decisions independently afresh from the 18 examples, using the same fixed procedure across tasks.

Compare this fit with shortest expanded guarded length and first encounter, and with blindly trusting all five renamings. All tie breaks use the shortest rule. Compare all three on the very same ordered set of distinct training-perfect programs from one stream. If the set is empty, abstain. With a terminal cap fixed in advance, freeze the three choices before opening the held answers. Report exact returned choices, held bits correct, and conditional pairwise wins on every syntactic pair with exactly one fully correct program. Report the number of episodes having such a pair, and how often a correct candidate existed. Arm comparisons keep all failures. Do not treat programs or different seeds of the same task as independent tasks.
