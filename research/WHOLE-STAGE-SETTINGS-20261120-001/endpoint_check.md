# What is visible in the parity trial

The source has three two-valued marks at each detector. The three bits on the left are independent and fair. Each passive pair of parity knots identifies its two terminals in value while keeping them distinct in identity. On fixing that value, its two internal shared bits have precisely two completions. Concatenating such pieces gives the same number of hidden completions for every three-bit input. The complete source's local parity constraints also have the same number of completions for every terminal word. The experiment checks those local constraints, their projections, and their actual ordinary births. [Preparation and ordinary computation](../../experiments/WHOLE-STAGE-SETTINGS-20261120-001C/WHOLE-STAGE-SETTINGS-20261120-001C-E001_masking/result.md)

For given independent hidden readiness labels u and v, interchange bits 0/1 under u and 1/2 under v, then repeat the same two interchanges. If either is off, the terminal correspondence is the identity. If both are on, the bit at Bob's mark 0 comes from a different Alice mark. Each observer's three marked bits are uniform for either far readiness label. The rule for preparing a correspondence uses its **one** hidden label. The four joins of the two choices have different consequences by composition. No setting has yet been supplied and no rule has compared the actual pair of settings.

For the two external address settings x,y, whole-stage funding is the ordinary condition F=[x=u][y=v]. The local predicate outputs its mark 0 if the detector acquired a child, and zero otherwise. On a funded trial the two selected bits agree in three of the four possible address pairs, and in the fourth they are independent. The other three quarters of trials at every setting yield (0,0). In outcome order (00,01,10,11), use 32 equal units at each setting. The first three setting pairs each give (28,0,0,4), while 11 gives (26,2,2,2). The correlators are (1,1,1,3/4) and the signed and maximum CHSH values are 9/4. Both observers see one with probability 1/8 in every setting.

The [independent checker](endpoint_check.py) enumerates the four pairs of hidden labels and eight three-bit input words, giving 32 equally weighted cases per setting after the constant extension factors cancel. It compares the joint local law of readiness label, entire three-bit word, structural birth flag and outcome. For the remaining local holdings and values in the declared observer regions the connected source calculation gives the same conditional law given those records for both far settings. This proves zero total variation for the *whole declared record*. The finite experiment admits arbitrary but prescribed observation ranges using identical additional passive pieces. It does not hide both labels from a reader allowed to inspect the whole source.

Several controls distinguish the effect. Replacing the two interchanges with commuting copies gives score 2. Ignoring the other party's shortage and letting the two parties complete separately gives 5/4. Always reading the terminal marks gives 3/2. Giving Bob's high readiness label probability 3/4 produces Alice's outcome TV 1/8 and whole-record TV 1/4. Revealing both preparation labels raises the whole-record TV to 1/2 for each own setting, or 1 conditional on an otherwise ready party at a known type. An intermediate reader who can compare the before/after triples around the far interchange can tell the far readiness label whenever the two interchanged bits differ. The enlarged records in the checker give TV 1/4 on each side. All these distances compare distributions for a free far setting.

## The choice of edit matters even without opening the apparatus

This supplemental diagnostic was defined after registration. It preserves the original address tables. Each observer can inspect which of their two addresses needs an opening. Let an independent message tell Bob to add one there or at his other address. If Alice keeps using a fixed address, her detector gets a child with probability 1/2 versus zero. Its whole-record TV is 1/2 and her reported mark's TV is 1/4. If Alice always supplies her own local shortage, the birth flag transmits Bob's message with certainty. Her binary mark then has TV 1/2. Each edit still attaches just one loose distinction at one's own holder.

More generally suppose the common birth flag is H=f_x(U)g_y(V), where the two parties know their own two possible readiness values and the receiver can read H. Bob's local instruction may choose the address maximizing g or the address minimizing g. The increase in the probability of a birth at fixed Alice setting is exactly the probability that Alice is ready and Bob's two choices differ. The first event includes the second in the common sample, so changes cannot cancel. No independence of the preparations is required. This gives a signalling test under freely selected *local policies*. The four fixed-address tests in the main experiment select the input in a different way.

## Quantum comparison

The four parity tables have no bipartite quantum realization with two binary measurements per observer. If three correlations E00, E01, E10 equal one, use the Hilbert–Schmidt vectors (Ax ⊗ I)√ρ and (I ⊗ By)√ρ for local Hermitian measurement contractions. Each has norm at most one. Each correlation of one forces equality of the two corresponding unit vectors. All four vectors then coincide and E11 must be one. The observed E11 is 3/4. This test concerns the full table even though the CHSH number alone falls below the quantum upper bound.
