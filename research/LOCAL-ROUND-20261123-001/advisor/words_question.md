Background, in the program's glossary words. A structure is a set of knots, each holding distinctions (its places); a distinction held by one knot is loose, by two is shared. A knot with a usable loose distinction is a member of the live roster. Growth proceeds by births: a new knot takes one loose distinction from each of its parents (one parent: an offshoot; two or more: a join) and receives fresh distinctions. In a complete round every member reads the roster once and all births are made together. A member is short when more joins are asked of it than it holds; under one treatment a short member "waits" (makes nothing this round, reads again next round), under another it "retires" (its loose distinctions are barred).
