# Class-4 pair failures with linear flows are #P-hard if gadget counts are local up to relabeling

Status: Proved

## Standard statement

Let p ≥ 5 be prime, r | p − 1, and G = N ⋊ C_r with N = exp 𝔫 for a graded nilpotent Lie algebra 𝔫 over F_p with a
grading-preserving C_r action. Fix a sector order d | r. Suppose the ideal 𝔪_d generated by the components of nonzero character
has class 4, and its class-3 quotient has a clean edge: a pair of characters failing the pair condition, with the clean-edge
conditions holding modulo γ₄. The gadget knots of the earlier construction then have equations in degree 4. This note proves an
implication. Suppose those gadget counts are local up to a triangular change of the degree-3 variables, and a single edge has a
linear structure. Then counting homomorphisms from π₁ of the closed 3-manifolds S³_{d/p}(K) into G is #P-hard. Both conditions are
verified, the first experimentally, for six census algebras.

## Statement

Notation as in E, F and A of the predecessor and this epic: slots θ₀ = α (vertex), θ₁ = β, θ₂ = γ = α + β; root sets R_i; leaves
ℓ₀, ℓ₁, ℓ₂; private data π = (π₁, π₂) of slots 1 and 2. 𝔪 = 𝔪_d has class 4, the layers are its lower central series, and the
gadget is the zero-jump gadget of E.

**Affine form.** Fix the data D of degrees ≤ 2 (vertex states and private data) on the variety: the private equations of degrees 2
and 3 hold for every edge. They involve no degree-3 data. The degree-4 equations are then affine in the degree-3 free data c:
E_Γ(c) = A_Γ c + b_Γ(D). Assemble the *local system* from single-edge gadgets:
- each edge's private rows;
- each vertex's rows: its own terms, plus the sum over incident edges of the single-edge row minus the same row at zero private
  data.

Call it E_loc(c) = A_loc c + b_loc(D).

**Hypothesis (L4): locality up to relabeling.** For every graph Γ and every D on the variety, A_Γ = A_loc and
b_Γ(D) − b_loc(D) ∈ im A_loc.

Then for each D, the solution sets of E_Γ and E_loc in c are translates of one another. In particular they have the same size.

**Condition (LS): linear flows.** For every pair of vertex states (σ_u, σ_v) of a single edge:
- the private solutions are S(σ_u, σ_v) = {(π₁, π₂) : π₁ ∈ S₁, π₂ ∈ U(π₁)}, with U(π₁) a linear subspace of the slot-2 data;
- the endpoint terms φ_u, φ_v are linear in π₂ for fixed π₁. These are the vertex rows minus their values at zero private data.
- The vertex rows vanish at zero private data.

**Condition (RW): the rank witness.**
- Every private row vanishes on the diagonal σ_u = σ_v, so F(σ, σ) = F_max.
- For some pair-failing Δ, zero private data solve at (0, Δ) and at (Δ, 0), and some private data do not. So 1 ≤ F(0, Δ),
  F(Δ, 0) < F_max.

**Theorem 7.** If (L4), (LS) and (RW) hold for an edge at some d | r, counting |Hom(π₁ S³_{d/p}(K), G)| over knot diagrams K is
#P-hard, for each fixed G.

**Corollary 7.1.** (LS) and (RW) hold for the edge (α, β) = (1, 1), slots (1, 1, 2), of free class 4 on the generator characters
(1, 1), (0, 1, 1) and (1, 1, 1) at d = 5. So, if (L4) holds for their gadget families, counting is #P-hard for these three
algebras. The same holds for their d = 10 versions (2, 2), (0, 2, 2), (2, 2, 2), which fail at order 5 with the same algebra up to
scaling characters by a unit. That is six census algebras, decided conditionally on (L4).

## Proof of Theorem 7

1. **(L4) gives the local count.** For each D, the number of degree-3 completions solving every degree-4 equation is the same for
   E_Γ and E_loc. The pinned count is C times the sum over D on the variety of that number, where C accounts for the top-layer
   free data. So it equals the count of the local system:

     P_τ(K_Γ) = C Σ_σ Σ_π Π_e [π_e ∈ S(σ_u, σ_v)] Π_v [Σ_{e ∋ v} φ_{v,e} = 0].

   Here π now includes the degree-3 data. φ_{v,e} is e's term at its endpoint v, a function of (σ_u, σ_v, π_e). The vertices' own
   terms vanish by (LS).
2. **Fourier duality.** Write [x = 0] = p^{−k} Σ_{λ ∈ F_p^k} ω^{⟨λ, x⟩} at each vertex:

     P_τ(K_Γ) = C p^{−k|V|} Σ_{σ, λ} Π_{e=(u,v)} Â(σ_u, λ_u; σ_v, λ_v),
     Â(σ, λ; σ′, λ′) = Σ_{π ∈ S(σ, σ′)} ω^{⟨λ, φ_u(π)⟩ + ⟨λ′, φ_v(π)⟩}.

   No conservation (φ_v = −φ_u) is needed.
3. **Non-negativity.** By (LS), with φ_u = M_u(π₁)π₂ and φ_v = M_v(π₁)π₂,

     Â = Σ_{π₁ ∈ S₁} |U(π₁)| · [M_u(π₁)ᵀλ + M_v(π₁)ᵀλ′ vanishes on U(π₁)],

   a non-negative integer, equal to F(σ, σ′) at λ = λ′ = 0.
4. **Hardness.** Subdividing every edge by a vertex ordered last gives P_τ(K_{Γ′}) = C′ Σ_x Π_{uv} M′(x_u, x_v), with
   M′ = Â Âᵀ symmetric, non-negative and integral.
   - On {x₀, x₁} = {(0, 0), (Δ, 0)}, the rows of Â restrict to (F_max, F(0, Δ)) and (F(Δ, 0), F_max), which are independent by
     (RW). So the principal minor of M′ on {x₀, x₁}, the Gram matrix of these two rows, is nonsingular.
   - M′(x₀, x₁) ≥ F_max F(Δ, 0) > 0, so x₀ and x₁ lie in one component, and M′(x₀, x₀) ≥ F_max² > 0 is a loop.
   - Unlike the conserved case, the diagonal of M′ need not be positive at every (σ, λ). The argument does not need it.
   - So M′ has a non-bipartite component of rank ≥ 2, and EVAL(M′) is #P-hard (Bulatov–Grohe; Cai–Chen–Lu, Theorem 2.1).
     Isolation by Lemma I and the total count across sectors follow as in E §5. ∎

## Proof of Corollary 7.1

For the three algebras, every component of 𝔪_d in its layer k has character k mod 5 for k ≤ 4. A component counts y or z factors
(character 1), and x (character 0) is outside 𝔪_d. The slots (1, 1, 2) give R₀ = R₁ = {1, 4} and R₂ = {2, 3}.
- **(LF)** Layer 1 has character 1 ∉ R₂, so slot 2 has no layer-1 data.
- **No slot-2 equations in degree 4.** Layer 4 has character 4 ∉ R₂.
- **Summand terms vanish.**
  - The vertex and slot-1 blocks have free data only at character 1. Character 4 occurs only in layer 4, the top. So each such
    block's representation is t ↦ exp(v(t) X) τ^{…} for one twisted cocycle v and one Lie element X. Multiples of X commute, so it
    is a homomorphism in every class, and its own obstructions vanish.
  - Slot 2's own terms would need two slot-2 data, of degree ≥ 2 + 2 = 4, at a character of R₂. Layer 4 has character 4, so none
    exist.
- **(LS), the solution sets.**
  - Slot 2's equations come from log W₂. Its monomials containing ℓ₂ have weight ≥ 1 + 1 + 2 = 4, and slot 2 has no equations in
    degree 4. So slot 2's equations do not involve π₂.
  - Slot 1's equations come from log W₁. Every monomial contains ℓ₂ (E, Lemma 3). Monomials with ℓ₂ twice have weight
    ≥ 2 + 2 + 1 = 5. Slot 1's own terms vanish. So slot 1's equations are homogeneous linear in π₂.
- **(LS), the endpoint terms.**
  - **W₀ and W₁.** Their contributions come from conjugators whose monomials contain ℓ₂: bundle-strand shifts, BCH terms with the
    copies' colours, partial-passage conjugations, junction twists. Every product of two such contributions has degree ≥ 6. So
    these contributions are linear in π₂ with no π₂-free part.
  - **W₂.** On the variety its parts below the top vanish. Its degree-2 part [ℓ₀^{(1)}, ℓ₁^{(1)}] has character 2 ∈ R₂ and is
    killed by the deficit equations. Every degree-3 monomial has character 3 ∈ R₂ and is killed by slot 2's degree-3 equations.
    So W₂ is central, and its shifts reach only slot 2's degree-4 duals, which do not exist.
  - **Vertex rows** vanish at zero private data, by the summand terms above.
- **(RW).**
  - On the diagonal, Y₀ = 1, so W₁ = W₂ = 1. The private rows are then slot terms, which vanish.
  - At (0, Δ) and (Δ, 0), with Δ ∈ V_{1,1} nonzero, zero private data solve: Y₁ = Y₂ = 1, so every W_j = 1 (E, Lemma 3). The
    degree-2 deficit equation [Δ, X]_{(2)} = 0 cuts the slot-1 datum X to the line through Δ, since [Δ, X] ≠ 0 for X ∉ F_p Δ.
- **The d = 10 versions.** At order 5 they are the same algebras with characters multiplied by 3 (2 · 3 = 6 ≡ 1 mod 5). Every
  condition above is invariant under scaling characters by a unit. ∎

## Evidence and checks

- **Experiment** `CLASS4-PAIRS-20261028-001B-E001` (frozen; verdict supported). It tests (L4) for the three algebras on seven graph
  shapes up to K₄ and three configuration types, with the cumulative design and an algebra with slot-2 layer-1 data as controls.
  It also samples (LS) and (RW). The result:
  - In all 63 configurations, the linear parts agree and the constant parts differ by an element of the column space. That
    column space is a proper subspace in every configuration.
  - The constant parts differ in 35 of the 63, so the relabeling is needed.
  - Single edges have the linear-flow structure and the rank-witness ingredients.
  - The cumulative design fails locality, already below layer 4. The algebra with slot-2 layer-1 data fails the linear structure.
  - For free class 4 on (1, 1, 1), no sampled datum admits a degree-3 completion, so its counts agree by both being zero.
- **`check.py`.**
  1. The character structure of the three algebras and of the order-5 coarsenings of the three d = 10 ones: components of layer
     k ≤ 4 have character k. Hence (LF), no slot-2 equations in degree 4, and data of the vertex and slot 1 at one character.
  2. For free class 4 on (1, 1), exhaustively over the 11⁵ private configurations at a diagonal and a pair-failing pair:
     - the solution sets split as S₁ × U(π₁) with U linear;
     - the endpoint terms are linear in π₂ with no π₂-free part;
     - every Fourier value Â(λ, λ′) at 200 random (λ, λ′) is real and non-negative;
     - (RW) holds.
  3. The experiment's result is bound by hash, and its verdict is read.

## Scope and gaps

- **Proved.** Theorem 7 (an implication) and Corollary 7.1 (the conditions (LS) and (RW) for six census algebras).
- **Supported, not proved: (L4).** What the experiment shows, and what a proof needs:
  - Blocks lying between moved strands are conjugated uniformly by each insertion, which is a gauge and leaves their equations
    unchanged.
  - The degree-3 conjugators also shift junction strands. The shift is absorbed upstream as a translation of another edge's
    degree-3 data (the relabeling). In the scoping, it was a translation of the preceding edge's slot-2 coordinates.
  - A proof needs a general absorption lemma: every junction twist lands at a character carried by a free degree-3 datum of the
    block that absorbs it. Equivalently, the degree-4 obstruction of the gadget knot (a Massey-type product) is local modulo its
    indeterminacy.
  - In the three algebras the twists are at character 3 = −γ, which the preceding slot 2 carries. That is because 2γ + α ≡ 0 mod 5.
    Algebras where this fails are untested.
- **Open.** The other twenty (K)-only census algebras (d = 7, 9), which need (LS) analysis and summand braids not in the library.
  The (B₀), (B₂′) and order-3 cross-layer families.
