**Cold review**

1. **P1 is settled by construction.** Chains are 42.4% and count as "proved" because growth is the population's entry criterion and L_any = L_ray by definition. P1 then needs only about 2.6 points, which P2's lower bound already implies. Report the forgetting share on the 7,582 non-chains as the headline, with chains as a separate, labelled row.

2. **P2 is loose and its denominators conflict.** The 8–25% band is wide around a sample from the same population. "43 of 300" and "reproduce 43 of 66" do not agree: 43/66 is 65%, outside the band unless weighting explains it. State the denominator, and whether the sample families are excluded from the test of P2.

3. **The undecided cell mixes two things.** "Non-forgetting" at a capped r is "none found", not "not forgetting". State the r cap, and separate families with a certificate of non-forgetting (for example a recurring non-rectangular product) from families that merely timed out.

4. **Empty products pass rectangularity trivially.** Count them separately, since the bound is vacuous on those paths.

5. **The first gate cannot test the census claim.** If forgetting forces growth, the gate only checks the code and the gap argument, against finite-depth "boundedness". Label it a consistency check.

6. **"Members anywhere" rests on the limit relations.** A bond into a side call does not pass through that call's limit relation. This matters most for the 657 stratum and is entirely the unreviewed argument's burden.

7. **Independence should start earlier.** The re-derivation must cover the limit relations and the R_i themselves, not only the products.

8. **"Proved counter" overclaims.** The result is a lower bound of 2 + ⌊s/r⌋ against an upper bound of L_ray, which is not a count. Report the distribution of r, since a large r makes the bound weak.

9. **The denominator hides a selection.** The share is over provable growers (13,279 of 223,261 draws); say so in the headline.
