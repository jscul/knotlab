For every m≥3 in the mutually sharing start and t≥1, the round's members consist of the ordered m-term sums of t−1 and m extra members, one attached to each sum concentrated in a single term. At t=1 all attach to the zero sum. A step between sums transfers one unit. For m=2 the first join fails for lack of a third witness. The first-round offshoots explain all 23 old census starts, including the two denser families. A cap of three breaks the four-entry family into parts in which at most min(2r+1,n) members of a part of size n are within r steps of any one member, with equality at the best member. The independent extended census agreed at all 4,274 reached observations; every knot at every reached stage passed under each knot law. Five of the twelve previously unused five-knot starts stalled. Under the most-held rule the two programs agreed on 3,305 first meetings per knot law.
