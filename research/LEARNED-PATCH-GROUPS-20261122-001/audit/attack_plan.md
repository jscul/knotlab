# Learned patch groups audit plan

The execution auditor owns this plan and its checker. The producer owns the
experiment implementation and releases. The audit begins result replay only
after the coordinator releases the scored run. Pre-run checks use source,
data-only census checks, and synthetic fixtures outside the registered panel.

## Run authorization

- Regenerate the bitmap census and independently verify orbit ancestry, exact
  images, ordering, per-class counts, and split membership. Check that the
  primary panel contains complete unseen shape variants, that no training
  orbit contributes a shape to it, and that the held quarter turns supply
  no training or tuning inputs. Verify matched control conditions as frozen.
- Trace raw-pixel acquisition through feature generation, anonymous bin
  presentation, action and environment reward, credit, and durable state.
  Verify that row keys, glyph descriptions, grouping semantics and test
  answers do not cross those boundaries. Require the blind process to reload
  from the physical export without retained numeric adaptive state.
- Count the exhaustive subset roster. Independently reproduce the exact
  objective and tie rule on small synthetic fixtures, including interchangeable
  bins and opposing optimal subsets. Permute IDs and test that training,
  selected subsets and predictions transform as registered. Check adjacency,
  pixel weights, bin order and geometry cannot choose among anonymous bins.
- Inspect the parity and not-all-equal open relations and their prepared
  executable alternatives. Challenge malformed physical state, accidental
  cache restoration, absent phase receipts, altered locks, prohibited
  metadata, and overwrite attempts. Check that the fit and blind release
  digests are anchored outside the runner before the next phase.
- Match learner, earlier centroid and numeric twin on reward exposure and
  training cases. Charge exhaustive subset selection and retention of any
  derived rule. Verify exact comparator equations and the prior decision rule.
  Use the registered finite weighting and show the class and orientation
  breakdown. Leave inference to unseen font populations outside the study.

## Result audit

- Independently regenerate the panel and recompute the fitting objective,
  winning subsets, ties, and every blind action. Reconstruct fitted state
  from the receipts and from physical checkpoint words independently. Compare
  the two laws, the numeric twin and the centroid on the same split cells.
- Reproduce the fixed-pixel cleared, donor, sham and restoration comparisons
  in both conventions. Count eligible reversals and fixed-action ties before
  drawing conclusions. Validate external anchor times and all phase bindings.
- Reproduce the shifted reward schedule with offset 17 in the fixed training
  order as a matched pairing control. For the registered post-release ID
  check, new bin i receives old bin (5i+3) mod 16. Transport an unordered
  partition and reflect both means when its represented side changes.
- Report per-condition, per-turn and per-letter counts, unseen-shape support,
  descriptor overlap and representative failures. Keep any post-score
  description separate from the frozen readout and score. This execution
  replay is separate from the project's formal scientific referee.
