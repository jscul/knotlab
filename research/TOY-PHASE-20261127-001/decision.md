## What was found

- **One knot is one toy bit.** Under both parity laws and all six coordinate choices, a three-place parity knot's four
  allowed states, three distinctions and three allowed changes are a toy bit's four underlying states, three questions
  and three shifts, with every incidence kept. The owner's choice between 00/11 and 01/10 is the toy bit's rule that
  each question is the sum of the other two. The not-all-equal knot is no toy bit: six allowed states, one allowed
  change.
- **The phase on one knot.** Exactly 4 of the 24 permutations of the knot's allowed states keep A's value. They form
  Z2×Z2, every one of order at most two. Two of them, the swaps inside one class, carry the B-read to the c-read. The
  third is the knot's own allowed change {B, c}, the literature's Z, which carries both other reads to their negations.
  So the model has Z; the toy theory adds two maps whose product is Z; quantum mechanics has S, whose square is Z.
- **Growth's closure under both theories.** Under all 216 assignments the even law growth makes is a pure toy state, and
  its 64 reading probabilities are the toy theory's. Under all 216 Pauli assignments the four knot operators multiply to
  −1, so the even law has no qubit state, and the odd law has exactly one, which reproduces the record's target. The
  best toy state scores 3/4 (over all 1,080). Seven of the 15 pairings give a toy state. In the other eight, the three
  knots that each hold both distinctions of a reader fail to commute pairwise.
- **Two forms.** With each reader's second read made from its first by a phase, the close is even for all 16 toy choices
  and odd for all 16 qubit choices. The closing phase is twice the sum of the readers' phases, which Z2×Z2 sends to the
  identity and Z4 to Z. Without a phase on the state no scored context is certain.
- **The supplied pieces.** In all three of the birth-sign study's coherent constructions, the two reads assigned to each
  reader anticommute, so their product squares to −1, and the four context operators multiply to −1. In two of them a
  read also flips other readers' distinctions; in the third each read takes every allowed word outside the eight. Value
  reads commute and score 3/4, and dropping the sign from the confined read gives 5/8.
- **Two readers.** Inside the stabilizer part, states and reads alike, the two-reader score is at most 2 (60 states). The
  sibling study's preparation is not a stabilizer state. With Pauli reads it scores at most 2, and its 2.1078 uses a
  non-Pauli read (best possible 2√10/3). With X and Z reads on both sides, a supplied state reaches 2√2. So the phase of
  order four carries the three-reader table on a stabilizer state, but a two-reader excess also needs to leave the
  stabilizer part.
- **One size past.** On the four-readings study's 115 certificates (79 on patterns growth closes), the toy reading makes
  every holder's law even, the certificate's operators make the total odd, and the gap is the cost of reordering in every
  case.

## Intuitions (exact links, not evidence)

- **A square root of the knot's own change.** The knot's allowed change {B, c} is the literature's Z on that knot. The
  odd close needs its square root S. Every operation on the knot's values that keeps a read is an involution, so the
  root does not exist among them; it exists only as a phase.
- **The cost of reordering is the phase group's doubling.** The account's identity (the closing sign is the cost of
  reordering) and the doubling in the phase group (the closing phase is twice the readers' phases) are one statement: in
  Z2×Z2 every doubling is the identity, so values never pay.
- **Rule R's pattern is the toy theory's knowledge rule.** On the closure, the pairs rule R makes fail to commute are the
  readers' pairs: exactly the pairs the toy theory forbids knowing together. Read as knowledge (values exist, one view at a
  time: the toy side, close to A294) the pattern closes even. Read as algebra (no joint values) it closes odd.
- **Two values are special** (row 4; [Gross 2006](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0602001)). For three-valued (and any
  odd prime number of values) distinctions the stabilizer states have non-negative Wigner functions, so the gap closes
  there.
