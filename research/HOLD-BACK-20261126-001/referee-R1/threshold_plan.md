# A threshold read at birth

This exploratory choice and sample were fixed before either law was run here. Each new knot may receive one more fresh distinction than the number it was given. With $p$ given distinctions, demand $D$ and the existing floor $L = \max(3-p,1)$, the fresh count is $\max(L,\min(D,p+1))$. The fresh count under the control is $\min(3,\max(D,L))$. The chosen ceiling is two for a one-parent offshoot given one distinction and three for a join given two. It may also differ at joins given more than two. The function and the one extra distinction are supplied choices. Whether the chosen ceiling actually gives a different count will be measured at every birth.

Draw four of the 28 distinct far-end names in the closed study's linked-pair file. Give each name equal weight, sort the names, and use `random.Random(2026112603).sample(names, 4)` in draw order. The first pair provides the timed pilot with the same settings as the rest.

All four far ends are four-knot starts. The final four digits before the at-sign give their loose counts, and the number after it identifies the site. The receiver consists of three mutually sharing knots holding one, two and two loose distinctions, sited at the one. Use the same meeting through two link knots, not-all-equal, waiting and no taking for both laws.

After rounds 3, 5 and 8 of each unchanged growth, add one loose distinction to a far member in a separate growth for each mark. Examine up to five far members linked to the receiver's own members and up to two unlinked, nearest by number of sharings and then smallest knot number. Read a mark only where both sides have own members and another round is available. Stop at round 24, after passing 6,000 knots or at the study's guard. Read only rounds completed by both growths. A hearing is the first difference in the receiver's own births, including parentage, fresh count and number of places. It is late after round 6. Require a subsequent birth in each growth and record whether both also make births in the common observation window.

Count equal and unequal fresh decisions on the actual inputs for each law, in the unchanged growths and in the continuations after the marks. Read the round of first disagreement. Compare hearings at far members with matching parentage in the two laws where available. A late hearing under the local ceiling with different decisions and continued growth would refute the need for a supplied constant of three on this sample. Universal agreement of decisions would leave those choices indistinguishable here. A negative under a differing rule would distinguish the two rules on these pairs and leave other rules open. A difference elsewhere in the growth alone cannot explain a particular hearing.

Use one process, under 2 GiB of address space and eight CPU minutes in total. If four pairs do not fit the timed estimate, retain a prefix in draw order and report the rest as not run. No outcomes from the local ceiling, marked or unchanged, were available when recording this plan.
