# Finite ancestor widths and what the current end can identify

One actual descendant of the seven-knot generator needs only six launch holders but requires fourteen compulsory holders at an intermediate size. A fixed antecedent width is therefore a different restriction from a width cap through all sizes. Separately, a periodic packet can keep its phase count forever while its exact loose counts grow. ## Literal ancestors at the bounded cuts

The checker rebuilt the seven-knot, 25-distinction source, the bare five-cycle with two loose distinctions per knot, and the four-knot live-center relay start. It checked each source's full projected parity star by wired deletion and each allowed local not-all-equal row by satisfiability. Both laws produced identical literal supports through the checked cuts, using a fixed allocation of exchangeable loose distinctions. Every allocation consumes one distinction from each actual parent.

For every terminal live knot $v$ at age $j$, calculate its compulsory ancestors $A_i(v)$ at every earlier age $i$ by taking unions of the *actual* parent sets, one age at a time. A launch subset $P$ at age $i$ can sustain an all-parent family to $v$ exactly when $A_i(v)\subseteq P$. Any family that includes $v$ must contain its ancestors. Conversely the sequence of mandatory sets is itself an all-parent family. Thus the minimum cap through all its sizes is
\[
 \max\bigl(|P|,\max_{i<k\le j}|A_k(v)|\bigr)
\]
for that specified launch and target. To reach any terminal, take the minimum over those $v$ compatible with $P$. This allows other births to be ignored. It is a finite parentage criterion and assigns no probabilities, physical origin labels, or recurrent recognition rule.

## Physical and witnessed-end readers

For the complete physical reader, at a positive complete cut of each fixture all live knots were born at the last step. None meets another live knot directly on a physical distinction. Erase them simultaneously and keep their input distinctions at earlier holders. This restores exactly the previous physical cut. The origin itself has a meeting between two live originals, which gives the stopping test on these fixtures. Independent joint permutations of the knots and distinctions passed this reconstruction at every measured cut. The method uses the whole accumulated presentation and the complete-cut promise. The weaker reader receives the current live knots, their loose counts and the shared-witness blocks. In the seven-knot and bare-cycle runs every previous live holder has at least two children and its child roster is a distinct visible witness block. Treat each current block as a previous holder. Its size is that holder's old loose count. Two such holders are adjacent in the old candidate relation precisely when their blocks intersect, since every old eligible pair occurs together in an actual birth. A triple of such holders is unheld in the recovered relation when their three blocks intersect in an observed child. In the tested identified transitions, each unheld complete triple occurs in a child and every triple in a child is unheld. For each old adjacent pair, the old held block is then the pair together with every third holder making a held triple with it. This last reconstruction uses the uniqueness of the witness of an old pair.

On these hypotheses the resulting previous end agreed with the exact physical scorer at all eight transitions of the seven-knot source and all nine of the bare five-cycle, including independent recodings of the current end. This is a finite reconstruction check. There is no inference from one successful inverse to knowing the original cut. The symbolic controls follow finite intervals in the identified unrolled map, beyond the full physical stage bound. A window's parent windows are its two endpoint deletions. For these returns each block traversal is uniquely witnessed. For a packet of primitive period $p$ and a launch window at least $p$ long with $p$ distinct reversal classes, a member $d$ sizes later requires precisely $\min(d+1,p)$ distinct launch phases. Thus a proper subset of launch phases cannot support that periodic packet indefinitely. The packet's abstract phase count stays $p$. 

The loose budget of a window is the sum of the candidate degrees of its members minus the sum of the sizes of its intervening witness blocks. In a traversed block, two old members' candidate-degree counts include their mutual meeting twice, and count each outside member twice. The subtraction removes both internal contributions and counts each available outside member once. The unrolled incidence is a tree, so no outside member is counted through two different traversed blocks. In both returns the degree sequence is $(3,3,3,2)$ and the block sizes are $(2,3,2,2)$ in traversal order. Hence adding one full period to a phase adds $p/2$ fresh distinctions, although the phase count and induced uncolored witnessed cycle remain unchanged. At launch sizes $16,24,32,40$ for periods $8,12,16,20$, the respective budget ranges are $10$–$11$, $14$–$15$, $18$–$19$ and $22$–$23$. The symbolic windows were not materialized as whole stages.
