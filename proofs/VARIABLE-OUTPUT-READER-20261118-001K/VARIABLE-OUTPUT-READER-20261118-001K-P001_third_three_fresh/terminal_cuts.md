# Bond distance five determines triples with one shrinking join

Consider the frozen source family with exactly one three-parent, two-output addition. Starting from the ternary seed, the first two additions are unary and each makes three fresh distinctions. A later three-parent addition makes three fresh distinctions, producing the unique six-place junction. All other additions make two. Every reachable rival that matches the scalar pair table through bond distance five gives the same answer for every marked triple. When a legal triple exists, the entries determine the entire set of junction supports. ## The two six-distinction supports a rival must supply

Write \(K=D+1\) for the minimum size of a bond containing a pair. The one-shrink argument forces every low-arity junction support, and the width and rank argument forces the rival to have one six-place junction and the same single shrinking join. Work on the shared distinction names after the proposed bijection. Delete the six-place junction, retaining all distinction names. The remaining ordinary incidence is connected. The first three sites are present, and each later low site took at least two parents, so at least one of those parents remains. Its two colors are therefore fixed up to their simultaneous interchange. [Rank and actual low sites](low.md)

Let \(U\) be the distinctions occurring at no low site and \(L\) those occurring at one low site. The \(U\) distinctions must be loose outputs of the six-place junction in either source. Every rival six-place support is \(U\cup S\) for some \(S\subseteq L\) of size \(6-|U|\). The rival's set of all loose distinctions is \(U\cup(L\setminus S)\), also of size six. In a simple bipartite ordinary incidence the chosen distinctions in \(S\) have distinct low holders of the same color. Both proposed six-distinction supports are parity words. Through size six every nonzero parity word is a bond. A proper subword would split it into two disjoint nonzero words, whose sizes must both be three because three is the minimum. The seed is the sole ternary word. Therefore both proposed supports must have every pair at \(K\le6\). These necessary tests use only the supplied readings and the recovered low sites. ## Exact cut records for every history

A subset of ordinary junctions contributes the symmetric difference of its selected parity supports. Call its size the cut size. The base frontier retains every live holder's loose count, their ordinary incidences and the projected neighbor sets of exhausted holders. These determine all legal continuations. The base also distinguishes the high holder while it is live and records whether the exceptional birth has occurred. First close the base frontier from the two three-site starts. Trim to the base records that can reach a post-birth, exactly-one-shrink record with a legal triple, by following the transitions backward. Every path to a legal triple in scope stays in this trim. [Exact base transitions and their literal replay](closure.md)

In the augmented state, retain the colors of the live holders and the high junction's color after its birth. Keep six slots for the high inputs and outputs. Each slot identifies its live low holder, an exhausted low holder, or an output with no low holder. Exhausted low holders in different slots are distinct by simplicity. For every choice of membership of the current live holders, and every pattern of crossing flags on the six slots after birth, retain the least cut size that attains those data. The initial three junctions can be enumerated directly.

At a new junction with \(p\) inputs and \(h\) outputs, try both membership choices. If the new junction is excluded, the total cut size stays fixed. If it is included and \(j\) of its parents were selected, the cut size changes by \(p+h-2j\). The current membership record supplies \(j\). On the exceptional birth, an input crosses exactly when its old holder and the new junction have different membership, and every output crosses exactly when the new junction is selected. On a later addition, consuming a loose high output toggles that slot's crossing flag if the new site is selected. Remap live memberships and incidence roles along the actual frontier transition, dropping exhausted holders. Two cuts with equal retained data have exactly the same possible future updates except for their present sizes, so the least size suffices in each record.

Before the shrinking join, discard cuts larger than seven. After it, discard cuts larger than six. Within this trim the one shrinking addition can decrease cut size by at most one, and every other future addition has a nonnegative change for either membership choice. This pruning consequently preserves every possible final cut of size at most six. In a terminal state the stored flags give membership in the cut for every high bit. Each other loose bit crosses precisely when its live holder belongs to the cut. A pair in the union of the high support and the loose distinctions has \(K\le6\) exactly when some retained record crosses both. Those are all pair readings needed for the rival assignment tests. Loose distinctions at the same holder are interchangeable by a renaming of the entire prefix. In particular all still-loose high outputs have the same crossing flag. The augmented search can follow one such choice per holder on each retained transition, and it does so until the entire finite state set is closed. [Cut update and assignment test](terminal_cuts.py)
