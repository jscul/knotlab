# Leads from the origin study

The owner selected single-meeting source recognition from the preceding present-holdings study. It now takes that direction and B214, the question of the cost of recovering an original start. The current proof and experiment are tracked under the new epic. Two further questions arose in the proof assignments and are recorded as open leads.

- **Cost for one marked birth.** The whole response to all pending joins can distinguish many candidate sources for a single current presentation. Holding one proposed parent set fixed gives a different target. Compare its actual immutable counts over compatible origins and seek matching lower and upper bit bounds. A proposed test uses three spent witnesses and a group of parents meeting them in different pairs. It has not been scored in this study.
- **Exact ambiguity after parity closure.** At an open complete boundary the set of live holders identifies the last cohort. At a closed parity boundary any single zero-fresh last holder is a candidate. Determine how many such candidates can give genuinely different source histories, modulo renaming, by testing the closed eight-knot presentation and then an asymmetric one.

Both are deferred for independent selection. This page records questions, with scientific outcomes in the study's proof and experiment records.
