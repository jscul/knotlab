# A newly written distinction at a later join

On the enrolled branch, one preparation produces a joint child and two one-parent children. The other produces four one-parent children from the same two proposed parents. A distinction created at H, then paid to one of those parents, carries the change. Under the primary not-all-equal reference it is the **only** difference in the six values the veto reads. Under parity the paid distinction also requires a deviation on a fresh companion. Either suffices to trip the veto. The alternative not-all-equal reference produces the same redundancy. ## Preparing and reaching the same proposal

The single founder holds the three initially private distinctions \((p,u,j)\). A deviation is the difference of a physical bit from its transported reference. The veto reads whether **any** mark on **any** proposed parent deviates. It reads the complete old holdings before allocation. A refused joint proposal releases its payments for one-parent births. Old bits persist. The parity reference and minimum-deviation write come from the earlier study. The two not-all-equal reference continuations are supplied in this test. Both pairs of loaded words are lawful on the identical founder. Exchanging the values at \(p\) and \(u\) preserves its relation, its reference, and the value at \(j\). The deviations are 101 and 011 respectively under either law. The only supplied change of actual values occurs before growth.

There is one live founder, so the first cut necessarily pays each of its three marks to its own child. Call their recipients H, U and J in the order of the **physical payments** \(p,u,j\). Their common parent witnesses every pair and witnesses the three together. The ordinary rule proposes the three separate pairs. Each first child therefore gets two fresh marks, enough for both proposed obligations and also the floor for a one-parent child. In the signal arm H and J are active on their inherited marks. In the diversion U and J are active. Thus every one of the three pairs is vetoed in **both** preparations. Each child pays both of its fresh marks to separate one-parent children. There are six births and all six get two fresh marks. Each has one sibling proposal through its own parent, and the one-parent floor is two. Let \(h_0,h_1\) be the two new marks at H. Let \(G_i\) be the child actually paid \(h_i\), with fresh marks \(a_{i0},a_{i1}\). One common older holder, H, witnesses the group \(\{G_0,G_1\}\). Neither has any separate partner in this cohort. This selects the same proposed join in both loaded arms before inspecting the values. In the ascending display with source marks 0,1,2, H is knot 1 and holds (0,3,4). Its two children are knot 4 with (3,9,10) and knot 5 with (4,11,12). These are the six physical marks read at the target. The old mark \(p\) remains shared by the founder and H. None of \(p,u,j\) enters either target parent. The diversion changes no funding or holder on this part of the history.

## The complete guard input

In the diversion H receives \(p\) at reference, its minimum write is its fresh reference, and both \(G_i\) are entirely at reference. This is the matched **locally quiet** target. The globally quiet founder takes a different course, described below. Use the same transported reference in both loaded histories. The following table gives the exact signal record relative to the diversion. At the one affected parent, bits are in the order (paid mark, first fresh, second fresh). The other parent’s triple always agrees with its reference.

| Law and transport | H’s fresh reference | Affected \(G_i\) reference | Affected \(G_i\) actual | All differences in the complete target read |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Parity | 00 | 000 | 110 or 101 | \(h_i\) and exactly one \(a_{i\ell}\) |
| Primary not-all-equal | 00 | 001 | 101 | \(h_i\) alone |
| Opposite homogeneous not-all-equal | 00 | 011 | 101 or 110 | \(h_i\) and exactly one \(a_{i\ell}\) |

Under parity H receives one deviation on \(p\). It must write an odd fresh pair. Both minimum pairs 10 and 01 are available, one at each carrier \(h_i\). Once \(G_i\) is paid that mark, its own unary parity equation requires another odd fresh pair. This accounts for every \(i,\ell\) in the table. No other old distinction carried a separate input into \(G_i\). Its two positive guard inputs have a common cause along successive births. Either input alone would make the supplied OR return veto.

Under primary not-all-equal, H receives actual 0 in place of reference 1. With paid reference 1 the lexicographically first legal fresh reference is 00. The actual fresh word 00 would make H constant, so its complete set of minimum writes is again 01 and 10. The chosen \(h_i\) carries 1 in place of reference 0. At \(G_i\) the lexicographically first fresh reference for paid 0 is **01**. This pair is mixed and works for either paid bit. Both of its fresh distinctions therefore stay at reference, under the unique distance-zero write. The full qualified parent records on the two arms differ solely at 3 when H writes 10 and solely at 4 when it writes 01. Each possibility is reached. With the frozen uniform choice among local ties, each named mark accounts for half the signal runs.

Under the alternative reference, a homogeneous paid value gets a fresh reference consisting entirely of its opposite. H still writes one of 01 and 10. At \(G_i\) the fresh reference is now **11**. The changed paid bit 1 would make the actual triple constant with that reference. Exactly one fresh bit must switch. This accounts for both alternatives in the last row. A read that omits the two H marks would still veto in this case and under parity, and would pass under primary not-all-equal. This is a check on the Boolean predicate on the reached states.

Even in the primary case, H has no independent one-bit write command on the same complete past. At fixed signal input its minimum writes can move the deviation between \(h_0\) and \(h_1\), and every choice vetoes the pair. Erasing the sole written 1 while leaving the signal history fixed makes H’s actual word 000. Conversely, at the diversion one can change a single \(h_i\) from 0 to 1 while satisfying the static relations at H and \(G_i\). That assignment violates the minimum write at H’s earlier birth, where the reference was already legal. The individual causal result concerns two reached histories whose **entire guard inputs** differ at one physical mark. Their first difference is the declared exchange of two founder values.

## Paid consequences and all minimum writes

Acceptance spends one private mark from each \(G_i\) on a common child. The two remaining private marks each fund a one-parent child. In the ascending allocation the joint child in the diversion receives (9,11) from (4,5), and the other two children receive 10 and 12 singly. Refusal spends all four marks on separate one-parent children in the signal arm. Their payments are 9, 10, 11 and 12 singly. Thus the observable distinction is whether two of these marks acquire a common new holder. Every mark is paid and the parent pair is funded in both arms.

The U pair has the reverse outcomes. The J pair vetoes under parity and under the opposite homogeneous reference. In both cases J’s changed founder input forces one changed fresh mark, which it passes to one of its two children. Under the primary not-all-equal transport the paid reference at J is 0, its fresh reference is 01, and J’s actual paid value 1 needs no change in that mixed pair. J is active in the second cut on its inherited \(j\), but the two parents of its third-cut proposal are quiet. The J pair therefore joins. Under this primary transport **every private mark of all six third-cut parents** agrees with its reference. The H veto reads a value that has already been paid and shared.

The outcome counts below are distinct full histories of absolute words on the ascending allocation, per loaded preparation. Each count includes all legal minimum writes. The three entries in each sequence refer to the consecutive cuts.

| Loaded feedback history | Births at each cut | Number of complete valued prefixes at each cut | Target outcome in signal / diversion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Parity | 3, 6, 11 | 4, 16, 64 | Veto / join |
| Primary not-all-equal | 3, 6, 10 | 2, 2, 2 | Veto / join |
| Opposite homogeneous not-all-equal | 3, 6, 11 | 4, 16, 64 | Veto / join |

For each joined pair the deciding roster has one joint and two one-parent births. For each vetoed pair it has four one-parent births. All deciding children have two fresh marks. In a joined family the joint child has two next proposals, each with a different one-parent sibling. Each one-parent child has its ordinary floor of two. In a vetoed family the floor also suffices. There are no shortages.

Under parity and the opposite homogeneous transport two first-cut parents each have two minimum choices. At the next cut the two changed marks go to distinct unary children, each of which again has two minimum writes. At the deciding cut the two active families are vetoed, sending each of the two changed private marks to a separate unary child with two minimum writes. Every other birth has a unique minimum. These factors give \(4\cdot4\cdot4=64\) separate histories per arm. In primary not-all-equal the two alternatives at the first affected H or U are the only ties. All later actual payments at the deciding cut agree with their references, so every final write is unique. The factors multiply because newborns have disjoint fresh banks and their paid values are already fixed. Each constructed reference and actual word is globally lawful by induction, since each new row is satisfied and no old value changes. On unrestricted triples and quadruples both local laws exclude a word, have a lawful word, and allow every assignment after one place is forgotten. Fixing a prepared input or calculating its deviation is a reading of these relations.

## Ordinary, quiet and identity controls

On the identical six-parent predecision holdings, the ordinary rule retains all three sibling pairs. It funds the same joint at H on either loaded record and gives nine births total. With the same value-writing convention its final history counts from the loaded founders are 64, 2 and 36 for parity, primary not-all-equal and opposite homogeneous not-all-equal respectively. This comparison keeps the **first two feedback cuts**. For the last count there are 16 reached predecision words. Two families each hold one changed private mark. Across the tied histories, the number of these marks sent singly instead of with a partner is 0, 1 or 2 in respectively 4, 8 or 4 prefixes. A singly paid change forces two possible minimum writes. In a joint child it makes the paid actual values mixed, so the fresh reference remains legal. The full count is \(4+8\cdot2+4\cdot4=36\).

Ordinary growth from the source follows a different paid history. It retains the three initial sibling pairs, which spend all six fresh marks of H, U and J on joint children. Neither unary recipient required for the target is born. All three resulting children then fund one triple. Parity closes there with no fresh mark. Not-all-equal supplies one fresh mark at that triple. The birth counts are 3, 3 and 1. Under either loaded input the complete ordinary histories number 4 for parity, 2 for primary not-all-equal and 4 for its alternative. Only the first cut offers a tie. Each binary child has one fresh place, whose minimum is unique, and the final birth has at most one fresh place. These are genuine missing targets in the ordinary-source control. The quiet feedback control also has 3, 3 and 1 births, with one complete valued history for each convention and the same missing unary recipients. In general, on a quiet word the actual paid values equal their references. The lawful fresh reference is available at distance zero and is the unique minimum. Induction keeps all subsequent marks at reference under either transport and under parity, including any legal zero-fresh closure. These traces demonstrate formation of a new record of a **prepared** signal. A fully quiet input supplies no first deviation.

Other admissible binders can choose different physical marks for the retained joint. The two H marks must still pay two distinct unary recipients before the deciding cut in both loaded arms, and their unordered pair has the same six-place read up to transported identities. Acceptance always produces a common child of the two recipients, while refusal always pays four different children. Coherently renaming the source, all payments, the reference-slot order and the designated \(p\) carries this result with the enrolled branch. Exchanging the upstream values at fixed \(p\) moves the effect to the other family. The two founder preparations have the same total birth roster up to exchanging H and U, and the same birth counts. The location relative to the externally enrolled physical mark detects the change. The guard itself needs only the actual and reference values on its proposed parents.
