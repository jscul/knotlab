# Frozen design for feedback from an allowed state into knot growth

The substrate is a finite connected binary parity presentation with each distinction held by one or two knots. The unaltered A104 funded complete round is the comparator. The target is a law whose next **physical incidence** or number of distinctions changes when the current allowed assignment changes while the old incidence stays fixed. A111 supplies one transported zero reference. We use the relative assignment throughout. Loose means held once, live means a knot has at least one loose distinction. All new empirical results are withheld until this design and its source hash are committed. Coordinates, ages, source roles, stored ring, distance roots and the rates below are available to the evaluator only.

## Closed candidate family and what an event means

The two possible readings of a proposed group of parents are 0, when every distinction on every parent has its reference value, and 1 otherwise. The reader sees the **whole current support** of each parent. It reads no parent name, ordering, growth history, radius-to-ring, rates or externally set branch bit. The zero-output comparison that reads only a knot's total parity and the spent-loose-input-only comparison are separate controls. One Boolean of observable variation, one changed entry of a table agreeing on the blank, and one unit of fresh count are three different notions of least. Reading the Boolean can inspect more than one physical distinction. No assertion of uniqueness across all possible local predicates or of minimum query cost is licensed.

Each table has two independently assigned entries, in order cold and hot. Each entry is P (ordinary), V (veto a multi-parent join), + (one additional fresh distinction) or − (one fewer, clipped at the parity floor). Enumerate all 4²=16, including constant tables and quiet-changing controls. One table per case has one vote only if reporting a count of rules from this family. The current law of knots and their physical states supplies neither this activity predicate nor this table.

1. Determine all original A104 multi-parent proposals from the present live knots and meetings exactly as the existing engine does. Evaluate the tables on that fixed current input, before any allocation. Keep proposals except those returning V. Each kept proposal needs one different loose distinction from every parent. If any parent is short after veto, the complete round is unavailable: record the shortage and stop. Every otherwise unspent loose distinction, including one released by a veto, has its ordinary unary child. V acts as P on such a mandatory child, while + and − read its parent's own activity. The rule itself never tests a global count to select which group to modify.
2. On that **actual** birth roster recompute the ordinary cheapest number of fresh distinctions for every child: the maximum of structural demand from next groups, 1, and 3 minus its number of parents. Keep a sole implied closure that consumes all loose distinctions at zero fresh with the ordinary closure convention. For other children + adds one, P or V leaves the number, and − subtracts one down to max(1,3−number of parents). The minus rule may leave next-round demand unfunded. A minus choice that clips is recorded. A deficit next round is tested after that next round's vetoes. The local bit modifies the old numerical policy; its pre-existing structural demand uses the A104 roster. One registered comparator must reproduce the entire old structural calculation.
3. Old distinction values persist. Each eligible port allocation can be used. Fill the fresh distinctions with the fewest relative ones compatible with the child parity. This is all zeros for even input and one arbitrary fresh member for odd input. It is an explicitly chosen state-extension law. Keep the one closure only if its inherited parity already holds. The aggregate allowable parity relation on a scored stage is computed from its actual knots. Do not add the guard or a trajectory-observer's knowledge as extra constraints when reading bond distance.
4. A round is an unordered set of old-to-new births under A104. Every serialization of a specified funded plan must give the same joint result, and the display order is no law. Stronger tests require independence under **all** choices of old loose identities and minimum fresh lifts, first up to a common renaming preserving the original coloured stage, then up to a renaming fixing every old distinction individually. Still stronger, test whether an independently allowed change of old values can be interleaved with growth in either order. Compare one complete round at a time, with no tick interpretation. A prematurely recomputed sequential growth rule is a distinct comparator. Report a failure at the strong reading even if the original comparator also fails there.

## Predictions registered before the new runs

1. P/P, V/V, +/+ and −/− are structurally insensitive to a change of input state. Only P/P is the old structural law on every allowed source. The other twelve respond somewhere. A quiet-versus-loaded five-ring with two loose distinctions on each original knot should distinguish the four actions (pass/extra/fewer give 2/3/1 fresh per binary child, veto gives ten singleton children).
2. All sixteen have a unique coloured outcome at every completed stage of the prepared shared-only sector up to old-state-preserving renaming. On arbitrary allowed input the stronger all-allocation property should hold **exactly when the hot entry vetoes** (four tables). If every old name is fixed pointwise we predict only V/V survives on all allowed inputs. On a three-knot path, each end has a loose zero, a loose one and an occupied shared distinction; both old shared distinctions are on, middle loose is zero. Assign the two ones to one binary child or to two unary children. Under any kept hot binary join, the two orders/allocations should give zero versus two occupied fresh distinctions. A three-knot ring with one loose per knot checks the implied-closure exception. Free reordering of an independently allowed value change with irreversible growth should defeat every structural dependence along that change.
3. With cold P and hot V, one bit and one changed table entry suffice on arbitrary states up to renaming. Each refused group of p parents gives exactly p−1 additional children when its slots become unary. For a bare source five-ring that predicts five additional knots; on each of the three singly attached sources it predicts eleven. At one ordinary knot step from the five original ring holders it predicts **five extra knots** on the singly attached sources: each of the five refused pairs whose two parents are hot contributes one. Plus alone predicts first width changes, equal first-round knot counts, and, on a shared-only load with cold P and the unchanged roster, a later extra unary birth per new spare if the next round is funded. Minus can have a future whole-stage effect through a funding shortage.
4. At least one bond-radius-through-five knot or distinction ball near the singly attached source will change under P/V at age one, even with the same attachment. Predict localized nonzero anchor contrasts and an unchanged far path mark at that age. Do not assume monotone decay in bond distance, equality at subsequent ages or any specific sign for each radius. Test whether the three attached isolated-rate variants agree in their first extra knot counts; equal birth contrasts alone do not settle their metric contrasts. No direct reading of the isolated exponential growth factor is in the law.
5. Zero-relative loose inputs give zero-relative fresh inputs forever under the chosen lift. Thus a raw source ring contributes its activity bit to exactly the source boundary, and any later difference is inherited structure. If its raw state is first toggled on the exhausted size-two source knots, there should be no later structural or ball difference at any radius. Reading only consumed loose values, or only each knot's even parity, gives no source-load contrast. On the matched size-two four-response we predict a first local structural change under cold P/hot V in the intact and cut starts when a hot live knot has a multi proposal.
6. No source first-round multi proposal can have a parent on each of the two rings placed four or eight steps apart, so first-round veto birth increments should add. Expect no first-round change in receiver-exclusive births when the donor alone is loaded, with a possible change later in mixed births or in a receiver neighborhood. Distinguish a common future funding stop, a coupled birth, a change in the receiver's own local incidence and an actual change of its five stored values. Without an admitted state update, those original values must persist exactly.

The pre-freeze advisor challenged whether a ball change adds anything beyond the chosen count, raising reference shifts and action replay as controls. Replay the same recorded multi and unary decisions on the identical geometry with the old coloring used only for grading; a residual structural difference would invalidate the implementation. Jointly translate the assignment and its reference by each available allowed test difference; the relative bits must agree exactly. Translating the assignment alone may change a birth. A111's reference is one simultaneous allowed assignment, so going around a ring does not entail a port-by-port convention. A local knot count, an old path-only count and the same local activity off the ring distinguish different meanings of a response. Success is a proved selection or obstruction **and** a measured, controlled local comparison in this family. It does not require a positive rate effect.
