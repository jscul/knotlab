# Can locally concealed demand hide a free message?

**Even with the shortage hidden, an observer can send a message under ordinary growth.** Bob always adds one spare. For one message he uses the address named by his old bit. For the other he uses the opposite address. Alice keeps her address fixed. Her marked distinction gains a holder on exactly 1/4 of trials for either message. But her old bit differs from that gain on **5/8 versus 3/8** of trials. She can guess a fair message correctly **5/8 of the time**. The same works with Alice sending. Independent referee pending. ## What the two messages do

Prepare one of four lawful ordinary starts with weight 1/4 before drawing the two free messages. The observers each have two editable knots, both visibly holding one loose distinction. Exactly one owes two. Which one owes two follows from other knots' holdings beyond the observer's pre-edit access. Each choice adds one spare to one knot. Both shortages must be repaired for any birth to take place. The local detector sees an old distinction gain a holder precisely on a funded trial. Its designated answer is its old marked bit on a gain and zero on a stall. The entire local record also retains the old bit and everything newly disclosed by local births. The two detectors are separated by 17 shared-holder steps, with no chain of witnessed live pairs between them. The complete [frozen definitions, start and controls](preregistration.md) were committed at `2180807278` before measurement.

On the four starts, each of the two controllers' **entire pre-edit record** has exactly the same law for all four types. This holds given every supported assignment to the exposed guard, the two witness ports and the locally held distinctions. Thus each controller's posterior probability that its first knot needs the spare is exactly 1/2. The *joint* law of the two old records can still depend on the prepared type. Revealing the outside holders' other holdings makes the shortage visible. The physical check retains the values passed from those holders when a birth occurs. ## What is proved, and where the proof stops

At every supported pair of pre-edit records, fix the receiver's own action. Independence of its subsequent conditional record from the far action is necessary and sufficient for independence under **every** locally informed policy. For necessity the sender changes its address on one class of its own observations. The receiver can test the event of having its own particular old observation and seeing a particular new record. Nobody needs access to the pair of old records. If common funding is the only joint input to readings already determined by each observer's old record, all-policy non-signalling therefore gives one common law for the four potential responses and CHSH at most 2. More sharply, when the reading is the old bit on a gain and zero on a stall, and each fixed receiver address preserves its gain probability under a far address change, an excess of δ above 2 guarantees a signal of total variation **at least δ in each direction**. The old-bit/complement instruction realizes the adjacent correlation difference. The ordinary parity example attains equality. The probability proof covers any law with these hypotheses. [Proof and sharp controls](../../proofs/LOCAL-EDIT-POLICIES-20261123-001A/LOCAL-EDIT-POLICIES-20261123-001A-P001_policy_test/proof.md)

Optional additions give a second obstruction. On a fixed old case, adding loose distinctions at already-live holders keeps the ordinary proposal groups and can only improve funding. If both parties may also do nothing and every far policy preserves the visible gain probability, the common funding decision then agrees with the empty/empty decision on every positive-weight old case and every action. When the parties' observed live components are distinct and persist, ordinary grants and local birth extensions can be generated from the full old case and local choices. Even readings first disclosed by a birth have a common local model under the stated old-state-preserving weighting and fixed per-parent allocation. A connected six-knot example checks the distinction: both observers learn the same fresh parity bit, previously undetermined even by their two observations together. The proof covers those observations. Changes that revive a spent holder, alter the proposal groups or expose an outside holding require a separate argument. This component specialization was derived after the freeze. [Separate births and the six-knot check](../../proofs/LOCAL-EDIT-POLICIES-20261123-001A/LOCAL-EDIT-POLICIES-20261123-001A-P001_policy_test/proof.md)

On the concealed-demand preparation, withholding the spare forces a stall. Comparing that action with a fixed addition changes the other observer's gain probability by 1/4 under either knot law. Keeping every observation and optimizing this wider menu gives parity variation 3/8. On the separate panel, 30 of 33 starts had the required pair of marked holders. All nine zero/one/two-spare pairs funded on each eligible start under each law (270/270), including three archived explosive starts. On each of two deliberately built shortage controls just 4/9 pairs funded per law. The counts describe those preparations and that weighting. [Panel denominators, eligibility and nine-cell tables](optional_edits.md)

A supplied control makes the remaining distinction precise. Conceal the two marked values until after the addresses and supply quantum-realizable conditional pair correlations (1, 1/2, 1/2, −1/2) on a funded trial. Mixing this branch with the common stall gives CHSH 17/8, including every trial. No compulsory local policy on the declared pre-edit information signals. The pair law and timing of disclosure were supplied. Giving either party the right to withhold its spare restores a readable funding signal of 1/4. Concealment of a shortage, concealment of an outcome and freedom to withhold are three separately tested choices. This study establishes an obstruction for the stated ordinary mechanism.
