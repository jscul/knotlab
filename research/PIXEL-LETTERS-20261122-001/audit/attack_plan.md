# Letter-learning audit plan

The execution audit owns this plan and its checker. The producer owns the
experiment source and scored outputs. The audit will retain the authorizing
digests before the producer runs and will begin result replay after the
producer commits its blind outputs and the coordinator releases the results.

## Run authorization

- Rebuild the fixture census without importing producer code. Verify five
  set bits per 64-bit input, complete class/style/pose balance, exact
  composition of each split and each bitmap's unique split membership.
- Trace inputs and feedback through acquisition, training, saving and blind
  inference. Look for class, style, pose, split, ID or ordering cues, hidden
  state in checkpoints, and any tuning informed by held-back outcomes.
  Verify how a binary correctness mark determines a binary action target.
- Check the same fixture access and feedback budget for learned comparators.
  Check equality of the numeric comparison map against the supplied knot
  setup, Hamming tie rules, and each intervention's admissible changes.
- Verify the knot gates of the supplied component relations independently.
  Inspect whether the physical snapshot is sufficient to reconstruct fitted
  counts and whether the inference path actually reads the changed marks.
- Test stage ordering, write-once outputs, and raw-output rejection with
  synthetic or census fixtures. Look for accepted forbidden fields, corrupt
  row identities, malformed bits, incomplete output and overwrite attempts.
- Match the exact score equations, thresholds and resource accounts to the
  contract. Treat the complete finite panel as one census and do not infer
  population uncertainty from repeated poses or multiple cells of one glyph.

## Result audit

- Rebuild the data and independently reproduce every action and score. Match
  class counts, worst-condition behavior, paired agreement with the numeric
  comparator, and the registered control and learning thresholds.
- Reconstruct action-indexed fitted quantities from the feedback trace and
  from the physical checkpoint by separate routes. Compare all predictions
  before and after cleared, swapped and sham action-mark interventions.
- Locate and explain the registered orientation/combined failure or transfer
  separately from translations. Check whether equal position-agnostic
  histograms lead to identical actions and whether a numeric calculation
  suffices for the fitted candidate's behavior on this census.
- Verify output seals before consuming scores, replay from a clean state,
  try representative invalid inputs and report claim limits. This audit
  does not replace the project's external scientific referee.
