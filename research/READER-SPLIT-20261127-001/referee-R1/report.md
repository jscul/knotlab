## How it was checked

The [population rebuild](population.py) reuses the previous referees' archived start roster and growth-history driver, which calls the permitted growth engine. This review rebuilt the pair-at-birth systems from the holders at the later distinction's birth. The [split search](split_search.py) branches on disjoint reader sets, eliminating impossible choices by binary linear algebra. The [minimum-weight search](minimum_failures.py) joins subsets by parity sum. The [whole-choice test](whole_extension.py) forces a pair excluded at birth, then independently checks its conclusion by counting every splitting word at each later positive closure. The [shape check](shape_audit.py) lifts holder types back to actual disjoint failing pairs. Each certificate sample records the settings, signs and normalized state.
