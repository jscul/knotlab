## The smallest change in the tested family

Keep ordinary growth whenever all the proposed parents are quiet against A111's transported reference. If any distinction on any parent differs, refuse their multi-parent join. Each loose distinction released by that refusal then grows its own single-parent knot. New distinctions take the fewest deviations needed to satisfy parity. The reader sees current distinctions on the joining knots. The ring marks, source, predicted rate and age are used only in scoring. The law reads one Boolean and changes one entry of the original two-entry table. Computing that Boolean can take more than one physical inspection.

We froze all sixteen combinations of four actions on quiet and active groups before measurement. All four constant tables ignore the state structurally. Ordinary growth on both readings recovers the predecessor. Exactly four tables give one complete coloured result on *every* allowed input when ties are compared up to a renaming preserving the old coloured stage. They all veto an active multi-parent join. Of those four, only the rule above agrees with ordinary growth on every quiet input. Hence it is the unique minimal modification **in this family under these requirements**. Parity supplies neither the activity predicate nor a reason to choose this feedback law. Refusing all multi-parent joins is the only table that also gives a unique result with every old distinction fixed individually. ## What carries into later growth

One layer of sensing can alter unlimited growth. For the bare five-knot ring, let $t$ count added layers after the starting five knots. The blank input has exactly $5(t+1)$ accumulated knots. Loading its five original shared distinctions once instead gives $5(t+1)(t+2)/2$. The refusal turns the would-be ring of new knots into five separate paths of candidates, and ordinary cold growth lengthens each by one knot per layer. On rings of five or at least seven knots a single allowed four-distinction change on the stated ordinary handover already changes cumulative growth from linear to quadratic. A continuing source of activity is unnecessary for this structural consequence. Sustained rates of the three altered attached arrangements remain open.
