# Can ordinary growth predict the next birth from nearby knots?

**Proved obstruction, independent program referee pending.** In the class with a fixed unrolled map, under ordinary parity growth with a funded complete round, no finite radius in shared-parent steps determines the number of loose distinctions a new knot gets. This statement includes a real last birth. It survives a change of names, the removal of all ancestry from the observation, and a requirement that the source be connected.

## Counterexample from four knots

Give a central knot one shared distinction with each of three other knots. Give each outer knot two loose distinctions. In the other source add a single loose distinction to the central knot. The sources have nine and ten distinctions. Both satisfy the physical knot test when each knot is read in the joint allowed assignments. They also pass the conditions identifying ordinary growth with clusters of a fixed map. One holder witnesses all three outer knots, which excludes a first triple. Each pair is wanted and funded. The three pair births each receive one loose distinction. Only the second source also makes a single-parent birth at the centre, with two loose distinctions.

At the first reached layer, the three common knots make an entire connected part of the growing end. Each pair shares a different parent. No one parent holds all three. The anonymous part, its loose counts and every witnessed set agree exactly between the sources. Every rooted ball, of every finite radius around any of those knots, therefore agrees. The extra single-parent knot in the second source belongs to another part.

Both ends propose the same ternary birth at the next layer. In a connected physical parity presentation, sum the parity rows of all old knots. Every internally shared distinction cancels, leaving the parity of all loose distinctions. Conversely every even assignment to the loose distinctions extends. A spanning tree of the connected old holders allows any even residual demands to be met by settling the terminal holders one at a time. In the first source the target three are the whole loose set. Exactly four assignments extend and the new parity is implied. Its arity is three and its future demand is zero, so the birth has zero fresh distinctions and growth closes. In the second source the other two loose distinctions allow either parity on the target triple. All eight assignments extend. The least fresh count preserving them is one. The other two loose distinctions have their own births. Neither update stalls.

This one pair works against **every** radius, including a reader allowed to see the entire connected part. Stretching the old meetings extends the obstruction to sources where the extra birth is arbitrarily many ordinary knot steps away. The same two existing loose distinctions have bond distance two versus three in both the short and stretched versions. That distance accounts for old constraints outside the observed growing part. Under not-all-equal each triple allows all eight words, gets one fresh distinction and continues.
